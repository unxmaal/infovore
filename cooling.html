cooling

Claims: 13 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Computer fan.

Cooling needs vary by hardware: the V10 CPU needs serious cooling, the personal IRIS has inadequate cooling especially on the PSU side where 5V busbars' capacitors can overheat and fail, and the Challenge S has poor airflow reaching 122F internal drive temperatures while the Indigo2 tops at 100F and the Octane is designed for better cooling. 123 Some chips require cooling, including cache chips and ASICs for CPU, FPU, and system communication, while extra heatsinks may actually worsen cooling. 45 Cooling can be improved by adding a double fan mod to the CPU area, placing extra fans strategically to enhance airflow around the CPU module, and replacing drives with SSDs. 67 Cooling failures can occur, as when a backup datacenter lost cooling overnight and Facilities took 12 hours to fix it, and the Tezro rack cannot use quad 1 GHz modules due to graphics blocking cooling. 89
