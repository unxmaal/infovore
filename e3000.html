E3000

Claims: 11

The E3000 is a desk-side server that requires a specific shelving and baffle setup for rackmounting, and like the UPA desksides, it had poor airflow. 12 The E4000 had more CPU slots than the E3000. 3 E3000 disks were prone to failure and were eventually phased out in favor of SSAs and SANs. 4 Fuses in the PPS of the E3000 can blow, while E3000 models are fine regarding e-cache parity errors, which were mostly a problem on Enterprise 6X00 and 10K machines. 56
