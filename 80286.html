80286

Claims: 3

The Poppy computer pairs an 80186 for CP/M with an 80286 for Xenix, with the 186 serving as an I/O processor for up to 10 dumb terminals. 1 Microsoft made a firmware for Jazz MIPS machines that includes an 80286 emulator. 2 The 80286 is often overlooked for introducing protected mode, despite the 68000, 68030, and 68040 receiving more recognition. 3
