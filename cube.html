Cube

Claims: 23

The SGI Cube is an early IRIX demo, and the official trademark name is the sgi cube. 12 The Cube supports FW400 and has no PCI slots. 3 The Cube can boot from a disk image on a SCSI drive using the 'Previous' feature, and it can boot after a battery swap. 45 The Cube's MO drive needs recapping or unplugging, and the main board's BR-2/3A battery should be replaced if flat to ensure booting. 6 Replacing the HDD and CDROM with SSDs and adding a Firewire CF card reader in the CD ROM slot provides flexible storage options for the Cube. 7 The largest SSD on the IDE bus for the Cube is 120GB. 8 The Cube's video card supports composite video input and output. 9
