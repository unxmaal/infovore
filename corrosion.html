corrosion

Claims: 11

Corrosion can occur when equipment sits in a humid environment, as seen with the Indigo. 1 Corrosion can affect various components, including corroded or stripped screws on an I2 system, severely corroded audio circuitry on the 1200, and SCSI drive boards that fail to appear on the bus. 234 Corrosion can originate from bad electrolytic capacitors, which can fail and sometimes cause the cap body to fall off, and from aggressive flux not fully cleaned off on factory-reworked joints. 56 Corrosion can cause functional problems, such as a PSU connector that smoked when tested, and can be difficult to remove, especially on high-density boards, though most boards can be restored. 78 Corrosion can be addressed by grinding off the top layer of corroded metal with a micro engraver without breaking component legs or grinding off the copper traces. 9 Corrosion can be prevented by painting and by selecting components for wide-temperature ranges, and corrosion on the exterior of a power supply may not indicate internal board damage. 1011
