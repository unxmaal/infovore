6502

Claims: 18 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see ARM architecture family.

The 6502 was designed to be inexpensive and mass-produced, and MOS's version was cheaper than Intel chips, including the 8080. 123 It is simple to program, cheap, and decently fast for consumer operations, and can be implemented with fewer components. 45 Most people use assembly for the 6502, which can use zero page as a bank of registers. 67 The 6510 is a variant of the 6502 with additional I/O pins and tristate bus capability. 8 It took 6 weeks and 3000 transistors to reverse-engineer the 6502 according to the visual6502 FAQ. 9
