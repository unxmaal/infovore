VRAM

Claims: 20 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Video random-access memory.

Double buffering uses two VRAM buffers—one for screen display and one for drawing—swapping once drawing is complete to ensure smooth graphics, though this requires more VRAM. 1 VRAM capacity affects what resolutions and graphics effects a system can support, as seen with the SGI V10, which can technically reach 1600x1200 but has limited VRAM for textures, making it impractical. 2 VRAM problems can cause graphics faults, such as the 'pinstripe of death' on GTX RMs, which produces vertical lines of stuck pixels and is unrelated to system RAM. 3 On Linux, the Infiniband HCA can RDMA directly into the VRAM of an Nvidia Quadro GPU. 4

Sources

  1. Double buffered means you have two buffers for your video output, one for screen display and one for drawing, with a swap once drawing is complete to ensure smooth graphics but requires more VRAM. (user-b1da, 2021-12-24, exchange 101368, check: supported)
  2. The V10 can technically support 1600x1200 resolution, but with limited VRAM for textures, it's not practical. (user-baf6, 2023-07-29, exchange 39399, check: uncheckable)
  3. The 'pinstripe of death' is a graphics issue on GTX RMs causing vertical lines of stuck pixels, often due to VRAM problems, and is not related to system RAM. (user-86e1, 2023-04-21, exchange 222999, check: uncheckable)
  4. On Linux, the Infiniband HCA can RDMA directly into VRAM of an Nvidia Quadro GPU. (user-ecd0, 2022-05-22, exchange 29394, check: uncheckable)
