U30

Claims: 5

The U30 is a SPARCengine Ultra AXi motherboard that uses the same slot CPU module as the U2/60/80 and has some 64-bit PCI slots and two UPA slots. 12 The U30 and later workstations had 3.3V PCI64 slots. 3 The U30 was discontinued early and only reached 300MHz, while the next step up for PCI machines was the U60. 4
