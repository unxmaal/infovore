MicroVAX

Claims: 17 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see X Window System.

Early MicroVAXes were QBUS based with the CPU on a QBUS card, whereas the VAX 6000 used XMI. 1 A DECstation 5000 shares the same CPU as a MicroVAX. 2 The MicroVAX can boot Ultrix 4 from a CDROM on SCSI2 and install to a second device, and IRIX 3 could run on a MicroVAX. 34 The MicroVAX can use a graphics card, and the VAXstation II was a MicroVAX with Q-bus graphics. 56 MicroVAX or VAXstation 3100 can be found for an okay price on eBay but are slow and require fighting up a 500 mile hill for peripherals. 7 The high schools had MicroVAX computers for record keeping, usually seen in main offices. 8
