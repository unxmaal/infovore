IRIX 6.1

Claims: 7 Part of IRIX 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX.

IRIX 6.0 is a 64-bit version branched from IRIX 5.2 using o32/o64, while IRIX 6.1 is more like IRIX 5.3 and introduced the o64 ABI, which caused compatibility issues with the GNU/gcc toolchain. 123 IRIX 6.0 and 6.1 will run COFF binaries but are not recommended except for R8K systems or those with a great desire for pain. 4 IRIX 6.1 has an Applications disc with the catalog number 812-0383-001. 5
