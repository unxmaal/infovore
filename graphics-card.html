graphics card

Claims: 55 Part of graphics Background: 8 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Graphics card, SGI Octane, Silicon Graphics.

MAME requires specifying the graphics card via the command line, supporting either -gio_gfx xl8 or -gio_gfx xl24. 1 An Octane2 can handle 1920x1200 resolution with the right graphics card, and higher resolutions than 1920x1080 may work with a new graphics card. 23 An Indy wouldn't accept any graphics card, booting headless but failing POST with XL8 or XL24. 4 If a graphics card is not installed, the system errors. 5 The Onyx4 compute node can be repurposed into a Prism by replacing the graphics card. 6 The graphics card is on a PCI riser. 7 An ET4000 graphics card can freeze up the system, requiring a hard reboot. 8 The C8000 has issues with its power supply, graphics card, IDE and SCSI controllers. 9
