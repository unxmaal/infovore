cycles render engine

Claims: 3 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Blender (software).

The Cycles render engine in Blender generates realistic output more easily than the previous Blender version and is used to represent metals accurately. 12 It works well for denoising and rendering scenes like the Cr2. 2 The Cycles render engine requires nodes, including a de-noising node, for proper output. 3
