Classic

Claims: 26 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Macintosh Classic, Free and open-source graphics device driver.

The Macintosh Classic was a low-end upgrade from the Macintosh Plus, and low-end Macs like the Classic and some IIs were made in Singapore around that time. 123 The Classic has 2 megabytes of RAM and a 24-bit address space. 45 The Classic's hard drive required a cold start and some time to spin up, and while the 250 megabyte drive needs this, the original Classic likely came with a 20 or 40 megabyte hard drive. 67 The Classic uses the older AMD7930 sound chip, which does not handle ISDN, and the LX and Classic use the same audio chip. 89 The Classic and IPC machines use AUI and 10BaseT for Ethernet, respectively; they can support 100BaseT but are too slow for 100Mbit Ethernet. 10 Most 80s Macs hold up well, but cheap Classic and LC units and Mac LC models suffer the most. 11
