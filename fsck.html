fsck

Claims: 11

fsck is a tool used to check and repair file systems, entered at the ok prompt. 1 It does not check the disk itself. 2 Running fsck might repair a corrupt disk, fixing minor issues, but it can actively worsen more severe problems. 34 It can be used to fix issues such as Jumpstart for Solaris 7 after a power outage. 5 The final field in /etc/fstab controls boot-time behavior: a value of 0 prevents forcing an fsck at boot, while any non-zero value forces one. 6 A/UX has issues with partitions larger than 2GB due to fsck problems, even in QEMU. 7
