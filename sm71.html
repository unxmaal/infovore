SM71

Claims: 6

  • SM71 and dual SM61 are good options for SS10; Hypersparc 180 for SS20. (user-a8c6, exchange 240366, 2024-10-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Later SM71 modules have an enhanced MXCC that can work with older SM71s. (user-08fb, exchange 240367, 2024-10-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The heatsink design is correct for an SM81 but could be an underclocked SM81 with a 85MHz rated MXCC, which isn't uncommon; the user has an SM71 with a 90MHz rated MXCC (user-08fb, exchange 240797, 2026-02-09; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The Sm61 and Sm71 on the same SS20 show similar performance scaling with clock speed, particularly in 7z benchmarks. (user-08fb, exchange 240804, 2026-02-11; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • I should benchmark the SS20 with the original SM71 and the 125MHz Hypersparc. (user-04a3, exchange 180546, 2021-03-09; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • OBP 2.12.4.2 is used on multiple OBP versions of the same seller's hardware, with some models having SM71s and others hyperSPARCs that are not recognized correctly. (user-08fb, exchange 187505, 2026-04-11; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
