Qlogic chips

Claims: 5 Part of QLogic

Qlogic chips were bidirectional SCSI transceivers used in host or initiator mode, handling command streams, bus timings, and DMA transfers. 1 Disk adapter sleds used Qlogic chips to emulate IBM disks, with an m68k for additional commands and VPD. 2 The qLogic chips have firmware that may be updated during install or via patches. 3 These Qlogic chips were out of production over a decade ago, making it difficult to use them now. 4 An FPGA could potentially replace Qlogic chips for SCSI handling, but would need bus drivers and might not source/sink enough current. 5
