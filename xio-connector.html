XIO connector

Claims: 19 Part of XIO

The O350 and derivatives have one NumaLink and one XIO connector on the back of the brick, while the Prism-GFX Brick has two NumaLink connectors. 1 The O300 has an XIO connector for an InfiniteReality graphics pipeline, but it may require a router brick for full functionality. 2 The XIO blanks have a lump of soft foam to cover the XIO connector, which can stick to the backplane and damage the connector on the next board inserted. 3 The XIO connector should not be messed up, so pictures of it should be taken for reference. 4 The XIO connector was hyper-miniaturized as a flex but made more fragile in the process. 5
