SunOS 4.0.2

Claims: 3 Part of SunOS

SunOS 4.0.2 and a beta of 4.0.3 were available before OpenWindows. 1 The Sun386i ran SunOS 4.0.2 and included a DOS emulator that was quite compatible, though only 8086. 2 SunOS 4.0.2 is the only version supported, with gcc-2.6 as the maximum toolchain version. 3

Sources

  2. Many years ago I had a Sun386i. It had a 25MHz 386 and ran SunOS 4.0.2. It was a pretty obscure platform, but it included a DOS emulator that was quite compatible (only 8086 though). I sometimes played Tetris. (user-03ad, 2022-05-31, exchange 182210, check: supported)
  3. SunOS 4.0.2 is the only version supported, with gcc-2.6 as the maximum toolchain version. (user-03ad, 2024-07-18, exchange 240338, check: supported)
