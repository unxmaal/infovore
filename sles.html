SLES

Claims: 13 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Prism.

Altix systems can run various Linux distributions, with SLES or RHEL being recommended, though the Altix 350 can boot only RHEL and SLES, often failing on other versions due to memory mapping issues. 12 SLES is acceptable as a distro but is not preferred as a modern one, and YaST, its tool for autoconfiguring the system, frequently makes mistakes requiring manual intervention. 34 SGI put tons of work into the kernel, releasing open source updates and bolt-on proprietary patches distributed with its customized SLES releases, and used SGI Advanced Linux on some of the bigger Altix systems, where Propack 4 was SLES. 56 The Prism requires SGI's specific version of SLES to work with accelerated desktops on a PCI card in an Altix, using SLES with a customized ATI fglrx driver for Itanium and dual AGP cards, and you need the matching propack for the correct SLES SP version. 789 QuickTransit has worked on SLES on an Altix but not on RHEL, possibly due to processor incompatibility, and did not work fully in a SLES VM, while its licenses work with 1.2 but require setting the date back or using libtimefake to avoid expiry in 2005. 101112 The Albatross can only run SLES. 13
