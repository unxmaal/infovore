SDL1.2

Claims: 7 Part of SDL1 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Simple DirectMedia Layer.

SDL 1.2 is no longer in active development, and SDL 2 is also becoming outdated. 1 Some programs depend on SDL 1.2: schismtracker requires it to run and play mod/xm files (with VICE included for PSID files), and C-Ray is a ray tracer that depends on it and can serve as a floating-point benchmark, with its compiling process also acting as a benchmark. 23 devilutionX failed to load due to alignment issues with the MPQ library rather than SDL 1.2, and it might work with SDL 1.2, though it is probably easier to build. 45
