temperature

Claims: 5

The `scsicontrol` command can retrieve temperature data from SCSI drives, returning the current temperature in Celsius via the subcode `0x1`. 1 Some machines were sold mostly for harsh environments, such as aircraft, where altitude, temperature, and vibrations could destroy a spinning drive. 2 A system originally controlled multiple PDP-11s that monitored temperature, humidity, and gas concentration samples from many sensors in a research greenhouse. 3 These machines had inconsistent reliability, often failing if the `reboot` command was not used or if the machine ran too hot. 4
