SATA drive

Claims: 3 Part of SATA

An IRIX system with a SATA drive may hang during drive self-test. 1 Swapping the SATA drive with one more appropriate for the system might help. 2 The boot filesystem should be on a SATA drive, and root and home on NVMe. 3
