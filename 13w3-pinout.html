13W3 pinout

Claims: 4 Part of 13W3

The 13W3 pinout evolved over time, with different configurations used by Sun, SGI, and others, affecting compatibility between adapters and displays. 1 The 13W3 pinout includes H/V sync pins, implying some video cards output that as well. 2 The Octane outputs via the 13W3 pinout. 3 The 13W3 pinout varies between SGI models, using 4 pins for older monitors and DDC for newer ones, and the Reality Engine uses one pin for powered peripherals. 4 Some dip switch cables can be used on O2 but may require a unit. 4
