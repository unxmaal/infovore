Glitchworks

Claims: 15

Glitchworks provides replacement and refurbishment Dallas chip modules for vintage SGI workstations such as the O2 and Octane/Octane2, as well as for Sun systems. 12 The company sells maintainable replacements for the DS1687-5 battery, including options with a coincell for the O2 and Octane that are preferred over the original. 34 GlitchWorks replacement modules fit the same footprint as the originals and are easily obtainable. 5 The company also offers an M48T08-1 battery board that fits an SS10 and can be used for an Ultra 5 with a M48t59y-70pc1, an M48t59 topper, and kits that assist with reprogramming the NVram battery and reviving a DS10 with ARCS PROM issues. 678 The 'board only' product does not include the coin cell holder and crystal and costs $10, while Glitchworks does not actively sell DS1386 modules. 910
