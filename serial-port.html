serial port

Claims: 53 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Serial port.

A serial port is a communication interface that can be directly tied to the processor in embedded platforms. 1 A serial port operates over a wide voltage range up to 15 V and typically uses TTL or CMOS logic requiring a threshold of 3V or 5V, allowing some devices to work with lower voltage supplies. 2 A serial port is not a video port and cannot be used to connect a monitor. 3 The serial port baud rate varies by platform, with values such as 38400 for IP35, 300 baud for the Apple IIc, and 115200 baud for some configurations. 456 Serial ports are used for a range of functions including console access, diagnostics, modem connections, and early internet access. 78910 A serial port and terminal emulator are sufficient for accessing the Internet. 11
