SRAM

Claims: 6

The powercap module board contains SRAM with a 4-bit wider address bus compared to the old Dallas chips, and the clock registers are at the top of the address space. 1 The DS1744P-70 contains the SRAM and the DS9034PCX provides the battery and crystal, connecting via tabs to pads on the SRAM module for compatibility. 2 SRAM features non-destructive reads and writes, 20-year data retention, and loses data without power, unlike the FRAM used in the Sega CD FRAM mod, which is used for permanent saves. 34 The JJ has a 68000 CPU with an EPROM and SRAM, and its serial port is connected to a SCN68681 DUART. 5
