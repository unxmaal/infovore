SIMM slots

Claims: 5 Part of SIMM Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

The STE has SIMM slots and requires 4MB RAM. 1 The machine had intermittent issues that were resolved by resetting RAM and deoxidizing SIMM slots. 2 The Indigo2 has 12 SIMM slots, and memory must be added in sets of four. 3 A Pixelated LX with a CPU/cache error turned out to have just dirty SIMM slots. 4
