EISA slots

Claims: 5 Part of EISA Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

EISA slots are physically deeper than ISA slots, and the added address lines do not connect for regular ISA cards. 1 In some systems, ISA cards can be used in EISA slots without an adapter, and 16-bit ISA cards might work in EISA slots on non-x86 systems. 23 The Indigo2 has EISA slots and supports some addon cards, such as an old Sound Blaster card. 4
