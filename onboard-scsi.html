onboard SCSI

Claims: 3

The HP 9000 series 300 workstations used a 68040 CPU with onboard SCSI, high-resolution color video, and networking. 1 The T1 AC200 is a small, quiet, and cheap machine, basically a Blade 100 in a 1U form factor with onboard SCSI, and it was very good for what it was supposed to do. 2 Using the XP1000 HAL may work, but the onboard SCSI may be incompatible according to a post on a German forum. 3
