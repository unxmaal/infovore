VFO

Claims: 15

A .vfo file can be used to fix sync glitches on green monitors, but writing one requires specific preprocessor defines for the MGRAS chip and board that are not well-documented. 12 Custom VFOs may be necessary for non-standard resolutions, and the compiler must be installed to create them. 3 The ESI board can display 1920x1080 at 56 Hz but with display artifacts, requiring multiple VFO compilations and macro value guesses. 4 Making a VFO for an Octane with a V10 can result in display artifacting issues, even with custom configurations. 5 The mla on a 1600sw sees a 1280x1024 resolution even when running a VFO that should support 1600x1024, causing vertical artifacting. 6 IRIX can handle a 1:1 aspect ratio with a custom VFO, and a 1600x1080 VFO can be compiled for an HD monitor with a 16:9 aspect ratio, resulting in 320 pixels of black on the right side. 78 SoftImage 3D requires a single-channel display for 1920x1080 resolution, otherwise it defaults to 1280x1024, and changing the VFO dynamically is not supported without logging out and back in. 9 To adjust the VFO, one needs to tweak the script to move the screen, and the VFO can be converted to time for more precise adjustments to allow for finer tuning of video timings. 1011
