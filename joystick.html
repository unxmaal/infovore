joystick

Claims: 6

Binding a rudder to a joystick's left and right axis is not recommended, as it can cause unwanted movements and simulate bouncing issues in flight simulators. 1 The Gravis Phoenix joystick uses a digital/analog hybrid design for improved tracking and can be used in digital mode with special drivers, but DOS games generally did not support this. 2 A 500 Amiga with an RGB2HDMI, a joystick, and a CF card set up with WHDLoad packed with games is 90+% of the Amiga experience. 3 The joystick requires a 100ohm resistor with +5v. 4 Someone has patched the modeler to use a keyboard instead of a joystick. 5
