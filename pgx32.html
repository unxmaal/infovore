PGX32

Claims: 4

The PGX32 is treated as a 2D accelerated framebuffer rather than as 3D acceleration, and XSun does not expose 3D components on the PGX32 or XVR-100 as 3D acceleration. 1 The Ultra 5 does not support UPA or 64-bit PCI, which limits upgrade options to PGX24, PGX32, or PGX64 cards. 2 The drivers for the graphics card are on the Solaris installation CDs, and the PGX32 and UPA cards like the Creator or Elite work well. 3 The PGX32 and UPA cards like the Creator or Elite are better for serious graphics. 4
