spinlock

Claims: 3

  • A spinlock is a mechanism for synchronization between threads or parts of the software, where one waits in a tight loop until a condition is met. (user-6c80, exchange 223877, 2024-05-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • A spinlock involves a CPU in a tight loop checking for the lock to be released, instead of waiting for signals or semaphores. (user-cdcd, exchange 223877, 2024-05-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • IRIX may use spinlocks for ensuring exclusive access to files during write operations to avoid interleaved data. (user-cdcd, exchange 223877, 2024-05-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
