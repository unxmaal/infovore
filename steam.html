Steam

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Ubuntu.

Steam is used to download game files, as demonstrated by running Morrowind on a Mac via OpenMW with manual configuration. 1 Proton is built into Steam on Linux by default. 2 The typical KDE Plasma desktop in Steam turns janky, and Lutris has issues with WINE and Esync. 3 Ubuntu had to include 32-bit libraries for Steam support. 4 Troubleshooting is often needed for Steam installs on Linux. 5
