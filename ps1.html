PS1

Claims: 31 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see MIPS architecture, PlayStation 2.

The PS1 uses a custom graphics API built on GTE macros, matrix functions, and primitive macros, with an OpenGL/GPU renderer that has less FLOPS than the CPU, making it a potential decelerator. 12 The PS1's CPU is an R3K MIPS processor, and Sony used MIPS CPUs for the PS1 and PS2 before switching to PPC for the PS3. 34 The PS1 and PS2 have firmware and a Unix-based OS in their BIOS. 5 The best PS1 model mentioned is the SCPH-7501, which has good video output and a Parallel I/O port, and PS1s are easy to fix and still useful for parts if beyond repair. 67 Development for the PS1 can be done using emulators and transferring programs over serial or parallel, or burning them to discs, with homebrew and hobbyist tools available for PC. 89 Writing bare metal for the PS1 requires a devkit or using a parallel port with a cheat module, but emulation is more accessible, and the PS1 is easier to get good performance out of without being a top-tier programmer. 1011 Gran Turismo 2 ran on the PS1, showing the hardware's capabilities improved over time, and Gran Turismo is optimized for the PS1, favoring polygon counts over high-quality textures. 1213
