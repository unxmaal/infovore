texture mapping

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIS GL.

Texture mapping is a key part of 3D visualization, reducing the need to ray trace every point. 1 The SGI RE pipelines were all about vertices, texture mapping, and pixel fills. 2 IRIX supported texture mapping through IRIS GL. 3 Texture mapping can be faked in software but is not suitable for real-time graphics; most 3D games require hardware texture mapping. 4 Texture mapping & fill are likely bottlenecks on a 2nd gen VPro card. 5 A fallback for older SGI machines without texture mapping can be added. 6
