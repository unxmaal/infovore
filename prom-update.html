PROM update

Claims: 6 Part of PROM

The PROM update command updates the list of hardware, but may require formatting and reinstalling IRIX for new hardware compatibility. 1 A PROM update might fix RAM issues, and a tool for PROM repair is available. 2 An Ultra 1 can be booted with a prom update from a Solaris 7 install CD-ROM. 3 Installing IRIX 6.5.22 on an O2 can result in a panic during bootp(), while 6.5.0 does not, and updating the PROM might be necessary. 4
