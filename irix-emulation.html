IRIX emulation

Claims: 4 Part of IRIX Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see QEMU.

IRIX can be compiled under IRIX emulation through QEmu, where a 10900K CPU runs IRIX at about 29-33% of speed. 12 The demand for IRIX emulation is low within a small community. 3 Network setup in IRIX emulation using OpenVPN requires bridging a TAP adapter to the local network connection, but updates of Windows 10 or 11 may require fixes. 4
