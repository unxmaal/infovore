Professional IRIS

Claims: 40

The Professional IRIS is a tower-based computer that came in both single-tower and twin-tower configurations, using VME as its system bus. 1234 It was the only SGI machine placed in a single-tower case, and a single-tower Professional IRIS must be a factory configuration if it uses VME. 34 The Professional IRIS has a yellow chromate chassis, while the PowerOne PSU of the single-tower PowerSeries is chrome-plated steel. 5 It has a 1000W PSU that draws 450W from the wall, indicating a substantial power budget for a 12-slot backplane. 6 The Professional IRIS has a built-in SCSI controller and offers ESDI as an additional VME board, and it supports SMD and IPI disks. 78 It uses VME as its system bus, and jumpers on the backplane configure the VME bus, with incorrect settings rendering the system non-functional. 2910 The VME ethernet card on the Professional IRIS is referred to as 'fxp' in the driver. 11 The kernel configuration for the Professional IRIS from IRIX 4.0.5 lists supported hardware drivers. 12
