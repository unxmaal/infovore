VGA

Claims: 66 Background: 18 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Video Graphics Array, Component video, Digital Visual Interface, Dreamcast.

VGA was the gold standard in the workstation world for a while, and it was the best standardized analog RGB format to come out of the PC platform, though UNIX 13W3 connections were technically superior but bulkier and more expensive. 123 VGA can support resolutions up to at least 2048x1536, and it can be adapted if the monitor supports the right features. 45 The DVI interface is compatible with VGA, and VGA can be passively converted using a cable or cheap converters. 67 Some monitors still have VGA input, though most modern monitors lack it. 89 The Propeller Chip can output VGA, though at low resolution. 10 VGA is a video standard that can be output by various devices, including the Octane, which outputs VGA that can be displayed on multiple HDMI monitors, though a signal issue occurs when using a VGA to HDMI scaler. 11 A monitor limited to VGA ports restricts the resolution to 1280x1024. 12 For VGA, all grounds should be tied together for signal integrity. 13 Monitors can sync to a wide range of signals, including SOG adapted to VGA. 14 A VGA card can run EGA. 15 VGA can be adapted from other connectors, such as 13w3 using a BNC to VGA adapter and a gender changer, or BNC to VGA or 13w3 connectors which have the same color order. 1617
