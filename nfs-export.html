NFS export

Claims: 3 Part of NFS

  • NFS exports need to be set to force a specific version, such as NFS3 or NFS2. (user-24f0, exchange 88540, 2025-11-03; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • You can mount SGI images on Linux with `mount -o loop,ro,norock,map=off $image /mnt` and then NFS export `/mnt` to an HP-UX box for use as a local CDROM. (user-237d, exchange 161284, 2021-10-08; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • IRIX 6.5.22 may require specific options for mounting NFS exports from FreeBSD; using `mount` alone may not suffice. (user-b98d, exchange 27331, 2022-03-17; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
