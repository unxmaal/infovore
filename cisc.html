CISC

Claims: 11 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Reduced instruction set computer.

The distinction between CISC and RISC has not been a relevant metric since SGI began using RISC microcode. 1 Modern x86 processors are RISC-like with a CISC hardware layer, and the Pentium 4 was the last true CISC processor. 23 The CISC vs RISC distinction was always marketing. 4 In CPUs, a pipeline is a series of stages that process instructions in parallel, but CISC instructions can be long and complex, making it harder to achieve full parallelism. 5 Most RISC chips have load/store operations for memory, while CISC chips have many addressing modes. 6 Classic RISC CPUs limit the number of faults a single instruction can cause to usually two, while CISC architectures like x86 can have up to 13 faults and potentially unlimited faults. 7
