AMX

Claims: 4

The AMX is a model of Avocent KVM switch that works well with vintage SGI machines. 1 It is compatible with all tested SGI systems but lacks IP capability. 2 It has not had trouble with BNC-HD15 adapters on SGIs. 3 The AMX requires Sun modules, which are hard to find, and the Java client works through a VM but has high latency for games. 4
