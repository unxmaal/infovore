firewire mass storage

Claims: 3 Part of Firewire

Firewire mass storage should work. 1 Picky drivers allow firewire mass storage to work on Fuel (and possibly Tezro) but not on O2/Octane despite both having PCI and the ability to take the same cards. 2 IRIX can see firewire mass storage as a mountable SCSI volume on IP35, possibly on O2/Octane, but firewire mass storage does not work on O2s with the DV card. 3
