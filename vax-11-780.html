VAX 11/780

Claims: 9 Part of VAX

The VAX 11/780 was the first VAX model, an overbuilt minicomputer. 12 It was originally going to be called the PDP 11/780 but the name was changed. 3 It had microcode for instructions, but it was slow and had weird datapaths. 4 DEC's RM05 disks were rebadged CDC disks and used a massbus adapter to connect to the VAX 11/780's MASSBUS interface, with manuals lacking visual details; adapters were about the size of a cabinet and could support up to 2. 5 The VAX 11/784 was a quad processor system, while the 11/780 was four VAXen with shared memory. 6 It is used as a computational device in the HOSC, with two designated as MIPS Primary and Backup, and two more planned for future expansion. 7 A joystick for a VAX 11/780 is relatively inexpensive given its rarity. 8
