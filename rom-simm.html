ROM SIMM

Claims: 4 Part of ROM

A ROM SIMM is required for the chime and startup process involving PRAM and clock. 1 The SE/30 motherboard does not fit in the SE because the ROM SIMM is too tall, and powering it on in the SE results in no chime or video output, though the machine turns on and the disk spins up. 2 ROM-SIMM has benefits and downsides, including the need for different system drives and incompatibility with a/UX. 3
