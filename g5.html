G5

Claims: 95 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Emacs.

With G4

Some open source software may not be compatible with all PowerPC processors, such as Apple's PowerPC G4 and G5. 1 The G5 was the fastest thing on the market and beats both the G4 and G3, while the G4 is slower than the G5. 234 The G5 supports PCIe GPUs, whereas the G4 does not, and the G5 can do more than the G4. 56 G4s are all plastic, unlike the metal G5. 7 A G5 is slightly faster than a G4 in 32-bit mode. 8 The last generation 'jet engine' G4s are noisy and not much better than lower end G5s. 9 G4 or G5 Xserves struggle with NFS handling at gigabit speeds. 10 G4 / G5 towers can run Linux or OS X. 11

General

The G5 was based on the POWER4 architecture, scaled down from it, which made it hotter than the G4 but not as hot as contemporary chips like the P4 or Athlon XP. 1213 First generation G5s had air-cooled single and liquid-cooled dual processors, while second generation G5s had air-cooled singles, duals, and liquid-cooled quads with PCI Express. 14 The early 2003 G5 had a 1.6 GHz CPU. 15 The G5 has poor reliability, with first generation models experiencing RoHS issues that led to reliability problems and one CPU often being faulty. 1617 The G5 with liquid cooler (LCS) gets very hot and may need servicing. 18 A G5 cannot run OS 9. 19 The G5 can use SCSI devices and can be useful for running older macOS versions like Leopard, though it has had issues with power supplies. 20 Recapping the PSU of a G5 can fix multiple failures and can be done with higher voltage ratings for longevity. 2122 The G5 is a PowerPC 970 with two altivec units, dual FPUs, a longer pipeline, and supports PCIe and DDR2 RAM. 23 The G5 reached its maximum clock speed with the PowerPC architecture; 3 GHz was the limit. 24 The G5 came out in 2003 with PCI-X support, and an Opteron motherboard with PCI-X was available around 2003 or 2004. 25 The G5 does not support CPU upgrades; only PCI cards and RAM can be changed. 26 You need to install memory in pairs in a G5. 27 The liquid cooled versions of G5 generated a lot of heat. 28 Blocking airflow on a G5, which produces a lot of heat, is not recommended. 29 The G5 can experience graphics issues at user input, possibly related to DVI ports. 30
