Nidec power supply

Claims: 4

A failing Nidec power supply can cause noisy output for an Indy/XL. 1 The Nidec power supply on an Indy is known to sometimes fail completely, with no LEDs or other indication, and can be fixable with recapping. 2 It is probably safe to keep an Indy with the Nidec power supply on for several hours on end. 3

Sources

  1. The noisy output for an Indy/XL can be due to a failing Nidec power supply. (user-d903, 2020-05-29, exchange 96951, check: supported)
  2. The Nidec power supply on an Indy is known to sometimes fail completely, with no LEDs or other indication, and can be fixable with recapping. (user-62ed, 2020-08-28, exchange 221049, check: supported)
  3. It is probably safe to keep an Indy with the Nidec power supply on for several hours on end. (user-59fe, 2021-05-15, exchange 205060, check: supported)
