Gotek

Claims: 14

The Gotek is a USB-based floppy drive emulator that can replace failing PS/2 floppy drives, which commonly fail after years due to bad capacitors, and can be retrofitted into an external floppy drive for an Amiga. 123 It stores floppy images for most games and can emulate floppy disk operations for games like Dogs of War and Lemmings, producing a distinctive sound. 45 New hardware such as the Gotek, pi1541, and SCSI2SD can be used with Amiga systems, and disk images can be transferred to an A500 and written to floppy using WHDload or the Gotek. 67 The Gotek is limited to floppy images when networked, requiring a serial-to-wifi bridge on a pi for other media. 8 The Gotek had bad firmware out of the box and required a custom harness to flash it, which would crash halfway through, and Flashfloppy firmware is available for it. 910 An unmodded Gotek is available on eBay for $57, an OLED mod for it is easy and cheap, and it can be mounted to the bottom of a case, though the hole for it is on top, making disassembly awkward. 111213
