IRIX 6.0.1

Claims: 6 Part of IRIX 6.0 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX.

IRIX 6.0.1 is based on IRIX 5.2 with added R8000 and 64-bit support, while IRIX 6.1 is based on 5.3. 1 IRIX 6.5 was preferred over both 6.1 and 6.0.1. 2 Installing IRIX 6.0.1 can fail with a readerror. 3 The IRIX Patches 6/95 disc is available for IRIX 5.2/5.3/6.0.1. 4 There is a more complete version in the oman archive, which can be found at https://forums.irixnet.org/thread-3419.html. 5
