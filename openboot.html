OpenBoot

Claims: 15

OpenBoot is a bootloader written in FORTH that is used on many PowerMac computers and some Sun systems. 12 It uses FCode for device drivers and is a loose standard that varies by hardware vendor. 3 OpenFirmware is based on OpenBoot, and both support FCode. 4 FCode has CPU ISA-agnosticness limitations due to incompatibilities between Apple Open Firmware and OpenBoot. 5 OpenBoot defaults to white on black text. 6 Selecting the boot device in OpenBoot involves using commands to specify the device. 7
