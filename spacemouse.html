Spacemouse

Claims: 3

The Spaceball and Spacemouse provide the same functions as dials, namely local graphical transformations such as rotation, pan, and zoom. 1 The SpaceNavd driver provides a custom protocol for 3D input devices, supporting both the Spacemouse and Spaceball protocols, and can be adapted to work with Xinput-compatible programs. 2 CATIAv4 supported buttons and dials on both IBM and SGI workstations, and the SpaceMouse was also used but not widely adopted by artists. 3
