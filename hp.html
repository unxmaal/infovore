HP

Claims: 293 Background: 9 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see QEMU, OpenGL, HP OpenMail, Itanium, Quake (video game), SGI Altix.

With Solaris

Solaris and HP-UX were among the UNIX-like operating systems available, alongside AIX, IRIX, A/UX, and SEIUX. 1 There is a significant difference between HP-UX and Solaris. 2 PHIGS was available on HP-UX but not Solaris, while GL was included with Solaris starting around 2.5.1 or 2.6 with U1E/U2. 3 AppleTalk works in HP-UX but not in Solaris. 4 The HP-UX version of WordPerfect is not update 4, unlike Solaris. 5 HP-UX uses `dld.so` for its runtime linker, while Solaris uses `ld.so.1`. 6

With SGI

HP, IBM, and Sun were involved with the internet before SGI, and both HP and SGI made 68k machines that ran UNIX, though SGI and Sun switched quickly to their own CPU designs while HP used the 68040 for longer. 789 Intel convinced DEC, SGI, and HP to abandon their own designs and join the Itanium project, which was supposed to dominate the market and kill AMD, and HP and SGI became the main Itanium vendors. 101112 The merger of Intel, HP, and SGI was problematic, and Belluzzo led both HP and SGI through controversial transitions to industry-standard solutions that led to financial losses for SGI. 1314 SGI provided HP with some of their CPU supply, and by the time HP bought SGI, it was already dead. 1516 HP now uses the SGI moniker for supercomputers running x86 CPUs with Linux distributions. 17

With Itanium

The development of Itanium started at HP before Intel joined, and the architecture was made primarily to replace PA-RISC, much as Alpha was considered new when Itanium was introduced. 1819 Itanium was used primarily by universities and 3-letter agencies, and ran several operating systems including OpenVMS, HP-UX, Windows, GNU/Linux, and possibly NetBSD and HelenOS. 20 HP-UX on Itanium has 32-bit ELFs, and the compiler can only produce 32-bit code, with no 64-bit version of GCC available. 212223 The Itanium could run PA-RISC code via HP-UX, which was generally fast though not as efficient as native PA-RISC, and it could run PA-RISC binaries without issues despite there being no direct software emulation layer. 24 IA64 Mathematica runs on Itanium HP-UX but with poor performance, and Patran 2006 R1 is available for HP-UX on both parisc and itanium architectures. 2526 HP has not sold a custom compiler for Itanium in some time, and the RX 2620, which has dual 1.3 GHz Itanium processors and 4 GB RAM and runs HP-UX, cannot currently boot into EFI. 2728 In ~2008, HP salespeople said they still had financial industry customers on VMS/VAX supported on custom contracts, and Itanium was met with ambivalence by customers who had moved or weren't bothering. 29 HP's Itanium effort did not fare well and they survived only due to financial backing. 30
