SCSI connector

Claims: 8 Part of SCSI Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The SCSI connector on an Octane is for external (ultra) wide SCSI. 1 The riser is a dumb adapter for SCSI that uses a 68-pin SCSI connector. 2 The O2 uses a weird SCSI connector for its dongle. 3 The plastic around the SCSI connector on the internal port is too close to plug a standard SCSI cable in. 4 The Indigo's 'hinv' command does not show a tape drive if the SCSI connector on the backplane header is not fully seated, since power connections are sufficient but not SCSI connections; reseating the connector carefully fixes this. 5
