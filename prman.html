PRMan

Claims: 13

PRMan is the original Reyes renderer based on micropolygons and implements the shading language, while BMRT was a one-man raytracer for the RenderMan interface. 1 PRMan added raytracing features and was rewritten to support RIS, which shifts focus to global illumination and requires using OSL. 2 RAT is the Maya to RIB translator plus SLIM and other tools, and there are versions of RAT for most versions of Maya and PRMan. 3 MayaMan 4.0 supports PRMan versions BMRT and Entropy, and many PRMan versions, including RenderDotC and Entropy, are compatible with Houdini. 45 Amazon Paint 3D uses PRMan for rendering 3D and has a working version on Linux. 6 Soft shadows in older PRMan versions were often created by blurring the depth map based on light position. 7
