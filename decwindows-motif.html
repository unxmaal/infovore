DECwindows Motif

Claims: 5

DECwindows/Motif is a graphical environment available in Digital UNIX. 1 On Alpha, it is a port of CDE like you'd find on any 90s unix, while on VAX it is something more basic; CDE requires an Alpha. 2 The VAXstation 4000/60 can run DECwindows Motif, but it is not a fast machine and DECterm is the primary application. 3 A 3000/300X gives you a 175MHz EV4 and is nice for DEC Unix 3.2 with DECwindows Motif. 4
