OpenRCT2

Claims: 7

OpenRCT2 was made by reverse engineering the RCT2 binaries and rewriting it all in C. 1 OpenRCT2 requires little-endian systems, causing issues on big-endian machines like the G5 Quad, while OpenTTD is compatible with big-endian systems. 23 OpenRCT2 requires SDL2 and version 2.0.1, and there is a working SDL2 binary for macOS PPC. 4 OpenRCT2 doesn't have stable OpenGL rendering yet and, for some reason, the multithreading isn't enabled by default. 5 Switching to a 64-bit OS on the Pi allows running OpenRCT2 at a higher resolution and smoother performance. 6 OpenRCT2 runs better on an Altix with a 733 MHz Pentium 3 compared to the speaker's childhood machine. 7
