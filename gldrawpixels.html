glDrawPixels

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

The SGI video output (vo) plugin may be broken or glDrawPixels may be very slow. 1 The SGI video output (vo:sgi:pixeltex) uses pixel_texture (rgb) and glDrawPixels for drawing. 2 Using OpenGL and glDrawPixels can significantly outperform X11's XShmPutImage on the Indigo2 Elan, especially for blitting operations, but other methods like textured quads and triangles are less effective on this hardware. 3 glDrawPixels is significantly faster than textured polygon rendering on the O2, even with vsync disabled, and is a clear winner for pixel rendering across different systems. 45
