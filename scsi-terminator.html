SCSI terminator

Claims: 5 Part of SCSI

A SCSI terminator can be constructed from wire and a diode. 1 Sun SCSI terminators are used to terminate Sun SCSI buses. 2 In an Indigo2, the SCSI terminator is located at the back of the CD-ROM tray. 3 The R3k requires a SCSI terminator to boot correctly, and using an external Apple drive causes issues with mounting ISOs or other data, specifically with the /dev/scsi path. 4
