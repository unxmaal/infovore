DG1

Claims: 4 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Tantalum capacitor.

The RV2 board likely has different video output timing circuitry compared to RV1 and RV1.5, with its raster portion probably similar to RV1 and its video portion possibly closer to DG1. 1 RV2 and DG1 are different, with RV2 being GTX and DG1 being VGX. 2 DG1 has an MTBF of 29700 hours, compared to 15648 hours for VGX, 15721 hours for GE6, and 6000 hours for RM2. 3 Tantalum capacitors in DG1 with the EV1 option board can fail and explode. 4
