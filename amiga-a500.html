Amiga A500

Claims: 3 Part of Amiga

The Amiga A500 felt painfully slow due to the OS in ROM and slow floppy drive. 1 There was a period of Amiga A500 and A2000 production where C= had to use static column memory chips due to a part number mix-up, requiring a small PCB to adapt the system without changing behavior. 2 The Amiga A1000 and A500 models did not use batteries, whereas the A4000 and A3000 did. 3
