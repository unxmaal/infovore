serial cable

Claims: 14 Part of serial

A serial cable can be used to remotely access a machine from a PC, transfer files, debug it live, or start a console on a serial port. 1234 The full hardware handshake and correct pinout are necessary, and a proper serial cable requires all modem control lines. 56 A missing pinout caused issues on a 0300, and the SS10 may need its serial cable pinned out correctly or a keyboard and monitor connected directly if it has a video card. 57 A serial cable can work even if it is not a null modem cable, but connecting a serial cable between computers requires a null modem adapter, while a straight-through serial cable is needed for console access. 8910 A simple RS232 to screw terminal adapter with dupont wires can be used as a serial cable for under $10, and the manual calls a serial cable the same type of serial cable the Mac used. 1112 There is not a good way to route the serial cable out of the machine, and the machine needs to be shut for good airflow. 13 Booting into console d mode and using a serial cable connected from a PC can resolve installation issues. 14
