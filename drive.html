drive

Claims: 156 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see RAM drive.

A drive may refuse to boot, be stuck, or take a long time to format, and these problems can be explained by drive errors, a bad cable, a bad drive, incorrect jumpers, or a corrupted drive. 1234 A drive that throws read errors, enters a loop when copying a specific block, or shows a blank or failed diagnostic message is likely dead, and a drive that spins but does not seek may be caused by unstable PSU voltages. 5678910 Formatting a drive can ensure there are no defect sectors, and powering a drive in a working machine can test whether it spins up. 1112 Cycling power to the drive after reseating can resolve read errors, and freezing a drive can sometimes resolve issues. 1310 The 'probe scsi' command can indicate if the drive is offline, which might help diagnose connection issues. 14 Cloning a drive with VPro from an O350 to a Tezro should work. 15 The drive can't read the disc, not the 512/2048B thing. 16 You can set the PROM variables from inside the OS to choose which drive to boot from, but physically swapping drives is not necessary. 17 The drive is incompatible with CD-R and DVD-R media, but works with Futurama. 18 Swapping the drive to another system can help determine if the issue is with the drive itself. 19 A replacement laser assembly can fix a dead drive in an Xbox. 20 The drive is non-yellowed. 21 A bad drive was the issue, not a damaged motherboard. 22 A zero file size can indicate an incomplete copy, especially when restoring an image, so check the file size in Windows and ensure the drive is properly ejected before reconnecting. 23 A running left motion can fill up a drive with too much logging. 24 It will take $50-100 to get an old drive up and running. 25 The Monterey installer can be difficult to run on an MBP, requiring multiple PRAM resets and formatting the drive to succeed. 26 For a 50-pin drive, you might need a SCSI card with a 50-pin header. 27
