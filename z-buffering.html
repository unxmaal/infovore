Z-Buffering

Claims: 4 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

Z-buffering is supported by OpenGL in every version, where drawing order only matters for alpha blending. 1 Z-buffering was present during the Indigo era. 2 The r4400 Indy can perform hardware span rendering for untextured polygons with gouraud interpolation and z-buffering. 3 BP3 boards in slots 10 and 11 are used for z-buffering rather than pixels. 4

Sources

  2. The Indigo era had Z-Buffering. (user-8de9, 2022-02-06, exchange 101991, check: supported)
  3. The r4400 Indy can perform hardware span rendering for untextured polygons with gouraud interpolation and zbuffering. (user-21b8, 2022-08-18, exchange 126804, check: supported)
  4. BP3 boards in slots 10 and 11 are used for z buffering, not pixels. (user-6c80, 2024-02-29, exchange 241173, check: supported)
