ioDrive

Claims: 6

The ioDrive was the first PCIe Flash drive, and it achieves over 2GB/s read speed on a system with a QX6850 CPU and an EVGA nForce 680i SLI board, saturating the PCIe system. 123 Each PCIe lane on the EVGA nForce 680i SLI motherboard only allows for 250MB per second, so the maximum speed for the ioDrive is 2GB per second. 1 The system generates significant heat, comparable to that of a Quadro Plex. 4 The IO Drive is not bootable. 5
