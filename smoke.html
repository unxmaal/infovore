Smoke

Claims: 13

Smoke and Flame are related software products with overlapping functionality, though they differ in some modules and features. 1 Later versions of both Smoke and Flame were released in 2008 and required importing frames for video editing. 1 Smoke requires more disk bandwidth than Flame and includes hardware checks. 2 Smoke can be run on an O2, but may require additional setup for video I/O and the tablet, which is not necessary for all features. 3 Although an O2 can run Smoke with a fast CPU and NVMe storage, hardware checks prevent it. 4 The AXUS fibre does not work with Flame 2008, but works well with earlier versions of Flame and Smoke. 5 Modern video formats may not be directly supported by Smoke or Flame on IRIX. 6
