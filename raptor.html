Raptor

Claims: 4

Raptor uses IBM's POWER9 implementation in its hardware, which features a dual socket board and a cheaper CPU, costing $2600, while AMD's POWER9 CPU costs around $2.8k per chip but incurs board overhead costs. 12 RISC-V and PowerPC boards are available, with SiFive providing RISC-V boards and Raptor offering PowerPC boards, though they may be sold out or overpriced. 3 The Raptor had a high markup on their hardware, similar to Apple's pricing strategy. 4
