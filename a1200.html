A1200

Claims: 42 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Amiga 1200.

The A1200 is an AGA computer that comes with internal IDE, a PCMCIA slot, and an onboard clockport that can be used for an RTC. 1234 It can use a Compact Flash card via an IDE-CF adapter to move data between Windows and Amiga, though a buffered CF-IDE adapter is needed for stability with Indivision AGA because the ports are directly connected to the CPU. 156 It can run AGA-only games and AGA-enhanced versions, and an A1200 with 4MB RAM and an internal IDE-CF drive can play almost every Amiga game with WHDLoad, which requires the correct A500 kickstart. 78 It can be accelerated with options such as a 50 MHz 030 card, a Terriblefire accelerator, a TF1230 with 128MB of RAM, or a TF1260, and a BPPC can run both 040/060 and PPC. 910111213 However, expansions for an A1200 can get pricey, and the machine has some issues, including video problems over RGB and composite, audio channel problems, SMD caps that are problematic due to high manufacturing temperatures, and a clock crystal that cannot exceed 14 MHz. 1415161718
