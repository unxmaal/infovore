BlueSCSI

Claims: 149

With ZuluSCSI

ZuluSCSI is a SCSI emulator that is faster than BlueSCSI, easier to use than SCSI2SD, and uses filename-based setup, while BlueSCSI is an open source community where anyone can contribute and is cheaper than ZuluSCSI, which is closed source though its hardware is open. 123 The biggest difference is that BlueSCSI and RASCSI are hobbyist projects, while ZuluSCSI and SD2SCSI are more business-oriented projects. 4 ZuluSCSI and BlueSCSI are basically the same, and both can achieve up to 20MB/s, saturating any 10MB SCSI bus. 567 They are great for old Macs, Amigas, SGI, and Suns, and changing out a hard drive for a BlueSCSI or ZuluSCSI can help with thermals. 89 SanDisk SD cards have been reported as causing issues with BlueSCSI and ZuluSCSI. 10

With RaSCSI

BlueSCSI is based on RaSCSI and can use the same images as RaSCSI, assuming you have a SCSI card in the IIgs. 1112 BlueSCSI uses a Bluepill STM32 microcontroller and is cheaper than RaSCSI but has less multifunction potential if all you need is SCSI emulation. 13 RaSCSI and BlueSCSI share core SCSI signaling but differ in their environments. 14 RaSCSI can emulate the BlueSCSI. 15 The BlueSCSI and RASCSI firmware fixes disk utility and apple-branded disks issues, improving compatibility. 16 There are preinstalled and blank images available for rascsi/bluescsi here: https://github.com/erichelgeson/BlueSCSI/wiki/Usage#premade-images. 17

General

BlueSCSI is a cheap SCSI emulation device that performs similarly to the v5 and feels faster than traditional solutions because it removes latency. 1819 It works well for Mac Plus, is compact enough for external use on a Mac and a w30 sampler keyboard, and can act as a USB/SCSI connector. 202122 BlueSCSI supports CD/DVD and other optical devices, making it a nice CDROM replacement with good ISO support and emulation capabilities for various operating systems. 2324 The BlueSCSI fork called GreenSCSI uses a Teensy and may support tape emulation, while the F4 version uses a blackpill with more space that could also support tape emulation. 2526 BlueSCSI does not work with SGIs, and AzulSCSI is an alternative with slightly better hardware. 2728 BlueSCSI is a SCSI emulation device that works with older Macintosh models using a DB24 connector, as well as with SGI systems and RS/6000 machines. 293031 It can emulate multiple SCSI units, with the SCSI ID determined by the image filename (for example, HD1_IRIX_63.img corresponds to ID1). 3233 Basilisk and SheepShaver both recognize BlueSCSI drives, and the associated extension ignores BlueSCSI so that hard drives are always read as raw files. 3435 The firmware version can be retrieved using the BlueSCSI API, and the BlueSCSI IRIX CLI tool is available on GitHub for testing. 3637 The WiFi version of the BlueSCSI works as a wireless connection, and the Mac OS identifies it as a Dayna SCSI-Ethernet adapter, making software transfer more convenient. 3839 The BlueSCSI is generally suitable for older SGI models but not for the O2/Octane2, and it is somewhat slow and designed primarily for classic Macs rather than SGI systems. 404142 It cannot be used as a CD emulator to install from because it lacks a way to change the CD image during installation, and swapping CDs can only be done through the OS's eject function. 424344 The BlueSCSI is inadequate for large transfers such as video files, but works well as a CD-ROM replacement on an O2. 45 BlueSCSI is a SCSI replacement device that can be used instead of an internal hard drive in SGI systems such as the Indy, Indigo, O2, and Octane, and can be installed on the 50-pin CD-ROM connector. 46474849 BlueSCSI drives are simply images, making it easier to use than SCSI2SD, which uses sectors on the flash. 50 BlueSCSI firmware remains GPL, same as always. 51 BlueSCSI is slightly less compatible with SGI systems. 52 BlueSCSI requires specific jumper settings, including setting the termination jumper to off if the Indigo has a terminator at the end of the SCSI cable. 5354 BlueSCSI firmware updates have caused issues, including Indigo 2s not booting after updating to v2025. 55 Network install is the best method for BlueSCSI installs, especially since CD drives are becoming harder to find or fix. 56
