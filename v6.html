V6

Claims: 53 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane, V10 engine, V12 engine.

With V10

The V6 and V10 share 32 MB of total memory, of which only 8 MB is available for texture memory, and both are limited to a 32 MB framebuffer. 123 The V6 and V10 processors are virtually identical, with the V10 differing only by a process shrink and a pixel clock bug fix that is easy to work around. 4 The V6 and V10 cannot support 24- or 32-bit colors, and their limited texture memory affects performance in Quake3. 56 The V10 is a bit better than the V8 from V6 onward, and is preferred for its VRAM needs. 78 The V6 is sufficient for Blender and Maya, which care more about CPU speed, and the V10 requires Xbow 1.4 while the V6 can use Xbow 1.3. 910 The V10 has no space to add RAM chips, unlike the V6. 11

General

It has 32 MB of memory split between 20 MB framebuffer and the rest for textures, plus 8 MB of texture memory. 1213 The V6 outperforms the MXE in geometry performance and TRAMS, and compares to the RealityEngine in performance. 14151617 It received more texture RAM than the MXE and is considered superior to the ESI. 1819 The V6 works with XBOW 1.3, and V6 and V8 models are supported by Xbow. 2021 It has 32 MB of memory split between 20 MB framebuffer and the rest for textures, plus 8 MB of texture memory. 1213 The V6 has a clock bug that only affects certain resolutions, and it cannot run 24-bit color due to insufficient framebuffer memory. 2223 Resolutions between 109Mhz and 193Mhz won't work on V6/V8 systems and will be ignored by xsetmon. 24
