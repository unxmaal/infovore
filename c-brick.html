C-brick

Claims: 13

The MPX, PCIX, and C-brick can be used as a 3-brick system with MPX connecting to C-brick over NUMA and PCIX over XIO; the biggest O3x0 without R-bricks is a system with two C-bricks and any two other bricks without a R1. 1 Beyond two C-Bricks, each additional C-Brick requires an R-Brick per rack. 2 Typically an O350 with an IO9 is just a CBrick unless it has graphics, too, and 'Base node' is also used in the documentation. 3 The Altix A350 c-brick can be made bootable with a SCSI-PCI adapter and external disk or netboot, and boots to EFI with specific partition details. 4 For physical access, connecting serial to the fastest C-brick or O300 (for booting) in L1 allows communication with other bricks on the router. 5
