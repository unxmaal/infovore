M1

Claims: 23 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Apple M1, AMD.

The M1 chip is built on a 5nm process and contains over 2.5x the transistors of a 290x, with many more transistors than AMD or Intel CPUs, including on-chip RAM. 12 The M1 is a fresh start architecture based on the iPhone A series, featuring a known ISA and a portable software ecosystem. 3 The M1's GPU is nearly 24% faster than a 1050 Ti, and its neural engine is a custom architecture processor for machine learning calculations, capable of up to 11 trillion operations per second to aid in voice recognition, image processing, and video analysis. 45 The M1 is considered the best single-threaded chip available and is brilliant in terms of performance per watt, beating every single laptop in its price range in performance. 678 The M1 is an appliance that cannot be upgraded like an AMD CPU, with limited upgrade options for RAM and storage, and its storage is soldered to the motherboard but could theoretically use regular NVMe. 91011 The M1, M2, and M8 SoCs are SMT capable and have no expandable slots. 12 OpenBSD 6.9 offers preliminary support for the M1, while Marcan and others are working on Linux support, which is not well supported. 1314
