Amiga 2000

Claims: 9 Part of Amiga

The Amiga 2000 is an OCS/ECS model with many expansion slots, but can be loud due to its fans. 1 It is used for time-sharing, but stopped working when multiple users tried to use it simultaneously. 2 A 2MB ECS model is sufficient for most needs, and a 5.5mm hex socket works on its ports. 34 The Amiga 500 and 2000 use the same ROMs, which are burned using the TL866 burner and the 27C400 adapter. 5 An Amiga 2000 cannot boot with an adapted SCA drive plugged into its SCSI controller. 6 The first Betacam editing suite in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, was installed in 1987, featuring an Amiga 2000. 7 The Amiga 2000, 4000, and maybe the 3000 are considered the only Amigas worth having. 8 Gateway Pentium, Amiga 2000, Mac II, PS/2 55SX, and Ultra 5 will be sold at deep discount prices by VCF, though the Amiga 2000 might not get a discount. 9
