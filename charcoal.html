Charcoal

Claims: 5

Charcoal was a reissue color for Silicon Graphics Inc. (SGI) products, replacing the earlier granite color, and it was applied to all enclosures including monitors, keyboards, and mice. 12 The SGI charcoal reissue extended to CD ROM, DVD ROM, and tape drives. 3 Charcoal CRTs were produced in both monitor styles. 4 The O2+/Octane2 switched to the charcoal color. 5
