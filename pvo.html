PVO

Claims: 5

The PVO is a video output card that connects directly into a MGRAS card via ribbon cables. 1 The PVO is an unsupported configuration for SE graphics in some Octanes, while DVO has SDI inputs and outputs. 2 The PVO card allowed for cable TV viewing on an Octane. 3 The Octane MAS options are PVO or the digital card, and PVO limits CPU and RAM types. 4 The SGI data sheet for the v6/v8 Octane 2 mentions a VPro compatible PVO which was canceled. 5
