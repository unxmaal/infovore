Octane PSU

Claims: 21 Part of Octane

The Octane PSU has arrived with previous issues resolved, using MOLEX connectors and large power rails. 12 It provides 12V with positive and negative terminals but no PWM, requiring high static-pressure fans due to its airflow design. 3 The 2x12 MOLEX header is similar to a 24-pin ATX connector, with keyed pins and shared ground connections. 4 The chassis is well-designed, but removing the PSU is not straightforward due to the backplane connection interface, requiring significant reverse engineering. 56 An Octane PSU can fail even with intact fuses due to bad capacitors, especially in series, which can leak. 7 It can emit a strong ozone smell, possibly due to overheating capacitors, and swapping a spare PSU can resolve the issue. 8 It may show no stable voltage on the white connectors when connected to mains, with readings decreasing as if capacitors were discharging. 9 It can be used in another system but may not be safe if the system has an unknown issue. 10
