Red Rock SCA

Claims: 5 Part of Red Rock

The Red Rock SCA is located in the bottom slot of the Tezro bays, which are U160. 1 The Red Rock SCA shows good performance with 4K reads and writes, though there are occasional spikes. 2 The SGI ST373405LC SCSI HDD shows significantly lower performance compared to the Red Rock SCA. 3 The Red Rock SCA + Intel DS3 S4610 1.82Tb disk performance varies significantly with different `diskperf` parameters, with some tests showing odd values and others showing more reasonable results. 4 The firmware B01F improves Red Rock SCA disk performance significantly compared to B01D, with close to 2x improvement in MB/s. 5
