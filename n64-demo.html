n64 demo

Claims: 5 Part of N64

  • Some N64 demos require an EFS partition to work, due to an error in old IRIS Performer libraries. (user-8547, exchange 217185, 2021-08-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The Everdrive allows running N64 demos on an SGI workstation; some, but not all, work. (user-d166, exchange 227156, 2020-05-05; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The n64 demo requires IRIX 6.5 and may need '-m GFXBOARD=MGRAS' and 'set rulesoverride true' to install, and 'export PFTMPDIR=/mnt' for EFS volume mounting; it fails on XFS. (user-59df, exchange 217184, 2021-08-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • IRIX 5.3 with the SDK 2.0I is recommended for using N64 demos due to better compatibility and no troubles. (user-45c8, exchange 227147, 2020-01-05; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • IRIX 6.5 had issues with demo executables and strange errors, while 6.2 had missing executables for most demos. (user-45c8, exchange 227147, 2020-01-05; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
