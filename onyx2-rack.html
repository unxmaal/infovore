Onyx2 rack

Claims: 6 Part of Onyx2

The Onyx2 rack consists of a compute module and a graphics module, where the compute module is an Origin 2000 with a different I/O controller and is connected to other compute modules via router boards. 1 The Onyx2 rack can have up to 2 graphics pipes and can link up to 8 bricks, while the Tezro rack can link up to 1 brick with Infinite Performance (VPro). 23 The Onyx2 rack has a larger floor footprint and greater height than two Onyx2 desksides combined, making it less space-efficient. 4 An Onyx2 rack is not interesting for hobbyists without presentable skins. 5
