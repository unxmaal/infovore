IP6

Claims: 28

The IP6 is a real hardware system that uses standard 30-pin SIMMs, including regular 9-chip parity modules, and is marked on the board. 123 It uses an R2000A CPU, which is technically an R3000, though it can also be a software IP6 with different processors. 456 The IP6 does not support 4MB SIMMs, though some Toshiba 4MB SIMMs can be used without triggering the PROM bug. 789 The IP6.5 is a re-layout of the IP6 for speed and cost-reduction, used for both the 4D/20 and 4D/25. 10 The IP6 does not support booting from CD-ROM. 11
