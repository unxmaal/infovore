3D graphics

Claims: 10 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, SGI Onyx2, SGI Visual Workstation.

The Amiga could do rudimentary 3D graphics two years after the NES. 1 SGI hardware running on an IBM 386 system was capable of real-time 3D graphics, though slower than native SGI hardware. 2 The Onyx2, the O2, and Visual Workstations are used for 3D graphics, with the O2 able to run 3D graphics on the CPU with limited texture RAM. 345 The BBC used an Octane for 3D graphics. 6 Interactive demos using Open Inventor can be immersive 3D graphics manipulated with dials and buttons while wearing 3D stereoscopic glasses. 7 Multiply-add is crucial for 3D graphics and matrix operations, significantly impacting rendering performance on machines without geometry engines. 8 The book 'Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice' switched to C in its second edition, focusing more on 3D graphics. 9
