NFSv2

Claims: 13

NFSv2 support is no longer enabled by default in the kernel, but the code may still be present and accessible through kernel configuration. 1 To enable NFSv2 and NFSv3, you may need to recompile the kernel, as NFSv3 is usually the default. 2 NFSv2 is compatible with IRIX, and NEWS-OS supports NFSv2. 3 NFSv2 works on IRIX 5.3, though you may have to force it. 4 The 6.5.30 kernel has issues with NFSv2 when only NFSv3 is available, and you may need to revert to NFSv2. 5 Netbooting Solaris from Linux requires setting RPCNFSDOPTS to include NFSv2, NFSv3, and NFSv4 in /etc/default/nfs-kernel-server. 6 FreeBSD hosts NFSv2 while Omnios hosts NFSv3 and NFSv4. 7 Re-sharing NFSv2 directories is acceptable if you stick to read-only. 8
