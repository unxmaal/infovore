router brick

Claims: 3 Part of router

The Altix 350 server is sometimes listed incorrectly as a router brick, though it can work for the O300, O350, and O3000. 1 An O3000 without a router brick would be pretty useless due to lack of disk access. 2 The O350 CPU does not support configurations with more than one router brick. 3
