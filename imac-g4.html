iMac G4

Claims: 4 Part of iMac

The iMac G4 provided a different experience than a PowerPlant Mac. 1 It had three USB ports on the back—one for the keyboard, one for the mouse, and one free port—plus an internal speaker and a 3.5mm audio jack. 2 The 1GHz iMac G4 came with macOS 10.2 and was upgradable to 10.3. 3 The iMac G4 is probably the slowest computer in their repertoire. 4
