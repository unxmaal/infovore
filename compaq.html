Compaq

Claims: 22 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Digital Equipment Corporation.

Compaq inherited Alpha/Tru64 Unix from Digital Equipment Corporation in 1998 and later consolidated their lines under HP, discontinuing Alpha/Tru64 and PA-RISC in favor of HP-UX on Itanium. 1 Digital UNIX/Tru64 had software for server tasks, and Compaq's free optimized compiler was useful. 2 SGI and Compaq executives thought Itanium was a good idea, but Compaq and other executives were unsure about what to do with Itanium, ultimately leading to the cancellation of the VAX line. 34 Compaq sold the IP to Intel, which killed the architecture. 5 DEC did not use Itanium CPUs; it was acquired by Compaq before Itanium was developed, and DEC's semiconductor fabs were sold off. 6
