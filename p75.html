p75

Claims: 4

The p75 is a Macintosh computer that some users report as being slower than a PowerBook G3 (R5K) in an iBook O2 form factor. 1 The P75 has compatibility issues with CF cards, even across different adapters. 2 Some p75 units fail to start and may require a capacitor recap and possible trace repair, as they use early SMD electrolytic capacitors prone to failure. 3
