Intergraph

Claims: 21 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Intergraph.

Intergraph developed proprietary technology, including the Clipper RISC CPU architecture and the CLIX version of Unix, and offered a FORTRAN compiler that was not installed on its machines. 123 Its Lynx IV geometry accelerator card contained 14 Analog Devices SHARC DSPs and could be coupled with other Intergraph cards to increase computing power. 4 Intergraph's systems, especially those with the Clipper architecture, were rare and special compared to typical x86 boxes, and the company was very closed about its software and documentation, so little survives. 56 SGI acquired Intergraph's graphics workstation division, possibly to gain GPU technology, and SGI bought out Intergraph, which competed with their VW lineup. 78
