A2000

Claims: 50 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Amiga 2000.

The A2000 is a robust, expandable, and durable machine with a processor slot, 5 Zorro slots for expansions, and local RAM and HDD upgrade options. 12 It is considered peak design and was better suited for workstations than home editions like the A500. 34 The A2000 can be upgraded with accelerator cards, such as the GVP G-Force 040, to make it faster than a stock model. 567 It can use SCSI drives, a SCSI2SD adapter, and a second floppy drive. 8910 The A2000 has issues with genlock and requires a Black Burst Generator or video camera for video input. 11 It can output digital RGB but not composite B&W. 12 The A2000 has expansion slots that are useful, and one owner paid $300 for their 2000 with an accelerator, a RAM card, and a SCSI card. 1314 The A2000 can boot either a real floppy or a GoTek drive with the early startup menu. 15 PSUs for the A2000 can fail, possibly due to SMD components. 16 The EtherBridge is not reliable for networking on the A2000 with a bridgeboard. 17
