Solaris 2.5.1

Claims: 11 Part of Solaris Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris.

Solaris 2.5.1 was released for SPARC and PowerPC machines, and Sun machines ran Solaris 2.5.1 through Solaris 10. 12 The Sbus has version limitations with Solaris, not working with versions above 2.5.1 or 2.6. 3 The firmware source and sample driver are available for Solaris 2.5.1. 4 Solaris 2.5.1 includes vsNprintf, but glibc was never ported to Solaris 2.5.1. 56
