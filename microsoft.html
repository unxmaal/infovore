Microsoft

Claims: 36 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Microsoft, Windows NT.

Microsoft supported many architectures, including MIPS, Alpha, and SPARC, and released operating systems such as OpenVMS 12, IRIX 7.0, and Solaris 11.5. 1234 Microsoft hired DEC's best OS engineers to develop NT, which was designed and implemented by engineers Microsoft poached from DEC. 56 Microsoft abandoned MIPS for WinCE and made a firmware for Jazz MIPS machines that includes an 80286 emulator. 78 Microsoft's removal of the hardware abstraction layer killed computers that were not IBM PC compatible and limited SGI's ability to run Windows. 91011 SGI sold its graphics patents to Microsoft, and Microsoft's removal of HAL support was another way it affected SGI. 1210 Microsoft dominated the office software market early on and had an illegal monopoly on x86, which had massive consumer adoption. 1314 Microsoft approached IBM about licensing OS/2 under the Windows name, but IBM said no. 15 Apple licensed NT source from Microsoft and received confidential documentation to integrate it, and Microsoft might have faced more antitrust issues if Apple had used NT. 1617
