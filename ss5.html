SS5

Claims: 16

The SS5 is a Sun workstation that can run Solaris 2.6, which can be installed from the same media without issues. 12 The microSPARC was used in all SS5 models except the 170 MHz version. 3 The S24 card is a 24-bit framebuffer for the SS5 and fits in a special slot behind the third SBus slot. 4 The SS5 is considered a nice machine for NEXTSTEP, with the SS4 or SS5 at 110 MHz able to run NEXTSTEP 3.3 or OpenStep 4.2, faster than the 10/20 SMP system, and supporting 24-bit graphics. 56 The SS5 upgrade board is less expensive than an Ultra 2 and offers better performance and additional features like PCI and onboard UW SCSI compared to U1 and U2 machines. 7 An SS5 cannot be upgraded to have an SS20 motherboard. 8
