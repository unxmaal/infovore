GTX

Claims: 12

GTX is a line of IBM graphics cards that constitutes the full graphics pipeline for a PowerSeries, and it is also known as Clover2, which was the initial graphics for the PowerSeries as found in the Professional IRIS 4D/70 GT. 123 GTX is usually broken to begin with and was failing in Iris systems around 2008. 45 An RV1 card is not compatible with GTX cardsets and is broken. 6 GTX setups can be benchmarked, and the geometry board on VGX, GT, and GTX has a serial connector for diagnostics that allows an interactive prompt but with limited options. 78 IP7 with GTX uses 1000w, IP17 with RE uses 1100w, and RE is more efficient per watt. 9 The 4D/85 brochure indicates it was released after GTX and thus after PowerSeries was introduced. 10
