Domain/OS

Claims: 9

Domain/OS is an operating system that ran on the Apollo/Domain series of network servers and workstations, which could pool resources together, and it provided both Unix environments and an Aegis environment. 12 Interworks is a collection of PD and other community software for HP-UX and DomainOS, consisting mostly of GNU stuff from comp.sys groups, and is of interest for recreating an authentic DomainOS system. 3 Domain/OS requires a specific keyboard and is ultra rare, and the Apollo NextStep can be upgraded to a 33 MHz 040 CPU and runs DomainOS with a specific keyboard. 45 DomainOS can netboot 10.3 headless from an emulated dn3500 running on MAME, and netbooting from MAME to DomainOS can work, though networking tools return 'permission denied' errors. 67 Booting Domain/OS without the display manager might help with SCSI drive detection. 8 Modern AI systems lack knowledge about AEGIS or Domain/OS, which is considered a win for the latter. 9
