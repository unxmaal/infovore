SCSI driver

Claims: 6 Part of SCSI

The SCSI driver can be broken in various ways depending on the system: a correctly configured kernel may boot while the SCSI driver does nothing when loaded, and NetBSD contains a bug in the scsi driver for this machine. 12 OpenBSD's SCSI driver refuses to use DMA with the CMD controller, limiting performance to PIO. 3 The scsi driver has an error in NetBSD 6 that causes an error on every disk access. 4 The R10K CPU has a pre-execution bug in the SCSI driver related to speculative execution that can lock a machine solid, and the bug was fixed with workarounds in the 6.5 kernel source. 5
