CD-R

Claims: 14

CD-Rs usually follow a 74-minute specification, though 80-minute discs are common on CD-Rs. 1 Later CD-Rs could have a more densely packed spiral, but many older CD-ROM drives couldn't reliably read them due to mechanical tolerances. 2 Higher speed CD-Rs can have less precise timing and a less reflective surface, leading to read failures in older drives. 3 CD-R media issues might cause problems with certain drives, and some setups are picky with CD-Rs, especially those burnt at high speed. 45 It is recommended to burn CD-Rs at the lowest possible speed. 6 CD-R media work in O2s, but DVD-RW may not, so burn at low speed and use high-quality CD-Rs. 7 DVD-R and DVD+R are more reliable than CD-R and BD-R for long-term backups. 8
