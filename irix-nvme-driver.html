IRIX NVMe driver

Claims: 3 Part of IRIX

The irixnvme driver requires an NVMe drive with DRAM and does not support TRIM/discard commands. 1 On R10000 CPUs, the driver requires cache invalidation after DMA operations for IO-to-MEM transfers due to speculative execution. 2 On O2 and Octane, the driver limits DMA operations to 1025 4K pages or 257 16K pages in one go. 3
