null modem cable

Claims: 23 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Null modem.

A null modem cable is used to connect to a serial port and can be used with standard RS232 adapters, USB-to-DB9 adapters, or a DB25 to DB9 adapter for compatibility. 1234 It connects RX to TX and vice versa, so it is required even when the pinout is otherwise the same, and a cable with directly connected RX and TX may not be a null modem cable. 56 A gender bender can convert a straight-through serial cable into a null modem cable. 7 Null modem cables are used for console access, boot prompts, diagnostics, and serial CD installation, often with terminal programs such as Putty, HyperTerminal, or a serial terminal. 8910111213 Serial diagnostics are typically done on port 1 at 9600,8N1. 14 A null modem cable may be the cause of a problem and can be checked with a serial port tester, multimeter, or by testing with and without a null modem adapter. 1516 Some components, such as a certain card, do not require a null modem cable. 17
