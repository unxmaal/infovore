IRIX 6.5.22

Claims: 215 Part of IRIX 6.5 Background: 10 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indy, SGI O2, IRIX, SGI Indigo.

With IRIX 6.5.30

The Booterizer image contains IRIX 6.5.30 and 6.5.22 and requires a 64GB SD card. 1 IRIX 6.5.22 and 6.5.30 are compatible, with 6.5.30 adding FireWire and USB support, and the differences between them are minor, with 6.5.30 dropping MIPS3 support. 23 PCI bridges are handled differently in 6.5.22 and 6.5.30, with 6.5.30 having better PCI bridge support than 6.5.22, and IP32's bridge handling being iffy with 6.5.30 being much better. 456 IRIX 6.5.22 has better compatibility, with Adobe apps running better on 6.5.22 than 6.5.30, and Netscape Navigator being included in the Communicator application on 6.5.22 but not on 6.5.30. 789 ESP Print Pro is required for printing on 6.5.30, while on 6.5.22 printing works without it. 10 Quake requires kernel tuning parameters to run on 6.5.30, unlike 6.5.22. 11 IRIX 6.5.22 and 6.5.30 do not segfault when running the emu, but it has badmatch issues launching X_CreateWindow with certain graphics settings. 12 The script for removing Embedded Support Partner (ESP) is compatible with 6.5.22 but may require additional checks with 6.5.30. 13

With Indy

The Indy supports IRIX 6.5.22, and a lower-end Indy running it serves as a good baseline for RSE work. 1415 Performance can be slow, possibly due to the R4600 CPU or a defective system, and the installer may hang if the CPU speed is overclocked. 1617 A standard installation takes up around 1.5GB without extras, and a 2GB drive was typical for an Indy running IRIX 5.3. 18 ZuluSCSI runs well with 128 MB of RAM, but performance can be severely impacted on a system with 64MB, making it borderline usable, while IRIX 6.5.22 page swaps with 32 MB. 1920 Installation on an Indy without a SCSI CDROM drive can be done with Booterizer, and converting an image from xl to xz graphics may result in conflicts requiring a reinstall. 2122

With O2

The O2 can run IRIX 6.5.22, which supports PostScript fonts in Photoshop. 23 However, users have reported numerous problems with 6.5.22 on the O2, including kernel panics during boot after an autoconfig command (fixed by upgrading to 6.5.30), panics during bootp() (which may require updating the PROM), panics during net install, and networking issues even after a full re-install. 24252627 Because of these issues, some users prefer to run IRIX 6.3 on the O2, as it has a smaller memory footprint and is marginally faster, though 6.5.22 can be run on a separate drive. 28 The O2 can also run IRIX 6.5.22 alongside NetBSD/Trunk, though NetBSD/Trunk will not recognize more than 256MB of RAM. 29 The NVMe driver was developed on IRIX 6.5.22 on the O2 and Octane. 30

General
