CRT

Claims: 83 Background: 9 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Cathode ray tube, Liquid-crystal display.

CRTs are through-hole components that are easy to repair, though the analog board is the hardest part to remove and the CRT has a fragile neck with packed components. 12 CRTs can suffer from capacitor issues that cause the picture to go off screen and make dramatic jumps, as well as issues with FETs and magnetics that require re-calibration. 34 The cathode of an SGI CRT can blow, which is dangerous especially since replacement CRTs are no longer as readily available. 5 CRTs can be adapted using a pin adapter and power resistor, and basic voltages and pinouts can be found in a Sencore CR70 set-up book. 67 Troubleshooting CRTs without proper equipment can lead to frustration. 8 Shipping a CRT requires a packing request that includes thick bubble wrap and cushioning material. 9 A CRT can be grounded by shoving a grounded screwdriver under the anode cap. 10
