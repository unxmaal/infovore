VxWorks

Claims: 17

VxWorks is a somewhat Unix-like real-time operating system designed for embedded systems and real-time applications rather than desktops or VMs, with good development tools and documentation. 123 It runs on a variety of processors, including 68k, PPCs, and MC68xx040-based systems, and is used in robotics, industrial controllers, and military applications such as jet fighters. 45678 VxWorks is considered reliable for embedded development, with one reported failure attributed to flash memory rather than the operating system. 9 Porting software to VxWorks requires creating a portability layer due to its non-POSIX nature, and it likely runs as a monolithic binary with a custom filesystem or flat data storage. 101112 VxWorks is expensive, and it is reportedly not used much anymore by the business, having been replaced by Linux and other systems. 1314
