Mental Ray

Claims: 19

Mental Ray is a ray tracing engine that can run standalone or on a master/slave system, with Maya Satellite usable with mental ray slaves to offload ray-trace renders to slave systems. 1 Mental Ray can perform network rendering and is supposed to be multi-threaded, though its threading capabilities may differ from modern implementations. 2 Mental Ray doesn't use a lot of RAM due to the blocking method, capping out at 1.2GB of memory. 34 Mental Ray needed a smart sampling configuration to work effectively, with specific subsampling and error thresholds, and can significantly slow down even powerful processors when used for complex tasks like object and camera motion blur. 56 Mental Ray proxies are called MIB files and were available in Maya from version 4.5 onwards, with features improving over time, including support in Irix versions from 3.6 early 2008. 7 Mental Ray in Maya is not fully compatible with all Maya features like Maya Fur, PaintFX, and Particle shading. 8 Mental Ray was used in many productions, with some issues on Tron Legacy and Speed Racer. 9 Micropolygon rendering was first used in the Star Trek opening sequence in 1982, with RenderMan and Mantra coming in 1986 and 1987 respectively, and Mental Ray in 1987. 10
