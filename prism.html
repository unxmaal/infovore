Prism

Claims: 171 Background: 12 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Prism, SGI Altix, OpenGL, QuickTransit, SGI Tezro.

With Onyx4

The Onyx4 and Prism use the same GPU, and the Onyx4 compute node can be repurposed into a Prism by replacing the graphics card. 12 The Onyx4 is faster and more reliable than the Prism, though the Prism has been difficult to work with. 3 The only configurations with a single AGP riser were the rack Prism and Onyx4, both dual-pipe. 4 The Altix 350 has FireGL X2.1 cards which are useful for Onyx4 due to Prism compatibility issues. 5 Altix and Prism systems are not fully compatible with Onyx4 systems due to differences in hardware and firmware. 6 My Onyx4 and Prism bricks cost the better part of a grand each. 7 The CBOB can be found in different configurations, including as part of a Prism setup or with an IP badge and Onyx4 USB ports. 8 Vertex buffers (VBOs) were not available on SGI V12 systems until Onyx4 (and Prism). 9

General

The SGI Prism is a visualization system built on Itanium 2 processors that runs Linux, and it can run IRIX software through QuickTransit, which SGI announced would first be available in October 2004. headless mode. 13141516 The Prism has DVI output, and its graphics can be provided by ATI FireGL X2/3 or ELSA Permedia cards, though the Altix 350's graphics require SGI-specific drivers such as the SGI ProPack kernel. 17181920 The Prism is essentially a Tezro chassis with different skins, and it can run with one CPU while Prism racks and Onyx systems can have multiple graphics pipes. 212223 The Prism is prone to crashing during installation, and it may not understand USB. 242516 It was released in 2005 and comes in both desktop and deskside models, with the desktop version being about the size of a Tezro. 262728 The Prism uses dual AGP graphics cards rather than PCIe, and it uses normal kernel modules for storage and networking along with EFI firmware. 29303132 The only officially
