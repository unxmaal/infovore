Agnus

Claims: 5

Agnus is a chip used in the Amiga and is for Chip memory only. 1 Polygon filling is used for rendering on the Amiga Agnus. 2 The CIDER card for the CDTV requires manual mounting and uses a different type of Agnus. 3 The old Agnus can be stubborn to remove due to oxidation and may require deoxit or heat, and prying gently with a clip probe can help without bending pins. 4 The A2000's AGNUS issue can be resolved by using the latest WHDload configuration. 5
