Ethernet

Claims: 56 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Ethernet, QEMU.

Ethernet is used to couple cluster systems, such as most HPE high-performance computing (HPC) systems, and is described as a 'sane cluster' connection alongside Infiniband. 12 Ethernet and TCP/IP are interdependent for network interoperability. 3 Infiniband has lower latency than Ethernet and is more closely related to Ethernet than to NUMA. 45 Ethernet won over competing technologies because it was cheap rather than because it was better. 6 Ethernet is limited to 10BASE-T. 7 Ethernet is considered unnecessary for a serial console. 8 Ethernet can be used for remote access, including telnet and remote serial ports, and for installing systems from another machine. 91011 The lack of Ethernet is a deal breaker and makes a system less useful. 1213 Ethernet is a network technology that can be used to connect vintage computers, such as the SGI 4D/70 (which used a CMC VME option board) and other machines, to modern networks. 1415 Ethernet is distinct from other network technologies such as T1, token ring, and FDDI. 16 Ethernet is used in a variety of systems and devices, including the EL-16, the MMSC, and the DECnet-over-Ethernet stack, where traffic inspection tools above DECnet will misinterpret the traffic. 171819 Ethernet performance can be affected by hardware limitations, such as the Turbochannel speed on /100s and Personals. 20 Ethernet can be emulated in systems like QEMU, and adapters such as the shoehorn support Ethernet. 2122
