Reality Engine 2

Claims: 3 Part of Reality

The Reality Engine 2 consists of multiple 10+lb daughterboards filled with custom processors. 1 The MAME emulation is for the Raster Engine 2, not Reality Engine 2; it is the rasterizer for the Personal Iris graphics. 2 The Onyx1 with Reality Engine 2 is likely slower than the R5k O2 and probably not fast enough to launch the game. 3
