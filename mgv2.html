MGV2

Claims: 3

The MGV2 is a video card related to the MGV3, with the MGV3 being an optional daughterboard for the MGV2, and the COSMO 2.0 with daughterboard is used for video compression/decompression. 1 The MGV2 is a full tape drive board connected to a COSMO 2.0. 2 The MGV2, which serves as the break out box for Indigo2 IMPACT video, is available for purchase. 3
