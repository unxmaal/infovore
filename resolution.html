resolution

Claims: 35 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Display resolution standards, OpenGL, SGI 1600SW, Video Graphics Array.

Offline editing involved downsampling the source material to a lower resolution and bitrate, then exporting the EDL and reprocessing the source footage on a faster machine. 1 The SuperVidel can achieve 1600x1200@24bit resolution. 2 The `xsetmon` command can change the resolution IRIX runs at, but a monitor that doesn't properly filter SoG signals may cause green coloration that can't be fixed with software. 3 1280x1024 resolution avoids redraw issues but requires recompiling the old resolution to confirm its responsibility. 4 The presenter has a resolution of 1024x768, with a higher resolution of 1280x1024 available. 5 An O2 struggles with 1600+ resolutions and may chop off screen portions. 6 Running 1920-resolution on Octanes and faster machines is possible and recommended for better usability. 7 3840x1080 resolution can be pushed on an Octane2, but it is close to the pixel clock limit. 8
