parts

Claims: 63 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Power supply unit (computer), Tape drive.

With machine models

The parts in question include a device with a weird docking port on the left side that can be used for Ethernet, though a full-size Ethernet jack is absent and a full dock can be purchased. 1 Disassembly involves two screws going into the side that opens and one into the topmost PCI slot bracket. 2 The Tezro/Origin 350 node boards use electrolytic cans near the voltage regulators, which some consider swapping for aluminum-polymer caps as a preventative measure. 3 SGI used cost-cutting measures in their hardware, such as using Kapton tape to adhere heatsinks to RDRAM on IMPACT texture. 4

General

Parts for SGI systems are difficult to find and often expensive, with variable pricing data that is not very useful. 5678 Non-functional SGI machines can be purchased as parts machines, and the parts themselves are valuable and can be reused or sold. 910 Complete systems can sometimes be cheaper than buying parts, especially when expensive upgrades are involved. 11 Selling SGI parts for less than the posted eBay price can be effective, and trading parts for other needed components is a viable strategy. 1213 Some parts can be tested independently of a system to check for failures or voltage issues, and a power supply can catch fire while the motherboard survives. 1415 Various SGI components exist, including SBus cards, SCSI cards, firewire cards, and power supplies using 48V with linear and switching regulators. 161718 Upgrading parts such as CPUs, memory, and drives can improve system performance, with upgraded SCA drives being quieter, faster, and cooler. 192021 The AV1 is a custom module with unlabeled passives and hard to find datasheets. 22 The frontplane has capacitors with multiple values, and its lights indicate XIO options even without an XIO installed. 23 Heat softens the potting and can cause it to crumble, which can be remedied with hot air or hot water; there are YouTube videos demonstrating both methods. 24 Research institutions extend the life of expensive instruments by using SGI parts, even if it means running them until they are completely used up. 25 The SGI tax on parts was surprisingly low, with a 2003 price list showing some parts at just three dollars. 26 The O2 has issues with injection molding in some parts. 27 The 68681 DUART uses address bit 3 to determine the channel, with two channels on the IP2 board. 28
