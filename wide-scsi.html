wide SCSI

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SATA.

Wide SCSI uses a 68-pin connector, whereas narrow SCSI uses a 50-pin/25 configuration. 1 Wide SCSI and SCSI-3 require fast hardware and tight timings. 2 IBM used a non-DB connector for wide SCSI, which was unusual. 3 To go significantly faster than fast-20 SCSI, wide SCSI support and eventually LVD would be needed, adding complexity but achievable with a 120MHz+ MCU and PIO. 4 Wide SCSI could theoretically capture uncompressed video, and an Indigo2 Impact 10K with a suitable video option or an Onyx with wide SCSI stripe can handle uncompressed video. 5
