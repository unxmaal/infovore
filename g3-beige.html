G3 Beige

Claims: 6 Part of G3

USB support on the G3 Beige can be enabled by downloading a specific file, copying it to a floppy disk, and installing the extensions, which allows use of USB mice and keyboards and enables mass storage support. 1 The G3 Beige can read 800K and 1.44 MB formatted disks but not 400K disks. 2 The 1GHz SONNET card is recommended for a G3 Beige. 3 The G4 upgrade CPU card is built specifically for the G3 Beige and is the only dual CPU option for this machine; it is extremely rare. 4 The XLR-8 MACh Velocity MPe CPU card upgrades a G3 Beige to dual CPU capability but requires repartitioning the OS9 disk; it is rare and expensive. 5 The G3 Beige can run with a single CPU and can be overclocked to 501 MHz. 6
