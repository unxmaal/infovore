EISA

Claims: 22 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Extended Industry Standard Architecture.

EISA is a 32-bit expansion bus standard used in some PCs and SGI systems like the Indigo and Indy, but not the Indigo2. 1 EISA is a dual-layer bus that was a response to MCA, which was invented before PCI existed. 23 EISA can support FDDI, as in a system using EISA scramnet. 4 EISA and MCA controllers are also available, and MCA systems have SBUS, EISA, and GIO slots. 56 PISA is a SBC standard using PCI/ISA with EISA connectors, and is a combo PCI+ISA slot that also works for ISA cards using a connector similar to EISA. 78 Zorro II bus was less competitive compared to EISA. 9
