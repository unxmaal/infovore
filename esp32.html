ESP32

Claims: 8

The ESP32's main line is based on xtensa, but the C3 series uses RISC-V, and both have been reliable for development support. 1 The Arduino core is used for prototyping with ESP32, though the ESP32 requires using RTOS instead of the Arduino ecosystem to avoid compiler issues with preprocessing in multithreaded code. 23 The ESP8266 had quirks, but the ESP32 was supposed to fix some of them. 4 ESP32 takes 11 clock cycles for GPIO to turn on. 5 An ESP32 could handle SCSI-to-iSCSI via WiFi, though timing is tricky. 6 ESP32 has three hardware UARTs, making it a better choice for software-controlled null modem than ESP8266. 7 The FujiNet board connects the GPIO pins of the ESP32 to the SIO bus, handling voltage and resistance considerations. 8
