68030

Claims: 11

In the Macintosh lineup, the 68030 appears in the SE/30 (an upgrade over the base SE, making it similar to a miniaturized Macintosh IIx) and in the Macintosh IIx, which received an "x" suffix to denote the 68030. 12 The 68030 was sometimes limited by a 16-bit motherboard bus, and it can be swapped in for a 68020 card in some systems. 34 The 68030 can cause power supply issues, indicated by an over current light appearing before other components start, and games such as Doom and Marathon ran poorly on a 68030. 56
