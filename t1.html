T1

Claims: 6

The T1 is a bundle of dedicated 56k lines providing 1.54 Mbps. 1 The T1 came out in 2001 and was a low cost telco system for space and power/heat constrained environments, optionally NEBS certified. 2 The T1 was the only reasonable choice for SGI at the time due to the lack of resources to design a modern pipeline and the availability of the Afara design. 3 Sun's T1 design was efficient but had software design issues due to its thread count. 4
