L3 cache

Claims: 5 Part of L3

Dual 1 GHz G4 PowerMacs have L3 cache. 1 The R18000 can handle up to 64MB of off-chip L3 cache and will have 2 floating point units. 2 The Z15 has 8MB L2 cache per core, 256MB L3 cache, a 5.2GHz clock speed, and 12 cores. 3 L2 cache on R5k can potentially be upgraded to 2MB, but R7k might need more address lines for 8MB. 4 Bigger L3 cache might make a difference in compression and decompression performance. 5
