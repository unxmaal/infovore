R4000 Indigo

Claims: 7 Part of R4000

The R4000 Indigo was in service for 13 years. 1 It has a badge instead of the logo on the door, whereas the R3000 Indigo has the logo on the door. 2 It appears to be an R4000 Indigo with the entry graphics option, 2nd revision, so it has no song and dance machine PCB. 3 Some GIO32 cards are compatible with the R3000 Indigo, R4000 Indigo, and Indy, though a few early or late models may not be fully compatible. 4 4.0.5F images run on the R4000 Indigo but support up to 128MB RAM. 5 The R4000 Indigo has SSD-level speed on IRIX 4. 6
