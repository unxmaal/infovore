RAMDISK

Claims: 3

A RAMDISK is an SSD made with DDR RAM instead of flash, features a 4X SAS connector, talks SATA1, and requires RAID0 stripping for high IOPs, primarily used for HDL simulation and video-editing. 1 IRIX does not have a built-in ramdisk. 2 There are several mentions of a RAMdisk for HP-UX. 3
