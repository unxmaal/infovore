General

Fans can be added or replaced in various SGI machines for cooling, such as a 70mm fan added to the bedrock for additional cooling, an additional fan added to improve air circulation in a PC case, and a 40mm Noctua fan controlling the main gfx chip. 123 Fans can be loud, as the Octane's fan can be very loud, especially in Fully Loaded Super Blower Fan Mode, and the Crimson's fan is notably obnoxious. 45 Fan swaps can reduce noise levels, such as replacing the fan in an O2's PSU with an Arctic F8, or replacing the Octane's main fan with Noctua sensorless high static pressure fans, but can also lead to a buildup of heat, potentially causing the system to overheat. 678 Fans can indicate or cause problems, such as a missing fan being bad, a broken fan on an Impact PSU being cannibalized for parts, and a GTX card overheating and damaging itself if the fan runs at low speed. 91011
