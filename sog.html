SoG

Claims: 43 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Component video.

Signal-on-green (SoG) is a video signal format in which the sync information is carried on the green channel. 12 Most SGI machines produce a signal-on-green (SoG) output, and display cards can also output SoG along with H+V and other sync signals, with configuration options. 134 The O2 outputs SoG as well as regular sync lines, which can confuse monitors, while the Indy uses SoG and the Octane uses regular signal. 5678 The Personal IRIS uses SoG for R, G, and B and has a VGA connector, and some systems also produced composite sync. 910 Many monitors handle SoG without issue, including many ViewSonic models, every ASUS LCD tried, and older Dell monitors, though newer Dell monitors are less reliable and typically do not support more than 60Hz analog. 111213 Some Dell monitors work with SoG and have been tested on V12, and most 4:3 Dell LCDs support it. 1415 The HP LP2065 monitor supports SoG and works with Octane and newer Suns, but requires a gender changer or VGA to DVI adapter for Indigo2. 16 Some quality tube monitors, CRTs, and TFTs can be SoG compatible. 1718 Monitors can sync to a wide range of signals, including SOG adapted to VGA. 19 Some TV models do not support SoG, and a TV might not display anything with SoG, but using a green tint can make the display readable. 20 A hacky 13W3 cable can be modified to work with an Indy by removing pins. 20
