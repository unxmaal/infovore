SI

Claims: 14

SI is a 3D graphics card that runs on Xbow and is 3D capable. 1 SI can do 1600x1050, though it may require reducing color depth and buffer, and disabling double buffering and the z buffer is necessary for 1440x900 resolution. 23 The 225 machine uses SE or SI, with SI having a nut on the heatsink, and an Octane with two SI or SE graphics cards boots with the secondary head inactive. 45 The 13w3-vga adapter works with SI and SSE boards on an Octane, and the O200 is a modular Octane where adding an SI or SE card requires a gigachannel. 67 The O200 can boot an SI with modifications, requiring a sacrificial XIO card and adjusting the latching mechanism, and modding an SI to an O200 is simple, with write-ups being scarce due to the straightforward nature of the process. 89 IRIX Joy Ride is a game requiring the V6+ graphics card, compiled with MIPSpro, with frame rate issues on SI and requiring GLUT installation. 10 SI is also a 3D animation package used in films and shows, developed in Toronto. 11
