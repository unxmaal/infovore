IRIX 5.2

Claims: 17 Part of IRIX 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Extent File System.

IRIX 5.2 can be run on an R4400 Indy, and the Indy can also run IRIX 5.1 using a special Indy version of that release, along with regular releases of 5.2, 6.2, and 6.5 up to 6.5.22. 12 Underclocking the CPU to 24 MHz is possible, and IRIX 5.2 works fine in MAME without the Indycam issue that is specific to IRIX 5.3. 34 IRIX 5.2 uses EFS by default. 5 IRIX 6.0 is technically a 64-bit version of IRIX 5.2 using o32/o64, while 6.1 is based on IRIX 5.3. 678 Software such as OpenInventor games, HotMix 8, and Softimage 2.66 requires or supports IRIX 5.2. 91011 IRIX 5.2 was likely released between March 1994 and November 1994, and the 68k architecture was used until IRIX 5.2. 1213
