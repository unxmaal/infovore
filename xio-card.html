XIO card

Claims: 16 Part of XIO

The XIO card's theoretical speed is slightly above what PCIe 1x1 could do, as documented in the SGI Octane technical report. 1 The dmediapro is an external box that connects to a workstation via a XIO card and provides an external RAID array. 2 There was a tensor processor available as an XIO card for SGI machines, but it was rare and had few customers. 3 The card edge connector on the XIO card is mostly for power. 4 Locking screws on Octane motherboards and XIO card carriers can shear off components. 5 The fast fan mode on an Octane can't be unset in software if triggered by an XIO card in the bottom right quadrant; it's automatically activated. 6
