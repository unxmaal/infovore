clang

Claims: 19 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Common Desktop Environment, LLVM.

Clang is a frontend for LLVM used as a compiler, and it is regarded as one of the best compilers for modern C++. 12 It is available as an alternative to gcc for modern toolchains, and on macOS gcc is symlinked to clang by default because Apple prefers LLVM. 134 Clang can build CDE on FreeBSD and OpenBSD may distribute a recent build of Clang, making it a viable alternative to GCC for building CDE. 56 Clang version 13 supports OpenMP, and IRIX clang version 14.0.6 is available, targeting mips64-sgi-irix6.5-gnuabin32. 78 The SGUG gcc and clang can cross-compile IRIX from Linux, with X11 and GL support but GL can be finicky to set up. 9 Building with clang can involve issues such as a limited address space from conflicting default load addresses with gcc-built shared libraries, and bootstrapping SGI packages is difficult due to libgcc and libdicl dependencies clashing, making it hard to mix gcc- and clang-built shared libraries. 1011
