WeatherStar XL

Claims: 10

The WeatherStar XL is a rare Silicon Graphics O2 workstation built as an O2 in a rack mount enclosure, containing a bare O2 mainboard and AV board with limited software versions available. 12 It runs on an O2 and outputs AV1 video frames for broadcast over the AV board, though it can be configured to use FTP and telnet for input. 34 The WeatherStar XL has a video card with a standard PCI connector, and the motherboard connector has been identified. 5 The Weather Star XL image can run on an O2 without a WeatherStar XL board, but without TWC's services it doesn't pull any data. 64 The TWC drive for the WeatherStar XL contains all assets, scripts, and templates as regular movie and sound files, and the editing suite that ran on an O2 is now lost media. 78 WeatherStar XL boot issues are due to a one-time installation that was never touched again. 9 The O2 was discontinued in September 2002, announced in May 2002, and some companies like TWC used it for products such as the WeatherStar XL. 10
