disk

Claims: 125 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Hard disk drive.

A disk may fail for various reasons, including a physical unrecovered read error in the middle of the disk's storage, a failing disk indicated by a distinct clacking noise from the disk compartment, or a dead disk that shows no LEDs or spinup. 123 When a disk is not detected, the cause could be a loose data or power connection to the disk backplane, a faulty disk, or a problem with the cable or FC controller. 45 Swapping the disk with a known good disk can fix the issue, though swapping the disk did not resolve the issue in some cases. 36 To fully erase a disk, run /exercise in fx, write only, select the entire disk range, and zero out the entire disk, creating a new label before exiting fx; however, for 300GB disks, using /exercise in fx to zero out the disk is not recommended. 78 The low level format option is for creating the sectors or remapping bad parts of the disk, and was a last ditch thing if a drive wouldn't partition or format in the OS. 9 Partitioning the disk can resolve installation issues, and to overwrite a disk, use the rootdrive option from the partition menu. 1011 Fresh installs can fail if a disk is missing or improperly copied, and the error message 'Copying installation program to disk. .... 10% ......' suggests a slow or failing disk during installation. 1213 Wiping the disk with fx may not resolve installation issues. 14 A disk can suffer various failures, including a resistor failure that caused it to burst into flames, a degaussing event that wiped the servo track and left the disk unspinnable, and a toasted disk that may have caused missing files. 151617 A bad disk can cause the format utility to fail and stop recognizing the disk, report a larger number of blocks if it has bad blocks, and produce a 'write failed' error during installation. 181920 Formatting a disk can fix problems, such as when a format corrected a disk so blocksize reported 33 GB instead of 2 TB, though formatting costs nothing but can be expensive for the disk itself and may involve redundant steps like erasing and formatting twice. 212223 Zeroing a disk, either with dd or by zeroing the first few MB to make it appear fresh, is possible, and a corrupt filesystem may cause the miniroot to crash when attempting to mount it, so formatting before installation is advisable. 242526 Diagnostics can help identify disk problems, though the system and disk may pass all diagnostics while issues still exist, and running the disk or power-cycling may help preserve data as a short-term fix. 2728 A disk can achieve close to 100-150MB/sec for read/write, exceeding what a 1GBit connection can handle, and two to three disks can fully saturate a 2GBit connection. 29
