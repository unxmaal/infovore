NVRAM chip

Claims: 8 Part of NVRAM

A dead or failing NVRAM chip causes the system to hang and prevents booting diagnostics over the network, because it produces a wrong Ethernet address. 12 The NVRAM chip stores the hostid and Ethernet address, and for newer machines these are often printed on the chip, with the first two numbers indicating the machine type. 3 The hostid and Ethernet address can be reprogrammed, and a battery replacement kit can convert the internal battery in the NVRAM chip into a CR2032 coin cell battery for Sun machines. 43 The NVRAM chip on ST systems can be accessed by cutting it in half to expose the leads for the crystal and battery. 5 SS10 requires a new NVRAM chip, after which their system is now fully working. 6
