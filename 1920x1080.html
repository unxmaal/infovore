1920x1080

Claims: 4 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The Irix can display at 1920x1080, but running this resolution is not possible; 1920x1200 works well instead, as Irix was designed around 1280x1024 resolution. 12 The Octane can display at 1920x1080 with ESSI graphics. 3 Setting the input resolution to 1600x1200 matches the Octane's output resolution, avoiding blurry display at 1920x1080. 4
