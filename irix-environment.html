IRIX environment

Claims: 3 Part of IRIX

Cross-compiling an IRIX environment requires an older version of binutils that can build on FreeBSD 10.x or earlier due to license issues with GPLv3. 1 SGI provided documentation for the IRIX environment, calling conventions, and linker usage. 2 The IRIX environment is separate from the rest of the system, which is its only saving grace for user-24f0. 3
