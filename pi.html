PI

Claims: 81 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Raspberry Pi, Amiga, SD card.

The PI is a MIPS-based SGI Iris model, described as a big R3k Indigo and the lowest-end SGI. 12 It runs IRIX versions including 3.3.2, 4.0.5, and 4.0.5EPL. 345 The PI has 32 MB of RAM, which can be doubled but is known to be flaky. 6 Its graphics use Raster Engines, with the GR1.2 board featuring four RE2 chips. 78 It lacks internal audio ports, though Magnum Audio can be installed. 94 The PI runs flight simulator applications, including the operators console in the dash8 sim. 101112 The PI can support up to 128MB of RAM, more than an Indigo R3K, and shares identical sleds with the Crimson. 1314 The PI does not recognize the Apple CD300e CD ROM, and its 1 GB hard drive is insufficient for Alias software. 1516 For late model PIs and Indigos, the mouse and keyboard connector is not minidin, and the PI and PS models ran the mouse via the keyboard. 1718 The PI is slow, has limited software, is difficult to set up, and is not very stable or easy to repair. 19
