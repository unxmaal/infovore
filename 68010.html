68010

Claims: 6

The 68010 added paging to the 68000, which had lacked it. 1 A 68010 running at 8 MHz can be used for rendering with a 768x480 linear framebuffer and 2MB of video DRAM. 2 An 8 MHz 68010 can provide a 5-10% upgrade in a 1040STF, but requires 1.62 ROMs that are not compatible with the 1040STF without a ROM adapter board. 3 SGI used custom MMUs in its 68010-based workstations to achieve independent Unix functionality, but performance improved with the 020 and 030 processors. 45

Sources

  1. The 68000 did not have paging; the 68010 fixed that (mostly). (user-cb4c, 2020-07-16, exchange 9643, check: supported)
  2. A 68010 at 8 MHz with a 768x480 linear framebuffer and 2MB of video DRAM can be used for rendering. (user-9305, 2021-03-13, exchange 79028, check: supported)
  3. An 8MHz 68010 CPU can provide a 5-10% upgrade in a 1040STF, but requires 1.62 ROMs, which are not compatible with the 1040STF without a ROM adapter board. (user-1361, 2021-12-12, exchange 152250, check: supported)
  4. The 68010-based SGI workstations required custom MMUs to run Unix independently, but performance improved with the 020 and 030 processors. (user-7b1c, 2022-03-25, exchange 175988, check: supported)
  5. Sun and I believe SGI used custom MMUs in their 68010-based workstations to achieve independent Unix functionality. (user-8de9, 2022-03-25, exchange 175988, check: supported)
