workstation

Claims: 26

Workstations are professional machines rather than home computers, such as the Quadra 950, and they can run operating systems including Solaris, HPUX, AIX, and Linux. 12 Sun workstations use 72-pin SIMMs with parity and specific error messages for configuration issues, while SGI workstations can be netbooted by pointing them at an .ini file and disk image. 34 Sun workstations can have two outages before becoming a paperweight, and netbooting a Sun workstation can be difficult with SGI CD installs reportedly unpleasant. 56 The O2 CPU was used in a workstation and a shrunk version for a console, suggesting it had potential for scaling in workstations, while the U2 has a UPA slot and can be workstationed. 78 The SGI workstation was used for developing games, though it lacked gaming capabilities and was focused on reliability and precision, and running games over X11 forwarding tends to result in an interactive slideshow. 910 The Everdrive allows running N64 demos on an SGI workstation, some but not all working, and a custom serial port adapter would be needed to interface N64 controllers with an SGI workstation. 1112 USB 2 is the flavor of choice for moving files between a Windows 10 laptop and an SGI workstation using a USB SCSI adapter. 13 Logic and Final Cut helped Apple compete with SGI in the workstation market, and the workstation supported certain features indicating it was considered pro stuff back then. 1415
