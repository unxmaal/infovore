PSU

Claims: 384 Background: 13 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Power supply unit (computer), SGI Octane.

With Octane

An Octane PSU can be tested by loading it, though specific methods are not widely known, and information exists about repairing a PSU that gives a click of death. 12 The Octane has a proprietary PSU that is complex, difficult to service, and was not designed for repair, reflecting its high-end pricing. 3456 The PSU is comparable in wattage to the SGI Octane's PSU at around 830W, is relatively large and heavy, and the Octane2 PSU is AAE0470026. 78 PSU failures can occur shortly after setup or installation, and reseating the PSU or motherboard can sometimes resolve the issue. 910 A broken handle can damage the PSU, causing a short and a pop when powered on, and the PSU may need recapping. 1112 If a PSU fuse blows immediately, replace the fuse and test by applying power without the PSU seated in the Octane, then test again after seating it. 13 A PSU replacement can resolve issues like spontaneous power on connect and shutdown problems, and fixing Octane PSUs can be challenging but not impossible. 1415

With Indigo2

The Indigo2 PSU is used for Indigo2 systems and comes in different types, requiring 3.5V rails. 1617 It uses stamped and bent sheet metal with an unusual, mechanically complex design that makes it difficult to repair, especially when recapped. 1819 The PSU commonly fails due to breaking MOSFETs and drying capacitors, particularly the 330uf 6.3V red ones, and re-capping is a difficult task rated 9 out of 10 that can cause damage if delayed. 202122 Failures can cause lockups, high-frequency noise during data transfer, and the PSU produces heat even when the machine is off, though it can withstand extreme heat. 23242526 Indigo2 systems are generally reliable, and a failed PSU can be fixed by replacing it with a working one from another Indigo2, though upgrading to an r10k motherboard and PSU may be required. 272829

With O2

Swapping the PSU can fix 'dead' O2 systems, including those with sudden death syndrome or an R5K processor that has PSU issues. 303132 PSU problems can be addressed by reseating the PSU or its connectors, unplugging and replugging a commonly failing resistor, or removing the system tray after unplugging and discharging the PSU. 3334353637 A failing PSU can cause various system errors, including drive issues and ECC errors, and can be verified with an oscilloscope to check ripple. 3839 The I2 PSU is 400W and has a -12V rail which can be inverted, and an O2+ with 1600SW can be found for $250, possibly needing PSU repair. 4041
