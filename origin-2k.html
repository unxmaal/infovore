Origin 2K

Claims: 13 Part of Origin

The Origin 2K is a system consisting of 1 or 2 modules plus a power supply in a single rack, and it uses 300-pin compression connectors. 12 The Origin 2K is related to other SGI systems: an Onyx2 is an Origin 2K, an Octane is a Numalinkless Origin 200, and the Origin 350 and Origin 3K are other configurations. 23 The PCI bus on the O2, Octane, and Origin 2K is 5V, and the backplane and chassis of an Onyx2 and Origin 2K deskside are distinctive, with the backplane being the Kego backplane. 45 The Origin 2K power supplies are smaller than g-brick PSUs, and you can put an SE in an Origin 2K, though the O2K card cage has extra fins that might prevent fitting a MXE or VPro. 67
