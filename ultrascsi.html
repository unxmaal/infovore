UltraSCSI

Claims: 6

Ultra SCSI is backwards compatible with earlier SCSI standards, though some older hardware may not fully utilize it. 1 Ultra wide SCSI is faster than Ultra SCSI. 2 UltraSCSI was far more commonplace in workstation towers, prompting a return to that form factor for the Blade 2500. 3 An O2 would benefit heavily from a proper UltraSCSI drive, as the emulators top out below what those UltraSCSI platters do. 4 It is possible to make a faster SSD solution by designing new boards that talk UltraSCSI, interfacing to NVMe or similar, though SATA or SD would also work. 5 An emulator could support UltraSCSI or Wide-SCSI3 interfaces, with a microcontroller nearly as powerful as an R10k. 6
