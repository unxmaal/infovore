Falcon030

Claims: 4 Part of Falcon

The Falcon030 is the A1200 of the ST lineup, combining the 68030 processor with the VIDEL chip, which the TT lacked and which the 16-bit motherboard bus limited the 68030. 1 It can be used as a virtual analog polysynth through its DSP. 2 It is considered the best computer for MIDI, though it is expensive and harder to obtain than a Quadra. 3
