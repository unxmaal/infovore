HFS+

Claims: 4

To partition a disk as HFS+, use the 'special > erase' option in Lido. 1 To read HFS or HFS+, use `mount -t hfs` or `mount -t hfsplus` respectively, and journaling must be disabled to write. 2 HFS+ is still supported for Mac drives. 3 Using a separate case-sensitive HFS+ volume might be a better option for reading UFS disks on a Mac, and there have been issues with OSX Server and NFS. 4
