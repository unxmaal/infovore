X3

Claims: 21 Part of X

The SGI X3 is not compatible with IRIX and requires additional power, while the X1 and X2 draw power from the AGP Pro connector's extra edge connector fingers. 12 The X3 runs at 380 MHz, whereas the X2-256T runs at 412 MHz, and dual 1.6GHz CPUs are the fastest for the X3. 34 The X3 does not have support for a jumper. 3 An SGI X3 can boot on a P3 AGP Windows machine, but the firmware might be necessary for full functionality, and ATIFlash does not detect the SGI X3 card, suggesting a firmware issue. 56 HP and SGI X3 firmware can be pulled, and an HP X3 can be flashed with SGI firmware. 7 XP has issues loading on an SGI X3 machine, possibly due to the NT kernel and SGI VBIOS. 8 The X3 won't work in the Onyx4 because drivers are only for FGL 9700 and two 9800 variants, and a Prism is not compatible with Onyx4 FireGL cards due to the X3 cards. 910 Flashing X3 cards in an AGP Pro machine can be done for Onyx4 compatibility, though an SGI BIOS is required for some models to avoid core dumping. 11
