SCSI emulator

Claims: 15 Part of SCSI

The SCSI emulator's performance can be affected by the computer's capabilities. 1 Zulu Compact is preferred as a SCSI emulator due to its small size, microSD, and bus power. 2 The NVMe board does not support native booting and requires PROM updates to enable it, so a SCSI emulator suffices for booting. 3 The SCSI emulator for NVMe is simple and requires only the kernel file to be placed in RAM. 4 For user-1a5a, the SCSI emulator is used instead of CDs. 5
