IDE to SATA adapter

Claims: 4 Part of IDE

An IDE to SATA adapter reduces speed but saturates the IDE bus. 1 It could be used for the Altix's IDE drive. 2 It is almost certainly not faster than using a SATA PCI card. 3 Sound can be added with a cheap and fast IDE-to-SATA adapter and an SSD. 4
