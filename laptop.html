laptop

Claims: 33 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Laptop, Fedora Linux, Motherboard.

A laptop can run various operating systems, including Linux (Ubuntu 20 provides a good experience on a laptop with nice graphics and an i7), Redox OS (for testing purposes), and macOS (which lacks middle button paste that some users come to rely on from Linux). 12 Laptops have been used for specialized tasks such as running an FTP server to transfer data to Amigas and Octanes, and playing games like xcom Enemy Unknown. 34 Some laptops suffer from hardware issues, including overheating that causes a 'Black screen of death' around the Turion II era, graphics issues under Windows 10 that cause frequent overheating and crashing, and battery swelling that can cause problems but still allows the device to function without the battery. 567 The on/off button's microprocessor can cause a fatal error when the laptop is preparing to sleep, leading to a frozen state and potentially damaging the battery. 8 Some laptops have distinctive features, such as a Scroll Lock key that might be a function key with a blue indicator requiring the Fn key to activate, a power bridge feature to charge over USB-C, and better graphics than typical MacBooks from the same era. 91011 Durability varies among laptops, with the 3000 series being the most durable SGI laptop featuring magnesium shells, while plastic components can easily break. 12
