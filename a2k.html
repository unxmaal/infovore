A2k

Claims: 3

The A2k was associated with a Commodore external SCSI tape drive that was sold with the A3k UNIX system or the retail kits for A2k/A3k users. 1 Third party expansions for the A2k were not compatible with the tape drive. 2 There were no issues with 3.X roms on A2k or A3k systems. 3
