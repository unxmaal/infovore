Bali

Claims: 9

Bali was a graphics architecture based on the Numaflex architecture, designed to be highly scalable but was expensive and not completed before SGI shifted to ATI graphics. 1 It was a complex multi-board multi-ASIC architecture that was too large and expensive to produce, aiming for a scalable multi-chip design possibly with a networked graphics brick design. 23 Bali was designed to be a single board for all graphics compute except display, allowing multiple boards to extend system power, similar to NVLink 20 years ahead of its time with router switching. 4 SGI's graphics division was acquired by Graphics Properties Holdings, leading to legal issues, and Bali was involved in a lawsuit with ATI in 2007 over patents related to frame buffer trickery and half precision floats. 56 Bali's patents may have been transferred to Microsoft or NVIDIA, but this is uncertain. 7 Bali's features became standard in desktop graphics by the later 2000s. 8
