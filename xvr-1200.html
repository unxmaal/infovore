XVR-1200

Claims: 24

The XVR-1200 is a graphics card that is two XVR-600s on one card and supports OpenGL 2.0. 12 It has native dual-head capability, but does not have dual DVI output and its OBP output can be analog only. 345 The XVR-1200 performs well with Quake III, but struggles with basic tasks like web page scrolling and redrawing a 1920x1080 framebuffer. 678 It is not significantly better than the XVR-600 unless both monitor ports are used, and it was cheaper than XVR-1000s. 910 The XVR-1200 can be used in a Blade2K, Blade 1500, and Ultra 5, but it will not fit in the 66MHz slot on pre-Blade 1500/2500 systems. 11121314
