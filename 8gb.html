8GB

Claims: 7

A 9 GB disk could be used with 8 GB for / and 1 GB for swap. 1 The Bigfoot IDE drive is 5.25" and not recognized by the system for drives larger than 8GB. 2 1GB sticks are the standard, with 8GB being the maximum per brick, and 1GB sticks are made by doubling the chips on a standard stick. 3 Solaris 10 runs well on 2GB but ZFS root requires at least 8GB, meaning B1k or later. 4 The stock disk interface is really bad, with an 8 GB boot volume maximum. 5 Octane2 takes 8GB max. 6 The SC5250-E has two Xeon processors and 8GB of memory. 7
