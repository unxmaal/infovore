UPA slot

Claims: 10 Part of UPA

The Ultra 10 towers have a UPA slot and some PCI. 1 The U2 has a UPA slot and can be workstationed; it has a pokey SCSI bus but supports Sbus FC cards for speed. 2 The UPA slot is expected to be used for multi-port gigabit LAN cards, faster FC, or faster SCSI. 3 The Ultra 5 can host a Creator3D card with UPA slot compatibility, though direct installation may require case modifications. 4 An extension cable for mounting a card in the U5's UPA slot would need to be homebrewed. 5 The Sun Ultra 2 Enterprise doesn't have a UPA slot. 6
