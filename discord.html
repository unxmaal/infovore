Discord

Claims: 25 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Discord.

Discord is a popular but controversial modern chat client that is outwardly hostile to CLI clients and may sell user data. 1 It uses a pure client/server model with one backend process per server, experiences server outages likely due to data balancing and noisy neighbors, and has issues with security, updates, and management. 234 Its free service is well-managed compared to IRC, which had frequent issues, and IRC is now used less, with Telegram and Discord being preferred. 56 There are IRC bridges between IRIX and Discord, and the IRC bridge is the main option for accessing Discord from within IRIX, though not all channels are accessible there. 78 Discord does not run on IRIX directly; the client for IRIX does not exist, and IRC bridge is used instead. 9 RSE Beta 6 allows IRC and Discord from an IRIX system, and there are IRC channels listed in channel descriptions for bridged IRIX Discord connections. 1011
