SunOS 4.1.x

Claims: 7 Part of SunOS

SunOS 4.1.x was sold as a separate product before being integrated into the Solaris line, and Solaris 1.X was based on SunOS 4.1.X. 1 The software came on 3.5" floppies and supports Solaris 2.4/5 and SunOS 4.1.x. 2 SunOS 4.1.x can be installed from QIC tape, but seek times may be problematic due to sequential access. 3 The suncube demo, which flashes different colors, is included in SunOS 3.x and 4.1.x but fails to compile with the source code available. 4 sun4c should stick to SunOS 4.1.x through 2.5, and 2.6 on the IPC should also stick to SunOS 4.1.x. 56
