FDDI board

Claims: 5 Part of FDDI

A FDDI board in a 4D/440 required cleaning and drying to function again after being reassembled. 1 The FTP site ftp.interphase.com and its mirror at ftp.jurassic.nl provide downloadable FDDI board and drivers for EISA systems. 2 FDDI board issues can be resolved by testing different cables and board ports. 3 The FDDI board requires installing FDDIXPress software, rebuilding the kernel, and rebooting; it appears in 'hinv' and can be configured in /etc/config/netif.options. 4
