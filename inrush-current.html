inrush current

Claims: 3

Resistors are used to limit inrush current to capacitors, and burning resistors suggests too much voltage or wrong resistor values. 1 There is concern about inrush current when there is no series resistor to limit it. 2 Replacement capacitors need low ESR, especially in high current output filter circuits, and while slightly larger capacity is fine, much larger capacity might cause inrush current issues. 3
