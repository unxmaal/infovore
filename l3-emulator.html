L3 emulator

Claims: 3 Part of L3

The L3 emulator is a stack installed on an ancient Red Hat system to interface with L1s via USB. 1 The L3 emulator is available for download from http://ftp.irixnet.org/sgi-tools/l3-emulator-linux.tar.gz. 2 The L3 emulator is also on IST discs, which also contain big iron diagnostics. 3 Support for Onyx is no longer available, making IST discs more relevant for RealityEngines. 3
