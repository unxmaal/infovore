64-bit PCI

Claims: 3 Part of 64-bit Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The Octane PCI cage supports 64-bit PCI, not PCI-X. 1 The Octane has 64-bit PCI at 33 MHz and runs at 5V, requiring 5V or Universal cards. 2 PCI-X is 133 MHz and backwards compatible. 2 This PCI adapter supports 3.3V and 5V, 64-bit PCI at 33/66MHz and PCI-X at 66/100/133MHz, with a bridge converting to x4 PCIe Gen 1. 3
