SCA

Claims: 33

SCA is a standardized connector type that combines power and data in a single connector and supports daisy chaining SCSI disks. 1 SCA makes it easy to plug in a new drive and, on IRIX, provided hotswap functionality. 23 SCA is a wide SCSI connector used in the O2, Octane, and O2K, while the Sparcstation 20 uses regular SCA. 45 The I2 system uses a drive tray/sled connector that is similar to SCA but incompatible with SCA drives. 678 The Indigo2 uses narrow SCSI with a 50-pin backplane connector that is not SCA, so an SCA disk cannot be connected directly. 910 The O2 uses a connector compatible with SCA, but force-fitting a device into an SCA slot does not guarantee it will work. 11 SCA drives are preferred for higher capacity. 12 SCA is not necessarily faster, as UW SCSI runs at the same speed as the 68-pin version. 13
