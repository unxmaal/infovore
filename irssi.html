irssi

Claims: 8

Irssi v1.1.2 supports SSL connections on port 6697. 1 irssi requires glib 2.28, though an older version might work. 2 irssi can be used as a console app by ssh-ing into another machine. 23 irssi handles Unicode better than IRCII-based clients, which had trouble adapting to it. 45 irssi handles unicode nicely on IRIX. 6 An IPX can run irssi, slrn, and mutt. 7 irssi requires specific sun packages for its libc compatibility and may not work without them. 8
