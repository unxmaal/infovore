Blade 150

Claims: 41

The Sun Blade 150 is an entry-level Sun workstation from the early 2000s, also known as the Sun Blade 150, that started as an Ultra 10 before being upgraded to the Sun Blade 2500. 123 It is based on PC architecture, making it the only Sun machine with PCI slots, and uses a US-2 motherboard. 45 The workstation has a 650 MHz CPU and is small, and its graphics were upgraded to the best for the model. 678 It can support up to 2GB of RAM and can do ATA66. 910 The Blade 150 uses PCI 2.2 or 2.3, while the U10 implements PCI 2.1. 11 It has a working NVRAM, though later Blades had NVRAM issues that prevented POST. 1213 The Blade 150 has both a timekeeper chip and a coin cell for NVRAM, but only on some revisions, and the coin cell was a workaround for a hardware issue. 1415 Programming NVRAM requires using a different system due to OpenBoot version differences. 16
