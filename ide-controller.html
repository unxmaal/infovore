IDE controller

Claims: 15 Part of IDE

The DS10L only works on NT4 in arc with 2.5 SCSI hdds and an IDE controller, in addition to a heat limitation. 1 An LS120 drive can be connected to an IDE controller without an FDC. 2 The IDE controller on the Ultra 5/10 is problematic, so a SCSI controller is recommended for better performance. 3 The Blade 100/150 has a PCB dividing the drive bay area from the PCI card/mobo area, requiring tricky cable routing. 4 The Blade 100/150 has a newer, better IDE controller compared to older models, though not as bad as the U5/10. 5 The U5 has a crappy IDE controller and network card. 6 The Blade 100 has OBP version 4 and an improved IDE controller. 7 A Disk On Module IDE flash drive in a Netra seems good but has the same IDE controller as the U5/10. 8
