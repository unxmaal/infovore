DB15 connector

Claims: 6

The DB15 connector appears on the frontplane of some SGI machines, where it may have been used for serial debugging or for testing the boards at the factory. 1 The DB15 connector is a common port used for joysticks, MIDI on PCs, and video on older Macs. 2 On Bondi iMacs, the front IR sensor connects with a MiniDIN8 under the plastic bottom cover, while the monitor connects with a DB-15 connector. 3 The DB15 port can be used for the keyboard and mouse using a serial connection rather than DB9, via an Lk201 or Lk401 style splitter cable that is compatible with DECstation 5000 and some VAXstation 4000. 4 The pinout for the DB15 connector is not standardized; the hardware reference manual specifies the transmit and receive lines, ground lines, and +5V/+12V for the keyboard and mouse. 5
