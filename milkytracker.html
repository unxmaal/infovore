MilkyTracker

Claims: 6

MilkyTracker runs on IRIX using SDL, though the IRIX audio system is considered unusual, and Milky Tracker or Sunvox might work on a Milky port. 12 MilkyTracker with GL-enabled SDL2 is slow, while the Neko version of MilkyTracker is fast. 34 milkytracker is available in nekoware, and schismtracker requires SDL2. 5 MilkyTracker can play Radix - Yuki Satellies.xm, tested with SDL CPU hog but functional. 6
