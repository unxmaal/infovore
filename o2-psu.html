O2 PSU

Claims: 25 Part of O2

The O2 PSU may spin up when the power cable is connected but do nothing else, and it can fail, smell acrid, and cause the system to cut off and not restart until the PSU is reseated or the power-on jumper is set. 1234 O2 PSU failures are similar to Indy Nidec PSU failures, as both machines are sensitive to power handling and can be damaged by opening them improperly. 5 An ATX PSU with 1200W is needed to provide enough amperage on the 3.3V line to replace the 170W O2 PSU, and recapping the O2 PSU can increase CPU clock speed according to hinv. 67 Refurbishing Octane or O2 PSUs could be a viable option for under $100, and an O2 PSU solution would be a significant improvement. 89 The O2 PSU has too many capacitors, including a 3.48V rail that couldn't be calibrated and a 3.3V standby rail at 3.3V with a main 3.3V rail at 3.43V providing up to 30A. 101112 The O2 PSU requires some pins to be jumped to power on out of the chassis, and it can be difficult to take apart, with the main shell connected only by a small plastic ribbon at the top. 1314 The O2 PSU sheet metal is soft like butter because it is fully annealed, and the PSU does double as its smokestack. 1516 An O2 PSU failure was caused by a capacitor issue, and a backup battery going into protection mode due to a direct short to ground likely prevented a more serious incident. 17
