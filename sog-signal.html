SoG signal

Claims: 3 Part of SoG

  • Some Dells get confused with both H/V sync and SoG signal; snipping sync pins on the VGA cable works around this. (user-6afa, exchange 22773, 2021-11-28; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The `xsetmon` command can change the resolution IRIX runs at, but a monitor that doesn't properly filter SoG signals may cause green coloration that can't be fixed with software. (user-b7c4, exchange 3982, 2019-10-25; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Digital monitors may not display an SoG signal properly, leading to black screens or incorrect colors. (user-56d4, exchange 103405, 2022-04-27; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
