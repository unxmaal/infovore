PCI bridge

Claims: 4 Part of PCI

An M.2 NVMe drive typically has 4 PCIe lanes, and installing it in an x4 PCIe carrier and an x16 to PCI bridge will likely run at x1. 1 PCI bridges are handled differently in 6.5.22 and 6.5.30, with 6.5.30 having better PCI bridge support than 6.5.22. 23
