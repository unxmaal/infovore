A4000

Claims: 25

The A4000 is a professional Amiga model that was better suited for workstations than home editions like the A500, A600, and A1200, and it was upgraded for use in SFX work. 12 The A4000's main advantage over the A2000 and A3000 for 3D work was better color display for previews, not the final product. 3 The A4000 is expensive, with some saying it costs at least 4000, and it is not affordable for personal use. 45 Prices vary widely, with an A4000/40 found for $200 that requires fixing a broken trace and recapping the board, while an A4000 with a CyberStorm sold for $3300 in March. 67 The CyberStorm is worth more than the A4000, and a rebuilt A4000 with a new 68040 board or a Furia graphics card promises good speed. 89 The A4000 can take an IDE Zip 100, run as a Hackintosh with Fusion 3.2 (though AppleShare causes a hard crash), and play SimCity 2000 without Mac emulation. 101112 The A4000 with a 060 CPU at 80 MHz struggles with MP3 playback and requires a decode board for sound, while a 040 CPU cannot manage MP3s but can with trickery in codecs. 1314 The A4000 with Zorro III memory emulation via ZZ9000 is slow for Mac emulation, and the A314-CP clockport version can be installed on an A4000's Prisma Megamix, requiring address adjustments for software compatibility. 1516
