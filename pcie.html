PCIe

Claims: 42 Background: 8 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see PCI Express, PCI-X, Accelerated Graphics Port, NVM Express, Raspberry Pi.

PCIe was introduced in 2003. 1 PCIe is electrically different from PCI and PCI-X, but PCI-X is 64-bit and faster than 32-bit PCI, and PCIe can be bridged by cheap adapter cards. 2 PCIe is not backwards compatible with PCI slots, though PCI and PCIe are both PCI from a software perspective. 34 PCIe is point-to-point, which helps with I/O compared to total bus speed, and requires a PHY unlike PCI. 56 Some early PCIe GPU cards were AGP to PCIe bridges, and PCIe devices should be up and running 100ms after PERST# is deasserted. 78 PCI is a shared bus, while PCIe uses a star network. 9 A PMC card with multiple 1394 chips behind a PCIe xbar and a PCIe-PCI bridge can work with most SBCs that have PCI-X root complexes. 10
