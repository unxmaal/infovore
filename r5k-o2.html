R5k O2

Claims: 15

Most O2 workstations are R5k O2s, and they can be inexpensive, with examples found for around $220 to $250, though some units may not be working. 123 It can boot with an R10K CPU and R10K tray, but this may require a different drive configuration, and using a PCI riser is unnecessary unless an R10k-class CPU is installed. 45 The system can run Linux distributions such as Gentoo and Debian, though it requires plentiful RAM. 67 The Onyx1 with Reality Engine 2 is likely slower than the R5k O2 and probably not fast enough to launch the game. 8
