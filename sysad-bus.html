sysad bus

Claims: 4

The O2 has two processors on the sysAD bus, and the RM7000C cache also sits on the SysAD bus, where it cannot run as fast as the CPU. 12 Theories existed for increasing the sysAD bus to overclock CPUs, but relevant pages are lost. 3 One could change the clock reference and overclock the sysAD bus, core clock, and cache clock, but this is not recommended. 4
