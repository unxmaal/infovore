USB keyboard

Claims: 15 Part of USB Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Field-programmable gate array, PS/2 port.

USB keyboards can be used in place of PS/2 keyboards, though compatibility depends on the connection method, as dumb wired adapters provide backwards compatibility while USB-to-PS2 adapters do not work. 12 USB keyboards can establish a proper boot in some systems, such as the B1000, while in others they work for general use but not for booting or graphics. 34 USB keyboards are supported as standard peripherals in FPGA systems and can resolve KVM issues when used as an extra keyboard. 56 USB keyboards are listed alongside other peripherals such as USB mice, PS/2 adapters, and audio dongles, and the USB keyboards from that era have similar quality to older style keyboards. 78 USB audio adapters are the only useful USB devices in IRIX, where USB mice and keyboards work in PROM but fail to load the OS. 9 The cable length of a USB keyboard needs to be tested. 10
