Arduino

Claims: 10

The Arduino is cheap, easy to use, and has good support for various peripherals. 1 The Arduino can implement digital logic and analog signals with simple opamp buffers, including polyrhythms and weighted random outputs. 2 An Arduino can be used to build a MIDI-controlled analog synth. 3 The Arduino uses TTL levels, incompatible with RS423 and RS232, so voltage level shifting is needed when connecting it to an Indy. 45 The Arduino program sets up serial communication at 9600 and 19200 baud. 6 The Arduino company has had trademark disputes. 7
