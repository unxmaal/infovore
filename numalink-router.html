Numalink router

Claims: 8 Part of Numalink Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Origin 3000 and Onyx 3000.

The Numalink router is required to connect multiple Origin 300 (O300) machines together. 12 For optimal setup, 2-4 bricks use an L2 Controller, 3-4 bricks use only the Numalink Router, and 5-8 bricks use both. 3 From L1, you can communicate with any other brick on the Numalink Router. 4 Serials can be configured on the O350, Fuel, Tezro, and Numalink Router when the Dallas chip is cleared. 5 The Numalink router (M-serials) runs on 110/240V, while the R-brick (L-serials) needs 48V. 6 The O350's NUMAlink port can be in a POWER DOWN state even if the system is powered on. 7
