Mashek

Claims: 21

Mashek sells high-end O2s, Octanes, and parts for Origin gear, and offers competitive pricing on O200s and R5k boards ($300), as well as vintage SGI systems at reasonable though still expensive prices, including quad proc user-8a09 systems for $2500 as recently as 2014-2016. 12345 Mashek primarily sells to companies using SGI hardware at premium rates but offers actual warranties and proper shipping, and may offer discounts to hobbyists, including on multiple items, though discounts are unlikely for rare parts. 678 Mashek is considered a reliable seller with good experiences, though it can be slow to respond, and shipping from the UK to the USA is impressively fast. 910 Mashek and Ian/SGI Depot set the upper limit for SGI part pricing, and Mashek or Ian's SGI Depot are reliable spots for getting a sled. 1112 A whole motherboard with V11 prom from Mashek costs around $150, and an Octane front plane from Mashek may not include a system ID button. 1314
