Powered Peripheral port

Claims: 3

The SGI Powered Peripheral Port can be identified by a DIN-8 plug and has 3 pins, possibly fewer on the 4D/210 or 4D/310, and is documented in man(7) serial. 12 Stereo glasses can be powered by the Powered Peripheral port, though they typically use a separate adapter. 3
