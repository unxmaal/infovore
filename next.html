NeXT

Claims: 49 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXT, NeXTSTEP.

NeXT was a company founded by Steve Jobs in the United States. 1 Steve Jobs combined various features and ideas into a workable, usable system for NeXT, which resembled a Mac with OS X 10 to 15 years later. 2 The NeXT hardware was not well-regarded, with the NeXTSTEP software being the better part of the company. 3 NeXT could sell you a CISC workstation and was working on a RISC system before they shut down their hardware side. 45 Apple bought NeXT and developed Mac OS X based on NeXT's system. 6 Apple's transition from NeXT hardware to software was a strategic move, despite the NeXT hardware being questionable. 7
