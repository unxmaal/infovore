sound card

Claims: 14 Part of sound

The Apple IIe can be fitted with a sound card, and its internal speaker can be made functional by cutting and soldering a 3.5mm cable so that audio works through the sound card. 12 The Apple IIe Platinum includes a sound card along with 1MB of RAM. 3 Some SPARC workstations can be adapted with a sound card, though this is not recommended. 4 The SGI O2 uses a separate sound card, though most O2 machines had basic audio outputs available. 5 The IBM POWER Intellistation 285 has graphics hardware and can accept some form of sound card. 6 Only on the Personal Iris sound card are electrolytic capacitors used rather than tantalum, and these can cause trace damage when they fail. 7 The Fuel can boot with 10MB/sec sound card performance. 8 On the 486, the CD-ROM needs to be connected to the sound card to work. 9
