ZX2000

Claims: 10 Part of ZX

The HP zx2000 and zx6000 are Itanium-based workstations similar to the C8000 series but with different cases and CPU limitations. 1 The zx2000 was a desktop workstation that could only take one CPU and had onboard audio. 23 It was a lower-end version of the zx6000 that might run VMS. 4 The zx6000 was thinner than the zx2000, leading to worse thermals with dual CPUs. 5 Itanium was primarily used in high-performance computing and databases, with only a few workstations supporting it, mainly the HP zx2000 and zx6000. 6
