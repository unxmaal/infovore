SGI 750

Claims: 5 Part of SGI

The SGI 750 is a rebadge of Intel's Itanium 1 reference design, sharing variants with HP, IBM, Siemens, and Fujitsu, and is the same machine as the HP i2000 with SGI branding. 12 It is a rebranded Intel offering that is Itanium-based and can run both HP-UX and Windows 2000 IA64. 34 The Merced system from SGI, the 750, was killed almost instantly. 5
