shutter glasses

Claims: 6

Shutter glasses work by blocking alternating eyes, which halves the refresh rate and can cause flicker, especially on low refresh rate displays like PAL CRTs. 12 Because they require a high refresh rate, shutter glasses are viable with CRTs for the most part. 34 They can also work with later LCD hardware. 5 An Arduino-based IR emitter can potentially fake different protocols for shutter glasses. 6
