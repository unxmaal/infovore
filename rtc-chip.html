RTC chip

Claims: 5 Part of RTC

The RTC chip can be involved in fixing soft power-off issues caused by a non-working power button on some systems, which may be resolved by adjusting a jumper next to the RTC chip. 1 Unlike some previous SGI machines (e.g. Indy, Indigo2), the O2 does not store its ethernet MAC address on the RTC chip; instead, it is located on a three-pin DS2502 chip on the PCI riser. 2 When changing the RTC chip on an O2, a resetenv and bypass jumper may not resolve boot issues, and existing guides may not work; a NVRAM dump from a functioning O2 might be needed to diagnose the problem. 3

Sources

  1. The soft power off through the power button not working on some systems can be fixed by adjusting a jumper next to the RTC chip. (user-bc18, 2019-07-11, exchange 95338, check: uncheckable)
  2. Unlike some previous SGI machines (e.g. Indy, Indigo2), the O2 doesn't store its ethernet MAC address on this chip (it is instead located on a three-pin DS2502 chip on the PCI riser), however, the RTC chip is responsible (user-bc18, 2019-08-11, exchange 2982, check: supported)
  3. When changing the RTC chip on an O2, a resetenv and bypass jumper may not resolve boot issues, and existing guides may not work; a NVRAM dump from a functioning O2 might be needed to diagnose the problem. (user-c831, 2020-01-22, exchange 5652, check: supported)
