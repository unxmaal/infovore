O2/Octane

Claims: 4

SGI made drivers for some cards that are recognized on O2/Octane but don't get /hw entries on pre-ip35 machines. 1 IRIX can see firewire mass storage as a mountable SCSI volume on IP35, possibly on O2/octane, but firewire mass storage doesn't work on O2s with the DV card. 2 For 3D tasks, consider an Indigo2 with Impact graphics or an O2/Octane with at least 256MB RAM. 3 The O2/Octane require a keyboard to power on (or at least show something). 4
