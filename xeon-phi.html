Xeon Phi

Claims: 6 Part of Xeon

The Xeon Phi is a Larrabee-based manycore x86 coprocessor that can be used as a many-core processor in a PCI slot and functions as an embedded Linux system. 123 It requires specific Linux kernels and cannot run standard x86 ELF binaries. 1 The Xeon Phi is not widely used due to lack of clear applications and did not work well on the T2 SPARC. 34 Drivers, compiler, and OpenCL runtime for Xeon Phi are still available. 5 Omnipath, a high-speed interconnect technology, was cancelled due to lack of demand for its associated Xeon Phi and Xeon Scalable F SKUs. 6
