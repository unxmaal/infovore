VAXen

Claims: 5

Most vintage machines are VAXen or Alphas, with fewer MIPSen. 1 The Raspberry Pi can emulate VAXen, though the MIPS emulation in MAME is buggy and uses more CPU than necessary. 2 Small VAXen range from 0.9 VUPS to about 40 VUPS. 3 OpenBSD could run on VAXen. 4 The DELNI is not a hub but counts as a certain distance of 10base5 cabling, and they came with AUI cables for VAXen. 5
