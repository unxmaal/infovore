Indigo1

Claims: 98 Part of Indigo Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

With Indigo2

The Indigo2 outperforms the Indigo1, is faster than the Indigo1 gio64, and requires fewer special peripherals. 12 The Indigo2 and Indigo1 use the same CPU modules for the same speed and spec CPUs, except for the CPU cooler, which can be swapped. 3 The Indigo2 was released with Extreme, while the Indigo1 topped out with Elan, and the Indy was released 6 months later with XL8/24, followed by XZ for both the Indigo2 and Indy a year later, with Impact coming to the Indigo. 4 The Indigo1 and Indigo2 allow for modifications like placing components on top, while the Octane does not support such modifications. 5 Indigo2 and Indigo1 drive sleds are not compatible, and the drive sled is for an Indigo1; the power connector differs from an Indigo2. 67 Indigo1 uses a different SCSI controller than Indigo2, and BlueSCSI firmware with phase control can fix Indigo2's SCSI issues. 8 The Cherokee, O2, indigo2 impact, and indigo1 are vintage SGI machines. 9

General

The Indigo1 is a low-end SGI machine that runs IRIX, ships with IRIX 4Dx, and can also run IRIX 6.5.22, Solaris, and older software such as Alias and Softimage. 10111213 It uses a proprietary keyboard, the last SGI to do so, so owners need a keyboard or mouse adapter. 1415 The Crimson is a low-end Indigo1 sold solely as a single-CPU graphics box with limited VME slots, and it cannot run IRIX 6.5. 161718 The Indigo1 is prone to PSU problems, and some units are rebranded without the SGI logo. 1920 The Indigo1 is a personal IRIS workstation that used the SGI bus and IRIS port, and was the last SGI workstation to use the IRIS port. 212223 It was available with an R3000 CPU at 30 MHz and with an R4K CPU. 242526 It runs 1280x1024 24bit 72Hz on a 20" CRT, and supports standard VGA output. 2728 It can switch endian and requires -5V and -12V for its power supply. 2930 It has a floppy drive, though FAT support for IRIX via floppy exchange was never implemented. 31 It requires a special keyboard and an adapter for Ethernet. 3233 It has a 100% failure rate, with CPU boards that do not work but functional graphics. 3435 It has power supply issues, including problems when booting IRIX due to full hardware initialization. 3637 The Indigo1 has an XS24Z graphics board and a R3000 CPU. 38 Modern computers lack the industrial design and front-loading design of the Indigo1. 39
