Indy R5000

Claims: 6 Part of Indy

The Indy R5000 and R4400 run IRIX 5.3. 1 The Indy R5000 can hang during netboot installation at various percentages, possibly due to issues with the disk drive or RAM configuration. 2 Installing IRIX over the network to ZuluSCSI on an Indy R5000 can terminate prematurely due to issues with onboard termination; disabling it may help. 3 The Indy R5000 board without the voltage regulator is a 150 MHz model, while the 180 MHz model requires a voltage regulator and has a fan connector on J9. 4 The base graphics use an 8-bit color palette. 5 The Indy has been hosted for 25 years. 6
