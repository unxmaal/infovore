Power Mac G3

Claims: 9 Part of POWER

The Power Mac G3 can run macOS 7.6 and has built-in PS/2 and VGA ports, along with an onboard NIC limited to 10 megabit. 12 It has a blue power button that glows green, and different models may have variations in their power button casings. 3 A common PSU issue is a weak 5V trickle, which is fixable with a recap or ATX conversion. 4 The Power Mac G3 and PowerBook G3 used hardware MPEG decoding, and the desktop model with a Bordeaux card can decode MPEG and DVD video. 56 It can be used to install a SCSI drive, and running Photoshop + iTunes + Dreamweaver on a 256MB model is possible but can be unstable. 78
