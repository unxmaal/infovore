Mini

Claims: 8

The Mini has a 16GB version and can only support two monitors: one via Thunderbolt/USB-C DP Alt Mode and one via the onboard HDMI. 1 The original drives the Mini shipped with were cheap laptop drives, IDE based, but an mSATA adapter and card can be used. 2 OS4 was never released; MorphOS runs well on a Mini but its license costs may not be justifiable. 3
