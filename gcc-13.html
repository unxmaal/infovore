GCC 13

Claims: 6 Part of GCC

GCC 13 is available as part of modern toolchains, with clang offered as an alternative to gcc13. 1 LLVM Clang 16 is faster than GCC 13 in 57% of tests. 2 The port of OpenJDK for IRIX is being worked on by aurxenon, who aims to make a version of GCC 13 for IRIX as a prerequisite for a zerovm. 34 The plan involves using this GCC 13 to cross-compile Zero VM, which will then be used to compile OpenJDK 8 with MIPSpro, requiring modifications to OpenJDK 8's non-java portions, all C++ 03 and c99. 35 The project is currently stuck on step 1 of making a GCC 13 for IRIX. 6
