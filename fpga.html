FPGA

Claims: 52 Background: 11 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Field-programmable gate array, Hardware emulation.

FPGAs are used to emulate various systems, including the Iris 1000-3000 models, the SGI 600 (which can serve as a docking port), and SparcStation machines such as the MiSTer. 1234 FPGA emulation can provide more accurate experiences and avoid issues like price gouging and hardware failures. 5 An FPGA can be considered a real CPU when configured to perform processing tasks, and building a fast CPU yourself requires using an FPGA. 67 The first step in designing a CPU is to design the ALU and then write it to an FPGA. 8 An FPGA design can use dual QSPI flash memories to boot in under 100ms for PCIe bus access. 9 FPGAs have been used in SGI hardware, including the RM7000C (later replaced by branded ASICs) and the TF1260, and the R10K CPU's clock speed on the O2 is limited by the FPGA. 101112
