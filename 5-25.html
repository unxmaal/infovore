5.25

Claims: 8

Full and half 5.25 and 3.5 inch drives are referred to by their physical size. 1 The O2 is the outlier, with no place on an Indigo, Indy or Octane to put a 5.25" drive, though some did have 5.25. 2 The Octane lacks a 5.25" bay for internal SCSI drives, requiring external units. 3 The RS/6000 can use a 5.25" floppy drive as an uncommon option. 4 The Greaseweazle is used for archiving and writing to 5.25" disks, providing raw flux access for accurate backups. 5 5.25" floppies could still be valid for TARGA software, working even without sleeves. 6 A 5.25" drive was erasing floppies constantly due to a stuck write head. 7
