PowerAnimator

Claims: 34 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see PowerAnimator.

It originated as an industrial design tool but has animation roots from Wavefront and Alias. 1 PowerAnimator was a key application that showcased the special hardware of SGI systems. 2 It can be used for animations, as seen in the creation of South Park. 3 DKC models were mostly made with PowerAnimator, according to a Rare interview. 4 The software suite includes a video texture map demo and manuals with tutorial projects. 56
