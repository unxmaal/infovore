SSP

Claims: 4

SSP is the IBM System/36 OS and can be ported to certain early PowerPC as/400s running os/400 SLIC without needing a license. 1 The System/34 and System/36 run different versions of SSP, and the System/36 SSP is not compatible with System/34 SSP. 2 SSP for System/36 has different versions for hardware revisions and is personalized. 3 The SPARC M3000 requires setting the time on the SSP before it can be powered on. 4
