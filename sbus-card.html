SBus card

Claims: 12 Part of SBus

SBus cards were used in specific storage servers from around 2000, likely for cache or write operations, and a Fibre-channel SBus card was used for NFS servers. 1 An SBus card with a MIPS or PPC CPU can be used for a serial console server. 2 The SBus card can run as a standalone machine with software emulation for VGA, USB, PS2, sound, and network, and running SunPC software on the SBus card provides the same experience as a full system. 34 No PC on a SBus card had a PS/2 port. 5 Rasterops made an SBus card similar to Sun's P4 device, a simple framebuffer. 6
