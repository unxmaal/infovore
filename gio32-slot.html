GIO32 slot

Claims: 3 Part of GIO32

The GIO32 slot on the Indy mobo responds to writes with the correct physical address but not reads. 1 The second GIO32 slot on the Indy mobo is mapped to a different address, possibly controlled by address decoding on the mobo. 2 The G130 network card for the Challenge S must be installed in a specific GIO32 slot to avoid conflicts with the SCSI system. 3
