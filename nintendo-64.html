Nintendo 64

Claims: 17 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Silicon Graphics.

The Nintendo 64 can be connected to a TV using an official adapter or a VCR. 1 The Nintendo 64's graphics were based on SGI's RealityEngine, and its hardware includes a cutdown Indy CPU capable of achieving similar graphical effects to SGI's workstations. 23 The Nintendo 64 is a cost-reduced version of the O2's architecture, mainly due to shared core hardware, and its graphics card is identical to the O2's. 45 Developers could use an Onyx with a RealityEngine to preview N64 games, and the O2 could develop for the Nintendo 64, with SGI emphasizing its affordability compared to other workstations. 26 The Nintendo 64 has interrupt and shared memory space, and the Ultra64 board can interrupt and reset it, a capability not present in a stock Nintendo 64. 78 IS-Viewer is a tool for Nintendo 64 development with support in the official SDK and is more obtainable than the Ultra64 board. 9 The Nintendo 64 is difficult to overclock due to the RCP being a bottleneck. 10
