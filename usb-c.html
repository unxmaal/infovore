USB-C

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Laptop.

USB-C is electrically superior, but most companies do not implement USB 3. 1 Modern smartphones have USB-C ports, which allow for multiple display outputs and LAN connections. 2 USB-C could theoretically work for a serial cable, but crosstalk would likely be a problem on a parallel bus. 3 Manufacturers sometimes switch from separate charging port modules to USB-C only soldered directly to the motherboard, which can cause issues with modern laptops like the T-series Thinkpad. 4 The laptop uses a power bridge feature to charge over USB-C. 5
