router boards

Claims: 6 Part of router

Router boards are components found in various SGI systems, including Metarouters, Origin 2000 systems, and Octane motherboards. 123 The Metarouter rack contains an O2K chassis with standard router boards in the back and a front filled with router boards, which explains the presence of many craylink cables and a grey fascia. 2 Parts from an Origin 2000 system available for sale include router boards, a midplane, IO6, and a CD drive/power switch with key. 3 Router boards need to be reseated to ensure proper functioning. 4 An Origin2000 deskside can hold two router boards, but typically has room for only one, and a null router is standard. 5 The two router boards in an O2000 deskside or rack talk to each other. 6
