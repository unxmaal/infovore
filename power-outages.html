power outages

Claims: 3 Part of POWER

Newer SGI machines are more resilient to power outages than older ones, with some models featuring built-in battery backups to prevent data loss. 1 Machines without battery backups may still lose data and require a full restart after a power outage. 2 Small lead acid batteries can be used in older machines to preserve encoder positions during power outages. 3
