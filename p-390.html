P/390

Claims: 12

The P/390 is a PCI card containing a cut-down CMOS S/390 mainframe CPU, and it was the first to develop a single chip on CMOS 5X equivalent technology. 12 It ran OS/2 and housed control apps, and it was used in integrated servers. 3 The P/390 processor is about as powerful as a single CPU 3090 from the mid 80s, with about 220K gates, which seems low for its power. 45 The processor is slow due to its microprogrammed implementation to fit in a CMOS die. 6 The 33G8469 is a P/390 peripheral supported on the ISA bus only. 7 To run a P/390 on an x86 machine, one needs to check the BIOS for 'read around write' support; otherwise, it likely won't work. 8 The AMI BIOS supports the P/390 but requires going through the self-test procedure; it can IPL the system. 9 A 200MHz Pentium desktop failed to drive a P/390. 10
