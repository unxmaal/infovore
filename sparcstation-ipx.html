SparcStation IPX

Claims: 4

The SparcStation IPX is a Sun computer that can run OpenBSD, 4.1.4, Solaris 7, or NeXT. 1 For the SparcStation IPC and IPX, the battery and oscillator can be replaced by removing the clock chip from its socket and cutting through the top layer with a hacksaw. 2 SparcStation IPX units are available for sale. 3 Building a SparcStation Classic / IPC / IPX tower of power is a goal. 4
