Softimage 2.66

Claims: 9 Part of Softimage

Softimage 2.66 runs on all ELF IRIX systems, including IRIX 4, IRIX 5.3, and SGI 5.3 with hardware rendering on Elan. 1234 It can also run on the Indy, where a 1994 version of SoftImage 2.66 is available. 5 For Softimage 2.66, use 'setenv SI_DO_NOT_CHECK_CD_DAEMON 1' to skip the CD DAEMON check. 6 SoftImage 2.66 uses a homegrown licensing system that is COFF-based and does not use FlexLM. 7 On QIC tape, SoftImage 2.66 can import video from VideoLab and VideoFramer but requires licenses. 8
