parallel port

Claims: 16 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see DMA attack, PCI-X, Serial port.

PLIP allowed DOS and early Linux systems to use the parallel port for networking, achieving speeds of 40kbyte/s, and newer ECP/EPP ports could reach megabytes per second but were more prone to issues. 1 Parallel port networking faced issues like IRQ/DMA problems and flaky drivers/OS, especially in DOS. 2 Disabling DMA on the parallel port can resolve issues with a Zip drive on Windows NT. 3 The parallel port on the IBM 9595 can be configured for bidirectional or EPP mode, and the DOS driver works on the first port. 4 The true parallel port allows adding another bit by adding another pin. 5
