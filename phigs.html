PHIGS

Claims: 6

PHIGS was a graphics API that existed before OpenGL. 1 PHIGS was available on HP-UX, AIX, and DEC MIPS systems, where it was supported while OpenGL was alpha only. 234 PHIGS did not support texture mapping, and SGI supported PHIGS through its final card. 35 ProE likely required an alternative renderer like PHIGS for platforms other than IRIX. 6
