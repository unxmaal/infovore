Traces

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Blender (software).

Blender is a 3D modeling program originally developed for SGI systems and later open-sourced, with its name inspired by the song 'Blender' by Yello, and its origins traced back to a program called TRACES for the Commodore. 1 The SGI version of TRACES is newer, more stable, and might bridge the gap between TRACES (Amiga) and Blender. 2 Traces runs on IRIX; it's now practically real-time. 3 Traces needs libimbuf.so, a 32-bit library. 4 Traces was available from 1987 to 1991, a year before the A600 came out. 5 There are calculators pre-existing for working out impedances on traces. 6 Troubleshooting the N64 may involve contact cleaning or repairing corroded traces near the CPU or RCP. 7 Repairing the PCB involved fixing 6 broken traces, with 3 repaired using wires. 8
