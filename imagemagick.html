ImageMagick

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see GIMP.

ImageMagick can handle terapixel-sized images and supports a wide range of formats, even on IRIX; it includes a hidden GUI for displaying images. 1 GIMP, ImageMagick, and GraphicsMagick support the .sgi format. 2 Software such as ImageMagick ran on the R3k Indigo, which used gcc 2.x. 3 YTP+ is a graphical shell that uses ImageMagick along with JavaFX and FFmpeg. 4 Compiling ImageMagick on an Indy may not be recommended, as they use GCC 8.2.0. 5
