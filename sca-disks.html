SCA disks

Claims: 10 Part of SCA

SCA disks are used in the O2, Octane, and Origin/Onyx systems. 1 SCA disks in plain brown boxes with black ink are for the O2 and Octane, while those in wooden boxes are for the Onyx2. 123 SCA disks with 300GB capacity are available. 4 The SS20 can use SCA disks as long as the hole spacing matches and the disk does not have protrusions exceeding the depth of the plate, but the outside bracket is incompatible. 56 Some SCA disks do not work with the U320 SCSI interface even with a proper adapter, and some newer SCA disks require a jumper to force single-ended SCSI mode. 78 Cheap SCSI SCA or LVD disks are more reliable than SD cards, especially for older systems. 9 BlueSCSI and ZuluSCSI emulators can achieve up to 20MB/s, but SCA disks can reach 100MB/s or more. 10
