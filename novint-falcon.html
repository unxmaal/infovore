Novint Falcon

Claims: 3

The Novint Falcon was a 3DoF force-feedback joystick with an optional pistol grip, marketed as a mouse replacement but not ideal for that purpose. 1 It was more suitable for thrusting motions than 2DoF joysticks due to its 3DoF arms. 2 The Novint Falcon saw renewed interest during the DK2 era of VR due to its replaceable handles and suitability for thrusting motions. 3
