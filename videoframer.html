VideoFramer

Claims: 12

The VideoFramer is a VME card (a 9U VME assembly with a SCSI port) that can be used inside the IRIS and as a breakout box for the VME 6U card 030-0185-00x, available without daughter boards. 123 It is also available for the O2, and may work with the Galileo due to similar connector and design, though the Galileo has no electronics. 456 The VideoFramer is actually called SlideShowFramer because it processes video frame by frame rather than in real-time. 7 It requires a single-frame capable video deck such as Betacam or U-Matic, a BlackMagic Hyperdeck, a VLAN-capable deck that understands VideoLan commands, a serial-controlled VTR, or a software emulator to interface with an SGI system. 89 The VideoFramer in the 4D/35 and the VideoLab in the 4D/380 have D1 digital video I/O, but the VideoFramer can only do single-frame I/O while the VideoLab can do real-time I/O and project live video as a texture on geometry. 10 Both the VideoFramer and VideoLab have digital I/O on the card but need a breakout box for analogue component video, and composite video is not supported by the VideoLab. 11 SoftImage 2.66 on QIC tape can import video from VideoLab and VideoFramer but requires licenses. 12
