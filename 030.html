030

Claims: 9

The Motorola 030 processor was used in various systems, including the Next Computer, which was based on the 030 processor. 1 The TerribleFire board is an 030, representing a massive jump up in performance from 68k processors. 2 Individual Computers' newer 020 and 030 accelerators are great and can handle a lot of RAM. 3 However, the fastest Individual Computers accelerators are either 030s or hobbled 040s, and are expensive. 4 A/UX is slow on a 030, and formatting an 18GB drive in a IIcx takes a long time with 16MHz of 030 power. 56
