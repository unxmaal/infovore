NVRAM battery

Claims: 8 Part of NVRAM

The NVRAM battery in a Sparcstation 4 often retains its functionality. 1 A failed NVRAM battery can cause issues such as long video output times in serial tests, leading to diag-mode and verbose POST. 2 A faulty NVRAM battery can cause a time discrepancy, which can be corrected by setting the time in a miniroot. 3 Resetting the NVRAM battery may resolve issues with the MAC address. 4 You need a new NVRAM battery, and the Ultra5/10 can reprogram it from the ok prompt, with a kit from glitchworks available to help. 56
