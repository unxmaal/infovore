n32 ABI

Claims: 4 Part of n32

The N32 ABI is used for running 32-bit code on a 64-bit OS. 1 The n32 ABI is not well tested, and codegen issues exist with LLVM. 2 MIPS codegen works with n32 ABI even on a poorly tested target. 3 QEMU-IRIX supports building 32-bit MIPS binaries using the n32 ABI, and does not automatically enable 64-bit mode with -march. 4
