gpubench_ogl1

Claims: 3

gpubench and gpubench_ogl1 are IRIX N32 binaries requiring OpenGL 1.1 and 1.0, respectively. 1 gpubench requires IRIX 6.5 for textures, while pre-6.5 IRIX 6.x systems can use the gpubench_ogl1 binary renamed to gpubench. 2 An O32 binary for older IRIX systems is available separately. 2
