QL2342

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The QL2342 is used in the Octane, O2, O2K/Onyx2/Origin 200, and newer systems, including the Octane2, which also has gigabit Ethernet and dual Ultra160 SCSI. 12 The QL12160 and QL2342 network cards require IRIX 6.5 or later, while the older gigabit Ethernet board can work with IRIX 6.4. 3
