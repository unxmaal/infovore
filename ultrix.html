Ultrix

Claims: 58 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Ultrix, VAXstation.

Ultrix is a Unix variant that ran on both VAX and MIPS architectures, including DECstations, VAXstations, and the Alpha-based Ultrix/DECchip AXp. 1234 It is a complex mix of V7, BSD, and System V interfaces, and is considered similar to SunOS 4. 56 DEC abandoned Ultrix for OSF/1 and Tru64 when the Alpha was released, and DECstations became the main platform for Ultrix by that point. 78 Ultrix can be installed over the network, including via netboot from NetBSD or RIS. 91011 It requires multiple small partitions for various purposes. 12 Ultrix came with a wide range of software, though its filesystem is not ideal for major mail hubs. 1314 Ultrix, OSF/1, Digital Unix, and Tru64 are Unix variants available on archive.org. 15 Ultrix is one of the few old commercial BSDs, is fairly 4.2BSD, and is already very old by today's standards. 161718 The fastest VAX for Ultrix is a CVAX machine, and DECstation MIPS systems run Ultrix faster than VAXstation equivalents. 1920 Ultrix supports DSSI, and the 4000/60 does not support Ultrix. 2122 Ultrix was also rather buggy. 23 gxemul runs Ultrix quite nicely, minus the broken tape implementation. 24 The license file works for Ultrix, OSF, and True64. 25
