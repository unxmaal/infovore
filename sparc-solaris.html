SPARC Solaris

Claims: 7 Part of SPARC

SPARC Solaris is supported by CCII drivers, which also provide drivers for IRIX, AIX, VxWorks, and SCO/Solaris for various hardware. 1 Xorg can run on SPARC Solaris with certain XVR cards, but it requires additional setup and is not shipped with Solaris 11. 2 SPARC/Solaris FireWire support is poor. 3 Minecraft on SPARC-Solaris is first in line, and compiling LWJGL natives for SPARC Solaris is necessary. 45 A hack for CATIA v5.17.5 on SPARC Solaris is available but untested, though no known v5 license exists. 6 A fork has been planned for IRIX and SPARC-Solaris, but only two SPARC-Solaris users have been found. 7
