13w3 connector

Claims: 5 Part of 13W3

The NeXTStation can use a 13w3 connector, but the extra pins can be used for communication with the soundbox ASIC, power, and off/on control. 1 Most SGIs use sync both in the green component and on separate sync pins in the 13W3 connector, but not all models, such as Onyx RE2 and InfiniteReality. 2 Disassembling is required to remove the 13w3 connector from the carrier. 3
