numalink cable

Claims: 12 Part of Numalink Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Origin 3000 and Onyx 3000.

A 12CPU system requires a NUMA link cable. 1 XIO is just a standard numalink cable. 2 The c-bricks and router connect to the bricks with a 2 meter numalink cable. 3 To resolve an issue with NUMAlink not coming up, ensure the NUMAlink cable is properly seated and the nodes are reidentified with serial numbers c00 and c01. 4
