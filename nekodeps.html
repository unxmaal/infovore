nekodeps

Claims: 5

nekodeps may hang due to unresolved dependencies in description, such as rss_glx not being present. 1 To install Blender on IRIX, nekodeps is used to download and install dependencies, while the Blender from obsolete requires additional steps. 2 glib1 and gtk1 are available in the nekoware mirror. 3 Changing permissions allowed nekodeps to work. 4 Tyr-glQuake with sgug-rse requires moving or relinking GL libraries and is not very good; compiling with nekodeps on mipspro would be better. 5
