LVDS

Claims: 4

The 1600SW used the LVDS interface. 1 The stock flat panel adapter converts a 24-bit signal to 48-bit, then to LVDS, and the card has a ROM to indicate it's a flat panel adapter. 2 A DVI deserializer + LVDS serializer + fixed EDID can replace the original for dual head output on a 1600SW monitor, or a Realtek IC can scale and output dual channel LVDS from VGA or DVI input. 3
