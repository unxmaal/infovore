OpenSolaris

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see FreeBSD.

OpenSolaris uses the Solaris kernel rather than Linux, and it required replacing chunks of the OS and years of planning to open source Solaris. 12 OpenSolaris does not run on SPARC. 3 illumos/OpenIndiana and OpenSolaris are the only open source SysV distros. 4 Some software builds on OpenSolaris or Solaris 11 but not on versions 7 through 10 due to missing headers. 5 OpenSolaris versions are more popular than Sun releases, and migrating off ZFS or to Linux-ZFS causes pain due to the rebase from OpenSolaris to ZOL leaving FreeBSD in a bad situation. 67
