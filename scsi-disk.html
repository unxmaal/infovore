SCSI disk

Claims: 27 Part of SCSI Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

SCSI disks are used in a range of workstations, including the Octane, Indigo2, NeXT Cube, Pro Qube 3, and Netra t1/105, where they can replace other storage such as the MO drive in a NeXT Cube. 12345 The Octane's performance depends on the speed of the SCSI disk, and a 300GB 10k SCSI disk on an Indigo2 could be slowed by the SCSI bus speed. 13 A SCSI disk can take a few minutes to start up after showing a missing file Mac icon, and its filesystem can be failing while the system still boots. 67 Popping the SCSI disk out allowed the system to boot up fine, and if diagnostics fail, the SCSI disk connection should be checked. 89 Data can be salvaged from a SCSI disk, reformatted with mkfs_xfs, and files extracted, and the Netra t1/105 has a SCSI disk on an onboard slot. 105
