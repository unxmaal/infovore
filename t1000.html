T1000

Claims: 15

The Sun T1000 is an UltraSPARC-T1 machine that requires at least Solaris 10, which it runs along with later versions. 123 It was slow, especially for data movement, with poor memory bandwidth compared to the V210/240. 1 The T1000 remains loud even after booting into Solaris, similar to the V880's experience. 4 A known issue with SP passing incorrect minspeed values for tachometers may prevent the firmware update from changing the warning and low fan rpm thresholds from 2240 and 1920 rpm. 5 The T1000 has a problematic DC-DC converter with leaky capacitors. 6 Jumpstart can install Solaris over the network on a T1000, showing package installation details. 7
