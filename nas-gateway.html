NAS Gateway

Claims: 4 Part of NAS

The Infinite storage NAS Gateway was released in 2005 and likely had 4x1GHz CPU boards, though it is not listed with a CPU speed in the 2006 pricelist. 1 Both NAS Gateway bricks were running at SGI in München, Germany, and are not available on eBay, indicating a market failure. 2 The 1GHz CPUs in the NAS Gateway were not particularly fast compared to the competition in 2006, and the memory bricks were expensive. 3
