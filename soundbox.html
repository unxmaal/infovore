soundbox

Claims: 4

Mono systems use custom NeXT Megapixel monitors with integrated keyboard and mouse ports and a single cable for peripherals and video, whereas color systems use standard monitors and need a soundbox. 1 A soundbox and keyboard are needed for a NeXT Color slab. 12 The machine came with a mono monitor, soundbox, and a non-functional cable, and after troubleshooting a replacement cable was purchased to make it fully functional. 3 The replacement soundbox for megapixel monitors costs $250 and relies on an out-of-production chip. 4
