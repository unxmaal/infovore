power connector

Claims: 10 Part of POWER Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

A PCI cage fix involved a power connector, which supplies proper voltages, ruling out soldering issues. 12 The power connector can melt, and the logic IC on the backside of the board can cause RAM parity errors. 3 A power connector can be identified by the diode and capacitor at its end. 4 All the older USRs had some type of 'goofy' or mini-DIN type of power connector, which need two voltages. 5 The power connector on a Multia can melt and brown, making it challenging to replace. 6 A barrel jack was put in for the power connector. 7 The drive sled is for an Indigo1, and the power connector differs from an Indigo2. 8 The +12V power connector on the motherboard goes to J502 on the CPU module, and possibly also to a connector near the back of the tray for dual CPU modules. 9
