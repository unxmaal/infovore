Classic II

Claims: 6 Part of Classic

The Classic II contains a 32-bit CPU on a 16-bit board, which limits its RAM and speed. 1 It can be fitted with a 68040 accelerator from Applied Engineering, but it remains capped at 4MB of RAM. 2 The analog board caps on a Classic II can gush and cause arcing, with solder joints charred. 3 The Classic II can run Professional MachTen 2.3 on top of System 7.1, with X11R6, 4.3BSD, and Mach 2.5 in a VM on top of classic Mac OS. 4 The Asante print server can be used to bridge LocalTalk to Ethernet, but it may not support IP routing, as the Classic II lacks Ethernet and requires a separate IP solution like the Apple Internet Router. 5 The SGI Classic II uses SMD components. 6
