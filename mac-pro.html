Mac Pro

Claims: 26 Part of Mac Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Mac Pro.

The cylindrical Mac Pro has a polished reflective aluminum design with a central thermal core and a single fan, and it was on sale unchanged for a record 2,182 days before discontinuation. 1 The Mac Pro's design attempted to fit Xeon server CPUs into a small form factor, but the thermal management was not optimal. 2 The Mac Pro still uses Intel Xeon processors, not Apple Silicon. 3 Unified memory reduces the viability of multiple CPU cards in Mac Pro. 4
