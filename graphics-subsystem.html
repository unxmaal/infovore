graphics subsystem

Claims: 6 Part of graphics

Crimsons were solely sold as graphics boxes, single-CPU only, with limited VME slots compared to 4D units, essentially an R4K Indigo1 with a large graphics subsystem. 1 The I2 had a dead PSU that had been bad for a few years, and the Extreme had a bad graphics subsystem that reset itself after sitting for a while. 2 The best any system did at the time of its release, in the software of its time ran demos that did not fully load the graphics subsystem and ran at the display refresh rate. 3 The graphics sub-system includes GR1/VGR with some features not installed. 4
