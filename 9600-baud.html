9600 baud

Claims: 6

9600 baud is sufficient for text services and is very safe, while higher rates like 115200 baud are faster but more sensitive to line conditions and hardware. 1 9600 baud can vary between systems due to different clock chip implementations, leading to potential lock-on issues. 2 The Netra LOM is serial-only and starts up at 9600 baud, and 12800 baud did not work. 34 To connect to an SGI via USB, plug in the dongle, which creates a /dev entry like /dev/tty.usbserialfoo, then use `screen /dev/tty.usbserialfoo 9600` to start a session at 9600 baud and ctrl-a k to exit. 5
