2.5

Claims: 8

The Indigo2 R10K can run Maya 2.5. 1 A 2.5" drive sled is required for this machine. 2 Some powerbooks used the 2.5" SCSI version. 3 2.5" SATA drives are a viable substitute for older HP EliteBook models with the right mounting bracket, but mSATA drives can be problematic. 4 A 2.5" IDE card reader can be used as an adapter for booting from an IDE flash module. 5 The O2 has issues booting IRIX with a 2.5" IBM SCA drive; PROM and FX work fine but the kernel is unhappy. 6
