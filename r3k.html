R3K

Claims: 30

With R4000

The Indigo2 was launched during the IRIX 4.x era, and 4.x runs on R3k and R4k Indigo1 and early R4k Indigo2 boards. 1 The power supply was changed between R3k and R4k boards, but the backplane is the same; the R4K PSU has a larger zener diode than the R3K, and an R3K may have an R4K supply. 234 The EEPROM location differs between R3k and R4k boards, and switching from an R4k to an R3k clears the EEPROM to FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF. 56 The 8-pin DIP containing the MAC address and environment settings is the same for R3k and R4k, but upgrading from R3K to R4K repositions the MAC address in the EEPROM while putting an R3K board back results in invalid values. 78 The R3K Indigo uses a socketed CR2032 battery with an elastic strap and a drop-in replacement, while the R4K uses a soldered connection. 91011

General

The R3K is a MIPS CPU type distinct from the R2K, and the R3000A is a 40 MHz R3K or an R3K core packaged as an R2K, in which case hinv shows R3K / R2K. 12 The PlayStation 1's CPU is an R3K, and an Indigo with an R3K can run ancient IRIXes. 1314 The R3K uses DIMMs that are hard to find, boots with 32MB RAM, and requires a SCSI terminator to boot correctly, since using an external Apple drive causes issues with mounting ISOs or other data via the /dev/scsi path. 151617 GCC 7.2.0 has better optimizations for the R3K target than 6.3.0. 18
