PCI card

Claims: 52 Part of PCI Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Peripheral Component Interconnect, SGI Octane.

A PCI card is a general-purpose expansion card that can host a complete PC, be used in almost anything with a PCI slot (software/drivers permitting), and, if universal, work in any slot. 123 PCI cards come in various forms, including a dual-wide or single-wide card, a full-length card, and an AGP card, which is a type of PCI card. 456 PCI cards serve many specialized functions, such as providing NVMe storage for high IOPs, passing VGA input and USB keyboard/mouse output, adding PS/2 ports, providing RS232 serial ports, converting analog satellite baseband signals, and enabling KVM functionality. 789101112 Some PCI cards integrate other computing components, such as a complete PC, an x86 SBC, or an Athlon XP, and can be used to run software like Windows inside a window in Solaris or PS2 software for debugging. 11314 PCI cards are compatible with a range of SGI and other systems, including the 150, O2k, Origin 200, Onyx2, Octane, Fuel, and Teraqube, and can be used with TB drives for additional storage. 11591617 PCI cards can present various issues, such as being problematic with Bluetooth drivers, crashing an Octane2 when writing large data, not being detected on the Fuel, not being listed with hinv, and having a PCI bus that may be too slow for modern games. 1819202122 Diagnosing and resolving PCI card issues may involve reflashing firmware, running tests, disconnecting the card, installing without the card inserted, and checking under the heatsink or obtaining the model number to identify the card. 2320244
