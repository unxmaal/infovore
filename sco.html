SCO

Claims: 13 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IBM AIX.

SCO, Solaris, HP-UX, IRIX, A/UX, and SEIUX were available as UNIX-like operating systems. 1 SCO attempted to sue Linux through a proxy. 2 Microsoft paid SCO to pursue IBM AIX, but SCO lost a lawsuit against IBM regarding AIX licensing, establishing that derivative works belong to the developer. 34 The migration from SCO to Linux was driven by cost considerations, particularly the lower cost of running Linux on PC hardware compared to RISC systems. 5 SCO provided more user-friendly administration tools compared to Solaris. 6
