AGP riser

Claims: 17 Part of AGP

The AGP riser is specific to rack Onyx4 and rack Prism systems, and is very uncommon. 1 The only configurations with a single AGP riser were the rack Prism and Onyx4, both dual-pipe. 2 The Onyx4 and rackmount Prism use the same AGP riser, but the Prism uses FireGL X2 or X3 cards while the Onyx4 uses ATI Pro 9700 cards. 3 The Onyx4 has an AGP riser, allowing it to use FireGL cards instead of VPro. 4 The ZX6000 comes with an AGP riser since it is a workstation. 5 The TIOCA chip is on the AGP riser, and node boards are identical in this regard. 6 The system supports an AGP riser and a FireGL card, and may support DRI with further configuration. 7 An AGP riser is required for some older SLES media, which can be obtained from Micro Focus for free after creating an account; SLES 10 is not available, and SLES 11 has display issues with a video console. 8
