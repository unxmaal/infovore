NVIDIA driver

Claims: 9 Part of Nvidia

The default NVIDIA driver in Solaris is the Quadro R340, which may need to be manually downloaded for newer GPUs. 1 On Debian 7, the NVIDIA FX5500 PCI card requires the `nvidia` driver for optimal performance, as `nouveau` may not work or perform poorly. 2 The NVIDIA driver for Wayland has been fixed but is not fully permanent. 3
