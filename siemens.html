Siemens

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Lisp machine.

Siemens made a Lisp machine and renamed SINIX to Reliant UNIX at some point. 12 Siemens rebadged workstations and ported their own SINIX to various CPU architectures including x86, NSC32k, and MIPS. 3 Siemens sold non-Unix machines and mainframes, including old Nixdorf stuff and later SPARC64 with BS2000 mainframe emulation. 4 Siemens merged Unigraphics and I-DEAS. 5
