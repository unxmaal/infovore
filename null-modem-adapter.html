null modem adapter

Claims: 3

A gender changer is used to convert a DB25 connector to DB9, allowing a null modem adapter to connect. 1 The null modem adapter requires reversing the TX and RX wires and connecting ground, TX, and RX, and a USB serial adapter is needed to connect a computer without a serial port. 2 Using a paperclip or pin to break out a female DIN port is a temporary solution, while a null modem adapter is recommended for permanent use. 3
