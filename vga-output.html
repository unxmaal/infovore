VGA output

Claims: 10 Part of VGA

VGA output is available on several systems, including the A3000, which has nice VGA output, and the Vaio UX, which provides VGA output through a dongle. 12 The O2 has VGA output, built-in audio, and can take 1GB RAM and a fast R10000 processor, but it had VGA output issues that persisted even after replacing the mainboard and PSU. 34 The IPSC/1 has VGA output and was used to control a Xenix system for programming, alongside a TI Explorer 2 and an Iris. 5 The RX2620 has a VGA output but Runelite may fall back to software rendering due to lack of GL support. 6 For the 8X300, VGA output is difficult to use, as a PIC32MX can act as a faster VGA output peripheral. 7 On Itanium blade servers, an iLO firmware update is required for VGA output and can be complex. 8
