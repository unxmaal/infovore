IRIX 4.0.5F

Claims: 8 Part of IRIX

IRIX 4.0.5F supports R4k i2s processors up to 250 MHz and 384 MB of RAM, though 200 MHz R4400 is the maximum for this version. 1 The default install of IRIX 4.0.5F compresses to 43.7 MB. 2 IRIX 4.0.5F and 4.0.5H have limited hardware support. 3 The installer for IRIX 4.0.5F may attempt to use a non-existent partition for the user directory instead of the root partition. 4
