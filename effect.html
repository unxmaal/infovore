Effect

Claims: 9

Flame is the SGI equivalent of After Effects, and Effect is a similar but lower-end tool. 1 Effect is a special version of Flame for the O2, functioning as a compositing and editing tool. 2 The O2 ran Flint and Effect, with the FlintO2 line later renamed to Effect and then Spark. 34 Effect runs well on the O2, behaving essentially like Flint. 3 The dlwacom driver ships with Flame/Smoke/Effect and can conflict with the Wacom driver by locking the serial port, requiring editing of init.cfg to use. 5 The 1600sw and flatpanel adapter can sometimes have issues with certain versions of Effect not finding the 1600x1024 resolution. 6
