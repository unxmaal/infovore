VAXstation 3100

Claims: 6 Part of VAXstation

The VAXstation 3100 can be found for an okay price on eBay, but it is slow and lacks a regular serial port, making peripherals difficult to obtain. 12 Modifications to BlueSCSI allowed Ultrix to run on the VAXstation 3100, and SCSI/SD adapters exist for the machine. 23 The VAXstation 3100 has 4MB of onboard RAM and 1KB of onboard CPU cache, both of which can fail in rare cases, requiring a replacement mainboard. 4 The VAXstation 3100 and similar models were very reliable. 5
