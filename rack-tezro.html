rack Tezro

Claims: 5 Part of rack

The Rack Tezro is an Onyx350 with factory-crippled firmware, limited to 4 processors and no numalink. 1 The rack Tezro is firmware-locked to support only one CPU nodeboard, and like the desktop Tezro, it has no NUMAlink ports. 12 Some rack Tezro models appear to be missing the blower on the PSU side. 3
