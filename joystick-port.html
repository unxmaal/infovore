joystick port

Claims: 5 Part of joystick

The Mac video connector is DA15, and the old PC joystick port is also DA15, whereas DE15 is VGA. 1 The Amiga could support 2 axes and 5 buttons per joystick port but was not used to its full potential due to a preference for Atari 2600 joysticks. 2 Model 70 works with the joystick port, but Model 95 has configuration disk conflicts. 3 Linux does not have built-in support for a joystick port. 4
