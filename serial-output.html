serial output

Claims: 3 Part of serial

Serial output can be monitored by turning on all the POST shinies, setting output-device to ttya, and watching during booting. 1 Serial output issues may be hardware related, whereas video output may require the system to be in the OS. 2 For example, an Octane's front plane failure prevents boot and serial output, leaving only fan operation upon power-on, even with a good PSU. 3
