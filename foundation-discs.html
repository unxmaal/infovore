foundation discs

Claims: 4 Part of Foundation

The 5.3 version of IRIX requires specific foundation discs. 1 Foundation discs are from the original 6.5 release, and all versions of 6.5 after that have overlay discs that patch the files on the foundation discs. 2 When installing IRIX 6.5.22, conflicts can arise with specific overlay and foundation discs, and starting over with a simpler list of discs can resolve these issues. 3 vfc is not installed by default on SGI machines; it can be found on foundation discs but is not included by default. 4
