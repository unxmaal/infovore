US-IV+

Claims: 4

The US-IV+ and US-IIICu were pin-compatible but not socket-compatible, and the US-IIICu could be taken from a CPU board of an SF15k machine. 1 The Blade2K could use US-IV+ CPUs, which are LGA, not BGA, but they were not upgradeable. 23 Dual US-IV+ CPUs in a Blade2K would have been great. 4
