PLX

Claims: 4

A few PLX and Toshiba optical drives have 68-pin connectors. 1 If the Mace bridge does not work, a different PCI-PCI bridge like Pericom or PLX could be used as a backup. 2 The card cage uses a PLX or PERICOM PCI-PCIe bridge, with a SEDNA adapter recommended for booting IRIX with an NVMe SSD. 3 The PLX and Pericom PCI to PCI-E adapters are similar but the Pericom needs a 3.3V power supply; a 3.3V regulator can be added to make it work on a 5V PCI motherboard. 4
