Octane VPro

Claims: 6 Part of Octane

The Octane VPro uses heat pipes and thermal pads rather than thermal paste. 12 It is not designed for easy video ingestion. 3 The NEC 2070VX monitor has DVI output, which works without issues on Octane VPro with DCD and Fuel/Tezro. 4 A VFO with a 1600sw and Octane VPro may have issues with artifacting. 5 When installing OCTANE DEMOS on an OCTANE VPRO, you can try inst -m GFXBOARD=MARDIGRAS, and there may be an equivalent for IMPACT->VPRO. 6
