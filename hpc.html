HPC

Claims: 7

Besides HPC, the only reason to have a single large machine is to run legacy software that requires a single system image, such as databases and business operations management. 1 The SPARC T3 has a high hardware thread count for throughput, comparable to a 16-core Xeon for HPC, designed for serving many users and virtualization with a built-in hypervisor, excels in UNIX tasks and can handle ZFS. 2 The second generation HPC added little-endian support. 3 T series servers were not suitable for HPC or complex databases, and customers found them limiting. 4 The Raspberry Pi is more performant than the Octane and thus better for HPC. 5 Sgi only really had an issue because they were in HPC. 6
