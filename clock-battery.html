clock battery

Claims: 14

The clock battery is a Dallas NVRAM that cannot explode, and in Indigos it is a Tadiran lithium thionyl chloride primary cell that is long-life and leak-resistant but can cause a corrosive mess if it leaks. 12 The clock battery in the Indigo is soldered, whereas R4k Indigos have a socket for the clock battery. 34 A dead clock battery can cause problems on various systems: M88k systems brick and are restored by replacing the battery, the display can indicate a dead clock battery, the 'TOOL' light may not be lit and the system may not boot past the BIOS screen, and the 'Can't set tod clock' error can be cleared by replacing the battery. 5678 The Indigo1 has clock batteries that can leak into the board, and it also has power supply issues. 9 A working clock battery is crucial for an old Sun SS10, and to boot the IDE on a disk on a pre-ARCS machine with a missing clock battery, use `boot -f dksc(0,3,8)ide.IP6 fe. 1011 To blast the password, either use a jumper or remove the clock battery. 12
