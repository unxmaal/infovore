Q610

Claims: 4

The Q610 is a Unix pizza box made by Apple, and the Centris 610 is a different model. 1 A/UX can run on Q610s with LC040 motherboards. 2 The Q610 runs acceptably fast but not great, while the Q950 provides a very good experience, especially with a 10k spinning disk. 3 The Q610 has a SCSI2SD, while the Q950 has an 18GB SCSI disk. 4
