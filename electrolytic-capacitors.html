electrolytic capacitors

Claims: 14

Failures can cause various problems, including power supply failures, display issues, and trace damage. 123 Signs of failure include bulging, corrosion, and the cap body falling off, and the caps often have red plastic on one end. 45 Failures can be caused by cooling issues, such as those affecting SS10/SS20 units, and capacitors under the heatsink are specific to the 'tane model. 67 They can be replaced with more heat-resistant types like poly caps or organic compound SMD caps, and Octanes have been found to have reliable electrolytic capacitors. 89 Removal involves using needle-nose pliers and soldering wick for the legs, with hot air being better for larger components but not necessary for leaky electrolytic capacitors. 1011
