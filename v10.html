V10

Claims: 85 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane, V10 engine.

With V12

The Octane2 can handle dual-head and DCD options with both V10 and V12, supporting up to four displays, and both cards require Xbox rev. 1.4 (Xbow 1.4). 123 V10 and V12 share the same ASIC and the same cutouts on the back, but V12 has twice as fast geometry processing as V10, though the practical impact is uncertain. 456 V10 and V12 use different RAM chip form factors, with V12 chips being flatter and smaller than the taller, larger V10 packages, and V12 cards often having more RAM but sometimes limited to 32MB. 78910 V12 was the only model sold with the DVI-I output connector, but V6/V10 can use it for DVI-I output. 11 The V10 was the base card in the Fuel and Tezro systems, and both V10 and V12 boards worked in a FUEL machine. 1213 V10 and V12 are not as slow as the V5 5500 in Quake III Arena, and not as fast as a GeForce 256. 14

With V6

The V6 and V10 processors are virtually identical, with the V10 having a process shrink and a pixel clock bug fix that is easy to work around, and the V10 requiring Xbow 1.4 while the V6 can use Xbow 1.3. 1516 The V6 and V10 are limited to a 32 MB framebuffer with 32 MB of VRAM but only 8 MB available for TRAM, and they cannot support 24- or 32-bit colors. 171819 The V10 has no space to add RAM chips, unlike the V6, and both have limited texture memory which affects performance in Quake3. 2021 The V6 and V10 have twice the geometry engine performance of the V6 and V8, and the V10 is preferred for VRAM needs. 2223

General

The V10 is a VPro graphics card that plugs onto connectors at the back of the baseboard, sticking out where the central exhaust fan normally goes, and was fabricated on a smaller process with a slightly higher clock and different RAM type than the older V6 and V8 cards, effectively doubling their clock speed. 242526 It has 16 RAM chips totaling 32 MiB (32 Mb of SDRAM), and its PCB is very thick with a Buzz revision 2B board. 272829 The V10 can handle resolutions up to 1920x1080, and can technically support 1920x1200 and 1600x1200, but with limited texture RAM these higher resolutions are impractical and cause analog artifacting and visible lines. 3031323334 The V10's video output has issues such as visible lines and blurry text, and it can use an HDMI mod due to poor composite or s-video outputs. 3536 The V10 runs out of RAM at higher resolutions and color depths, making it unsuitable for rendering at those settings, and electropaint runs slowly on it compared to an Octane. 3738 The V10 can fail due to issues with CPOP (compression connector) connectors, which can shift and cause link errors, and replacing the CPOP connector fixed the issue. 3940 The V10 can run Odyssey diagnostics, and software for it is scarce. 2841 The V10 is a graphics processing unit with multiple sets of geometry engines, bit planes, texture mapping, and raster generation. 42 The V10 has a DVI output. 43 The V10 can use a MultiLink for video output, but this may cause issues with artifacts. 44 The V10 had issues with analog video from VPro and MultiLink, showing jailbars and artifacts, and the quality of caps in MultiLink and V10 can affect video output. 4546 The mainboard has limited space for the Vpro and blower, especially with a V10 or Tez Vpro housing installed. 47
