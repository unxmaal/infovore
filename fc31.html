fc31

Claims: 6

The RSE approach uses RPM for package management, gaining the entire source tree of fc31 and allowing for building relevant software packages for IRIX. 1 RSE is based on FC31 and adopted RPM after extensive consideration and proof of concept with pkgsrc. 2 fc31 targets systemd, not sysv init. 3 The ncurses version in fc31 is broken due to a modified version, so users can build from the official GNU 6.1 tarball. 4 pre-gtk4 from fc31 runs normally on a RM Tezro 4x800 MHz. 5 To build fc31, install the srpm from the fc31 upstream repo, then copy the spec and source/patches into your ~/rpmbuild dir. 6
