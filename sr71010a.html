SR71010A

Claims: 5

The RM7000 and RM5271 are 99.7% pin compatible, differing only in one cache-related pin and the extra DNC pins on the SR71010A. 1 The SR71010A has different pin assignments for the 'DNC' pins compared to the RM7000A, which are related to tertiary cache, and using the SR71010A in an RM7000 system requires disabling the tag RAM and removing the tertiary cache. 2 Momentum Ocelot boards can use RM7900 and SR71010A chips. 3 RM7900 and SR71010A chips are rare. 4 The SR71010A boots to system PROM but reports incorrect cache sizes and fails to boot into Irix. 5
