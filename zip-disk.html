Zip disk

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Macintosh SE/30.

The Zip disk can be used as a hard drive, especially the SCSI ones, and can be cleaned to fix read/write errors. 1 The N64 devkit software was found on a zip disk, but it was just Windows-related material. 2 The SE/30 can use a 2GB drive and can boot from a 7.5 installer on a zip disk after installing 7.1 to it. 3 PCDOS 7 can be installed from a DOS boot disk with a Zip drive GUEST.EXE driver and a Zip disk with the entire PCDOS 7 CD copied to it, as the installer doesn't care if it's not a CD. 4
