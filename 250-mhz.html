250 MHz

Claims: 5

The 250 MHz O2 runs faster than the 195 MHz O2 despite both having R10000 CPUs. 1 The O2 also had issues connecting to an NFS server that was not running. 1 The Indigo2 R4400 250MHz XZ was used for rendering in the first few seasons of ReBoot. 2 250 MHz R4400 processors seem rare these days; one came with an impact midplane but not the PSU. 3 Deep Runner runs fast on an O2 with a 250 MHz R10000 CPU but seems unfinished with limited completion on time; controls feel like Descent on an Octane. 4 The O2 with R10000 CPU at 250 MHz has 256 MB of RAM. 5
