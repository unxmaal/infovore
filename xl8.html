XL8

Claims: 7 Part of XL

The -gio64_gfx option in MAME specifies the video board using the options xl8 and xl24, and using -gio_gfx xl8 can fix issues with off-screen rendering. 12 The XL8 and XL24 are single-height boards, while the XZ is double-height, and the R5000 Indy is best with a single-height XL24 board. 3 XL24 has an ICS1562M clock regeneration chip that XL8 lacks. 4 An XL8 or 4000PC Indy would be faster than an Aesthedes. 5 The r4600-133 CPU, PSU, and XL8 are non-functional, with the R5000 PROM being the only usable component. 6
