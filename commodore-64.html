Commodore 64

Claims: 7 Part of Commodore

The Commodore 64 is a boxed computer that was commonly sold with the 1541 disk drive. 1 The Commodore 64 never changed its specifications, and all units are essentially the same. 2 MOS chips used in the Commodore 64 had poor yield, especially for that machine. 3 The Commodore 64 had paint applications such as Doodle and Advanced Art Studio for pixel art. 4 BBS boards on a Commodore 64 allowed users to log in, download messages, write replies, log off, and then log back in to send the replies. 5 VICE can run a full-speed Commodore 64 with the 'old' x64 emulator. 6
