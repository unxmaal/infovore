VW320/540

Claims: 6 Part of VW

The SGI VW 320 / 540 uses ARCS, a boot loader compatible with SGI MIPS machines. 1 VW 320/540 memory is particularly difficult to install due to its design. 2 Windows 2000 Exkernel works with the VW 320 / 540, with everything but the userland being typical SGI stuff. 3 A quirk of SGI's graphics implementation on the VW320/540 is that the yellow ! will always be there even when the driver is working. 4 SGI made WRL exploration demos for the O2, Octane, and VW320/540 systems, but the O2 is not default installed and is on the demo CD; the Octane and VW320/540 have their own WRL demo CDs. 5
