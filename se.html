SE

Claims: 39

SE and LVD use the same voltage levels but differ in signaling method, with LVD maintaining better signal integrity at high speeds. 1 SE and LVD SCSI disks are incompatible on the same bus, and mixing SE and LVD drives on the same bus can cause problems. 23 Devices can autosense and use both SE and LVD, but connecting LVD/SE devices to an HVD bus without adapters can cause issues, and every device on an HVD bus must be an HVD device. 4 Most U160 and U320 cards support LVD or SE, but typically use SE for Fast/Wide modes and below. 5 The LVD device must support downgrading to SE, which this SSD does not. 6 The SE and LVD versions of the Red Rock disk are separate SKUs, with the LVD version having differential transceivers. 7
