PATA

Claims: 4 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see PCI-X, SATA.

PATA (parallel ATA or IDE) is a storage interface that is slower than a SATA1 link, as a project using a pATA interface with a SATA-to-PATA chip integrates this slower interface. 1 PCI-X is 64-bit and faster than 32-bit PCI, while PCIe is electrically different but can be bridged by cheap adapter cards, similar to how SATA relates to PATA. 2 SATA and PATA controllers may be fully removed from Linux, which would affect HDD/SSD and CD support. 3 A U160 SCSI bus exceeds PATA's 133MB/s, making a U320 Acard limited to 133MB/s on a U160 bus unnecessary. 4
