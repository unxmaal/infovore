SATA controller

Claims: 5 Part of SATA

The Altix is not recognizing the DVD drive or the SATA controller, likely due to an unknown model of controller on the IO10. 1 A SATA controller can be placed on the 32-bit 66 MHz PCI bus, while a graphics card can use the 64-bit 33 MHz PCI bus, allowing the SATA controller to potentially reach SATA I speeds without bus limitations. 2 To boot IRIX from an SSD attached to a SATA controller, an alternative is to load the Unix kernel from a CD-ROM instead of a TFTP server. 3
