keyboard

Claims: 126 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Computer keyboard, IMac G3, KVM switch, USB.

With mouse

Some systems come bundled with a keyboard and mouse, such as the iMac G3, the Indigo R4400 Elan, and the Aspire retro PC, which had a matching set made by Acer. 123 A keyboard and mouse can work with vintage systems, and serial-based DEC peripherals use +/- 5v for the keyboard and +/- 12v for the mouse. 45 Some systems support USB peripherals, with IRIX supporting mouse, keyboard, and possibly audio devices. 67 The Onyx 2 can run a 4-player Quake match with a separate keyboard, mouse, and monitor for each player. 8 A halfway decent mouse and keyboard are sufficient for most users. 9 People often forget to plug in essential peripherals like a mouse and keyboard, and are unaware of how to connect a monitor. 10 No boot chime can indicate that no keyboard or mouse is attached, the Dallas battery is dead, or the monitor can't handle sync on green. 11 The keyboard and mouse stop working on boot with the converter, though they work in PROM. 12

General

SGI keyboards are considered good for old machines, with one user's SGI keyboard being better than any other they have. 1314 The keyboard from a particular eBay link is a 9500 801 model designed for the Indigo R4000. 15 The Indigo can use keyboards with optical mice or those that shipped with the Indigo, and Indigo1 was the last machine to use a proprietary keyboard. 1617 Plugging a damaged keyboard into an Onyx can cause component failures, and the keyboard's thickness prevents feeling CPU heat. 1819 The SGI keyboard is quiet, and there is a wiring diagram for the SGI keyboard available online. 2021 The Dell XPS 9560 laptop has a bad keyboard that flexes and requires key replays when dusty. 22 Keyboards come in various forms and connectors, including granite or charcoal models for the O2, Japanese USB keyboards that support both USB and PS/2, and IRIX keyboards with a DB port. 232425 Keyboard pinouts are documented in the IRIX manpage for keyboard.7, which also provides the pinout for the mouse connector including KTXD, MTXD, GND, +8Vdc, KRCD, and NC. 2627 Several systems require a keyboard to boot or initialize graphics, including the O2, Indigo2, and PowerMac G4 AGP, while Indigo systems are noted to lack working keyboards. 28293031 Some keyboards are compatible across systems, such as the Indigo keyboard requiring an adapter for the Indy, while others have specific requirements, such as the Sun U60 needing a Sun keyboard for graphics. 3233 Keyboards can suffer from various failures, including the zbook's two-stage failure where metal tabs bend and keys pop up and crack, and the Sparcbook's damaged mylar. 3435 The iBook G4 has an excellent and replaceable keyboard, with new replacements available cheaply on eBay. 3637
