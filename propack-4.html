Propack 4

Claims: 10 Part of ProPack

Propack 4 is a version of SLES, and it can only be installed on SLES9. 12 SLES 9 with ProPack 4 is considered the best option for an Itanium box, including for systems with dual AGP weirdness that breaks typical drivers. 34 The last version of Linux and Propack that can be installed on a Prism is SLES9 SP4 and Propack4 SP4, and ProPack 4 brings gfx support to the Prism. 56 ProPack 4 does not work with SLES11, but SLES11 can work with it, though it lacks graphics. 7 The installation media for SLES9 Ia64 can be found at https://archive.org/details/SLES9Ia64RC5CD1, which is Propack 4, not 3 as mentioned in the guide, but the SLES version is the same. 8 Performer 3.2.2 for Windows and 3.2.4 for IA64 Linux are available in Propack 4, and Flexlm in Propack 4 is a direct port from IRIX. 910
