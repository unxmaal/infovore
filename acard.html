ACARD

Claims: 14

ACARD is the name brand for a common SCSI to IDE converter that made IDE and SATA adapters, with high demand from commercial users leading to low used supply. 12 ACARD's IDE and SATA adapters use custom ACARD silicon, including RISC-based ASICs for IDE/PATA to SCSI conversion, with some models using separate chips for SATA to PATA conversion. 34 ACARD used custom silicon to handle high data throughput in SCSI CD-ROM adapters, restricting lower-tier models to prevent higher-end use, and had multiple SCSI adapters with the same chipset but different firmware limitations. 56 Acard made SCA to SATA adapters and a SATA converter that converts from 68-pin, but it was expensive, costing around $400. 789 The O2 can use an old ACARD for booting, and one in the O2 had the `force single-ended` jumper set. 1011
