RiscPC

Claims: 7

The RiscPC can use a 233 MHz StrongArm processor and onboard graphics, or a 233 MHz StrongArm with a 133 MHz 5x86 PC Card. 12 A RiscPC with a 486 is hamstrung by slow memory and hardware but can handle early 486 software and Win95 for office work and Civ2. 3 The RiscPC can resource share its video and sound with other systems and runs an IBM 5x86 CPU at 125 MHz. 4 The SunPCI allows running Win98 on a RiscPC, but it requires significant setup. 5 A Logitech mouse can be repinned to work with the RiscPC's quadrature mouse port. 6
