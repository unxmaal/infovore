mouse

Claims: 41 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Computer keyboard, IMac G3.

With keyboard

Some systems come bundled with a keyboard and mouse, such as the iMac G3, certain G4 towers, the Indigo1, the Indigo R4400 Elan, and the Aspire retro PC, which had a matching set. 1234 A keyboard and mouse can work with vintage systems, and NOS keyboard and mouse units are available. 56 Some systems use serial connections, with the DEC keyboard using +/- 5v and the mouse using +/- 12v. 7 USB supports mouse, keyboard, and audio on Irix, along with basic USB devices such as a mouse and keyboard. 89 The Onyx 2 can run a 4-player Quake match with a separate keyboard, mouse, and monitor for each player. 10 A halfway decent mouse and keyboard are sufficient for most users. 11 People often forget to plug in essential peripherals like a mouse and keyboard, and are unaware of how to connect a monitor. 12 No boot chime can indicate that no keyboard or mouse is attached, the Dallas battery is dead, or the monitor can't handle sync on green. 13

General

On pre-PS/2 SGI machines, the mouse uses the same keyboard port and plugs directly into the machine. 14 The SGI-protocol keyboard and mouse work well, but mis-wiring can damage components. 15 The middle button of the mouse on an Indigo² acts like a second right button. 16 Center-click-paste is an X feature available on most Unix systems using X. 17 Without a mouse, you cannot click 'Continue' or 'Enter command monitor' buttons in the graphics interface, requiring serial access. 18 A PS/2 compatible USB optical mouse registers as a mouse on anything that can take one. 1920
