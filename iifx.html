IIfx

Claims: 49 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Macintosh IIfx.

The IIfx is the fastest Macintosh II and the 3rd fastest 68k Mac, faster than the IIcx and some Quadras and Mac IIs. 1234 It has a 50MHz CPU running SDR, 32kb SRAM as cache, accelerated IO, and processors for sound among other tasks. 456 It can have 68MB RAM, though IIfx RAM is difficult to obtain. 78 It is highly modular, allowing replacement of video cards and other components, and can have multiple video and Ethernet cards. 910 It can run A/UX natively with a full 68040 CPU, as well as a classic Mac OS version of Transport Tycoon. 111213 It is on the expensive side for Macs but not ever super valuable, and is cheaper than an SGI. 1415 It can suffer from issues like missing PROM batteries, wrong monitor, adapter settings, or a bad video card, leading to no video output. 16 The IIfx uses replacement RAM chips and can support 16 MB SIMMs, which were not natively offered by Apple. 1718 An IIfx requires both batteries to function properly. 19 The IIfx runs well with proper era software and OS, performing very well. 20 The Spigot card is very picky about the OS and QuickTime version on the IIfx; it crashes if requirements aren't met. 21
