graphics board

Claims: 19 Part of graphics Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Read-only memory, SGI Octane.

The Indigo2 uses a 13w3 adapter for graphics, and issues persist despite reseating the graphics board; the adapter might be the problem. 1 The SGI Fuel can boot without a graphics board, and the board above the 13w3 connector is not part of the graphics; that board is the I2 VINO option, allowing for an Indycam or other connections via breakout cables. 2 The ROM is on the graphics board, under the cover. 3 Ian's guide to upgrade a graphics board without reinstalling IRIX works. 4 The graphics board connects to the IP6 using a longer-than-50-pin ribbon cable. 5 A known good graphics board is used for the Octane. 6 Freezing during the power on diagnostics test indicates a bus error when writing to the graphics board, which could be due to a PCB issue. 7 The O2 uses the same graphics board. 8
