GIO

Claims: 8

The Indy used an SIO card for its bus, known as GIO, and the SGI GIO documentation lists the connector names. 12 FDDI adapters were available for SBus, Microchannel, and GIO buses, and FDDI is an alternative to Ethernet that requires GIO and EISA FDDI cards, which are hard to find. 34 Configuring both GIO and built-in Ethernet interfaces in Irix 6.5.22 can lead to IP and hostname conflicts, but setting up distinct addresses and hostnames for each interface is possible. 5 Finding DMA backpressure details in MC, GIO, and REX3 documentation was difficult. 6 QEMU GIO-based systems are promising for future SGI emulation. 7
