8GB RAM

Claims: 5 Part of 8GB Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see POWER9.

The Pixel Slate is equipped with an i5-8200Y processor, 8GB RAM, and 128GB storage. 1 A power9 system is 4-core with 8GB RAM. 2 Dual-headed MXE/SE configurations with dual 600 MHz processors and 8 GB RAM were possible on VPro carriers with modifications, though VBOB is not used in dual-head configurations for VPro systems and dual-head Octane setups are unsupported. 3 The T3400 supports 8GB RAM along with PCI, PCIe, USB, parallel, serial, floppy, IDE, and SATA with no driver issues on Windows or Unix. 4 University surplus sells older i5 machines with 8GB RAM for affordable prices. 5
