R4000 CPU

Claims: 14 Part of R4000 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo.

The R4000 CPU appears in several SGI systems, including the Indigo, the Indy (at 100 MHz), and the Indigo2. 123 An early Indigo2 with a 100 MHz R4000 CPU cannot be upgraded to a faster CPU like a 200 or 250 MHz R4400 without a PROM upgrade. 4 The EEPROM configures R4000 CPU settings such as endianness and cache timing. 5 The R4000 CPU may cause bus errors with too much RAM, and an Indigo2 may lock up due to a faulty power supply, a problematic R4000 CPU, or general aging hardware. 36 The R4000 CPU is also used in NCD terminals, such as the HMX model, and in an Indigo2-based client. 78 In emulation, the R4000 CPU is a pure interpreter that serves as a major performance bottleneck, as seen with SoftPC from 1992 running at ~20 MHz. 910
