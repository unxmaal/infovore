Apple III

Claims: 10 Part of Apple

The Apple III was designed to be backward compatible with Apple II software, but with limited capabilities in Apple II Mode. 1 The Apple III is limited in software selection and is not very good as an Apple II, with Apple having crippled the Apple III in IIe emulation mode. 23 The Apple III had heat issues with 12V RAM models, but later models were better and the III+ was fine. 4 The Apple III model 380 is SCSI. 5
