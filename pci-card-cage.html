PCI card cage

Claims: 6 Part of PCI card Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The PCI card cage for the shoebox had at least seven revisions. 1 A PCI card cage with a compatible gigabit NIC may not initialize the card properly, indicating a potential driver issue. 2 An AGP riser and a pair of FireGL X3 cards are more expensive than a PCI card cage or an Indigo2. 3 The Onyx2 is a desk-side system that can hold up to 4 nodes for 8 processors, though it loses XIO slots, and it can still have a PCI card cage. 4 The Octane2 has a PCI card cage worth around $215 and potentially more. 5
