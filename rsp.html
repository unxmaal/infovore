RSP

Claims: 12

The RSP is a MIPS 4300 processor, specifically a MIPS III variant with 8-lane 16-bit vector capabilities as a coprocessor, a design similar in concept to the CRM graphics of the O2. 123 The RSP and ICE are 95% identical, with the main differences being that ICE includes 8-bit and 16-bit versions of instructions, has larger instruction memory, and access to all of RAM. 4 The N64 GPU consists of the RSP and RDP chips, using custom display list calls, with the RSP using RSP-encoded data for display lists (DL) flushed to the RSP every frame as a GFX task. 56 The RSP is partly responsible for sound on the N64, and the O2 architecture is more optimal with separate chips. 7 The RCP likely does not have debug features, but the RSP has some debug bits in the Status Register $c4. 8 Some N64 documents, like RSP and RDP manuals, are publicly available for hardware understanding, and ion64 can now DMA in and out of RSP and control the RSP/MSP. 910
