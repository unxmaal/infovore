GNOME

Claims: 19 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris.

GNOME is a desktop environment that has been in use for a long time and is considered more consistent than Windows UIs. 1 GNOME, KDE, XFCE, SWAY, and i3 are all considered productive desktop environments. 2 GNOME is used full-time on Linux workstations and works well on laptops. 34 The GNOME environment includes a background tool for versions 9, 10, and 11. 5 The new GNOME (post-3.x) is moving away from the single-headed touchscreen device paradigm. 6 GNOME has been ported to and maintained on various platforms, including Solaris, Solaris 9, NetBSD (Sun 4/280 under the SPARC port), and SGI IRIX. 78910 The Pinephone runs a phone version of GNOME and supports KDE, with Phosh as a current option. 11 GNOME automounts USBs, and Orca, a GNU/Linux screen reader, depends on GNOME libraries. 1213
