IP7 board

Claims: 5 Part of IP7

The IP7 board is a composite NTSC generator, and its cable provides black-burst sync. 1 The 4D/380 VGX can drop into a 'PON>' prompt due to issues with the IP7 board, particularly the boot master CPU. 2 The IO3 and IP7 PROMs are EPROMs that must match the CPU PROM revisions, and firmware versions can affect booting. 2 CPU tests on an IP7 board take 50 minutes with minimal RAM and no disk test, and test time increases exponentially with more RAM and CPUs. 3
