Amiga 4000

Claims: 7 Part of Amiga

The Amiga 4000 can run the original Quake at about 15 frames per second at 320 x 240 resolution without sound, but this requires 32 megabytes of RAM and performance degrades when sound is enabled. 1 Lightwave for the Amiga 4000 was a render box with no user interface and required an Amiga 4000 for MIPS executables, while Toaster 4000 used four MIPS R4400 series processors to render 3-D animation in seconds. 23 The Amiga 3000 and 4000 have a CPU card with a right-angle connector that is parallel to the motherboard. 4 The Amiga 4000 runs WinUAE faster and more compatibly than the original hardware, and the Amiga 2000, 4000, and possibly the 3000 are considered the only Amigas worth having. 56 Capacitors on the Amiga 4000 were found in rough shape, prompting concerns about aging capacitors and a search for related experience. 7
