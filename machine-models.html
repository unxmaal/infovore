machine models

Claims: 88

With parts

The machine has no full-size Ethernet jack; instead, a docking port on the left side can be used for Ethernet, or a full dock can be purchased. 1 Two screws go into the side that opens, and one screw goes into the topmost PCI slot bracket. 2 The Tezro/Origin 350 node boards use electrolytic cans near the voltage regulators, which some consider swapping for aluminum-polymer caps as a preventative measure. 3 SGI used cost-cutting measures in their hardware, such as using Kapton tape to adhere heatsinks to RDRAM on IMPACT texture. 4

General

The Sun Blade 100 can use USB keyboards through its USB ports. 5 A 32 or 64 core Epyc processor is now available. 6 Dual CPUs provide more performance than a single CPU. 7 You can boot into single user mode from the PROM, but it will ask for a root password. 8 The SS5 has a sticker indicating the CPU speed, which is not present on the SS20. 9 The UV1000 runs SUSE Linux and lists CPUs with /proc/cpuinfo. 10 You can buy 486s still. 11 Xdm runs on the Origin to provide XDMCP, even without graphics hardware installed. 12 The Blade 1500 is similar to a Fuel but with a RM7000 CPU. 13 The IIci offers integrated video, a slight performance boost to 25 MHz, and a PDS slot. 14 The Indigo2 with ZuluSCSI RP2040 and SCSI2SD V6 feels fast enough, while the same machine with SCSI2SD v6 feels slower than a spinny disk. 1516 OS/2 3.5 with the 3500 kernel can install smoothly on a PS/2 Mod 70 with a McIDE adapter and CF card but may hang after rebooting with a blinking cursor. 17 The Sun 3/50 uses radial electrolytic capacitors, some of which may be faulty affecting board functionality. 18 550 MHz processors and VXI 073-20770-01 voltage regulators work with a VW 540, but 700 MHz and 900 MHz processors do not boot with the same regulators. 19 The Indy, Indigo2, O2 and Octane are considered middle SGI machines, while those with 'Iris' in the name are early SGI machines. 20 The PS2 can run Linux but may freeze under load with certain kernels. 21
