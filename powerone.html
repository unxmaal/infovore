PowerOne

Claims: 6

PowerOne was one of the power supply OEMs used for SGI systems, appearing in single tower PowerSeries machines alongside OEMs such as LH Research, ACDC, and Cherokee. 1 The PowerOne can fail by shutting down, whereas the Cherokee in the Crimson can burn a PCB and is harder to repair. 2 The Cherokee PSU is a drop-in replacement for the PowerOne, with a modular design and documented Molex wiring, though the bus bars differ. 3 Refurbished PowerOne SMP5 series PSUs were used to repair failed 4D PSUs, but the original backplanes were eventually reinstalled after testing. 4 The Power-One replacement changed the reported GPU version from gr1.5 to gr1.2. 5 Two PI4D PSUs are dead and the user is unsure whether to replace caps or get a meanwell or PowerOne replacement. 6
