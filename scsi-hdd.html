SCSI HDD

Claims: 5 Part of SCSI

The Q950 can run A/UX and has a key just to turn it on; its SCSI HDD self-terminates if the jumper is not set correctly. 1 Backing up a SCSI HDD using `tar -cvf /dev/sd(whatever) /files-to-be-backed-up` creates a large tarchive without partitions or magic, though some systems may prevent this. 2 External SCSI CD-ROM and external HDD enclosures are nice to have but not necessary. 3 A 24-bit XL GPU with a SCSI HDD is available for purchase. 4
