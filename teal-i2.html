Teal I2

Claims: 5

The Teal I2 is a computer that differs from the standard I2 in its slot configuration, having 3 GIO64 slots and 4 EISA slots, whereas the standard I2 has 4 GIO64 slots and 3 EISA slots, with the opposite arrangement of GIO64 and EISA buses. 1 The Teal I2 can run IRIX 4 and can be configured with up to 384MB of RAM using 8x32MB modules. 23 The Teal I2 was user-9a53's development machine. 4
