Blade 100/150

Claims: 12

The Blade 100/150 workstations are affordable, making them a good starting point for Solaris, and they are compatible with Solaris 8 or newer. 123 They are less beefy than the Blade 1500/2500 series. 4 They can use the same unbuffered PC100/133 ECC RAM as the PC300GL/Intellistation E Pro. 5 The Blade 100/150 has a PCB dividing the drive bay area from the PCI card/mobo area, requiring tricky cable routing. 6 It has a newer, better IDE controller than older models, though only marginally better than the U5/10. 78 The onboard Mach64 is not disabled when another card is installed, unlike on the U5/U10. 9 The DOSDUDE IDE SSD is better than the Kingspec one for the Blade 100/150. 10
