IRIX XFS

Claims: 5 Part of IRIX Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see XFS.

XFS support in Linux may not be fully compatible with IRIX XFS, and direct xfsrestore may work. 1 IRIX XFS requires specific handling in Linux due to its age and lack of support, and multi-partition .img files can be problematic. 2 IRIX XFS can be mounted on Linux, but not all distributions carry it by default and may require different flags. 3 Support for IRIX XFS in Linux kernel 3.16 is unreliable and accidental, and support for IRIX XFS in Linux is not to be relied upon. 45
