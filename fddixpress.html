FDDIXPress

Claims: 3

FDDIXpress was a separate disc for IRIX 4 and was included in OS discs since IRIX 5. 1 The FDDIXPress package includes utilities like smtconfig, smtstat, and smtinfo that interact with the session manager. 2 The FDDI board requires installing FDDIXPress software, rebuilding the kernel, and rebooting; it appears in 'hinv' and can be configured in /etc/config/netif.options. 3
