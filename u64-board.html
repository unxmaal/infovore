U64 board

Claims: 5

The U64 board is a Nintendo 64 development board that lacks security circuitry and includes RAM-ROM, connecting to the Indy's VINO input to display in a window. 12 It uses ASICs, including split RDRAM FPGAs, without labeled pinouts, making it difficult to replicate its functionality. 3 Only a couple of prototype ROMs would run on the board, lacking a CIC. 4
