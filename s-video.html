S-Video

Claims: 17

S-Video carries separate luma and chroma signals and is not RGB, though it was acceptable for low-end professional video work when the system was new. 123 Home consoles usually did not provide anything better than S-Video. 4 The N64 outputs only S-Video and composite, so a passive adapter is needed for component input, while the Atari 800 outputs S-Video rather than RGB. 56 S-Video is considered 'DVD' quality and easy to connect to anything. 7 The O2 has composite and S-Video inputs, accepts video input over S-Video and pushes it out over composite, and can handle different video inputs such as S-Video, composite, and potentially O2 cam, though not all configurations are tested. 89 The O2 accepts video input over S-Video and pushes it out over composite, and the Satscan board has a separate power supply connected to the O2's AV board via S-Video. 1011 The AV1 module supports O2 cam connections and has extra ports like S-Video, and a custom board has two video inputs, one for SDI and one for analog that adapts to S-Video, connected to the O2 cam port. 1213 Indy can capture 240p via composite or S-Video. 14
