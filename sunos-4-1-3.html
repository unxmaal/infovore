SunOS 4.1.3

Claims: 7 Part of SunOS

SunOS 4.1.3 runs on a Sun SPARC with a 50.3 MHz clock rate and an LSI L64814 FPU, and it was the first version to support the SS10 (SunOS 4.1.1 does not run on the SS10). 12 SunOS 4.1.1 and earlier have a problem with disks larger than 1GB, and there is a patch for SunOS 4.1.1. 3 SunOS 4.1.3 is recommended over SunOS 4.0.3, 4.1.1, and 4.1.4, which is the latest version. 34 The 'date' command in SunOS 4.1.3 cannot set the date past 2000. 5 SunOS 4.1.3 is listed as one of the systems that usually require digging for documentation. 6
