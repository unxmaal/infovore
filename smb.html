SMB

Claims: 3

SMB is one of the networking options available on a TrueNAS server, alongside NFS and FTP, with both NFS and SMB being used for different shares. 123 NFS sharing is less secure than SMB but can still be useful, particularly for connecting old Unix machines, and is used on a TrueNAS server due to the home network context. 12
