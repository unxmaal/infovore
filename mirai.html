Mirai

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Lisp machine.

Mirai was a licensed program programmed in Lisp. It bundled Xemacs for Irix, though it was not as powerful as N-World's 'Wide Open World'. N-World was ported to Irix. Mirai had a Y2K bug and worked when the date was set to 1999. The Mirai caused graphical glitching and corruption for the user's O2. 123456
