DES

Claims: 5

Encryption algorithms from the 1990s, like DES, were commonly used for file encryption, but RSA was more likely used to establish session keys. 1 Exportable DES was limited to 64-bit, of which only 56 bits are used, and is now easily brute-forced. 2 The char limit is 8 due to DES. 3 The pre-v7 Alias software uses DES encryption combined with Base58 encoding and a checksum scheme for license keys. 4 Old Softimage (3.0 and earlier) uses a DES implementation, possibly tailored, which is the ancestor of SPM licensing used in later versions of Softimage. 5
