PCI USB card

Claims: 6 Part of PCI

A PCI USB card can be used to connect a mouse and keyboard to an Altix system. 1 The Ultra 60 uses a Sun-type keyboard by default, and a PCI USB card allows using a USB keyboard and mouse in Solaris 9 or 10 but not in firmware. 2 A PCI USB card works in a Netra, but the card guide may need repair. 3 Putting a PCI USB card in an O2 would provide power but not USB functionality. 4 The machine has two 6-port Ethernet cards, a PCI USB card, and a Quadro FX 580 graphics card. 5
