Solaris 7

Claims: 29 Part of Solaris Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris.

Solaris 7 can run on all Sparcstations and 90s ultras, though it lacks IPv6; it can run on a Sparcstation 1, though this is not recommended, and on a Sun IPC with 36 MB RAM and a stock CPU. 123 Solaris 7 has no /dev/random or /dev/urandom by default, but a specific patch can add a /dev/random, which is essential for SSHD; the patch is hard to find. 456 Cylinder counts over 32,768 are not compatible with Solaris 7. 7 MAE 3 runs on Solaris 7/ultraSPARC 64-bit, and LuBu OpenMagic on Solaris is a repackaging of OPENSTEP/Solaris for Solaris 7 and later on 64-bit machines. 89 Solaris 8 and onward come with Gzip, but Sunfreeware has multiple versions of gzip for Solaris 7. 1011 The IRC crowd considers Solaris 7 the best version to use. 10 Solaris 7 OpenGL guide indicates that SX, GX, GX+, TGX, TGX+, and S24 cards perform OpenGL functionality in software. 12
