MPlayer 1.4

Claims: 10 Part of mplayer

MPlayer 1.4 is being developed with newer code to support more recent video formats, and it supports FFmpeg 4.1, whereas MPlayer 1.1.1 supports FFmpeg 0.11. 12 MPlayer 1.4 is slower than the older 1.0rc1 version, especially with color space conversion, bitmap stride, and 1080p resolution, though optimizations from 1.0rc can improve performance while some issues remain. 34 MPlayer 1.4 performs better than 1.1.1. 5 MPlayer 1.4 can be built with MIPSpro and can play YouTube videos on an SGI Octane2 with Python3 and netsurf, and it is available for download from nekoware. 678 It is uncertain whether FFmpeg 5.0 can be used as a drop-in replacement for FFmpeg 4.1 or if adjustments are needed. 9
