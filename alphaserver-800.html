AlphaServer 800

Claims: 7

The AlphaServer 800 is a rack-mountable machine that can sit on top of a filing cabinet, though this raises the printer height. 1 It contains two 50-pin SCSI hard drives that are custom mounted via glued-on sleds. 2 Double-height modules from PC servers' Pentium Pro processors work in the AlphaServer 800 but not in an SGI Ultra 10. 3 tru64 5.1B with the latest patch kit runs on the AlphaServer 800. 4 The AlphaServer 800 cost 400 euro and needed a power supply adapter. 5
