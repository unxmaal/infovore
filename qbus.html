QBUS

Claims: 10

QBUS was created to reduce costs by multiplexing pins. 1 A BA23 is an 11/23 chassis with QBUS, and early MicroVAXes were QBUS based with the CPU on a QBUS card. 23 Qbus cards were dual or quad height to accommodate more board space, and DEC never made a Unibus J11 board. 4 Prereq classes used PDP-11 and later hardware labs used MicroVAXes and QBUS as learning platforms; they were well-documented, simple to understand in pieces, but not trivial. 5 Some PDP/11 cards are available on eBay, specifically Qbus cards that can work with BA23 or BA123, and are MFM for RD disks, not SCSI, and these systems are generally very reliable. 6
