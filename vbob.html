VBOB

Claims: 34

A VBOB (video breakout box) is a video breakout box that converts lines from the back of a graphics card into usable signals for standard hardware, often used for analog and digital video, genlock, and other purposes. 1 DMedia was a company that made hardware suites for video editing and composition; DMediaPro is what the video breakout box is labelled as, more commonly called the VBOB. 2 A VBOB interfaces with the DM2 or DM3 through specific connections, and for SDI capture, a DM2 or DM3 is needed in addition to the VBOB. 34 The DM3 card is the interface card for the VBOB, which occupies the XIO slot just above the V12 in a Tezro system, and the VBOB requires the DM3 card to be an XIO card, not a PCI. 56 DM2 and DM3 cards are hard to find and used with VBOB for video input, with DM3 having a card edge XIO connector for Tezro and Onyx350. 7 The VBOB handles video-out by connecting the graphics to the DM5 in the VBOB with DVI cables, and outputs SDI or HD-SDI video. 89 The DM5 board inside the VBOB handles graphics to video out for the Octane v12 with DVI port. 10 The VBOB has an ASIC that can be updated with newer versions of Xbow. 11
