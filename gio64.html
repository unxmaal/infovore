GIO64

Claims: 6 Part of GIO

The UPA bus is compatible with SBus-shaped cards but not PCI-shaped cards, using different physical form factors for its cards, and the Indigo2 uses GIO64 slots with a distinct form factor. 1 GIO64 is as fast as PCIe 1.0 x1, UPA is very fast to a single device, and UPA was shared. 2 The Indigo2 with an R10K and Maximum IMPACT can perform well, though the GIO64 bus can be a limitation. 3 The -gio64_gfx flag disables graphics for the gio64 slot, and -ioc2:rs232b can be used for a terminal. 4
