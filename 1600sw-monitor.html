1600SW monitor

Claims: 11 Part of 1600SW

The 1600SW monitor is a 1600x1024 display that can be used with Linux and operates as a dumb screen relying on external control from the card or software. 12 It is a nice option for O2s, but the required video card is hard to find and the O2 lacks the output port needed for the monitor. 34 The most common compatible monitors for the 1600SW are factory cards, third-party cards, and some third-party monitors. 5 There was an adaptor to run the 1600SW monitor from DVI, but no similar adaptor exists to convert O2 flatpanel output to DVI. 6 The monitor is not a suitable replacement for a 21" CRT, and its picture quality is below average in 2024, with blacks not being truly black and uneven backlight. 78 The manual for the Prism mentions using a 1600SW monitor. 9
