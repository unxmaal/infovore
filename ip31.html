IP31

Claims: 3 Part of IP

IP31 is a hardware IP only that runs IP27 software. 1 The IP31 board includes five 350 MHz IP27 processors with R12000 CPUs and R12010 FPGAs, two 400 MHz IP27 processors with R12000 CPUs and R12010 FPGAs, and a total of 8448 Mbytes of main memory. 2 IP31 and IP27 node boards can be mixed and matched, but the connector issue must be resolved. 3
