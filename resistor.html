resistor

Claims: 16

A resistor is needed to limit the current supplied to an LED, which requires a current source rather than a voltage source. 1 In some SCA/68pin type adapters, a resistor is used for current limiting of an LED. 2 If an optocoupler is connected to a bad resistor through another resistor, it is likely bad. 3 There is a resistor that commonly fails in the PSU, and unplugging and replugging it can sometimes start the system if the resistor is the issue, though fully reliable operation requires replacing the resistor. 4 A resistor in the power supply likely popped and arced, causing soot, but this might be a symptom rather than the cause. 5 A burnt resistor can be cleaned with vinegar and water. 6
