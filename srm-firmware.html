SRM firmware

Claims: 5 Part of SRM

Some Alpha systems have both SRM and ARC firmware, but many do not; the first and last Alphas were SRM only, and some non-DEC systems are ARC only. 1 The SRM firmware from 1997 may not support installing OSF/1 2.0 or 3.0. 2 Updating SRM firmware to V7.0 is required for certain functionalities. 3 SRM firmware for later machines is built from the same codebase, but not all machines running the same version get all features. 4
