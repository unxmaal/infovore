gigabit Ethernet

Claims: 18 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

Gigabit Ethernet is used on various systems, including SGI machines, where it can be installed and made to work, though early SGI models have fussy drivers. 123 Some SGI models have built-in gigabit Ethernet, such as the Tezro and O350, while others like the O2 do not officially support it due to CPU limitations. 45 Gigabit Ethernet is useful for network tasks such as NFS root, FTPing files, and running network attached storage, and can be faster than ATA100 for certain tasks. 678
