adapter board

Claims: 5 Part of adapter

An adapter board reroutes traces and pulls some address pins high to accommodate the wider address space of the DS1744W. 1 A 4D/30 or 4D/35 can use a GR2 with the adapter board. 2 Someone made an adapter board with EEPROMs so it picks a proper video mode and uses regular DVI cables for the Onyx350 with DCD. 3 The Octane can use an adapter board to replace its power supply, which is often dead, and can have up to 4GB of RAM with proprietary sticks. 4
