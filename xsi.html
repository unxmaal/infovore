XSI

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Softimage (company).

XSI was a Windows application that served as the successor to SoftImage, which became a legacy product after version 3.8. 12 It was initially bundled with Softimage|3D to complement its feature set, but later shipped independently at a higher price. 1 On SGI systems, XSI runs slower than Softimage3D because it operates as a Windows application running on a translator for IRIX. 34 SPM versions of XSI can be installed but may not work reliably due to dongle date checks. 5
