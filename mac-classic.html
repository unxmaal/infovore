Mac Classic

Claims: 4 Part of Mac

The Mac Classic is a robust computer, though the color Classic and later models are prone to plastic brittleness while the Mac Classic up to the SE/30 are not. 1 It can be modified with a reloaded board and an HDMI output, and the 0194 model requires a specific cable pinout. 2 The Mac Classic can also be used to help with learning to read through text-to-speech. 3
