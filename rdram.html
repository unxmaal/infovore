RDRAM

Claims: 23 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see RDRAM.

RDRAM was considered the future of graphics memory, but it had poor performance and was prone to failure. 1 It provided higher bandwidth in systems like the N64 and PS2, potentially allowing for board cost reductions by enabling the use of fewer layers. 2 RDRAM was better than original DDR 266 but not by much, and it is less common, making it more collectible. 3 It was faster for streaming memory but not for general use, and DDR eventually caught up through latency tuning and became a more cost-effective solution. 45 Termination is needed only if not all RDRAM slots are filled, and motherboards came with them. 6
