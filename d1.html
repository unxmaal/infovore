D1

Claims: 3

D1 is an operating system designed to look like IRIX, running on a SOC ARM board with an Ethernet adapter meant for the O2 PCI slot, but it got stuck on software problems related to remote display. 1 The D1 could be expanded to support many retrocomputing platforms and possibly fit into a pcmcia card for use in most laptops and desktops with an adapter. 2 The D1 bypasses the web, which is the largest hurdle to functioning among modern computers for older systems. 3
