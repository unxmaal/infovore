Extreme

Claims: 12

The Indigo2 was released with an Extreme model as a step-up from the Indigo1, which topped out with Elan, and the Extreme was among the configurations the I2 most typically came with, alongside Impact and XZ. 123 The SGI Extreme can survive harsh conditions but has limitations in graphics performance, and it is highly reliable, outperforming PCs of its time. 45 The AB1 adapter allows Extreme to work in a Crimson and is a rare part with no existing photos, while the AB2 adapter for Onyx uses PowerPath2 and is incompatible with Crimson. 6 The graphics type reported as INV_GR2 in sgi invent.h is mapped to EXPRESS, possibly a typo for EXTREME. 7
