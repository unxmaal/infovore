SCSI backplane

Claims: 8 Part of SCSI Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

SCSI backplanes appear in several SGI systems, including the Indigo2, Netra t1, and Octane, where the Octane's backplane connects to the frontplane. 12 Power for SCSI drives is supplied by a white connector on the PCI riser, which usually connects to a SCSI backplane. 3 The IO9's connector above the SCSI port is likely for a SCSI backplane and CD-ROM and may require jumper settings for configuration. 4 An O350 can be two expansion units with an IO9 and SCSI backplane, but lacks kbd/mouse ports and works fine as a standalone system. 5 On a system with SCA, the SCSI backplane has active termination, so the controller has the other side of the termination and no device termination is needed. 6 The Netra t1 uses a SCSI backplane and does not support IDE hard drives natively, requiring an external SCSI CD-ROM for installation. 7 If multiple slots are affected, the SCSI backplane might be damaged. 8
