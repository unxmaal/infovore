VAX 6000

Claims: 7 Part of VAX

The VAX 6000 used the XMI bus, while early MicroVAXes were QBUS based with the CPU on a QBUS card. 1 The VAX 6000 and DECstation are larger than an Onyx or E450 deskside. 2 The VAX 6000 or DECsystem 5800 were rather impressive SMP machines. 3 VAX 6000s had a mix of PDP and newer drives, with the buttons' lights visible in a dim room. 4 Meanwell PSUs provide 5V@100A and are suitable for building vintage systems like the VAX 6000, with ratings often exceeding actual needs. 5
