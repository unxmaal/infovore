CUDA

Claims: 9 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

CUDA and OpenCL are APIs for GPU computing, with CUDA being more popular in data centers due to higher adoption and better support. 1 CUDA is preferred over OpenCL for GPU computing, as Nvidia's CUDA support outperforms AMD's OpenCL support even with optimization. 23 NVIDIA made CUDA simple for physicists to use, while OpenCL is more complex. 4 CUDA is exclusive to NVIDIA GPUs and is in high demand for mining. 5 CUDA for ppc64el exists but there is no known userland implementation of OpenGL or Vulkan from nvidia for ppc64el linux. 6
