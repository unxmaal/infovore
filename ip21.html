IP21

Claims: 7 Part of IP Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx, SGI Onyx2.

The IP21 is a CPU board used in the Onyx, which typically uses R8K processors as indicated by the IP21 designation. 12 The IP21 CPU board uses one or two R8000 microprocessor sets with a customized cache controller, a separate floating point unit, and two tag RAM and two SRAM cache units. 3 The IP21 is one of the CPU boards included in the Onyx and Onyx2, alongside the IP19, IP25, IP27, IP29, and two CPU options. 4 The InfiniteReality and i-Station systems are not available with IP21 CPU boards. 5 The system controller monitors backplane voltages but does not monitor the IP19, IP21, MC3, IO4, or SCSIBox. 6 Swapping chips from an IP21 into an IP26 might be possible with modifications such as clock and voltage adjustments. 7
