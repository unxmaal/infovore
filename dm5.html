DM5

Claims: 8

DM2 and DM3 are used for VBOB connections, while DM5 is inside the VBOB. 1 The Octane2 uses a VBOP with DM5 and DM3 cards, but cables are unavailable. 2 The DM5 outputs graphics to SDI. 3 The HD-GVO can connect to a DM5 in a VBOB but not directly to a screen; adding a DM2 or DM3 enables a Discreet Inferno setup. 4 The DG5-TVO connects to the DM5 in the VBOB via LVDS over DVI. 5 The vbob handles video-out by connecting the graphics to the DM5 in the VBOB with DVI cables. 6 The DM5 option allows graphics output to video, but not the reverse; with DM2, video into graphics is done through memory. 7 The DM5 card with DVI ports is separate from the VBOB and goes into the workstation. 8
