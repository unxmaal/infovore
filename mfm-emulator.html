MFM emulator

Claims: 5 Part of MFM

An MFM emulator is needed for an Iris due to its drive pickiness. 1 The Interphase controller requires jumper settings to successfully communicate with an MFM emulator or ST506 disk, but no documentation exists for these settings. 2 The MFM emulator slurps the bits. 3 The MFM emulator allows the machine to be powered on and off normally without affecting the Linux hard drive. 4 The MFM emulator from decromancer.ca allows using MFM drives, but the RQDX3 is needed for SCSI compatibility, increasing the cost. 5
