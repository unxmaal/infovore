Sun Studio 11

Claims: 7 Part of Sun Studio

Sun Studio 11 is compatible with Solaris 9, and it may also run on Solaris 2.8. 12 Sun Studio 11 is supported on Solaris 8, which does not require a license code, and it can be run from the Companion CD, which includes GCC. 3 The Sun Studio 11 installer requires Java 1.4.x or 1.5.x, or JRE 5 or 6, while JRE 8 works with missing symbol errors. 45 GCC from OpenCSW may have issues due to dependencies. 1
