ribbon cable

Claims: 11

Ribbon cables connect various components, such as the interface board to the panel (a proprietary connection), the graphics board to the IP6 using a longer-than-50-pin cable, and the secondary drive connector on a 320. 123 Ribbon cables can fail in different ways, including a torn cable, a connector that falls off, and a display ribbon cable that may not stop video output. 435 Repairing a torn ribbon cable can be difficult, often involving removing plastic and painting a new trace, and there may be no room for most solutions without rigging up a 90 degree turn in the ribbon cable. 46 The 20-pin ribbon cable uses an IDC connector rated for 26-28awg with a single ferrule used for crimping, and the original Indigo2 disks use a ribbon cable with jumpers that need to be set manually if using an adapter. 78 Sufficient components, including SCSI cables, adapters, ribbon cable, and crimp-on connectors, may be needed to complete repairs, and a spare ribbon cable might work. 910
