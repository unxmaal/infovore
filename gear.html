gear

Claims: 11 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see RAM drive.

SGI gear has been sold at high prices by sellers who inspired others to set high prices for vintage SGI gear, though much of the SGI gear liquidated by P&G in 2006 was worthless, and some SGI gear has been obtained for free from an ex-SGI engineer. 123 Some drives may have a specific gear made of different plastic that ages and breaks, and the O2 drives can get stuck due to a dislodged gear. 45 A gear that was about to fail can eventually fail, after which replacing the gear allows the drive to work fine. 6
