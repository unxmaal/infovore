Clover1

Claims: 9

Clover1 graphics hardware was used in the early 4D/50, 4D/60, 4D/70, and 4D/80, running two sets of matrix multiplication GEs in parallel and a custom flat shading processor. 1 The 4D/60 originally shipped with the R2300 and Clover 1 graphics. 2 The 4D/70 GT started life as a 4D/70 'G' with purple top hats. 3 NEWS was ported to 4D1-3.0 and ran on Clover1 and Clover2 machines, but was replaced by Xsgi in IRIX 4. 4 RISC/os source code from around the IRIX 4.0 days shows it is an independent branch of UNIX without shared libraries or NeNews desktop, including MIPS R2300 firmware but not SGI's modifications for Clover1. 5 It is not certain that any documentation for Clover1 exists, and SGIStuff calls it a 'raster subsystem'. 6 Clover1's graphics board is believed to be functional, but the system it was in lacked RAM. 7
