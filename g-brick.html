G-brick

Claims: 29 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Origin 3000 and Onyx 3000.

The G-brick is a graphics brick for Silicon Graphics systems, using IR (Image Rendering) graphics technology. 12 It is a small form factor, 18U-high system that requires its own power supply for graphics. 34 A G-brick contains two pipes: a 2-RM card pipe like an Onyx2 and a 4-card pipe. 5 It requires NUMALink cables, needing two cables for both IR channels. 67 It is an XIO device that does not require serial number matching, and there are two flavors, one of which uses a NUMALink style. 89 The L1 controller USB connection links directly to an L2 controller L1 port in a single-rack configuration, and the r-brick and g-brick require USB connections to access their L1s. 1011
