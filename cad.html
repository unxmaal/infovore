CAD

Claims: 24 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Computer-aided design.

CAD is 3D modelling and animation software, and it is mostly used on Windows, while simulation is probably mostly on Linux. 12 CAD has been used on a variety of machines, including older Intergraph machines, the IBM 5080 mainframe graphics system, RS/6000 machines (used by Renault and Dassault), SGI machines, and HP machines. 345678 Some software has CAD-related features, such as GOM's import of CAD as IGES and NX's 'through curves' function similar to loft. 910 CAD can be run on certain systems by setting the PATH variable to include /usr/local/srd/cad/bin and /usr/local/srd/cad/options, and exporting isometric wireframe views from CAD projects converts arcs to splines. 1112
