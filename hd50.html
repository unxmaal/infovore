HD50

Claims: 9

HD50 is a ribbon cable style connector used for various SGI computers, where it is the rarest for older SCSI-1 devices. 12 Suns used HD50 connectors alongside HD68 and CN50 connectors. 3 Octane and Blade 2000 external SCSI can be converted to HD50 for connection to UNIX workstations. 4 HVD can use standard HD50 connectors in addition to its specific connector. 5 Some vintage Sun accessories use Centronics style SCSI connectors, requiring adapters for HD50 connectors. 6 The Indigo has a HD50 to 50 pin SCSI port where the SCSI ID comes from a 10 pin connector carrying 5V and 12V. 7
