Onyx350

Claims: 77 Part of Onyx Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Tezro.

With Tezro

The Onyx350 is a Tezro and a V12 in a 2U enclosure, and an Onyx350 with VPro is just a Tezro. 12 The rackmount Tezro is an Onyx 350 that was factory-crippled to be less expandable and sold cheaper, limited to four processors and 8GB. 345 The rack Tezro can only be installed in a system with two bricks or less, can be converted to a rack Onyx350, and can be linked with seven more bricks. 36 The desktop Tezro has more PCI slots than the rack Tezro, and both have an IO card slot like the Onyx350 that supports 3.3v or 5v. 78 You can swap the CPU board between a Tezro and an Onyx350. 9 The DMediaPro DM3 board can be used with the SGI Onyx 350 dual-head graphics system and Tezro workstations. 10

General

The Onyx350 can comprise 1 to 8 Origin350 nodes with either IP or IR graphics, and compute nodes do not come with graphics as these are provided by separate bricks. 111213 Onyx350 bricks containing a V12 processor are called 'Infinite Performance' and have slightly different doors. 1415 The Onyx350 can be identified by the label 'Onyx 350 InfinitePerformance' for models with GPUs and 'Onyx 350' for those without. 16 The Onyx350 can use either an O350 with IP-gfx or a G-brick with IR-gfx, and can boot without the SCSI backplane though it requires an external drive and serial cable for booting. 1117 The Onyx350 has a working node board, PSU, fans, and some RAM, but the IO9 and PCI riser are dead. 1819 The Onyx350 can mount a VPro and a CPU board in the same chassis, and can use an R16k processor for compiling. 2021 The Onyx350 is faster than any Octane and runs cooler than an Octane2 despite similar hardware. 2223 The Onyx350 can use SGI part no 030-2097-002 fiber channel cards and needs addon DCD cards for DVI. 2425 The Onyx 350 is essentially an Origin 350 with 3D pipelines, available as a deskside unit plugged into an InfiniteReality3 or 4 or as a rackmount that is not well-known to the average SGI fan. 262728 Onyx350 nodes are base nodes, while middle nodes are expansion units that may have memory-only boards or quad-1GHz CPUs. 29 The Infinite Performance in the Onyx350 was due to having multiple V12 CPUs working together, and it can drive two G-bricks for a total of four pipes, each with specific texture memory capacities. 3031 An Onyx350 with Infinite is a much different beast than an Onyx350 with VPro, and V bricks are 4U and just graphics, so an Onyx350 is superior in terms of also providing compute in the same amount of rack units for two V12s. 3233 The command `make rmws 0` changes the firmware to identify the hardware as an Onyx350, allowing it to be used in a larger cluster, and the O350 brick may not require a serial change when added to an Onyx350 setup. 3435 Onyx350s with graphics are equivalent to O350s with graphics and work with a router, and an Onyx350 setup can boot when disconnected. 3637 Onyx2s were commonly used for stereoscopic 3D environments, typically involving four projected surfaces, though Onyx350 or Onyx4 systems could achieve the same with less space. 38 Capture boards, ImageSync cards, and the like would be nigh impossible to get along with an Onyx 350 visualization workstation, and the hybrid may have compatibility issues with the VPro and FireGL cards coexisting. 3940
