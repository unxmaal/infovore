Display Postscript

Claims: 4 Part of display

Apple discarded Display Postscript in favor of Display PDF, along with removing several legacy Unix features, a transition that contributed to the creation of OS X. 1 Photoshop 3 on Irix 6.5.30 requires Display Postscript, and downgrading it can conflict with other packages. 2 SGI used MEX until 1988, then NeWS until 1991 before switching to X, and it likely used Display Postscript with NeWS. 3 The user is attempting to downgrade Display Postscript for X. 4
