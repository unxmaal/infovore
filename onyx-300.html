Onyx 300

Claims: 4 Part of Onyx

The Onyx 300 is a desk side equivalent of a short rack of 12U-14U housing 4 or 8 CPUs. 1 It has a single card cage with 2x IP25 and 2x MC3 boards, 8x R10K processors, and HVD SCSI disk boxes. 2 The Onyx 300 was not complete. 3
