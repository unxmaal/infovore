GPU enablement kit

Claims: 3 Part of GPU

The T640 GPU enablement kit is essentially just a power cable (and possibly a PCIe riser). 1 Installing the T640 GPU enablement kit requires removing the motherboard and adding external fans that cover the PCIe slots, even though the server has a full row of large fans inside. 2 The T640's GPU kit configuration is unusual. 1
