13W3

Claims: 36 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Video Graphics Array.

13W3 is the name for the proprietary display port on Silicon Graphics Systems, though it is not proprietary but an old analog standard used in other fancy UNIX boxes. 1 The RGB port on SGIs is called '13w3' and isn't fully compatible with other systems like Suns and IBM, sometimes requiring adapters. 2 Suns are usually not SoG, while SGI 13W3 to VGA adapters are wired for composite sync, and cutting a pin on Sun adapters makes them work with SGI's, and Octanes are SoG like other pre-DVI SGI machines. 3 13W3 pinouts differ slightly between Sun and SGI, but RGB bits are the same. 4 A Sun 13W3 has a different pinout compared to SGI's. 5 SGI's 13W3 ports have at least three different pinouts, and compatibility depends on the monitor's ability to handle the video and sync signals, especially with the Reality Engine's +10VDC on one of the 10 pins. 6 SGI and Sun used 13W3 connectors for their monitors because older machines used BNC jacks. 7 13W3 uses the coax cables to transmit RG and B. 8
