IO3

Claims: 9

The IO3 board is necessary for a functional VME bus in Crimson, and the IO2/IO3/IO3B bridge VME to PowerPath while the GM board bridges PowerPath to graphics, with both having massive connectors. 12 The SCSI controller on the IO3 is a WD33c93, and the IO3 and IO3B controllers use different WD33C93A chips, with IO3B using AMD chips which have a quirk, possibly fixed in newer versions. 34 An IO3 or IO3B can connect both drive bays to separate SCSI channels, while an IO2 with a single channel requires a jumper to connect all bays to one chain. 5 The 4D/380 has an IO3, IP7, and MC2 that require testing; a minimal 8MB RAM setup is sufficient for diagnostics, which include CPU, bus, memory, path, IO, FPU, and MP tests. 6 By putting both secondary CPU mezzanines on one IP7 and upgrading the PROM to match the IO3, an IP7 that failed earlier was made to work again. 7 The PowerSeries chassis was redesigned halfway through its product life due to the release of the IO3, drive module inefficiency, and the arrival of high density RAM. 8 The IP7 uses two CPU halves with minimal shared components; early 1990s PowerSeries tech refresh included 33MHz CPUs, IO3, VGX graphics, and Single Tower chassis, improving drive efficiency. 9
