libgcc

Claims: 5

The libgcc and libstdc++ libraries are required on the target for C++ programs. 1 bash needs libgcc in LD_LIBRARY_PATH for .bash_profile to work. 2 libgcc opens, libc fails. 3 IRIX executables built with GCC may or may not link against libgcc depending on the code. 4 Bootstrapping SGI packages with clang is difficult due to libgcc and libdicl dependencies clashing, making it hard to mix gcc- and clang-built shared libraries. 5
