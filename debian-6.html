Debian 6

Claims: 9 Part of Debian

Debian 6 runs on the Altix 350 with io9, though it installs on that platform but not on SLES9 or Debian 7, possibly due to issues with the IO9 driver. 123 Debian 6 provided partial console output but lacked access to SLES 11 repositories. 4 The ATI driver for Debian 6 did not work properly with the FireMV 2200. 5 Debian 6 on an IA64 system gives an illegal instruction error in X, possibly a kernel issue, while Debian 7's kernel hangs and says CPU0 not responding. 67 Debian 6 or 7 and SuSE 11 were available options, with Suse 11 being good if you don't need support for prism graphics; Debian never installed cleanly but booted with a manually set up elilo bootloader. 78
