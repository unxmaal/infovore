IRIX64

Claims: 6 Part of IRIX Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see XFS.

Meson is producing an unsupported makefile for the irix64 system with cpu family ip35. 1 The uname reported by the system may indicate IRIX vs IRIX64 as different architectures, but the binaries are compatible and should run fine. 2 The sgug-rse works on O2s with a patch to report IRIX64 to RPM. 3 An O2 with a 250 MHz R10 CPU is approximately half as slow as an IRIX64 250 MHz R10K CPU. 4 The diskperf tool on an IRIX64 fuel 6.5 machine shows XFS data subvolume performance with various request sizes. 5 An R4k Indigo2 runs IRIX or IRIX64; one can check with uname. 6
