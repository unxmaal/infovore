FRAM

Claims: 6

FRAM is a type of non-volatile RAM that retains data even when powered off. 1 FRAM is not affected by soft errors caused by radiation, unlike DRAM and SRAM, and has a very low terrestrial Soft Error Rate. 2 Silabs offers microcontrollers with FRAM technology that persists data when powered off, and claims 150uA/MHz and 10nA in sleep mode, reportedly lower than flash power consumption. 3 FRAM can be used to save state in a Commodore SX-64, allowing for resuming operations where you left off. 4 The Sega CD with FRAM mod uses FRAM for permanent saves, unlike the standard SRAM which loses data without power. 5 The PC Engine Duo and Saturn can use save carts, but modifying the system for FRAM saves is preferred. 6
