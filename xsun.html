Xsun

Claims: 17

Xsun was the default X server for Solaris, used for SPARC systems, while Xorg and XFree86 supported x86 systems. 12 Xsun is poorly designed and may not perform well, being slow even on hardware that is not particularly fast. 23 Xsun has limited graphics card support on Solaris x86, especially before Solaris 9 or 10, and limited drivers for certain cards on SPARC compared to x86. 45 Most ATI Rage XL PCI cards work with Xsun for Solaris, though some may require reflash, while Rage 128 cards may not work with Xsun on SPARC even with a SPARC OpenFirmware ROM. 67 Xsun does not expose 3D components on PGX32 and XVR-100 as 3D acceleration, treating them as 2D accelerated framebuffers. 8 XVR-100 works with Xsun, Xorg, and CDE on Solaris 10, and Solaris 10 on SPARC uses Xsun instead of Xorg, using `fbconfig` for configuration. 910 Xsun might not support USB keyboards/mice well, and can start without a mouse if the 'AllowMouseOpenFail' option is set, though Xsun does not have this by default. 1112 CATIA requires Xsun or old OpenWindows, not Xorg or modern CDE. 13
