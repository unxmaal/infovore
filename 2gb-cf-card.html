2GB CF card

Claims: 3 Part of 2GB

Using a 2GB CF card adapted to the original IDE controller is considered less sketchy than SCSI2SD. 1 Warcraft 2 installation issues on a 2 GB CF card with DOS 6.22 may indicate a CF card problem or a CDROM driver conflict. 2 The T5200 can be modified with a BIOS update and a 2GB CF card. 3
