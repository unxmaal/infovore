3000

Claims: 6

The HP 3000 was a minicomputer developed by Hewlett Packard, and engineers reportedly discovered something while developing it. 1 The HP 3000 PA-RISC minicomputers have an emulator available, though it is not a real machine. 2 The DEC 3000 was an Alpha system with turbo-channel, while the DECstation 5000 used MIPS. 3 The DEC 3000 requires a Hawley mouse, a DEC keyboard, and an adapter box. 4 The 3500 has a faster backplane and an extra slot for CPUs, supporting 8 instead of 6. 5 MPE could be downclocked to 200MHz on a 3000 that was actually an rp2450, and would run fine with dual 650MHz CPUs. 6
