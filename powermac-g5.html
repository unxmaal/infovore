PowerMac G5

Claims: 19

The PowerMac G5 uses a non-ATX power supply that is incompatible with standard ATX form factors, as are SGI systems, the Sun Blade 1000, and Dell Precision workstations. 1 Validating the PowerMac G5 power supply involves measuring the 5V standby voltage line, and its power supply is not compatible with an Octane. 23 Replacing the PowerMac G5 power supply requires 27 steps. 4 The Power Mac G5's fan system is unreliable but can be very quiet when it works. 5 The Power Mac G5 experienced temp sensor or coolant leak failures, unlike the iMac G5, which had GPU failures. 6 The PowerMac G5 can be upgraded to 3 GB of RAM, can stream 720p YouTube and browse normally, and can run IDEA using an open JDK power build on Linux. 789 The PowerMac G5 can experience random freezes when running Xorg under FreeBSD, and a HME card can be made to work with OpenFirmware in a PowerMac G5. 1011 The Power Mac G5 is a significant upgrade from G4 towers but requires more power. 12
