swap space

Claims: 3 Part of swap

Swap space can be insufficient for a system dump when a system has large amounts of RAM. 1 Installing programs with CSW may require more swap space than is available, so a larger disk may be needed for Solaris 10. 2 Logical volumes cannot be used for swap space on systems with a single disk, nor for the root filesystem. 3
