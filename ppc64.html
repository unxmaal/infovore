PPC64

Claims: 6 Part of PPC Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see PowerPC.

The short form for the PowerPC CPU architecture is PPC, and its variants include PPC, PPCel, PPC64, and PPC64EL. 1 Power5 is PPC64 and can run Linux. 2 Debian and Fedora/RedHat no longer support PPC64. 3 Debian does not support ppc64, only ppc64el for Power8+, which is rarely used. 4 Gentoo still supports PPC64 big endian. 5 OpenBSD is adding ppc64 support but it's LE, requiring at least a PowerPC P8. 6
