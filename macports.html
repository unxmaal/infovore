MacPorts

Claims: 6

MacPorts installs to /usr/local without requiring root permissions, and adds its paths to .profile, though these are not reflected in $PATH under zsh until the export command is moved to .zshrc. 12 When installing a new version of a program, MacPorts binaries should be placed in the PATH before system binaries. 3 MacPorts has a larger community and better documentation than Tigerbrew, which has issues with PPC support, and it has been used to build tools on a Quad G5. 45 Copying /opt/local via tar is an easy way to transfer MacPorts between G4s. 6
