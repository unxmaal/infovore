Pentium 3

Claims: 8 Part of Pentium

The cutoff for retro is Pentium 3, while the cutoff for usable is a high end Westmere such as an i7 980X (or the Xeon equivalent) at 4.2GHz. 1 SGI's L3 controller was a 3100, a 1U Pentium 3. 2 Apple beat the Pentium 3 with the 7450 due to cycle efficiency, as the G4 with good AltiVec is a few years newer than the P3, the G5 was the fastest thing on the market, and the early G3s were fast and survived much. 3 A Pentium 3 at 800 MHz achieved 594 MIPS, while an FX at the same speed achieved 786 MIPS in a 7z benchmark. 4 Pentium 4 ISA boards are upgradeable from Pentium 3, and Pentium 4 is about 20% slower IPC than Pentium 3. 56 Prescott had a pipeline 310% longer than Pentium 3, so every branch misprediction takes 3x longer. 7 Pentium 3s and 4s, and very low end AMDs were supported by Windows ME. 8
