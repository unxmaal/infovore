FireGL X1

Claims: 11 Part of FireGL

The FireGL X1 is a classic card supported by HP-UX, but it requires HP firmware rather than SGI firmware. 12 It has excellent raster performance but poor geometry performance. 3 In an Onyx4, the FireGL X1 is significantly faster than the V12 for GPU-intensive tasks. 4 The Prism can take a FireGL X1, X2, X3, or some other variant with AGP, and the SGI BIOS can be flashed to support PC cards. 5 In SLES9, 3D acceleration works with the FireGL X1 on a zx6000 using QuickTransit, but 2D acceleration did not work with Red Hat. 6
