SPARCstation 4

Claims: 6

The SPARCstation 4 is a SPARC workstation, also known as a SPARCStation 4. 1 It is considered one of the top models in the SPARC line, alongside the Ultra 5 and SPARCstation 5. 2 The Netra is a repackaged SPARCstation 4 with NEBS certification and possibly extra shielding, and its PROM identifies it as a SPARCstation 4. 3 The VGER's internals are similar to a SPARCstation 4. 4 Some vintage machines, including the SPARCstation 4, are still operational today. 5
