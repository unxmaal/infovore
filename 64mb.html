64MB

Claims: 8 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indy, XFS.

The O2 came with 1GB of RAM and can be upgraded to 64MB. 1 IRIX 5.3 was optimized for 64MB of RAM and performed better than 5.1 on an Indy with 32MB, with 6.x recommended for 128MB or more. 2 The indigo2 is R4k I2I and should order 64MB to avoid being stuck with 128. 3 With 64MB, only serial console or DECwindows would probably be used. 4 CDE on Solaris 9 is tight on 64MB but works with 512MB. 5 64MB for $50 for 30 pin SIMMs. 6 OSF/1 3.2 works with 64MB, and 128MB is mostly fine. 7
