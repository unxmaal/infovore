4D/380 VGX

Claims: 6 Part of 4D/380

The 4D/380 VGX is a tech refresh of the 4D PowerSeries, featuring 33MHz IP7 CPUs, VGX graphics, and high-density SIMMs, and it is a CMN A006. 12 It can boot in PON mode after replacing dead CPUs and firmware, and it can drop into a 'PON>' prompt due to issues with the IP7 board, particularly the boot master CPU. 34 The IO3 and IP7 PROMs are EPROMs that must match the CPU PROM revisions, and firmware versions can affect booting. 4 The 4D/380 VGX has a 250A 5V rail, while the O2K/Onyx2 PSU provides 375A at 3.45V, and improperly fastened current-carrying screws can cause voltage drops and hotspots under load, so secure fastening at 50N is recommended. 5 A user with a 4D/380VGX 'Predator' rack bought several crates of boards from someone who had parts from SkyWriter's systems. 6
