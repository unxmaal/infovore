OS 9

Claims: 64 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Mac OS 9, IMac G3.

The QuickSilver G4 was the last model to support OS 9, including dual-CPU models up to that point. 1 The 1.25 GHz version was the fastest OS 9 machine Apple officially sold, while the 1.42 can boot OS 9 though not officially supported. 23 A G5 cannot run OS 9, and the QuickSilver on Tiger cannot boot OS 9. 45 The Mac Mini requires special patches to run OS 9, but it is not officially supported. 6 The hacked installers from macos9lives can put OS 9 on unsupported PowerPC Macs. 7 OS 9 allows mounting any disc and dragging the system folder to create a new bootable drive with drag and drop, which is not as easy in OS X. 8 OS 9 and OS X did not recognize a .image file, and changing the extension to .iso is not recommended. 9 OS 9 is a classic Macintosh operating system that can run on vintage hardware. 10 The first model line to drop OS 9 support was the 1.25GHz Mirrored Drive Doors model. 11 OS X versions 10.0 to 10.2 had poor performance but were more reliable than OS 9. 12 The disk utility in OS X was able to repair a disk error that OS 9 could not resolve. 13 OS 9 is generally slow to boot. 14 Boot failures on OS 9 require a PRAM battery, whereas Tiger works. 15 OS 9 does not dynamically manage memory, requiring users to manually allocate memory to applications like Photoshop. 16 OS 9 has a memory limit of 1.5 GB and no software on OS 9 can use more than 1.5 GB of RAM. 17
