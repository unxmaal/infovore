Photoshop

Claims: 41 Background: 10 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Adobe Photoshop, GIMP, SGI O2.

Adobe ported Photoshop, along with Illustrator and Premiere, to IRIX, with licenses available from resellers. 12 The IRIX version of Photoshop is a Mac version running on a translation layer, and the binary was rewritten or recompiled. 34 Photoshop runs on Solaris using the same framework used for porting Mac OS software. 5 Photoshop can be obtained from a bounty release for IRIX, though it costs $950/1500 as of November 2022. 6 Photoshop can easily convert images to 8-bit color and downscale them, doing a better job of preserving detail than GIMP. 789 Earlier versions of Photoshop required a key to unlock, and it could run on 8 MB. 1011 Photoshop's Lensflare render module uses a bitshifting approach that was broken on the new MIPS RK CPU. 12
