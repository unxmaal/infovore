MXE

Claims: 42 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The MXE (also known as EMXI) is an enhanced version of the TRAM with a higher clock speed, alongside the SE and SSE. 12 An MXE and an SSE are the same board, with the SSE lacking a bolt and becoming an MXE with texture modules added. 34 The MXE and an SSE differ only in the number of TRAMs, and the MXE requires both TRAM boards for full performance. 35 MXE likely has faster TRAM than MXI, though the exact speed is not specified. 6 The V8 is faster than MXI and MXE, and the V6 has more than twice the geometry performance of MXE. 78 The V6 outperforms the MXE with TRAMs, and the V6/V8 got more texture RAM than MXE. 910 The VPro V8/V12 offers more TRAM than MXE, but may not be practical for common resolutions and monitor adaptors. 11 Most SGIs use OpenGL 1.1, even the Octane with its MXE. 12
