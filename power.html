POWER

Claims: 45 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Central processing unit, FreeBSD, IBM AS/400, Power supply unit (computer), PowerPC.

POWER is the high-end server and workstation CPU family, including RS64, POWER, PowerPC, and RS/6000 architectures. 1 POWER and SPARC CPUs can operate in both big and little endian modes, with SPARC capable of loading data in either mode and POWER's mode determined by firmware. 2 PowerPC is exclusively for big endian, while POWER is bi endian. 3 Modern RISC architectures have become more CISC-like, as seen in ARM, SPARC, POWER, and RISC-V. 4 POWER is much more power hungry than ARM. 5 Anonymous sources indicate that Power and ARM processors do not perform as well as x86 in heavy workloads. 6 The market was too small for all RISC CPU players, with IBM's Power doing well but others struggling. 7 POWER support for IBM stuff requires a userland implementation of APIs through DRM to nvidia drivers. 8
