What to mark
Mark every message that states or confirms a durable technical fact: something still true and useful to someone who never reads this conversation. Part numbers, settings and jumpers, versions, quirks and workarounds, procedures, compatibility, where to get software or manuals, what something sold for and when. Grey messages are earlier context and cannot be marked. When you press Done, every unmarked message is saved as no fact.
Keys: j / k or ↓ / ↑ move, space toggles, Enter saves, ← / → move between exchanges.
