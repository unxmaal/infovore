What to judge
Judge the WHOLE exchange. relevant: it is about tech, computers, SGI, IRIX or retrocomputing at all; if you see computer or tech words, check that most of the exchange is on-topic. irrelevant: off-topic chatter. bad grouping: it mixes unrelated conversations or cuts one off. Grey messages are earlier context from the parent exchange. Judging again replaces nothing; every judgment is kept.
Keys: r relevant, i irrelevant, b bad grouping (each saves and moves on), ← / → move between exchanges.
