mainboard

Claims: 26

Mainboards may require reseating to function, as Octanes sometimes need multiple reseats of the main and xio boards, and reseating the mainboard can fix a keyboard port issue. 12 Mainboards can suffer from various hardware faults, including bent compression connectors, dodgy connector pins, damaged oscillators, broken SCSI chips, and surface rust. 34567 Mainboard problems are often associated with power supply issues, such as a dead O2 PSU that spins up but does nothing, or an Octane2 whose fan spins up while the power button has no effect. 891011 Mainboards may carry extra components such as storage and sometimes RAM chips, an extra SCSI connector on proto and early Fuel runs, and a black handle on first-gen VPro Octane 1.5 models. 121314 Mainboards can be replaced or modified without being a critical issue, though the CPU module is bolted to the mainboard and removal risks bending pins. 1516 Mainboards may have fit or compatibility constraints, working in their original position but not another case, requiring a specific revision for MRI use, or having limited space for the VPro and blower. 171819
