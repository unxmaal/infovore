backplane

Claims: 37 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Router (computing).

Some systems, such as Altixes, do not have a backplane, while others require jumpering for certain configurations. 12 Backplanes vary in interface and function: some are SCSI, some are not SCA, and some use a SAS switch to solve the Octopus configuration. 345 Backplanes connect components such as nodeboards, routers, and external SCSI devices, and may include control logic, a power distribution unit, or a system controller board. 67891011 Backplane size correlates with a machine's era and speed, and smaller backplanes can fit into larger ones. 12 Backplanes are prone to damage and failure from issues such as bent pins, connectors requiring significant force, toner-like dust, or a failed aux voltage regulator. 13141516 Backplane problems can be isolated using external SCSI enclosures or by testing components in a working system. 1718
