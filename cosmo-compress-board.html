Cosmo Compress board

Claims: 4 Part of Cosmo Compress

The Cosmo Compress board is worth more than the whole Indy. 1 It is compatible with Galileo video on Indigo systems, though it may not be necessary for Indigo models with built-in video I/O. 2 The board's second monitor is limited to 8-bit. 3 The Indy AVID setup requires the Cosmo Compress board for video, but detailed information is scarce and possibly just advertised. 4
