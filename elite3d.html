Elite3D

Claims: 24

With Creator3D

The Creator3D and Elite3D were separate product lines that both used 3DRAM. 12 The Ultra 60 shipped with an Elite3D GPU, while the Ultra 10 shipped with a Creator3D GPU. 3 The Creator3D series 2 and 3 support 1600x1200 resolution, while the Elite3D maxes out at 1280x1024. 456 Both the Creator3D and Elite3D use system RAM for texture storage via UPA. 7 The high poly/no texture mode works better on machines with slow texture support, such as the Creator3D or Elite3D. 8 Both cards support OpenGL, and both were supported by the XGL API. 9

General

The Elite3D is a Sun graphics card that is part of a different product line from the Creator cards, which use the AFB framebuffer. 1011 It offers faster geometry processing and limited texturing compared to the Creator3D, and its capability was similar to SGI's Max IMPACT. 1012 The Elite3D can be used in a UPA extension cable and in the Ultra2, E450, and various enterprise servers. 1314 It can run on Linux/BSD but only in 2D, and on Solaris 8 with OpenGL, though earlier releases might require supplement CDs for drivers. 1516 The Elite3D only supports analog connections, while the XVR series cards support DVI output. 17
