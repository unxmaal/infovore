RM11

Claims: 8

The RM11 has 4 times the texture memory and 32 times the raster memory compared to the RM10, with a fill rate of 10200 million pixels per second. 1 Each RM11 has 1024MB of texture memory, with total texture memory being used in parallel for performance. 2 RM11 cards in an IR4 pipe can provide up to 4 GB of texture memory. 3 An IR4 configuration includes a GE16-4, RM11, and DG5. 4 The G-brick in an Onyx3 is limited to four RM11s due to power draw, and compute bricks are powered separately with grounding. 5 A plan is to use two RM11s in an Onyx2 for a cave setup. 6 GE14-4 boards can work with RM10 and possibly RM11 graphics cards. 7
