SGI mousepad

Claims: 4 Part of SGI

The SGI mousepad is a quick, light text editor available on IRIX and Solaris. 1 Original keyboards and mice from the 4D series work well with Indigo or Onyx mouse pads, which may scratch up and behave erratically. 2 An SGI mousepad can be cleaned with warm water, a little dish soap, and a microfiber towel, but soaking is not recommended. 3
