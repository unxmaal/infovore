AutoCAD R13

Claims: 8 Part of AutoCAD

AutoCAD R13 is available for IRIX, with the IRIX version available on archive.org. 12 It requires IRIX 6.2 and installs into /opt/acadr13, generating menus and config files. 314 Installation may require root access, and a new cfg file is needed on startup for successful installation. 513 It may require the Display PostScript distributable, which is not included with IRIX 6.5.30 and must be obtained separately. 6 Setup notes may be needed to resolve graphics errors, and the software does not start on indigo2/high impact. 47
