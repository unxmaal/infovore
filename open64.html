Open64

Claims: 9 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Open64.

Open64 originated when SGI retargeted MIPSpro to IA64 by removing the licensed MIPS backend and frontend, then open-sourced the result as Pro64, now known as Open64, which includes the intermediate WHIRL representation. 1 Open64 uses GCC as a front end and is MIPSPro without the autovectorization backend. 2 AMD forked Open64 for x86-64 development. 3 Open64 support for IRIX was dropped around 2011 when GCC also stopped supporting IRIX. 4 Open64 no longer supports MIPS and is missing a lot of stuff from the original. 5 Continuing with a WIP GCC for IRIX support is considered a better option than Open64. 6 Open64 is a decent compiler. 7
