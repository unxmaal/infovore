MorphOS

Claims: 15

MorphOS runs on several PowerPC platforms, including the G4 Mini, G4s, and G5s. 123456 It offers a browser, good UAE integration for games, and the ability to run Amiga software. 713 It provides better compatibility than the OS that ships with the same hardware, and on a G4 Powermac gives access to ports of convenience software not available for 68k. 89 However, it is described as unstable and underperforming on high-end G4 hardware, and as feeling disjointed with subsystems that don't fit together well, unlike AmigaOS. 51011 Its browser is not WebKit, and its license costs may not be justifiable. 72
