Mac SE

Claims: 9 Part of Mac

The Mac SE can run System 7, but it is slow and does not have an MMU. 1 The Mac SE has 4MB of RAM and can run SubText BBS via SCSI->MacTCP. 2 The Mac SE can boot from a Miniscribe 20 Mb drive. 3 The Mac SE can have a voice coil quantum drive, which is mechanically reliable but may fail to function due to timing track issues, making it unfixable. 4 The MFM drive in a Mac SE is prone to failure and replacement costs around $200. 5 Stepper motor drives in Mac SEs make a distinctive 'COMPUTER THINKING' sound. 6
