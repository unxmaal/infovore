68-pin connector

Claims: 4

Some drives require a 68-pin connector to work, while others work with a 50-pin cable. 1 The external SCSI cable for an HP DB68 drive is the same physical size as the internal 68-pin connector, allowing for a simple external connection if the drive has onboard termination. 2 The 68-pin connector is required for wide SCSI compatibility with SGI systems, and an adaptor exists but may need soldering. 3
