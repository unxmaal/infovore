SBus

Claims: 28 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SBus, SPARCstation 10.

SBus is a standardized expansion bus that debuted on the Sparcstation 1, while Sun3 systems used VME. 1 SBus is more limited, and ZX cards are very rare. 2 The Ultra 10 has a SPARC CPU and uses SBus. 3 The E10k also supports SBus. 4 The E3000/4000/5000 series supports SBus cards and has onboard Ethernet, SCSI, and FiberChannel; at least one of each type is required. 5 Sun encouraged cloning of their hardware, with many companies producing SPARC and SBus clones. 6 The Sbus has version limitations with Solaris, not working with versions above 2.5.1 or 2.6. 7
