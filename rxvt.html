rxvt

Claims: 4

rxvt is used as a component in materm, which has a part ripped from rxvt that works but fails to open another terminal on IRIX due to a pty opening routine issue. 1 In Mrxvt, setting TERM=rxvt is best. 2 The default terminals in IRIX are poor, with xterm and rxvt being better alternatives. 3 xterm in IRIX is problematic, and rxvt from nekoware is suggested as an alternative. 4
