Atari TT

Claims: 10 Part of Atari

The Atari TT was originally designed for UNIX and was a powerful 32-bit machine from that era. 12 It can run Mint, which provides multitasking with freemint, and bash 5 even runs on it. 345 The Atari TT can run native apps via NFS on IRIX, providing high compatibility with a few issues. 6 Its FPU can be overclocked to 48 MHz, increasing float math speed by 250% and potentially terminal performance. 7 The Atari TT supports VME and can be extended with a VME ET4000 graphics card. 8
