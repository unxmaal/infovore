cage

Claims: 5

The cage is a component of the system module that can be affected by rust without impacting the motherboard. 1 The cage acts as a heat spreader for the CPU heatsinks by making contact with them to help distribute heat. 2 The PCI riser is attached to a cage with a metal tab that holds it onto the module cage, and the plastic cover needs to be removed to access it. 3 The Onyx350/RM Tezro has a special cage with a plastic air baffle and a side hole for the remaining blower. 4 The better cage has firm PCB connectors and extra white power connectors for better compatibility and power handling. 5
