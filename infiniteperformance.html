InfinitePerformance

Claims: 5

The InfinitePerformance was an Onyx350 configuration featuring multiple V12 CPUs working together. 1 Onyx350 units with V12 processors inside have slightly different doors and are called 'Infinite Performance'. 2 The InfinitePerformance came with a USB card, and the bulkhead area for PS/2 and extra serial ports is missing, suggesting the USB card replaced it. 3 HyperMesh crashed on an Onyx350 InfinitePerformance, killing both the program and the Xserver with address errors. 4

Sources

  1. The Infinite Performance in the Onyx350 was due to having multiple V12 CPUs working together. (user-24f0, 2024-10-01, exchange 112239, check: uncheckable)
  2. Onyx350 bricks with V12 inside have slightly different doors and are called 'Infinite Performance'. (user-b1da, 2019-11-23, exchange 95796, check: uncheckable)
  3. The InfinitePerformance came with a USB card; the bulkhead area for PS/2 and extra serial ports is missing, suggesting the USB card replaced it. (user-b1da, 2023-03-20, exchange 36798, check: supported)
  4. HyperMesh crashed on an Onyx350 InfinitePerformance, killing both the program and the Xserver with address errors. (user-b1da, 2024-10-18, exchange 49925, check: uncheckable)
