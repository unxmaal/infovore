Celeron G5905

Claims: 3 Part of Celeron

The Celeron G5905 is a low-end dual-core system with 4MB of cache. 1 It can be used in a pfSense router, where it can provide better ping times but slower speeds, and turning on CODEL can make things faster. 2 However, people recommend against using the Celeron G5905 as a router and suggest choosing something else. 3

Sources

  2. pfSense with a Celeron G5905 can provide better ping times but slower speeds, and turning on CODEL can make things faster. (user-5510, 2022-05-29, exchange 207107, check: uncheckable)
  3. People tell me the Celeron G5905 is a bad idea for a router and to get anything else. (user-5510, 2022-05-31, exchange 157115, check: uncheckable)
