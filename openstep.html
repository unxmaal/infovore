OPENSTEP

Claims: 26 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenStep, VirtualBox.

OpenStep is essentially NeXTSTEP 4.0 and later, and is essentially the API and library set, with the desktop environment and applications coming along as demos. 12 OpenStep runs on top of Solaris on SPARC hardware, and SPARC systems ran Solaris, OpenStep, and BSD/Linux. 34 OpenStep might run on P1 hardware, but it is picky about hardware. 5 x86 hardware for OpenStep is similar to optimal hardware for OS/2 Warp 4, including a Socket 7 CPU, ISA soundcard, IDE hard drive, PS/2 keyboard and mouse, and AGP or Matrox graphics. 6 OpenStep specs are available. 7
