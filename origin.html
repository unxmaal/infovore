Origin

Claims: 40

With Octane

The Octane is capable of heavy lifting, but the Origin handles it better. 1 Mashek is still selling high-end O2s and Octanes, along with parts for Origin gear. 2 Modern SGI machines like the O2, Octane, Fuel, and Origin can use the local network without issues. 3 Applications can run on the Origin and export their display to an Octane desktop, which uses Barrier to connect to a regular Linux desktop. 4 The Alias7 renderer gives a bus error on Octane, Origin, and O2 systems. 5 The shoeboxes for the Octane and Origin are incompatible, though XIO boards can be converted between Octane and O2K forms by replacing the latch. 67 Mopar modified Octane graphics boards to fit into Origin systems. 8 The 320 VW and 540 VW were considered less impressive than the Octanes and Origins in terms of prosumer graphics hardware. 9

General

NUMALink is a high-speed interconnect for clustering SGI Origin or Onyx nodes into a single-system-image with low latency, typically 1.6 Gb/s or 3.2 Gb/s. 10 SGI Origin racks had NUMA cables. 11 The SGI Origin is a powerful machine, and rack-mounted Origin/Onyx systems are very powerful and heavy. 1213 Later Origin/Onyx3000 systems can scale from 4 to 1024 CPUs. 14 SGI called their servers 'Challenge' before 'Origin'. 15 The Origin has two serial ports and a console serial port. 16
