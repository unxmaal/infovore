DRI

Claims: 3

The Radeon HD3450 uses the radeon driver but experiences slow performance due to software rendering, indicating issues with DRI and DRI2, possibly related to version mismatches in xorg-xserver-video-ati and libdrm. 1 The system supports an AGP riser and a FireGL card, and may support DRI with further configuration. 2 Building a kernel with default values can require compiling again if essential modules like agpgart and dri are missing. 3
