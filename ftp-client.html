FTP client

Claims: 3 Part of FTP

The FTP client in IRIX does not list files starting with a period, but they can still be accessed via FTP and other clients. 1 IBM i's FTP client lacks standard prompts and uses non-intuitive syntax, possibly due to its non-UNIX nature. 2 The FTP client uses the QGPL library. 3
