68k

Claims: 40

The 68k was a versatile CPU used in many systems in the 80s and 90s. 1 It has divide and multiply instructions, which MIPSIV lacks. 2 FPGA cores are available for hardware-level emulation of the 68k. 3 Both HP and SGI made 68k machines that run UNIX, and SGI dropped 68k support by 1989. 45 Vendors like SGI and Sun switched quickly to their own CPU designs after using 68k, while HP used 68040 for longer. 6 The 68k became complex when an MMU was involved and restartable faults were needed. 7
