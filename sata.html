SATA

Claims: 47 Background: 14 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SATA, NVM Express, PCI-X.

SATA is a serial storage interface that is electrically different from but command-set compatible with SCSI and ATA (PATA/IDE), and it is commonly supported through SCSI-class drivers in Linux because SCSI is a command set protocol. 12345 SATA is generally slower than SCSI and PCI, with a SATA1 link being slower than a pATA interface that integrates a SATA-to-pATA chip. 61 SAS cards generally can also do SATA, though old SAS cards could not, and SAS-1-speed PERCS do not support SATA drives without a disk interposer. 789 Converting between SCSI, IDE, and SATA involves inexpensive adapters, though the transition from SCSI to IDE to SATA is described as unnecessarily complex. 1011 SATA is considered useful on systems such as the Ultra 45, and can be used for VM storage while NVMe is used for OS storage. 1213
