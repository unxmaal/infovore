R5000

Claims: 166 Background: 8 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, Bash (Unix shell), IRIX, R5000, SGI Indy.

With O2

The SGI O2 can use an R5000 or R10000 CPU, with the 300 MHz R5000 and 250 MHz R10000 recommended, and can also run an R4k CPU (untested) or an R5k at 200 MHz with 1 MB cache. 1234 The O2+ either came with an R5000 or had a transplant, and was supposed to use R7000 and R12000 CPUs, but issues with the R7000 led to the use of the 300 MHz R5000 instead. 56 The R5000 in the Indy is not pin-compatible with the R5000 in the O2. 7 The O2 with +AV option is a base R5000 model. 8 An O2 with a 250 MHz R10000 is significantly faster than an O2 with a 300 MHz R5000, due to the improved architecture. 9 The performance range between an 180 MHz R5000 O2 and a 400 MHz R12000 is about 4x. 10 The R10k is the minimum CPU for smooth MAX Impact performance, with an R5000 200 MHz O2 struggling. 11 The O2 with R5000 can handle renders but may be CPU-bound for Mental Ray. 12

With R10000

The R5K is a cut-down version of the R10K that lacks L2 cache, making 180 MHz versions particularly slow. 1314 Both processors use the MIPS4 ISA, but the R5K has an eight-stage pipeline that was reduced to five stages while the R10K did not go much deeper. 1516 The R10K gets much hotter than the R5K and requires a longer PCI riser and a different backplate. 1718 An R5K can be installed on an R10K mainboard with the lower connectors to get two PCI slots. 19 Upgrading from an R5K to an R10K may require a rebuild. 20

With Indy

The SGI Indy can be configured with an R5000 (R5k) processor, and an upgrade to an R5000 CPU is available for some Indys. 2122232425 The first Indy used a 100 MHz R4400 CPU, while 180 MHz models used the R5000. 21 An Indy with an R5k is faster than an Indigo unless doing 3D work, and R5k Indys can handle hardware geometry calculation well. 2627 An Indy with an R5k can run Doom and Quake1, and Blender works on an Indy R5000 for animation and rendering tasks. 2829 The Indy's R5000 with 8-bit graphics can render at approximately 50Mpixels/s without Z-buffer and about 10Mpixels/s with Z-buffer. 30 An Indy with an R5000 should maintain 24-bit graphics capability, and most R5K Indys come with XL24 graphics. 3132 The 24-bit Newport video chipset is recommended for R5K on the Indy. 33 The R5k in an Indy runs IRIX, with IRIX 5.3 with XFS supporting R5000 on the Indy. 3435

With R4000

The R5000 and R4000 processors are 64-bit, and the Indigo2 can be upgraded with new CPU cards, with an idea for a higher-binned R4k or R5k part enabling a 400 MHz R5k. 3637 The XZ requires an R4k or R5k for optimal performance, and includes a geometry engine that is faster than the CPU FP on R4k but slower on R5k. 3839 The R4k cannot run mips4 binaries, which can be a pain, while the R5k can, which is a bonus. 40 The kernel for R4k and R5k is similar but requires differences in cache operations and barrier instructions, with potential workaround/errata. 41

General

The R5000 is a superscalar, Pentium-class, in-order CPU with no dynamic branch prediction and no SIMD, featuring 2-way superscalar integer execution and a lot of TRAM. 424344 The R5000 is backwards compatible with MIPS3 software, which runs on it thanks to MIPS4 being backwards compatible. 45 The R5000 180 MHz was always sold with second-level cache, unlike the R5000 150 MHz which could be sold without it, and the IP22 R5k has secondary cache and 128MB of memory. 4647 The R5000 does not use its secondary cache, which is write-back and not conflictive with main memory. 48 Cisco routers initially used MIPS processors like the RM7000 and R5000, later switching to Broadcom. 49 The R5K is emulated in MAME, though not perfectly, and an installation of IRIX 6.5.19 works with MAME using R5000 emulation but may crash. 5051 The R5000 was codename TRITON and was anemic by 2000, with the R5K already several years out of date. 525354 The R5000 had a midlife upgrade to R5200K, and the R5k can be upgraded to an 800 MHz R7k. 5556 The R5000 without cache is a 24-bit XL running at 150 MHz, and the R4400 is slightly older tech that runs a little hotter. 5758 The R5000 has a 2-way mapped cache, making every load/store a 2-way check instead of 1. 59 The R5000 reads its Xilinx PROM chip via a pin for bootstrap mode bits, and the IP32 PROM version V2.3 executes cleanly with ./gxemul. 6061 The PS2's CPU is a variant of the R5000 with 128-bit matrix extensions, two VUs, and DMA pipelines. 62 The SiCortex SC072 system consists of 12 Linux compute nodes with 6-core, 700MHz R5000-based processors. 63 hinv reports R5000 for RM5200 chips, and R4600 support was never shipped. 64
