Pericom

Claims: 4

Pericom is a PCI-PCI bridge that can be used as a backup when the Mace bridge does not work, alongside options like PLX. 1 The NVMe driver for IRIX uses a PCI-Pcie bridge with Pericom and PLX options, achieving initial success on Octane but facing bus errors. 2 The PLX bridge works fine in IP30, similar to Pericom, and setting the barrier flag on read requests fixes hanging issues with certain drives. 3 The PLX and Pericom PCI to PCI-E adapters are similar, but the Pericom needs a 3.3V power supply, which can be added via a 3.3V regulator to make it work on a 5V PCI motherboard. 4
