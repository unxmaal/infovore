72-pin SIMMs

Claims: 8

72-pin SIMMs are standard memory modules, though some manufacturers produce non-JEDEC-compliant variants. 123 Indy RAM consists of 36-bit wide 72-pin SIMMs (FPM, parity, 70 or 60ns), and compatible RAM is standard rather than proprietary. 1 Several systems use 72-pin SIMMs, including the 660AV, the Sun workstation (with parity), the DX4-100 Overdrive with VLB board, and the T3200SX. 4567 The T5200, by contrast, uses proprietary memory rather than 72-pin SIMMs. 7 Old RAM testers can identify faulty 30- and 72-pin SIMMs as well as SDRAM DIMMs up to 100 MHz. 8
