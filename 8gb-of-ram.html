8GB of RAM

Claims: 7

Several SGI workstations could be configured with up to 8 GB of RAM, including the O350 (which also supported 4 processors and expansion via additional bricks and PCI cards), the Onyx backplane (which held up to 4 CPU boards and 8 GB of RAM with an IO4 required), and the Octane (which could reach 8 GB of RAM in 2000, with third-party modules costing around $9,175 and original SGI modules more so). 123 The Octane 2, equipped with dual 300 MHz V10 CPUs and 8 GB of RAM, sold for its specifications' worth when resold. 4 8 GB of RAM was considered enough for the next 5 years. 5 A 2013 MacBook Air with an i7 and 8 GB of RAM struggled to run Opera under Big Sur. 6

Sources

  1. The O350 can have up to 8 GB of RAM and 4 processors, and can be expanded with additional bricks and PCI cards. (user-2189, 2020-05-05, exchange 8090, check: supported)
  2. The Onyx backplane can hold up to 4 CPU boards and 8GB of RAM, with an IO4 required. (user-0251, 2021-08-09, exchange 19100, check: supported)
  3. An SGI Octane could be configured with up to 8 GB of RAM in 2000, with third-party modules costing around $9,175, while original SGI modules would have been more expensive. (user-3e2d, 2025-09-06, exchange 213010, check: supported)
  4. The Octane 2 with dual 300 MHz V10 CPUs and 8 GB of RAM sold for its specifications' worth when shifted and sold. (user-27ce, 2024-07-30, exchange 210738, check: supported)
  5. 8GB of RAM is enough for the next 5 years (user-b1da, 2020-10-12, exchange 11445, check: supported)
  6. Big Sur on a 2013 MacBook Air with an i7 and 8GB of RAM struggles to run Opera. (user-9c31, 2021-01-07, exchange 13673, check: supported)
