Basilisk II

Claims: 8

Basilisk II is mature and stable for 68k Mac emulation, available as SGI freeware. 1 Basilisk II has been ported to Unix with X11 (Linux i386/x86_64, Solaris 2.5, FreeBSD 3.x, IRIX 6.5), including a port that emulates a Macintosh on IRIX. 23 Basilisk II can emulate an IIci ROM to run SysV.6.1, allowing backup of an emulated install. 4 Blender 2.45 and Basilisk II require n32 binaries. 5 To run certain apps on a Mac emulator, a Quadra 650 on Basilisk II with specific OS updates is needed. 6
