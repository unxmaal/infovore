R210 II

Claims: 5

The R210 II is a quiet, x86 server that runs well on CloudStack with KVM. 1 It is used for hosting backups, alongside R720 servers. 2 The R210 II uses 5-pin fans wired directly to the motherboard with plastic pegs for mounting. 3 It uses a 4-pin power connector, unlike most Dells which use 5 pins. 4 Sandy Bridge servers are cheap; a R210 II with a 4C/8T Xeon, 8GB RAM, and 1TB HDD can be sold for $110. 5
