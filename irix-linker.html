IRIX linker

Claims: 4 Part of IRIX

The IRIX linker cannot find the 'alloca' symbol, even though there is an alloca.h header and GCC compiles it fine. 1 The GNU linker does not understand the -n32 option, while the IRIX linker does. 2 The IRIX linker has known bugs, especially on older systems, and is difficult and can produce broken binaries, as evidenced by struggles with building Mario 64 decompilation. 34
