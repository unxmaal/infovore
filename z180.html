Z180

Claims: 12

The Z180 is basically a Z80 with an MMU, and it has an integrated UART and timers along with extra available peripherals. 123 The Z180 has a 10 ns gate delay compared to the Z80's 3 ns, and a Z80 can run at 20 MHz while the Z180 runs at an unspecified higher frequency. 45 A 33 MHz Z180 can be used, and the processor supports CP/M and is being used for homebrew OS development. 67 Minix can be run on the Z180, and NetBSD runs on a Z180. 89 A homebrew system with a Zilog Z180 CPU at 33 MHz includes 512KB SRAM, YM2203 and TMS5220C sound, a color 640x480 display, a custom keyboard and possibly a mouse, 128K ROM, 512K removable cards for storage, and an 8MB SSD. 10
