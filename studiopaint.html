StudioPaint

Claims: 4

StudioPaint was a 2D and 3D art program available on IRIX, alongside software such as Amazon Paint, Barco, ImageMagick, and the Adobe suite. 1 The original StudioPaint was available for large machines during the 5.3 era. 2 StudioPaint was bundled with early versions of Alias, along with Eclipse, and consisted of COFF binaries requiring SGI 5.3 or earlier. 3
