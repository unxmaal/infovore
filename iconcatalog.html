iconcatalog

Claims: 5

The Icon Catalog requires special icons, desktop files, and a compiler. 1 The software can be launched from IconCatalog without using the runon command. 2 Reinstalling some system packages might be necessary if the icon catalog is corrupted. 3 Installing sgug into your path by default breaks Maya iconcatalog due to differences in the output of /bin/ls and /usr/sgug/bin/ls -Hl; changing 'ls -Hl' to '/bin/ls -Hl' in /usr/lib/filetype/install/aw*.ftr fixes it. 4 iconcatalog has been crashing on the system, along with 4dwm, fm, and clogin. 5
