SS20

Claims: 81 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARCstation 10.

With SS10

The SS10 and SS20 are pizzabox machines from the same generation, and either can be a good choice if you want a machine from that generation. 12 The SS10 has 3 fans facing the CPUs while the SS20 has no fans, and overheating issues are common with these systems, especially in max configuration. 34 Both the SS10 and SS20 require two 501-2840 Fan Cards. 5 Uncached SuperSPARC modules run at the speed of the Mbus, which is 40MHz in the SS10 and 50MHz in the SS20. 6 The SS10 motherboard jumpers configure the SuperSPARC module to run at either 40MHz or 36MHz (newer model) or 40MHz or 33MHz (model 20), with 40MHz recommended unless using very old modules. 7 The SS10 cases are more durable than the SS20 cases, and SM50 modules in the SS10 are underclocked, losing 20% of the clock rate. 8 SM51X modules can be mixed in the SS10 and SS20 but not in the SS6XXMP. 9 The SS10 and SS20 use the same AT&T DBRI chip for audio, with a similar audio chip but a possibly different analog stage that may improve sound quality outside the computer. 1011

General

The Sun SS20 is a 32-bit workstation that uses the ST chip and includes HyperSPARC processors, with a different form factor than other models but physically similar; it can have Ultra RAM which is not recognized by the system. 12131415 It supports SuperSPARC CPUs with large caches, which offer better performance than HyperSPARC CPUs despite having less MHz, and dual SuperSPARC 50 CPUs provide broader compatibility compared to HyperSPARC. 1617 The SS20 has an external SCSI connection and uses SCA drives, and it gets hot due to its quad-processor design and high power density, especially with two 10k SCSI disks. 181920 The HDD fan on an SS20 can have issues and be noisy, and the machine has issues with NVRAM and compat-mode. 2122 The SS20 runs a web server, NextStep works on it, and it should drop into the ok prompt unless it autoboots, testing RAM after the slot probe. 23242526 The SS20 is a well-built machine with durable components, especially the CPU, and is one of Sun's best designs, though it is a furnace whose replacement is not. 2728 The SS20 contains a Creator3D, a printer card, and a Sun Video card, and it uses SCA SCSI for its hard drives while its CDROM drive takes a Molex power connector. 2930 The SS20 can use SCA disks as long as the hole spacing matches and the disk has no protrusions exceeding the depth of the plate, though the outside bracket may be incompatible. 3132 The SS20 can use x86 Solaris drives but should avoid hot devices such as the ZX framebuffer, and it can hold up to 5 frame buffers. 3334 The SS20 with two 150 MHz HyperSPARC processors running NetBSD 9.3 can compress and decompress data using 7-Zip, achieving the speeds and MIPS ratings shown, with MP mode slightly faster than SP mode. 3536 The Sm61 and Sm71 on the same SS20 show similar performance scaling with clock speed, particularly in 7z benchmarks. 37 The SS20 has 30 RAM chips, and graphic corruption might be due to one or two bad chips. 38 The SS20 is the same as Mbus for compatibility and did not have ISDN support. 3940
