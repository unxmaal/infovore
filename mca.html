MCA

Claims: 18

MCA (Micro Channel Architecture) was invented by IBM, which patented it along with the PS/2 line; PCI did not exist at the time, and EISA was developed as a response to MCA. 12 MCA systems can involve resource conflicts that the DOS driver may cause and that MCA cannot arbitrate, and an OS/2 MCA system requires a boot manager. 34 MCA and PCI variants of boards and cards are generally not compatible with each other, though some products were made in both MCA and PCI flavors. 56 MCA appears across a range of hardware, including EISA and MCA controllers, PCI/MCA combo machines, and boards supporting MCA. 78 Some systems combine MCA with other bus technologies, such as VLB and MCA machines, and MCA PCMCIA slots that also support (E)ISA. 910
