Visual Workstation 320

Claims: 6 Part of Visual Workstation

The Visual Workstation 320 is a rare, non-IBM-compatible SGI x86 machine that can be used for rendering models. 1 It is a dual Slot-1 machine with 1GHz Coppermine processors on Slockets, though motherboards with Tualatin support are preferable. 23 It runs models from 3D Studio MAX R3 at 60Hz but performs slowly in Performer with software rendering, indicating rendering bottlenecks even without textures. 4
