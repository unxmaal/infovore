MacPro 5,1

Claims: 8

The MacPro 5,1 can be maxed out with RAM and CPUs, and uses PCIE NVMe disks and a nice GPU. 1 Upgrading a MacPro 5,1 to 10.13 required creating a 10.12 installer, installing 10.12, then running the 10.13 installer to install the firmware update, and finally installing 10.13. 2 The MacPro 5,1 can run Ventura without hardware changes like wifi+bt. 3 The PCI express 4x NVMe carrier only supports one NVMe board in the MacPro 5,1's x16 slot, with the x16 slot intended to support up to four boards but limited by macOS. 4 NVMe booting is super fast on MacPro 5,1 but limited to one drive. 5 A Mac Mini M4 can outperform a Mac Pro 5,1 or 7,1 in many tasks, but the Mac Pro still offers easily replaceable parts, particularly the GPU. 6
