1600SW

Claims: 60 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI 1600SW.

With O2

The 1600SW can be used with an O2 through an adapter card, but not with a PC. 12 The 1600SW is the maximum resolution for an O2 with a digital connection, and it can achieve a higher resolution than the O2 over VGA. 34 The O2 benefits from the screen real estate of the 1600SW, even though an extra monitor takes physical space, and it looks gorgeous with the 1600SW. 56 The O2 and 1600SW won't whine so loud as to cause a headache after 5 minutes, and OLED displays will burn in, though not as badly as CRTs. 7

General

The SGI 1600SW is a 17-inch ultrawide monitor with a native resolution of 1600x1024, and it is the most common LDI monitor. 8910 It is a barebones panel whose signal processing is handled by the Multilink adaptor (MLA) or flat panel adapter (FPA) card, and it uses the LVDS interface. 111213 The 1600SW was SGI's only consumer-facing product, costing $1200 with a Multilink, and it was rebranded as a Mac display. 1014 It can be used with Macs via an adapter card, and an Indy can use a 13W3 to VGA cable. 1516 The monitor can exhibit display issues, including a 'no sync detected' error after a factory reset, psychedelic display issues when no keyboard or mouse is attached, and vertical artifacting. 17181920 Its fluorescent tubes can degrade, producing brownish light and flicker, and Kyocera tubes are a 1:1 replacement. 2122 The 1600SW supports 16 million true colors and can use LVDS panels with appropriate scaling and EDID settings. 2324 The GAL16LV8D-3LJ-C008R01 chip on the 1600SW can be read via serial. 25 Flat panel adapters for the 1600SW are hard to find but can be inexpensive when available. 26 The 1600SW and flat panel adapter can sometimes have issues with certain versions of Effect not finding the 1600x1024 resolution. 27 An Octane can use a 1600SW via a DVI-MultiLink-VGA chain, providing a good experience. 28
