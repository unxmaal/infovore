9600

Claims: 3

The 9600 fares better under OS 9 for 2D usage, as it is not limited to 256 colors and works better for dual-monitor use paired with a 9200/9250 PCI. 1 The 9600 is unstable in general, while the MDD G4 is unstable and janky under OS 9. 2 Running a native SCSI hard drive and not using interleaved RAM improves the 9600's performance, though it is not perfect. 3
