QNX

Claims: 16

QNX is a cooperative multitasking system that can be distributed via QNET. 12 It can run on very small systems, such as a 1.4mb floppy with a GUI, networking stack, and JavaScript-capable web browser, and a well-optimized setup can be quite slim. 34 It has been used in a variety of settings, including a 486 PC for Reuters' news-feed service, a Pentium 166 CNC machine for making scoreboard ad panels, National Semiconductor demo tablets, and dSpace SCALEXIO hardware. 5678 It supports a customizable desktop with customizable window decorations, and applications such as a DOOM port, an application launcher, and other small utilities have been written for it. 91011 QNX appears to have lost some of its coolness before 2010 and does not seem very willing to be open. 1213
