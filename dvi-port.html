DVI port

Claims: 11 Part of DVI Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

Later VPro models replaced the 13w3 port with a DVI port, and the Fuel and Tez VPro models have DVI ports. 12 The Onyx350/Fuel/Tezro VPro cards had a DVI port instead of 13w3, and the DCD adds two more ports, giving a total of three DVI outputs, though the original one might not work. 3 The Apple flashed system has a DVI port that supports 1280x1024 resolution but not 1920x1080. 4 The DM5 board inside the VBOB handles graphics to video out for the Octane v12 with DVI port. 5 Plugging a monitor into the wrong DVI port can cause a Power-On-Diagnostics (POD) screen before loading EFI. 6
