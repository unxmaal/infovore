SPARCstation 10

Claims: 18

The SPARCstation 10 can be fitted with upgraded CPU modules, such as a single 125 MHz HyperSPARC mbus module, and installing a Ross CPU significantly improves performance. 123 A SPARCstation 10 with dual SM71 processors runs Solaris 2.6 well, while another configuration has 64 MB of RAM, a 2 GB hard drive, a 50 MHz SuperSPARC CPU, and runs Solaris 9. 45 The machine uses a Dallas part that can be replaced for $30 on Digikey, and it also uses the ISDN chip for audio. 67 The SPARCstation 10 does not require power on the internal SCSI bus, and it can use a SCSI2SD if supported. 89 The Sun 13W3 cable has a different pinout than SGI, which can cause compatibility issues with the SPARCstation 10. 10 The SPARCstation 10 is considered extremely reliable, and cooling guidelines are based on its behavior. 11
