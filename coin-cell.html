coin cell

Claims: 4

Some systems use a coin cell to power NVRAM, such as certain SGI models and the Blade150, though this applies only to some B100 models and some Blade150 revisions. 12 The Blade150 also contains a timekeeper chip alongside its coin cell. 2 The Dallas RTC could be modified to use a replaceable coin cell. 3

Sources

  1. The coin cell powers the NVRAM on some SGI systems, but not all B100 models. Replacing the NVRAM module or dremeling the board can fix issues. (user-5510, 2022-07-16, exchange 239787, check: uncheckable)
  2. The Blade150 has both a timekeeper chip and a coin cell for NVRAM, but only on some revisions. (user-b7c4, 2023-08-04, exchange 39484, check: uncheckable)
  3. You could mod the Dallas RTC to use a replaceable coin cell. (user-bc18, 2019-08-21, exchange 220755, check: uncheckable)
