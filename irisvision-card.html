IrisVision card

Claims: 4 Part of IrisVision

The IRISVISION card was based on SGI's graphics technology, similar to how 3dfx and NVIDIA later developed their own graphics solutions. 1 The card has an optional z buffer and 24-bit frame buffer, but lacks the geometry engine. 2 It is limited on DOS/Windows but works with later AIX software supporting IrisGL or PHIGS, and although discontinued, it can still be used with specific software. 34
