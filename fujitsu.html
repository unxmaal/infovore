Fujitsu

Claims: 18 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARC.

Fujitsu built its own SPARC systems and continued producing them until recently to support customers still using the platform. 12 SPARC servers were declared dead by Fujitsu and Oracle, though most SPARC innovations now come from Fujitsu, which is considered to have the most interesting SPARC systems. 345 Fujitsu planned a new SPARC lineup for the following year. 6 Fujitsu chose to create the A64FX processor in a different architecture instead of improving its existing SPARC64 designs, pivoting due to Solaris winding down, poor Linux SPARC support, and a shortage of ARM engineers. 78 Fujitsu uses ARM cores. 9 Most hard disk drives are OEM Seagate or Fujitsu, and Fujitsu produced the MBA3300NC, a late 2009-2010 SCA HDD that is quiet and reliable. 1011 Lenovo acquired the former Fujitsu laptop division. 12
