The a-Series case was made by Olivetti, but the motherboard by DEC. 1 Some motherboards require maintenance such as recapping (IIsi) or replacement (IIci, T4-1). 234 Reflowing a motherboard can work but is risky and often damages the board, while modern motherboards are designed to withstand reflow processes though home reflowing can fail due to improper technique or component issues. 56 Some motherboards can power on without a CPU but may fail to power off or function properly. 7 The motherboard of the modern PC does not support PCI, only PCIe. 8 PSUs can fail, potentially damaging the motherboard, so PSU health should be ensured to avoid damage. 9 Several SGI systems have documented motherboard failure issues, including the Blade2000 and IPC, where an unknown keyboard problem on the IPC was resolved by replacing the motherboard. 1011 The O2 motherboard is difficult to separate from its metal backplate, and the plastics are brittle and have not aged well, so the system should be unplugged from the mains and left for ten minutes before removing the motherboard or AV modules. 121314 The O2 motherboard can produce a slightly cooked video output that manifests as excessive green, possibly due to issues with a bridge pack between the ports and the motherboard. 15 The official Dell warranty fix for the 'initializing CPLD' issue was to replace the entire motherboard, and it affected multiple systems. 16 A G5 Quad's power supply or motherboard can cause issues, and the AT Gateway case may be usable but the motherboard likely needs a battery replacement. 1718 The SGI230 motherboard needs capacitor replacement, and the TDZ2000 and Challenge2000 require specific drivers and terminal cables. 19 The IP14+ motherboard is similar in shape to the IP12 and IP12+ boards but has socketed chips that the IP12 and IP12+ do not, and it might be a modified IP14. 20 Newer motherboard RAM is incompatible with older Octane motherboards, and NICs can be replaced or transferred between Octanes when upgrading or replacing the motherboard while keeping software licenses. 2122 The custom Powervr set linked is AGP and includes a motherboard for $1000. 23 A system that fails motherboard, PCI cage, or XIO cards, ensure the system is off and unplugged to avoid damaging the motherboard. 26 The +12V power connector on the motherboard goes to J502 on the CPU module, and potentially causing issues. 28 The IIcx motherboard recapping is straightforward. 29
