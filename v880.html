V880

Claims: 20

The Sun V880 is a large, heavy server that can have up to eight CPUs and 64GB of RAM, making it unsuitable for living rooms. 12 The V880 and V890 differ in their power supplies, IO board, and Fireplane, though the Fireplane is identical between the US-III/IV/IV+ CPU modules. 345 The V880 motherboard can support UltraSPARC IV+ with a specific part, but this combination was not shipped, and the V880 can also take an XVR-500 PCI card. 67 The V880 can blow a transformer, uses a simple MOSFET shifter for power management, and had a known failure of the SSC050 I²C backplane controller that can be fixed by soldering in a replacement. 891011 The V880 is loud, getting quieter after booting but still remaining loud, similar to the T1000's experience. 1213
