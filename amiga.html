Amiga

Claims: 224 Background: 44 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Amiga, Quake (video game), LightWave 3D, Atari ST, Blender (software), Commodore 64, Emulator, IRIX, Vim (text editor).

The Amiga ran its own Workbench OS and was considered the best computer for gaming in 1987 by a significant margin, making it ideal for games. 123 The Amiga could do rudimentary 3D graphics two years after the NES and ran LightWave 5 for 3D furry animations. 45 The Babylon 5 season 1 CGI was done using Lightwave networked between Amigas, with scenes designed on Amigas and rendered on MIPS systems running NT and Lightwave, eventually switching to SGI machines. 6 Amiga systems require PAL compatibility for full functionality, with no exceptions, and running in NTSC mode vs PAL mode generates different resolutions and Hz rates, making online guides and settings machine-specific. 78 The Amiga has a complex and expensive RAM setup, with multiple types of RAM that can be upgraded, and Amiga is an expensive hobby. 910 Amiga systems have a special slot that exposes the internal bus for expansion, and GVP made a run of SIMMs for Amigas. 1112 The Amiga can recognize and use SCSI drives regardless of the manufacturer, but Mac-formatted partitions may not be recognized by default, and the scsi2sd v5 is finicky on Amiga and the multidevice feature benefits hardware that can't address larger drives. 1314 Running Unix on an Amiga is straightforward by extracting the bootloader to the DOS partition and placing the kernel in the 'auto' folder, and early Amiga, Atari, and Macintosh 68K machines could have run *nix with FPU and MMUs by default. 1516 The Amiga is a line of m68k-based personal computers. 1718 The original Amiga had a 68000 CPU, 1MB of RAM, and an operating system stored in ROM. 19 Professional models such as the A2000, A3000, and A4000 were better suited as workstations, while home editions like the A500, A600, and A1200 were used for other purposes. 20 The Amiga was primarily used as a gaming machine in the UK, unlike in the US where consoles were more popular. 21 The Amiga had a color palette of 4096 colors at once in 1985, though most original hardware could only display up to 640x400 in 8 colors. 2223 The Amiga can run software such as Test Drive and Elite. 24 The Amiga has unique custom hardware. 25 Amiga machines have risen in price due to increased exposure from tech youtubers and growing demand, leading to higher prices on eBay. 26 The Amiga was a fully bespoke piece of hardware, not just a graphics board stapled to a Commodore 64. 27 When it came out in 1987, Commodore's A2000 was competitive with Apple's Mac Plus and IBM's PC-AT, and the platform was the best of its day but didn't evolve as much as it needed. 2829 It has vibrant demoscene communities and some of the best games of the era, but it was weak for gaming while strong for desktop publishing and music. 303132 The Amiga requires specific hardware add-ons for Ethernet, USB, RAM, and audio, and its daily driver potential declined with the need for expensive upgrades and old drive interfaces. 3334 It never caught on big in North America, making it hard to find, and it didn't lift off with the PPC transition as the company went bankrupt and people switched to PC and Mac. 3536 There is still a die-hard group of Amiga people who daily drive them. 37 The Amiga was originally a company that made games and peripherals for game consoles before being bought by Commodore. 38 The Amiga was primarily viewed as a games console with a keyboard, though it was also used for work and music by some. 39 It was designed for 2D games with scrolling backgrounds and sprites, excelling at that but becoming less relevant for 3D games. 40 The Amiga's custom chipset operates agnostically with the CPU as long as it receives expected data, making accurate emulation as good as the original. 41 The Amiga can use a 68060 CPU, and Quake runs well on a BFG9060 with a 100MHz 060 because its faster RAM access is what Quake needs. 4243 The PiStorm can be used in an Amiga with CPU expansion slots, supporting a 1200 with a 100MHz 060 CPU, and running Quake on it is technically still an Amiga. 4445 Lightwave was used for VFX in TV shows and films, especially on Amiga, but switched to WindowsNT on x86 machines for faster rendering. 46 Traces, which became Blender, started on the Amiga in the early 90s and was renamed by 1995. 4748 The Amiga is a computer platform that supports a variety of hardware add-ons and software. 4950515253545556 A PiStorm runs 68k code and uses a Pi only for some utility features, and is still considered a real Amiga. 49 Amiga and Mac systems can support PCI cards, even on 68k systems like the Amiga 3000 with a community-designed board, and the Amiga supports Voodoo boards with PCI. 5051 The Video Toaster 4000 works in any big box Amiga with a video slot, supporting AGA wipes and being backwards compatible with OCS. 52 A third party addon board can convert a CD32 into a regular Amiga and provides a 68030 accelerator. 53 On Amigas, a Gotek drive can boot from the HDD by default if no floppy is 'inserted', and this can be
