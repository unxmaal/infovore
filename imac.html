iMac

Claims: 22 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see IMac.

The iMac is a classic design considered the best budget computer of all time, with the G3 model praised as a good system that has good speakers. 12 The iMac uses a G3 processor running at 500 MHz, the same speed as the user's iMac. 3 The iMac is not as expandable as the Molar Mac, and 8 GB of RAM was considered low for an iMac in 2013. 45 The iMac supports USB drives, though a replacement drive may not fit the original cover. 67 Power6 would not have solved many Apple issues, as it was a server processor and not something Apple could drop into an iMac. 8 SGI's T21 was an iMac-like product. 9
