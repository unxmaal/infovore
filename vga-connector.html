VGA connector

Claims: 6 Part of VGA

The VGA connector has separate r/g/b shield pins which are not labeled as ground but as shield or return, indicating they are part of the signal reference rather than earth ground. 1 The Personal IRIS uses SoG for R, G, and B and has a VGA connector. 2 The VGA connector is more user-friendly than 13w3. 3 A broken connection on the VGA connector can cause issues, and connecting a cable to the 13W3 port might resolve it. 4
