XIO board

Claims: 12 Part of XIO Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The XIO board for PCI-X support is only mentioned for Altix systems and not confirmed for Onyx2. 1 Octanes require multiple reseats of the main and xio boards to function. 2 The stock XIO board on an Octane is 1Gbps, while a QLA2342 card can support 2Gbps, but the Octane's PCI-X bus may run at half speed due to its 66MHz bus. 3 The frontplane connector on the XIO board has flat pads similar to component contacts and can be cleaned with compressed air or alcohol. 4 The non-door plastics on I1's and Octanes are generally fine, though XIO board handles on some Octanes can get damaged due to their protruding nature. 5 The PCI cage for SGI systems comes in two varieties, with the newer one using a ribbon cable for the XIO board and the older one using fiberglass. 6 HIPPI4.0 software is for the XIO board and may not include drivers for the EC PCI board. 7
