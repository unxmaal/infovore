ZX

Claims: 28 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARCstation 10.

The ZX is an SBus graphics card, also known as user-5510, that is often associated with XGL cards and was designed by Sun. 123 It was released about a year after the Sun Indy and supported systems such as the SS1/2, and sometimes the 5/10/20, U1, and U2, but not the SS4. 24 The ZX can reach a resolution of 1280x1024x24-bit and displays multiple 8-bit palettes simultaneously with a 24-bit desktop, a feature that required custom logic in the ASICs. 56 It generates a lot of heat and benefits from a fan board, and can cause overheating issues in some systems such as the SS10 or SS20. 789 The ZX runs better with X than on the console, supports 24-bit color, and can run colormap software, though it will not run NeXTSTEP. 1011 It supported SunPHIGS and potentially XGL, with OpenGL added later, but XGL is not supported on Solaris 2.8 after version 2-02. 1213
