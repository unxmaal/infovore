M88k

Claims: 6

The M88k was a high-performance RISC processor that failed to gain widespread adoption despite initial support from multiple companies, similar to Itanium. 1 The first RISC CPU available for Mac was the M88k, but it could not run Mac OS. 2 The M88k did not have a built-in 68k emulator or backwards compatibility hardware. 3 The Omron Luna represented the peak of the m88k architecture, with the AViiON being a different class of machine. 4
