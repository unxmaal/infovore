MIPS R10000

Claims: 6 Part of MIPS

The MIPS R10000 is an out-of-order architecture that can execute one instruction per clock and one floating-point instruction per clock. 12 A computer running IRIX 6.5.22 used a MIPS R10000 CPU at 194 MHz. 3 NetBSD 11.99.4 runs on a MIPS R10000 CPU with 64 TLB entries and a 16MB max page size. 4 The MIPS R10000 is one of the visually impressive computer chips, alongside the MIPS R3000, R5000, and DEC Alpha. 5 The RIGC processor runs much cooler than the MIPS R10000. 6
