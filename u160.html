U160

Claims: 9

U160 is a SCSI bus speed that can be reached with U160 or U320 disks attached via a SCA80 to 50pin SCSI adapter, which is recommended for cheap storage. 1 Replacing 40MB SCSI controllers with U160 or U320 controllers would significantly increase disk speed. 2 Most U160 and U320 cards support LVD or SE, but typically use SE for Fast/Wide modes and below. 3 An U160 SCSI bus exceeds PATA's 133MB/s, making an U320 Acard limited to 133MB/s on a U160 bus unnecessary. 4 Attaching a U160 device to a U320 bus can slow down the entire bus to U160 speed, but U320 and U160 can coexist without a speed penalty. 56
