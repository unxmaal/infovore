Weitek

Claims: 4

The uCode words use a 40-bit format in the Weitek, addressed as low word/high byte, with the actual data bus consisting of 10x 4-bit SRAM chips. 1 The Iris Vision uses HQ1 chips for instruction fetching and Weitek 32-bit floating point data paths. 2 The SPARC PowerUP from Weitek could be installed in SS2 and IPX IPCs, but it did not significantly improve performance. 3 The Weitek SPARC PowerUP runs at double the internal speed but maintains the same external bus speed. 4
