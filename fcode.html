FCode

Claims: 4

FCode is a firmware language supported by both OpenBoot and OpenFirmware, which is based on OpenBoot. 12 OpenBoot firmware uses FCode for device drivers, with four manuals released—three by user-5510 and one by Firmworks—and it is a loose standard that varies by hardware vendor. 1 FCode for user-5510 OpenBoot and Apple Open Firmware should be the same, but hardware vendors' implementations can differ, preventing compatibility. 3 FCode has CPU ISA-agnosticness limitations due to incompatibilities between Apple Open Firmware and OpenBoot. 4
