4D/70 GT

Claims: 12

The 4D/70 GT requires two RMs, and working RM1 boards are hard to find; it crashes when loading X11 or NeWS due to a VME interrupt error from wrong jumpering. 1 The 4D/70 GT started life as a 4D/70 'G' with purple top hats, and it can be run as a 4D/50 with an IP4 at 8 MHz. 23 The 4D/70 GT and 4D/50 boards use the same PROM, which can be found at https://www.vdheijden-messerli.net/sgistuff/nekochan/prom/IP4_Professional_IRIS/. 45 BetXen from Switzerland donated a 4D/70 GT to the Musée Bolo in Lausanne, Switzerland, which may not be on display. 6 The working 4D/70 GT will be moved into a new chassis with a perfect PSU, leaving the original chassis with dead internals. 7
