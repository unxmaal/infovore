bad sectors

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Hard disk drive.

Bad sectors can be marked by adjusting retry settings or by marking blocks of sectors, though a drive with many bad sectors may be better replaced. 1 Nearly all modern disks reallocate bad sectors on write, and there is an advanced setting to change these parameters, but using it is not recommended. 2 A hard drive with slowly developing bad sectors can cause software to fail intermittently. 3 Running a full sequential test and stopping on errors can add bad sectors. 4
