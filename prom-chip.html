PROM chip

Claims: 4 Part of PROM

The PROM chip is located on the bottom of the mainboard, and desoldering it allows for modifying the ARCS firmware. 1 A socket that fits the PROM chip's footprint would allow switching between RM5271 and RM7900 chips. 2 The boot strap mode string may be found in a PROM chip, which could be useful for SGI systems. 3
