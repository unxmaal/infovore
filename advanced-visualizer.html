Advanced Visualizer

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Wavefront Technologies.

The Advanced Visualizer, known as Wavefront, had versions from 1985/86 to 1997, with the last known version being 4.3.2. 1 It is used for modeling, animating, and rendering with Wavefront's classic Model-Preview-Image modules, and has basic tools for navigation and rendering. 2 Older users often use non-obvious names for software, such as 'wavefront' and 'alias' instead of 'advanced visualizer' or 'power animator'. 3
