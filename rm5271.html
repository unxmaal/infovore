RM5271

Claims: 11

The RM5271 is the real name of the r5k 300MHz module. 1 The RM7000 and RM5271 are 99.7% pin compatible, differing only in one cache-related pin and the extra DNC pins on the SR71010A. 2 A socket that fits the PROM chip's footprint would allow switching between RM5271 and RM7900 chips. 3 The daughtercards are mostly the same, and the last revision is even called the RM series, supporting both RM5271 and RM7000A. 4 The RM5271 can have 512kB, 1MB, 2MB, or 4MB secondary cache, depending on the PROM configuration, and the PROM emulator can set the fractional multiplier on the RM5271. 56 The RM5271 has quirks similar to the RM7000, and its cache ICs are QFP packages that can be hard to find. 78
