archive.org

Claims: 26

archive.org hosts a variety of old software and documentation, including Irix software (Irix 4.0.x demos, IRIX 6.5.17, and the IRIX version of AutoCAD R13), Solaris patches, Red Hat Enterprise Linux 7 (a single DVD), SLES 9 SP2 and SP3, SLES 9 RC5, Junos OS images, INTRALINK (2001 UNIX version), and CHDs for SGI_mips. 1234567891011 The site is a main source for loose SGI files and old SGI documentation, and it contains a backup of reality.sgi.com. 121314 Archive.org does not provide easy-to-use non-scraper compressed packs, its tooling is slow and requires manual retries, and zipped files and torrents work better than direct downloads. 151617 To find SGI files, use the second search bar on archive.org's homepage, and to verify the authenticity of a Windows ISO, use SHA1 or SHA256 hashes from trusted sources like Archive.org. 1819
