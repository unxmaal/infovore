DB9

Claims: 11

The DB25 port is not standard across all computers, as seen with the I2 and other systems using DB9. 1 A gender changer is used to change a DB25 connector to DB9, allowing a null modem adapter to connect. 2 The DB25 port on the Ultra5 is used for the PROM, not the DB9 port. 3 DB25 -> DB9 -> gender changer -> USB is a method to connect to the Ultra5 for diagnostics. 4 The DB25 to DB9 adapter is likely a straight through adapter. 5 SGI offers official MMJ adapters for DB9 and DB25 connections, and also provides an official RJ45 adapter. 6 The dual side of the DB9 connector should go to the workstation. 7 The DB9 pinout for the unpopulated connector on the PCB is the same as the SGI impact port, with stereo sync on 1, GND on 6 and 7, and +12v on pin 8. 8
