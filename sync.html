sync

Claims: 3

On SCSI-1, asynchronous transfer ran at about 3MB/s while synchronous transfer ran at around 5MB/s. 1 In all SCSI protocols, the async protocol roughly doubles the bandwidth compared to sync. 2 An adapter cable can cause sync issues on an Indy but not on an Octane, and not all monitors support sync on green. 3
