TI S1500

Claims: 3 Part of TI

Some machines, like the TI S1500, DG AViiON, and NEC EWS4800, require a programmed NVRAM that matches the hardware serial number to boot, even if the NVRAM is fully programmed. 1 The TI S1500 has a licensing card in an expansion slot, and without it, the system boots to single user mode where new licensing information can be entered. 2 The MUX cards in TI s1500 were more popular than the network controller. 3
