resetenv

Claims: 7

resetenv resets PROM variables to their defaults, not the root password. 1 It can be run in the PROM to reset environment variables, and running it might help with missing password jumper issues. 2 To preserve PROM settings, one should print them with 'printenv' and reset them with 'resetenv'. 3 However, resetenv does not provide a usable set of NVRAM contents, and setting certain variables like 'root' does not prevent the system from hanging during initialization. 4 Checksum errors to the NVRAM were not resolved by enableall/resetenv, and when changing the RTC chip on an O2, a resetenv and bypass jumper may not resolve boot issues, requiring a NVRAM dump from a functioning O2 to diagnose the problem. 56
