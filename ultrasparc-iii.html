UltraSPARC III

Claims: 24 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see UltraSPARC III.

Sun engineers considered SPARC's raw performance lacking when the UltraSPARC III competed with the Pentium 4 and early Opteron. 1 The UltraSPARC III was considered comparable to the Merced (Itanium) CPU for its intended 64-bit purpose, and an 800MHz Merced Itanium was considered worse than a traditional RISC like the UltraSPARC III. 23 UltraSPARC III machines were preferred over the UltraSPARC T2 due to better performance, and the UltraSPARC III is now considered to be looking good. 45 The UltraSPARC III was used in systems such as the Bullfrog laptop, which ran two UltraSPARC IIIIs at 1.28 GHz, and the 480, which had four UltraSPARC III CPUs at 900 MHz. 67 Solaris 9 can run on a 512MB UltraSPARC III, and the feckin *libc* version installed by the GNOME background tool requires an UltraSPARC III. 89 Tribblix runs on UltraSPARC III and newer models. 10
