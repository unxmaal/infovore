Stereo glasses

Claims: 3

Stereo glasses were used for data visualization on SGI Octane and other models, not for cinematics. 1 The presenter module is also used for the Stereo Glasses, and some demos have built-in functionality, such as PowerFlip. 2 Stereo glasses can be powered by the Powered Peripheral port, though they typically use a separate adapter. 3
