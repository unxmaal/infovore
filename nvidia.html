Nvidia

Claims: 68 Background: 16 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Nvidia, Silicon Graphics, Wayland (protocol), AMD.

Nvidia is a member of the RISC-V organization, which could be a major game changer due to the importance of GPUs in modern computing, though if Nvidia makes ARM licensing terms restrictive, RISC-V has only a slim chance to achieve relative parity or a niche market role, as restrictive licensing is considered a given. 123 Nvidia has never truly supported open sourcing its drivers, releasing binary blobs and rarely releasing specs on older hardware, unlike AMD, and its proprietary drivers are not as open to development of open source alternatives as AMD's. 45 Nvidia introduced NVLink to connect multiple GPUs at 10X the PCIe bandwidth, enabling 8 GPUs in a single server to be connected in a point-to-point network called a hybrid cube mesh, and the DGX station uses Tesla V100 GPUs with 4x NVLink, which Nvidia does not sell separately. 67 CUDA is exclusive to NVIDIA GPUs and is in high demand for mining, and Nvidia's CUDA support outperforms AMD's OpenCL support, even with optimization, as Nvidia made CUDA simple for physicists to use while OpenCL is more complex. 8910 Nvidia cards generally outperform AMD cards in games and compute tasks, though the RDNA 2 GPU has noticeably worse VR performance than Ampere, so some users stick with NVIDIA. 1112 Nvidia still develops graphical drivers for Solaris, including x64 Quadro drivers due to NASA's use, and provided IA64 drivers for their FX series GPUs. 131415 GPU passthrough for multiple VMs on a single GPU requires specific configurations that many cards do not support, and Nvidia may block it on certain models, though Nvidia recently removed the code 43 error that would appear when passing through a GeForce. 1617 Only GTX/RTX cards don't play nice with passthrough, as it has to be Quadro, Tesla, or Grid, and CentOS 8 can be used as a base for GPU passthrough with KVM, though Nvidia may require blacklisting consumer GPUs. 1819 SGI lost graphics talent to Nvidia, which gave Nvidia a competitive edge, as SGI's graphics team went to Nvidia in the early 2000s after a settlement and transferred their graphics staff as a result of their decline. 2021222324 SGI's business model was not set up to produce graphics cards like Nvidia, leading to a missed opportunity, and if SGI had become Nvidia they might have cannibalized their own high-end graphics business and lowered costs for consumers. 2526 Nvidia cards outperformed ATI cards in geometry by that time, and Nvidia cards require firmware blobs not available for ia64, while Java 1.6 and possibly 7 were the last versions to work on ia64. 2728 Nvidia is moving away from monolithic GPUs and is making the cooler product, though Nvidia cards are power-hungry, with a 5090 card consuming 500-600 watts. 293031 OpenPOWER's partnership with Nvidia for supercomputer contracts did not materialize as expected, and Nvidia stayed out of that until only a few years ago. 3233
