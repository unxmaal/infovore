VPro graphics

Claims: 15 Part of VPro Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

VPro graphics can be installed in older Octanes as long as the Octane has the proper power supply and Xbow (crossbar switch) revision, including Xbow 1.4. 123 Cherokee power supplies are higher wattage and can run VPro graphics; VPro V6 and V8 require a Cherokee power supply. 45 The VPro graphics for Octane had fundamental bugs that required a new chip design to fix, due to implementing a theory without proper clipping, recursive rasterization, and Hilbert space memory organization. 6 The VPro graphics had issues with polygons close to W=0 after perspective correction, leading to performance degradation and visual glitches. 7 The Tezro runs the second revision of VPro graphics, which runs at double the clock speed of the first gen VPro and is on a 1Ghz node board. 8 DCD options are available for VPro graphics. 9 Setting up IRIX software can be difficult, especially on Octane systems with VPro graphics; not having MGRAS can prevent software from installing. 10 The IRIX Software Manager allows installing Octane/Impact Compression for IRIX 6.5 software in spite of conflicts with VPro graphics using the 'rulesoverride' setting. 11
