ZuluSCSI Wide

Claims: 10 Part of ZuluSCSI

ZuluSCSI Wide is a SCSI controller for vintage SGI systems that has not yet hit production. 1 It can be used with the O2 or Octane via their rear-facing external SCSI port. 2 It can push 33MB/sec on reads, and new 15K SCSI drives for the Cheetah can achieve 33MB/s with ZuluSCSI Wide for around 20-25 each. 34 Its performance can be improved with a fast SD card and the official formatter. 5 On an Indy with 64MB of RAM running IRIX 6.5.22, its performance can be severely impacted, making the system borderline usable. 6 The ZuluSCSI Wide, ZuluSCSI Wide SCA, and Blaster SCSI drives work fine in an O2. 7
