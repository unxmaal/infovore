clogin

Claims: 3

clogin is a process that handles icons in IRIX format. 1 A JIT compiler miscompiling instructions caused processes including clogin to get stuck sleeping and unkillable, which was resolved by fixing 512K aliasing for lowmem. 2 clogin has also crashed on the system, alongside 4dwm, fm, and iconcatalog. 3

Sources

  1. The icons are in IRIX format, similar to how Clogin handles them. (user-b03b, 2026-09-04, exchange 214573, check: uncheckable)
  2. The JIT compiler was miscompiling instructions, leading to issues with processes like xdm, 4Dwm, and clogin getting stuck sleeping and unkillable, which was resolved by fixing 512K aliasing for lowmem. (user-3e2d, 2026-09-12, exchange 220688, check: supported)
  3. 4dwm, fm, iconcatalog, and clogin have been crashing on the system, with fm producing a corefile and one process generating a trap that led to its termination to prevent an infinite loop. (user-7b5f, 2026-09-12, exchange 220690, check: supported)
