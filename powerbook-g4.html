PowerBook G4

Claims: 20

The PowerBook G4 is a well-built and tough machine, and a functional 17-inch (A1139) model can be acquired. 123 It was Apple's premium laptop, more expensive than the iBook, and can be used for paying work such as spreadsheet and C programming tasks. 45 It can run a knock-off of IRIX's Electropaint, and Sorbet is significantly better than MorphOS for daily use. 67 Installing Classic was difficult, and Apple does not want Mac OS 9 on these machines. 6 The PowerBook G4 requires a hard drive, and it can have a SCSI drive that isn't detected due to formatting issues, requiring Lido for formatting. 18 Keylocks on vintage systems were for user convenience rather than security and could be bypassed by those who knew the system. 9 PowerBook G4 keyboards often have missing or damaged keys, and a bad 74LS32 chip can cause self-test failures, for which a replacement from ReActiveMicro is available. 1011 Forcing a power-off requires holding the power button long enough, and for older models the key combo is Shift-Function-Control-Power. 12
