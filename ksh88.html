ksh88

Claims: 3 Part of ksh

ksh88 can be installed on a host PC. 1 mksh is 100% compatible with ksh88, is faster and more actively developed, and fixes some bugs such as color processing. 2 On IRIX 6.5.30, /sbin/sh is ksh88 in bourne compatibility mode, which behaves differently from ksh and rksh when invoked as sh; setting _XPG to 1 fixes the issue, and mksh is being used as an alternative. 3
