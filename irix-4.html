IRIX 4

Claims: 74 Part of IRIX Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX.

IRIX 4 was the first version of IRIX and was based on System V Release 3, as were IRIX 3 and IRIX 4 (with IRIX 5 and newer moving to System V Release 4). 123 The main change from IRIX 3 to 4 was that X11 became the default desktop, and GLX did not exist until IRIX 4. 45 IRIX 4 is 32-bit only, predates 64-bit designs, and is limited to 256MB of RAM, while IRIX 5.x increased the limit to 2GB. 67 On IRIX 4, time_t, const long, and int are 32-bit, and the system date will wrap back to 1/1/70 in 2038. 8 IRIX 4 can be run on the Indigo2, and support advantage CDs with various software titles were available, including Hotmix, Silicon Junction Unplugged, Desktop Special Edition with SoftWindows, Iris Explorer, IDO versions 4.0.1 and 4.11, and NFS for Irix 5.3. 910 The Extreme boardset supports IRIX 4. 11 IRIX 4 is the minimum operating system supported by the Indigo. 12 The desktop interface of IRIX 4 is similar to that of 4sight. 13 IRIX 4 can run with 16 MB of RAM, though 32 MB was preferred for better performance. 14 IRIX 4 is considered the best OS for Indigo to be snappy and period correct, though it lacks software. 15 Hotmix CDs started with IRIX 4, offering a reasonable amount of software. 1617 Alias and possibly SoftImage were available for IRIX 4. 18 FDDIXpress was a separate disc for IRIX 4 and was included in OS discs since IRIX 5. 19
