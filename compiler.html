compiler

Claims: 38 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Compiler.

A compiler is a program that translates code, and compilers can be complex and challenging to develop. 1 Writing your own compiler ensures you know all its bugs. 2 Compilers can be used for cross-compilation, such as the SGI compiler built on a different machine. 3 The compiler can encounter errors, such as running out of memory during global optimization in sha512.c. 4 The compiler may not detect certain type constraints at compile time, leading to runtime errors. 5 The compiler can be customized with flags, and benchmark results depend on the compiler used. 67
