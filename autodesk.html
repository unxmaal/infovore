Autodesk

Claims: 17 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Autodesk Alias.

Autodesk has acquired several companies, including Discreet Logic, Softimage, Wavefront, and SGI, and integrated their products into its software lineup. 1234 Autodesk acquired Softimage, which had the ICE system, and integrated it into Maya. 2 Alias was originally made, then bought out Wavefront, then SGI, then Autodesk. 3 Autodesk no longer holds Wavefront's IP as they were destroyed upon acquisition, with focus on Maya. 5 Autodesk's updates for Max and Maya are small and focused on licensing fees rather than innovation. 6 Autodesk ceases support and license issuances for software older than 3 years. 7 Work has donated to Blender for years due to Autodesk's licensing changes and the need for alternatives. 8 Autodesk closed its Neuchatel office, leading to a riot. 9
