ACARD adapter

Claims: 4 Part of ACARD

  • ACard adapters can achieve 40MB/s SCSI speed. (user-7fd9, exchange 222908, 2023-03-10; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The ACARD adapter allows a modern slot load serial ATA optical drive to connect to a 50-pin SCSI interface, fitting in the floppy drive slot with some cabling. (user-2a3c, exchange 227003, 2026-09-09; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The Acard adapter can connect 68-pin U2 to IDE, allowing IDE drives to be used on the same bus as U2 HDDs without a speed penalty, but some models have issues with CD-ROM audio. (user-22f9, exchange 112544, 2024-10-25; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Some users installed SSDs in Octane2 systems using ACARD adapters. (user-6afa, exchange 206093, 2021-12-17; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
