Ultra 1

Claims: 35 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Ultra 1.

The Ultra 1 is a SPARC-based workstation that uses standard SCSI drives and can run Solaris 2.5, 2.6, RedHat 4.2, and Tribblix. 123456 It requires a hard drive, which can be a half-height drive (though it may produce a noisy whine), and can use disks ranging from 2GB to 9GB. 718910 The Ultra 1 can be booted with a prom/ROM update from a Solaris 7 install CD-ROM, which offers the upgrade during first boot if a 64-bit compatible ROM is not detected. 1112 It can reconfigure its ports using jumpers or software, and its NVRAM uses the ST M48t59y chip. 1314 The Creator model Ultra 1 has a UPA slot, wide SCSI, and a better CPU, while the standard mainboard is SBus only with no UPA slot. 1516 The fastest supported graphics card in an Ultra 1 is a Creator3d series2, and a 13W3 connector issue can cause color loss. 1718 The Ultra 1 was disappointing and less common than the SS20, which runs MAE significantly faster, and it may have a CPU fan depending on its speed. 19202122
