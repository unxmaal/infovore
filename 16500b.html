16500B

Claims: 6

The HP 16500B logic analyzer runs HP-UX on a 68ec020 CPU and boots HP/UX, allowing remote control over the network. 12 The original hard drive, likely a 160MB Quantum IDE drive, does not work and can be replaced with a CF card, while a replacement PSU is a different revision, complicating troubleshooting. 34 The power supply can fail due to overcurrent protection tripping, possibly from leaky capacitors in the output voltage/current sensing circuit. 5 The fan layout is designed to cool a dense chassis, making the unit noisy. 6
