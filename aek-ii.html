AEK II

Claims: 4

The AEK II requires flashing the Pro Micro to work over USB. 1 ROM03 has 1.25MB RAM and a user-replaceable battery, and it supports keyboard LEDs on AEK II. 2 AEK II keyboards use the same switch as SGI keyboards, while AEK I uses a better switch. 3 The original AEK had two versions (orange and salmon), and the AEK II had four versions. 4
