Informix

Claims: 8

Informix ran on Solaris, where it was rock solid and fast, and it could also run on the E3000, where it outperformed newer Compaq+Win2k3 machines with 4x 400 MHz CPUs. 12 Informix from 1997-2008 did not support 64-bit addressing, requiring 2GB extents for disks larger than 2GB, and it could be run on a raw disk. 34 Informix was used as the database backend for Entrust PKI, but was prone to crashes that were easier to recover from by reinstalling the OS. 5 Informix on Solaris and IRIX experienced issues primarily due to user errors, such as failed log backups. 6
