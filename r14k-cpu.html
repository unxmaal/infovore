R14k CPU

Claims: 8 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The R14k CPU was available in 550 MHz and 600 MHz versions, with the fastest MIPS CPU in an Octane being a dual 600 MHz R14k. 12 The R14k CPU is used in the Octane 2 and heavily upgraded later Octanes. 3 The R14k CPU in the Octane 2 uses large heatsinks, possibly due to high air flow or a smaller process shrink. 4 The R14k design focused on high CPU density, consuming 17W. 5 A PVO card stops working at 1.5 GB of RAM or with an R14k CPU, and the compression card likely has the same issue. 6
