emulation

Claims: 60 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Hardware emulation.

Emulation simulates hardware, whereas virtualization passes hardware through, and semi-virtualization examples like Mac emulators for Amiga trapped certain instructions. 1 Emulation is inherently slow due to the speed difference between software and hardware operations, and there is a lot of overhead in that emulation. 23 Emulation progress is advancing, with more modes being discovered based on software usage, and you can already emulate plenty of machines faster than real time. 45 Understanding a CPU at the transistor level is crucial for accurate emulation, especially for hardware with undocumented quirks, and the lack of documentation is the main barrier to SGI emulation. 67 The graphics hardware is the challenging part of SGI emulation, and the emulation of the Reality Engine is very unlikely due to its complexity and lack of documented hardware or microcode interfaces. 89 Emulation may become necessary to preserve SGI technology in the future, and it is more practical for preserving vintage SGI systems than hardware preservation. 1011 Emulation is a core part of preservation, and it is more likely to survive than the hardware itself. 1213 Emulation is important for people who cannot afford the machine, usually because there is an extremely limited supply of surviving machines, and some people see emulation as superior to original hardware, which can be a valid perspective, especially in the past. 1415 Emulation allows testing hardware checks and license hacking, and it can provide software learning opportunities, though not for everyone. 1617 Emulation and real hardware require different troubleshooting steps and should be separated, and FPGA-based systems are using emulation to create new vintage console experiences, which could potentially apply to vintage computing. 1819 Emulation can offer better performance and scalability through JIT, and modern CPUs should be able to handle certain emulation targets without too much difficulty. 2021
