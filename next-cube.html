NeXT Cube

Claims: 20 Part of NeXT Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXT.

NeXT made several computers, including the NeXT Computer, NeXTcube, and NeXTstation, along with turbo and color variants of the NeXTstation. 1 The original NeXT Computer came with a MO drive and an optional hard drive, while the NeXTcube and NeXTstations came with both. 2 Frog Design worked on the Apple IIc, the NeXT cube, and the SparcStation 1. 3 The NeXT Cube is considered spiffy but not practical for running NeXTSTEP, and it is expensive and incompatible, not worth its $10k price. 45 For similar price and performance, an Apple IIfx or an SGI IRIS Indigo is a better option. 56 The NeXT Cube lacks color graphics, while the NeXTstation (Slab) needs accessories or a pin short to power on. 78 A SCSI disk can replace the MO drive in a NeXT Cube, and it uses a 19-pin cable at least 2m long. 910 A switch can power on and off the NextCube and NextStation, and a relay could be used for this purpose. 11
