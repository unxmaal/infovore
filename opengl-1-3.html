OpenGL 1.3

Claims: 7 Part of OpenGL

OpenGL 1.3 is the oldest version of ES that some systems cannot support, and OpenGL rendering in certain Qt demos can break software rendering. 1 The PC port of Mario 64 requires OpenGL 1.3 due to its backend rather than Nintendo's original requirements, and its OpenGL 1.3 renderer could potentially be ported to IRIX. 23 Some systems with ATI cards may have OpenGL 1.3 but still be unable to run xsgi. 4 The Radeon 7000 supports OpenGL 1.3 but lacks hardware T&L, has only one pixel pipeline, a 64-bit memory bus, and no HyperZ. 5 OpenGL 1.3 and later versions include multitexturing as a core function, though SGI hardware likely did not support it in hardware, and other graphics issues exist in the US and EU ROMs for OpenGL 1.3. 67
