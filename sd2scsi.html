SD2SCSI

Claims: 5

The SD2SCSI is a potential purchase for an Indy with a 2gb drive from 1997, and the user has fitted a VFO for easy overclocking. 1 AzulSCSI is essentially the SD2SCSI stack on different hardware. 2 The biggest difference is that BlueSCSI/RASCSI are very much hobbiest projects, while zuluscsi and sd2scsi are more business projects, better by that nature. 3 To use an SD card as a zip drive, you either write directly to it (SD2SCSI) or use an image file since the SD card has its own filesystem. 4 SCA drives are newer than 50 pin drives and zuluscsi and sd2scsi are slower. 5
