Quake III

Claims: 9 Part of Quake

Quake III runs on both OS9 and macOS on a G3 iBook. 1 The XVR-1200 performs well with Quake III, and Infinite Reality 4 offers the best Quake III performance on SGI systems. 23 The Creator3D can run Quake I/II but not Quake III without an XVR-500/600/1000/1200. 4 The Pentium Pro system can run a PCI card, but the PCI bus may be too slow for modern games like Quake III. 5 Quake III on the V880z (XVR-4000) with light map instead of vertex shading can cause a texture RAM-related glitch. 6 A Tezro's Quake III image quality shows texture anomalies and jaggies compared to an Onyx2 IR4, and the Onyx2 produces a lot of noise and heat, while the Tezro is preferred for its compact form factor and quad CPU power. 73 The system aims to run Quake III at 30 FPS in max settings at 1280x1024 resolution and achieves better Quake III performance than a Quad 1GHz Tezro with a V12 sound card. 89
