ZZ9000

Claims: 8

The ZZ9000 is a processor board used with Amiga models such as the A4000/40, often paired with an Emplant board and AX. 1 Performance can be poor in some cases, such as with Marathon on an A4000/40, due to Zorro III RAM speed, and the A4000 with Zorro III memory emulation via ZZ9000 is slow for Mac emulation. 23 Upgrades for the ZZ9000 would be more expensive and consume all expansion slots. 4 The ARM cores in the ZZ9000 have coprocessors that are rarely utilized, and Warp3D type support is not available in the ZZ9000 drivers. 56 The ZZ9000 can scan-double native video by tapping into the video slot and outputting over HDMI. 7 It has drivers that support Poseidon, allowing one slot for USB, video, 16bit audio, Ethernet, co-processor, and Z3 memory. 8
