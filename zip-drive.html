Zip drive

Claims: 23 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Zip drive, DMA attack.

The Zip drive is a removable storage device that improves on floppy disks and was used for aircraft nav/terrain databases, replacing dozens of floppies, and is now being replaced by a USB solution. 12 The Zip drive has multiple interfaces including SCSI, IDE internal, parallel, and USB external. 3 Jumper wires from a DVD drive do not work for the Zip drive, and disabling DMA on the parallel port can resolve issues with a Zip drive on Windows NT. 45 Zip drive drivers for DOS and Win95/NT can be found on old Zip disks or at sites like Winworldpc and the Internet Archive. 6 A Zip drive can be emulated by handling an image file with care, as advanced OSes may hang if the drive is not present, and an SD card can be used as a Zip drive either by writing directly to it (SD2SCSI) or by using an image file. 78
