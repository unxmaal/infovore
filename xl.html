XL

Claims: 12

The SGI XL is a rackmount version of the O2 with the plastic case removed, and it is rare and expensive due to SGI's proprietary software and hardware. 1 The dual CPU board is for Challenge L or XL, or Onyx1. 2 The GR version of the L could take a GFX option, but there was no GR L; it was an XL, which was basically an Onyx I. 3 Skywriter refers to the XL equivalent size 4D system with a backplane that supports two graphics sets, often designed for VGX graphics or VGXT with RM3 boards instead of RM2 boards. 4 The top board of the XL and the Extreme graphics boards for the Indigo2 are identical, suggesting they might be interchangeable. 5 The EV1 on the Indigo pairs with graphics options like the XL, which has more space for additional video cards. 6 An R5000 with XL performs better 'in many cases' than XZ, which implies that it depends on the particular situation. 7 Converting an Indy IRIX 6.5.22 image from xl to xz graphics may result in conflicts and require a reinstall. 8
