NeXTDimension

Claims: 11

The NeXTDimension is a diskless system that can netboot BSD or act like a thin client using proprietary, old, and obscure software. 1 It has an i860 processor and up to 32MB of RAM, boots its own internal OS that is incompatible with standard NeXTSTEP bootstrapping, and requires +3V power not carried on the NeXTBus, which is driven from the motherboard. 234 Its software is limited and underutilizes its hardware, does not do justice to the i860 processor, and its firmware is aggressively silly and overcomplex, with unoptimized graphics routines. 567 NeXT's documentation for the i860 was closed-in, requiring communication through DPS and Color DPS, which is slow and makes the OS treat the board as a separate computer. 8 The NeXTDimension uses an LED to indicate processor status. 9
