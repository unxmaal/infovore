eject

Claims: 3

On an SGI machine, you can eject a drive by clicking its icon and selecting 'Eject' from the Special menu or dragging it to the trash can. 1 The CLI has eject and inject commands, but using them during an install while in Sash or not is uncertain and may require loading libraries beforehand; using them on an O2 can be kinder to the drive. 2 The CD-ROM ejects but has not been tested further. 3
