Z buffer

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Framebuffer.

The Z buffer is connected to the RV on the back via connectors, some of which are for a Z buffer option. 1 It connects the Z buffer to brown headers. 2 The Z-buffer for Personal IRIS TG graphics is on an option board, specifically the Turbo option was on a separate board with XMAP5 ASICs and an R3000 in VME form factor. 3 With 8 bytes/pixel framebuffer, you can't have 24-bit color with zbuffer and double buffering, while 16 bytes/pixel allows quad-buffer and z-buffer with 10-bit color. 4 With 16-bit RGB double-buffered visuals, 4 bytes can be used for the color buffer, leaving 3 for a zbuffer or 4 for a zbuffer and stencil. 5 The machine has the Z-buffer and geometry engines; it is a maxxed out config with no special options. 6
