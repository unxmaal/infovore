RE2

Claims: 4 Part of RE

RE2 outputs 5vdc on one of its pins, which could cause issues with monitors not designed for it. 1 An RE2 cannot display 2304x1440 resolution, but an InfiniteReality Onyx can. 2 A Sirius set requires a paddle board on the Sirius board and one on the DG of the RE2, with flat cables connecting them. 3 VGX systems are more reliable than Engine, RE2, and IR, and VGX is nearly as fast as user-24f0 Engine for non-textured applications. 4
