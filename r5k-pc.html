R5k PC

Claims: 5

The R5k PC lacks cache, which can significantly impact performance in some use cases, with a 40% difference in some loads, though adding cache might be feasible. 1 An R5K PC running at 150MHz with 24-bit graphics has no cache but still works. 2 The R5k PC performs better for emulation compared to R4k due to differences in L2 cache control. 3 Instruction decoding cache experiments showed mixed results but may still be beneficial for R5k emulation. 4
