NeXT mouse

Claims: 6 Part of NeXT

Steve Jobs envisioned a continuity from NeXT to Apple, and the NeXT mouse featured a right-click function that he later criticized on Mac products. 1 The NeXT mouse uses a digital encoder for its vertical roller, which can fail and can be tested with a scope or meter to check the phototransistor's Vf and I(ce) ratings. 2 A NeXT mouse uses a YA cable that can have an open circuit, which causes issues in one direction, and beeping out the cable is a useful diagnostic. 3 The NeXT mouse design is more comfortable for the hand than the Apple ADB mouse, and it is a bit strange but works alright. 45
