SunBlade 2000

Claims: 11

The SunBlade 2000 and SunBlade 2500 use the same PA810 case, and the SunBlade 2000 was Sun's answer to the Ciao's Prism, offering dual Itanium or dual UltraSPARC configurations. 12 The SunBlade 2000 uses a SEEPROM for NVRAM, and not changing the NVRAM battery can cause the machine to malfunction. 34 A SunBlade 2000 can give a RED State Exception if the CPU is not properly seated, and SunBlade 2000s aren't particularly reliable. 56 The SunBlade 2000 is not super common, but it can be found for $100 online, and a broken one with a 1.2GHz CPU but no GPU can be had for $40. 789 OpenSSL 3.0.7 can be made to work on a SunBlade 2000, and it may also work on IRIX with some effort. 10
