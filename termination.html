termination

Claims: 7

SCSI drives should work in an external enclosure the same as internally, but termination must be paid attention to. 1 Termination is internal, so the active termination jumper on the drive should be checked. 2 The black sockets are for termination, while the white pins are for setting the SCSI ID. 3 Most SCSI devices become annoyed if termination is not properly set, and a data exception on SCSI probe suggests missing termination. 45 SCSI, multiple busses, and termination on SGI is not easy. 6
