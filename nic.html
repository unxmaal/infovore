NIC

Claims: 26

The Altix 330 ships with a NIC and theoretically has everything needed without an IO9 or PCI riser, though additional components may be required for netboot. 1 The NIC is a cheap Realtek chip. 2 The NIC is a battery-shaped component on the motherboard that, when dislodged, can prevent booting. 3 The NIC was part of a package deal including the XL24 and R5K 150SC for $300. 4 The NIC may need reflashing to resolve network issues. 5 The generic HP NIC model can be used in Tru64 and VMS after changing the model identifier. 6 You must source a NIC from another frontplane. 7 A modern NIC should do the crossover internally, but it won't break otherwise, it'll just not work. 8
