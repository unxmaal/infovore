caps

Claims: 12

G5 systems can suffer from tin migration and lead-free thermal issues, especially with caps. 1 The 4 10000uF caps on the 5V rail of the bus bar leak, and the personal IRIS PSU has a common failure where the caps on the 5V rail leak, though some still work. 23 Caps like this sometimes fail due to repeated thermal stress, causing material failure; they are optional, especially on chips that stabilize voltage. 4 Corrosion from bad caps can be difficult to remove, especially in high-density boards, but most boards can be restored. 5 Replacement caps need low ESR, especially in high current output filter circuits, and replacing bad caps with low ESR variants of Wurth, Rubycon, or Kemet can help stabilize the PSU. 67
