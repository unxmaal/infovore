IOC3

Claims: 4

The IOC3 is documented in the OpenBSD manual page ioc. 1 The base PCB is the same for io6 and io6g, and the ioc3 is also the keyboard controller, while the aisc pad might be another serial port controller. 2 The IOC3 might work for a VPro/MGRAS unsupported dual-head configuration, but it has not been tested. 3 The Propack contains custom kernels and tools for the compositor, and has better drivers for the ioc3 than the MIPS versions. 4
