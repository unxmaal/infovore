SLES 10

Claims: 11 Part of SLES

SLES 10 supports Itanium and is compatible with Oracle's JRE 6 for Itanium. 12 The standard radeon driver is included in SLES 10, while the fglrx driver is part of Propack. 3 The fglrx driver was available for SLES 10 due to certification for the A450, but SGI did not release a custom version for it. 4 Propack would not work on SLES 10 due to the wrong libc version. 5 SLES 10 can be installed via PXE booting on an A350 without an IO9 or IO10 card. 6 The user has ProPack 6 for SLES 10 on IA64. 7

Sources

  1. SLES 10 supports Itanium. (user-1446, 2020-04-20, exchange 7656, check: supported)
  2. SLES 10 is compatible with Oracle's JRE 6 for Itanium. (user-1446, 2020-05-13, exchange 75179, check: supported)
  3. The fglrx driver is part of Propack and the standard radeon driver is included in SLES 10. (user-1446, 2020-07-30, exchange 75279, check: supported)
  4. The *fglrx* driver was available for SLES 10 due to certification for the A450, but SGI did not release a custom version for it. (user-59b9, 2020-06-12, exchange 216321, check: supported)
  5. Propack would not work on SLES10 due to the wrong libc version and likely not on SLES11. (user-1446, 2020-06-11, exchange 75194, check: uncheckable)
  6. PXE booting can install SuSE SLES10 on an A350 without an IO9 or IO10 card. (user-bc5d, 2021-04-29, exchange 17045, check: uncheckable)
  7. I have ProPack 6 for SLES10 and SLES11 on IA64, and ProPack 6 for SLES11 on x86_64 and IA64. (user-03ad, 2026-05-28, exchange 219767, check: supported)
