IRIX 5.x

Claims: 24 Part of IRIX Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX, SGI O2.

IRIX 5.x was the first IRIX release to include a full web browser, which may have been bundled with TCGWare in IRIX 5.3. 12 The R3k Indigo runs IRIX 5.x and offers a user experience similar to later PI models. 3 IRIX 5.x supports proper networking and increased the maximum RAM limit to 2GB, up from the 256MB limit of IRIX 4.x. 45 IRIX 5.x is an o32, 32-bit system, and can run COFF objects so that older binaries should work. 67 The Sun Indy suffered from memory leaks in IRIX 5.x before 5.3, requiring weekly reboots on entry configurations with 16MB of RAM. 8 IRIX 5.x was outdated by the early 2000s. 9
