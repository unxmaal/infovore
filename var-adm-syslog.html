/var/adm/SYSLOG

Claims: 3

/var/adm/SYSLOG is a Unix path used for system logs. 1 It can be consulted to diagnose issues, such as a Wacom tablet driver that fails to initialize and shows errors there, which prevents the control panel from opening. 2 It is also used to check for Ethernet link problems by looking for 'no carrier' messages after unplugging the cable. 3
