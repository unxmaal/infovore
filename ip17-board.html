IP17 board

Claims: 5 Part of IP17

The IP17 board addresses the lock array, SBCC, PT timer, three DUARTs, EPROM, a serial EEPROM, RMP, RMI, and secondary cache. 1 The board likely came from a system with R4400 CPUs and may be dual 100 or 150MHz. 2 The Crimson PROM provides specific LED status codes for different POST stages, including cache sizing, memory sizing, DUART test, and FPU test. 3 The IP17 board in the Kleinanzeigen listing is missing the CPU heat sink, which is not a good sign. 4
