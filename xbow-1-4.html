Xbow 1.4

Claims: 11 Part of Xbow Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

Xbow 1.4 is the latest version of Xbow, an operating system for SGI machines, with earlier versions being upgradable. 12 Octane2 has Xbow 1.4, while Octane1.5 has Xbow 1.3. 3 V12 and V10 machines need Xbow 1.4, which may require V10 graphics for DVI upgrades. 45 An Octane can use VPro graphics with the right frontplane and PSU, including Xbow 1.4. 6 An Octane2 has Xbow 1.4, a later power supply, VPro, and a later 'heart' board for 8GiB support, and its PSU can fail even with a Xbow 1.4 frontplane. 78
