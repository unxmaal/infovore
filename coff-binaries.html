COFF binaries

Claims: 5 Part of COFF

COFF binaries are compatible with early IRIX versions, which support them using the COFF format. 1 IRIX 5.3 supports the older COFF binaries, while 6.5 is ELF only. 2 IRIX 6.0 and 6.1 will run COFF binaries but are not recommended except for R8K systems or those with a great desire for pain. 3 StudioPaint and Eclipse were bundled with early versions of Alias; they are COFF binaries requiring SGI 5.3 or earlier. 4
