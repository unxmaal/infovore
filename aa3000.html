AA3000+

Claims: 4

The Amiga runs at 100 MHz and the AA3000+ runs at 50 MHz; 100 MHz can cause SCSI instability. 1 The DSPs in the AA3000+ are underutilized like those in the Apple 840av. 2 The ReSDMAC firmware fixes motherboard SCSI issues in AA3000+ and allows BFG9060 to run at 50 or 100 MHz without SCSI errors, also resolving DSP crashes. 3 The A4000D/T (or AA3000+) provides AGA, supports the fastest 060 accelerator (BFG9060), and allows for RTG and PPC cards, offering more expandability than the A1200. 4
