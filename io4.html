IO4

Claims: 8

The IO4 is a board used in SGI workstations, including the InfiniteReality, Onyx, MC3, Indy, and Indigo systems. 1234 The IO4 contains four D-chips that handle data passage between the processor board and MC3, and a DANG chip that bridges the IO4 to the GIO bus. 54 A suspected issue with the InfiniteReality machine is dead Dallas chips on the system controller and IO4. 1 The D-chips on the IO4 can be pulled and reseated with Deoxit. 5 The early revision of the IO4 has compatibility issues with R4x00 CPUs. 6

Sources

  1. A suspected issue with the InfiniteReality machine is dead Dallas chips on the system controller and IO4. (user-a079, 2025-01-14, exchange 241272, check: uncheckable)

  5. The D-chip on the IO4 handles data passage between the processor board and MC3; it can be pulled and reseated with Deoxit, and there are four of them. (user-22f9, 2023-09-16, exchange 223353, check: uncheckable)
  6. The Early rev of the IO4 has compatibility issues with R4x00 CPUs. (user-86e1, 2026-06-05, exchange 244045, check: uncheckable)
