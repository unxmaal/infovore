R7k

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

The R7k was available for the O2, while the R6k and R9k were not commonly used. 1 The O2 takes R7k or R12k CPUs, with options such as 350 MHz R7k and 400 MHz R12k. 2 The R12k has more cache than the R7k, with 32k/32k primary and 2MB secondary compared to the R7k's 16k/16k primary and 1MB secondary. 3 R10k/R12k CPUs get much hotter than R5k/R7k CPUs. 4 A 600 MHz R7k might have been on par or slightly behind a 400 MHz R12k due to the R12k's larger cache. 5 The 600MHz R7k mod works after fixing solder ball issues, and one user runs their R7k at 800 MHz stably. 67 The R5k can be upgraded to an 800 MHz R7k. 8
