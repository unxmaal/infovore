DOSBox

Claims: 25 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see DOSBox.

DOSBox is an emulator that emulates the CPU, sound and graphic cards, and other peripherals of a PC, all at the same time, with the speed of an emulated DOS application being adjustable. 1 DOSBox can be run natively on Irix and on the O2 using a MIPSPro port, though it may not run well on the O2 due to its JIT not being optimized. 2345 The MIPSPro build of DOSBox has issues with some programs, such as crashing with 'Exit to error: Illegal CallBack Called' when loading Warcraft2, while the GCC build works fine. 678 On MIPS, there is less hardware acceleration of graphics and sound compared to the x86 version, which uses SSE and MMX optimizations, making DOSBox slower on MIPS than on a modern PC or even a P3. 9 DOSBox may not run well on the O2, and it is much slower than SoftWindows for running games, though Warcraft2 runs well in DOSBox on a Tezro. 101112 DOSBox is available in the `wipnonautomated` branch of the repo but no RPMs are provided yet, and building from source is required for the latest version. 1314
