SCSI chain

Claims: 4 Part of SCSI

Tape libraries can be connected to the SCSI chain through the MFIOP. 1 Adding a terminator to the SCSI chain can cause boot issues with an external CD-ROM but not with no external termination. 2 The IP6 system may have a bus-related issue preventing it from detecting the GR1.1 board or the SCSI chain, and eliminating the SCSI chain can help diagnose issues. 34
