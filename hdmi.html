HDMI

Claims: 21 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see HDMI, Field-programmable gate array, Video Graphics Array.

HDMI uses TMDS differential pairs for video transfer and I2C for control, and can supply around 150 mA @ 5V to external peripherals, though not all implementations support this properly. 1 HDMI cables lack analog pins, making them equivalent to DVI-D, and HDMI boards can be made cheaply with more lax PCB tolerances since differential pairs can handle high length mismatch between signal lines. 123 HDMI is burdened by patents and HDCP, but is fun to work with otherwise. 4 FPGA systems can support standard peripherals like HDMI, USB keyboard, MIDI, or serial. 5 SDI is easy to use and can be converted from HDMI and DV. 6
