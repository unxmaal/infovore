VW

Claims: 9

The VW is an O2 with Xeons instead of MIPS processors, which are derived from the N64. 1 It is a basic x86 workstation compatible with SGI rack control software if you install ancient Red Hat. 2 SGI sold the VW as an L3 controller, though it was expensive. 3 Maya 4.5 runs well on the VW. 4 Linux might be a better option for the VW, potentially with custom builds and Xorg support. 5
