Maya 6.5

Claims: 34 Part of Maya

Maya 6.5 was the last version of Maya with IRIX support and was intended for big irons, with releases for Windows, Mac, and Irix but not all tagged. 12 Maya 6.5 is a 32-bit application that can only address 2 GB of RAM. 34567 Maya 6.5 has oddities on many systems except Octane/Tezro V12, is clunky even on higher-end SGIs, and has a very specific list of 'certified' Irix versions. 8910 Maya 6.5 can be used on IRIX, where it can model planets with correct size ratios and render scenes including the Voyager II probe, and can be used for particle generation and other features not available in Blender 2.79. 111213 Maya 6.5 on IRIX does not support Satellite render distribution but supports Dispatcher for render farm setup with IRIX and Linux machines. 14 Maya 6.5 is basically fully PowerAnimator at that point. 15
