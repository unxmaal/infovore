AV card

Claims: 5

An AV card can cause system panics and visual corruption upon boot and when pressing enter, making it likely a faulty card. 1 Reseating the AV card and the system board did not fix the issue, so opening the case to check the circuit level may be necessary. 2 An SGI's AV card might be salvageable, and the lack of a boot chime is not a concern if the AV card is removed because it's broken. 34 The O2 can run a data sender with an AV card. 5
