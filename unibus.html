UNIBUS

Claims: 8

The UNIBUS appears in both PDP-11 and VAX systems, and the BA11-K is a UNIBUS chassis used primarily by PDP-11 models 780, 750, 730, and 725, with some 8000 series models also supporting UNIBUS. 123 The VAX-11/730 was the third VAX introduced and the slowest UNIBUS implementation of the VAX architecture. 4 The 11/780 VAX runs VMS and has UNIBUS compatibility issues, with UNIBUS adapters available but the UNIBUS cabinet missing, which might be problematic but is not strictly needed. 5 Qbus cards were dual or quad height to accommodate more board space, and DEC never made a Unibus J11 board. 6 There were 3rd party graphics options for VAX systems, including a Matrox card for QBUS that might work with a UNIBUS through an Able Qinverter, but details are scarce. 7
