Ripcord

Claims: 6

Ripcord is a third-party Discord client designed for power users, and also functions as an alternative client for Discord and Slack built with Qt. 123 It is entirely software rendered and does not use OpenGL, and can be built without several Qt modules including qml/qt-declarative, database stuff, web engine, and qt stylesheets, as well as without C++ exceptions or RTTI. 4 Ripcord has limitations around 2^53 channels and 2^63 members. 5 Ripcord is a legacy IRC client that has never had issues with Discord banning third-party clients. 6
