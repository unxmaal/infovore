L1 serial port

Claims: 4 Part of L1

The L1 serial port is a standard DB9 connector. 1 Flipping the PSU power off and on in rapid succession can make the L1 serial port on a Fuel computer come up reliably. 2 To reset IP settings and enable DHCP on the NL4R, run `l2 ip reset` from the L1 serial port and reboot the system. 3
