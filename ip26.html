IP26

Claims: 7 Part of IP

The IP26 and IP28 are SGI workstations. 1 The IP26 has an r8k CPU, and its processor type is more important than its clock speed. 2 The IP26 has a 2 MB secondary cache, while the IP28 has only one. 3 The IP26 stores around 8 songs in an array and plays them with Karplus-Strong string synthesis as part of its boot process. 45 The maximum memory of 128+256+512 MB might have carried over to the IP28, despite the 128 MB limit not existing in IP28 hardware. 6 Swapping chips from an IP21 into an IP26 might be possible with modifications such as clock and voltage adjustments. 7
