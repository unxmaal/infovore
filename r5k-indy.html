R5K Indy

Claims: 20

The R5K Indy is a used machine that can be purchased for $75, and at least one unit was owned by an SGI engineer and still has working 50pin SCSI drives. 123 It has a Rev A motherboard that takes two sticks of MT48LC4M16A2 RAM, runs IRIX 6.2 with various webforce software, and suffers from a problematic, possibly unfixable PSU and overheating issues. 4567 It can sync 15kHz signals, which the O2 cannot, and performs better with a 24-bit board than with an XZ board. 89 The XL24 graphics can be used on the R5K Indy, though its full potential may not be realized due to multitasking limitations, and it can handle non-textured rendering decently. 1011 XZ graphics are unusual for the R5K Indy because the software can handle 3D work faster than the XZ hardware. 12 Installing IRIX 6.5 on an R5K Indy from an R4400 Indy does not require a reinstall but may encounter disk space issues, and a unit with a 18gb drive can run out of space on root, unlike R4400 or 4600 models with the same drive. 131415
