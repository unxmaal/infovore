Solaris 2.6

Claims: 36 Part of Solaris

Solaris 2.6 runs on an SS5, and can be installed from the same media onto an SS5 without issues. 12 Solaris 2.6 can be dual booted with OpenSTEP on an SS10, and runs well on an emulated SPARCstation 20 after formatting disks. 34 Solaris 2.6 runs on the Ultra 5 (with 512MB RAM and a UltraSPARC-IIi at 360MHz), the IPX (with a 400 megabyte disc), the Zulu (which has DHCP support), and the SPARCstation 10 with dual SM71 processors. 5678 The SPARCstation 5 can run Solaris 2.6 or 7, with 2.6 recommended for lower spec systems, and requires a native C/C++ compiler toolchain that can differ from gcc 2.9x. 910 Solaris 2.6 runs on the SPARCbook without issues, though it requires tadpole patches, while Solaris 7 is too heavy for it. 1112 Solaris 2.6 can be used to partition the harddisk, does not like swap files (but single root plus a swap slice should be fine), and can be installed from CD to recover production when root disks failed. 131415 Solaris 2.6 on an Ultra-1 can act as a FDDI-Ethernet bridge/router with a DEC FDDI adapter. 16 Solaris 2.6 can be found in physical storage, and its ISO is an ISO 9660 filesystem. 1718
