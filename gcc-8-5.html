GCC 8.5

Claims: 8 Part of GCC

GCC 8.5 runs on Solaris 10 SPARC and is the final version supporting Solaris 10. 12 It is packaged, tested, and working on the author's system, and is available with new GNU binutils. 23 Building GCC 8.5 on Solaris 10 with a GNU binutils-based binutils and /usr/sfw/bin/gcc/g++ results in a fully compatible toolchain. 4 GCC 8.5 with multilib can take 18 hours to compile on a 4.9.4 platform, and it supports C11 but not C17. 5 The Blade2K can run GCC 8.5 with a GNU toolchain, requiring linking /usr/local/lib to /lib. 6
