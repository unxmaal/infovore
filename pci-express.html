PCI Express

Claims: 4 Part of PCI

PCI Express compatibility allows hardware from different architectures to work with minimal issues. 1 MXI-Express x1 allows physically separating the measurement or automation system from a host PC or laptop and combining PCI Express, CompactPCI, CompactPCI Express, PXI, PXI Express, and CompactRIO devices into the same. 2 SGI could have developed VPro for AGP Pro or PCI Express on x86-based systems. 3 The RP2350A/B microcontroller does not support PCI Express, making USB mass storage a non-starter for this generation of hardware. 4
