P6

Claims: 3

The original 100MHz QDR bus was too slow but sold a lot, and the performance regression to P6 designs suggested a flop. 1 Intel cashed out after NetBurst and returned to P6 style designs. 2 On IRIX, the user partition is p6, while other partitions serve different purposes such as swap (p1), the root partition (p7), and the volume header (p8). 3
