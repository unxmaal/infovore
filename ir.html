IR

Claims: 12

IR is the name given to the G-brick graphics in these systems. 1 IR is more of a big render pipeline than a real-time 3D renderer. 2 IR was initially released in 1996 with OpenGL 1.1, and later revisions focused on upgrading geometry engines and raster managers. 3 IR should support almost all OpenGL SGIxxx extensions except one, as found in appendix D of the specified PDF. 4 The TVO option for IR outputs 1080i SDI video and can be converted to HDMI but remains interlaced or low res with a VBOB. 5 IR runs IRIX 6.5, but Onyx2 runs it better. 6 Rendering fisheye views in real-time with IR might be challenging. 7
