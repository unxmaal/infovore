RapidApp

Claims: 7

RapidApp is a GUI creation suite for IRIX's flavor of Motif, usable for IRIX and Motif development. 12 It is a slimmed-down version of BXpro and requires a license (NetLS or FLEXlm), though it should not crash without one. 3 RapidApp is known to run on IRIX 5.3 and can be found on Varsity Developer discs for IRIX 6.4. 3 There are IDEs available for IRIX, such as SGI RapidApp and ProDev Workshop. 4 The jrra.zone copy of RapidApp for IRIX 6.5 is an ISO and more complete. 5 RapidApp 1.1 requires a newer version of eoe.sw.base than is compatible with IRIX 6.5.30, so upgrading RapidApp or eoe.sw.base may be necessary. 6
