VLB card

Claims: 4 Part of VLB

  • Micronics JX30GC VLB cards do not like Cirrus GD5429s and run Am5x86/133 at 100MHz. They have PS/2 ports, not AT, and may need an ATX case. GD5426 is compatible, and GD5429s can be harvested for RAM. (user-7b1c, exchange 7387, 2020-04-12; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • CL-GD542x series VLB cards have a 16-bit communication path to the CPU. (user-e872, exchange 204757, 2021-02-13; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The S3 card may have a Mac video port, and the VLB card could be useful. (user-d903, exchange 231463, 2021-04-21; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • VLB cards, especially low-end ones, were often more expensive and sought after than their PCI or EISA counterparts. (user-59df, exchange 234778, 2026-07-07; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
