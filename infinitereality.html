InfiniteReality

Claims: 20 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx.

The InfiniteReality is a graphics system that was one of the favorite aspects of SGI tech, serving as the main graphics solution for server SGI systems. 12 It was an improvement over earlier designs, with PCBs featuring metal reinforcements that made them more rigid, and Onyx2 further improved this. 3 The geometry subsystem handles geometry transforms, while the raster managers handle the conversion from normalized floats to pixel coordinates. 4 OpenGL commands and data enter the InfiniteReality via a high-bandwidth proprietary IO bus, where they are received by the Host Interface Processor (HIP) that decodes and dispatches OpenGL command streams. 5 The InfiniteReality and i-Station systems are not available with IP21 CPU boards. 6 The Onyx can be upgraded with InfiniteReality or a Crimson to RealityEngine. 7
