R7000

Claims: 4 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

The R7000 is a CPU that can run at 400 MHz, though a 600 MHz R7000 would be cheaper. 1 A 600 MHz R7000 CPU should be as fast as a 400 MHz R12000 CPU but is cooler and less power-hungry. 2 The O2+ was supposed to use R7000 and R12000 CPUs, but issues with the R7000 led to the use of the 300 MHz R5000 instead. 3 A configuration includes two R7000 and one vice. 4
