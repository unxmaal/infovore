AmigaOS 4

Claims: 4 Part of AmigaOS

AmigaOS 4 runs on PowerPC hardware, while AmigaOS 3.x runs on 68k hardware, and AmigaOS 4 emulates 68k for legacy programs using UAE, similar to running Classic on an OSX system. 12 The Amiga X5k can run AmigaOS 4 or MorphOS, but performance with modern Linux distributions is poor. 3 The Mac mini G4 can run AmigaOS 4, but it is unsupported and requires a rare and expensive system. 4
