NL4R

Claims: 7

The NL4R is a card that was added to flexion's L2-WUI alongside the Altix 350 and Altix 3000, and it is backwards compatible with the Altix 350, as shown by Prism (Altix 350 with graphics) photos that display the NL4R. 12 Some Altix 350 systems had grey doors, and the speaker's 16-brick Altix 350 contained a single NL4R. 3 The NL4R is not compatible with Origin hardware, and the `router| rtr` command on the NL4R indicates 3800 series mode, though there is no Altix 3800, only the Origin 3800. 45 To reset IP settings and enable DHCP on the NL4R, run `l2 ip reset` from the L1 serial port and reboot the system. 6
