FusionIO cards

Claims: 3

FusionIO cards are PCI-based storage devices that offer high IOPs, though they exhibit latency and use a proprietary drive protocol. 1 The cards are built on SLC flash with 75PBW endurance, were OEMed by SanDisk and NetApp, and wear out over time. 12 They require specific drivers for Windows, Linux, and macOS, but Linux support is limited and the driver is no longer maintained. 1
