G4 Digital Audio

Claims: 7 Part of G4

The G4 Digital Audio supports Mac OS 9.2.2 and can be set up for dual-boot with OS X. 1 It can run OS 9.2.2 with a RAGE 128 PCI graphics card, even when a Radeon 9800 Pro AGP card is installed. 2 OS 9 under the G4 Digital Audio feels sluggish despite the CPU running at 1.4GHz. 3 With OS 9.2.2 and a Rage 128, it can run Quake 3 but displays a grey screen with a movable mouse cursor. 4 A 450MHz G3 with a Radeon 9200 LE runs better than the G4 Digital Audio with a Rage 128 under the same OS. 5
