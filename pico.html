Pico

Claims: 9

Pico was not under a free enough license, leading to the development of a clone now under the Apache license. 1 Most *nix programming was done with pico/nano. 2 Pine and Pico had limited undo and paste buffers due to low-spec hardware. 3 The DECstation 3000 had 32MB RAM and could support a few dozen users simultaneously running pine and pico via telnet. 4 The Pico can function as a CD drive, boot drive, and Wi-Fi card in a Quadra 700, though the Wi-Fi is slow. 5 The pico has only one USB port, requiring a hub or keyboard with ports, and Bluetooth is an alternative for keyboards. 67 3D-printed cases are made for Picos, with PCBs epoxied inside the case. 8
