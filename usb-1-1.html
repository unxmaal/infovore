USB 1.1

Claims: 3 Part of USB

USB 1.1 devices were more common than USB 1.0 devices, a result of USB's slow market adoption. 1 IRIX USB support is limited to OHCI USB cards, which implement most of the USB specification in hardware and support USB 1.1 along with limited device types, requiring custom drivers for mass storage. 2 A USB-attached ZuluSCSI RP2040 can be used as a USB SCSI controller, with throughput limited to about 768 kilobytes/second due to USB 1.1 hardware constraints. 3
