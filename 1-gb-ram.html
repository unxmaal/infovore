1 GB RAM

Claims: 13 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

Several SGI workstations support 1 GB RAM, including the Octane (400 MHz R12000 CPU), the O2 (which runs IRIX), and the Sun Ultra 10 (440 MHz CPU, with a maximum of 1 GB RAM and an optional Creator 3D card). 1234 The 400 MHz R12K Octane with 1 GB RAM runs SE/ESI graphics with no TRAM, which limits its performance, while a 400 MHz O2 with 1 GB RAM performs extremely well. 536 Other machines with 1 GB RAM include a 500 MHz G3 Pismo upgraded with a 120 GB SSD, a 250 MHz R10k system, the Proliant 6000 Netware server, the P/390E (which had a 1 GB variant by stacking RAM chips), and a Raq with a 550 MHz K6-3. 7891011 The VWs can allocate 768 MB of 1 GB RAM to graphics. 12
