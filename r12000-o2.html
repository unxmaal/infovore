R12000 O2

Claims: 3 Part of R12000

An Origin 300 with two compute modules can provide eight CPUs, and setup is straightforward with the correct hardware. 1 The mplayer from nekoware does not support H264 format from YouTube, but a newer mplayer with a newer ffmpeg can play such videos. 2 For a 300 MHz R12000 O2, use 512xY for 21:9 aspect and 480xY for 16:9 aspect videos. 2 A 400 MHz R12000 O2 with sufficient RAM is a good configuration, and R5000 O2s with the same RAM are less performant. 3
