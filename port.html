nekoware has an old 21.something port. 1 Slick screensavers are needed, and the current port does not work. 2 The PS/2 designation refers to the type of device, not the port, as some serial number checks indicate a PS/2 mouse even if the port is different. 3 Running sgirc with specific IRC server and port allows login but causes window closure and socket error upon joining a channel. 4 Fixes for the GCC port are portable and address issues with test suites and breaking changes, making the effort worthwhile. 5 Multi-lib issues were partially resolved, with some symbolic links causing problems. 5 A cable with SGI-style minidin3 on the computer side and DB9 on the emitter side can be used to connect the white model. 6 The digital port on Galileo video is used to connect a camera or a cosmo compress card via external cable. 7
