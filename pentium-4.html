Pentium 4

Claims: 24 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Pentium 4.

The Pentium D is a dual-core processor combining two Pentium 4 cores. 1 The Pentium 4 had a TDP of 130W, which was considered high in 2004 but normal by modern standards. 2 The Pentium 4 caused hardware issues, like desoldering itself. 3 The motherboard is Socket 478, a 32-bit Pentium 4 motherboard with ISA DMA. 4 Pentium 4s, Ds, Core2Duo/Quads, and K10 Phenom II X4 945 can be overclocked. 5 The Intel 915 onboard graphics are incompatible with some Pentium4 systems, unlike the Intel 865. 6
