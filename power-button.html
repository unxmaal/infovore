power button

Claims: 13 Part of POWER Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Motherboard.

The power button is part of the motherboard, and it may be missing from the machine while the switch remains behind a separate bezel. 12 The power button does nothing if the PSU is dead, and it may also do nothing if the PSU fails to power up or down, which can be caused by the power on jumper on the motherboard. 345 A long-press on the power button is the recommended way to gracefully shut down without a serial connection, and pressing it may indicate a bad PSU or a short circuit that requires tools and care to diagnose. 64 The power button on the keyboard generates a keycode that can be used to power on the machine, and the soft power off through the power button not working on some systems can be fixed by adjusting a jumper next to the RTC chip. 78 The O2 power supply can fail to power up or down, and setting the power on jumper to the correct state can resolve the issue if the power button is prone to sticking. 5 The Octane2 fan spinning up and the power button having no effect with a blank light bar may indicate a mainboard issue. 9 The power button on the Tezro deskside and Prism deskside is a surface mount switch that is worn and lacks a click. 10 The Power Mac G3 has a blue power button that glows green, and different models may have variations in their power button casings. 11
