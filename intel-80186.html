Intel 80186

Claims: 3 Part of Intel

The Intel 80186 is a CPU that appears in several older devices. 123 IBM Xstation terminals used the Intel 80186, and old IBM Xterminals also had an Intel 80186 CPU alongside good industrial design. 13 The HP 200LX has an Intel 80186 CPU running at 7.91 MHz that can be overclocked to 15.8 MHz, with 1, 2, or 4 MB of memory, of which 640 KB is RAM and the rest can be used for expanded memory (EMS) or memory-based storage. 2
