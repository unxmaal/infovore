SDL

Claims: 27 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL, SGI O2, Simple DirectMedia Layer.

SDL is available for IRIX, with both SDL1 and SDL2 versions, and has been used for IRIX porting. 12 Several programs have been ported to IRIX using SDL, including milkytracker, prboom-plus, MESS (an OpenGL version by Axatax), SM64, and SM64ex. 34567 Compiling SM64ex on IRIX requires the latest RSE and SDL from nekoware or RSE, along with X11 libraries that have GL support, which may need recompilation from the wip-testing branch to fix SDL errors. 78 SDL configuration on IRIX 6.5 can show an accelerated VPRO/B/128 renderer with texture support running at OpenGL and SDL version 1.2. 9 SDL sound combined with X11 video should work, though SDL-using clients may have issues with Xorg but work with Xsgi, and the SDL in sgug was broken but is now fixed. 10111213 SDL's --enable-sdl-dlopen option allows it to handle X11 symbols with dlopen, which can affect linking. 14
