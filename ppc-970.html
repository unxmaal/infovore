PPC 970

Claims: 5 Part of PPC

The PPC 970 (G5) is a POWER4 subset CPU with added VMX instructions (altivec), and VMX was not widely distributed on the servers until power 6. 1 There aren't really any precompiled releases for PPC 970. 2 Dual core PPC970s existed but were not power efficient versions. 3 In-order execution limited the Xbox 360, PS3, and PPC 970 by 2002, but the PPC 970 was OOO by 2002. 45
