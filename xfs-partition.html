XFS partition

Claims: 7 Part of XFS

  • The fix_vdi template for rhosts removes an unnecessary large XFS partition, reducing rsync time. (user-2189, exchange 76917, 2019-10-21; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • XFS partitions can hang the system, which can be repaired through a netbooted recovery environment. (user-bc18, exchange 97845, 2020-11-13; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The BlueSCSI reports 3 MB and 7 MB for XFS partitions. (user-1a5a, exchange 224915, 2025-04-18; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • XFS partitions from SGI workstations may not be compatible with newer OSes. (user-7973, exchange 207912, 2022-11-08; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • EFS and XFS partitions cannot coexist on the same hard disk on O2s; some use two drives and scripts to manage NVRAM values. (user-e4d0, exchange 209639, 2023-11-27; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The O2 can experience kernel panics when initializing IRIX, which may require repairing the XFS partition to recover data. (user-bc18, exchange 6685, 2020-03-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • XFS partitions may exist on IRIX systems but their contents are unknown. (user-3b50, exchange 60553, 2025-11-16; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
