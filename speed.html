speed

Claims: 5

Compiler compatibility, speed, and code readability are issues. 1 The Vampyre II claims to be at least four times faster than a 100 MHz 68060. 2 The STM32's model significantly impacts the speed, with moving from F1 to F4 doubling it, and bus speed to GPIO is also a factor. 3 The SGI branded EMC array had a Thor controller and a speed of 10MB/sec or slower. 4 The speed and 68K emulation were the primary issues, with the 50 MHz version being slower than the original 6100s. 5
