FC card

Claims: 10

An FC card for Octane was XIO and very slow, taking hours to catch up after being away. 1 The FC card for SGI was a 2Gbit card. 2 Linux can act as a FC target with an old enough FC card and a tiny Linux box with SATA SSDs. 3 Linux/FreeBSD with SCSI target and a supported FC card, and an old QLogic HBA in the SGI box can be used to install IRIX on a RAID. 4 For the FC card, it can be placed on a carrier or in a shoebox and tested, as long as it supports the slot voltage. 5 Discreet systems have a FC card for fast scsi use. 6
