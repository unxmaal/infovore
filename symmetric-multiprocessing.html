symmetric multiprocessing

Claims: 4

Classic Mac OS does not support symmetric multiprocessing due to cooperative multitasking in the CMP environment. 1 Early multi-CPU systems, like the dual Pentium Pro, did not fully support symmetric multiprocessing. 2 The Daystar Genesis MP supports symmetric multiprocessing but requires programmer intervention for full utilization. 3 On Windows NT, even applications not explicitly supporting multithreading could benefit from better overall system responsiveness due to symmetric multiprocessing. 4
