IDO 7.1

Claims: 5 Part of IDO

IDO 7.1 is the compiler version used for codegen analysis. 1 IDO 7.1 is the last compiler to support MIPS N64 code generation. 2 No N64 game used IDO versions other than 5.3 or 7.1 and above, due to the mulmul fix, which IDO 6.x lacked but IDO 7.1 and beyond included. 34 pd uses a mix of IDO 5.3 and 7.1, differing in handling libc functions like __f_to_ull, __ull_to_d, and _Ldtob compared to other games. 5
