Origin 3000

Claims: 28 Part of Origin Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Origin 3000 and Onyx 3000.

The Origin 3000 (also known as IP35) was part of the Origin/NumaFlex line, which replaced the Onyx2 and offered better performance, though most systems used ATI FireGL cards that did not support older IrisGL software. 123 The Origin 3000's bricks and fans are designed as separate components, and its bricks use 48V power, so they need a power bay or can be rewired for AC. 45 An Origin 3000 without a router brick would be nearly useless due to lack of disk access, and the NL3 router comes in AC versions (used with Origin 350s) and DC versions (used with Origin 3000s). 67 The Altix 350 and Origin 350 use the same cables as the Origin 3000, though the Altix 3k and Origin 3000 had different cable colors and connector designs. 89 The Origin 3000 is harder to use as a workstation, and its I-Brick does not have Gigabit Ethernet, which the later Origin 300 series does. 1011
