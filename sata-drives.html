SATA drives

Claims: 13 Part of SATA

SATA drives require controller support for SAS connections, as with modern HBAs and RAID controllers, and they will work with SAS ports but SAS drives will not necessarily work with SATA ports. 12 SATA drives have NCQ, but SAS drives offer more features beyond cable length and drive count. 3 PCI can enable the use of SATA drives, though BIOS updates may be necessary for large drives. 4 The T3-1 takes SATA drives, and you can use a Prism/Altix backplane with Origin bays for SATA drives. 56 OpenVMS has been updated to support non-server hardware like SATA drives. 7 RDX drives are SATA drives designed to replace tape backups. 8
