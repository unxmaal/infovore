Toaster

Claims: 12

The Toaster was a video production system that was eventually abandoned in favor of a PC migration, while its sibling product, the Flyer, remained focused on democratizing video. 1 A Toaster occupies two slots, requiring a Zorro RAM board for an additional slot, leaving only one slot available for other components. 2 The Toaster benefits from additional chip memory, with 2MB expanders being used effectively. 3 Lightwave and Toaster can run on an Amiga, though SGI's GL performance in Lightwave is superior. 4 The Toaster software could be controlled through ARexx commands, and tools existed that interfaced with the Toaster over ARexx, though not from a modern PC. 56 The 888 DUI was later replaced by a version using an SGI O2, which was affectionately known as the Toaster. 7
