MIPS III

Claims: 8 Part of MIPS

Nekoware is a collection of software primarily for MIPS III processors, which limit software compatibility and usability for daily tasks. 12 The R4k is a MIPS III processor, and MIPS IV is a superset of MIPS III that is compatible with all existing versions of MIPS. 34 qemu can emulate a MIPS III processor, but running IRIX on it is impossible without additional emulation layers. 5 A 1GB 195MHz R10K MXI+TRAM Indigo2 is a MIPS III machine, and the MIPS-IV version compiled with MIPSpro does not necessarily perform better than the MIPS-III version compiled with GCC. 67 MIPS-III generates TLB exceptions at specific addresses, indicating potential issues with virtual to physical address mapping. 8
