General

Unix is a standard, and POSIX is a subset of Unix that can be implemented in any operating system. 12 Some DEC machines ran Unix, including variants such as Ultrix, OSF/1, Digital Unix, and Tru64. 34 SGI's IRIX is based on SYSVR4 and is a real Unix, and its trademark was cancelled in 2020. 5 Sun workstations, HP machines, and SGI machines all run Unix. 67 XNU is POSIX compliant but is not a Unix kernel, while the MacOS kernel is open source along with most of the other Unix bits. 89 Unixes generally cannot swap out kernel code and data, though Mach can but infrequently uses it. 10 The Unix operating system, first officially named Unix, was released in 1970 on the PDP-11, though it was not written in C at that time. 11 Unix and VMS expect interactive usage, unlike many mainframe OSes. 12 OSF/1, Digital UNIX, and Tru64 are different names for the same operating system, with the Alpha versions being rebranded from OSF/1. 1314 Going from Digital UNIX 4.0 to Tru64 was just a name change with minor differences in admin tools. 15 Windows NT was technically inferior to Unix in the 90s, with drawbacks including slowness, crashes, and difficulty in administration, yet it took over the datacenter. 1617 Unix is a family of operating systems that includes BSD, though it is not the same as AT&T Unix, which no longer owns the Unix trademark. 181920 Several systems are certified Unix, including macOS and OS X, though certification for Macs was obtained recently and configuration is needed to be fully Unix-like. 212223 Unix has been ported to many platforms, including Risc-based Macs, Alpha systems running DEC UNIX, and PDP hardware with 256k RAM, and it requires an MMU. 2425262728 Digital UNIX, whose name replaced OSF/1 in version 3.2G, was the most popular OS on Alpha in the late 90s and offered DECwindows/Motif as a graphical environment. 293031 Unix ideas and architecture remain relevant, and Linux implementing Unix paradigms makes it hard for Unix to be displaced soon. 3233 A PowerMac 8100 with a PPC 601 at 80 MHz could play MP3s but not do anything else during playback or encoding. 34 We can consistently boot Unix in serial mode, even multiuser. 35
