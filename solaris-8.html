Solaris 8

Claims: 47 Part of Solaris Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris.

Solaris 8 runs well on the Ultra 5, and can also run on the SunBlade 60, the Blade 2000, and the Blade 150. 1234 It can emulate different environments, including Solaris 8, 9, or 10, and Linux, using branded zones for additional functionality. 5 Solaris 8 and later versions come with Gzip, and Sun's last version with OpenWindows was version 8. 67 Solaris 8 and under use UFS for the file system. 8 The format utility can create partitions larger than 5.7GB on a 40GB disk, and the maximum drive size exceeds any practical SCSI capacity. 910 Solaris 8 supports USB mass storage, but not FireWire, which was supported by Solaris 9. 11 For less than 1GB of RAM, use Solaris 8 or 9; for more, use 10. 12 A Blade 1k can be upgraded to Solaris 10 after a minimal installation of Solaris 8 and firmware update. 13 Solaris 8 can be installed graphically with 32MB of RAM, and CDE starts with 32MB, with 64MB being better and 128MB suitable. 1415 The Blade 100/150 is compatible with Solaris 8 or newer, and a Blade 100 with 512MB of RAM can run both Solaris 8 and 10, though performance is better with upgrades. 1617 Solaris 8 made it easier to obtain, and Solaris 10 could be downloaded from Sun's site. 18 On Solaris 8, XPutImage fails with a shared memory block error. 19
