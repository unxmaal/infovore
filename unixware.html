Unixware

Claims: 12 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris.

SCO Unix comes in different versions like OpenServer and Unixware. 1 UnixWare was a choice for *nix on X86 before Linux, especially if you didn't want RISC stuff. 2 UnixWare can run on a Pentium Pro like Solaris. 3 UnixWare is fast, reliable, and supported big servers, but its development halted after SCO's legal troubles. 4 Installing UnixWare with development system and updating to 2.03 requires a working set of installation disks, which can be difficult to find. 5 SINIX-Z 5.42 is based on SVR4 and is similar to Unixware, though SINIX-Z is not as advanced. 6
