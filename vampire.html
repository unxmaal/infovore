Vampire

Claims: 11

The Vampire is a homebrew Amiga single-board computer that can emulate the Amiga as a keyboard and I/O dock, and can run on a bench without the original hardware. 1 The standalone Vampire model removes the interface, requiring no original hardware. 2 The Vampire is an accelerator board for emulating the CPU, not a complete chipset replacement. 3 The Vampire lacks an MMU. 4 On a metric of straight CPU performance, the experimental pistorm/emu68k builds benchmark 830 MIPS, versus Vampire's advertised 340 MIPS. 5 The current mainline emulator runs as fast as a top 040 CPU, while emu68 is significantly faster, multiple times faster than a Vampire. 6 All Amigas can be upgraded to an 060 processor, and the 600 and CDTV can take a Vampire for more power. 7
