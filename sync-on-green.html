sync on green

Claims: 18

SGI workstations use sync on green, with other pins reserved for monitor auto-detection and the 13w3 connector supporting separate sync for flexibility and future-proofing. 12 All SGI machines use sync on green, including the Indy and Onyx2, and a monitor that accepts sync on green is required for proper display. 13456 Sun adaptors can work on SGI if the monitor supports sync on green, though some monitors may not display an image, and a $20 eBay dipswitch adapter cable can make some cheap LCDs work by connecting or disconnecting conductors. 74 Some monitors do not properly interpret sync on green, causing images to appear green, and a known failure condition of monitors not supporting sync on green could explain display issues. 8910 For SGI sync on green, setting dip 6 is sufficient, and sync on green tolerant cables can be used but may require specific settings. 1112 The Benq BL912 monitor supports SGI sync on green, the 6019 had sync on green and required a TTL sync mixer for PC use, and the RetroTink 4K supports VGA input and sync on green. 131415 A device can convert 13w3 with sync on green and analog or SPDIF audio to HDMI, and a PC CRT with sync on green support is a good choice for vintage computing. 1617
