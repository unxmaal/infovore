HP Vectra 5/90 Series 3

Claims: 3 Part of HP

Windows 95 cannot boot from an NTFS partition on the HP Vectra 5/90 Series 3. 1 When using an IDE to SD adapter, the HP Vectra 5/90 Series 3 requires manual entry of drive geometry via `idediag` or `whatide`. 2
