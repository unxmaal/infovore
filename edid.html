EDID

Claims: 4

Some cards required a PC BIOS for proper initialization, while others had issues with i2c and EDID, leading to resolution problems. 1 The 1920x1024 mode built using EDID timings may not work on CRTs due to reduced blanking. 2 A DVI deserializer + LVDS serializer + fixed EDID can replace the original for dual head output on a 1600SW monitor, or a Realtek IC can scale and output dual channel LVDS from VGA or DVI input. 3 Killing EDID and setting resolution via PROM options fixes KVM booting to X login on O2 machines. 4
