Impact PSU

Claims: 8 Part of Impact

The Impact PSU is highly complex, containing many logic ICs, which makes fault finding difficult. 1 It is more difficult to work with due to small electrolytic caps in hard-to-reach places, especially if the flat copper 'cable' is not removed. 2 In contrast, the Impact PSU can be dismantled easily, unlike the non-Impact model. 3 An Impact PSU with a broken fan can be cannibalized for parts. 4 One user has basically fully recapped an Impact PSU but it still does not work. 5
