thermal paste

Claims: 8

Replacing thermal paste can help address overheating issues, such as those reported with the AMD DV6 laptop, which runs very hot and may require cleaning vents, replacing thermal paste, and possibly reflowing the Northbridge die. 12 Thermal paste can last longer than the useful life of a computer, so older systems may still function well with their original paste. 3 New thermal paste should be considered for the CPU heatsink and fan, and replacing thermal paste on the CPU can serve as a stopgap measure until higher quality pads are available. 45 Thermal paste can cause damage if applied incorrectly, as occurred on a 486 board where the CPU socket was melted but the CPU and board still worked. 6
