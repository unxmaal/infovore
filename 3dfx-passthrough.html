3dfx passthrough

Claims: 3 Part of 3dfx

3dfx passthrough works in VirtualPC 3 but not in SLI. 1 Voodoo3 cards, especially PCI variants, likely work well for 3dfx passthrough because they lack a VBIOS/firmware of their own. 2 Voodoo3 cards, excluding V4/V5, might work for 3dfx passthrough. 3
