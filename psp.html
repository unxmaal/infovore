PSP

Claims: 11

The PSP is a game system that uses a MIPS processor, specifically an R3000 CPU, and is MIPSel rather than the same flavor as other MIPS CPUs, so CPU-specific code fixes may not be simple. 123 The PSP can run PSP, N64, and possibly Dreamcast games, and some MIPS-based systems like the PSP had better N64 emulation than contemporaries due to their MIPS architecture. 45 The PSP is not region-locked, and the PS3, PSP, Wii, and Xbox 360 do not need renewed certs to work. 67 The management engine and PSP are significant vulnerabilities for x86 systems. 8 Porting IRIX to the PSP with its R4400 processor is not realistic but an interesting idea. 9
