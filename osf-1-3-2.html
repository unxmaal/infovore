OSF/1 3.2

Claims: 4 Part of OSF/1

Installing OSF/1 3.2 can crash with a kernel memory error, possibly due to a faulty CPU cache or insufficient cooling. 1 OSF/1 3.2 reports write errors that are fixed on the next boot. 2 OSF/1 3.2 works with 64MB of memory, and 128MB is mostly fine. 3 An old DE500 version works with 3.2, and PCI allows getting 100Mbit ethernet. 4
