IP25

Claims: 7 Part of IP

IP numbers in Onyx desksides indicate the processor board installed, with IP25 corresponding to the R10K processor. 1 The IP25 uses a dual board setup for each processor, with one board stacking onto another and the CPU on top. 2 Early revisions of the IO4 part# require R4K CPUs to work, especially for Onyx/Challenge boards intended for IP25 and InfiniteReality. 3 The Onyx300 has a single card cage with 2x IP25 and 2x MC3 boards, 8x R10K processors, and HVD SCSI disk boxes. 4
