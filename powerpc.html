PowerPC

Claims: 73 Background: 15 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see PowerPC, IBM AIX, FreeBSD, Gentoo Linux, QEMU, Video games and Linux.

PowerPC (Performance Optimization With Enhanced RISC – Performance Computing) is a CPU architecture whose short form is PPC, with variants including PPC, PPCel, PPC64, and PPC64EL. 12 PowerPC adapted the POWER architecture, and many enhancements were added to provide a low-cost, single-chip, superscalar, multiprocessor capable, and 64-bit processor. 3 PowerPC and Power merged, with Altivec in Power6+ and 970 being PPC64. 4 PowerPC implementations can differ in physical silicon, process, and feature support, even among compatible implementations from different manufacturers. 5 Some open source software may not be compatible with all PowerPC processors, such as Apple's PowerPC G4 and G5. 6 Apple adopted the PowerPC architecture for their systems. 7 PowerPC is a CPU architecture used for consumer and embedded applications, as well as some lower-end workstations, and it is distinct from MIPS and POWER. 89 POWER is the high-end server and workstation CPU family, which includes the RS64, POWER, PowerPC, and RS/6000 architectures. 10 PowerPC is exclusively big endian, whereas POWER is bi-endian. 11 SGI produced servers based on PowerPC and SPARC architectures, and its only PowerPC system contained an L2 controller. 1213 Some hard drives contain PowerPC CPUs, such as the one used in Octanes. 14 PowerPC runs a single thread per CPU, whereas MIPS runs two threads per CPU. 15 Debian can be challenging to get working on PowerPC hardware. 16
