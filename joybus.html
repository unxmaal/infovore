Joybus

Claims: 3

A low-end FPGA or CPLD might be a viable solution for converting Serial to Joybus, and there is existing educational material on implementing Joybus on an FPGA, though no complete solution is available. 12 Joybus assumes one device on the port, while USB Host with an FPGA for bitbanging Joybus is considered. 3
