SPARC hardware

Claims: 3 Part of SPARC

SPARC hardware was produced by SGI for a longer period than commonly expected. 1 OpenBSD may have issues on SPARC hardware, so NetBSD is suggested for a lightweight system. 2 Doom II fails to run on SPARC hardware under NextSTEP or OpenSTEP, exiting with a 'Bus error' due to an unused CG framebuffer in a sbus slot. 3
