drive caddy

Claims: 3 Part of drive

The Octane drive caddy requires modifications to add mount points and holds the SCA to 68 board in the back, with an SCA to 68 adapter useful for acards or tape drives. 1 To reassemble the drive caddy, look at the gaps at the back of the lane instead of pin 1. 2 The SCA connector on the drive caddy sticks out 2mm too far, preventing the front latch from engaging the locking pins. 3
