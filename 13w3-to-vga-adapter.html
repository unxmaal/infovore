13W3 to VGA adapter

Claims: 14 Part of 13W3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Component video.

The Sun 13W3 to VGA adapter is not compatible with an Octane. 1 A 13W3 to VGA adapter is needed for SGI machines on a KVM that supports Suns, and it can work with an OpenLDI converter. 23 A 13W3 to VGA adapter is needed if the screen supports SoG; otherwise, a syncstripper can be made, though it is not trivial. 4 There are adapters that convert 13W3 to VGA, and a VGA monitor capable of sync on green is needed, but a BNC step is unnecessary. 56 A 13W3 to VGA adapter with DIP switches ensures the right pinout, which is necessary for compatibility, and can work without changing the switch positions, indicating a compatible video and monitor setup. 75 You can get a 13W3 to VGA adapter for SGI machines easily. 8
