PCI cage

Claims: 8 Part of PCI

The PCI cage for SGI systems comes in two varieties, with the newer one using a ribbon cable for the XIO board and the older one using fiberglass. 1 It uses screws with the same thread pitch as VGA connectors. 2 An Octane can have a PCI cage with a serial port expander for MRI controls, and a PCI cage would enable NVMe support. 34 A Voodoo2 SLI setup and Voodoo3 2000 PCI cards require a PCI cage. 5 An Octane may lock up at POST with a faulty PCI cage, preventing mouse and keyboard from working; removing the cage may resolve the issue. 6 When removing the motherboard, PCI cage, or XIO cards, ensure the system is off and unplugged to avoid damaging the motherboard. 7
