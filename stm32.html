STM32

Claims: 3

The STM32 uses a Vivante GPU capable of 26 million polygons per second, compared to the 14 million polygons per second of the SGX530 GPU, making the STM32 better overall for graphics. 1 A guide on Digikey's blog successfully helped set up a working Debian system on an STM32. 2 The STM32's model significantly impacts speed, with moving from F1 to F4 doubling it, and bus speed to GPIO is also a factor. 3
