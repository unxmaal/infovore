Mojave

Claims: 8

Mojave is a macOS version that has been installed on various Mac computers, including MacBook Pro and Mac Pro (cMP) machines. 12 The Mac Pro 5.1 runs Mojave and will continue to do so until support ends, after which it may be patched or moved to Linux. 3 Mojave works better with stock firmware, so it is recommended to keep the GPU card stock for Mojave. 4 Mojave runs on a Ryzen 2700X and is stable for daily use, with dual booting options for Windows and Hackintosh using OpenCore. 5 Mojave runs productive tasks like Python poorly but runs games like Minecraft and World of Tanks well. 6
