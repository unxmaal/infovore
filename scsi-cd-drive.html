SCSI CD drive

Claims: 4 Part of SCSI CD

Installing 5.3 requires a SCSI CD drive, which may be expensive and not widely available, since it can be installed from a single CD. 1 A SCSI CD drive can be controlled at the drive level. 2 Linux checks if a SCSI CD drive is writable before allowing read-write access, unlike IRIX. 3
