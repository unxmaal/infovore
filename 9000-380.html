9000/380

Claims: 4

The HP 9000/380 is a machine based on the 68040 processor. 1 It uses proprietary RAM, and a minimum of 16 MB is needed for HP-UX 8 or 9. 2 Some units have 8 MB of RAM and originally had none. 3 An HP 9000/380 with a tape drive add-on lacks RAM and requires an HP HIL keyboard and mouse to function. 4
