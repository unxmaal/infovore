lock bar

Claims: 4

The SGI Indigo2 contains a component called a 'lock bar', which is a solid piece of steel that fits in the hand fairly well. 1 Lock bars did not feel great to handle but were not brittle, and security cables were likely installed into the O2's lock bar. 2 The lock bar between the slot modules can cause the 'ass portion' to break off if not removed before taking out the modules. 3 The lock bar's orientation can compress the compression connector, as it is the latching mechanism. 4
