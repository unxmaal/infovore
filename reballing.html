reballing

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Central processing unit.

Reballing involves adding flux to the IC and using a stencil to keep the balls in place, with timing being crucial for removing the stencil. 1 With proper heat application, a jig can make reballing look easy. 2 Reballing an SSD requires specialized tools and solder paste, while reballing the CPU is possible but requires extra work. 34 The stencil for reballing can be ordered from JLCPCB, but it is not necessary as CPUs often come with balls pre-applied. 5 Reballing the analog board of a Macintosh Plus can be painful and potentially carcinogenic due to cap juice. 6 Someone harvests SGI chips from equipment and reballs them; they may come from China and be broken or fake, but his past work has been reliable. 7
