Indycam

Claims: 31 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2, SGI Indy.

The IndyCam is a video capture device that connects through an HDCI port, which is also used by the Indy's video option and the Indigo2's Indycam port. 12 The Indycam can capture digital video through vino, and its software can be set to 640x480 resolution, looking better on 24-bit graphics. 134 The Indycam loads in IRIX without issues, and Indycam support is validated for IRIX 6.5.22. 56 The Indycam has been used to capture images of a dance floor for display on a club's website, and a basketball hand tracking demo existed for it. 78 The Indycam may not be compatible with all Indy hardware, and the Indigo2 does not recognize the IndyCam. 910 The VINO kernel modules are not installed, causing the IndyCam to not be recognized by the system, and Irix 5.2 Hinv lacks the 'inv_vino_indy_nosw' software check, preventing IndyCam detection even with the necessary packages and kernel driver installed. 1112 The Indycam struggles with limited RAM, and stops working when the video for impact is faulty and a stable genlock on the composite input is not achievable. 1314 FFmpeg can convert Indycam video (.mv) to MP4 but may fail after the first second due to a known issue. 15
