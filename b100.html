B100

Claims: 3

The Ultras include slabs (U1, U5, B100, B150), a big slab (U2), and a small tower (U10). 1 Blade 1500s can play Quake 3 and Unreal Tournament, while B100s and U10s can play Doom and Quake 1, with performance varying by graphics card. 2 Quake and Doom with original binaries run well on B100 with an Elite3D lite card, but require dropping resolution to 1024x768 for optimal performance. 3

Sources

  1. Of the Ultras (including Blade), U1, U5, B100 and B150 are slabs, U2 is a big slab. U10 is a small tower. (user-7b1c, 2022-05-22, exchange 29401, check: uncheckable)
  2. Blade 1500s can play Quake 3 and Unreal Tournament, B100s and U10s can play Doom and Quake1, but performance varies by graphics card. (user-237d, 2024-01-19, exchange 218270, check: supported)
  3. Quake and Doom with original binaries run well on B100 with Elite3D lite card, but require dropping resolution to 1024x768 for optimal performance. Forcing XSun into 8-bit mode with Elite3D is challenging and may not be (user-c831, 2024-01-19, exchange 218271, check: supported)
