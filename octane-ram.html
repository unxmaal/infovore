Octane RAM

Claims: 12 Part of Octane

Octane RAM was made by Transcend and differs from IP35 RAM, being closer to standard RAM than O2 RAM but still proprietary and different. 123 It is not expensive, with 1GB costing about £80-£90 and being cheaper than O2 RAM. 456 Installing it involves pulling the motherboard, inserting the RAM, and re-installing the motherboard without touching the pressure pads. 7 It is sought for refurbishing or upgrading, and orders from multiple sites were cancelled as out of stock within a day. 89 It is available for bid on eBay, but vendors selling it through online stores frequently do not ship the purchased items, and it can be unreliable, with some multi-stick orders experiencing bad sticks. 101112
