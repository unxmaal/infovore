SINIX

Claims: 3

SINIX was a UNIX variant that ran on Siemens terminals, such as the SINIX for MX300/Intel. 1 Siemens rebadged workstations and ported its own SINIX to various CPU architectures, including x86, NSC32k, and MIPS. 2 Siemens renamed SINIX to Reliant UNIX to compete with other UNIXes. 3
