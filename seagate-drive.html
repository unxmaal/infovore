Seagate drive

Claims: 10

The Seagate drive is a server model that is not quiet, though it was reported to be quieter and faster than the Fujitsu 15k drive, though benchmarks were not performed. 12 It was purchased for £30 new. 2 The drive requires an SE vs LVD jumper setting, with the force-SE jumper removed for compatibility. 3 It causes the O2 to produce loud vibrations and sonic noises that are amplified when touching certain parts of the machine. 4 The Seagate drive has temperature limits that can be exceeded with the lid off, and the PSU fan speed affects the temperature. 5 The largest IRIX programs are a couple hundred megabytes, so the entire commercial software library could fit on a Seagate drive. 6
