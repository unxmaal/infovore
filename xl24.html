XL24

Claims: 14

The XL24 is a single-height graphics board that is the most common boardset in Indys, and it is more common than the XZ card. 12 hinv can distinguish between the XL24 and XZ graphics cards, and the XL24 can be specified in MAME using the command-line options xl8 and xl24. 31 The XL24 has an ICS1562M clock regeneration chip that the XL8 lacks, and it acts as the main graphics with behavior similar to a console=g1 setting. 45 On R5k systems, XL24 is the preferred setting, and the R5000 Indy works best with a single-height XL24 board. 67 An Indigo2 R4400/200 with an XL24 was a standard unit for various tasks, and the XL24 can be used on an R5k Indy, though its full potential may not be realized due to multitasking limitations. 89 A 150 MHz R5000 Indigo2 with an XL24 can emulate an Indigo2 or Indy at about half speed, with 72 cores allowing parallel emulation of 72 instances, and the XL24 was part of a package deal including the R5K 150SC for $300. 1011 The XZ is extremely uncommon and can be slower than letting the R5k do all of the work depending on workload, and software rendering with a 180MHz R5k and XL24 beats hardware rendering on an XZ. 1213
