MJPEG

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see MPlayer.

MPlayer can decode MJPEG on IRIX. 1 The O2 is good for MJPEG and SGI videos but inefficient for 90s codecs, taking hours to compress a video. 2 Hardware compression options from SGI were limited, with Cosmo Compression doing 640x480 MJPEG only on-the-fly. 3 Rendering to mjpeg, mpg, indeo, or QT mov over NFS or local disc is an option for Indys. 4 The Avid NLE setup required video capture, MJPEG, and SCSI cards. 5
