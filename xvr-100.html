XVR-100

Claims: 19

The XVR-100 is an ATI Radeon 7000 card (Mac Edition) with Sun firmware, running at 66MHz despite being 32-bit. 12345 It can be reflashed from an ATI Radeon 7000 Mac Edition and used in an Ultra 5 for 3D purposes. 16 The XVR-100 has native dual-head capability, unlike other cards. 7 It supports OpenGL and can run at resolutions higher than 1280x1024. 8 The chip has 3D acceleration, but Solaris drivers (including Xsun) only expose 2D acceleration, using a software renderer instead. 910111213 Under Linux, the XVR-100 can do 3D acceleration like a Radeon 7500, though there are no good Linux drivers for it. 1214 It works with Xsun, Xorg, and CDE on Solaris 10, and is supported by OpenBSD (with better results for the XVR-100 than the XVR-1200). 151617 On the SGI 2500, the card required a kernel recompile with a lower securelevel to initialize properly. 18
