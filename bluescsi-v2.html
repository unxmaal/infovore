BlueSCSI V2

Claims: 28 Part of BlueSCSI Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see A/UX.

BlueSCSI V2 is an open hardware and software fork of ZuluSCSI's SCSI2SD implementation, described as a significant improvement over version 1 and likely suitable for many systems. 1 It is one of the cheapest SCSI emulators, with boards and BOM build costing around $50-70 for five units from jlcpcb or pcbway, plus a pi pico and SD card. 23 It works well on Fuel and O2, providing faster seek times and quieter operation for the O2, and works well in an SGI Indy for disk imaging. 456 It works on the R10k Indigo2 for CD emulation but is slow for main HDD use, and works on the R12k O2 and R4k Indigo2, though it requires a clock-delay option on the O2 to avoid write errors. 78 It is considered a better fork than ZuluSCSI for SGI use, with better firmware for SGI compatibility, though it is a cost-cut derivative of ZuluSCSI from a hardware perspective. 91011 It is cheaper and compatible with SGI systems, but SD cards sometimes have issues, and high-speed SCSI emulation with ultrascsi-320 speeds is desired but currently unavailable. 12 On IRIX it lacks a hardware eject button and cannot cycle CD-ROM images by dragging to trash, though the hardware has a switch that could be used to switch CD images during installation. 1314 It can cause issues with the user-interface for 4DW on an Indigo2, including menu function inoperability and loss of user-theming, and may have firmware issues not present in Zulu firmware. 1516
