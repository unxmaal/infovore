HIPPI

Claims: 15

HIPPI is a high-speed networking system used in HPC between nodes, more efficient than Ethernet at the time. 1 HIPPI operated at 800 Mbit/s or 1600 Mbit/s, faster than FDDI (100 Mbit/s) and typical ATM speeds (25, 45, 155, or 622 Mbit/s). 2 HIPPI was expensive and exclusive to HPC, and was used for fast networks before Ethernet became prevalent. 34 SCRAMNet and HIPPI were parallel technologies. 5 SGI systems such as the Challenge XL, Onyx, Onyx2, Octane2, and Origin350 supported HIPPI, and the Challenge XL used HIPPI VME boards for network communication. 678 HIPPI supported MPI over networks, allowing MPI programs to spawn threads across different nodes, and can provide multiple point-to-point links using serial cards with multimode optics. 910
