Prism brick

Claims: 12 Part of Prism

The Prism brick requires a separate Altix compute module to operate, using 'g' notation for graphics slots. 1 The Prism brick has an Altix IO topology with specific PCI segment, PCIBUS, brick, rack, slot, bus, and connection details. 2 The FireGL X1s with HP firmware do not work in the Prism brick; the system locks up when trying to load the EFI. 3 The Prism brick requires specific AGP card configurations; it crashes with a GeForce 2 Ti and doesn't detect a Radeon 9500. 4 Prism setup with Altix 350 base node and Prism brick provides DVI output via ATI FireGL X3 cards. 5 The Prism brick drops to CAC mode when connected to a 4K monitor via DVI to HDMI adapter but works fine with a VGA monitor. 6 The Prism brick can get stuck at 'L1 Booting' and outputs 'setup_tdrdata_shub()' when not in the rack, and moving it fixes it. 7 Ordi-spare in France has an out-of-stock Prism brick. 8
