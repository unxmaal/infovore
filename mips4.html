MIPS4

Claims: 22 Part of MIPS Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

MIPS4 is backwards compatible with MIPS3 software, which runs on MIPS4 CPUs such as the R5000. 123 MIPS4 CPUs cannot run COFF software. 4 The R8000 implements the MIPS4 instruction set, which includes reciprocal and reciprocal square root instructions. 5 The MIPS4 architecture does not have vectorization instructions that GCC can compile for directly, nor does it have the byteswap instruction found in MIPS32. 67 N32 binaries are compiled for MIPS4, as indicated by the string. 8 There are performance differences between MIPS3 and MIPS4 on SGI machines such as the R10k. 9
