IRC

Claims: 17 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Discord.

IRC is a cross-platform, text-only communication protocol that works on a wide range of machines and operating systems, from DOS and Apple II to Windows 10. 12 It is a surprisingly simple protocol, though it has both advantages and disadvantages with room for improvement. 34 IRC is used less now, with Telegram and Discord being preferred. 5 Discord's free service is well-managed compared to IRC, which had frequent issues such as annoying 'Connection reset by peer' errors. 67 IRC can expose IP addresses, but this can be mitigated with a VPN, and chat history is easily enabled in most clients. 89 Because it is text-only, avoiding emojis and GIFs, virus sharing is harder. 210 Matrix, which is end-to-end encrypted, FOSS, and has bridges for IRC, Slack, and Discord, now uses Mattermost to show IRC messages on Matrix. 1112
