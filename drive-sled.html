drive sled

Claims: 21 Part of drive Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

A drive sled is a metal component marked 'SEMCO' that uses a rotating, locking handle mechanism to move onto pins. 12 The metal serves as EMI shielding, and the rails narrow toward the front to hold the drive snugly. 3 Drive sleds can be challenging to install and some can break, with HP plastic being brittle and prone to frequent breakage. 45 The front plastic piece of a drive sled has broken off and ended up inside the case. 6 A drive sled can be made from cardboard, and a little tape on either side can keep it locked in place. 78 Drive sleds from a non-indigo system can be used with 3.5-inch SCSI drives. 9 Ideally, an adapter would allow mounting an M2 SSD in a drive sled for SGI systems. 10
