Power Challenge

Claims: 7 Part of POWER

The Power Challenge is a Silicon Graphics workstation that ships with an R8k CPU, and can be upgraded to a Power Challenge L 10000 by swapping in a quad R10k board. 12 The Onyx 10000 used an R10k CPU, and there was no Challenge 10000 model. 2 A PowerChallenge can run with 3x quad R4400@250MHz, 3x dual R8000@75MHz, or 3x quad R10K, and is used for compiling, running IRIX 6.2, and 3D rendering. 3 IRIX 6.0 supports up to eighteen processors and 16 gigabytes of physical memory on POWER CHALLENGE/POWER Onyx platforms. 4 The PowerChallenge can render textured polygons quickly but struggles with untextured ones, possibly due to fillrate limitations or framebuffer reads. 5 A PowerChallenge can fail when rebuilding a Crimson, and restoring an Onyx can also be disrupted by hardware failures. 6
