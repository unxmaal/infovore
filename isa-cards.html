ISA cards

Claims: 10 Part of ISA

ISA cards can be used in EISA slots without an adapter in some systems. 1 EISA cards have intentionally longer fingers and more notches to prevent ISA cards from interfering with EISA signals, and the EISA bus slot has five keying notches to prevent ISA cards with long traces from extending into the lower part of the slot. 23 Amiga bridgeboards allowed the use of ISA cards with Amiga systems by connecting to a Zorro slot. 4 ISA cards work with PPC NT and likely other cards work too, though they were primarily for NT. 5
