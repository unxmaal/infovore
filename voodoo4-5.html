Voodoo4/5

Claims: 3 Part of Voodoo

Voodoo4/5 used multiple VSA-100 chips, but multiple cards could not be combined for SLI. 1 AGP cards are fragile because interleaved pins can be damaged if not fully inserted, especially in later models such as the 6800 Ultra. 2 Voodoo4/5 require BIOS initialization for video encoding settings. 3
