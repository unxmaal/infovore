AV board

Claims: 4

The AV board is a SAS HBA with flash cache rather than a full RAID controller, contains SPARC chips, and can be used with ZFS as JBOD. 1 The O2 can be upgraded to 400 MHz and has 800 MB of RAM, an A/V board with a Moosecam port and FPA, and does not need additional components. 2 The Moosecam port is optional, and some boards may have the port without the circuit. 2 The SGI AV board supports composite SD I/O, while the Ensemble Carbon breakout supports component (analogue) I/O. 3 The WeatherStar XL runs on an O2 and outputs over the AV board, but without TWC's services it does not pull any data. 4
