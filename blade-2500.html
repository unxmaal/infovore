Blade 2500

Claims: 21

The Sun Blade 2500 is one of the only vaguely modern SPARC workstations, alongside the Blade 1500 and the Ultra 25/45. 1 It started as an Ultra 10, was upgraded to the Sun Blade 150, and then became the Sun Blade 2500. 2 The 2500 can be considered a dual-CPU 1500, but they are different. 3 The Blade 2500 is faster than the SS20. 4 The Blade 2500, V240, and V245 differ mostly cosmetically, with the V245 using SAS drives and the Blade potentially having faster CPUs. 5 The SGI Blade 2500 is equivalent to the V240 with a desktop case and integrated sound, but has one BGE instead of four. 6 The V250 is similar to a Blade 2500 without the sound module and with better SCSI. 7
