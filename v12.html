V12

Claims: 111 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane, V12 engine.

With V10

The Octane2 can handle dual-head and DCD options with both V10 and V12, supporting up to four displays. 1 An Octane2 upgrade from V10 to V12 may require ensuring the V12 carrier is fully seated, otherwise the system may not boot. 2 V12 and V10 have the same cutouts on the back and require Xbox rev. 1.4 (Xbow 1.4). 345 V10 and V12 cards use different RAM chip form factors: the V10 uses taller, larger packages, while the V12 uses flatter, smaller ones, and the V8 and V12 chips are twice the size of the V10 chip. 678 The texture memory capacity of V6 and V10 is 8MB, and for V8 and V12 it is 104MB, with total memory of 32MB for V6 and V10 and 128MB for V8 and V12. 9 V10 and V12 models can have different RAM configurations, with V12 cards often having more RAM but sometimes limited to 32MB with resistors and a Dallas EEPROM chip. 10 V12 models have twice as fast geometry processing as V10 models, but the practical impact is uncertain. 11 V12 was the only model sold with the DVI-I output connector, but V6 and V10 can use it for DVI-I output. 12

General

The V12 is a graphics card that can display a resolution of 3840x1200 and supports all display modes for 1920x1080 and 1920x1200 by standard. 1314 The V12 uses higher capacity memory chips with the same board layout. 15 The V12 can support Dual Channel Display (DCD) for two DVI ports, but the V8 and V10 may run out of VRAM. 16 The DCD for a V12 is expensive, with the DCD alone being worth a fair bit. 17 The V12 is comparable to TNT2 in performance and was more powerful than the V6. 1819 The V12 has display issues like clipping and serious glitches when running certain software. 2021 The V12 is more versatile and is recommended for running really old software. 2223 A V12 can be obtained for $300. 24 The V12 is a powerful machine with great specs, and it has more resources than the V6, though it is not a significant upgrade over the V6. 252627 The V12 was about twice as fast as the Cobalt, and it is preferred by some users. 2829 The Octane was originally green with MXE and can be upgraded to a 600 MHz V12, and people often upgraded to 600 MHz after having a V12 CPU. 3031 The V12 can potentially be upgraded to 256MB VRAM using 128 Mbit SDRAM by modifying the memory chip, but compatibility and system support are uncertain. 32 GeForce 3 had 64MB VRAM as of February 27, 2001, and 128MB as of October 1, 2001, while the SGI V12 had 128MB on January 12, 2001. 33 Quake3 gets about 30fps on a V12 in most configurations due to IRIXisms that cap FPS at half the monitor refresh rate. 34 The Onyx4 UltimateVision loses compatibility with ATI cards but is much faster than a V12, and the FireGL X1 in an Onyx4 is significantly faster than the V12 for GPU-intensive tasks. 3536 The Tezro has 1GB of texture memory and outperforms the V12 in both geometry and graphical performance when running VMD, and the Tezro graphics have better memory bandwidth for V12. 3738
