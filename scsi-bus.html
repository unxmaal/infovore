SCSI bus

Claims: 26 Part of SCSI Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SCSI, Solid-state drive.

The SCSI bus is a shared connection used to link internal and external devices, as on the Indy where both share the same bus. 1 The SCSI bus is often slow, limiting performance for hard drives, as seen on the Indigo2, Octane, and most SGI machines. 234 The Indigo's SCSI bus runs at 5MB/s, and an SSD upgrade can improve SCSI bus performance and random I/O. 56 The SCSI bus can be the limiting factor for sequential I/O, so an SSD is not faster than a fast spinning disk in that case. 7 Termination matters: a hung SCSI bus can prevent booting, and the Indy's bus can be problematic without a terminator. 89 Sun SCSI terminators are used to terminate Sun SCSI buses. 10 The drive's ID affects the SCSI bus; ID 3 causes hangs while ID 1 does not. 11
