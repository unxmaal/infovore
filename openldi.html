OpenLDI

Claims: 11

OpenLDI was proposed as an industry standard for digital flatscreens in the late-90s, but DVI eventually gained more traction and took off instead. 12 OpenLDI was designed to interface directly with a bare LCD panel, requiring the video card to handle all scaling and interfacing, while DVI displays had internal scalers. 34 SGI standardized a few things but made them proprietary, with OpenLDI being a notable example. 5 OpenLDI works only with OpenLDI monitors, which are limited to a specific model. 6
