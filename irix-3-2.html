IRIX 3.2

Claims: 9 Part of IRIX 3

It is expected to have SCSI problems similar to IRIX 3.3, and the PI 25 has SCSI errors when installing IRIX 3.2, which do not occur with the Professional Iris. 12 IRIX 3.2 may be incompatible with EPROM version 3.0, which might be too old. 3 Unlike RISCos 4.5 from 1991, IRIX 3.2 from 1990 has shared libraries. 4 A 4DDN 1.1 tape for IRIX 3.2 exists. 5
