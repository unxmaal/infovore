VME card

Claims: 7 Part of VME

The Iris 4D/35 Computer ships with a VME card. 1 The VME card requires the base option disc for GPIB Maint software to be useful; without it, the software is useless for 4.0x and older systems. 2 The VME card has an original HDD, but its current state is unknown. 3
