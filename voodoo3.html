Voodoo3

Claims: 17

The Voodoo3 is a PCI graphics card that supports 2D rendering and uses Warp3D for 3D graphics acceleration. 123 It runs at 215 MHz with a core voltage of 2.65V, requires active cooling, and can be used as a pure 3D accelerator with manual setup of GPU, memory, and clocks. 45 Voodoo3 and Voodoo4/5 video cards do not require VBIOS execution to function. 6 A 200MHz 603e processor is fine with a Voodoo3, and because it is PCI, it does not benefit from AGP, so there is no performance loss when using PCI. 78 People use PCI graphics cards such as the Voodoo3 in Elbox PCI Amigas, and a ppc+060 Amiga 3000 with a Voodoo3 is one such configuration. 910 The PI runs on a Voodoo 3 and can also run on a Colbalt in OpenGL mode. 11 An 800 MHz G4/Voodoo3 runs Quake III Arena playably, and modding the cards allows dual Voodoo3 PCI cards to work with FXAA. 1213
