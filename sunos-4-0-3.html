SunOS 4.0.3

Claims: 3 Part of SunOS

SunOS 4.0.2 and a beta of 4.0.3 were available before OpenWindows. 1 The 'setenv version2? false' command is necessary to make SunOS 4.0.3 boot on a machine with a version 2.X boot prom, and it works on the IPC but not on the SPARCstation 1. 2 SunOS 4.0.3 is not recommended for new features; SunOS 4.1.4 or 4.1.3 are better options. 3
