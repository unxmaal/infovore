5V

Claims: 9

SAS or SATA controllers for the Ultra 10 must be 5V and 33MHz/32bit PCI, since it does not support PCI-X or higher voltage controllers. 1 The Indy uses 72pin DIMM RAM at 5V with parity, while mid-'90s Power Macs and PC Compatibility Cards take 5V, 168-pin, FPM/EDO DIMMs. 23 A MIPS-based Cobalt RaQ uses a PSU providing both 12V and 5V, with the bottom system board converting 12V to 5V; a missing load on 12V can cause issues such as resets during kernel loading. 4 One PSU provides four isolated outputs—twice 12V and twice 5V—with terminals 1, 4, and 5 connected to common ground, making terminal 2 provide -5V and terminal 6 provide -12V. 5 Compute logic runs on 5V, hard disks on +12V, and the fan tray on -12V in a PowerSeries PSU. 6 A PCI adapter supports 3.3V and 5V, 64-bit PCI at 33/66MHz and PCI-X at 66/100/133MHz, with a bridge converting to x4 PCIe Gen 1. 7

Sources

  1. SAS or SATA controllers for Ultra 10 must be 5V and 33MHz/32bit PCI, as it does not support PCI-X or higher voltage controllers. (user-237d, 2020-12-05, exchange 239273, check: supported)
  2. The Indy uses 72pin DIMM RAM at 5V with parity. (user-0251, 2021-08-22, exchange 99792, check: supported)
  3. The mid-'90s Power Macs and PC Compatibility Cards take 5V, 168-pin, FPM/EDO DIMMs. (user-baf6, 2022-05-25, exchange 182124, check: supported)
  4. MIPS-based Cobalt RaQs use a PSU that provides both 12 V and 5 V, with the bottom system board converting 12 V to 5 V; missing load on 12 V can cause issues like resets during kernel loading. (user-6e7b, 2023-12-29, exchange 240218, check: supported)
  5. This PSU provides 4 isolated outputs: twice 12V and twice 5V. Terminals 1, 4, and 5 connect to common ground, making terminal 2 provide -5V and terminal 6 provide -12V. (user-86e1, 2023-10-18, exchange 109229, check: supported)
  6. This is a PowerSeries PSU. Compute logic runs on 5V, hard disks on +12V, and the fan tray on -12V. The fan tray likely runs from -12V to ensure a load, and the IRIS 3K might be similar. (user-86e1, 2023-10-18, exchange 109229, check: supported)
  7. This PCI adapter supports 3.3V and 5V, 64-bit PCI at 33/66MHz and PCI-X at 66/100/133MHz, with a bridge converting to x4 PCIe Gen 1. (user-757f, 2026-01-08, exchange 225884, check: supported)
