Solaris 2.3

Claims: 3 Part of Solaris Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARCstation 20.

Solaris 2.3 runs on a SPARCstation 20 with a 75 MHz SuperSPARC-II and 128 MB RAM. 1 Building GCC binaries for Solaris 2.3 was challenging. 2 The _curses_ library builds on Solaris 2.3 but not on Tru64, suggesting Tru64 has issues or the library is not compatible. 3
