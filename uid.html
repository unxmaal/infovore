UID

Claims: 4

Most Unix-like operating systems represent the UID as an unsigned integer with varying sizes, from 15-bit to 16-bit, allowing up to 32767 or 65536 unique IDs. 1 Changing the UID requires re-chowning the home directory for NFS permissions. 2 Keeping the same UID as the SGI ensures seamless NFS permissions. 3 Multiple passwd names with UID 0 have been used. 4
