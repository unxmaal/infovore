Netra

Claims: 15

The Netra is a telecom-certified line that is a repackaged SPARCstation 4 with NEBS compliance, 48V supply options, earthquake ratings, and possibly extra shielding; the PROM identifies it as a SPARCstation 4. 12 It has a PCI slot for options like Ethernet, SCSI, or crypto accelerators. 3 The U60 mainboard can use 440 MHz CPUs, as evidenced by a Netra version using this configuration, and the Netra with the U80 board should work with compatible CPU modules. 45 The original Netra fans are ball bearing and rated for 60,000 hours at 25C, while the replacement sleeve fans are rated for 40,000 hours at 40C, which is comparable. 6 A Disk On Module IDE flash drive in a Netra seems good but has the same IDE controller as U5/10, and the Netra version of the machine hangs when attempting DMA, requiring the driver to be forced to use PIO. 78 A PCI USB card works in a Netra, but the card guide may need repair. 9
