TRAM module

Claims: 12 Part of TRAM

The TRAM module can resolve texture RAM complaints in SGI machines, but may not be necessary for all configurations. 1 HighImpact has texture memory that can be expanded to 4MB with a TRAM module, while SolidImpact does not have texture memory. 2 The SSE with a TRAM module can render GL, while the other SSE requires software rendering. 3 To fix a failed TRAM module, reflowing the solder connections with a soldering iron and flux, or a hot air rework station, can restore functionality. 4 For Indigo 2 TRAM issues, detailed guides and forums exist, such as those on sgi.sh and irixnet.org, which describe the process of fixing TRAMs. 5
