OpenGL extensions

Claims: 3 Part of OpenGL

GL_ARB_multitexture and GL_SGIS_multitexture are extensions to OpenGL, with ARB being more modern and supported on modern hardware. 1 Quake 1-3 check OpenGL extensions for compatibility, but their method is incompatible with OpenGL 3.0 and up. 2 The x11 version of the CAD system runs well at high scale, but the xgl version seg faults despite having the correct libraries and GLUT installed; it lacks necessary OpenGL extensions. 3
