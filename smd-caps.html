SMD caps

Claims: 3

The A4000 Rev. B motherboard requires removal of a Varta NiCd barrel battery and cleaning of the area around it to avoid damage to memory bus traces, and its SMD caps tend to leak and should be replaced. 1 The SMD caps in the Octane are not the best, especially from the 90s. 2 On the Plus, recapping the logic board is pretty unnecessary unless there's visible damage, whereas for the classic and SE/30, SMD caps often fail. 3
