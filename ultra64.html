Ultra64

Claims: 14

The original development hardware for the N64 was an SGI Indy with an Ultra64 dev board, and prototype Ultra64 consoles exist. 12 The Ultra64 board plugs into the Indy, is rare, and has a typo on its silkscreen. 3 The Ultra64 uses regular RDRAM, and it is possible to add more memory using a second set of RDRAM chips. 4 The board has problems, and the longer it sits, the more damage occurs to the PCB. 5 The Ultra64 board is flaky and prone to overheating and drawing too much power, requiring a fully spec'd and refurbished PSU to avoid issues. 6 The RDP in the Ultra64 is a prototype ASIC still in an experimental phase. 7 The Ultra64 board schematics are available in older leaks, except for the CPU, which is in an encrypted HDL format. 8 The Ultra64 emulator is spread out with different pieces in different places: IOSIM, RDPSIM2, RSPSIM, and TOOLS\-EMULATE, and requires compiling them as libraries, then compiling and linking them to your game to run. 9
