R4400 Indigo2

Claims: 5 Part of R4400

The R4400 Indigo2 with XZ is not necessarily the same speed as an R4400 Indy with XZ. 1 Impact graphics, especially in the R4400 Indigo2, had texture memory limitations before Quake, though the UPA bus is fast enough that dedicated texture RAM is not a critical issue for Suns. 2 The R4400 Indigo2 has a PSU that can be recapitulated, but the user is a beginner and needs to research parts. 3 vim 5.6 runs great on the R4400 Indigo2, much better than vim 7. 4 The R4400 Indigo2 maxes out at 384MB RAM. 5
