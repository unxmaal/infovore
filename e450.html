E450

Claims: 10

The Sun E450 is a deskside-sized server model capable of holding a large number of CPUs, RAM, and disks, competing with machines such as the Challenge L. 1 It is known for its ability to handle a large number of disks with hardware RAID support. 2 The E450 is a server model with only PCI slots, while the Ultra 450 and Enterprise 450 have UPA slots for horizontal video cards. 3 The horizontal versions of the Elite3d fit the Ultra2, E450, and various enterprise servers. 4 The Ultra 80 is a well-designed machine with limited expansion options compared to the E450. 5 The O2000 might be faster than the E450 series on CPU and memory-bound workloads with equal CPU count and RAM size. 6 The E450 is overkill for diskless use, but it offers good hardware for that purpose. 7 To configure an IP for a Sun E450 over network boot, add the Ethernet address to /etc/ethers and the corresponding hostname to /etc/hosts. 8
