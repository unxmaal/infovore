tantalum capacitor

Claims: 20 Background: 13 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Tantalum capacitor.

The outer two leads of a tantalum capacitor are internally connected together, which makes it impossible to install the capacitor backwards and simplifies hand assembly and early machine insertion. 12 A tantalum capacitor may fail when powered for the first time after a long period. 3 A tantalum capacitor on the VME mezzanine board failed short, and the tantalum capacitor in the 4D/380 can fail, releasing magic smoke; the same PP2 assembly is used in PowerSeries and Crimson desksides. 45
