PSU fan

Claims: 11 Part of PSU

Replacing the PSU fan with a Delta fan can help with cooling issues on SGI systems. 1 On the O2, replacing the PSU fan with a Pabst fan can improve cooling, while Noctua fans are not recommended for O2 PSU replacements due to insufficient CFM. 23 The Octane's PSU fan cools the entire system rather than just the power supply, serves as its one main active exhaust, and can be loud. 45 The PSU fan is the primary mover of air on MGRAS Tram option graphics boards, with heat escaping through upper grilles and dual CPU setups. 6 The PSU fan is noisy, as are the ancient SCSI drives. 7 The PSU fan spins but the Fuel has no sense line to detect it. 8 The PSU fan is manually controlled and spins fine. 9
