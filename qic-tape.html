QIC tape

Claims: 20

QIC tape is most likely used only for storage. 1 SunOS 4.1.x and SYS-V/88 can be installed from QIC tape, though the SYS-V/88 process is tedious and seek times may be problematic due to sequential access. 23 SoftImage 2.66 on QIC tape can import video from VideoLab and VideoFramer, but this requires licenses. 4 A QIC tape for a 3/470 is available but untested. 5 AIX servers used 8mm or QIC tape types. 6 The Archive units in most old SGI machines are unreliable for QIC tape recovery, so a Tandberg TDC3660 is recommended. 7
