SysV

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Unix.

SysV ran on many SGI machines, where most versions of Unix offer either a poor SysV or a poor BSD environment, and the SysV environment is often better for building GNU software. 12 Persistent Unix on SGI machines combines SysV and BSD features. 3 SunOS 4 was a good BSD combining 4.2, 4.3, and some SysV extensions. 4 illumos/OpenIndiana and OpenSolaris are the only open source SysV distros. 5 The A3000UX runs SysV, which can be run on WinUAE for easier use. 6 SYSV detects the serial card and reconfigures the kernel, but the system locks up on reboot. 7 Being SysV was never a technical goal and changed a lot over time. 8
