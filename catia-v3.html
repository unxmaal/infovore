CATIA V3

Claims: 8 Part of CATIA

CATIA V3 was the last mainframe version of CATIA, with V4 being entirely UNIX-based. 1 CATIA V3 ran on AIX v2, which was used for the IBM RT PC (6150/6151). 2 CATIA V3 was ported from the mainframe to the RT-PC and RS/6000 with varying GPU support. 3 Initially, CATIA V3 required an older IBM graphics terminal on RS/6000 hardware, but later supported newer graphics options. 4 By 1993, CATIA V3 ran on an RS/6000 with IBM GPU cards. 5 CATIA V3 for AIX RT requires a 5085 graphics unit. 6 The 5080 can do raster graphics, but CATIA V3 typically shows a blue background. 7
