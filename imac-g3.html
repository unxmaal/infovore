iMac G3

Claims: 21 Part of iMac Background: 8 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see IMac G3.

The iMac G3 came with a keyboard and mouse, and its CPU speed can be found on the sticker in the I/O cubby. 12 The iMac G3 saved Apple after 1999, and iMac G3s were discarded but are now sought after in good condition. 34 Blueberry iMac G3s can yellow over time, especially the gray plastics under the clear plastic, and a yellowed white subframe might be brittle and unsuitable for repair. 56 A multicolored iMac G3 'Harlequin' could be assembled using various parts, and a DIY G4 CPU, a Sonnet Harmoni G3+FW CPU, and/or a Voodoo 2 Mezzanine card could be installed in the iMac G3. 78 The iMac G3 can run System 7 due to its CHRP remnants, and the front IR on the Bondi iMacs connects with a MiniDIN8 under the plastic bottom cover, with the monitor connecting with a DB-15 connector. 910 The monitor port on the iMac G3 can be used to troubleshoot power faults, and the iMac G3 might have a video cable issue after being flipped upside down. 1112 The iMac G3's inner bezel issue is difficult to repair and may require a replacement or 3D printing a new part, and the first generation of an iMac G3 is likely a Rev A. 1314
