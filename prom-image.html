PROM image

Claims: 4 Part of PROM

The O2 can be modified to run a 600 MHz CPU with a firmware update, which involves burning a specific PROM image. 1 The PROM image for Irix has a header recognized by /sbin/flashinst, which is not written to the flash chip but only the payload after 0x100; removing the flash IC from an O2 motherboard and dumping it won't include this 2 The md5sum of the PROM image may vary between machines due to environment segments, and the provided md5sum does not match known IRIX installation disk PROM images. 3
