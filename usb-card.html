USB card

Claims: 10 Part of USB

The InfinitePerformance came with a USB card, and the bulkhead area for PS/2 and extra serial ports is missing, suggesting the USB card replaced it. 1 An O2 can have a USB card installed in the PCI slot, though it may not be fully supported, and installing a USB card and booting through PCI device is an alternative method. 23 A button cell battery is potted in a specific form factor for a USB card, but can be modified by soldering a CR2032 holder. 4 The Octane indicates the presence of a USB card but may not provide detailed information about it. 5 A USB card and USB mouse/keyboard can be used for serial console access. 6 The Sun USB card uses an NEC USB controller, and a specific driver is needed for the USB card to work with the machine. 78
