Model M keyboard

Claims: 16

The IBM Model M keyboard was designed for a professional environment. 1 It has a super key, which is missing on some other keyboards. 2 The Model F PCB is more durable than the Model M membrane, and the Model F has a sharper, crisper tactile feel with a lighter tactile bump that takes less force to actuate. 34 The new-fangled reproduction Model F keyboard is a mixed experience compared to the regular Model M. 5 The granite Bigfoot keyboards are not comparable to the Model M, as they use relatively tame and quiet Alps switches and have a different layout. 6 A Model M keyboard was available for 85 € on local eBay. 7
