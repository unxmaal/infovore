powerflip

Claims: 9

powerflip is a 3D model viewer where stuff spins around and can display animated 3D backgrounds like a star field with spinning SGI cubes. 12 It is included in the basic demos on the IRIX 6.5 foundations discs, alongside amesh, doom, and others. 3 powerflip runs decently in windowed mode on the Indy, but it does not run most of the demos because it requires a Z buffer. 45 For a 3D demo like PowerFlip to run at 60fps on an O2, it requires specific specs, as demonstrated by Dodoid's O2 running PowerFlip slowly. 6 The CRC screen compare in diagnostics shows images from the left screen of the powerflip model on big iron before InfiniteReality was released. 7 powerflip is not available in the SGI demos disk images. 8
