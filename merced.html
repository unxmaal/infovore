Merced

Claims: 12

Merced was the IA-64 project, which was canceled due to fabrication issues and falling behind Intel and HP. 1 The Merced system from SGI, the 750, was killed almost instantly. 2 The Merced processor had a software emulation mode for x86 instructions, but it never had hardware emulation. 3 Merced can transparently boot x86 OSes with appropriate firmware and loader/emulation. 4 The SGI Itanium (Merced) box includes Windows, Linux, HP-UX, AIX, and VMS as OS choices. 5 There was an experimental port for Merced on the Origin 2000. 6
