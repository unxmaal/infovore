OpenStep for Solaris

Claims: 4 Part of OPENSTEP Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXTSTEP.

OpenStep for Solaris is a series of libraries and tools that can be installed on top of Solaris 2.6-10 (SPARC only, 32-bit, Sol9/Sol10 need a special repackaging) to provide OpenStep libraries and the NeXT DE. 1 OPENSTEP for Solaris can run on Solaris 8/9/10 as 32-bit binaries, and it works on a Blade 1000. 2 OpenStep for Solaris can be convinced to work on Solaris 10. 3
