SSD

Claims: 101 Background: 24 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Solid-state drive, IMac G3, SCSI, SGI O2.

SSDs can offer faster disk read and write speeds than traditional spinning-head disks, including better performance than Maxtor Ultra 320 and U320 15K drives for random writes (9.74 MB/sec versus 1.24 MB/sec) and for compiling. 1234 However, SSDs are not always faster: they can be outperformed by high-capacity HDDs for the first 256MB of sequential data, are not faster than fast spinning disks for sequential I/O when the SCSI bus is the limiting factor, and can run slower than HDDs under IRIX 6.5.22. 567 SSD performance depends on the workload and interface, with SCSI-to-SSD converters allowing connection to older SCSI busses but at high cost and with speed limitations, and boot time improvements depending on file system structure. 68910 SSDs can boot on various systems, including IRIX, and support multibooting via partitions and USB drives, though some configurations fail to boot, such as an SSD on an IP35 SAS adapter. 11121314 SSDs are used in many machines to improve general speed and usability, though they do not improve SGI emulation performance, and some systems have IDE bus capacity limits such as a 120GB maximum for the Cube. 151617 SSD reliability and economics vary, with vendors caught swapping NAND types, consumer drives not recommended over enterprise drives, costs potentially too high, and read-only operation theoretically avoiding write wear. 18192021 SSDs offer significantly higher IOPS than HDDs, with drives like the Samsung EVO 860 reaching 60,000 IOPS compared to an HDD's 100-200 IOPS. 22 This higher IOPS can improve performance in various systems, including faster compile speeds on an O2, better responsiveness on older hardware like a Sun Blade 150 or Power Mac G4, and improved virtual memory speeds. 232425 SSDs can be used on an O2, which has a 40MB SCSI controller that can use an SSD, though it is not very useful. 2627 An O2 can run 24/7 with an SSD. 28 SSDs can reduce power consumption compared to spinning disks and may improve cooling, though typical 2.5" SATA SSDs do not get very hot during operation. 2930 Performance improvements depend on the SATA level, and some SSDs may come up at half speed due to compatibility issues or be limited by SATA bandwidth. 313233 SSDs can be connected to older systems through various adapters, including IDE-to-SATA, SATA-to-SCSI, and M.2 SATA to IDE adapters. 343536 Some SSDs, such as certain Hitachi and NetApp branded drives, may lack the ability to set sector size and require careful low-level formatting. 37
