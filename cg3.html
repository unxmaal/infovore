CG3

Claims: 8

The CG3 is an analogue, frame-by-frame video board that produces genlocked PAL or NTSC video from the RGB output of a 4D IRIS, requiring programming to do anything. 12 The CG3 uses standard V/HSYNC, while the CG6 supports CSYNC (and possibly SoG). 3 The sparcclassic has a CG3 onboard video, while the IPX has a CG6, and the CG3 is better. 4 IPC systems were upgraded to CG3, and Nettrek was played on Classics in the computer lab, possibly using IPX protocol. 5 Specific SBus expansion cards like CG3, Videopix, and GT are not compatible with the adapter, suggesting it has limitations such as DMA issues. 6 The CG3's have cap problems. 7
