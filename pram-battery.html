PRAM battery

Claims: 8

The 660 series Macs require a good PRAM battery. 1 Removing the main battery and PRAM battery may allow the PowerBook to turn on if the PRAM battery is dead. 2 The PowerBook G4 17" has a PRAM battery that can be replaced like a gameboy cart SRAM battery. 3 The PRAM battery on a PowerBook G4 has two 3.7V lithium coins wired in parallel with a non-standard layout. 4 The lipo version of the PRAM battery in a PowerBook G4 is meant to retain time only for a short period when the system is unplugged. 5 The NeXTStation requires a good PRAM battery to turn on; the battery is used for soft power on. 6
