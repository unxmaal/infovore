CPU issue

Claims: 3 Part of CPU

  • The K6-2/500 had CPU issues that caused system lockups on certain tasks. (user-27eb, exchange 11774, 2020-10-25; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • To resolve CPU issues on an O2, ensure the CPU is reseated after handling the system, and always disconnect the power cord before removing the motherboard to drain the PSU. (user-8547, exchange 102501, 2022-02-19; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The CPU issue was caused by a spark on the motherboard, likely due to a sense cable causing incorrect voltages, and was resolved by replacing the motherboard. (user-ba81, exchange 156633, 2021-12-31; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
