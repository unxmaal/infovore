o32

Claims: 7

IRIX supports three ABIs, including o32, the old 32-bit ABI standard on IRIX 5 systems. 1 o32 was the legacy COFF calling convention for very old IRIX, pre 5.3. 2 The older IP19 could run IRIX 5.3, so the o32 vs. n32 distinction was relevant then. 3 N32 and O32 are other 32-bit nomenclatures in MIPSpro. 4 A 6.5 system can run o32 and n32/n64 binaries, but not ancient COFF binaries from IRIX 4.x. 5 user-1a5a supports both ELF and o32. 6 The binary is o32, which is 32-bit for MIPS, as indicated by the 'file' command output. 7
