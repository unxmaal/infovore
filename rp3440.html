RP3440

Claims: 8

The RP3440 is a PA-RISC server that uses swappable CPU modules, and it is limited to one CPU. 12 Some PA-RISC servers require replacing the entire system board if the CPU is removed, due to alignment issues and service manuals recommending replacement. 3 Some PA-RISC servers can be upgraded to Itanium CPUs using similar modules. 4 The RP3440 and possibly R3410 CPUs could be installed in an RX2600, and similarly for RP4440 in RX4640, though official support for these conversions is known. 5 The RP3440 and C8000 could surely do the same, but there is nothing official. 6 The blue plastic latches on the drive sleds of the RP3440 can break. 7
