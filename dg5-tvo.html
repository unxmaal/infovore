DG5-TVO

Claims: 3 Part of DG

The Onyx2 desk-side unit with an IR4 and DG5/TVO includes a DM2 and is considered more practical than a rack with a G-brick, though the user has not yet run Inferno on it. 1 The TDMS ports on the DG5-TVO are part of the DMediaPro HD-GVO, which supports graphics-to-video at resolutions up to 720p or 1080i. 2 The DG5-TVO connects to the DM5 in the VBOB using LVDS over DVI. 3

Sources
