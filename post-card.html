POST card

Claims: 4 Part of POST

AGP cards are not supported, and the system may need a POST card to diagnose further issues. 1 The POST card can help troubleshoot by confirming if the system can see the CPU, showing voltages and bus activity. 2 The POST card uses a buzzer to indicate the presence of a signal on the PC speaker pins, distinguishing between with and without a load. 3
