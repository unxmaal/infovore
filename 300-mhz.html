300 MHz

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

Several processors have been released at 300 MHz, including the R12000, the 12k used in the O2, and the RM5200, which was released in July 1999. 123 Dual CPU setups on the Octane provide a noticeable performance boost over single CPUs running at or under 300 MHz, especially for multi-threaded tasks like compiling or rendering. 4
