glxinfo

Claims: 6

glxinfo reports the capabilities of the X server, not the local machine's GPU. 1 The visual ID in glxinfo output specifies the visual type, depth, and buffer sizes. 2 Framebuffer settings in xsetmon can impact the visual modes reported by glxinfo, such as the bit depth and doublebuffering. 3 A debugger can be used to break on glXChooseVisual to inspect arguments and sanity-check display, screen, and context attributes against glxinfo output. 4 The '20 bombs' error in glxinfo is a placeholder value indicating an unknown or invalid error code. 5 IRIX 4 does not have glxinfo. 6
