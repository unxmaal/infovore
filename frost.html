Frost

Claims: 10

Frost is software for creating on-screen graphics packages with genlock capabilities, functioning as a live 3D set generator that can incorporate live video. 12 It works with live video through the SDI board but genlocks the monitor with Avocent, and it does not support video compression or transcoding. 34 Frost requires an Onyx2 system with the digital media board and allows switching between virtual sets with camera input on IRIX. 56 Telnet was the preferred method for live interaction with Frost, involving another computer feeding it commands, and the video output works until Frost takes over. 78 Frost is too slow on an O2, and 6.5.30 has extra disks for Mozilla and Java, which can cause conflicts during installation, so 6.5.22 is generally preferred. 29
