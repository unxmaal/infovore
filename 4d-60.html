4D/60

Claims: 16

The 4D/60 is a unique Professional IRIS that uses a MIPS R2300 CPU, which is unique to SGI and rare. 123 Its R2300 motherboard was adapted from a MIPS Magnum computer, and the CPU board is a reference design from MIPS Computer Systems. 43 The 4D/60 originally shipped with the R2300 and Clover 1 graphics. 56 The 4D/60T is an upgrade for existing 4D/60 owners that replaces the R2300 MBC with an IP4. 7 PRIME resold the 4D/60 from day one bundled with their application software, which had been ported to 4D1 / GL. 8
