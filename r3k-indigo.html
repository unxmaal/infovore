R3K Indigo

Claims: 22 Part of R3K

The R3K Indigo is a cheaper, more plentiful version of the PI, being a miniaturized IRIS 4D/35 with less RAM and an inability to run IRIX 3.3.2. 12 It is a 32-bit system that can take up to 32MB of proprietary RAM (with 4x4MB being the minimum) and can run IRIX 5.3 with 32MB of RAM. 345 It ran gcc 2.x and software such as ImageMagick, audio converters, MUSH servers, apache, and UAE. 6 The standard Indigo uses a GIO backplane for graphics, unlike the R3K Indigo which includes a variant with VME. 7 A top-spec IP12 (4D/35) is performance-wise equivalent to a top-spec R3K Indigo, differing only in the inclusion of Gio32 on the Indigo and VME on the PI. 8 An R3K Indigo variant is available for $300 but is slow. 9
