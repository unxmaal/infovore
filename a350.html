A350

Claims: 22

The A350 is a DEC Alpha machine that comes in a white-sleeved model and three black models, and is described as expensive, heavy, loud, and hot. 12 The A350 shares its case, including blowers, with the O350, and both machines use a PCI-X riserboard that is supposed to be compatible, though the part numbers differ and some A350 systems may lack the XIO slot. 345 The O350 and A350 have different 2U Interface Boards but share the 2U_RISER part number, and the bottom unit is a 2U PCI-X brick rather than a 4U P-brick. 36 The A350 has two SCSI disks and two Ethernet controllers, and the QLogic QLA 12160 works in both the O350 and A350, requiring custom SCSI wiring if no backplane is present. 78 A350 nodes can be connected in a ring structure with each node having two Numalink connectors, and while a router is not required, it may be used for star wiring. 910 The A350 can run Debian 6, SUSE Linux Enterprise, and SLES10 or SLES11 via PXE or Netbooting even without an IO9 or IO10 card, though the latest PROM is 5.4 or 5.04 and Advanced Linux Environment 3 causes the A350 to enter POD. 111213141516
