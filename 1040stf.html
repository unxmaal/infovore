1040STF

Claims: 6

All Atari ST computers should have MIDI, with the 520ST using an external power supply and the 1040STF using an internal one; the 1040STF includes a floppy drive. 1 An 8MHz 68010 CPU can provide a 5-10% upgrade in a 1040STF, but requires 1.62 ROMs, which are not compatible with the 1040STF without a ROM adapter board. 2 The 520ST and 1040STF were cheaper and more expandable than the Mac, and outperformed it in color graphics. 3 A 1040STF can play most games with a floppy emulator. 4 The 1040STF requires the CPU to be socketed, which may involve removing shielding to fit in the case. 5 The 1040STF's performance improves with later TOS versions; a 2.06 version worked best, though the memory tester YAART still gives errors likely due to line length. 6
