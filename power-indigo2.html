Power Indigo2

Claims: 10 Part of POWER

The Power Indigo2 can run IRIX 6.2 and not 5.3. 1 The Power Indigo2 has a moderately fast processor that struggles with some tasks, and running IRIX 6.5.22 is slow, comparable to an IP6 running IRIX 5.3. 23 The Power Indigo2 requires FDDI to start, while the Octane2 can start without it, and a wide-to-centronics SCSI cable is needed to read a CDROM. 4 The PSU for the Power Indigo2 project at VCFMW failed due to bad capacitors. 5
