SPARC v7

Claims: 7 Part of SPARC

The SPARC v7 architecture does not have multiplication or division instructions. 1 The SPARC FPC compiler targets SPARC v8 but not v7. 2 The 68020 is slower than the SPARC v7, being the slowest while the SPARC v7 is the fastest. 34 The SM100 is a dual 40 MHz SPARC v7 CPU, equivalent to two SPARCstation 2 on an Mbus module, officially supported only in the SPARCserver 600 but sometimes used in a SPARCstation 10, requiring additional work to use. 5 The SPARCserver 600MP had two SPARCv7 CPUs on a module, allowing for up to four CPUs in total, but practical use was limited. 6 Adobe Photoshop for Solaris 3.0.1 is compatible with Solaris 2.5 and requires approximately 32MB of disk space; it supports SPARC v7 and v8 chips but may not work with different window managers or zones/containers. 7
