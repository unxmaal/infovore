serial connection

Claims: 10 Part of serial Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Null modem.

The L3 software can control devices over a serial connection, not just USB, and Indy systems can drive CNC welding machines via serial connection. 12 The serial connection uses a null modem cable with 9600 8N1 settings and no flow control. 3 To test a serial connection, ensure the correct settings and use a null modem cable or a regular RS232 cable, and try with and without a null modem adapter. 4 A null modem cable can be used to monitor console messages via a serial connection and Putty for debugging purposes. 5 Crosstalk might be a problem if the serial connection works. 6
