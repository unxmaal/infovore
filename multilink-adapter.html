multilink adapter

Claims: 7 Part of MultiLink

The multilink adapter is included with the machine and serves as the monitor controller, with the monitor having no logic of its own. 12 It is used for connecting multiple devices and provides DVI input on the O2, potentially replacing the need for an OpenLDI card. 34 However, the multilink adapter on the O2 may disable VGA input through an adapter, and a DVI-to-VGA adapter may be needed if the multilink adapter does not convert analog signals. 56
