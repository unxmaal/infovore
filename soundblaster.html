SoundBlaster

Claims: 11

The SoundBlaster is a sound card that appears in various computer setups, including a PS/2 55SX with XGA-2 and McIDE, which is considered a satisfactory configuration. 1 Later machines were too fast for the original SoundBlaster due to bus speed issues. 2 The IBM SoundBlaster compatibility layer requires a lot of memory. 3 The M-ACPA SoundBlaster drivers can be found on the Ardent Tool website. 4 The Windsurfer card is similar to the SoundBlaster but includes a modem and lacks a MIDI port. 5
