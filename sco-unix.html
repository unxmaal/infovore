SCO Unix

Claims: 13

SCO Unix is a PC Unix, one of many that also include Interactive, and it comes in different versions such as OpenServer and Unixware. 12 SCO Unix on x86 systems would boot in VGA text mode, lacking the graphical polish of SGI or SUN systems, and used the X11R5 Motif environment, which was easy to use and stable. 34 SCO Unix required a VAR to provide third-party drivers, and it had a kernel resource exhaustion issue with open sockets that required a reboot to resolve. 56 The new logo was introduced around mid-1999 to better compete with NT and Linux. 7
