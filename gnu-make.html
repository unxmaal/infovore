GNU make

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Glibc.

GNU make is a build tool that can make compiling easier, and it is often called 'make' and can be renamed to 'gmake'; some installations, such as 'sgug', may install 'gnumake' or 'gmake' specifically. 12 GNU Make is widely available on most platforms and can be obtained from various sources including the dbis bootstrap, optlocal bootstrap, nekoware mirrors, tgcware, and old SGI freeware. 34 GNU Make has implicit rules like `%.o: %.c` for building object files from source files, and it includes many extensions not found in other implementations such as BSD make or SysV make. 56 GNU make is used for most personal projects, and one library uses GNU make and MIPS Pro for builds. 78 You might be able to compile glibc with gcc and GNU make from the ibiblio link, though too new a glibc may not compile. 9 A tutorial on GNU make covering concepts applicable to any make is available at http://user-21b8.mutantstargoat.com/articles/make/. 10
