Cray

Claims: 40 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Cray, SGI Origin 2000.

Cray provided SGI with talent, buildings, and manufacturing facilities, and Peachtree became a sales office when Cray was sold. 12 Cray systems ran Unicos on their vector CPU, and the NWS operated a Cray with a low-level OS and compiler/assembler. 34 Catamount was open sourced but is no longer available, as Cray moved to its own Linux environment. 5 Cray systems required special power, using 400Hz converters and, in older models, 400V generators typically provided by the US Navy. 67
