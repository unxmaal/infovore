ADB

Claims: 13

ADB is a serial-based bus that is proprietary and cannot be used for general serial devices, so Apple included actual serial ports on most Macs for modems and printers. 1 Apple's ADB was standard across their computers from the later 80s to the iMac. 2 NeXT used ADB for its mice, and the Apple IIgs had a daisy chainable serial peripheral port with ADB, allowing for versatile connectivity. 34 The beige G3 has ADB, built-in SCSI, and old-style serial ports. 5 NU_IO supports both ADB and non-ADB keyboard connections, and can be modded to support both depending on the ROM version, which determines ADB support. 6 NeXTStation systems require the latest ROM to support ADB, and a turbo with ROM v74 is needed for ADB, as a mono slab or mono turbo cannot use ADB. 789 ROM v74 is ADB compatible and requires flashing for use. 10 ADB peripherals can be disconnected or connected on a running system, which can either fry something or not. 11
