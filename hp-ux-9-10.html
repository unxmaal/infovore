HP-UX 9.10

Claims: 6 Part of HP-UX 9

HP-UX 9.10 for the series 300/400 has substantial differences from HP-UX 9 for the series 800. 1 The site http://tenox.pdp-11.ru/hpux/ provides software for HP-UX 8.0 and 9.10. 2 HP-UX 9.10 can be installed using network installation on a 755 server. 3 The Apollo NextStep can be upgraded to a 33 MHz 040 CPU and runs HP-UX 9.10 and DomainOS with a specific keyboard. 4 HP released HP-UX 9.10 for these machines, including Y2k patches, and the machine could be somewhere between a 50 MHz 68030 and a 33 MHz 68040. 5
