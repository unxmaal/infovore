fastfan mode

Claims: 4 Part of fastfan

Fast-fan mode is triggered by the controller when a fan fails or when multiple fans fail, indicating an impending shutdown. 1 A RealityEngine requires a 6-fan module and disabling the fan controller to run in fast fan mode, while an early PowerSeries Crimson might have only 4 fans. 2 Fast-fan mode is noisier than the already noisy Octane. 3 Booting a 16U O200 in fast-fan mode is loud, large, and visually impressive, though not faster than a good Octane. 4
