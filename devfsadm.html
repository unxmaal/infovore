devfsadm

Claims: 5

devfsadm is used in Solaris for autoconfiguration and to create device nodes in /dev and /devices. 12 It can be run as root, and it can replace boot -r for hotplugging USB or 1394 disks. 13 A reboot can be avoided by using reboot -- -r or by running touch /reconfigure;reboot. 2 If warnings persist, a reconfiguration reboot with reboot -- -r or running devfsadm may be tried. 4 Solaris does not support HFS formatted Jaz drives; running devfsadm and volcheck may help, but newfs may be necessary. 5
