VRML

Claims: 10

VRML is a deprecated standard, with three.js considered its modern equivalent. 1 VRML is still supported in robotics software such as Webots, due to legacy reasons. 12 VRML export remains available in UG-NX as legacy code. 3 CosmoWorld manuals serve as a resource for VRML. 4 VRML can re-encode video files to work with GL, requiring specific texture dimensions. 5 The O2 supports VRML with hardware textures and is considered better for VRML due to its high bandwidth for textures and video, though Octane models with hardware textures are required for optimal performance. 6 VRML demos are available on 6.5 for Indy, though they are not fast. 7
