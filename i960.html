i960

Claims: 7

The i960 is a RISC processor that saw limited use alongside the 88k and i860 processors, which also had limited use. 12 The i960 was released with fanfare and was successful in some embedded systems, while the i860 never achieved commercial success and was terminated in the mid-1990s. 3 The i960 CPU is used in various devices including video circuits, Ethernet, and switches. 1 The i960 is used as a RAID controller and requires 72-pin RAM, and Windows 7 drivers are available for it. 4 The HP XDS69 is an i960-based X display server, with later revisions being MIPS-based. 5 Some early models use i960 and Intel RISC CPUs, while others use MIPS, and they can use IBM PS/2. 6
