UPA

Claims: 14 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SBus.

UPA is tied to the UltraSPARC architecture and is used for fast graphics, appearing in machines such as the U2, U5/10, U60, and Ultra 10. 12345 UPA is not SBus, though it is similar in form factor, and it runs at 83 to 120 MHz, faster than SBus at 25 MHz. 67 UPA is very fast to a single device but was shared, and its graphics slots are shared with PCI controllers. 89 Creator3D and Elite3D use system RAM for texture storage via UPA, and these UPA cards work well with drivers on the Solaris installation CDs. 1011
