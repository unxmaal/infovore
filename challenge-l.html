Challenge L

Claims: 30 Part of Challenge

The Challenge L is a multi-CPU capable system. 12 It has 5 main bus and 6 VME slots, more than the Onyx's 3 main bus and 3 VME slots. 34 The dual CPU board is used in the Challenge L or XL, or Onyx1. 5 The IP21 boards have dual R8K CPUs at 75 MHz, are sensitive to ESD, and are used in Challenge L machines. 6 Some older SGI systems, including the Challenge L, do not have the MAC address in PROM. 7 The Challenge L has a keyboard port whose purpose is unknown; it may have been reused from the Onyx. 8 It has red and green PSU LEDs with non-intuitive meanings. 9 The Challenge L has a ROM on its m68HC11 SoC with a 192-byte bootstrap section and a 4k section. 10
