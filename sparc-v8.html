SPARC v8

Claims: 3 Part of SPARC

SPARCv8 is an IEEE standard, whereas SPARCv9 might not be open, and SPARC is more complex than RV32I due to its register windows. 1 The SPARC FPC compiler targets SPARC v8 but not v7. 2 The `file` command can determine if binaries are SPARCV8 or SPARCV8+/SPARCV9. 3
