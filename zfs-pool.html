ZFS pool

Claims: 3 Part of ZFS

Bootstrapping a NAS from a ZFS pool can be complex and risky due to potential single points of failure. 1 ZFS pools can be exported and remounted in other operating systems, providing some redundancy if the boot drive fails. 2 A ZFS pool with a z2 configuration can add a disk without maintaining redundancy, potentially losing data. 3
