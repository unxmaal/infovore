MSP

Claims: 7

The MSP has 6KB data RAM, 4KB instruction RAM, a 32-bit scalar unit, and a 128-bit vector unit with one multiply accumulate per clock. 1 The N64 RSP has 8-element short vector instructions, while the MSP has 16-element byte vector instructions. 2 Resetting the MSP with the clock disable latch cleared does not start it. 3 ion64 can now DMA in and out of RSP and control the RSP/MSP. 4 The Vice emulator supports MSP and BSP CPUs with partial bitstream instruction support, and has working DMA and interrupt handling. 5 CRIME RE shares VICE's memory with the MSP and uses DMA features. 6
