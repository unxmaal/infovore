IRIX 5

Claims: 21 Part of IRIX Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indy, Video games and Linux.

IRIX 5 is a version of the IRIX operating system, with the main differences between IRIX 5 and 6 including 64-bit support and the XFS filesystem. 1 The Indy could only run IRIX 5, and IRIX 5 versions below 5.2 were slow and unpopular with low-end models. 2 IRIX 5 had bloat and bugs making it slow and unstable until IRIX 5.3, which became acceptable again. 3 The 4DDN is compatible with IRIX versions 4, 5, and 6, with support ending in IRIX 6.2. 4 DeskArtes Industrial Design software is available for IRIX 6 and IRIX 5 with downloadable examples and manuals. 5 Alias 3 is available as a coff binary for IRIX 5. 6 Wavefront Advanced Visualizer 4.2.1 works under IRIX 5 on Indigo2 but is unstable under IRIX 6.x, and both versions can use a patch from Gizanix available on TheUnixFiles FTP. 7 FDDIXpress was a separate disc for IRIX 4 and included in OS discs since IRIX 5. 8
