PowerPC 601

Claims: 4 Part of PowerPC

The PowerPC 601 has been big-endian since its introduction. 1 Windows for PowerPC requires the NT 4.0 version and runs on a RS/6000 40p with a PowerPC 601 CPU. 2 Apple used the 68040 extensively because the PowerPC 601 was not suitable for laptops and low-end machines. 3 A 060 accelerator for Mac was planned but the PowerPC 601 made it obsolete. 4
