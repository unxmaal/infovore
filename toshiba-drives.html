Toshiba drives

Claims: 3 Part of Toshiba

SGIs require a SCSI CDROM that supports 512 byte blocks, and Toshiba drives are a good choice for this purpose, though they may have door opening issues. 1 Toshiba drives for SGI act like hard drives and get reset after booting to a CDROM. 2
