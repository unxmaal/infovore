XZ graphics

Claims: 7 Part of XZ

XZ graphics provide 3D acceleration, 24-bit color, and offload the CPU, making 3D tasks faster. 1 An Indy R4400@200MHz with XZ graphics can run Quake, and a 150 MHz R4000 Indigo with XZ graphics was found for $100, considered a lucky find. 23 An Indy with XZ graphics cannot use other GIO cards, and there is a separate board for XZ graphics, as the Presenter 1280 may not be compatible with the Indy Corona presenter board. 45 XZ graphics are unusual for an R5K Indy, as the software can handle 3D work faster than the XZ hardware. 6 An Indy with XZ graphics and the Webforce badge is rare, as most have XL graphics. 7
