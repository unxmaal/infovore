4D/440

Claims: 10

The 4D/440 is a 4D/440 VGX that uses an R3010 FPU, which can throw errors under load and can be isolated and replaced. 1 The 4D/440 requires IP15 boards for proper operation; without them, the PSU does not start. 2 The 4D/440 can be revived, and one unit is currently running as a 4D/420 with a missing heatsink. 3 PowerSeries is a 4D/440 VGX that originally had one CPU board and was labeled as a 4D/420. 4
