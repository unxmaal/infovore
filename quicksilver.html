QuickSilver

Claims: 15

The QuickSilver has a stronger 12V rail and slightly weaker 5V rail than the Digital Audio, which aids external power coupling to cards such as the Radeon 9800 Pro, which can be installed in the QuickSilver and the MDD. 12 The QuickSilver does not support cards requiring an extra power coupling, whereas a G5 can benefit from such cards. 3 The MDD was a modest refresh of the Quicksilver with a faster system bus and more RAM, though an upgraded Quicksilver could compete. 45 The QuickSilver and MDD can use Startech 40-Pin IDE to SATA adapters, and a One Stop Shop bridge card upgrades a QuickSilver with ATA 133, USB 2.0, and two extra FireWire ports. 67 The QuickSilver cannot boot OS 9 on Tiger, but it can run 10.4 Shuriken and OS 9 in dualboot, with OS 9 possibly requiring a special disk, and it can boot from a G5's SSD only after a clean install due to compatibility issues. 8910 Maya ran well on the QuickSilver. 11
