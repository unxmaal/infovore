soldering

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Tantalum capacitor.

Solder joints on the back of the facia can fall off from vibration and may need to be wire-wrapped or soldered in a specific way. 1 Preemptive soldering can fail if traces are damaged or wrong caps are used, and tantalum capacitors can cause board damage if not derated. 2 Fresh solder and a decent soldering iron can improve desoldering and soldering tasks. 3 It is recommended to start with simpler machines to learn soldering and rework. 4 The original Apple One's value comes from the specific soldering by one of the Steves, and replacing components diminishes its value. 5 The author can solder if the other person programs, and the author learned soldering 1/4"/Bantam patch cables in college. 67
