libc

Claims: 8

libc is OS-specific, and this has led to nearly a complete reimplementation of libc because every UNIX system implemented its own version. 12 Some fixes for libc and headers can be enabled via CFLAGS to improve compatibility with tools like MIPSpro, and uninstall procedures should leave the system unchanged. 3 IRIX's libc sets errno even when self-allocating, leading to errors in certain operations. 4 strtoull implementations may be necessary for PA64 libc. 5 Running ld directly requires manually linking with libc using -lc, or alternatively using a different version of gcc (up to 3.x) or building with mipspro (cc) may work. 6
