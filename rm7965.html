RM7965

Claims: 5

The RM7965 is 256 TPGA like the RM7065 and requires an interposer to work, but is not pin compatible with the RM7900 and lacks L3 cache. 12 The RM7965 has limited L3 cache support, whereas the RM7900 CPU has external cache. 34 The RM7965 datasheet includes external cache mode bits even though the RM7965 does not support external cache, leading to the discovery of similar bits for the RM7900, for which no useful datasheet exists. 5
