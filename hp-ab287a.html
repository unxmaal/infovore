HP AB287A

Claims: 3 Part of HP

The HP AB287A is a rebadged S2IO/neterion card that is compatible with the SGI Onyx4 but not all SGI systems, and there are slight differences between the SGI and HP versions of the card. 1 The HP AB287A can be used for fiber connections, whereas the Xframe II does not work. 2 A regular and cheap HP AB287A works for a 10Gig card, as SGI sold the same card. 3 The card can get hot when used with a GBIC/adapter. 1
