FDDI

Claims: 37 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Fiber Distributed Data Interface, PCI-X.

FDDI is a networking technology that offers 100 Mbit/s speeds, which is slower than HIPPI (800 Mbit/s or 1600 Mbit/s) and SCSI in 2000, but comparable to or faster than fast Ethernet in some cases. 12 FDDI can be used in various systems, including the Challenge XL, which has over 100 SCSI channels and various HIPPI, FDDI, and Gig Ethernet connections. 3 FDDI was cheaper than Sun HME cards for networking unix machines. 4 FDDI can be used for iSCSI, but it's a rare combination of systems that support both. 5 FDDI uses a 4K MTU compared to 1500 for Ethernet, reducing interrupt load. 6 FDDI can be configured over IPv6 in IRIX, allowing router advertisement and static route setup for IPv6 communication. 7 Linux 6.12 in Debian 13 still has the defxx and skfp FDDI kernel modules, and Debian 13 supports FDDI. 89
