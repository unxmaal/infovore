framebuffer

Claims: 26 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Framebuffer, Display resolution standards.

The framebuffer can be accessed as points, lines, spans, or blocks, and REX3 provides autoincrementing address features for memory access in specific modes. 1 The framebuffer could perform Turing-complete operations and run floating-point shaders, with SGI contributing shader technology to the r300 graphics architecture. 2 The framebuffer had a recursive rasterizer following a Hilbert curve to avoid expensive triangle clipping. 3 The framebuffer comes in 8 and 24 bit flavors, with 24 bit making the boards a type 5 microchannel adapter. 4 With 8 bytes/pixel framebuffer, you can't have 24-bit color with zbuffer and double buffering; 16 bytes/pixel allows quad-buffer and z-buffer with 10-bit color. 5 The framebuffer uses 15x8 tiles (120 total), while a 1920x1080 framebuffer uses 15x9 tiles (135 total), exceeding the 128 available tiles. 6 Setmon/xsetmon changes framebuffer settings. 7 The -DDB32=1 option is used to handle framebuffer overflow. 8
