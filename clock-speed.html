clock speed

Claims: 10

Clock speed is not the only factor affecting performance; the work done per clock cycle also matters, and performance improvements come from both architectural differences and clock speed, adding up to a noticeable increase. 12 It is possible to improve the L2 cache as well as increase the clock speed, which can make a big difference to the performance of some applications. 3 The Z15 has 8MB L2 cache per core, 256MB L3 cache, a 5.2GHz clock speed, and 12 cores. 4 The R4000 reports its clock speed as half the oscillator clock speed due to a timing loop in the BIOS PROM code. 5 The Spicy O'clock mod allows adjusting the LC575's clock speed past 50MHz. 6 ISA slots and cards were standardized to an 8.33 MHz clock speed, contributing to ISA's longevity. 7
