L1 firmware

Claims: 4 Part of L1

The L1 firmware allows changing the date and time before the PROM or OS boots, which is useful for cluster synchronization in rack-mounted systems like Tezro and Fuel. 1 Upgrading from an earlier L1 firmware to a later one on a Tezro may have problems. 2 The L1 firmware can be updated in a laddering procedure, but minimum stepping is required and specific flashers are needed for each Irix L1 image/revision. 3 A user reported that their NUMAlink port was snapped off and they had L1 firmware issues preventing boot. 4
