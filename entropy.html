Entropy

Claims: 17

Entropy is a raytracing tool based on the RenderMan standard, and it was similar to RenderMan, leading Pixar to sue it. 12 It was a commercialized version of BMRT that faced legal troubles, was purchased by Nvidia, and was subsequently withdrawn from the market. 3 Both BMRT and Entropy are raytracing tools, with Entropy having more features, though both can be tricky for soft shadows and reflections. 45 Entropy has an early 2000s look with some features. 6 RAT can be configured to use the Entropy raytrace server for rendering, which processes raytracing calls and maps results onto geometry, while BMRT is not a full replacement for Entropy. 789 Entropy handles conversions in Maya's hypershade, and MayaMan can convert to RIB automatically for it. 10
