cg6

Claims: 9

The CG6 is a fairly quick 8-bit graphics card that should be backwards compatible with earlier graphics cards. 12 It is built into the IPX, and the SS20 ships with a CG6 alongside 128 MB of RAM and dual Ross Hypersparc CPUs at 90 MHz. 34 The SparcClassic has a cg3 onboard video rather than a cg6, and the cg3 is better. 5 Performance on the slow SparcClassic improves with a CG6, while the IPC has a 25 MHz SPARC CPU and no onboard color frame buffer. 6 The CG6 supports CSYNC (and possibly SoG, though the manual's mention is unclear), while the cg3 and ffb use standard V/HSYNC. 7 If a machine boots with a cg6 but does not POST with the ZX, then the ZX is likely broken. 8
