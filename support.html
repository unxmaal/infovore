support

Claims: 4

Support policies vary by product, as some devices are plug-and-play and can be verified through user manuals for standard operating guidance (SOG). 1 Old software and hardware can remain supported when there is a financial incentive, but support is dropped once that incentive disappears. 2 Support for legacy systems is not always prioritized, and developers may lack access to the systems they need to support. 3 Third-party support for Dell R720/730/740 servers costs about $250 per machine per year. 4
