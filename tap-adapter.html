TAP adapter

Claims: 9

The TAP adapter is a virtual network adapter used to set up networking in MAME, typically obtained by installing OpenVPN in Windows. 12 The taptun driver can be installed separately from OpenVPN and can coexist with other VPN software. 3 Networking setup involves bridging the TAP adapter with the local LAN/WiFi network connection, though updates of Windows 10 or 11 may require fixes. 14 The TAP adapter is sometimes connected after MAME starts running. 5 The host side of the TAP adapter is typically configured with a fixed IP address, such as 192.168.137.1 or 10.0.0.2, while the guest (e.g., an Indy running Irix) is given a corresponding address such as 192.168.137.2 or 10.0.0.3. 6738 To route network traffic between a MAME guest and a host PC, configure the TAP adapter with fixed addresses and enable routing on the host. 7
