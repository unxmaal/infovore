  • Xterminals can be emulated in MAME, but not useably. (user-237d, exchange 216110, 2021-08-29; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))

  • The X-Terminal concept involves remotely accessing a Unix machine's GUI, exemplified by the NCD19 gifted to the speaker. (user-237d, exchange 74709, 2025-03-08; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
