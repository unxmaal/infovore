floptical drive

Claims: 14

Floptical drives were standard on most Indys and Indigos, and were not typically a regular floppy drive. 1 The Indy has a floptical drive with a dual molex power connector. 2 The Floptical drive has an automatic eject feature, and the motor in the back of the drive is problematic and ejects the disk. 34 Floptical drives are known to spontaneously die, and a working floptical drive is extremely rare. 56 A new internal floptical drive on eBay is $385, and one owner's drive is dead. 7 The Floptical drive requires a SCSI hard drive, such as a ZuluSCSI compact, and can be booted with a base IRIX 5.3 image. 8
