Genera

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Video games and Linux.

Genera is an emulator for Symbolics Lisp Machines, accessible via Docker. 1 It can run on Linux using a tru64 emulator in a VM, with an old Debian or Xubuntu version. 2 Genera was the usual graphical OS for Lisp machines and provides an entire environment rather than just a compiler or editor. 34 It gained support for Common Lisp in later versions. 5 Genera runs on top of Tru64 but is as close to bare metal as possible due to its interpreter running in CPU cache. 6 The console for Genera is phase-encoded digital video, and the keyboard can be obtained from usual sources. 7
