NuBus

Claims: 6

NuBus is a standard developed by MIT and used by Apple among others, with NeXTbus being a faster implementation of it. 1 NuBus supports bus mastering between cards, but Apple never implemented it in hardware; one card adds this functionality but is not supported under MacOS. 2 The technical documentation for NuBus and 040 PDS cards includes schematics and PAL/GAL equations, allowing for recreation of these cards. 3 A standard NuBus or VME style 3-row pin header could have been used instead of card-edge connectors, and custom NuBus slots make expansions rare. 45 The gbscsi device offers above average transfer rates for old systems like the Apple NuBus and Amiga Zorro 2, and includes Wi-Fi functionality. 6
