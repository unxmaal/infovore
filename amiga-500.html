Amiga 500

Claims: 13 Part of Amiga

The Amiga 500 is a 68k-based machine whose hardware equivalent is the Atari STe. 12 It can be upgraded to 1.2 MB of RAM, with configurations such as 2MB RAM plus a trapdoor 512kb expansion available. 34 The Amiga 500 and Amiga 2000 use the same ROMs, which can be burned using a TL866 burner and 27C400 adapter. 5 Amiga 500s can switch between NTSC and PAL modes using a solder bridge, though an NTSC Fat Agnes chip runs at PAL but 60 Hz, making everything appear too fast. 67 It can use its parallel port for networking, with InterLnk being faster than serial. 8 Various add-ons are compatible with the Amiga 500, including the PiStorm, the Borland turbo (without soldering), RGB2HDMI, and a 1-1 port of Missile Command adapted for planar graphics. 910111
