Impact

Claims: 72 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, XFree86.

With Indigo2

The Indigo2 IMPACT is an Indigo2 model, and the 13w3 system is one such Indigo2 IMPACT. 1 Indigo2 Impact motherboards have factory-applied patches held in place with clear glue. 2 Indigo2s, especially R10k/IMPACTs, had long lines. 3 The dual 75MHz IP21 boards do not work with IR graphics or IMPACT in the Indigo2. 4 Power supplies are the #1 weak spot of every SGI, and older non-impact Indigo2s will be more reliable than Impacts. 5 To install IRIX 5.3 with IMPACT via LOVE on an Indigo2, one needs a ZuluSCSI kit. 6 Upgrading the motherboard to an r10k and PSU is required for Indigo2, but Impact units may not need a PSU upgrade. 7 The Indigo2 Impact may have TRAM, but the specific model is unclear. 8

General

The SGI Impact is a graphics card that was used in the R10K-based Impact systems and came with R4000 processors in R4K Indigo2s, where 200 and 250 MHz speeds are common. 91011 Impact can have up to 4 TRAMs, with configurations including Solid (no texturing), High (dual slot), and MaxImpact (tripleslot), and it supports 8-bit palettized textures. 121314 IMPACT machines with 1 MB of TRAM only support up to 16-bit textures, and the Impact requires the height and width of textures to be the same, using 256x256 textures for 3dfx compatibility. 1516 IMPACT and VPro systems use microcode, and certain Impact and Mekton demos are limited to Impact hardware or require VPro compatibility to run properly. 171819 Xfree86 has support for some Impact variants, and some demos work on a good variety of hardware while others are explicitly flagged for specific hardware. 2021 Impact runs Quake1 and Quake2 adequately in speed, and the MIPS3 build runs well on Impact, dipping to around 15 FPS at max draw distance but still playable. 2223 An IMPACT build for an Onyx2 requires an IO6G board. 24 An Impact system had its other SCSI channel available externally for uncompressed video, and it lacks imaging extensions, so it cannot easily do compression without extra hardware. 2526 The Impact power supply must be Impact or Impact Ready, and an Impact power supply can be used with non-Impact graphics cards, but not the other way around. 272829 The Impact can be tested live by soldering copper wires to the LV board and connecting incandescent lamps to +5V and +3.5V outputs, with a jumper enabling the supply. 28 No R8K PROM has support for IMPACT. 30 vpro has newer OpenGL features such as Phong shading compared to Impact. 31
