SLES9

Claims: 34

The Prism uses SLES 9 as its official/intended OS, with ProPack required for full hardware support and graphics, and it may also work with older or newer OSes (including possibly SLES 11 and older Red Hat Enterprise versions); headless mode allows more OS options. 123 SLES9 and SGI ProPack are available on archive.org, and ProPack 4 can only be installed on SLES9. 45 The SLES 9 installation process reboots after loading CD contents into RAM, and the installer requires direct device access to display properly (rebooting occurs without it). 67 The Altix 350 with a graphics module requires special installation instructions for SLES 9, as it may not boot/install properly from DVDs without them, and installing the base 5.3 version from CD results in constant SCSI bus errors which are resolved by removing the CD drive. 89 For graphics acceleration, SLES9 requires either SLES9 or an old version of Red Hat, and it runs with the fglrx driver. 1011 The fglrx driver is available in SLES 9 but requires a compatible PCI card, and SLES9 might not support certain PCI cards with the fglrx driver. 1213 Disabling SGI-specific extensions might allow the fglrx driver to work. 14 The FireMV 2200 is not supported by the SLES 9 and SGI ProPack kernels, causing X to crash with 'cannot read V_BIOS' even with the radeon module. 15
