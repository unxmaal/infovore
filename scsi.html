SCSI

Claims: 223 Background: 20 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SCSI, SATA, Silicon Graphics, Solid-state drive, Zip drive.

With IDE

SCSI was generally considered better than IDE, though IDE matched or surpassed SCSI for transfer speeds in the 486 era with UDMA standards. 12 IDE was cheaper for PCs because its interface card was just an electrical connection, whereas SCSI required an ISA bridge on other systems. 3 SCSI outperformed IDE in CPU usage and responsiveness, causing minimal iowait, which made IDE unsuitable for server environments except in very low-budget scenarios. 45 SCSI supported CD-ROMs natively, unlike IDE, which required special extensions. 6 SCSI was easy to use in the 80s/90s by checking IDs and termination, while IDE required checking jumpers but did not need termination. 7 SAS is to SCSI what SATA is to IDE, sharing a similar protocol but using serial rather than parallel transmission. 8 Converting from SCSI to IDE to SATA involves an inexpensive adapter, though the transition is described as unnecessarily complex. 910 The Blade 2000 cannot handle base IDE, but its Fibre Channel and SCSI are capable, and on IP35 the IO9 has both SCSI and IDE controllers. 1112

General

SCSI is a bus standard offering advantages such as DMA, bus mastering, daisy chaining, robustness, standards compliance, external device expansion, tagged command queuing, tape support, multiple LUNs, and error checking and correction commands that SATA lacks. 131415 SCSI is faster than SATA, though it may not be fast enough to make a difference for SSDs. 1617 Most SGIs use SCSI, which can be challenging to deal with, especially disk sleds, and SCSI can be picky about IDs, especially when connecting external devices to Macs, requiring specific terminations. 1819 SCSI issues can be caused by a wide-SCSI drive with a narrow-SCSI cable, target-initiated sync negotiation on some drives, parity mismatches, or incorrect terminations, and can sometimes be resolved by using new cables. 2021 SCSI enclosures without DAT or CD-ROM drives have a 50-pin connector, and the SCSI pin configuration is unusual. 2223 SCSI ID 0 is the controller. 24 SAS is different from SCSI in terms of compatibility and limitations; SAS controllers can support SATA drives but not SCSI drives. 25 The creator of SCSI wanted to call it Sexy. 26 SCSI is a storage interface used across a wide range of systems, including Amigas, Macs, PS/2 machines, PCs, and Sun machines, and some systems have it built in. 27282930 SCSI is distinct from SATA, IDE, and ATA, and regular SSDs will not work directly in SCSI machines. 3132 SCSI is less prone to compatibility issues with older systems compared to IDE, though SCSI hardware can be problematic, especially with SCSI2SD. 3334 The SCSI ID is likely set by jumpers on the leftmost pins, and the rightmost pins are for other purposes like the BUSY LED. 35 The system recognizes the device at the assigned SCSI ID. 36 Some SCSI drives require termination packs, and determining if something is self-terminating can still be challenging. 3738 SCSI drives are often used in music equipment and DVD duplicators. 39 SCSI is a bus interface with defined electrical and physical characteristics, including bus width, signaling voltage, and maximum cable length, with the HD68 connector used for wide SCSI-2 or faster (which is not hot-swappable or powered). 4041 SCSI has no addressing limits like IDE, and you can hook up a 20TB hard drive using SCSI. 4243 SCSI drives can be hot-swapped without damaging hardware, though this can cause kernel panics, and reseating a drive can improve performance by avoiding cold boot issues. 4445 Probing SCSI devices from the PROM is faster than waiting for a BIOS or OS to start. 46 SCSI controllers are supported by various systems, including the VAXstation 4000/90 and VAX 4000/300 (via NetBSD patches), the Indigo (with a generic SCSI chipset), the E3000/4000/5000 series (with onboard SCSI), and the HP C8000 workstation (which has issues with SCA disks). 4748495051 The Sun Ultra 5 lacks built-in SCSI but can use SCSI boards via PCI, while the Fuel has faster built-in SCSI and USB support. 5253 SCSI emulators are often incompatible with machines that use a full proper SCSI command set, and SCSI errors are expected in emulation. 5455 SCSI to SSD converters allow connecting SSDs to older SCSI busses but are expensive and have speed limitations, and a Pico ZuluSCSI for $50 can serve as a cheap SCSI to SD card adapter achieving nearly 10MB/sec reads. 5657 SCSI was used as an interconnect for various devices and networking, working well in many setups, and was also used for flatbed scanners. 5859 SCSI can be implemented over FC, SAS, iSCSI, or FireWire. 60 Modern $40 ZuluSCSI/bluescsi devices are easy to use and can saturate any 10MB SCSI bus. 61 SCSI debugging can provide verbose debug info useful for troubleshooting, and the BIST/IPL code list is useful for debugging SCSI issues, though the codes alone may not help with all problems. 6263 You need multiple bins of SCSI cables, adapters, ribbon cable, crimp-on connectors, and luck to have enough components. 64
