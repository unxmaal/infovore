PCI devices

Claims: 6 Part of PCI

PCI devices are a focus for FCode driver writing. 1 The 'show-pci-devs' command in OpenBoot lists PCI devices, and 'setenv diag-switch? true' can help if devices are not probed. 2 The DVlink is listed in the PCI devices but not showing in hinv, with software installed. 3 Kernels newer than 4.14 have a bug where PCI devices do not work. 4 Between 4.1.1 and 4.0.9, something related to PCI devices is broken and causes the system not to boot. 5 VxWorks bootloops if it can't find the desired PCI devices. 6
