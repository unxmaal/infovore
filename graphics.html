graphics

Claims: 62 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

SGI graphics cover a wide range of products, from the dinosaurs in Jurassic Park to Mario in Mario 64 to complex architectural work, and they do not all output the same way. 123 SGI graphics can be programmed to place sync in the green or blue instead, though the PROM may not respect this setting. 4 The Indy's graphics were one of the few cases where SGI published detailed information, leading to its emulation. 5 Emulating the entire SGI system, including graphics, bus interfaces, and ASICs, is extremely difficult and requires countless hours of work, though emulation without graphics is possible, albeit slow. 67 A render for a game using SGI graphics has been posted, and another version without annoying lighting is available. 8 SGIs were used for graphics and software was archived and cracked. 9 SGI graphics often feature high-res solid-looking 3D with attributes like gouraud shading, environment mapping, and off-line rendering. 10 An Indigo with an R3000 CPU and entry-level graphics is likely priced reasonably. 11 The Fuel is based on an Origin 300 in an ATX case with graphics, and the Tezro is an Origin 350 in a case with graphics that is similar to other Chimera systems. 1213 The Tezro rack cannot use quad 1 GHz modules due to graphics blocking cooling. 14 O2 systems received CPU and graphics upgrades during their operational lifespan, and there are SGI datasheets for O2 graphics. 1516 The code for SGI support in NT's source leak is incomplete and only supports entry graphics, and the source lacks a kernel and graphics, has some troubleshooting utilities and base drivers. 1718 Reinstalling a bunch of stuff and rebuilding the kernel may be needed to get graphics working in IRIX. 19 Without a keyboard, you won't get graphics during boot, but you should still see boot messages and a console. 20
