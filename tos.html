TOS

Claims: 9

TOS is the Atari ST operating system, which is user-friendly and includes features like an auto folder for autoloading programs and accessories. 1 GEMDOS is the underlying file system of TOS. 2 RAM expansion and TOS replacement are possible. 3 EmuTOS is an advanced version of TOS that can be burned for autoboot on modern ACSI>SD devices without changing ROMs, includes hard disk drivers, runs in RAM to fix 'bad dma' issues, and has good game compatibility. 4 MiNT is a preemptive version of TOS for Atari STs. 5 The Falcon dual boots TOS and FreeMint. 6 Notator requires a ST or STe running plain TOS and won't run on a TT or Falcon. 7
