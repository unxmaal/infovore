IPL

Claims: 6

IPL stands for initial program load, which is similar to booting the OS and involves options such as entering a diagnostic environment or saving system code. 1 The IPL program function makes changes to the IPL by writing to an eeprom. 2 IPL is also used in openpower systems. 3 The RS/6000 POST and IPL can take a long time after a battery replacement or in service mode, but not once configured. 4 The machine may not IPL well if the other half of the expansion chassis is unpowered. 5 You can IPL the system from disk and then GO LICPGM and dump the licenses from that menu. 6
