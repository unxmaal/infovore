netsurf-motif

Claims: 10

netsurf-motif is the newest/best web browser available for IRIX and can be installed via tdnf. 12 It runs on sgug-rse, which requires setting up an environment as described in the README. 34 In SGUG-RSE it requires a 24-bit display and is incompatible with IRIX 6.5.22, causing 4DWM and possibly all of X to crash on an Indy. 5 It also ran on the indigo2 without sgug motif, but xsginet did not. 6 When launched, Netsurf-Motif gives console errors indicating issues with GTK3-Motif and RANDR, and reports a memory error when clicking a button despite having free memory, possibly due to system limitations. 78 It could be ported to Nekoware, a useful and compatible browser. 9
