PS/2 port

Claims: 19 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The PS/2 port can be rewired from PC-AT pins, though testing the system with wires directly connected to the pins is not recommended. 1 A USB Wyse keyboard has a PS/2 port on the side. 2 Unplugging and replugging devices into the PS/2 port of a running system can cause issues. 3 The PS/2 mouse can have issues with the PS/2 port, sometimes only working in certain parts of the boot process and stopping after X starts, and can be fixed by restarting the graphics server. 4 Connecting an SGI keyboard/mouse to a PS/2 port using adapters may not work. 5 No PC on a SBus card had a PS/2 port. 6
