Expert3D-Lite

Claims: 8

The Expert3D-Lite is a 3D graphics card in the 3Dlabs Wildcat series, which also includes the Expert3D, XVR-500, XVR-600, and XVR-1200. 1 It uses a Wildcat 4110 chipset and supports 3D acceleration on both Solaris and OpenBSD. 23 The XVR-500 is similar to the Expert3D-Lite but faster, though it lacks acceleration under OpenBSD. 4
