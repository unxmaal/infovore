cu

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Gentoo Linux.

cu is a classic serial terminal application on UNIX, alongside screen, and is included in the uucp packages. 12 Gentoo users can use cu, tio, or minicom for serial connections. 3 On macOS, screen and cu can be used to connect to serial ports, with screen defaulting to 9600 baud and cu serving as an alternative. 4 cu is not included in IRIX 6.5.30 by default but can be installed, and screen is available. 5 cu can be configured to work without issues, but enabling the serial port requires correct settings, and either the tty device or cu should work for serial communication. 67 cu and minicom cannot send to the Iris serial console without proper flow control settings. 8
