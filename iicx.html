IIcx

Claims: 24 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Macintosh IIcx.

The IIcx is a 16 MHz 68030-based machine with limited expansion and a single floppy drive, and it has a RAM limit of 1MB in the II and IIcx models. 12 The IIcx is considered the fastest 68k Mac, though the IIfx is faster than the IIcx. 34 The IIcx is compatible with 1.44MB Mac floppies and can be used to create 800k floppy disks. 56 The IIcx can support up to three monitors, and its sound chip is 8-bit stereo at 22 KHz. 78 The IIcx struggles with Internet browsing due to its 16MHz 030 CPU and limited RAM, and NetBSD on an IIcx is painful, taking hours to generate SSH keys. 910 Formatting an 18GB drive in a IIcx takes a long time with 16MHz of 030 power. 11 The IIcx and IIci have similar hardware but the IIcx is rarer and may have issues with its ROM or startup circuit. 12 Some IIcx boards have a soldered 68030 CPU, making CPU upgrades difficult without a PDS. 13
