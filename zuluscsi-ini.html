zuluscsi.ini

Claims: 7

Editing the zuluSCSI.ini file requires copying all disk and CD images to an SD card before the final step of copying the .ini file, and checking the log for continuity. 1 Creating a 2GB disk image can help diagnose issues with zuluSCSI, and different image sizes may require different zuluSCSI.ini settings. 2 A proper zuluSCSI.ini file will most likely be needed, as the defaults often do not work correctly when you have large disk images on older operating systems. 3 The SectorsPerTrack and HeadsPerCylinder settings can be adjusted for compatibility, and problems with SCSI devices can often be fixed. 4 The ZuluSCSI adapter required configuration changes in the zuluSCSI.ini file to work properly. 5
