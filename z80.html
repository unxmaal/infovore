Z80

Claims: 15

The Z80 is a Zilog 8080 clone with some enhanced features, and the 8085 was designed in response to the Z80. 12 The Z80 is available in a 40-pin DIP package. 3 The Z80 can address up to 64K per bank, with hardware allowing for more banks but not beyond 64K per bank. 4 An NMI on a Z80 stops it completely and restarts on address 0x0066. 5 The Z80 is recommended for a basic computer and is used in embedded systems for simple control units. 67 The DEC Rainbow has a dual processor, Z80 and 8086, and requires customized DOS to run. 8 The Game Boy uses a variant of the Z80 CPU. 9 The Sega Genesis board includes M68K, Z80, and a bespoke video subsystem. 10
