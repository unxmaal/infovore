POWER6

Claims: 10 Part of POWER

The POWER6 manual claimed a noise level of 65 decibels, but the actual level is at least 100 decibels, possibly due to fan issues. 1 POWER6 systems are 4U rack boxes that can run PowerVM, and they can run AIX 7.1/i 7.2. 23 OS/400 can be installed on a POWER6 running PowerVM in a short time, and POWER6/7/8/9 run both IBM i and other operating systems. 45 AIX is reliable but difficult to use. 6 An R620 upgrade would be cheaper than a POWER6 server. 7 POWER6 was a server processor and not something Apple could drop into an iMac. 8 IBM increased clock speeds significantly with the POWER6 architecture, reaching 4.7 GHz in a PowerMac G6. 9 The PPC970 (G5) is a POWER4 subset CPU with added VMX instructions (altivec), and VMX was not widely distributed on servers until POWER6. 10
