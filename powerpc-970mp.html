PowerPC 970MP

Claims: 4 Part of PowerPC

The PowerPC 970MP is a 64-bit PowerPC processor from IBM, introduced in 2002 alongside the PowerPC 970, PowerPC 970FX, and PowerPC 970GX. 1 It is a POWER4 processor cut down for desktop usage, featuring dual FPUs and Altivec support, and running OpenFirmware rather than AIX. 2 The PowerPC 970MP is a single processor containing two cores, and can run at 2.5 GHz air-cooled. 3
