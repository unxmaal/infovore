180 MHz

Claims: 5

The R5000 CPU is used in the Indy and runs at 180 MHz. 1 The O2 R5K modules come in 180 MHz (PGA, 90 MHz SysAD x2) and 200 MHz (BGA, 100 MHz SysAD x2) variants. 2 The 180 MHz variant can be overclocked to 200 MHz by changing a resistor, while the 200 MHz variant supports only integer multipliers. 2 An R5K CPU with 180 MHz can sometimes swap, and adding more RAM is generally beneficial. 3 For the R5000, 180 MHz with second-level cache is important for better performance. 4
