O2 cam

Claims: 8 Part of O2 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

The O2 cam generates random numbers by hashing the video stream, and it is available as part of an Octane video option alongside friendly video inputs. 12 The O2 can handle different video inputs such as S-video, composite, and potentially the O2 cam, though not all configurations are tested. 3 The O2 cam is worth only $50, and it is considered fun but useless, serving mostly as a party trick to show off at VCF. 45 user-24f0's box contains an O2 cam. 6
