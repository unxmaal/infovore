LUNs

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SCSI.

SCSI devices can have logical unit numbers (LUNs) to identify disks within the device, even if not part of a RAID, and a SCSI CD changer can report multiple LUNs for its disks even under the same SCSI ID. 12 SCSI tape devices use LUNs. 3 The Amiga can configure a SCSI device as separate LUNs, allowing it to see all volumes simultaneously but periodically interrupting reads by polling other LUNs. 4 LUNs is the FC term, those can also be called volumes or virtual drives or something else. 5 Xserve RAID can expose LUNs over FC and SGI can make XFS filesystems directly on those LUNs. 6
