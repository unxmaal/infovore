MACE

Claims: 17

The O2's MACE chipset is in early stages of reverse engineering, and the MACE docs include the System Verilog for the block. 12 Duplicate memory addresses in the O2's BGE, MACE, and PCI components can make hardware debugging difficult. 3 MACE can support multiple controllers, though PCI parallel limitations may restrict additional connections. 4 MACE enters a fault state if it receives too many PCI RETRY signals. 5 MACE has compatibility issues with Voodoo1, which are due to specific bugs rather than incompatibility with Voodoo2 or other cards. 67 These issues include conflicting texture upload speeds between MACE and Voodoo1, a DMA byteswap that always uses 32 bits, and problematic 16-bit LFB writes that require a shadow buffer for 32-bit writes. 89 The Voodoo+MACE combination limits the framerate, with a single TMU card performing slightly better on PC. 10
