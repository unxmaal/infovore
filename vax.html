VAX

Claims: 111 Background: 11 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see VAX, OpenVMS, Ultrix, Xterm.

With DEC

DEC's hardware and software, particularly from the PDP-11 era, were known for durability and innovation, with a strong appeal to academic and scientific institutions. 1 The VMS operating system can run on VAX and Alpha systems, and there are efforts to support it on AMD64 architecture. 2 VAX binaries were translated to Alpha using software tools, and mixed architecture clusters are supported with VAX and Alpha, and Alpha and Itanium. 34 WP for VMS was available for both VAX and Alpha, though CDE requires an Alpha while on VAX only DECwindows Motif is available. 56 OpenVMS includes limited software and requires licensing for additional software, especially on VAX and Alpha systems. 7 OpenVMS 8.4 on Alpha has stricter password requirements than VAX. 8 Most of DEC's marketshare was due to VAX back compatibility and NT. 9

General

The VAX is a complex CISC architecture whose instruction set was harder to decode than x86, a factor that contributed to its downfall. 1011 The VAX can run historic Unix systems, including Ultrix (which shared a codebase with Ultrix-32) and OpenVMS. 1213141516 NetBSD has a VAX port that can build most pkgsrc pieces, and OpenBSD also runs on VAX. 171819 GCC has a target for VAX, and Mosaic is available for it. 2021 Writing your own kernel on a VAX involves using PALcode, which for VMS implemented VAX atomic queue instructions and interrupt handling running uninterruptibly at a higher privilege level than kernel mode. 2223 The VAX can potentially netboot from NetBSD via RIS or DECnet, though it may fail to load the kernel from an NFS-mounted root. 24252627 Emulators such as simh can run VMS and VAXen, with modern simh providing graphical emulation. 2829 VAX hardware is inexpensive to buy on eBay, cheaper than a typical SGI. 30 The VAX is a 32-bit mini computer (possibly a supermini) architecture from the 1980s that descended from the PDP line and was software compatible with it, though PDP/11 compatibility may not have been a major selling point. 313233 The VAX was faster than the PDP/11 and capable of more than 4MB RAM, and it was part of multi-user systems with software portability benefits due to VMS and PDP/11 compatibility mode. 32 The original VAX instruction set was large and complex, leading to some parts being implemented in microcode. 34 VAX machines are slow, with the VAX-11/730 being the third VAX introduced and the slowest UNIBUS implementation of the VAX architecture. 3536 The fastest single CPU VAX was 50 VUPS, and the fastest VAX for Ultrix is a CVAX machine. 3738 VAX machines ran Unix, including Ultrix on both VAX and MIPS architectures, and ran OpenVMS up to version 7.3, although that might be rather slow depending on the machines. 394041 VAX binaries can be run on IA64 VMS if the code does not depend on specific OS versions, but older VAX software may not support newer VMS versions. 4243 The VAX is suitable for running Unix and executing simple C and Fortran 90 programs, as well as basic internet services like web browsing, email, IRC, and FTP. 44 The VAX has a 55 MHz CPU, regular SCSI, a color graphics card, and 104 MB of RAM split into 2x32, 2x16, and 2x4. 45 It has a 4 GB hard drive, exceeding the 852 MB max specified in the documentation. 46 8" floppies often contained bad sectors that could corrupt data, as experienced when sampling electrical signals and performing Fourier transforms on a PDP or VAX. 47 Software compiled for OpenVMS on VAX can be difficult to use if the developer is no longer available. 48 The VAX runs by executing an .ini file with screen for session management. 49 Some VAX models have trace corrosion issues, especially on the battery area, affecting most except late models like the 4000; the extent of damage varies. 50 Unix and a small player like mpg123 can improve performance. 51 UG never ran on mainframes, only on larger VAXen. 52
