WSL1

Claims: 4 Part of WSL

WSL1 runs a POSIX layer provided by the NT kernel, allowing it to run an ext4 or XFS filesystem. 1 It also runs a standard GNU userland. 2 However, WSL1 did not support hardware resources like raw sockets. 3 For many tasks on Windows, msys2 is considered better than WSL1. 4
