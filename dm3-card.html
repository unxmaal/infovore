DM3 card

Claims: 4 Part of DM3

The DM3 card is the interface card for the VBOB, which occupies the XIO slot just above the V12 in a Tezro system. 1 The VBOB requires the DM3 card to be an XIO card, not a PCI. 2 The Onyx2 uses the DM3 card, and the HD DMedia Pro card has BNC connectors. 3
