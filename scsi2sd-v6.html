SCSI2SD v6

Claims: 20 Part of SCSI2SD Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indy, SGI O2.

The SCSI2SD v6 is the fastest available SCSI emulator, though hardware revisions are needed for further speed improvements, and it is faster than rascsi, which is slower due to lack of synchronous transfer support. 12 It can perform better than conventional 10k and 15k RPM disks on a Fast SCSI (10MB/s) bus, except for sequential write, and the quality of the SD card is very important to overall performance. 3 The SCSI2SD v6 adapter improves performance over v5, which supported wide SCSI; v6 enhanced support to up to seven devices. 45 The SCSI2SD v6 works well on a variety of systems, including the A3000, SGI Indy, Indigo1, Indigo2, Onyx2, DEC 3000s, Challenge S, O2, and older Macs such as the Quadra. 67891011 It can be used as a SCSI network adapter and supports various SCSI devices including CD-ROM, ZIP drives, and hard drives. 12 A v6 SCSI2SD card requires an 80pin SCA->50pin adapter and a 50pin ribbon cable. 13 The SCSI2SD v6 is expensive, and incompatible chips may cause errors and double PANICs on the Octane2. 1410 The firmware for the SCSI2SD v6 on the 840av causes disk corruption on write with newer versions, but the older v6.5.2 works fine, and the author is working on fixing it. 15
