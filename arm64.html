ARM64

Claims: 6 Part of ARM Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see QEMU.

ARM64 is a major platform for porting software, and QEMU builds natively on it. 12 The new dynarec supports x86_64 and arm64, while the old one supports i386 and x86_64. 3 MAME can run SGI emulation on an Apple M1, but DRC for ARM64 could make it faster. 4 ARM64 was chosen by Apple for profitability rather than architectural superiority. 5 7-Zip 24.08 for arm64 supports multi-threaded and single-threaded compression and decompression with specified dictionary sizes, and reports performance metrics including speed, usage, and MIPS ratings. 6
