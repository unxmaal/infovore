T2

Claims: 12

T2 is a processor that has better single-threaded performance than T3 but worse single-threaded performance than T4. 1 T2 has bugs that prevent it from booting properly, especially in video mode, and may not work with certain hardware like USB to serial adapters. 2 T2 for the O2 IIC has been recently started by René, while support for the Octane is much longer. 3 T2 has the advantage that you can cross-build most of what you need beforehand on some fast x86 machine, or just build a rootfs with t2trap and an ia64 ISO. 4 Running modern Linux on a T2 requires supporting the Prism FireGL X3s for graphics acceleration. 5 The T2 on the Prism is more versatile, though SLES9 with QuickTransit is not ideal. 6 T2 is sensitive to disk read errors and can fail to recognize a connected drive. 7 The T2 was great for handling Solaris software with many blocking threads doing tiny work, but struggled with single-thread performance; replacing with libaio and a C service improved performance by 100x. 8
