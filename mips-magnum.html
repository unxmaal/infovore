MIPS Magnum

Claims: 14 Part of MIPS Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see ZFS.

The MIPS Magnum is a rare MIPS workstation, unlike common MIPS workstations such as SGI's. 1 It supported Risc/os Unix and RISC OS for ARM, and used 150 MHz R4400ish processors that are not commonly available for sale. 23 It required a network adapter and a hard drive, and is not Y2K compliant, requiring 4-digit years in the operating system. 45 It has hardware OPL3 sound capabilities but lacks drivers, and RISCompiler and RISCwindows are available for it. 67 It is emulated in MAME and Qemu, though Qemu cannot emulate SGI. 89
