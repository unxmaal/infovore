iBook G4

Claims: 9 Part of iBook

The iBook G4 is a cheap and plentiful retrocomputer that packs away without taking much space and has a built-in CD drive, making it worth considering. 1 It has an excellent and replaceable keyboard, and new keyboards can be found cheap on eBay. 23 Its trackpad is not as good as some older Mac laptops. 4 It can have a SATA card installed, and it can have a 7447B chip running at 1500 MHz, though its ROM needs to be patched for this. 56 The 1.33GHz model needs reassembly. 7 It was used to emulate DECstations with GXemul in 2005, running at what felt like near-native speed. 8
