Haiku

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see FreeBSD.

Haiku runs only on x86, and the most historically accurate non-x86 platform for it would be PowerPC. 12 Haiku uses a forked version of GCC 2.95, released in 2001, to maintain BeOS software compatibility, while supporting a hybrid GCC 2/GCC 7 environment, and the GCC version of Haiku's build tools has errors compiling GCC. 34 Chromium, GTK, and Wine are being ported to Haiku for the next beta release, and Falkon is the browser ported to Haiku. 56 Haiku does not have an X server, and the PCI III may need a special IDE driver for Haiku. 78 Haiku is one of the best compromises between PC operating systems, for personal use anyway. 9
