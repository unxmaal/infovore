IRIS models

Claims: 3 Part of IRIS

IRIS models run at clock speeds less than 50 MHz, a range for which the MiSTeR FPGA is best suited. 1 VME cards with drivers, especially those with boxy front panels for EMI, are specific to IRIS models, though some versions exist without the boxy front panel. 2 Schematics for the VB-3 Indy XZ Graphics, HP-1, GR-4 Board for the Indy Elan Graphics, and some IRIS models are available on bitsavers. 3
