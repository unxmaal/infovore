604

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see QEMU.

The 604 is comparable to the PowerPC G3 in many uses. 1 Powermacs of the 604 era used EDO DIMMs. 2 The P200MMX can emulate an A500 with reasonable accuracy for a 604, though modern emulation is more comprehensive and slower. 3 QEMU's PowerPC emulation may be slow, but it can emulate 603 and 604 processors and can emulate Mac startup chimes. 4
