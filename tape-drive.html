tape drive

Claims: 33 Part of tape Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Tape drive.

Tape drives vary widely in reliability and condition: the 85 series drives are unreliable, with many non-functional and tapes that are marginal even when the drive works, while other drives are described as having all options enabled for demonstration purposes. 12 Tape drives come in several models, including the HP 9144a, the 3310 drives, and drives installed in systems such as the SGI Personal Iris, the 3000UX, and the A2k. 34567 Tape drives can be physically difficult to handle, being heavy and awkward to move, soldered to the PCB with removal requiring the off of BNC nuts, and featuring a release screw. 8910 Tape drive functionality can be affected by hardware issues, such as a SCSI connector not fully seated on the Indigo's backplane header, a faulty front motor that does not serve a function for tape ejection, and a drive that refuses to eject the tape even with power supplied. 111213 Tape drives can be lost or unused, as when a system lost its tape drive and suffered numerous errors and an out-of-space condition, or when a system had a tape drive but no tape was ever used, and some users prefer an SD solution instead of a tape drive. 14615 Tape drives can affect system operation, such as an OS setting a 'bomb flag' that locks out all jobs until a backup completes if a job times out trying to talk to the tape drive, and AIX can create bootable tape and CD/DVD backups whose functionality may depend on regular use. 1617
