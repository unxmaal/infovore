VGA cable

Claims: 8 Part of VGA

Some VGA cables can be modified by removing pins to resolve display issues, such as snipping the HSync and VSync pins from a Dell LCD cable to improve scaling modes, or pulling out ID pins (including pins 12 and 15) to disable EDID. 1234 Removing the hsync and vsync pins from a VGA cable resolves green image issues and enables sync on green (SOG). 5 A cheap VGA cable can fix the RGBS to RGsB issue by enabling SOG. 6 The O2 outputs all signals over VGA and starts with VGA, so it can be connected to a VGA monitor using a standard VGA cable, though this can confuse some machines like the HP SoG CRT. 7
