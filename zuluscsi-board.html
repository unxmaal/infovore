ZuluSCSI board

Claims: 5 Part of ZuluSCSI

A ZuluSCSI board can be used for external SCSI connections. 1 A 50-pin SCSI-3 interposer is needed for the ZuluSCSI board. 2 The board can serve as a modern solution for old computers with unreliable HDDs, and can fix problematic CD-ROM trays through a replacement or the board itself. 34
