50-pin SCSI

Claims: 4

Machines with 50-pin SCSI may benefit from emulators or have no worse performance with 50-pin to SCA adapters. 1 The 80-pin SCA adapter to 50-pin SCSI requires specific jumper settings for compatibility with the Indigo 2, and IRIX's partitioning tool may need additional configuration. 2 The MVME3600 has a PMC slot that is not populated, along with 50-pin SCSI and VGA, while the 761 board provides serial, though the serial console is not working. 3 The 50-pin SCSI on the 16702a option 3 board is on the same SCSI chain as the internal drives, allowing SCSIKnife to be hung off it for both the reinstall CDROM and boot HDD. 4
