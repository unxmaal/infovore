Pentium D 945

Claims: 4 Part of Pentium

The Pentium D 945 has a dual-core architecture with 4MB L2 cache and runs at 3.4GHz. 1 The Pentium D 945 at 4.4 GHz outperforms the 7130M at 3.2 GHz in OpenSSL speed tests, sometimes by a factor of two, which might be explained by the Pentium D 945 having twice the L2 cache of the 7130M. 23 The Pentium D 945 at 3.4 GHz with SMT has better compression but lower decompression performance compared to the Xeon 5080 at 3.73 GHz. 4
