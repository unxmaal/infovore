IP21 boards

Claims: 3 Part of IP21

The IP21 boards come in dual 75MHz configurations using dual R8K CPUs, and are used in Challenge L machines. 1 These boards are sensitive to ESD, and while the 90MHz and 75MHz IP21 boards are not broken, the cache is a common failure. 12 The dual 75MHz IP21 boards provide better floating-point performance than a single 150MHz R4K but similar integer performance, making them suitable for compute servers but not for Onyx or with IR/IMPACT. 3
