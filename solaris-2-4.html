Solaris 2.4

Claims: 11 Part of Solaris

Solaris 2.4 is a good release for exploring old Solaris, as it implements SVR4 and has interesting features. 1 Solaris 2.4 is considered an early release, covering the early to mid 90s. 2 Solaris 2.4 requires SunVideo and SunPC support, limiting options for users, and is the last version to support these. 34 Workshop 4.0 is the last version that runs on Solaris 2.4. 5 Photoshop 3 was for Solaris 2.4. 6 The AppleTalk driver requires Solaris 2.4 or later to work. 7 Solaris 2.4, SunOS 4.1.3, hp-ux 9.0, dec's osf/1 2.0, irix 5.2, and bsd/os 1.1 usually require digging for documentation. 8
