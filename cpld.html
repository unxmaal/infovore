CPLD

Claims: 9

A CPLD is a smaller, simpler FPGA that is 'burned' like a ROM. 1 CPLDs are used for various bridging and interface functions, including GIO-to-PCI bridging on Phobos ethernet cards, enabling the PI's GPIOs to interface with the 68000 bus so that native ARM applications can drive Amiga hardware, and implementing security features in the Q3D 50SB D3D driver. 234 A CPLD can serve as a cost-effective and smaller alternative to TTL logic, as may be the case with the 60GX, and a low-end FPGA or CPLD might be a viable solution for converting Serial to Joybus. 56
