Btrfs

Claims: 7

Btrfs is a modern filesystem that offers snapshot capabilities, which XFS lacks, though Btrfs has been associated with stability issues. 1 Btrfs is considered stable in certain circumstances, such as on Synology NAS devices, while XFS is regarded as suitable for basic needs. 2 Btrfs partitions can be dependent on the system memory page size, which may cause issues when mounted on a system with a different page size. 3 Btrfs was experimental on RHEL and was replaced by stratus, a LVM wrapper, and is not used in RHEL production. 45 Btrfs is used by Fedora but not in RHEL production. 5 The Synology DS2419+ claims a 4GB/s transfer speed but achieves only 250-300MB/s in practice with WD Red pro drives and BTRFS. 6 One user migrated their Linux system from BTRFS to XFS. 7
