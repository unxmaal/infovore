PC300GL/Intellistation E Pro

Claims: 3 Part of PC

The PC300GL/Intellistation E Pro requires a specific firmware version to support 256MB and ECC RAM. 1 It can use the same unbuffered PC100/133 ECC RAM as the Sun Blade 100/150. 2 The latest firmware for the PC300GL/Intellistation E Pro allows the use of a Pentium III processor. 3
