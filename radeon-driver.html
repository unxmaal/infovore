radeon driver

Claims: 14 Part of Radeon

The radeon driver is included in SLES 10, while the fglrx driver is part of Propack. 1 On Debian 7 with XFCE, the radeon driver runs on an Altix 350 with an ATI FireMV 2200, where 2D graphics worked well but 3D was more challenging. 23 The FireMV card causes unaligned memory errors when using the radeon driver, whether with one or both cards installed. 4 SLES9 does not boot with a FireGL R200, and its radeon driver does not support direct rendering on newer cards. 56 The radeon driver supports the FireGL X1s but not direct rendering. 7
