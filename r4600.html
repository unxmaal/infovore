R4600

Claims: 28 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see R4600.

The R4600 is a MIPS processor that replaced the R4000 in the product lineup as a cheaper chip for midrange Indys. 1 It is a redesign with the core redesigned by an outside agency, dropping advanced features for basic Unix workloads. 2 The R4600 can only run MIPS3, like the R4000 and R4400, whereas the R5000 and R8000 or higher can also execute MIPS4. 3 It was offered with secondary cache, and an R4600/133 with cache might hold its own against an R4400/100. 45 The R4600 is slower than the R4400 despite being a newer model, making it more of a sidegrade than an upgrade. 67 The R4600 is rare, and it was also used in the DeskStation TynE on an AT-style motherboard. 89
