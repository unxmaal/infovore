DB25

Claims: 8

DB25, HD50, and HDI30 cables are used for various SGI computers, with HD50 being the rarest for older SCSI-1 devices. 1 DB25 serial ports are standard, though the db25 port is not standard across all computers, as seen with the I2 and other systems using db9. 23 No jumpers are needed for DB25 to RS232 conversion. 4 A DB25 to DB9 adapter, likely a straight through adapter, can be used with a gender changer to change a DB25 connector to DB9, allowing a null modem adapter to connect. 56 The DB25 port on the Ultra5 is used for the PROM, not the DB9 port, and DB25 -> DB9 -> gender changer -> USB is a method to connect to the Ultra5 for diagnostics. 78
