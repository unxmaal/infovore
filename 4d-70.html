4D/70

Claims: 11

The 4D/70 has a unique color scheme of dark grey, raspberry, and beige. 1 MIPS introduced the R2300 MBC in May '86, but the 4D/60 was delayed due to software issues and insufficient performance, leading to the 4D/70 taking over. 2 Clover1 graphics hardware was used in early 4D/60, 4D/70, 4D/50, and 4D/80, running two sets of matrix multiplication GEs in parallel and a custom flat shading processor. 3 4D1-1 was exclusively for the 4D/60, while 4D/70 and 4D/50 did not get supported until 4D1-2. 4 4D/50 and 4D/70 should be working in the next release, but no graphics at all. 5 The 4D/70 and Personal IRIS do not have power supplies for the 12-slot TwinTower chassis, and a 4-pin power supply option for the Personal IRIS is listed but not observed. 6 The ENP10 is rebadged as a Motorola MVME330, and manuals for the MVME330 help emulate the ENP10, allowing 4D/50 and 4D/70 to connect to the internet. 7
