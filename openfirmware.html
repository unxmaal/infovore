OpenFirmware

Claims: 27

OpenFirmware is a firmware standard used across various systems, including Sun SPARC workstations, Powerstack II machines, and Motorola/Bull AIX 4.3.2 systems running on OpenFirmware and PPC1Bug flavors. 123 All PPC Macs used Open Firmware in some form, though IBM's OpenPower systems did not use OpenFirmware. 45 OpenFirmware is based on OpenBoot and both support FCode, though FCode has CPU ISA-agnosticness limitations due to incompatibilities between Apple Open Firmware and OpenBoot. 67 Open Firmware is purely forth, and you can enter Open Firmware instead of BIOS. 89 The SPARCstation firmware, including OpenFirmware, is open source. 1 There's an Open Firmware version for ARM too. 10
