LSI SAS3041X-R

Claims: 4

The LSI SAS3041X-R is a SAS/SATA PCI card featuring 4 internal SAS/SATA ports. 1 It is a compatible PCI-X HBA for SGI systems, alongside the LSI SAS3442X, SAS3442X-R, SAS3080X-R, and SAS3800X. 23 These cards allow access to SATA drives after the kernel is loaded. 3 The LSI SAS3041X-R supports SATA II SSDs and is faster than NVMe due to a wider/faster bus. 4
