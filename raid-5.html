RAID 5

Claims: 7 Part of RAID

RAID 5 can be unreliable, especially during rebuilds, which can damage HDDs, and software RAID 5 can fail due to hardware component failures such as the backplane or SCSI chip on the controller. 12 A Pentium 100 box with 4x 2.1 gig drives in a RAID 5 runs DOS and Netware. 3 The parity in a RAID 5 array is distributed across all drives, so the resilvering process depends on the number of drives and how full the array is. 4 RAID 5 and higher do not have parity calculation overhead when using XVM. 5 Accessing a RAID-5 over all disks made a memorable sound. 6
