NFS share

Claims: 21 Part of NFS

NFS shares can store disk images created with dd. 1 NFS shares can hold IRIX installers and development packages, and properly chosen paths can serve as installation prefixes for cross-compilers, allowing direct installation on an SGI. 23 BSD or Solaris are better choices for an NFS server than macOS. 4 The boot process can reboot and run INST with a new selections file pointing to an NFS share for MIPSpro stuff. 5 NFS shares can be used to transfer files to IRIX, and an IPX can copy files to an NFS share. 67 A conditional mount in fstab allows the NFS share to connect on demand, avoiding issues with systems not always on. 8 To mount an NFS share from IRIX to Linux, add the share to /etc/fstab with appropriate options and click the unmounted drive icon in the file manager to mount it on demand. 9 Moving /usr/src to an NFS share on a gem ethernet could improve performance. 10
