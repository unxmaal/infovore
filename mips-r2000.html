MIPS R2000

Claims: 4 Part of MIPS

The MIPS R2000 is one of the devices defined by DEFINE_DEVICE_TYPE in the MAME project, alongside the R2000A, R3000, R3000A, R3041, R3051, R3052, R3052E, R3071, R3081, and SONYPS2_IOP. 1 It has a 16-bit address bus that is word-addressed because the lower 2 bits are chopped off, a configuration managed through CPU signals and address decoder circuitry. 2 For NFS setup, older systems like the MIPS R2000 are stuck on version 4.0.5, which is why version 5.3 is used for them. 3
