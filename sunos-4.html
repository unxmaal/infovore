SunOS 4

Claims: 19 Part of SunOS

SunOS 4 was a good BSD, combining 4.2, 4.3, and some SysV extensions. 12 It was ported to the SPARCclassic and later SPARCstations at customer request, but was not expected to run on the TurboSPARC, with the last supported model being the 110 MHz MicroSPARC. 34 The Solbourne S4000DX ran OS/MP, a version of SunOS 4 with Solbourne-specific patches. 5 It came with SunView and OpenWindows/OpenLook as graphical environments, while X11R5 with Motif was available for custom binary X server support. 67 OracleDB ran on SunOS 4, and IPX, IPC, and swapping over NFS were also supported. 8910 SunOS 4 and Ultrix were similar, with a slightly different flavor. 11
