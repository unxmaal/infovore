UltraSPARC II

Claims: 15

The UltraSPARC II supports 120 MHz dual CPU modules and includes an MPEG-2 decoding extension. 12 The UltraSPARC II was the last CPU from Sun to use external memory controllers, a design choice possibly prioritizing compatibility over newer memory technologies like DDR. 34 A 400MHz UltraSPARC II can saturate a 100Mbit Ethernet interface, but machines at 400MHz or less struggle with gigabit networking due to CPU speed. 56 The UltraSPARC II has similar integer performance to the R10k, but the R10k excels in floating-point operations. 7 Linux performs better than OpenBSD on UltraSPARC CPUs, especially on UltraSPARC II and newer models, which work best while earlier models have compatibility issues. 89
