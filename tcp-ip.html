TCP/IP

Claims: 11 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Ethernet.

TCP/IP is used for online play in StarCraft, which relies on TCP/IP and UDP ports 6112. 1 TCP/IP was supported on systems where Gigabit Ethernet was unavailable and ATM was used with LANE. 2 IRIX included support for TCP/IP, and SGI used it. 3 AppleShare became irrelevant due to TCP/IP, except for very old Macs using LocalTalk. 4 SSP for as/400s does not support ethernet or TCP/IP, even if the machine has an ethernet IOA. 5 Most SysV vendors offered TCP/IP as an option, some of which were bought in from a company named 'Lachman'. 6 The store controller, which runs on the VM, can communicate over SNA and TCP/IP, and possibly has Java and a telnet server. 7 Ethernet and TCP/IP are interdependent for network interoperability. 8
