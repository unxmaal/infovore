SCSI cable

Claims: 19 Part of SCSI

A SCSI cable is a cable used to connect devices, and it can connect a drive bay or drive cage backplane, which may also receive power. 123 The Indy has an external SCSI port that can be adapted to 50-pin IDC or O350 external SCSI using an external SCSI cable, and the O350 has a 68-pin SCSI connector on the enclosure. 4 A SCSI cable with self termination can be used instead of a separate terminator, and the BlueSCSI requires the termination jumper to be set to off if the Indigo has a terminator at the end of the SCSI cable. 56 A SCSI cable can be routed from an internal port on an R10k chassis by drilling or modifying a drive sled, and a complex SCSI cable setup can still work. 78 If a SCSI cable is damaged, it may need to be cut out and replaced, and a system not booting can indicate a hardware issue such as an improperly connected SCSI cable. 910 The CD-ROM and HDD are not detected with a SCSI cable, and autodetection of IDs works on startup but throws register errors, suggesting poor contact or a faulty cable. 11
