SDRAM

Claims: 12

SDRAM became an issue when 72-pin RAM became obsolete, with RAM speeds ranging from 66 to 133 MHz. 1 All Apple Macintosh machines from that era use SD RAM, with G4 systems typically needing 133 MHz and benefiting from faster options. 2 Standard SDRAM is used, save for being ECC and unbuffered. 3 The O2 runs at 128-bit wide 133 MHz SDRAM, while the Octane uses 256-bit wide 133 MHz SDRAM. 4 The O2 uses off-the-shelf SDRAM in a different DIMM format. 5 The Zx10 uses SDRAM which is less expensive and more reliable than RAMBUS technology incorporated with the i840 chips. 6 A dual Tualatin board supports DDR RAM but uses SDRAM, and the dual P3 can use either RDRAM or SDRAM. 78 Designing a PCB for SDRAM within timing constraints is a challenging task. 9
