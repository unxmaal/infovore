R10k/195

Claims: 4

The R10K-195 is the maximum processor for a R10K O2, though it can still be sluggish on a R10K-300 O2. 1 An R10k/195 runs rings around a P2/266. 2 An R10k/195 could probably handle a SCSI DVD drive, but an R4600/133 almost certainly can't. 3 A 060-0021 power supply can run an R10K/195 with High Impact if it has the blue impact connector, though it might require an upgrade. 4
