IBM PC300PL

Claims: 5 Part of IBM

The IBM PC300PL, also known as the Intellistation, is a thick slab similar in size to an Indigo2. 1 It takes standard SDRAM. 2 It can run DOS and Deskmate with a P3/700 CPU and a 3D Rage Pro Turbo 8MB video card. 3 There is an IBM PC300PL running OPENSTEP. 4
