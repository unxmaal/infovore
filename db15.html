DB15

Claims: 4

The DB15 connector is used on deskside SGI systems, while the 4D/20 and 4D/25 use 9-pin connectors and the Onyx and Indigo use miniDIN 6 connectors, and all use the same protocol so they can be rewired between each other. 1 The DB15 port is often referred to as an AUI port, commonly used for connecting network interfaces. 2 The 4D systems keyboard has a DB9 or DB15 system end, with an optical mouse attached. 3 A DB15 to serial splitter cable is needed. 4
