R16000

Claims: 26 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see R10000, SGI Tezro.

The R16K is a MIPS IV derivative that began at 700 MHz, representing a clock-speed bump of the R14K with few microarchitectural improvements. 12 The R12K, R14K, and R16K pushed boundaries to keep up with Intel's Pentium II and III advancements, and R16K development was costly. 34 The R16K is part of the standard architecture covering everything through the R16K. 5 The R16K can be used in various SGI systems, including the Fuel, Onyx350, O350, IP35, and IP53. 678910
