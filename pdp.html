PDP

Claims: 11

The PDP is a line of mini computers produced by DEC, alongside the VAX and AXP systems. 12 Early PDPs used 74000 series logic chips, and DEC's hardware and software, especially from the PDP-11 era, were known for durability and innovation with strong appeal to academic and scientific institutions. 34 PDPs could be paired with early VAX 11 systems. 5 Moonlander was a text-only game played on a PDP, with a graphical version later made by DEC in 1973. 6 A PDP could be attached with a massive framebuffer to make a CG computer, and 8" floppies often contained bad sectors that could corrupt data during operations such as Fourier transforms. 78 To create a zuluscsi bootable image for the PDP, use xxdpdir.pl to extract programs from another disk image and xxdpdir to initialize an MSCP image. 9
