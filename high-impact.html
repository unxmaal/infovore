High Impact

Claims: 11

SGI High Impact systems were available for around 300 dollars at a VCF event a few years ago. 1 High Impact and Max Impact have 1MB textures, while Solid Impact has two cards and High and Maximum Impact have different configurations. 23 High Impact has texture memory that can be expanded to 4MB with a TRAM module, whereas Solid Impact does not have texture memory. 4 High Impact and Solid Impact do not share the same base PCB. 5 High Impact breaks compatibility with earlier software and requires TRAM cards for 24-bit textures. 6 Only frustration with High Impact is you don't get 24-bit textures unless you add extra TRAM. 7 High Impact works well, although 1 MB of TRAM on it is kind of rough. 8
