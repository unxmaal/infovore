IRIX 6.4

Claims: 12 Part of IRIX 6

IRIX 6.4 was marketed under the brand name Cellular IRIX, which was used specifically for the Octane and its 6.4 applications. 12 It ran on the Octane, Onyx2, O2000, and O200, though it was not tested. 3 Installation can require up to 7 CDs, including a patch disk, and newer versions including 6.4 include working Zip drivers. 45 IRIX 6.4 does not support R12000 CPUs, though booting from specific patches may allow installation. 6 MIPSPro Pascal versions ranged up to 7.1 for IRIX 6.4. 7 Patch sets for IRIX 5.3 to 6.4 existed as free downloads or bootable CDs. 8
