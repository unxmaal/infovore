cache chips

Claims: 6 Part of cache

The cache chips on the R10K module require a 3V3 supply. 1 The R8k module is sensitive to static, especially the cache chips with copper foil, and grounding oneself helps prevent damage. 2 Overclocking the cache chips of an O2 can increase the cache miss rate, leading to worse performance. 3 Cache chips can fail, as evidenced by dead cache chips in SGI CPUs. 4
