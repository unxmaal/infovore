MP3

Claims: 9

MP3s can be played on a range of older hardware, including a PowerBook 540c (68LC040@33MHz) using Mpegdec, a reasonably-specced Indy via XMMS, the Falcon through its DSP, and an Indigo R3000, which was the slowest machine to play MP3s. 1234 MP3s were also supported on other devices and systems, such as the Zire 71 (which handled MP3 and video), NetBSD (via a VAX mpg123 package), and media players that supported FS. 56 In one case, a vinyl record was converted to MP3 for editing to simulate VHS quality. 7
