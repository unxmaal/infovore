Solaris 9

Claims: 40 Part of Solaris Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris.

Solaris 9 is not the first regular x86 release of Solaris. 1 Solaris 9 can run on a Blade 150 with a 650 MHz UltraSPARC IIe, 2 GB RAM, XVR-600, and 80 GB HDD, and a Blade 1500 with an XVR-600 can run it without special patches. 23 Solaris 9 can run on a minimum-spec SS4, but it is not recommended due to JDS being a memory hog, and the last versions of MAE will work on UltraSPARC and anything that runs Solaris 9 or lower. 45 Solaris 9 is compatible with GNOME, and CDE, Window Maker, or Icewm are recommended. 67 Solaris 9 supports FireWire storage, whereas Solaris 8 supported USB mass storage, and FireWire storage can be made to work with a TI PCI-1394 card. 89 Solaris 9 is not officially supported with original SunPCI hardware, and Sun Studio 12 can run on Solaris 9 SPARC systems, but JDK 1.4 is the latest available. 1011
