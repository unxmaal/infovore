InfiniteReality4

Claims: 9 Part of InfiniteReality Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx2.

The Origin 350 can be connected to an InfiniteReality 3 or 4 in theory, and a deskside Onyx350 is an Origin 350 plugged into an InfiniteReality3 or 4. 12 InfiniteReality4 is available for the Onyx2, providing high graphical performance, and is the fastest SGI graphics workstation. 34 The InfiniteReality 4 had 6.4 billion vertex shader operations per second, while the X850 XT had 5.7 billion. 5 Infinite Reality 4 offers the best Quake III performance on SGI systems. 6 InfiniteReality4 does not support SGIX_cube_map, which is from 1998 and was exclusive to SGI hardware. 7
