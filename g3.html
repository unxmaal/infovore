G3

Claims: 78 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see PowerBook G3, Mac OS 9, Quake III Arena.

With G4

Deus Ex requires a fast G3 or G4 and a decent GPU, specifically an ATI Rage 128 or 3dfx, but runs well on modern OS 9 GPUs; it can be played on macOS with a Glide wrapper. 1 G4s are slower than G5s and G3s. 2 High end G4 beats high end G3 easily. 3 G5 beats both G4 and G3. 4 Dual core G5 is faster than G4 and G3. 5 A G3 can support up to 1GB of RAM, while a G4 can support more. 6 Many G3 and G4 towers had Zip drives. 7 The gap between a lower-end G4 and G3 is not significant. 8

General

The G3 is a PowerPC processor that can run Mac OS 9, OS X 10.2, and Rhapsody, and can share the same OS and programs as G4 Macs. 9101112 The G3 is a low-power processor, and its performance can be modified by overclocking with jumpers. 1314 The G3 has 400 MHz CPU, 256 MB RAM, and a large HDD, and its cache significantly improves integer performance. 1015 The G3 can do S-Video and composite video, and the ATI Theater 550 Pro and AV breakout are compatible with it. 161718 The G3 is ATA-2 with an IDE limit of 128 GiB, but upgraded disk interfaces and SCSI adapters can improve drive performance. 19202122 The G3 can run Quake 3, which requires 3D acceleration, and the Radeon 7000 runs GLQuake faster than the Voodoo3 2000. 2324 The G3 with a Radeon 7000 has a patch to enable Quartz Extreme, and the 128MB Apple version of the Radeon 9200 is the fastest card for it. 2526 The G3 outperforms SGI machines in Blender, and upgrading it is cheaper than upgrading a real UNIX workstation. 2728 The G3 is a PowerPC system, with a beige model released in October 1998 and an early clamshell variant. 293031 The first G3 had onboard floppy disk hardware and an early nVidia chipset. 3233 G3 and G4 PowerPC systems run PowerPC software faster than earlier models. 34 The G3 can use a modem module, boot Tru64, automatically install USB drivers when reinstalled, and run LISP with Allegro Common Lisp as a licensed dev kit. 35363738 A G3 upgrade on a Power Macintosh 6500 with a 500MHz 740 CPU improves performance and usability in OS X compared to cache cards. 39 Toast 9.2.1 installs on a G3 and runs on macOS. 40 The G3 architecture was not SMP capable from the start, and IBM had to modify Espresso to support multicore processing. 41
