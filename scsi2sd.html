SCSI2SD

Claims: 107 Part of SCSI

It is a cost-effective choice that has been reliable for old RS6000, old SGI, old Mac, and DEC systems. 1 It can emulate a CD drive compatible with A/UX, provide two separate 4GB drives, and serve both primary storage and OS needs. 234 It allows partitioning an SD card to act as multiple SCSI LUNs, simplifying disk management and enabling complete system backups by imaging the SD card. 5 However, it is not high-performance and can be corrupted by power outages. 6 Sequential write performance is slow, around 150-300 kB/s, and it can be a bottleneck for disk I/O on machines like the Indy. 78 It has been reported as less reliable and slower than 50-pin drives, with random read/write errors possibly due to SD card or filesystem issues. 910 The red SCSI2SD connectors have had issues, and it requires the computer to be shut off to reload anything. 1112 SCSI2SD is a flash-storage adapter recommended for older SGI machines that use SCSI, though it may be expensive and finicky with certain machines. 13141516 It can emulate a CDROM and a HDD at the same time, but does not support structured files like tapes, only flat image formats like .iso. 1718 The scsi2sd utility creates a disk of any size less than the SD card, best under 2G for old computers, and requires a file path rather than a directory. 1920 Some systems require specific vendor strings for SCSI2SD to work, such as 'DEC' for booting on a DECstation. 21 Performance-wise, most 7200 RPM or better UltraSCSI-2 disks beat a SCSI2SD but have higher latency, making SCSI2SD more responsive, though it is slow in U160/U320 systems and compared to RaSCSI. 222324 The microcontroller is the main limitation for SCSI2SD speed, with no wide version available due to pin limitations. 25 SCSI2SD is a solid-state storage solution that can replace CD-ROM drives and emulate multiple devices, though it may be slow for large media files and may have worse performance than a 15k SCSI drive. 262728 The SCSI2SD uses FPGA acceleration for faster transfer speeds, moving away from bitbanging. 29 SCSI2SD requires manual configuration using a special tool that differs between versions 5x and 6x. 30 The old SGI IO boards reject SCSI2SD, and performance degraded significantly on Indigo2 models after the 5.3 version. 3132 A good SCSI2SD setup is more expensive than enough 15k disks to fully populate two Fuels. 33 The PowerBook 170 can use a SCSI2SD card and is fully functional without tunneling. 34 ZuluSCSI exists because parts for SCSI2SD are dried up and it's hard to find an SD2SCSI v6, and it is preferred for generic SCSI disks over SCSI2SD. 3536 ZuluSCSI is a newer version of SCSI2SD available from https://shop.rabbitholecomputing.com/products/zuluscsi-rp2040-compact. 37
