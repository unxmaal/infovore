PWS

Claims: 6

The PWS requires different RAM modules than the 164LX, and the board should be placed on a non-conductive base such as cardboard when powered with direct current at a maximum of 12 V. 1 The PWS can accept registered/buffered ECC modules, as its documentation shows a 64-MB DIMM with buffer chips. 23 A DEC PC might not work with memory from a PWS. 4 Windows 2000 and Tru64 cannot coexist on a single PWS machine. 5 The RRD45 CDROM is a generic SCSI drive that can do 512k per sector and can be replaced with any SGI compatible SCSI drive, newer PWS can boot from an IDE CDROM, but VMS requires a SCSI CDROM. 6
