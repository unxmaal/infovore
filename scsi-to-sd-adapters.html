SCSI-to-SD adapters

Claims: 3

SCSI-to-SD adapters should use good-quality SD cards rather than the cheapest options, and users should make backups. 1 SCSI to SD adapters do not support TRIM commands, which can lead to performance degradation over time on SSDs. 2 SCSI-to-SD adapters may not offer optimal performance on machines with 68pin or SCA SCSI, where Fast SCSI 2 can be a good option. 3
