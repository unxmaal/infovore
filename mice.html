mice

Claims: 12

Old UNIX boxes can be picky about mice and may fail to display video over a KVM due to USB requirements, showing errors such as those on the zx6000. 1 Some systems may not recognize mice that present as multiple HID devices. 2 Standard keyboards and mice are more reliable than fancy ones, and old 6-inch-high keyboards and mice that cause CTS within an hour are disliked. 34 SGI made some nice keyboards but not nice mice. 5 Granite keyboards and mice use the same protocol and are nicer for I1, Onyx, and PI systems. 6 Indigo keyboards and mice can be adapted using specific cables for compatibility with other SGI systems, and Indigo or Onyx keyboards and mice can be used with these systems. 78 Sun mice require specific mousepads, with Type 4 pads working correctly and Type 5 pads being extra slow and incompatible. 9 Some mice support PS/2 mode with an adapter. 10
