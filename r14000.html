R14000

Claims: 27 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see R10000, SGI Octane.

The R12000 and R14000 are CPU models used in SGI machines, with the R12000 starting shipping in February 1999 at up to 300 MHz and subsequent generations offering boosted clock speeds but few microarchitectural improvements. 12 The R12K, R14K, and R16K pushed boundaries to keep up with Intel's Pentium II and III advancements, with better clockspeed and more cache memory. 34 The R14k module is substantially different from the earlier processor modules, having different board layouts and CPU chips, and is a soldered chip unlike the earlier carrier style. 5 An R14K CPU for the Octane can run at 550 MHz, and a 600 MHz R14000 CPU is available. 67 The R14K supports DDR external cache and possibly faster SysAD bus speeds compared to R10k/R12k. 8 The R14k performs more operations per cycle than the Pentium III, which wastes cycles on setup, so a 600 MHz R14000 CPU will outperform a 600 MHz Pentium III CPU. 910 The r12k and r14k are the final r10k revisions that mattered, and their emulation could reach similar performance to a 1GHz r16k, which was just a clock bump of the r14k. 11
