IIc

Claims: 21 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Apple IIc.

The Apple IIc is a highly integrated, self-contained Apple II system similar to a Macintosh but smaller and simpler for home users, featuring a built-in floppy drive and the only Apple II with a GUI. 12 It was designed by Frog Design, which also worked on the NeXT cube and SparcStation 1. 3 The IIc+ is an upgrade option for the IIc. 4 The IIc has a composite monitor and a 15-pin port for analog RGB. 56 The IIc's serial port tops out at 300 baud without issues, though it may not be working on some units, such as an early ROM 255 unit. 789 The IIc has a slow floppy drive, and floppy disk drives can be unreliable, especially when upgrading ROM. 1011 The IIc had a RAM chip decode issue fixed by replacing a single IC, and the connection is press-fitted, not soldered. 1213 The IIc LCD had a small viewing angle that made it unsuitable for working or playing games, but aftermarket LCDs could improve its usability. 141516
