VESA

Claims: 6

The design includes multiple ports for different sync signals, including VESA and SGI, with plans for USB. 1 VESA and SGI stereo signals have opposite polarity, so an inverter is needed. 2 VESA is slow, and this can be noticed when moving windows. 3 OPENSTEP 4.2 runs every card in dumb framebuffer mode, so VESA is as good as any due to how DPS works. 4 X on SLES11 requires a specific kernel module and may crash with radeon or vesa, and Debian might be an alternative but lacks the SGI_AGP module. 5
