PowerBase 180

Claims: 4

The PowerBase 180 is a 180 MHz PowerPC 603 or 603e-based system with a 36 MHz bus, alongside machines such as the StarMax 3000/180, Umax C500/180 (Apus 2180), and C600/180 (Apus 3180). 1 It has 96 MiB of RAM, with some units missing a PCI bracket for a Voodoo card. 2 The PowerBase 180 runs Netscape and has Frogfind, 68k.news, and MacGarden FTP. 3 The boards inside the machine are likely intact despite minor cosmetic damage, but a power cable and plug are needed for testing. 4
