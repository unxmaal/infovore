AV1

Claims: 18

The AV1 is an analog video option that supports composite, svideo, and audio, and includes a camera connector. 12 The AV1 is essential for the software to run; without it, the system won't start, and the system can be accessed via a serial console with a null modem cable. 3 The AV1 provides two in/outs and a genlock port, and with the Miranda device, you retain all analog ports plus an SDI in/out. 4 The AV1 can provide SDI in/out with the Miranda VIVO, though its inputs and outputs are otherwise all analog. 56 The AV2 is the digital video option that offers better video quality than the AV1 but requires an SDI setup. 178 The default media recorder can output the IRIX desktop to an AV1 or AV2. 9
