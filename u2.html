U2

Claims: 28

The U2 is a Sun workstation analogous to the Indigo2, with the Blade 1000/2000 analogous to the Octane. 1 It has a UPA slot (or two, allowing dual Creator 3D cards) and 4 SBus slots, supporting Creator3D, Elite3D, or CG6 graphics. 2345 The U2 can take up to 2GB of RAM, dual disks, and bare CPU modules, and it improved significantly over the U1 with better CPU options, more RAM, faster disks, and 24-bit accelerated graphics. 6748 It supports Sbus FC cards for speed, with its SBus HME interface working without trouble and the SBus GigaSwift running close to wire speed. 910 The U2 was powerful enough for firewalls and servers, remained relevant for some time, and was not significantly slower than the U60 until the Ultra 80 or Blade 1000. 1112 U2 systems have plastics that degrade over time, and the non-UPA U1 had limited graphics and could not take multiple CPUs. 1314
