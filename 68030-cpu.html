68030 CPU

Claims: 7 Part of 68030

The Motorola 68030 is a CPU used in a variety of machines, including the Sun 3/80, the Apollo 5952, the Cisco 2500, and the Amiga 2000/2500. 123 The LC III has a 68030 CPU running at 25 MHz, while the LC III+ uses a faster 33 MHz 68030. 4 The Apollo 5952 has a 20 MHz 68030 CPU paired with a 68882, 4 MB to 16 MB of RAM, a black-and-white framebuffer at 1024x800 or 1280x1024, and optional SCSI with 100, 200, or 400 MB disks, as well as optional Ethernet, Apollo token ring, or IBM connectivity. 2 The Cisco 2500 has a 68030 CPU, whereas the Amiga 2000/2500 has a 68000 CPU, and the 2500 may have an accelerator board for dual-head support. 3 A machine with a 68030 CPU and an HP-IB interface lacks SCSI, which prevents the OS from booting. 5
