riser board

Claims: 5 Part of riser

The I2 can boot over serial but cannot output video due to potential riser board issues. 1 The ATI Fire GL X1 drivers were buggy and the performance was poor, leading to the design of a new riser board with AGP Pro slots. 2 The SGI Altix350 line only sold bootable base-bricks and compute bricks; MPX and PCI-X bricks were not available. 3 The A350 compute brick lacked the riserboard with PCI-X slots. 3 The A350 compute nodes are not bootable without the io9 or io10 and a riser-board, which are sold separately and can be expensive. 4 Altix350 compute bricks were the cheapest Itanium machines for many years, starting at $30, but required a riserboard for booting which provided 4 PCI-X slots. 5
