DM2 card

Claims: 4 Part of DM2 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The DM2 card is for video input/output and requires a breakout box. 1 It is used for the HD DMediaPro option on Onyx2 and Octane2, using a 200-pin connector with a custom breakout cable. 2
