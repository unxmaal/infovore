EFS volume

Claims: 3 Part of EFS

An EFS volume can span multiple cylinders with varying block counts, as shown in the partition table, with a total capacity of 658770 blocks. 1 Softimage 3.7 may work with Irix 5.3 on an EFS volume. 2 Irix 5.3 can be installed on an 8gb EFS volume successfully, though software manager may initially report strange results regarding free disk space. 3 After a few Irix patches are installed, software manager reports correctly and the full 8gb EFS volume is usable. 3

Sources

  1. The partition table shows an EFS volume spanning multiple cylinders with varying block counts, and a total capacity of 658770 blocks. (user-7973, 2021-12-27, exchange 80573, check: supported)
  2. Softimage 3.7 may work with Irix 5.3 on EFS volume. (user-74cb, 2021-11-17, exchange 217309, check: supported)
  3. I installed Irix 5.3 on a 8gb EFS volume fine, however I remember software manager reporting strange results re free disk space. After a few Irix patches installed, swmgr reported fine and the full 8gb EFS volume was bo (user-ce6b, 2022-02-09, exchange 240990, check: supported)
