VAXstation

Claims: 22 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see VAXstation.

The VAXstation is a DEC machine that ran OpenVMS as its standard operating system, and could also run Ultrix. 12 It used SCSI storage and offered 2D acceleration, while some high-end models added 3D acceleration with an Intergraph card. 345 Its video connectors provided mono and color options, but the machine was not compatible with a VT220 display. 67 It is considered among the slowest machines ever, slower than a VAX 4/233, an Ultra 1, and a DECstation MIPS system. 8910 The fastest VAXStation was significantly slower than a 466 MHz Alpha processor. 11
