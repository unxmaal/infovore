winterm

Claims: 20 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Xterm.

Winterm is the terminal emulator that ships with IRIX, not the shell, and it can run any shell. 12 The blue winterm window is the primary terminal used for normal operations, and it is opened from the Admin menu's 'UNIX console' option. 34 Winterm can be configured to increase scrollback lines on IRIX, and it renders properly when run from the vizserver window. 56 Winterm is not fully compatible with various tools, and some outputs may not display correctly in it. 78 Winterm lacks support for many commands when TERM=ansi or ansiterm, and ncurses is not well-supported in it. 910 Winterm is fundamentally incapable of displaying ls --color. 11 In winterm, setting erase to ^H makes backspace work correctly, while in Aterm it makes backspace return ^H. 12 xterm can be installed to address winterm problems and is more compatible with a variety of tools. 138
