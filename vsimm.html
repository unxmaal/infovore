VSIMM

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARCstation 20.

The SX24 is the VSIMM option available in the SPARCstation 20 and a couple of other models. 1 On the SS20, the VSIMM provides native 24-bit color on the motherboard framebuffer. 2 The Sparcstation 20 can have an odd number of RAM slots with a VSIMM installed, leading to amounts like 400 MB with 64 * 6 + 16 RAM slots. 3 The VSIMM is not a great graphics card for anything other than 24-bit graphics, as the basic GX was 1989 technology. 4 The VSIMM in LX provides a minor resolution bump. 5 The VSIMM has a main chip that can have a wire bond come loose, which can be fixed by snipping the bond. 6
