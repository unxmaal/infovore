Fuel PROM

Claims: 3 Part of Fuel

The Fuel PROM can be upgraded by flashing or configuring it, which allows the CPU to be upgraded. 1 The Fuel PROM does not allow modification of the boot disk number. 2 The stack limit causes network drops during initialization, and the Fuel PROM lacks a command to show the Ethernet address, requiring a bootp to discover it. 3
