U5

Claims: 23

The U5 is a slab in the Ultras family, alongside the U1, B100 and B150, while the U2 is a big slab and the U10 is a small tower. 1 The U5 runs at 400 MHz, the same speed as the Octane and Powerbook. 2 The U5 can support up to 512MB of RAM, while the U10 can support up to 1GB, because the original RAM modules are double-height and require the removal of the floppy drive to fit. 3 The U5 has an IDE controller and network card that are considered poor, with the network card being slower than the Sbus HME. 45 The U5's IDE works but does not support UDMA, and both the U5 and U10 IDE controllers could only do PIO Mode 4 unless properly configured. 67 The UPA graphics card does not fit in the U5 due to physical size constraints, and the onboard Mach64 is disabled if another card is installed. 89 The U5 has reported PSU issues, memory errors that persist despite new memory, and problems detecting memory in the DIMM slots. 101112
