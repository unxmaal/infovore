IRIXNet

Claims: 21

IRIXNet was formed in 2017 to offer an alternative to Nekochan.net due to restrictions on commercial software sales and previous downtime. 1 IRIXNet has a lot of mirrors of things, including FTP mirrors for IRIX software at http://usftp.irixnet.org/. 23 IRIXNet provides IRIX images as tgz files, not ISOs, but user-22f9 uploaded image contents for IRIX 6.5.26. 4 The files needed for emulating IRIX on MAME can be found linked from a Pinned post on the IRIXNet wiki, and emulation speed can be adjusted for better responsiveness. 56 The IRIXNet wiki tries to aggregate information from various sources but hasn't fully succeeded. 7 IRIXNet has plans to retrieve and wikify PSU documentation, and has permission to provide hard copies of Nekonomicon. 89
