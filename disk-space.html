disk space

Claims: 7 Part of disk

Several programs require disk space for their operation: Building Compilertron requires a significant amount of disk space, but 75GB should be sufficient as it is a virtual machine and can be provisioned with additional storage if needed. 1 Running 'inst' requires a certain amount of disk space under /var/tmp or an equivalent directory, and it is advisable to have this space available or to move the untarred files before running 'inst'. 2 Booterizer requires enough disk space to download and untar disc images. 3 Old SGI systems require more memory and disk space for undo history. 4 Running out of disk space from log files or user directories is less of an issue in IRIX compared to systems with less than 1GB disks. 5 Binaries on the disk do not consume resources other than disk space. 6
