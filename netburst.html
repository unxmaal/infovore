NetBurst

Claims: 4

The NetBurst microarchitecture is an out-of-order superscalar design featuring a QDR front-side bus, which is slower than the in-order superscalar UltraSPARC III with an SDR or DDR bus. 1 NetBurst maintained performance leadership through the Athlon XP era due to higher clock speeds, but was overtaken by the Athlon64 because of improved instructions per clock. 2 Intel later abandoned NetBurst and returned to P6-style designs. 3 The NetBurst architecture, used in Xeon 7140M processors, featured a 16 MiB L3 cache on a dual-core die, making it capable of handling around 200 users for IPTV services. 4
