Zulu

Claims: 32

The Zulu is a hybrid design cost-reduced from SCSI2SD hardware, featuring more flexible firmware with elements from BlueSCSI. 1 The Zulu is a clone of the Zulu Wide, and is considered a hardware perspective improvement over the BlueSCSI V2, which is a cost-cut derivative. 23 The Zulu is the fastest non-acard flash that is bootable on a PCI system. 4 The Zulu works without issues on multiple SGI models including Indigo, Indy x2, and Indigo 2 x2. 5 The Zulu can scan the whole bus and dump all drives at once, and can be used for xfsdump with NFSv1 or SMBv1 share. 67 The Zulu provides both termination and power for a SCSI connection, allowing a direct SCA to IDC cable. 8
