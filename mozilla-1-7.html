Mozilla 1.7

Claims: 6 Part of Mozilla

Mozilla 1.7 was the last official Mozilla suite for IRIX before it became Firefox. 1 IRIX 6.5.30 ships with Mozilla 1.7 as the default browser. 2 SGI's original Mozilla 1.7 bundled the ChatZilla client, which is used for a GUI experience. 3 The UnixFiles.org site is compatible with Mozilla 1.7 in IRIX 6.5.30 and serves as the primary source for software after a fresh IRIX installation. 4 The site is fully functioning in Mozilla 1.7 bundled with IRIX 6.5.30. 5
