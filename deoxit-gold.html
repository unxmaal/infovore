DeoxIt Gold

Claims: 5 Part of Deoxit

DeoxIt Gold is used as a contact spray to clean and protect male backplane connectors and male connector pins. 12 Applying it to DIMM and card edge contacts is a routine maintenance practice, and it can improve connectivity by wetting the other side while reducing insertion force and fixing crushed pins. 324 It requires careful handling to avoid overspray, and it must be fully dried before assembly. 45
