Powermac SE/30

Claims: 4 Part of PowerMac

The Diimo is the only Power Mac SE/30 model that works with A/UX, and a 040 CPU breaks compatibility with System 6. 1 Troubleshooting the Powermac SE/30 requires focusing on the video circuitry before addressing the CPU side. 2 Some drives have issues with PCBs touching the casing, causing short circuits, and electrical tape can help but may not always stick properly. 3 A 68-Pin to 80-Pin adapter can be used for compatibility, but some may short out with the metal casing of the HDD. 4
