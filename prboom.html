PrBoom

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX.

Various Doom ports are available for SGI, including PrBoom, PrBoom+, XDoom, and Doom Legacy with OpenGL support. 1 PrBoom and Doom Legacy have OpenGL renderers for IRIX. 2 PrBoom+ is a version of PrBoom that supports OpenGL, including OGL 1.1 and 1.2 for vpro, to utilize hardware acceleration when available. 3 Packing issues with animdef_t required a pragma pack to fix the MIPSpro build for PrBoom on IRIX. 4 The Software Manager installed SDL files in /usr/local/lib, which PrBoom does not check. 5 PrBoom crashes in OpenGL mode due to a warning related to GL texture size, which might be 2GB. 6
