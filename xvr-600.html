XVR-600

Claims: 17

The XVR-600 is a 3.3 V-only card, and it does not work in the 5 V U5 slots because of the voltage difference. 1 It supports OpenGL 2.0 and provides performance comparable to a V12 CPU in an Octane, about 80% as fast. 23 It is faster at most things but not at tasks that require more RAM. 4 It is confirmed to work in the Blade 150, the Ultra 60, and the V480, where it draws 25 Watts and allows up to three cards. 567 To boot into a new screen resolution, one must use the command `boot -r`, and the XVR-600 on Solaris 10 cannot run without vsync. 89
