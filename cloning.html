cloning

Claims: 4

Cloning involves copying an installed drive to avoid repeating the installation process. 1 Sun encouraged cloning of their hardware, with many companies producing SPARC and SBus clones. 2 Cloning an SGI disk can cause system hangs if the target disk has a malfunction. 3 Cloning the OS disk to a zulu increases startup time by about 5 seconds, which is not noticeable, and the performance boost compared to a spinning disk is minor. 4
