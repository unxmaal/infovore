EISA bus slot

Claims: 3 Part of EISA

The EISA bus slot is a two-level staggered pin system whose upper part corresponds to the standard ISA bus pin layout. 1 It has five keying notches to prevent ISA cards with long traces from extending into the lower part of the slot. 2 EISA cards have intentionally longer fingers and more notches to prevent ISA cards from interfering with EISA signals. 3
