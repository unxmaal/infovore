Quake2

Claims: 29 Part of Quake Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Quake II.

Quake2 is a game that runs on a wide range of hardware, from IRIX workstations to the Raspberry Pi and Atari Falcon. 123 On IRIX, Quake2 can only run in the screenmode set by the OS, and changing the OS screenmode affects the game's resolution and performance. 4 Quake2 works fullscreen on IRIX, unlike Quake1 GL which runs in a window. 5 The Voodoo card can cause Quake2 to freeze on specific points in the game, requiring a full system reboot to recover. 6 Quake2 uses a DLL-based engine, unlike the simpler QuakeC engine used in Quake1. 7 Quake2 has graphics and texturing comparable to Half-life. 8 The Yamagi source port of Quake2 adds OpenGL2/3 support. 9
