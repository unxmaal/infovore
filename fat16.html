FAT16

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX.

FAT16 is supported by IRIX 5.3 for removable disk drives and works on IRIX. 12 FAT16 is not natively supported by IRIX for USB mass storage. 3 Windows NT 4 can boot from a FAT16 partition on the HP Vectra 5/90 Series 3. 4 Windows 2000 requires the system partition to be something other than FAT16, possibly FAT32. 5

Sources

  1. IRIX 5.3 supports hfs and FAT16 for removable disk drives. (user-ce6b, 2019-12-17, exchange 92442, check: supported)
  2. FAT16 works on IRIX. (user-d903, 2022-06-06, exchange 222305, check: uncheckable)
  3. IRIX does not natively support FAT16 or FAT32 for USB mass storage, and XFS is incompatible with many modern operating systems. (user-22f9, 2025-01-17, exchange 52809, check: uncheckable)
  4. Windows NT 4 can boot from a FAT16 partition on the HP Vectra 5/90 Series 3. (user-22f9, 2020-04-14, exchange 233825, check: supported)
  5. Windows 2000 requires the system partition to be something other than FAT16, possibly FAT32. (user-59b9, 2022-02-18, exchange 102491, check: supported)
