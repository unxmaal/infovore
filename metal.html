Metal

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

Metal is incompatible with GLSL and uses a custom C++ shader system. 1 Metal can be used to draw the screen, with Metal and Vulkan backends available. 2 A 2011 Mac without Metal support cannot run Metal-based applications. 3 Some specific graphics cards allow an AMD-based iMac to handle Metal with a patched OS. 4 Metal works well for GPU performance in inference engine work, and it ultimately ships for Nvidia hardware but serves as a good development environment. 5 Apple could build its own GPU and use Metal for better performance and thermal management on Macs. 6
