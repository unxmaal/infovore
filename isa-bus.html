ISA bus

Claims: 5 Part of ISA

The ISA bus is used in a variety of systems, including the Z250, which runs on an 8080 CPU and has an unused ISA bus that can be modified to replace a Zip disk with a hard drive, and an Alpha server. 12 The 33G8469 is a P/390 peripheral supported on the ISA bus only. 3 The PIO can interface with the Atari expansion bus, ISA bus DMA, and emulate a TMS9918 chip. 4 ISA bus saturation with full clock speed monitoring on all lanes would be around 4.5 Gbps, but this is unlikely to occur. 5
