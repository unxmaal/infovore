InPerson

Claims: 9

InPerson is SGI factory video conference software that may be available on some app CDs or FTP, though its availability is uncertain. 1 It works with IRIX 6.2 and can connect to netmeeting-compatible software, requiring a license for operation. 234 It allows calling by username and machine name, potentially requiring port forwarding or a VPN for cross-network calls, and requires a point-to-point setup or a gateway for routing calls to enable more complex configurations. 56 It can handle video transmission without additional video boards for resolutions like 180x120 at 10 FPS. 7
