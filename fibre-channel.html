Fibre Channel

Claims: 48 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SCSI.

Fibre Channel has lower latency and is more reliable than iSCSI for storage applications, even though iSCSI over Ethernet can be faster and uses standard networking hardware. 1 Fibre Channel can run at 4Gbps over copper or fiber, with short copper runs being the limit, and its name was changed to fibrechannel to reflect its use of both fiber and copper. 23 NVMe over Fibre Channel delivers 58% higher IOPS and 34% lower latency than SCSI-based Fibre Channel protocol. 4 InfiniBand outperforms Fibre Channel in many scenarios, is more expensive, requires TCP/IP or iSER/SRB configuration, and cannot boot x86 servers, whereas Fibre Channel is plug-and-play with zoning. 56 Fibre Channel usually has removable optics while Ethernet cards have soldered-in optics, and the 2xDB9 port is FC copper at 1GBit, 1st gen hardware for these is hard to find. 7 Fibre Channel is a possibility for the fiber links within the rack. 8 The Blade 2000 cannot handle base IDE, but its Fibre Channel and SCSI are capable. 9 On x86 servers, using Fibre Channel with built-in tools can be difficult, often requiring access to the HBA's BIOS. 10
