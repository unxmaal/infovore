R12000 CPU

Claims: 46 Part of R12000 Background: 8 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, SGI Octane.

With O2

A 400 MHz R12000 O2 configuration has 1 GB of RAM. 12 The O2 with an R12000 CPU runs at 400 MHz but has less performance than a 400 MHz Octane. 3 The O2 runs IRIX 6.5.30 and can be maxed out on RAM. 452 The O2 can take an R12000 CPU, though some require an updated boot prom, and installation may involve removing the PCI riser, using a higher plastic holder, and adjusting the VCORE regulator or updating the PROM or resistor straps. 678 The O2 can overheat, especially with a 400 MHz R12000 CPU and maxed RAM, though some users report no problems. 9

General

The R12000 is a MIPS CPU that ran in various Silicon Graphics workstations and servers, including the Octane, Octane 2, O2, JavaStation, and Origin 200. 10111213141516 The 400 MHz R12000 was the fastest single-version option for the O2 and was commonly used in the Octane. 1315 The Octane 2 could be configured with either a 400 MHz R12000 or a 600 MHz R14000 CPU, and a dual 600 MHz option was available but expensive. 1214 The R12000 CPU was more expensive, costing around €100 to €125. 17 The R12000 CPU requires a specific CPU board revision for the Octane, with faster models potentially compatible with older boards. 10 The Origin 200 with R12000 CPU was used for SETI and achieved top 100 rankings. 16
