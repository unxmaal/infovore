SSC050 chip

Claims: 3

The SSC050 chip is located on the midplane of UltraSPARC machines, where it controls fans and identifies CPU module part numbers over I2C, and it can fail. 1 The SSC050 chip is available in bulk, so spares are still obtainable. 2 The SSC050 chip can be replaced to fix fan control and CPU module identification issues on UltraSPARC machines. 3
