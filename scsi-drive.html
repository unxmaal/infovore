SCSI drive

Claims: 72 Part of SCSI Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see FireWire, Ultra 1.

SCSI drives are generally compatible across systems, with any SCSI drive expected to work in an external enclosure the same as internally, provided termination is handled correctly. 12 SGI machines typically work with any SCSI drive that supports mode select, regardless of jumper settings. 3 A SCSI drive is likely faulty if it fails to work with a known-good drive and jumpers, and a dead drive will not show up in hinv or cannot be written to with software. 45 Various systems can use SCSI drives, including the PowerBook G4, PowerMac G3, FireWire systems, and the O2, though some may require specific handling such as formatting with Lido or using a 4x IBM SCSI drive for CD-ROMs. 6789 Some systems have limitations, such as the A500 possibly not recognizing a SCSI drive properly, the Cube booting from a disk image via the 'Previous' feature, and the G3 enforcing an IDE limit of 128 GiB even when a SCSI drive is installed. 101112 Data recovery may be needed for failing Indy SCSI drives, and a SCSI2SD adapter can replace a failing drive, though installing a new drive and recovering data using a system supporting SGI partitions and EFS/XFS filesystems may be simpler. 1314
