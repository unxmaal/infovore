FujiNet board

Claims: 3 Part of FujiNet

The FujiNet board registers itself as a SIO device and responds to commands on the SIO bus, connecting the GPIO pins of the ESP32 to the SIO bus while handling voltage and resistance considerations. 12 Different FujiNet boards are needed for different platforms due to differences in the bus systems, such as IEC for Apple and ADAMNET for Atari. 3
