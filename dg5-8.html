DG5-8

Claims: 4

IRIX considers the entire Render Engine to be a single graphics pipe, even when a DG5-8 is installed. 1 The DG5-8 can support up to 128 displays using multipipe, though software limitations may apply. 2 The DG5-8 from the Onyx2 has a bad channel 6. 3 DG5-2 boards with dual DVI out were added in later revisions for VBOB or graphics compositor, but no DG5-8 with TMDS exists. 4
