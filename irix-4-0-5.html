IRIX 4.0.5

Claims: 44 Part of IRIX Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Bash (Unix shell), IRIX, SGI Indigo.

IRIX 4.0.5 is a version of the IRIX operating system, with 15 different versions released, 4 of which were variations of 4.0.5H. 1 The PI runs IRIX 4.0.5, and the PI4D has a 'Photo' icon for capturing screen regions. 23 IRIX 4.0.5 lacks the Fortran compiler, and Bash builds in it but should be used with caution. 45 It requires significant disk space, which SCSI2SD v6 can expand. 6

  2. The PI runs IRIX 4.0.5 and has Magnum Audio installed. (user-0251, 2020-10-05, exchange 97643, check: supported)

  6. IRIX 4.0.5 requires significant disk space; SCSI2SD v6 can expand storage capacity. (user-8de9, 2022-05-29, exchange 188403, check: supported)
