g++

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Dbx (debugger).

The compiler options -woff 1184,1552 and -c99 are not recognized by g++, which may indicate a mismatch between the compiler and the Perl installation. 1 mipspro was slightly faster with -mips4 and g++ was slightly faster with -march=r14k, both were slightly slower than without those options. 2 dbx is an inferior debugger compared to sgug gdb, which has a minor bug but is still preferable; the great ancient compiler and g++ have different symbol mangling but gdb can handle both. 3 Building a new OpenBSD/sgi 7.3 base system on an Octane encountered a make error during the libcrypto build, then g++ crashes the machine, possibly related to memory issues. 4 SGI IRIX 6.5.22 or newer supports newer g++ and SDL2 with SGUG-RSE, but may lack 1.2. 5 Efforts to expand IRIX and add new features like USB support and updated versions of g++ may be impractical due to complexity and lack of interest. 6
