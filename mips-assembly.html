MIPS assembly

Claims: 8 Part of MIPS

MIPS assembly is used in N64 development, where it is straightforward with simple interrupts and memory-mapped I/O, and the RDP can be used for various tasks. 123 A MIPS assembly routine can swap the endianness of a 32-bit value using 10 single-cycle instructions. 4 MIPS assembly is easy to memorize due to a few rules and guidelines, and it requires register definitions, but a C stub is not necessary. 56 The book 'See MIPS Run' is a solid guide for MIPS assembly, with first editions covering SGI era CPUs and second editions for later embedded CPUs, but the digital version often lacks chapters and pages. 7 MIPS assembly might not be as practically useful today, but it seems cool to learn. 8
