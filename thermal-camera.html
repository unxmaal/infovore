thermal camera

Claims: 4

A thermal camera can be used to detect heat leaks and track objects, as well as to check for hot spots on a motherboard to identify potential issues, especially on older models like the Indy. 12 The S61 has a thermal camera that is much more expensive than a standalone thermal sensor, offering a lower resolution over a smaller area. 3 Thermal cameras for checking hardware can have issues with parallax and battery life, making them inconvenient for some users. 4
