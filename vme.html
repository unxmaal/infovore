VME

Claims: 28

VME is a bus architecture that is not processor-specific and can be used for hobby computer builds with DIY components. 1 All the boards in VME are hot-swappable. 2 VME is primarily used on Motorola-based systems and some Sparc systems, and also on the Atari TT. 3 The Atari TT supports VME and can be extended with a VME ET4000 graphics card. 4 Sun3 systems used VME, while SBus debuted on the Sparcstation 1. 5 The VME version of the Indigo is a Chrimson. 6 Crimson uses the 14-slot MP backplane from the PowerSeries with three buses: PowerPath, VME, and graphics, each with specific board roles and limitations. 7 The CMC ENP/10 is a VME 6-port serial board with the same ROM as the 4D/70 GT's version. 8
