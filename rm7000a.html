RM7000A

Claims: 5

The fastest RM7000A was limited to 600 MHz by its PROM. 1 The RM7000C cache sits on the SysAD bus and cannot run as fast as the CPU. 2 The SR71010A has different pin assignments for the 'DNC' pins compared to the RM7000A, which are related to tertiary cache, and using the SR71010A in an RM7000 system requires disabling the tag RAM and removing the tertiary cache. 3 Fake RM7000, RM7000A, and RM7000B CPUs can be used, but they may not work properly without system PROM work. 4 The daughtercards can be swapped as they are mostly the same, and the last revision, called the RM series, supports both the RM5271 and RM7000A. 5
