VW540

Claims: 12

The VW540 is a slot 2 system, compared to the slot 1 VW320, and can use MIPS CPUs with a maximum of 8-16GB of RAM. 12 Some VW540 configurations could match a low-spec Onyx2 IR2. 3 The VW540 uses a hard disk sled, though a part number is sought. 4 550 MHz processors and VXI 073-20770-01 voltage regulators work with a VW540, but 700 MHz and 900 MHz processors do not boot with the same regulators. 5 The built-in partitioning tool on a VW540 may not work with a 300 GB SCA disk, and an SGI Linux installer or an old Debian might be needed to create a new partition table. 6 The VW540 did not run newer Windows, making them cheap when available, and the Quad Zeon CPU was the fastest spec but was never released. 78 The VW540 costs over 20k with 1 GB of RAM. 9 The VW540 achieves 13.7 FPS in Quake III Arena at 1280x1024 resolution, which is lower than the 20.9 FPS from the VW320. 10
