GNU binutils

Claims: 6 Part of GNU

For consistent builds, the first ld in PATH should be from GNU binutils. 1 GNU binutils can produce a shared library that rld cannot successfully relocate under certain conditions. 2 Rebuilding GCC with GNU binutils as as and ld is a fix for a bus error during linking. 3 Building GCC 8.5 on Solaris 10 with a GNU binutils-based binutils and /usr/sfw/bin/gcc/g++ results in a fully compatible toolchain. 4 An old version of GNU binutils can help with a broken ancient MIPS toolchain. 5
