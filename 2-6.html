2.6

Claims: 4

The Appletalk driver is for 2.4 and does not work past 2.6 or so, and definitely does not work with Solaris 8. 1 Some people recommend sticking to SunOS 4.1.x when using 2.6 on the IPC. 2 One person has 2.6 running on their SS10. 3 If you do not like running 2.6, you could image the hard disk and restore from the image, assuming you have disk space on another machine to hold the image. 4
