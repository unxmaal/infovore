DMediaPro

Claims: 11

DMediaPro is a video breakout box, more commonly called the VBOB, and is the label applied to the device made by DMedia, a company that made hardware suites for video editing and composition. 1 The dmediapro is an external box that connects to a workstation via a XIO card and provides an external RAID array, and it stands for a series of 3rd party devices primarily used for video-editing, but also sound or Firewire. 23 DMediaPro was used in the Octane2, Tezro, Octane MXE (with SDI), PowerSeries, Personal IRIS, and Indigo workstations. 4567 Discreet configurations typically use dual FC, DMediaPro, and RAD audio, with 4x 700MHz CPUs and 5GB RAM. 8 The dmediapro gives an Octane an SSD-like experience. 9 Shipping an Octane2 with a dmediaPro and a monitor can cost $200. 10
