Jaleo

Claims: 18

Jaleo is a video editing and compositing system that can send frames either through Ethernet or SCSI, and a disk capture box using Jaleo could send completed frames via SCSI or Ethernet. 12 Jaleo can run on SGI machines with OS versions 6.5.13 and possibly earlier, but not on 6.5.30, and it identifies 600 MHz CPUs as 500 MHz ones. 3 Jaleo runs on an Octane, and Jaleo 2.7 is available for MXI/MXE Octanes, while Jaleo 3.0 and 3.5 (3.5 HD, O2, Octane, Onyx) includes VLAN Drivers and a tablet driver. 456 Mistika is a version of Jaleo with a different UI, available for SGI and Linux. 7 Jaleo was used to demonstrate real-time uncompressed video at a trade show in Amsterdam in 1995. 8 The 3D DVE in Jaleo has tracking capabilities, and Jaleo can generate EDLs. 910 Jaleo lacks save functionality and can result in data loss during crashes. 11
