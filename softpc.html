SoftPC

Claims: 5

SoftPC is a program that runs Windows 95 on Sun hardware at 800x600 resolution. 1 SoftPC from 1992 works on an emulated R4000 CPU at approximately 20 MHz. 2 MAME emulates a 20 MHz R4K running SoftPC at 60% on a 4 GHz i5-4670k. 3 SoftPC 3.03 is found on early Hotmix discs and requires Irix 5.3. 4 A version of SoftPC was produced for NEXTSTEP, though it is unclear whether it was SoftWindows or Soft-PC. 5

Sources

  1. The G4 runs Windows 95 on a Sun version of SoftPC at 800x600 resolution. (user-b7c4, 2019-04-17, exchange 1393, check: supported)
  2. SoftPC from 1992 works on an emulated R4000 CPU at ~20 MHz. (user-ce6b, 2019-12-17, exchange 92442, check: supported)
  3. Mame emulates a 20mhz R4K running SoftPC at 60% on a 4ghz i5-4670k. (user-ce6b, 2019-09-17, exchange 220121, check: supported)
  4. SoftPC 3.03 is on early Hotmix discs and requires Irix 5.3; search old SGI newsgroups for more. (user-ce6b, 2024-05-20, exchange 218404, check: supported)
  5. I have whatever Connectix did for NEXTSTEP, but I don't recall if it was SoftWindows or Soft-PC. (user-22f9, 2024-10-27, exchange 218576, check: uncheckable)
