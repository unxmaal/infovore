accelerator

Claims: 9 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Amiga.

SuperSPARC CPUs were accelerators and faster than the equivalent supersparc by a good margin. 1 An Amiga can use a Raspberry Pi accelerator for display through its native video, HDMI, or as a graphics card. 2 Installing an accelerator on a ST required removing brackets from PLCC sockets and shielding. 3 A 16 MHz accelerator for a ST costs 25 GBP, and 32 MHz STE accelerators cost 49 GBP. 45 The accelerator is by design almost identical to the A1200 card edge expansion bus with some extra signals, making it Zorro 2 compatible but not Zorro 3, and an optional adapter for existing A1200 cards is available. 6
