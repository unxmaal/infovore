Turbochannel

Claims: 5

TURBOchannel is an expansion bus used by Sun, some VAXstation 4000s, and some MIPS DECstations, and is DEC's equivalent of SBus. 12 DEC aimed to make it an industry standard but switched to PCI. 1 The DEC 3000 was an Alpha with turbo-channel, while the DECstation 5000 was MIPS. 3 /100s and Personals run Turbochannel at half the speed of /200s, affecting Ethernet and SCSI performance. 4 Turbochannel is a computing interest in terms of money. 5
