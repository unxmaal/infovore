DELNI

Claims: 3

The DELNI is a passive device similar to 10base5 cable that acts as a segment extender without repeating signals, and it is not a hub but counts as a certain distance of 10base5 cabling. 12 The DELNI can serve as a hub for AUI connections, and it came with AUI cables for VAXen as well as a twisted pair MAU for Token Ring. 3
