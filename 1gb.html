1GB

Claims: 12

1 GB is a common capacity for removable Jaz SCSI storage devices, which also come in 2 GB versions and are typically connected via parallel port, with SCSI versions being rarer. 1 1 GB sticks are the standard memory configuration, with 8 GB being the maximum per brick, and 1 GB sticks are made by doubling the chips on a standard stick. 2 1 GB requires 128 MB modules, which did not exist for quite a while. 3 The Sun Panther 501-4559 board supports a maximum of 1 GB of memory, 6 PCI slots, and one UltraSPARC IIi CPU module. 4 The initial 256 MB limit on the O2 in IRIX was due to an AIC7880 DMA transfer issue, now resolved to support 1 GB. 5 1 GB is considered pretty solid for IRIX. 6
