GPU

Claims: 76 Background: 10 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Graphics processing unit, Accelerated Graphics Port, PCI Express.

The GPU is likely an ESI or SI, paired with an R10K CPU and presumably not much RAM, and is supported in vms and tru64. 12 A GPU can be added to a system with sufficient power supply modifications, though it may interfere with management features. 3 You could use a DSP to emulate the GPU command set, and the GPU is supported in vms and tru64. 42 Putting a GPU in a DL360 G8 may override the default BIOS VGA output, potentially losing remote VGA access. 5 The paired GPU is faster than a 1050 Ti in raw compute. 6 A 256MB Octane V10 has a working GPU, while another Octane V10 has a dead GPU but works in an IP17. 78 The Raspberry Pi's GPU is limited in gaming tasks, especially with multipass and alpha blending. 9 OpenSUSE supported everything except WiFi out of the box on a specific GPU. 10 The second working GPU is SSI, and the third is ESI. 11 Some early PCIe GPU cards were AGP to PCIe bridges, and PCIE connectors could be reworked to replace the second GPU with M.2 slots. 1213 The CPU is not socketed and the GPU is integrated, requiring a motherboard upgrade to replace it. 14 The graphics driver cannot use cached memory, as it causes the GPU DMA to overwhelm the SHUB and crash. 15 The Prism deskside has a DVI port for single monitor output, and on the rackmount, the bottom GPU corresponds to pipe zero. 16 The Indy has a GPU, and Octane2 ships with heavy specs but a basic GPU, possibly for medical use. 1718 Offloading 3D and imaging operations to the GPU can improve usability, especially on Octane and Onyx, by accelerating the imaging and rendering pipelines on UMA systems. 19
