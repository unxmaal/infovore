HQ1

Claims: 4

The Geometry Engine 5 (GE5) is an assembly that includes the Weitek chip along with other components such as HQ1, buffers, FIFO, microcode RAM, and GRF1. 1 The GRF1 intercepts signals on the graphics bus and outputs them to the HQ1 for address decoding, with the GRF1 only seeing 4-byte word addresses. 2 The HQ1 requires the host to set the HQ MAR with upper address bits and then access the data at a base address offset for ucode or data RAM. 3 The HQ1 works enough to reach a graphical PROM, suggesting it is not a power issue but possibly a hardware problem. 4
