heat gun

Claims: 3 Part of heat

A heat gun can be used to remove SMD components, though SMD rework stations and hot plates are preferred for this task. 1 When replacing an IC with broken legs, a heat gun can be used with light pressure and tweezers to lift the chip, which is then installed with flux and a soldering iron while avoiding too much pressure or drag. 2 A heat gun, together with a preheater, is available for PSU repair. 3
