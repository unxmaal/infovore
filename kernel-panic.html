kernel panic

Claims: 10 Part of kernel

A kernel panic may display its information for a few seconds before the system reboots, or the details may be shown over a serial connection. 1 Kernel panics have been caused by plugging in a broken I1 keyboard, by Premiere when used for recording composite video, and by a failed hard drive on a fresh install that prevented booting. 234 Upgrading to IRIX 6.5.30 fixed a kernel panic but introduced other issues, including problems with xvm and fam. 5

Sources

  1. Kernel panic info might show for a few seconds before rebooting or over serial. (user-ba81, 2021-04-12, exchange 98748, check: uncheckable)
  2. Plugging in a broken I1 keyboard can make bad noises and cause a kernel panic, but it may reboot with no visible damage. (user-b7c4, 2021-10-29, exchange 221639, check: uncheckable)
  3. Premiere causes a kernel panic when used for recording composite video. (user-24f0, 2024-02-11, exchange 44531, check: uncheckable)
  4. The hard drive failed and caused a kernel panic on a fresh install, preventing boot. (user-ec52, 2025-07-28, exchange 212800, check: uncheckable)
  5. Upgrading to IRIX 6.5.30 fixed a kernel panic but introduced other issues, including problems with xvm and fam. (user-8547, 2023-02-13, exchange 217912, check: supported)
