Darkplaces

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Quake (video game).

Darkplaces runs well on the R12k 400MHz variant of the O2. 1 Darkplaces runs on Octane. 2 Quake on an O2 works fine but Darkplaces has graphics bugs. 3 In ioQuake3 and darkplaces quake on a VPro, texture warping/flashing occurs only when moving, not in Quake2 or other OpenGL applications; resolution and color depth changes do not affect it. 4 SGIQuake in GL mode on a VPro system has issues; DarkPlaces from Nekoware runs better. 5 Only SGI quake works with o2, darkplaces only works with odyssey, and nothing works on octane Mgras. 6
