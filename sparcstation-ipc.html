SPARCStation IPC

Claims: 11

The SPARCStation IPC is a 25 MHz, 32-bit SPARC machine released in 1990. 1 It uses Sun-4c architecture, which is incompatible with NeXTSTEP (that supports Sun-4m); the SPARCclassic is close to compatibility but still incompatible. 2 The SPARCStation 1+ is faster than the SPARCStation IPC due to a faster clock speed and SPARC V8 architecture. 3 Netbooting the SPARCStation IPC can be problematic, especially with incomplete tools like sunboot. 4 For the SPARCStation IPC and IPX, taking out the clock chip from the socket and cutting through the top layer with a hacksaw can replace the battery and oscillator. 5 The SBUS VGA card on the SPARCStation IPC outputs at 320x200 resolution and lacks a color channel. 6 Compiling Perl on a SPARCStation IPC under RedHat 4.2 took 28 hours. 7
