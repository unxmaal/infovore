DNS

Claims: 18

DNS is a hierarchical system where queries start at the root servers and follow a chain of authoritative name servers until an IP address is resolved, with each step potentially introducing delays and configuration risks. 1 DNS query replies have Time To Live fields that indicate how long a record should be considered valid. 2 Changes in DNS records require propagation to secondary name servers and expiration of cached data, which can take up to the TTL duration. 3 The TLD of your DNS doesn't affect your legal jurisdiction, and to have data outside western jurisdiction, you must physically place it outside their jurisdiction. 4
