UPA bus

Claims: 4 Part of UPA

The UPA bus is compatible with SBus-shaped cards but not PCI-shaped cards, using different physical form factors for its cards, while the Indigo2 uses GIO64 slots with a distinct form factor. 1 The UPA bus has a bandwidth comparable to system memory, especially with dual-channel SDRAM. 2 The UPA bus is fast enough that dedicated texture RAM is not a critical issue for Suns, though Impact graphics, especially in the R4400 Indigo2, had texture memory limitations before Quake. 3
