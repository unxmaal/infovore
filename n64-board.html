N64 board

Claims: 5 Part of N64

The N64 board is exclusive to the Indy, and its Indy version allows loading ROM data into a RAM buffer, which can be replicated with an Everdrive 64 and a retail console. 12 A proposal exists to use USB instead of RJ11 for the N64 board setup. 3 The ED64 X7 has a late N64 board with known reliability issues, especially in certain colors, and its cartridge slot can damage the N64 connector with every insertion. 4
