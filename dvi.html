DVI

Claims: 41 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Digital Visual Interface, Video Graphics Array, HDMI.

DVI is a specific interface with various connector types, including DVI-I and DVI-D, and is digital, making it a lot more reliable. 12 DVI and DMS-59 form factors are the same for GPUs, and the DVI interface is compatible with VGA. 34 DVI input is straightforward for high resolutions, just requiring the right resolution to be set, though any kind of cable chaining degrades DVI. 56 Most modern monitors lack VGA input, but DVI works with a DVI-HDMI adapter, while DVI to DisplayPort requires an active adapter. 78 A monitor limited to DVI or VGA ports restricts the resolution to 1920x1080 or 1280x1024 respectively. 9
