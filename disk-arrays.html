disk arrays

Claims: 3 Part of disk

Disk arrays are storage systems that are often implemented using Fibre Channel, typically in rack-sized enclosures for hard drives. 1 XIO Fibre Channel cards were available for the Octane, and SGI had branded disk arrays for it. 2 Tape libraries and disk arrays often used High Voltage Differential (HVD) for long-distance connections. 3
