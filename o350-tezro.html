O350/Tezro

Claims: 5

The O350/Tezro was the first platform to support PCI-X, preceding the Onyx2. 1 The O350/Tezro only supports USB keyboard and mouse. 2 The IP35 allows different binaries to run on its platform compared to the O350/Tezro. 3 In IRIX, the O350/Tezro uses a serial port mapping where console=3, 2=4, 3=5, and 4=6. 4 An O350/Tezro can use 3d printed jigs to mount DL360 G7 rails with minimal drilling, though adapting the rail sides for rack holes is needed. 5
