Apple IIe

Claims: 12 Part of Apple

The Apple IIe is a color-capable computer with monitor output, joystick input, cassette I/O, and a built-in keyboard, built using 31 off-the-shelf chips. 1 It runs ProDOS, an Apple disk operating system, not to be confused with Apple DOS. 2 It can be upgraded with a Ramworks III board for 1MB RAM, and accelerated with faster CPU cards, typically starting when the stock 1 MHz CPU hits its limit. 34 It is one of the slowest computers available, analogous to the PC in the 16-bit world: expensive but with lots of software and not too many capabilities stock. 56
