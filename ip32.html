IP32

Claims: 14

IP32 is a 64-bit hardware family. 1 The ip32 can run IRIX up to version 6.9 but lacks Xenocara support for SGI. 2 Registration determines O2+ by reading the IPxx and offering a selection list, which for IP32 includes O2 or O2+. 3 Compilation times vary: around 40 minutes on dual 300 IP30 machines and 1 hour 12 minutes on a 400 MHz IP32. 4 IP32 has phantom devices with bar0 size set to 0 that don't respond to mmio, while IP30 and IP35 do not have this issue. 5 Bar0 size 0 indicates a dead device, but the driver does not try to access nonexistent registers. 6 IP32's bridge handling is iffy, with 6.5.30 being much better; 6.5.22 and 6.5.7 sources mention external PCI extension box support but did not test it. 7 The built-in NVMe driver works on IP32 with up to 256MB RAM, with one or more bridges, and works on ip30 and ip35. 8910
