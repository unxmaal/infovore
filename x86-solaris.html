x86 Solaris

Claims: 4 Part of x86

LWJGL was never compiled for SPARC, but it supports x86 Solaris. 1 In the early years, Sun supported x86 Solaris on Serverworks chipsets, Mach64 graphics, Intel, AMD, 3Com, and possibly DEC ethernet. 2 Sun's implementation of Win32 for x86 Solaris is not pretty but functional and fits in the chip holder. 3 x86 Solaris was an option for home Unix users before Linux and the BSDs became popular due to its cost compared to RISC hardware. 4
