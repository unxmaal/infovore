microcontroller

Claims: 12 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see BIOS.

A microcontroller can generate square waves that can be filtered to produce audio. 1 A microcontroller did not fully shut down due to a reverse-working Power Indicating LED providing low current, which was hard to diagnose due to changing light conditions. 2 The Prusa i3 is highly regarded for its community support and attention to detail in basic machine perfection, despite using an older microcontroller. 3 You would need an insanely fast microcontroller if you're going to keep up with bus traffic. 4 Using a microcontroller and WiFi could potentially increase the transfer speed for similar projects. 5 The microcontroller is the main limitation for SCSI2SD speed, with no wide version available due to pin limitations, and limits on the speed of bitbanging SCSI from a microcontroller are a factor in the speed of open source SCSI emulators. 67 The firmware update and extended fuse change enable automatic reset and brown-out detection on the microcontroller, and these changes can be applied using avrdude with the appropriate parameters. 8
