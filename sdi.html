SDI

Claims: 17

SDI is a digital video format that SGI systems used for video editing and for integrating into production environments, but it never reached the home market. 12 SDI can carry 4 channels of audio, has a wire rate of 270mbps, and its actual disk rate is lower due to compression. 34 SDI is easy to use and can be converted from HDMI and DV, and monitors supporting SDI and HD-SDI can be purchased for good prices. 56 Several SGI systems offered SDI, including the O2 (via the vbob card, the AV2, or a Miranda on the cam port), the Octane (via a board with a digital SDI version, typically used by 2000), and the Sirius video option for Iris workstations with RealityEngine. 789101112 Some systems used only analog connections, such as the vbob card's analog input (which adapts to S-VIDEO) connected to the O2 cam port, and a system whose inputs and outputs are all analog except for its connection to the AV1's digital camera port. 1314 The DVW-500P Digibeta recorder plays SDI and component analogue but not analog Betacam SP, requiring an A500P in Europe for Betacam SP playback. 15
