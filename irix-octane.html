IRIX Octane

Claims: 4 Part of IRIX

Starting Emacs on an IRIX Octane can take about two minutes. 1 To set up an IRIX Octane for network access, /etc/hosts and /etc/sys_id must be correctly configured with the Octane's IP and hostname, then the network service restarted. 2 The Ansible playbook master_setup.yml configures an IRIX Octane, but users should review and customize it as needed. 3 The GNU ld on an IRIX Octane workstation generates a ROM binary that is 100% garbage when converting sm64.u.elf to sm64.u.bin, with no recognizable content and unexpected bytes, and pads early to 8MB. 4
