Indy R5K

Claims: 6 Part of Indy

The Indy R5k is a MIPS-based workstation that runs as a snappy home webserver with Jekyll. 1 It runs at likely ~1 GHz and would be faster than a N64. 23 An emulated Indy R5k can run MIPS IV code, though it's limited to IRIX 6.5.22. 4 It can freeze when loading a miniroot over the network, causing a complete freeze. 5 The PCBs are for a ps/2 to Personal IRIS 4D/2x keyboard converter that can also work on Indigos with an adapter cable. 6
