DM2

Claims: 19

The DM2 is a video board that interfaces with the VBOB through specific connections, and an external VBOB unit is required for it to function. 12 The DM2 system requirement is a V10 or V12 vPro CPU. 3 The DM2 does 720p60 and 1080p25/1080i60, and uses a significant amount of bandwidth over XIO. 45 The DM2 is compatible with Octane2 for video capture, and the Octane's VBOB and DM2 can capture NTSC video. 67 For SDI capture, a DM2 or DM3 is needed in addition to the VBOB. 8 The DM2 and DM3 cards are used with the VBOB for video input, are hard to find, and are available from sgidepot.co.uk. 910 The DM5 option allows graphics output to video but not the reverse, while with DM2, video into graphics is done through memory. 11
