Geometry Engine

Claims: 11

The Geometry Engine connects to the RV on the back via connectors, some of which are for a Z buffer option. 1 It performs matrix transformations, clipping, and coordinate transformations, and was the initial SGI chip in 68K IRISes and the first 4D Professional IRIS. 2 The IR2E and IR4 models have the same Geometry Engine but differ in texture RAM. 3 The geometry engine for the IRISvision card can be obtained from an IBM dealer. 4 Modern GPUs integrate all functions, returning to the initial design approach of the Geometry Engine. 5
