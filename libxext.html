libXext

Claims: 7

The libXext library from the sgUG source does not contain a specific symbol, _XSGISetSpecialDestroyHandler, which is present in the version from Irix. 12 The missing symbols reside in libXext, which is dragged in by libX11 at link time. 3 The newer libXext in RSE conflicts with IRIX's version due to SGI-specific callbacks, requiring linker flag adjustments. 4 The SGUG-RSE OpenGL situation involves linking issues with libGLcore, undefined symbols from libXext, and architecture-dependent symbols, requiring specific linker flags to resolve. 5 Linking an OpenGL app against libX11 and libXext can cause crashes, but omitting them allows the app to work. 6 The proposed .pc file for avoiding libX11 includes -I/usr/include -L/usr/lib32 -lX11, but remains ambiguous about where to pick libX11 if an RPATH is set. 7
