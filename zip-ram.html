ZIP RAM

Claims: 6 Part of Zip

ZIP RAM is a package type, like a SIP soldered to a ZIP footprint, on a IIcx system. 1 The vast majority of the RAM is Zip RAM. 2 The expansion boards use ZIP RAM, and the total memory is approximately 6MB. 3 The Clovers are VLSI-intense and can fail due to bad ZIP RAM. 4 A2620 comes with 2MB of RAM and can be extended to 4MB with ZIP sockets and ZIP RAM. 5 Testing Zip RAM requires reverse engineering. 6
