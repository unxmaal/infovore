Google

Claims: 12

Google invented bfloat16, a datatype for machine learning that provides the same range as 32-bit float but with reduced precision by using only 8 exponent bits. 1 bfloat16 has become a standard used by Intel, Nvidia, Google, and some ARM and AMD architectures. 2 Google uses VP9, a patent-royalty-free video codec. 3 Google was a significant contributor to the Linux kernel but has dropped off recently. 4 Google contributes to the community to avoid maintaining diverging patches and because the contributors are involved. 5 Oracle Linux has poor license terms and has legal issues with Google. 6
