OPL3

Claims: 5

The AlphaStation 200 has a YMF262 sound chip, also known as the OPL3, and another version of the OPL3 sound chip is a crystal clone. 12 The ESFM, an OPL3 clone, sounds close to the original but can sound muddy in certain games like Doom or Raptor: Call of the Shadows or Wing Commander II, while the CQM sounds better in those games. 3 The OPL3 sound chip is supported by Adlib Tracker II. 4 The Avance Logic ALS-100 based card has CD-ROM interfaces for the ALS100-MCD variant, OPL3 and OPL4 ICs, and SCSI done through a dedicated 53C80 chip. 5
