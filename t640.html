T640

Claims: 3

The T640 GPU enablement kit is an unusual configuration, consisting essentially of just a power cable and possibly a PCIe riser. 1 It requires removing the motherboard and adding external fans that cover the PCIe slots, even though the server has a full row of large fans inside. 2 This may be necessary because the T640 can support up to four 300W Tesla or Titan cards. 32
