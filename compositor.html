Compositor

Claims: 13 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Digital Visual Interface.

The compositor can take four inputs and produce a single output, handling spatial composition of up to four inputs. 12 It can tile render and use multiple streams for anti-aliasing and smoothing. 3 The compositor connects via USB, but multipipe drives it, and it syncs multiple cards. 45 VPros with Compositor can use either analogue or digital signals, and simultaneous analogue and digital with a DVI-I cable, whereas the PROM and Texport on Tezro are analogue only. 6 iris-gui gets a raw buffer from the compositor, unaffected by scaling changes in iris.exe. 7 The Propack contains custom kernels and tools for the compositor, and has better drivers for the ioc3 than the MIPS versions. 8 The compositor might be useful for serious work but its application is unclear. 9
