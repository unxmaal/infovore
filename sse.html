SSE

Claims: 13 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Voodoo2.

The SSE is a dead graphics board that can be used for 3D acceleration in an Octane, alongside multiple Voodoo 2 cards. 12 The SSE is an enhanced version of the TRAM with higher clock speeds, and it is the same board as an MXE except for the number of TRAMs. 34 The SSE requires two TRAM modules, and an SI with an older TRAM module will not work on an SSE. 5 The SSE with a TRAM module can render GL, while the other SSE requires software rendering. 6 Booting an SSE is easier than using Booterizer, and the 13w3-vga adapter works with SI and SSE boards on an Octane. 78 The SSE card may be removed in favor of a single PCI slot card for networking, and to get better results, install vfc and compile with reduced blanking specs for your display; 1920x1200 was achieved on the SSE with a Dell 2407, but compatibility depends on the SGI model and compiler version. 910
