Voodoo2

Claims: 28 Background: 10 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Voodoo2, Quake (video game), SGI Octane.

The Voodoo2 is a PCI card that requires a passthrough setup rather than functioning as a standalone 2D VGA chip. 12 It outperformed the Rage 128 Pro in 3D graphics and provided good 2D and 3D performance. 34 The Voodoo2 is faster than the Voodoo, can operate at higher clock speeds (85-95MHz versus 45-55MHz), and can perform multitexturing in a single pass, a capability even VPro cards cannot match. 56 It is limited to 800×600 resolution and has texture swapping issues at high frame rates, though using a single large texture for multiple smaller ones can be more memory-efficient. 78 A Pentium 2 400 MHz PC with a Voodoo2 could run 3D rendering software like 3Ds Max 4.2, and Quake runs well on a Mac with the card. 910 It works with an Octane, where multiple Voodoo2 and SSE cards can be used for 3D acceleration, though it is not necessary and Bondis have mezzanine slots for it only rarely. 11121314 On IRIX, Voodoo2 cards can be used with some root permissions and Glide tweaks, but acceleration is limited, and the card works in basic dumb framebuffer or generic driver mode. 1516 In dumb FB mode, the Voodoo2 is not capable of 2D rendering except possibly for rendering onto a single giant surface. 17
