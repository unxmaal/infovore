mmap

Claims: 5

mmap may have limitations on IRIX, so malloc should be tried instead of mmap. 1 mmap's benefit of not committing pages until use is not significant for games due to potential performance hits. 2 mmap is used instead of malloc for heap allocation, possibly due to not linking with libc. 3

Sources

  3. memGrowthAllocate fails with an error raised by mmap, which is used instead of malloc for heap allocation, possibly due to not linking with libc. (user-21b8, 2024-08-12, exchange 71492, check: uncheckable)
