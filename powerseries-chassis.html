PowerSeries chassis

Claims: 4 Part of PowerSeries

The PowerSeries chassis was redesigned halfway through its product life due to the release of the IO3, drive module inefficiency, and the arrival of high density RAM. 1 The Professional IRIS has a yellow chromate chassis, while the single tower PowerSeries uses a chrome plated steel PowerOne PSU. 2 PowerOne PSUs are reliable and fit in the PowerSeries chassis, though they may have aging issues, and were cheaper and more reliable than the original PSU. 3 The Clover2 graphics board is compatible with Professional Iris and Power Series chassis. 4
