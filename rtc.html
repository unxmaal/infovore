RTC

Claims: 30 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Raspberry Pi.

Some SPARC systems require the RTC to be running for POST, and booting from IDE devices requires a working RTC. 12 Raspberry Pi systems do not have a built-in RTC chip, so without internet connectivity they cannot function due to the lack of a battery-backed RTC. 34 The 1386's RTC is 'different' plus pinout. 5 An R4k Indy's RTC can fail even if the battery is still functional. 6 Some implementations write to the RTC and read back to check for Y2K compatibility, and Venix or Xenix did this, which could fail and damage CMOS RAM on some clones. 7 The PSU isn't the issue, suspect RTC. 8 The M3002-16P is an Apple RTC used on some old Apple computers. 9 The ST chips used in older Suns have a visible seam where the top and bottom halves are constructed. 10
