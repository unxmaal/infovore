FPU

Claims: 26 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Floating-point unit, Nintendo 64.

An FPU is a separate processing unit that performs floating-point math. 1 An FPU adds a significant amount of complexity, with the Intel 8087 FPU having 45,000 transistors compared to the Intel 8086 CPU's 29,000 transistors. 2 An FPU is not necessary in the first CPU design and would add a lot of complexity. 3 Not much software uses the FPU. 4 The CPU and FPU are socketed into the machine. 5 The ip26 has the FPU on a separate die from the CPU. 6 The PS2's FPU lacks support for doubles and is about half as fast as a Pentium MMX @ 233 MHz for scalar code. 7 Quake is heavy on the FPU, which was buggy in early Pentium chips. 8
