cache card

Claims: 5 Part of cache

The IIci has a 25 MHz CPU, supports 040 upgrades or cache cards, 128 MB RAM without Mode32, and has 3 Nubus slots. 1 The iici can handle UT with 32 MB of RAM, a Supercmac video card, Asante Ethernet card, and a working cache card. 2 Adding a cache card can make a IIci not suck. 3 The PS/Valuepoint has a cache card in it but the BIOS says it's disabled; there is a jumper you will need to try. 4
