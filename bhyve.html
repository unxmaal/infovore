bhyve

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see FreeBSD.

KVM and bhyve are used for VMs, and both are well-established hypervisors with significant development effort over the last decade, making them suitable recommendations for modern use. 12 Xen KVM and bhyve were tried for virtualization. 3 bhyve lets you use the filesystem-in-ZFS trick for the VM's disk for performance and backup by sending snapshots to a backup server. 4 TrueNAS with bhyve can run FreeBSD 13 in VMs, supporting services like Kerberos. 5 FreeBSD has good integration between its ports system, bhyve, and containers. 6
