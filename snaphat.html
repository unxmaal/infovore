Snaphat

Claims: 9

The Snaphat is a part that physically covers a chip, named for its resemblance to a hat, and it attaches to a device with a keyed connector. 12 It serves as the battery backup for the onboard SCSI RAID, which upgrades the onboard SCSI to RAID, and it stores PROM variables and system variables. 34 The Snaphat handles environment monitoring and the PROM environment, while the Dallas chip handles IDs and L1 variables. 54 The Snaphat is associated with the IO9 board, which has the SNAPhat and stores configuration things. 26 The IP35 uses a different Dallas chip than the Snaphat, and a prototype for the Snaphat exists but couldn't be programmed. 7 For longer-lasting batteries, use the larger snaphat model. 8
