CD-ROM drive

Claims: 39 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see CD-ROM, Optical disc drive.

CD-ROM drives can suffer from a variety of mechanical and electrical problems. 1234567 Tray mechanisms may get stuck, which can be fixed by moving a white gear on a shaft, while the O2 drive can have trouble returning to its original position. 12 The manual eject rod can break off and needs to be epoxied back, and the black-plastic insert inside the front bezel can break, causing the button to be held down about half the time. 56 Some issues are resolved by adjusting SCSI settings, such as setting termination back to 'on' on the DIP switches, checking for a terminator jumper, enabling termpwr, or disconnecting the drive to avoid SCSI bus problems. 891011 The SCSI ID is typically hardcoded per slot but can sometimes be changed using jumpers on the drive or the SCA adapter, and jumpers should be checked, particularly on the bottom side. 1213 The drive may have issues with certain media, as high reflectivity CD-RW and CD-R discs work better, while 700 MB discs are not typically a problem for O2 drives. 14 A CD-ROM drive that can read the entire disc, including the margins, is needed for an SGI disc. 15 The CD-ROM drive works fine on the Octane, but the Octane itself lacks a built-in drive and instead relies on network connectivity, NFS mounts, and external SCSI enclosures. 1617
