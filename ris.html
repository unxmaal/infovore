RIS

Claims: 4

RIS is used for netbooting DEC machines, such as Ultrix on a VAX+laptop combo, which requires a direct cable connection and produces MOP packets visible in Wireshark, though the VAX console may not show anything. 12 During netbooting, the machine receives all of the bootloader, is then sent the binary piece generated by RIS that includes the hostname, and sends out a MOP packet with the hostname, sometimes receiving nothing. 3 Netbooting from other DEC machines is quite easy when using RIS, and some may support it via SCSI command. 2 prman added raytracing features and was rewritten to support RIS, shifting focus to global illumination and requiring the use of OSL. 4
