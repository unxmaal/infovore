SCSI2

Claims: 10 Part of SCSI

The V6 board supports SCSI2, offering better performance than V5.x boards, and the Indy can't do more than SCSI2 at 10 Mbit/s, so v6 is perfect for it. 12 SS10 is SCSI-2, fast/narrow. 3 The IC is an active terminator specifically for SCSI-2, which makes sense given the HD50 connector. 4 Scsi2 speeds are bottlenecked with scsi io on the o2. 5 The ZuluSCSI USBLINK allows connecting SCSI devices via USB, supporting SCSI 1 and 2, and enables direct copying to a micro SD card for imaging drives. 6 Disabling SCSI 2 support allows BlueSCSI to pass diagnostics on an Indy. 7 The Indigo2 has a known issue causing timeout problems; disabling scsi2 or enabling phase delay can fix it. 8 The Indigo1 is not SCSI2 compatible; disabling SCSI2 in the configuration prevents issues. 9
