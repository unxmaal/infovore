GPU acceleration

Claims: 5 Part of GPU

GPU acceleration can be problematic on Debian when running on baremetal, but VMware can provide acceleration within a VM. 1 On IRIX, GPU acceleration for terminals may not provide as significant a speedup due to the lack of hardware buffers and optimized 2D routines. 2 GPU acceleration in terminals can provide features like blur, transparency, and blending, and non-bitmapped text can look nicer, which is an argument for GPU acceleration in terminals. 34 For FEA, GPU acceleration is not commonly used, possibly due to VRAM limitations. 5
