Sun Lunchbox

Claims: 9 Part of Sun

The Sun Lunchbox is a narrow, small form factor system. 1 It is suitable for casemodding, including with an SFF HP chassis. 2 A casemod of an SGI system demonstrated that proper airflow is crucial to avoid excessive noise even with a modest power setup. 3
