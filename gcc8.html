gcc8

Claims: 9 Part of GCC

gcc 8 is running on their system. 1 Compiling gcc8 on an O350 is fast. 2 gcc8 in irixports is bootstrapped from onre's gnu toolchain tardist, while gcc8 from didbs differs in behavior and bug fixes compared to onre's toolchain. 34 binutils should be used with the latest gcc8 build, not IRIX `as` or `ld`. 5 The author was able to get libsodium to build and run the testsuite with similar results on both gcc8 and gcc4 on IRIX. 6
