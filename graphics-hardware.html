graphics hardware

Claims: 9 Part of graphics

Graphics hardware accelerates number crunching, but its performance is limited by display hardware capabilities, similar to modern systems. 12 SGI's graphics hardware alternated between CPU/DSP-based microcode and ASIC/FPGA designs depending on silicon performance for a while. 3 SGI reused old graphics hardware in the 2000s, limiting desktop boards to avoid competing with more expensive Onyx hardware, and did not keep its graphics hardware up-to-date, leading to a lack of new features for 4+ years. 45 Linux and BSD can use SGI's graphics hardware, but only for 2D. 6 The graphics hardware is the challenging part of SGI emulation. 7 The CAVE system is expensive, costing half a million dollars in graphics hardware alone. 8
