Cobalt

Claims: 11

Cobalt was a company known for making Linux mini-servers in the late 90s. 1 Cobalt machines can run modern Linux kernels and sidestep security holes of the original OSes, and it is possible to run Slackware 14 and 15 on them. 23 The OS can boot from specific SCSI cards or onboard IDE, but runs on any storage once booted. 4 The display and LEDs cannot be used when booting with the stock ROM, but other hardware works. 5
