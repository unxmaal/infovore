SiFive

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see RISC-V.

SiFive makes small development boards to prove it can bring silicon to market and compete with microcontrollers, but these boards are not intended to make money. 1 SiFive sells RISC-V SoCs for development and support, and it is the only company doing higher-end RISC-V. 23 SiFive's P270 is superscalar and can run the scalar ALU, vector ALU, and load/store unit in parallel. 4 RISC-V boards are available through SiFive and PowerPC boards through Raptor, though they may be sold out or overpriced. 5 RISC-V's popularity has declined, largely due to SiFive's influence, as ARM is often cheaper and easier to use with better support. 6
