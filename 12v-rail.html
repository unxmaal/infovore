-12V rail

Claims: 6

The -12V rail is often used for serial and possibly audio, and can be omitted for basic testing. 1 The IRIS uses a -12V rail for components like the system fan, which operates between 7 and 18 volts and is not driven by the same 12V bus as other system components; insufficient voltage can lead to overheating during CPU. 2 The I2 PSU is 400W and has a -12V rail which can be inverted. 3 Cleaning the ebus with deoxit resolved a fault related to the -12V rail but introduced an issue with the -5V rail. 4 The 12V rail is being removed from new PSUs, which won't work for SGI systems. 5
