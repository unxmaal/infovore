SunOS 4.1.4

Claims: 10 Part of SunOS

SunOS 4.1.4 is the latest version of SunOS, and its version number is also used for Solaris 1.1.2. 12 It works on multiprocessor SuperSPARCs, though this is not officially supported, and it works fine on similar machines. 34 SunOS 4.1.4 is recommended over SunOS 4.1.1 and earlier, which have a problem with disks larger than 1GB, and over SunOS 4.0.3, which lacks newer features. 56 A source code dump is available, and installation documentation can be found at https://kb.pocnet.net/wiki/SunOS_4.0.3c_SPARC. 27
