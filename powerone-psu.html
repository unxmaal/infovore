PowerOne PSU

Claims: 10

The PowerOne PSU can be used as a replacement for the Cherokee PSU in a Crimson, but its -5/-12/12V module can cause system disk parity errors and prevent the Crimson from powering up. 12 The Power One PSU is problematic, with the 170 amp bus bar needing to be split and desoldered with 500 watts of heat, and it has bad small signal caps in the control circuit. 34 A fan in the bias inverter control circuit is crucial for the computer to turn on, and shorting the inhibit input pin can increase the bias supply voltage and enable the output modules. 5 A 1250W PowerOne PSU can provide 65W per slot for 19 slots, suggesting a high power budget for VME cards. 6 The Professional IRIS has a yellow chromate chassis, while the PowerOne PSU of the single tower PowerSeries is chrome plated steel. 7
