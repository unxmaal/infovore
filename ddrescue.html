ddrescue

Claims: 9

ddrescue is a tool used for imaging drives, particularly when a drive is failing. 12 It can save state, start from the beginning or end of the disk, and perform other recovery-friendly operations to recover as much data as possible from failing drives. 3 ddrescue can be used for disk imaging with specific options, and it may be necessary when a drive is failing. 24 It can be built for other systems and run over the network, or a drive can be connected via USB using dd_rescue as an alternative imaging method. 56 ddrescue is suitable for archiving data, though a drive may crash it and require repairs to obtain a complete image. 78
