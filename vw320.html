VW320

Claims: 24

The SGI VW320 is a slot 1 machine, while the VW540 is a slot 2. 1 The VW320 supports Windows NT 4 and 2000, but specific builds that can boot from ARCS are required. 2 The VW320 can boot from few SCSI cards and has an easy-to-replace CPU; 1000/100 Coppermine P3s are cheap and available on eBay. 3 The heatsink on a Slot 1 Pentium II from a VW 320 is not soldered and may have retention clips, and the plastic cover can be removed. 4 The heatsink is compatible with an SECC2 CPU but not with a SECC CPU like the original. 4 slockets for the VW320 are on the tested list and should work with the newest firmware and appropriate VRM. 5 The VW320 can use a 14-drive stripe for storage. 6
