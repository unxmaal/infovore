Origin 300

Claims: 33 Part of Origin Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Origin 3000 and Onyx 3000.

It can run with 16 CPUs, and a configuration with two compute modules can provide eight CPUs. 12 Multiple Origin 300s can be connected using a Numalink router, and the Origin 300 can also connect to an Origin 350. 34 The Origin 300 replaced the Onyx2 and offered better performance, though most configurations used ATI FireGL cards that did not support older IrisGL software. 5 The system is available for purchase on eBay, where listings may include part 030-1909-008, implying VPro graphics. 67 The Origin 300 has a good power-to-computing-power ratio but is noisy, and running out of rack space is a limitation for its setup. 8910
