PCI-X bus

Claims: 3 Part of PCI-X Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The stock XIO board on an Octane operates at 1Gbps, while a QLA2342 card can support 2Gbps, but the Octane's PCI-X bus may run at half speed due to its 66MHz bus. 1 The HP AB287A 10GbE NIC runs at about 6Gb due to the 100MHz PCI-X bus limit. 2 A 66MHz PCI-X bus should support 1 GByte/sec on large MTU, assuming fast enough disk and switches, with potential packet checksum calculations on the MIPS CPU affecting performance. 3
