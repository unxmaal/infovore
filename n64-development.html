N64 development

Claims: 6 Part of N64 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx.

Documentation for N64 development can be found at <https://ultra64.ca/files/documentation/online-manuals/man-v5-1/>. 1 N64 development shifted to PCs around late 1997 to early 1998. 2 The Indy was used for N64 development but can be slow; the Indigo2 is probably better but not tested. 3 The Onyx was used for heavy lifting during early N64 development, including rendering. 4 N64 development requires source code stepping line by line in the debugger, which gdb does not support well. 5 IRIX 5.3 had issues with N64 development, frequently giving errors requiring reinstallation. 6
