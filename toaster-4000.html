Toaster 4000

Claims: 4 Part of Toaster

The Toaster 4000 for the Amiga 4000 used four MIPS R4400 series processors to accelerate 3-D animation rendering to seconds, and the Video Toaster Screamer used four MIPS R4400 CPUs running at 150 MHz with 64 MB of RAM, rendering Lightwave 3D 40 times faster than a Toaster 4000. 12 A Toaster 4000 can be used in an A2000 or A3000, has headers for the Flyer and extended AGA video slot pins, and works fine in a 2000. 3 A Toaster 4000, Flyer, vlab motion, Toccata, AD512, Vampire 68080, and other full boards are available for SGI. 4
