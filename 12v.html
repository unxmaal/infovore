12V

Claims: 8

The Octane PSU provides 12V with positive and negative terminals but no PWM, requiring high static-pressure fans due to its airflow design. 1 Going 12V only is practical for wide-ranged hardware compatibility and adding UPS batteries. 2 -12V was used for RS-232 signaling, -5V for RS-422/423, and +/- for onboard audio; ECL circuitry used -5.2V which SGI avoided. 3 All 12V, 5V, -12V, -5V, and the other one are 0V when 48VDC is 49V. 4 This PSU provides 4 isolated outputs: twice 12V and twice 5V, with terminals 1, 4, and 5 connecting to common ground, making terminal 2 provide -5V and terminal 6 provide -12V. 5 This is a PowerSeries PSU where compute logic runs on 5V, hard disks on +12V, and the fan tray on -12V, which likely runs from -12V to ensure a load, and the IRIS 3K might be similar. 6 MIPS-based Cobalt RaQs use a PSU that provides both 12V and 5V, with the bottom system board converting 12V to 5V; missing load on 12V can cause issues like resets during kernel loading. 7 The SGI OEM speaker set takes 10V 300mA from the Octane I/O board; 12V should be fine. 8
