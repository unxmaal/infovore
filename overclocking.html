overclocking

Claims: 7

The G3 300 Minitower can be overclocked to 458MHz with 768MB RAM and a Radeon 7000. 1 The G3 beige can run with a single CPU and can be overclocked to 501 MHz. 2 Overclocking the MAME emulator reduced the load time from 8 minutes to about 1 minute, allowing real-time mouse operation. 3 The O2 can be overclocked to 200 MHz following a specific guide; it worked for user-17cb, who gained 20 MHz for free. 4 Theories existed for increasing the sysad bus to overclock CPUs, but relevant pages are lost. 5 One could change the clock reference and overclock the sysad bus, core clock, and cache clock, but this is not recommended. 6 The PS2 can be overclocked due to shared base clock between the GS and EE. 7
