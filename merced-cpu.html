Merced CPU

Claims: 4 Part of Merced

The Merced CPU was produced in about 7-50 thousand units. 1 It was competitive with contemporary 64-bit RISC CPUs like SPARCs and was comparable to the UltraSPARC III for its intended 64-bit purpose. 23 The Merced CPU can experience issues where the system only sees part of its cores, which reseating the CPU can resolve. 4
