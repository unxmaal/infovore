NeXTstation

Claims: 41 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXTstation, NeXT.

The NeXTstation is a computer produced by NeXT, which also made the NeXT Computer, NeXTcube, and turbo and color variants of the NeXTstation. 1 The NeXTstation runs a 68040 CPU at 25 MHz and can operate NeXTSTEP, OpenStep, Lighthouse Design apps, and some BSD variants, though support for the latter is limited. 23 The NeXTstation can display 24-bit graphics with a 040 CPU, though it can show only 4 shades of gray in 8-bit grayscale mode. 45 The NeXTstation can use ADB keyboards and mice, and non-turbo models have 72-pin SIMM sockets and can boot from CD-ROM, though not directly. 67 The NeXTstation came with a branded power cable and original drives, and some systems have fully functional original 200MB SCSI drives. 89 The NeXTstation requires a good PRAM battery to turn on, as the battery is used for soft power on. 10
