R10000 CPU

Claims: 18 Part of R10000 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, Computer fan.

The R10000 CPU can become invisible, and it can be fixed by swapping parts or repairing the MC3 board. 1 The R10k extreme has a R10000 CPU, and the clear plastic piece securing the R10000 CPU screws into the motherboard. 23 The O2 can accept an R10000 CPU, but removing the PCI riser card is necessary; some users worry about structural issues. 4 An R8000 CPU at 75MHz is equivalent to a 150MHz R4400K in integer performance and can achieve up to 300 MFlops, while an R10000 at 200MHz outperforms it in floating point and integer tasks. 5 The purple Indigo2 models were the Indigo 2.5, offering better graphics options, improved power supplies, and an R10000 CPU. 6 The Indigo2 uses an R10000 CPU; its Octane module was damaged. 7
