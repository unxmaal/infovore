MDD

Claims: 42

The MDD is a super expandable, hardware-compatible machine whose motherboard bolts to the inside of the lid and contains the power, drives, and cooling. 1 It is significantly lighter than other SGI machines and is least likely to cause back problems. 2 The MDD uses DDR memory, unlike the G4's PC133, and none of these machines need ECC. 3 The MDD is a modest refresh of the Quicksilver, with a faster system bus and more RAM, though an upgraded Quicksilver could compete. 45 The MDD has dual 1.42 GHz PowerPC 7455 G4s and different graphics cards depending on the operating system, and the V12 graphics are the same as in earlier systems. 67 The MDD has a better memory architecture than the SawTooth, so its 1.25 GHz chip will likely outperform the 1.4 GHz SawTooth chip, which is 12.5% more powerful from a CPU standpoint. 8 QS and MDD CPU cards are not compatible between boards, and the CPUs are not physically compatible due to issues with the Uninorth chipset. 910 MDD and Xserve G4 CPU cards are interchangeable, but dual 1.42 GHz CPUs are rare in MDDs. 1112
