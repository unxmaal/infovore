Apple

Claims: 96 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Apple Inc., LLVM, MacOS, NeXT.

Apple collaborated with IBM on systems to run macOS or AIX, including CHRP/PREP, and licensed NT source from Microsoft along with some confidential documentation to integrate it. 12 Apple's transition to ARM processors is partly driven by iOS app support for easier platform change, with plans to unify their operating systems and development environments. 34 Apple uses clang, a frontend for LLVM, as its compiler, with gcc symlinked to clang by default on macOS. 56 Apple is discontinuing x86 compatibility, which was a key selling point for virtualization on Macs, and will not support Intel-compatible graphics cards, indicating a shift towards proprietary solutions. 78 Apple stopped using NVIDIA GPUs due to issues with GPU soldering and a desire for more control over the driver stack, and ended its relationship with AMD GPUs. 910 Apple could build its own GPU and use Metal for better performance and thermal management on Macs. 11 Apple bought NeXT and developed Mac OS X based on NeXT's system. 12 APFS is the new Apple file system container format. 13 Apple leveraged BSD to bootstrap OS X and other devices, and has open-sourced some BSD software for OS X. 1415 Apple considered using Solaris on PowerPC and had valuable IP, including cancelled research projects and a relationship with IBM via AIX. 1617 Apple went back from bash because of GPLv3. 18 Apple's current design language of flat aluminum is similar to beige PC cases. 19 Apple briefly switched to IBM chips towards the end of the MDDs life to fix the heat problem, and Apple and IBM's PowerPC systems used heatsinks for cooling, not fans. 2021 Apple aimed for simplicity and minimal effort in consumer computers, which appealed to non-techy users. 22 Apple's transition from NeXT hardware to software was a strategic move, despite the NeXT hardware being questionable. 23 Apple's support policy for old Macs has been discontinued, making it difficult for owners of old Macs. 24 Apple and other companies founded ARM as a joint venture to further develop the Acorn RISC Machine processor for use in the Newton project. 25 Apple is the primary manufacturer of modern UNIX workstations. 26 Apple's hardware is not sold at a loss, unlike some other companies. 27 Apple no longer sells user-installable CPU upgrades, and third-party creation is not allowed. 28 iBook G3s had GPU issues that Apple fixed with a plastic wedge, and were replaced under warranty due to GPU desoldering. 2930 Apple's first DVD drive, the SuperDrive, was the Pioneer DVR-A03 / DVR-103. 31
