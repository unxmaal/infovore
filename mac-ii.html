Mac II

Claims: 15 Part of Mac

The Mac II series includes models II, IIx, IIfx, IIcx, and IIci. 1 The Mac II can run A/UX with an MMU, which other Macs cannot. 2 The Mac II was the original soft power Macintosh, needing a dedicated battery for video circuitry power-on, while later models used the PRAM battery for this purpose. 3 The Mac II requires PRAM batteries, one keeping PRAM and RTC alive and the other powering the soft power circuitry. 4 The new Mac II has issues with the video card and floppy disks, and its motherboard requires recapping. 56
