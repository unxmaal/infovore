rendering

Claims: 16 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx.

Rendering does not typically use a graphics system, but rather number crunching, and libraries handle most of the math required for rendering. 12 Rendering has been performed on a variety of machines, including the Cray X-MP, Sun systems, SGI systems, Onyx, Rack O2K systems, and workstations such as the Dual 400 MHz Octane. 345678 Rendering can be distributed across multiple machines, as with Flame version 8 running Burn on other SGIs or Linux workstations, and Vizserver using a remote SGI's hardware. 910 Rendering on a workstation is not ideal, and skipping rendering of 10,000 polygons allows avoiding their transformation, lighting, scanlining, rasterization, and per-pixel depth testing. 1112
