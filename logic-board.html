logic board

Claims: 7

Logic boards have many layers and can develop breaks in areas with continuity across a battery and connector area, which can be prone to damage. 1 A damaged logic board can be repaired by transplanting parts onto a new PCB, and recapping is generally unnecessary on the Plus unless there is visible damage, whereas SMD caps often fail on the classic and SE/30. 23 Desoldering logic boards requires care to avoid damaging pads, and hot air can damage capacitors but is fine for ICs. 4 The technical manual contains tests for diagnosing logic board issues but lacks full board schematics. 5
