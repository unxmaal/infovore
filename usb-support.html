USB support

Claims: 7 Part of USB

USB support varies significantly across systems. 123 IRIX has poor USB support, often forgetting the keyboard on boot, and 6.5.17 is needed for USB support. 14 Octanes did not have USB support. 5 USB support on older PCs was spotty, often requiring firmware updates. 2 USB support in classic MacOS is hit or miss, being better on 9.1 or 9.2. 3 The Beige G3 requires a special driver, which fits on a floppy disc, to provide USB support. 6
