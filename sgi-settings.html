SGI settings

Claims: 5 Part of SGI

The horizontal Toolchest setting changes the orientation and click-to-focus/focus-follows-mouse behavior in SGI settings; these can be changed independently after login. 1 Basically any of the SGI settings will work, in my experience. 2 The system retains settings and MAC address when powered off for a week. 3 A dead snaphat indicates that settings are incorrect on cold boot. 4 Switching the SGI settings to PAL causes the menu to freeze, but unplugging and replugging the video cable results in a single frame appearing before the screen goes black. 5
