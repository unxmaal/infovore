Premiere

Claims: 17

Premiere was ported to IRIX by Adobe, alongside Photoshop and Illustrator, and was available in a box with multiple versions, including version 4.2, from resellers. 123 Adobe planned to ship Premiere on SGI's O2 platform, where it was primarily bundled and ran with Latitude compatibility libraries that added video features not available on Mac. 425 Premiere on O2 was clunky but functional, though it had limited format support, may have slow or unaccelerated video playback, and saw limited speed improvements on Intel CPUs. 6789 Premiere works well with digital video but may slow after extended recording sessions, and it causes a kernel panic when used for recording composite video. 1011
