Solaris 11

Claims: 17 Part of Solaris Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARC.

Solaris 11 can be downloaded without an expensive support contract, and an intermediate version between Solaris 10 and 11 has been developed in FOSS-land as an interesting project. 12 Solaris 11 uses SMF for init scripts, IPS for both software packages and kernel patches, and the pkg format changed between Solaris 10 and Solaris 11 while Solaris 11 still supports the old package type. 3456 Solaris 11 can be set up as a netboot server with the public IPS repository, can run a desktop environment like XFCE with 3D acceleration limited to proprietary drivers, and some software builds on OpenSolaris or Solaris 11 but not on versions 7 through 10 due to missing headers. 789 Solaris 10 ZFS pools are compatible with OpenZFS but not Solaris 11 pools, and illumos has a better community support story for hobbyists compared to Solaris 11. 1011
