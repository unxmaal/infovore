VPro card

Claims: 8 Part of VPro

The VPro card can be used as a second head on an Octane, but it should be installed in the top slot to avoid heat issues, and multiple VPro cards can cause overheating and are not recommended. 1 The VPro card fits mechanically in an Origin2000 but requires swapping or designing a cam locking bar. 2 The VPro card maxes out at 900 MHz and performs similarly to a PS2 at 30FPS in fullscreen mode. 34
