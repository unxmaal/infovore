Wings3D

Claims: 7

Wings3D is a popular free 3D modeling program available on IRIX that can import models from other 3D programs. 1 It is a box modeller that was ported to IRIX but is now on a road to discontinuation, serving as a modern alternative in an era when early 3D tools were scarce. 231 Setting up Wings3D requires 128MB of RAM and more swap space, and it does not require GL or a 3D card from Disney. 45 Compiling Wings3D for ppc requires XCode and Leopard, using prerequisites not available in Tiger. 67
