Toshiba

Claims: 10

Toshiba made CPUs used by SGI, including the R4400MC (which SGI mainly sourced from Toshiba and NEC) and the final MIPS chips, and it shipped a SysV port of Unix with its portable. 123 Toshiba made DVD drives, including a newer model with the same layout as the O2 that allows replacing the stock drive, and these drives used the same faceplate connector through at least the DVD era so tray ends are interchangeable. 45 Some Toshiba SCSI DVD drives may not open due to cheap plastic cogs, and one such drive is mostly worthless except for a missing PCMCIA Ethernet adapter. 67 Toshiba's CT/MRI image viewer software is called alatoview. 8 Some Toshiba 4MB SIMMs can be used in an IP6 without triggering the PROM bug, which is not confirmed to affect all IP10 PROM versions. 9 A partnership with Sony and Toshiba could have been beneficial for SGI. 10
