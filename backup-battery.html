backup battery

Claims: 3 Part of backup

The ThinkPad 380XD, or THICCpad, can run with a backup battery to bypass 163 and 165 errors but may still hang in POST. 1 A backup battery going into protection mode due to a direct short to ground likely prevented a more serious incident, as an O2 PSU failure was caused by a capacitor issue. 2 The rs6000 uses a backup battery, either a 4.5v alkaline or a 6v lithium pack, which stores the MicroChannel config and the IPL config. 3 A dead battery requires the machine to detect and configure every part of the system. 3

Sources
