CF to IDE

Claims: 5

CF to IDE should work, but you must have a card that runs in fixed disk mode, and issues can be resolved by trying a different CF card. 12 CF to IDE supports PIO mode 4 and various DMA modes, while SD to IDE converters are usually PIO-only and some support DMA modes. 3 The speed of CF to IDE and SD to IDE depends on the card itself, and CF to IDE is less troublesome than SD to IDE in experience. 45
