Weitek chip

Claims: 3 Part of Weitek

The Weitek chip is a full FPU likely used for geometry calculations and is part of the Geometry Engine 5 (GE5), an assembly that also includes components such as HQ1, buffers, a FIFO, microcode RAM, and GRF1. 12 The Weitek chip is connected to the microcode RAM via a 34-bit code port and a 32-bit X-port linked to the GE5 data bus, which also connects to the lower word/upper byte of the microcode RAM. 3

Sources

  1. The GR1.2 board has three RAMDACs for R+G+B outputs, and the Weitek chip is a full FPU likely used for geometry calculations. (user-b1da, 2022-07-26, exchange 31438, check: supported)
  2. The Geometry Engine 5 (GE5) is an assembly including the Weitek chip and other components like HQ1, buffers, FIFO, microcode RAM, and GRF1. (user-b1da, 2023-05-23, exchange 223134, check: supported)
  3. The Weitek chip is connected to the microcode RAM via a 34-bit code port and a 32-bit X-port connected to the GE5 data bus, which also connects to the lower word/upper byte of the microcode RAM. (user-b1da, 2023-08-12, exchange 108284, check: supported)
