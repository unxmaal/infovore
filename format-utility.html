format utility

Claims: 4 Part of format Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Hard disk drive.

Under Solaris 8, the format utility can create partitions larger than 5.7GB on a 40GB disk. 1 The format utility's command-line mode can create a Sun disklabel, which is necessary for partitioning a blank disk. 2 The format utility may fail to recognize disks, possibly due to a controller board issue, or a bad disk can cause the utility to fail and stop recognizing the disk. 34
