Iris Indigo

Claims: 22 Part of IRIS Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo.

The IRIS Indigo and the Crimson share identical entry-level graphics, and the Indigo with Elan Graphics is considered superior to the standard IRIS Indigo. 12 The IRIS Indigo has texture RAM, uses the same GIO32 expansion bus as other Indigos, and can use SGI midplanes for the fastest CPU option. 345 It can run IRIX 4.0.5 with 96 MB of RAM, and installing the IRIX 5.3 developer toolbox requires the IRIX Development Option. 67 The IRIS Indigo was problematic, and its field service manuals exist but were not written by SGI. 89 The TL-2450 battery fits the IRIS Indigo and can replace the original TL-5186, and it can also boot with a 3V CR battery. 1011 It is often recommended as a better option than the expensive and incompatible NeXT Cube for a similar price and performance. 12
