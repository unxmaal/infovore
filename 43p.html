43P

Claims: 11

The 43P is a mid-range server comparable to the Sun Ultra-10 and IBM 43P. 1 The 43p naming is inconsistent and confusing across different machines in the RS/6000 line. 2 The 43P has a tiny, GPU-sized heatsink, 4mm hex-head combo flat blade screws for the device bays, and a mix of PCI and ISA slots on the backplane. 345 The 43P has actual firmware you can watch on the console after the self-test runs, making it more functional than systems that rely solely on BIST codes. 6 The 43p can use an RS6000 CPU with modern firmware, and a gig Ethernet card works with AIX 5.1 in a 43p. 78 Gig Ethernet cards may not work in AIX 4.3 or 5.1, may have issues in a 32-bit slot requiring a new card, and the 4.3.3 version of the 43P runs a 10/100 card but gives an 'out of memory' error due to a PCI memory address issue. 9710
