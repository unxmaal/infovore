switch

Claims: 17

Nearly every problem with booterizer is caused by Windows, single-porting the network, or running the network through a switch. 1 The same LAN can use different VLANs on the same switch with different ports. 2 The switch does not NAT and can forward tagged packets based on L3 information. 3 The BlueSCSI v2 hardware has a switch that, when not wired to reset, could be used to switch CD images during IRIX installation. 4 A switch can power on/off the NextCube/NextStation, and a relay could be used for this purpose. 5 Battery holders with a switch are preferred for managing battery depletion. 6
