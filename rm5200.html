RM5200

Claims: 28 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, R5000.

The RM5200 is a die shrink of the RM7000 with higher clockspeed and more cache, and it runs software from its era reasonably well, though it is slower for rendering video due to CRM limitations. 12 It is a proxy for the R5000 core in the PS2's emotion engine, lacking some features of the R10K, and hinv reports R5000 for RM5200 chips. 23 The RM5200 is relatively low-end and superscalar compared to the R12000, scoring 8 in SPECint95 versus 14.5 for the R12000. 456 The 300 MHz RM5200 was released in July 1999 and is a modest upgrade that is much quieter and cooler than the R10/R12K. 789 The RM5200 can be upgraded to a 600 MHz chip, but it is slower than the R12K by a lot due to lacking architectural gains. 101112 The replacement board for the 300 MHz RM5200 requires a new bitstream chip and the removal of the original RM5200 board. 13
