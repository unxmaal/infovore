GPIB

Claims: 9

GPIB is an industrial control bus used for test equipment, originally called HPIB and later known as GPIB or IEEE-488, with slow speeds of about 1 MByte/s. 1 GPIB is limited to 1 MB/s (or 8 MB/s for HS488), making it unsuitable for MRI image data but plausible for talking to scientific or metrological equipment. 2 HP used GPIB on minicomputers for storage, such as the HP 9000/300, which uses GPIB for storage and has no built-in storage or SCSI. 34 GPIB could interface with tape decks or control devices like the GC3 board in a VME slot. 5 The HP 712 requires GPIB for emulating floppies and hard drives. 6 GPIB is available as PCI and USB cards. 7
