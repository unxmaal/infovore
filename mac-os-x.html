Mac OS X

Claims: 16 Part of Mac Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXT.

Mac OS X was developed by Apple based on NeXT's system after Apple bought NeXT, and some of its packages carried properties over from NeXTSTEP, which may have influenced permission handling. 12 Mac OS X has no technological relation to or shared codebase with pre-10.0 classic Mac OS. 3 Mac OS X became a Unix OS in the 2000s that was heavily graphics-oriented and reminiscent of IRIX in spirit, and it has always used Unix line endings. 45 Mac OS X has no inherent limit on drive size, but the ATA controller has a limitation. 6 Mac OS X had fewer exclusives and less optimized performance compared to P4/Athlon XP-era PCs, and Mac OS 9 had more games than Mac OS X. 78
