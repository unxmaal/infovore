vcxsrv

Claims: 4 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

vcxsrv is a Windows X11 server that is considered better than Xming, especially for some clients that may exit early due to issues. 1 vcxsrv claims to have OpenGL support. 2 You can use VcXsrv for X11 forwarding over SSH with OpenVMS. 3 XDMCP from vcxsrv works without additional configuration. 4
