ZuluSCSI Blaster

Claims: 5 Part of ZuluSCSI

The ZuluSCSI Blaster is a board tagged 'ZuluScsi RP2350B'. 1 It can achieve 20 MB/s, faster than earlier models at around 10 MB/s. 2 Diskperf tests show it has better read and write performance than the regular bluescsi on an IRIX IRIS 6.5 machine. 3 For users who have had issues with 10 MB/s mode, a timing bug was identified and a hot fix firmware update is available. 4 The ZuluSCSI Blaster does not honor drive geometry from the .ini file, causing issues with the fx program in both SGI IRIX 6.2 and 6.5 versions. 5
