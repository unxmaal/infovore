IRIX 10

Claims: 3 Part of IRIX

On IRIX 10 and 11, x86/amd64 is a first-class citizen, less so before. 1 On IRIX 10 32-bit, you can run Win16, while on 10 64-bit you need a 32-bit VM for Windows, and the Windows on Windows layer uses NTVDM for some tasks. 2 Running IRIX 10 with CDE is preferred over Gnome for compute power efficiency. 3

Sources
