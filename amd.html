AMD

Claims: 37 Background: 9 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see AMD, Nvidia.

AMD has historically competed with Intel, fighting hard to keep up with the company, which saw Intel CPUs subject to speed limitations in software like Premiere due to optimizations, while AMD's approach differed. 12 AMD has mastered 7nm fabrication and is working on a hyper-integrated SOC inspired by the M1. 34 AMD's 64-bit standard prevailed while Intel's Itanium failed and SGI declined, though AMD once considered building an AMD-built SPARC CPU based on Opteron technology, a Sun declined. 56 AMD chips run on aarch64 and AMD options are available in Thinkpads, and an AMD machine can be usable. 789 AMD discontinued the Polaris architecture and Vega GPU, the latter a failure due to high temperatures and lack of support, and AMD GPUs have issues like fan curve problems. 101112 AMD has used the strategy of adding a second GPU to cards to increase performance claims, and its shader cores moved away from VLIW due to complexity issues. 1314 AMD drivers have gotten much worse, though Forceware drivers have always installed cleaner and been less hassle compared to AMD, even back in the ATI days. 1516 NVIDIA has never truly supported open sourcing their drivers, releasing binary blobs and rarely releasing specs on older stuff, unlike AMD, whose proprietary drivers are not as open to development of open source alternatives. 1718
