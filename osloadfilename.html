OSLoadFilename

Claims: 3

OSLoadFilename specifies the boot image for an r4k-based IP22 system from a SCSI CD-ROM device. 1 Autoboot failed due to an empty OSLoadFilename environment variable, which was fixed by a wiki entry. 2 An IRIX installation on an Octane with a preexisting installation on the included HDD will not boot from the HDD due to a blank OSLoadFilename in the PROM environment variables. 3
