AltiVec

Claims: 6

AltiVec is a vector processing unit that was integrated into PowerPC and Power processors, appearing in the Power6 and later models as well as the 970, which are PPC64 architectures. 1 For many big-endian ppc64 distributions, AltiVec is a bigger requirement than big-endian itself, since it is available on the 970 and Power6+. 2 AltiVec was used for video acceleration in Ffmpeg and TenFourFox. 3 The PowerPC G5 does not support AltiVec instructions, a fact that some people mistakenly associate with modern Power8 processors. 4 The Amiga X5k series, particularly the T20xx, supports AltiVec, unlike the X5k. 5 AltiVec is not available on current embedded PowerPC processors. 6
