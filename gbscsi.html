GBSCSI

Claims: 12

GBSCSI is a cost-reduced version of ZuluSCSI that shares the same firmware and features, being functionally identical to it. 123 It provides good speed for SCSI-2 and saturates SCSI-1. 4 GBSCSI is compatible with the Raspberry Pi Pico 2, which uses the RP2040 chip. 5 It works well in Indys and Sparcbooks, on R4k Indigo, and as well as ZuluSCSI Compact. 678 It supports multiple drives, but incorrect termination causes an echo on the SCSI chain. 9 It requires a reverse SCA to 50pin adapter to work in Octane and higher systems, and costs $150 without it. 10 The ribbon on the drive sled for the I2 is too short to reach the GBSCSI mounting holes, requiring the GBSCSI to be placed inside the anti-static bag for use. 11
