IREEPROM

Claims: 5

The IR4 graphics pipeline works despite a corrupted IREEPROM, but the machine fails to boot due to the corruption. 1 The failure occurs when the IREEPROM is written to, specifically when the resolution is changed. 2 To check the IREEPROM, run 'ireeprom -c' over serial. 3 The startgfx command can bring the Graphics Pipeline online even if the IREEPROM is corrupt, but a working IREEPROM is needed for this. 4 If the IREEPROM becomes corrupted, it cannot be re-flashed using the flash utility in IRIX. 5
