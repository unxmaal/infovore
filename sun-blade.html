Sun Blade

Claims: 17 Part of Sun

The Sun Blade chassis contained 48 drive bays, with each blade having its own network connection and the center housing a switch for the whole chassis. 12 The load balancer in the Sun Blade center used a MIPS processor. 3 The Sun Blade shipped with a LITEON CD drive, though any IDE drive could be used, and no SATA/SAS cards that work are bootable in the blade. 45 The Sun Blade ran software to control an MRI machine and had patient scans stored on it, and it also ran X-ray machine software with patient scans. 678 Depending on the graphics card installed, a version of Quake could be run on the Sun Blade. 9
