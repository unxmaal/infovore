IRIX 5.3 XFS

Claims: 7 Part of IRIX 5.3

IRIX 5.3 XFS can handle up to 1TB, though it maxes out at 33.6 GB on some drives, and it can be installed on disks larger than 8-9 GB. 12 Using the filesystem requires the IDO (irix development option) and specific patch sets to work properly. 34 It can be installed on an R4K CPU with no more than 1MB cache after applying patch sets. 5 IRIX 5.3 XFS is not compatible with the Linux implementation of XFS. 6 It had severe bugs and was not as mature as other features, so it is not recommended for use. 7
