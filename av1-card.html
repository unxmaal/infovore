AV1 card

Claims: 4 Part of AV1

The AV1 card communicates with the MACE, which handles PCI, but the AV card does not use this interface directly. 1 The AV1 card on an O2 requires a Miranda dongle for capture, but they are hard to find. 2 The AV1 card has plastic tabs that are prone to breaking, and the I/O holes are too small for some RCA plugs. 3 If an AV1 card boots and plays audio in an O2, the video inputs are likely to work, though partial functionality has been reported; diagnostics may be reliable but not guaranteed. 4
