IO9

Claims: 38 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Altix.

The IO9 is an SGI IO card that provides SCSI connectivity and is required to boot several SGI systems. 1234 An IO9 has two SCSI ports and external SCSI, delivering ports to the system through an edge connector that connects to the ports' riser. 235 The IO9 can be configured with a CPU module and SCSI backplane to make a bootable O350, and an O350 with an IO9 is typically a CBrick or base node. 164 The IO9 differs from the IO8, which is the Origin 300's IO card and has a different edge connector. 7 The IO9 supports various operating systems, including Debian 6 on the Altix 350, Linux kernel 4.9.231 (with issues), t2 and 4.19.325 on the Altix 3000, and SCSI & IDE controllers on the IP35. 891011
