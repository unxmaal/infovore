R12000

Claims: 138 Background: 16 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, R10000, SGI Octane.

With O2

The O2 can use an R12000 or R5000 CPU, with the R12000 being the preferred choice as it can be upgraded to the R12K, whereas the R5000 cannot. 123 The O2 takes an R12000 CPU, with the 400 MHz model being the fastest available and the maximum specification, though the 270 MHz and 300 MHz versions were also official R12K offerings. 45678 The O2 can run with up to 1 GB of RAM, and the 400 MHz R12000 version can complete benchmarks in 10 minutes. 91011 The O2 with a 400 MHz R12000 is not a high-performance machine, especially considering its cost, though it can hold its own against an Octane for non-textured tasks. 1213 The O2 with an R12000 CPU runs at around 96-98 degrees Fahrenheit and draws 100W, making it efficient as a server. 141516 PCI riser cards are needed for R10k and R12k CPUs in an O2 chassis, and the R12000 can be replaced with a quieter fan with similar specifications. 1718

With R10000

The R12000 improves upon the R10000 microarchitecture by inserting an extra pipeline stage to improve clock frequency, increasing the number of entries in the branch history table, and modifying instruction queues. 19 The R12k architecture had improvements over the R10k, though the R12k had only minor changes from the R10k and the R14 and R16 were just die shrinks. 2021 The R10k had clock speeds from 180 to 360 MHz, and the R12k was 270 MHz. 22 Upgrading from R10000 to R12000 CPUs involves replacing the CPU module. 23 R10k modules can be upgraded to R12k 300MHz by adjusting resistors. 24 An R12000 CPU provides a significant performance boost, likely doubling the speed of an R10000 CPU from 225 MHz. 25 Upgrading an Octane from a 250 MHz R10k to a 400 MHz R12k can significantly improve performance. 26 IRIX install may carry over on an R10k to R12k upgrade. 27

With Octane

The Octane is available with a dual 300 MHz R12000 CPU, 256 MB RAM, and a 146 GB 10k RPM hard drive with V6 graphics, and it needs more RAM. 28 The Octane 2 has a 360 MHz R12000 CPU, 2 GB of RAM, V10 graphics, and an 18 GB hard drive. 29 The R12000 CPU was introduced with the Octane2 and Fuel systems. 30 The Octane 2 with a V8 costs around $800 to $1000. 31 V10 is still very good if you don't need the additional RAM, and Ian Mapleson recommends for a hobbyist Octane to max it out at dual 400 MHz R12Ks and a V10. 32 The Octane can run Natron and has an R12000 CPU at 400 MHz. 33

General

The R12000 and R14000 are CPU models used in SGI machines. 34 The R12k processor significantly improves performance over the R5k, with minor pipeline improvements, improved branch prediction, and 4-way execute, though there is no software difference. 35363738 The R12K has more cache than the R7K, with 32k/32k primary and 2MB secondary compared to the R7K's 16k/16k primary and 1MB secondary. 39 R12k to R14k to R16k have better clockspeed and more cache memory. 40 The R12000 can use only the bottom three RAM banks, and the R12K CPU should not use the top two RAM slots as it gets overheated. 4142 IRIX 6.4 does not support R12000 CPUs; booting from specific patches may allow installation. 43 The R12K can run video playback and will run Quake2 at around 15-20 fps on an r12k @ 270mhz. 4445 The R12k can run a SCSI external drive. 46 The R12000 CPU started shipping in February 1999 at up to 300 MHz, with subsequent generations like the R14000 and R16000 offering boosted clock speeds but few microarchitectural improvements. 4748 The R12000 is much more superscalar than the RM5200, scoring 14.5 in SPECint95 compared to 8 for the RM5200. 4950 The R12000 uses a socket design with spring contacts similar to LGA, and the cast heatsink design came with the R12000 processors. 5152 Some systems have a 300 MHz or 400 MHz R12000 CPU with 1 GB of RAM and a cast heatsink. 53
