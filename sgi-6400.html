SGI 6400

Claims: 6 Part of SGI

The SGI 6400 uses the Alchemy architecture, which was also used in several machines including the Powerbase. 1 It has a 64-bit data bus CPU and an integrated graphics card. 2 The SGI 6400 can be a 200 MHz model and is not in the Quadra 8x0 case. 34 The 6400 has a theoretical RAM throughput of 2560 Mbps, compared to 1200 Mbps for the 6218CD. 5
