Blade 1000

Claims: 17

The Sun Blade 1000 is a fast UltraSPARC machine for its time, with configurations including 2x 1.2 GHz UltraSPARC IIIi processors. 1 It uses 232-pin DIMMs in sets of four, and ZFS works better on a Blade 1000 with maximum RAM. 23 OPENSTEP for Solaris can run on a Blade 1000, and OpenBSD can run on it. 45 The Sun Blade 1000 is now in parts, and some Sun Blade 1000 servers are still in use at the University of Minnesota. 67 Sun Blade 1000 servers are quiet and powerful, but they use non-ATX power supplies incompatible with standard ATX form factors. 78 Some Blade 1000 units have issues such as bad RAM, a potentially fixable dead motherboard, missing faceplates, lack of video output, and SCSI termination problems. 9101112
