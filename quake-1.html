Quake 1

Claims: 14 Part of Quake

Quake 1 runs on several RISC workstations and servers, including the Indy (with an r5k), SPARC systems, the G4 Cube, the O2, and the Triple-seat Octane. 123456 On the O2, Quake 1 runs with software rendering, including an R10k optimization, and can run smoothly at 1024x768 with a quad processor, though software rendering is less efficient. 457 The Triple-seat Octane can run Doom, Quake 1, and Quake 2 multiplayer with the software renderer, but lacks the TRAMs for optimal performance. 6 Quake 1 also runs in software mode on a ROSS hypersparc and runs adequately in speed via Impact. 89 Quake 1 can be ported to AlphaNT using Visual C++ 6, but the Win32 port depends on i386 assembly, which impacts performance, and the Alpha has a basic 2D card, making 3D performance poor. 10 Quake 1 uses the simpler QuakeC engine, compared to the DLL-based Quake 2. 11 Quake 1's renderer was optimized for the Pentium's instruction pipeline and FPU, leading to poor performance on other systems. 12 Three instances of Quake 1 will not use 8 GB of RAM. 13
