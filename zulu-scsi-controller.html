Zulu SCSI controller

Claims: 5 Part of Zulu

The Zulu SCSI controller is about 20% slower for reads and 70% slower for writes compared to a 10k SCSI drive, but firmware updates and different microSD cards can improve performance. 1 It might require geometry parameters to be set, and setting DMA masks for disks but not for the Zulu SCSI controller may cause issues. 23 Win2K RC2 Alpha can be installed on a Zulu SCSI controller. 4
