NVMe driver

Claims: 11 Part of NVMe

The NVMe driver was developed on IRIX 6.5.22 on O2 and Octane and on 6.5.30 on Fuel. 1 The O2 can run an IRIX image with the NVME driver built, and the built-in NVMe driver works on IP32 with up to 256MB RAM. 23 The NVMe driver works on IP32 with one or more bridges, and booting from NVMe on IP32 is planned. 4 The NVMe driver requires cache invalidation after DMA completes to ensure data consistency, even though it may race with concurrent DMA. 5 The NVMe driver improves small write performance but maxes out earlier than the spinny disk. 6 The NVMe driver is available on GitHub and has a booting guide. 7
