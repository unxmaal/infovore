SunOS

Claims: 32 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris.

SunOS came with a K&R C compiler and an older version of Workshop for SunOS 4. 1 SunOS and early Solaris versions had applications such as MATLAB, FrameMaker, SoftWindows, and MAE. 2 Some software existed on SunOS and Solaris, as well as AIX. 3 WordPerfect 6.0 included versions for SPARCstation (SunOS, Solaris), HP-UX, IBM RS/6000 AIX, SCO, UnixWare, AT&T GIS 3000, SGI, and Siemens Nixdorf (SNI). 4 Blender Publisher 2.25 supports both IRIX and SunOS. 5 Cadence 1994 all apps for SunOS were distributed on 3 CDs, with the installer named SoftLoad. 6 Swapping over NFS was supported by SunOS, Solaris, AIX, IRIX, Ultrix, and HP-UX. 7
