A64FX

Claims: 5

The A64FX is a 48-core ARM V8.2 CPU with 512-bit vector extensions and 32GB HBM2 onboard, used in the world's fastest supercomputer. 1 The three fastest supercomputers currently run on A64FX, POWER9, and POWER9 respectively. 2 Fujitsu chose to create a new A64FX processor in a different architecture instead of improving their existing SPARC64 designs. 3 Fujitsu pivoted to the A64FX due to Solaris winding down and Linux having poor SPARC support, and a shortage of ARM engineers. 4 A64FX would be better for the enterprise due to upgradability and large RAM counts. 5
