Atari

Claims: 23

Atari had to do extensive shielding in their computers to meet strict FCC rules, which they later revised, saving other companies like Apple money. 1 The Atari went through three redesigns, including a light beige case similar to the 128. 2 The Atari system and OS are simple with a ROM-based backend and DOS compatibility. 3 Atari made a PDP-11 arcade PCB with the T-11 CPU. 4 The Atari has software like Test Drive and Elite. 5 Atari did not use Woz's design for Breakout because they could not replicate it. 6 The Atari has its 50th anniversary this year. 7 Atari's 68k boxes, Jaguar, 7800, and Lynx, and some repackaged 8 bits were developed under Tramiel's management. 8
