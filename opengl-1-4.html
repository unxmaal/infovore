OpenGL 1.4

Claims: 5 Part of OpenGL

OpenGL 1.4 introduced ARB extensions for programmable shaders, which became core in OpenGL 2.0. 1 Blender 2.76 and earlier require OpenGL 1.4, while Blender 2.80 and later require OpenGL 3.3. 2 IRIX supports at most OpenGL 1.4, and most IRIX machines do not support OpenGL 1.4. 23 The cards in the Onyx4 support OpenGL 1.4, but IRIX itself may not recognize it. 4 The R300-series Radeons are AGP only, limiting them to OpenGL 1.4, but they are still better than no acceleration and less expensive than FireGL X3s. 5
