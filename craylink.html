Craylink

Claims: 6

Craylink is SGI's marketing name for Numalink2, while Numalink1 is a protocol for interconnecting SGI NUMAlink systems that was previously known as CrayLink. 12 The O200 uses Craylink for CPU-to-CPU communication and XIO for peripherals. 3 An O200 can be Craylinked to another O200, but the XIO hardware is disabled after initialization and the O200 requires a specific board for the gigachannel XIO option. 4 Origin 200 pairs can be connected using Craylink cables, which are expected not to boot without a boot brick. 5 The firmware on Origin 200 models likely restricts communication with Origin 2000 via Craylink due to licensing or hardware incompatibility. 6
