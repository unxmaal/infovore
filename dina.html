DINA

Claims: 3

DINA can be used for IRIX installation and NFS-server setup. 1 Nekoware auto-updates on DINA until 2014 or 2015, then stops, and it is not maintained much since then. 2 Netbooting IRIX with DINA requires at least VMware Player, setting up the distribution images, and configuring the network interface with BOOTP, and the package manager was replaced, making it harder to manage software. 3
