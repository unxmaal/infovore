R4K Indigo

Claims: 20 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX.

The R3K Indigo and R4K Indigo were technically different machines, with the R3K Indigo being a 32-bit system and the R4K Indigo being a full 64-bit system. 1 The R4K Indigo (CMNB003B) has the Elan badge, while the Elan door is for the R3K Indigo (CMNB003). 2 The R4K Indigo can run IRIX 6.5.22, and IRIX 5.3 is a confirmed version for development tools that runs well on an R4k Indigo. 34 The R4K Indigo, Indigo2, and Indy all share the same 72-pin FPM RAM. 5 The R4K Indigo or Indigo2 is preferred for serious work. 6
