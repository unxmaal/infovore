MIDI

Claims: 18 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Field-programmable gate array.

The ST had integral MIDI and a floppy drive that could talk to PCs. 1 MIDI on Macs traditionally used serial ports. 2 The DB15 connector on the frontplane of some SGI machines is a common port used for joysticks, MIDI on PCs, and video on older Macs. 3 MIDI support is not installed by default in IRIX, but MIDI can be connected via extra hardware and is used via serial. 456 There is MIDI on IRIX and a control panel exists to configure it. 7 RS-232 serial to MIDI adapters allow connecting MIDI devices, and Octane uses RS-232 DE-9 ports. 8 Linking /dev/input/MIDI3 to /dev/tty4d3 enables RS-422 mode on the serial port. 9 The dmedia_eoe data music folder contains MIDI files. 10
