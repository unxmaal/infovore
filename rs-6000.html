RS/6000

Claims: 69 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see A/UX, Doom (1993 video game), IBM AIX, PowerPC.

The RS/6000 is a family of IBM workstations and servers that uses POWER-family CPUs, including POWER1, RS64, and PowerPC architectures. 12 The RS/6000 can run AIX, Solaris PPC, experimental Linux kernels, Debian, and PReP OSes, though running some operating systems requires specific models or building components from source. 3456 The RS/6000 has various models, some of which take a long time to boot, particularly after a battery replacement or in service mode, and serial communication can be challenging. 78 The first-generation RS/6000s shipped with 16MB of RAM and ran at approximately 25MHz. 9 The RS/6000 can draw more power but is cheaper than a system with six CPUs. 10 The RS/6000 was used extensively. 11 The RS/6000 was based on the design language developed for the PS/2 line, which predates it. 12 IBM provided buttons and dials for the RS/6000. 13 The RS/6000 has a troubleshooting manual with BIST/POST codes. 14 The RS/6000 uses a backup battery, either a 4.5V alkaline or a 6V lithium pack, which stores the MicroChannel config and the IPL config; a dead battery requires the machine to detect and configure every part of the system. 15 The RS/6000 requires a lot more +5V current than most ATX PSUs provide, and the shape and connectors of the PSU may be challenging to find. 16
