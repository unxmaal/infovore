Cobalt RAQ

Claims: 4 Part of Cobalt

  • The Cobalt RaQs use NFS for booting, not TFTP. (user-6e7b, exchange 240282, 2024-03-27; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • I have a confession to make. I have got DooM running on a cobalt RaQ under SLACKWARE 14.2. (user-7ded, exchange 122254, 2026-03-27; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • MIPS-based Cobalt RaQs use a PSU that provides both 12 V and 5 V, with the bottom system board converting 12 V to 5 V; missing load on 12 V can cause issues like resets during kernel loading. (user-6e7b, exchange 240218, 2023-12-29; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Cobalt RAQ machines use MIPS processors in early models. (user-0251, exchange 101386, 2021-12-28; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
