4 CPUs

Claims: 7

The Intel SC450NX is a 100 MHz Pentium II/III Xeon motherboard supporting 4 CPUs and using EDO or FPM RAM. 1 The U80 had 4 CPUs, while the Octane competed against it. 2 The SGI Onyx 2 deskside can have a maximum of 4 CPUs in a 2-node configuration, but some models are limited to 2 CPUs. 3 The system's terminator display shows a random pattern and indicates 4 CPUs during initial POST, then initializes the front panel. 4 The system has 4 CPUs and 5120 MB of enabled memory. 5 The Altix machine has 4 CPUs, with only 3 ever used, two pegged at 100% and one fluctuating between 80% and 100%. 6 The Octane can panic when trying to install 4 CPUs. 7
