Debian 10 LTS

Claims: 3 Part of Debian

The booterizer project stopped working when Debian 8 repositories were moved to archive.debian.org, breaking the VM image. 1 Updating the VM image to Debian 10 LTS allowed it to work, but it failed to mount the shared folder due to a virtualbox guest FS filesystem issue. 2 The required packages still work under Debian 10 LTS, but it will go out of support in June 2024, and should be moved to Debian 11 or 12 for continued support. 3
