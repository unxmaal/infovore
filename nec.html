NEC

Claims: 10

NEC manufactured the PC-98, a distinct architecture not fully compatible with IBM PCs, for the Japanese market starting in 1997. NEC also created the R4300i variant of the MIPS R4200, which MIPS had licensed to NEC. NEC was one of the main suppliers of R4400MC CPUs for SGI, alongside Toshiba. NEC also rebadged SGI systems. 12345 NEC, along with Dell, is mentioned in relation to displays. Some NEC LCDs have been reliable, and many second-hand NEC LCDs support SOG without requiring prom settings changes. However, NEC displays can have inconsistent backlighting and produce a lot of heat. A NEC or Dell screen can be made to work without DDC signaling by cutting small pins from the 13W3. 678910
