xterm

Claims: 26 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Xterm.

XTerm is a terminal emulator, part of XFree86, that originated as a terminal emulator for VAX and is known for its orange and blue XT logo. 1 It is a common terminal emulator that was used on Unix workstations, including on a Mac, and was used in early *nix installations on m68k systems. 23 xterm reads the $SHELL variable to determine the default shell, and changing the xterm shortcut to /usr/bin/local/xterm -e /usr/local/bin/bash sets the default shell to bash. 45 The command setenv TERM=xterm sets the terminal type to xterm. 6 The .xinitrc for VUE systems was simply 'exec xterm' by default. 7 xterm is not exactly slow even on things like a SPARCstation 2 or a VAXStation 4000/VLC. 8 Xterm version 223 from 2007 fixes bugs with Nano in IRIX. 9 xterm does not respect /etc/passwd for shell settings on IRIX. 10
