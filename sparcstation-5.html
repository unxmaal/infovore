SPARCstation 5

Claims: 19

The SPARCstation 5 is a SPARC-line computer that runs on a SPARC processor and is considered one of the top models in the line, alongside the Ultra 5 and SPARCstation 4. 12 It uses a TurboSPARC processor and its onboard controller (the STP2000QFP) supports Fast Narrow SCSI, 100Mbit/sec Fast Ethernet, and a parallel port. 34 It can run Solaris 2.6 or 7, with 2.6 recommended for lower-spec systems, and it can also run NeXTSTEP. 56 Solaris 2.6 for the SPARCstation 5 requires a native C/C++ compiler toolchain, and its installation works from CDROM but not from hard disk unless NVRAM aliases are corrected. 78 The machine is now rare, may be discarded from the workplace, and can be traded. 91011
