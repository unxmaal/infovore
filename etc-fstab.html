/etc/fstab

Claims: 6

In the installer, you can drop to a shell and edit the IRIX equivalent of /etc/fstab to configure mountpoints. 1 Adding NFS entries to /etc/fstab allows mounting without GUI tools, and failing to do so can prevent booting. 2 The mnt_freq and mnt_passno fields in /etc/fstab control the dump frequency and fsck pass number, not whether the filesystem must be present at boot. 3 Solaris uses /etc/fstab instead of /etc/vfstab. 4 To unmount disks, edit /etc/fstab. 5 To mount an NFS share from IRIX to Linux, add the share to /etc/fstab with appropriate options and click the unmounted drive icon in the file manager to mount it on demand. 6
