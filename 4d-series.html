4D series

Claims: 8 Part of 4D

The 4D series is the professional/user-24f0 Iris, based on MIPS. 1 SGI's 68k and 4D series machines used optical mice, with the very first 68k machines using rollerball mice which are rare. 2 The 4D series manuals are color-matched to the IRIS Professional, featuring dark gray with a purple stripe. 3 The old 4D series can be powered via a powered peripheral port, which is a serial port with power in a DIN connector. 4 Ultra 320 disks can cause problems in a 4D series because they don't negotiate back far enough. 5 Netbooting an old 4D series with FDDI initially loads the miniroot with 10Mb/s Ethernet, but the miniroot supports FDDI for 100Mb/s installs. 6 In Q3 '87, SGI had $28M from 4D series sales, with a professional IRIS list price of ~$75K, likely mostly IP4 systems. 7
