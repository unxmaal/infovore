ELF

Claims: 18 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Sun Microsystems.

ELF is an executable format that was superseded by COFF and then ELF, and was introduced by SVR4, a joint project of AT&T Unix System Laboratories and Sun Microsystems, which was inherited by IRIX 5 and later. 12 ELF is a format that can be embedded in various ways, and bootloaders like U-Boot can load kernels in an ELF format. 3 o32 binaries are ELF and can run on MIPS3 systems from IRIX 5.x and later, while mips1 binaries can run on R3k systems from IRIX 5.x. 4 IRIX 5.3 supports the older COFF binaries, while 6.5 is ELF only. 5 A system running IRIX can give you compatibility with both COFF and ELF executable formats. 6 The IRIX implementation of rld is part of the ELF implementation, necessary for proper shared libraries. 7 Alias switched to ELF in version 5.1. 8
