disk drive

Claims: 10 Part of disk Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Computer fan.

A T3-1 server with two FC20 flash PCIe cards and one disk drive can boot at 300W and idle at 233W. 1 The disk sled is easy because the disk drive comes with the connector already exposed for the backplane. 2 The mouse damage did not affect the power supply and disk drive, which are in mint condition. 3 The error 'Unable to execute dksc(0,1,8)/sash: media not loaded' occurs when a disk drive is accessed with a PROM that might be too old, even though it was recently installed. 4 The iBook G3 disk drive uses a plastic latch mechanism that can wear down over time. 5 The 386 complex was a special configuration that used only one memory riser and had a 1.44 MB disk drive. 6 The LC did not include a disk drive or floppy disks. 7
