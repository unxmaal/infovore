RAD

Claims: 3

The RAD was intended for octane-era machines, while the dm8 was intended for tezro/fuel/o350 machines. 1 RAD + dual serial was the original configuration that came with the tezro, and a replacement quad USB 2.0 was also available. 2 All of these configurations work in other slots. 2 ADAT optical devices are limited to the RAD audio on IRIX. 3
