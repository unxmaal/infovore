ARM Macs

Claims: 4 Part of ARM

ARM Macs perform better at certain tasks, such as machine learning, but are not worse at anything for software compiled natively. 1 ARM Macs are not supported for external GPUs. 2 The OpenVMS license can be obtained by registration and may be available for x86 systems via QEMU on ARM Macs. 3 A program built on an old Intel Mac for maximum compatibility has been reported to run perfectly on ARM Macs. 4
