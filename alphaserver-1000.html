AlphaServer 1000

Claims: 5

The AlphaServer 1000 comes with a Compaq QVision graphics card. 1 It uses 8 sticks of 16MB 72-pin SIMM RAM that are FPM and may work in an Alphaserver 1000 but not in a DEC 3000/300. 2 When using the onboard SCSI with a daisy-chained CD-ROM and hard drive cage, the AlphaServer 1000 requires a terminator on the external SCSI connector. 3 The AlphaServer 1200 uses the same sleds and storage cans as the AlphaServer 1000. 4 The AlphaServer 1200/1000/DS20 and BA350/DS-BA356/BA370 take removable disk canisters designed by DEC, which were also used in the RZ28 and green canisters around 1995. 5
