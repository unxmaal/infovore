3D acceleration

Claims: 6 Part of 3D

XSun does not expose 3D components on PGX32 and XVR-100 as 3D acceleration, treating them as 2D accelerated framebuffers. 1 The machine has basic 2D acceleration suitable for general desktop use, but 3D acceleration is not functional. 2 Some GPU drivers work but have poor 3D acceleration. 3 The Newport driver lacks some 3D acceleration features, requiring pixel-by-pixel feeding for textures and Z buffers, and it has a 16-entry FIFO limitation; it needs kernel module support for DMA and interrupt handling. 4 For general desktop usage, 3D acceleration is not necessary, so hardware emulation does not provide a speed advantage in the Irix emulator. 5 3D acceleration over PCI would significantly increase the usefulness of the Altix bricks. 6
