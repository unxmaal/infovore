SAS drives

Claims: 3 Part of SAS

SAS drives have deeper command queues and better warranties, though they are more expensive than SATA drives. 1 SATA drives have NCQ, but SAS drives offer more features beyond cable length and drive count. 2 SAS drives support dual porting for redundancy and higher voltage, allowing longer cables and better reliability, bundle multiple channels into a single cable, and can have higher theoretical throughput. 3
