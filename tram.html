TRAM

Claims: 61

With Octane

The Octane has a TRAM of 10.8 MB and offers only one TRAM option. 12 An Octane without TRAM cannot run many Octane-era demos and has limited capabilities for games and tech demos. 34 An Octane with TRAM or VPro is recommended for optimal performance. 5 The TRAM addition improves demo display on the V12. 6 Octane CPUs can overheat, especially the TRAM, and half the battle with Octanes is getting TRAM to use. 78

General

TRAM (Texture RAM) is used in various SGI graphics cards, though not all include it: the original IRIS Indigo had no graphics options with TRAM, the ESI graphics card does not include the TRAM module, and the ESI without TRAM lacks support for textured environments. 9101112 The Indigo 2 Impact can hold up to 4 MB of TRAM, with some models having 1 MB that can be upgraded to 4 MB, though Octane systems only ever have 0 MB or 4 MB TRAM. 91314 The SI card has TRAM but only 4 MB, and one TRAM module is sufficient for SE graphics, though TRAM for SI/SSI is slower. 1516 SE, SSE, and MXE are enhanced versions of TRAM with higher clock speeds, and MXE likely has faster TRAM than MXI, though the exact speed is not specified. 1718 Cards without texture RAM are decent for non-textured objects but require TRAM for textured objects, and the SE graphics card cannot run Octane demos due to TRAM requirements, being limited to R4k era software. 1920 The XVR-1000 is a UPA card with 256 MB of TRAM, 72 MB of framebuffer, and 32 MB of system RAM for the MAJC CPU, while the V6 has 32 MB of TRAM split between 20 MB framebuffer and the rest for textures. 2122 The O2 has TRAM built in but uses system RAM as graphics RAM with no dedicated framebuffer, and the Indy can use system RAM as TRAM, though it is done by the CPU rather than the raster units. 232425 IMPACT machines have limited TRAM and may require larger textures in system memory, though this was not commonly implemented, and IMPACT machines with 1 MB of TRAM only support up to 16-bit textures. 2627 TRAM is RDRAM memory consisting of 8 megabit chips for each megabyte of logical texture memory, using 8 megabits of DRAM per megabyte of memory. 2829 The TRAM boards have a tendency to fail with detached chips because of board bending and thermal cycling. 30 TRAM is write-only for the CPU and can cause crashes and freezes, including X server crashes sending back to the login screen, as well as visual corruption. 3132 The software requires TRAM support and has been tested on Fuel V10 and Octane2 V12. 33 Machines with TRAM normally have white screws, and the ImpactVideo card is a daughter card that fits on the stack and can be used for video as texture and real-time color space conversion. 3435 High Impact works well, although 1 MB of TRAM on it is kind of rough, and the Indigo2 EXTREME has no TRAM, which limits its use for image editing. 3637
