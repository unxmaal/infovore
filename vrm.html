VRM

Claims: 14

VRMs regulate voltage for CPUs, such as a buck switching regulator that steps down from +5V to the voltage a CPU requires. 1 VRMs are especially crucial components, as seen in the G3 logic board, which requires a VRM alongside a ROM DIMM, personality card, and PSU. 2 The Octane's CPU runs directly from PSU rails, with a VRM providing Vcore at higher voltage. 3 VRMs must be compatible with system components, as VW series CPU upgrades require a matched pair, the most recent BIOS, and a compatible VRM. 4 Sockets for the VW320 are on the tested list and should work with the newest firmware and appropriate VRM. 5 The VRM for the IBM RT Personal Computer was written in PL/8. 6
