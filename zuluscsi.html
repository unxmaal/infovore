ZuluSCSI

Claims: 164

With BlueSCSI

ZuluSCSI is a SCSI emulator that is faster than BlueSCSI, easier to use than SCSI2SD, and uses filename-based setup. 1 ZuluSCSI is closed source, though its hardware is open. 2 BlueSCSI and RASCSI are hobbyist projects, while ZuluSCSI and SD2SCSI are more business-oriented projects. 3 BlueSCSI is an open source community where anyone can contribute, and it is cheaper than ZuluSCSI. 4 ZuluSCSI and BlueSCSI are basically the same. 5 SanDisk SD cards have been reported as problematic with both BlueSCSI and ZuluSCSI. 6 BlueSCSI and ZuluSCSI emulators can achieve up to 20MB/s, saturating any 10MB SCSI bus. 78 ZuluSCSI and BlueSCSI are useful for old Macs, Amigas, SGI, and Suns. 9

With Indigo2

The ZuluSCSI kit is useful for drive imaging on the Indigo2, and it works fine in the Indigo2 without requiring a config file. 101112 However, the ZuluSCSI may require special configuration or be difficult to get working on an Indigo2. 13 Some users report SCSI interface parity errors when using the default settings, and backing off to SCSI1 and enabling parity does not resolve the issue. 14 The ZuluSCSI on an Indigo2 is slow due to compatibility settings and may not benefit from BlueSCSI V2 without hardware changes. 15 ZuluSCSI and SCSI2SD are acceptable for an Indigo2, with faster access times even if spinning rust may have more throughput for larger transfers. 16 The PROM on an Indigo2 may need replacement if it fails during the UTLB miss in the PROM installer and causes no video output, and the ZuluSCSI issue might be unrelated. 17

General

ZuluSCSI is a SCSI adapter that supports disk images and direct SD access, and can emulate multiple devices including HDD and CDROM. 18 It runs at Fast Narrow SCSI-2 and targets SCSI-1 and SCSI-2 rather than SCSI-3 replacement. 1920 ZuluSCSI exists because parts for SCSI2SD have dried up and it is hard to find an SD2SCSI v6. 21 It is cheap to add a floppy power connector when ordering one, and costs about $50 USD. 2223 It is a good choice and a decent alternative for speed compared to SD2SCSI v6. 2425 Benchmarks show ZuluSCSI gets 4-5MB/sec, versus about 1.5MB/sec for RaSCSI and close to 10MB/sec for SD2SCSI v6. 26 It works well in Indy systems, including the IO3b, and can replace a boot disk on a Challenge S. 27282930 A ZuluSCSI connected to the external port of an Octane does not require additional power, and it can operate on the SCSI bus power without a dedicated power supply. 3132 ZuluSCSI is a newer version of SCSI2SD available from Rabbit Hole Computing, and GBSCSI is a cost-reduced version of ZuluSCSI that shares the same firmware and features. 3334353637 ZuluSCSI is SCSI-2, which limits its performance when replacing a drive in an Octane but is fine for an Indy. 38 ZuluSCSI works well for SCSI but not 44-pin IDE, and it performs as well as or better than 50pin SCSI disks on older machines that only have 50pin SCSI. 3940 ZuluSCSI supports exFAT out of the box and allows IRIX to recognize an exFAT formatted SCSI disk as a regular SCSI disk, requiring partitioning with fx and creating an XFS partition using mkfs_xfs or mkfs. 4142 ZuluSCSI can operate in initiator mode, which allows it to copy the whole drive to an SD card and be used for installing IRIX via LOVE and Reanimator without a CD swap button. 4344 The RP2040 and RP2040 Pico models support SCSI initiator mode for reading SCSI drives via ZuluSCSI, which can copy data to an SD card. 45 ZuluSCSI can simulate multiple drives, and it can have a CDROM device on SCSI ID 1 with up to three disk images, but another device on the same ID will not work; the internal hard disk is typically on ID 0. 4647 The ZuluSCSI firmware update might fix issues with SD card compatibility and functionality, and ZuluSCSI with new firmware is faster. 4849 ZuluSCSI is a SCSI emulator that can emulate multiple SCSI devices, including HDD, CD, tape, and removable media, in a single box. 5051 It can expose individual disk images as separate USB Mass Storage endpoints, which requires setting EnableUSBMassStorage=1 in the ZuluSCSI.ini file. 5253 All versions of ZuluSCSI support wide SCSI, and it can work with a wide range of hardware, including MCA RS6000 systems, specific AIX versions, and IRIX 4.0.5 and 5.3. 545556 ZuluSCSI can be used to clone an IRIX installation from a HDD to an SD card, connect a 68-pin cable to a SCSI bus, and back up a disk with an ATX power supply and a short 50pin SCSI cable. 575859 ZuluSCSI does not support SCSI ethernet, does not work well with older SGI systems, and may reset itself after a write attempt. 606162 It can operate in initiator mode, which was historically used to copy other SCSI devices to a file on an SD card. 63 A ZuluSCSI setup can emulate a Daynaport for an Amiga, bringing Wi-Fi capabilities. 64 Blaster and ZuluSCSI initiators are compatible. 65 The newest ZuluSCSI firmware can display status over USB and can sequentially image multiple drives. 66 In initiator mode, ZuluSCSI uses a fading LED pattern to indicate status, which can be confusing and differs from the blinking pattern mentioned in the documents. 67 Base EFS images can be made with ZuluSCSI for easier jumpstarts, and it runs well on older hardware with EFS and ZuluSCSI, though XFS may not be supported. 6869 The Indy requires SCSI2 disabled to recognize ZuluSCSI drives.
