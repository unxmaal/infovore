GM1

Claims: 4 Part of GM

GM1 is part of the clover set, which was managed internally by a 68k. 1 The 68K PROM in a GM1 contains microcode for the PP and a limited text port, and the GE subsystem uses command tokens that index into this microcode. 2 The GM1 outputs specific diagnostic messages at startup, indicating successful tests of various components and initialization of the GM PROM with a known revision date. 3 The GM2 has a monitor similar to the GM1, and its firmware is stored in a format that may be truncated. 4
