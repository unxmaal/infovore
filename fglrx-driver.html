fglrx driver

Claims: 5 Part of fglrx

The fglrx driver is part of ProPack, while the standard radeon driver is included in SLES 10. 1 The fglrx driver in ProPack is tailored for SGI-supported setups and requires SGI-specific extensions. 2 Disabling SGI-specific extensions might allow the fglrx driver to work. 3
