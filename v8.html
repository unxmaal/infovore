V8

Claims: 40 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane, V10 engine.

With V12

The V12 is from around 2001 and is a die shrink of the V8. 1 The V12 and V8 are considered good systems, and V8/V12 machines are close in power, with V8 being ahead of the curve. 23 The V12 CPU in the Tezro is twice as fast as the V8 CPU, but the Tezro has a faster bus and RAM, making it faster overall. 4 The texture memory capacity of V8 and V12 is 104MB, and the total memory is 128MB. 5 There is no significant difference in performance between V8 and V12 for games like Quake and Quake3 due to the 2x geometry performance of V12 not being the bottleneck. 6 V8 and V12 graphics cards are rare in Octane machines, with V8 prices around $175, and finding a V8 or V12 is challenging. 78

General

The V8 is faster than MXI and MXE, and has 4x the RAM. 910 It has better VRAM/TRAM but still suffers from the clock bug. 11 The Expert3D was between V8 and V10 in performance. 12 The V8 can use Xbow 1.3, run 16:9 displays, and use DCD, though the Octane with a V8 and DCD was not officially sold that way. 131415 The ABGR vs RGBA texture format choice makes no difference on the Octane2/V8/IRIX 6.5.2x. 16 The V8 is less in demand than the V800, but still a good card; it is not available for the Fuel/Tezro. 17 An Octane 2 with a V8 costs around $800 to $1000. 18 The V8 causes IRIX crashes when used as the primary display, not booting fully. 19
