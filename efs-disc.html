EFS disc

Claims: 3 Part of EFS

  • Multisession efs discs are disk images and should work with dd, though practical results may vary. (user-c9fb, exchange 82896, 2023-01-24; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • There is no CD authoring software for IRIX to burn EFS discs directly. (user-baf6, exchange 108157, 2023-07-24; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Use `safecopy` with 512-bit sectors to extract from an EFS disc, then mount with EFS drivers on Linux, export over NFS, and mount on IRIX for archival. (user-2189, exchange 216964, 2020-07-13; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
