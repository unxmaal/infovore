RedHat 4.2

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Digital Equipment Corporation.

RedHat 4.2 ran on the DEC Alpha Multia, and the first Linux machine to run permanently was RedHat 4.2 for DEC Alpha (Multia). 12 AXPPci systems ran RedHat 4.2 as a firewall, including the DEC AXPpci33. 34 RedHat 4.2 came with a printed manual for Alpha systems. 5 Building Perl on a Sun IPC running RedHat 4.2 took 22 hours, while compiling Perl on a SparcStation IPC under RedHat 4.2 took 28 hours. 67
