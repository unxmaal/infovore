VideoLab

Claims: 8

The VideoLab is a video processing card that offers digital video I/O, including D1 digital video I/O and digital 601, as well as component analogue video using a VBOB. 123 It can project live video as a texture over geometry and supports real-time I/O. 12 The VideoLab in the 4D/380 VGX offers parallel digital I/O and can be used with the Miranda SER100-E and SER100-D for D1 digital to SDI conversion. 42 It has digital I/O on the card but requires a breakout box for analogue component video, and composite video is not supported. 56 The VideoLab can be placed in the rightmost slot (19) or the fourth slot of a 4D/380, but requires a special 4-slot edge connector for the video option. 7
