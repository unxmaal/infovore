Grub2

Claims: 4 Part of GRUB

Grub2 can set up UEFI booting, but may require a UEFI wrapper on a BIOS system. 1 The boot filesystem should be on a SATA drive, with root and home on NVMe. 2 Grub needs to be able to load the kernel and initramfs from a bootable partition. 3 Grub2 can load the kernel into RAM and continue booting from there. 4
