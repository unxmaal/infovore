terminator

Claims: 12

Terminators are used on SCSI chains, such as the external SCSI port, which uses IDs 1 and 2 with a terminator. 12 Adding a terminator to the SCSI chain can cause boot issues with an external CD-ROM but not with no external termination. 1 Terminators should generally be disabled on the drive, and some machines should never need a terminator at all. 34 When converting from a wide SCSI device to a narrow one, use a cable that terminates the upper half of the SCSI bus, as some third-party cables lack this terminator. 5 An Alpha server came with a 68-pin terminator but requires a 50-pin terminator. 6 Terminators and cables for the RL01 are needed for the 11/34. 7 Terminator is also the name of a terminal application, whose installation can fail due to missing dependencies. 8
