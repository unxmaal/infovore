IIgs

Claims: 44 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Apple IIGS.

It has superior graphics and sound capabilities compared to the Mac SE, and superior sound hardware compared to the Atari ST and Amiga, with graphics on par with the Amiga. 12 The IIgs has a more advanced sound chip than any 8-bit machine and can display many on-screen colors, and it can play .MOD files. 345 A IIgs with upgrade cards such as TransWarp can have better graphics and sound and be as fast as an LC with IIe card. 67 It has 8 expansion slots and near full backwards compatibility with 8-bit Apple IIs. 8 The IIgs is slow on floppy but improves with a hard disk, and a SCSI2SD or BlueSCSI can be used with it, assuming you have a SCSI card. 91011 The IIgs can be used to make a SNES development system. 12 The IIgs has PSU failures, including broken floppy drives and high voltage caps that can violently explode and cause fire hazards. 1314 The Apple IIGS has an RGB monitor. 15 The Apple IIGS has a limited software selection, with most good software running on non-IIGS Apple 2s, making them more collectible. 16 Apple deliberately limited the IIGS to avoid competing with the Mac market. 17
