IRIX ld

Claims: 4 Part of IRIX

IRIX ld requires that symbol references be resolved before they are called, unlike GNU ld, which can be wasteful but avoids this issue. 1 The IRIX ld issue is due to problems with IRIX libraries, not GNU ld. 2 IRIX ld reports unresolved symbols at runtime, and `rld` can be used for details. 3 The working build environment uses nekoware GCC with IRIX ld. 4
