Apple Silicon

Claims: 6 Part of Apple Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see MacOS.

Apple Silicon is a RISC-based chip design that uses less power than x86/x64 for the same performance in benchmarks. 12 It is capable of video encoding and 3D modeling. 3 The design makes RAM upgrades less feasible. 4 Current macOS on Apple Silicon includes Rosetta for running Intel binaries, but it can be complex to use. 5
