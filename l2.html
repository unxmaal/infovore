L2

Claims: 17

L1 is total control of a brick, L2 is control of a rack, and L3 is control of a network. 1 The L2 is a PPC Linux box with a USB hub that controls a rack of Origin bricks, intended for rack servers like the O300, O350, and O3000. 2 The L2 uses a 50-pin connector for power, which is unusual and requires specific wiring, and it can be powered by a +48V DC brick if the correct pinout is known. 34 The L3 is Linux software that emulates the L2, and L2/L3 controllers are needed to talk to the Router, which connects to L2 or L3 over USB rather than L1. 567 The L3 software communicates with L2 on the brick to reflash L1, and the fuels L1 USB-B port can connect to an L2 or L3 controller. 89 The L2 is handy for changing the serial numbers of L1s in individual blocks and recovering systems without serial numbers. 10 The env command in L2 controls automatic power-down and must be on for the system to power down automatically. 11 CTRL-T in an IRIX shell through L2 returns to L2, and CTRL-D returns from L2 to IRIX. 12
