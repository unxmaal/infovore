system PROM

Claims: 4

System PROM may not significantly limit L2/L3 cache addressing beyond 1MB on R5k/R7k cards. 1 Fake RM7000, RM7000A, and RM7000B CPUs can be used, but they may not work properly without system PROM work. 2 The system PROM can now be reflashed via USB, which is useful for rescuing motherboards with corrupted PROM and, most importantly, for PROM development, as a new PROM image can be flashed by typing a command on the host. 3 The SR71010A boots to system PROM but reports incorrect cache sizes and fails to boot into Irix. 4
