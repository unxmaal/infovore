Termtris

Claims: 5

Termtris works on VT100 and VT240/340 terminals, with some color limitations on VT240, and supports 9x16 characters on MSDOS with EGA/VGA custom fonts, potentially better than VT340's 8x16 patterns. 12 Termtris uses graphical characters, and if the terminal doesn't support it, it defaults to ASCII mode with # and + symbols. 3 Termtris v1.9 supports VT52 and optimized drawing for 2400 baud, with binaries available for IRIX mips4. 4 The VGA ASCII CP437 font might be causing the issue with graphical characters in Termtris. 5
