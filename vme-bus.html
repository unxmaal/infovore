VME bus

Claims: 9 Part of VME

The VMEBus is implemented in MAME. 1 The Personal IRIS is the smallest SGI with a VME bus, using the VME external connector on the system. 23 The Indigo model with a VME bus is a unique variant. 4 Professional IRIS systems use VME as their system bus, configured by jumpers on the backplane, where incorrect settings render the system non-functional. 56 PowerSeries / Crimson systems use PowerPath but have VME compatibility through a bridge. 5 SBS bus adapters connect VMEbus and PCI bus, enabling seamless operation and superior performance. 7 The VMEbus is generic and can be used to automate a factory with IRIX or other drivers, and documentation exists for IRIX to directly interact with the VMEbus. 89
