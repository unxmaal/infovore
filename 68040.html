68040

Claims: 19 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXTstation.

The Motorola 68040 was used extensively by Apple because the PowerPC 601 was not suitable for laptops and low-end machines. 1 It was capable of competitive performance against other 32-bit RISC CPUs, depending on system architecture, though at the same clock speed a MIPS R3000 outperformed it. 23 The 68040's FPU was much faster than the 68882. 4 Motorola 68040 CPUs had heat issues that limited safe clock speed increases, and a 25 MHz E42K can probably be overclocked to around 50 MHz, but doing so is risky. 56 Some machines can be upgraded to a full 68040, such as the HP9000/380 (to 33 MHz by changing the CPU and crystal) and the Quadra 605. 78 LC040s are sometimes marked as full 040s, and old masks are more desirable, as are higher clock speeds. 9
