Minigl

Claims: 4

Minigl is a subset of OpenGL for Quake engine games that was used before a full ICD was available. 1 It works at higher resolutions on a V5 5500, but not on a Banshee for resolutions above 800x600. 2 With the same settings, miniGL runs Quake2 at 13.9 FPS while MesaFX runs it at 12.4 FPS. 3 Only MesaFX supports multitexturing, while miniGL does not, resulting in similar performance of 14.4 FPS to 15.7 FPS. 4

Sources

  2. Minigl works at higher resolutions on a V5 5500, but not on a Banshee for resolutions above 800x600. (user-17cb, 2020-07-07, exchange 9458, check: supported)
  3. miniGL runs Quake2 at 13.9 FPS, MesaFX at 12.4 FPS with the same settings. (user-757f, 2026-03-30, exchange 215943, check: supported)
