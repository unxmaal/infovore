GR1.5

Claims: 6

The GR1.5 uses a RE2 but has different connectors than the GR1.2, and its schematic shows jumper settings for the block next to the RE, allowing clock source choice and data line connections. 12 The GR1.5 has an HD15 video connector and on-board genlock, while the GR1.2 might not. 3 GR1.2 and GR1.5 machines might not have had significant differences beyond video output options, as `hinv` indicates similarities. 4 GR1.2 and GR1.5 firmware versions can be swapped by not updating the firmware. 5 GR1.5 with turbo boards has graphics issues not visible in prom but present in the OS, suggesting problems with bit-planes or geometry engines. 6
