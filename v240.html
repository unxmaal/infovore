V240

Claims: 9

The V240 is cosmetically similar to the Blade 2500 and V245, with the V245 using SAS drives and the Blade potentially having faster CPUs. 1 The SGI Blade 2500 is equivalent to the V240 with a desktop case and integrated sound, but has one BGE instead of four. 2 The V240 idles at about 250W and consumes more power than a typical firewall. 3 The V240 is among the SPARCs that are terribly loud. 4 The V240 was priced at $3450 from Sun, while the V440, which has two CPUs, was priced at $9950. 5 The Navy used V240 machines alongside Solaris T5220, 280r, and Blade 2000 machines. 6 The SAS backplane and controller in a V240 might support SATA. 7 If the V240 is powered on an even number of times in a 2-minute interval, it will not boot, as occurred when a field tech mistakenly turned it on an even number of times. 89
