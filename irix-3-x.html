IRIX 3.x

Claims: 10 Part of IRIX

IRIX 3.x is based on UNIX System V Release 3 with 4.3BSD enhancements and incorporates the 4Sight windowing system. 1 It is System V Release 3, as SVR4 introduced ELF, first seen in IRIX 5. 2 It uses MIPS 2.20 compilers, while IRIX 4.x uses MIPS 3.x compilers. 3 It ran on IRIX models 1000 and 2400, with the 1000 being a terminal and the 2400 a standalone workstation. 4 The miniroot has been the preferred way to install IRIX since at least IRIX 3.x. 5
