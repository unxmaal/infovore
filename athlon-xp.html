Athlon XP

Claims: 9

The Athlon XP usually had an edge over the Athlon in SDRAM configurations. 1 Netburst maintained performance leadership through the Athlon XP era due to higher clock speeds, but was overtaken by Athlon64 due to improved instruction per clock (IPC). 2 Athlon XP systems can run slow or not at all with later software due to lack of SSE2. 3 Athlon XP and a Quadro fx 2000 can run legacy games efficiently. 4 An Intel Pentium 4 with Hyper-Threading and the SMP patch can improve performance in SMP-capable applications, though it still lags behind an Athlon XP on a NF2 board. 5 Athlon XP builds can draw more than modern ATX PSUs on the 5V rail. 6 The Athlon XP board has a single ISA slot that may be bridged from PCI, limiting sound compatibility with OpenSTEP. 7 That's an Athlon XP on a PCI card that lets you run Windows inside of a window in Solaris. 8
