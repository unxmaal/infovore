n32

Claims: 15

N32 is a 64-bit software variant for IRIX, and IRIX 6.x introduced the o32 ABI to distinguish it from the n32 and n64 ABIs. 12 N32 and O32 are also described as 32-bit nomenclatures in MIPSpro, and configure scripts for IRIX expect mipspro ld with -n32 rather than GNU ld. 34 N32 binaries are compiled for MIPS-4, and the 'file' utility can identify the ABI version of an executable such as N32 on MIPS, which was supported by newer IRIX versions. 56 N32 seemed the best for binary sizes on machines with more than 4GB of RAM, but provides no benefit with less than 4GB of RAM. 78 IRIX 6.2 with N32 allows 64-bit register operations but has limited 256MB of RAM, leading to confusion about true 64-bit support. 9 The older IP19 could run IRIX 5.3, so the o32 vs. n32 distinction was relevant then, and N64 is available for both N32 and 64-bit kernels on the R4400 in the IP19 Onyx and Challenge. 1011
