Itanium 2

Claims: 9 Part of Itanium

The Itanium 2 was an emergency redesign to improve upon the original Itanium model. 1 The Itanium was bad, but Itanium 2 and Montecito became a compelling option for some manufacturers like SGI and HP. 2 The Altix 330 was a 1U Itanium machine from SGI released in 2005 with Itanium2 1.5 GHz CPUs and was on the market for about a year. 3 The Itanium 2 can run 32-bit binaries, but their installation failed. 4
