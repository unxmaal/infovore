V12 graphics

Claims: 7 Part of V12 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The V12 graphics are the same in both MDD and earlier systems, and Tezro systems were only officially offered with V12 graphics from SGI, not running with FireGL cards. 12 An Octane with dual R14K CPUs at 600 MHz and 2GB RAM costs around 600 Euros, while a Tezro 4x700 with 2GB RAM costs $2,400 and other Tezro systems with V12 graphics can be found for around $4,500 on eBay. 345 The sgug-rse port works on Octane² with R12k CPUs and V12 graphics, though fps may be limited, and a video shows Baldur's Gate running on an Octane2 with 2x R14K CPUs at 600 MHz and V12 graphics. 67
