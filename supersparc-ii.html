SuperSPARC II

Claims: 8 Part of SuperSPARC Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARCstation 20.

SuperSPARC II is rarer than SuperSPARC and HyperSPARC, and it was slower than a 486. 12 It was designed to replace the MicroSPARC. 3 Sun increased clock rates on the SuperSPARC-II by relaxing FPU timings, removing the double-pumped clock system, and adding pipeline stages, making it more clock-efficient despite being slightly less efficient per clock. 45 The MBus slots can hold either a SuperSPARC-II CPU or an SBus card that looks identical, and the SPARCstation 2 board may work with a SuperSPARC-II PSU. 67 Solaris 2.3 runs on a SPARCstation 20 with a 75 MHz SuperSPARC-II and 128 MB RAM. 8
