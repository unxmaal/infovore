parallel

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Capacitor.

InterLnk can be configured to use either serial or parallel ports. 1 Development for PS1 consoles can be done by transferring programs over serial or parallel, or burning them to discs. 2 To get a capacitance, capacitors can be wired in parallel to increase capacitance or in series to decrease it. 3 The capacitance of two 10 µF and two 6 µF capacitors in series, then in parallel, is 7.5 µF. 4 An FPGA could implement legacy genlock compatibility and provide serial, parallel, and joystick/mouse ports. 5
