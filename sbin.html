/sbin

Claims: 3

On some Unix systems, static versions of binaries were stored in /sbin to allow fixing systems after messing with shared libraries. 1 Binaries go in bin, statically linked in sbin. 2 The IRIX kernel does not care about the contents of /bin or /sbin; it just runs init and halts when init exits. 3
