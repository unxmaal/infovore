plastic

Claims: 25

The O2 and Indy are both made of plastic, with the Indy's being particularly durable due to its need to support a heavy CRT, while the O2 case is made of cheap plastic and often breaks. 12 Later systems used flame retardants in plastic, but O2 plastic can be brittle and potentially harmful. 3 The reaction causing plastic to become brittle is not related to flame retardant additives but is a natural reaction to UV and/or heat, affecting ABS and some other plastics. 4 The O(-2) from UV light reacts with bromine in plastic, restoring its original color without damaging the plastic's texture. 5 Acetone works by melting plastic and bonding it back together, while other glues add an adhesive layer; acetone is preferred but may not work on very old plastics. 6
