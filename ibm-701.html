IBM 701

Claims: 5 Part of IBM

The IBM 701 has two programmer-accessible registers: a 38-bit accumulator and a 36-bit multiplier/quotient. 1 Its arithmetic speed was largely set by memory speed, with addition requiring five 12-microsecond cycles and multiplication/division requiring 38 cycles (456 microseconds). 2 The IBM 701 used Williams tube memory that required periodic refreshing, inserting refresh cycles into the 701's timing, while its core memory was the same speed (12 microseconds) but without refresh cycles. 34 An IBM 701 could outperform a VIC-20. 5
