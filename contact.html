A flat connector socket contact can get shoved back when the mating pin smashes into the end of the socket rather than being held by friction. 12 To prevent this, insert a piece of plastic between the rows of pins to keep the socket open. 1 Heat from the CPU may cause minor warping of the board, which can also affect contact. 3
