ASIC

Claims: 23 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Field-programmable gate array.

ASICs appear in a range of hardware, including ACARD's IDE and SATA adapters, which use a custom ACARD ASIC, and the Bedrock, which is the main ASIC that communicates with CPUs, memory, XIO, and NL. 12 The SparcStation 5's ASIC supports Fast Narrow SCSI and 100Mbit/sec Fast Ethernet, while other designs pair one ASIC per bank of inputs with a PPC 405 CPU and a few FPGAs. 34 The ASIC is socketed, possibly due to availability in a PLCC package, and is not more prone to fail than other components. 5 One ASIC is undocumented and difficult to find unless it is torn out of a NeXT printer, and it can be resoldered using hot air to remove it and an iron to solder it down. 67
