2000

Claims: 8

The 2000 series includes the 2300, which had a local 22 MB ST506 disk, while the 2000 and 2200 were diskless and network/serial only. 1 The 2000 was the only one of three Amigas the author liked. 2 The 8375 Agnus chip is expensive on eBay, and certain revisions are needed to upgrade 2000 chip memory to 2MB for Toast. 3 The Blade 1000/2000/Fire 280R uses 232-pin DIMMs in sets of four. 4 The Visual Workstations can run NT4, 2000, and Linux, but Linux support for the video pipeline is unaccelerated. 5 200 and 2000 work, 300, 350, and 30000 work, but not 200 and 300, and it doesn't like a standard SCSI cable. 6 2000's have blown motherboards. 7 Win9x were such horrible daily drivers that 2000 and XP felt like a gift from heaven. 8
