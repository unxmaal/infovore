hardware acceleration

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Field-programmable gate array.

The r128 card does not work with hardware acceleration. 1 Hardware acceleration is found interesting and cool, coming from ASICs and FPGAs. 2 On SGI systems, if hardware texturing is not available, the CPU handles texturing while hardware acceleration is used for geometry and z-buffering. 3 The Indy lacks hardware acceleration. 4 Converting videos to play on an O2 requires specific formats and codecs, and IRIX may not support hardware acceleration. 5
