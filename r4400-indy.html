R4400 Indy

Claims: 5 Part of R4400

The R4400 Indy can run IRIX 5.2. 1 It can handle multiple tasks without crashing. 2 It can perform hardware span rendering for untextured polygons with gouraud interpolation and zbuffering. 3 Installing IRIX 6.5 on an R5K Indy from an R4400 Indy does not require a reinstall but may encounter disk space issues during installation. 4 An R4400 Indigo2 with XZ is not necessarily the same speed as an R4400 Indy with XZ. 5
