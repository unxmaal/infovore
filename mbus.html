Mbus

Claims: 6

Uncached SuperSPARC modules run at the speed of the Mbus, with the SS10's Mbus running at 40MHz and the SS20's at 50MHz. 1 SS10 motherboard jumpers configure the SuperSPARC module to run at either 40MHz or 36MHz (newer model) or 40MHz or 33MHz (model 20), with 40MHz recommended unless using very old modules. 2 The Sun4m version of the cg12 blocks one Mbus slot in an SS10. 3 The XVR4000 has two Mbus and eight Sbus slots. 4 Mbus is half the speed of SuperSPARC at the same clock speed, and SS20 is the same as Mbus for compatibility. 56
