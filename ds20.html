DS20

Claims: 16

The DS20 is a smaller, lighter (40 kg) Alpha workstation that has two 500 MHz EV6 processors and can hold up to seven hard disks. 12 It is one of the last SGI machines to run Windows officially, though Alpha NT 4 can run on it. 34 The Tsunami HAL works on the DS20, but setting os_type to nt requires running ARC manually or adding it to the nvram script. 5 The DS20 has unsupported or disabled onboard USB and SCSI, never received faster processors, and its terminal could not display all processors per partition. 67 The DS20, DS20E, and AlphaPC 264DP are different revisions of the same hardware, with the DS20E offering more upgradability despite similar board designs. 89 The DS20 used StorageWorks bricks, while the DS10 and everything later used the metal Compaq sleds. 10
