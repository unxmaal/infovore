MAE

Claims: 14

MAE is an official Apple product that emulates Macintosh on Solaris, and it runs on HP-UX and Solaris but not IRIX. 123 WordPerfect runs on both MAE and WABI but is more usable in WABI, while MS PowerPoint runs in MAE on HP-UX. 45 The last versions of MAE work on UltraSPARC and anything that runs Solaris 9 or lower, and MAE 3.0.4 runs well on Ultra 5. 67 MAE has worked on older Solaris versions, but on Solaris 10 it segfaults after accepting the EULA. 89 faketime is a shared library that sets the epoch time to March 12, 1998, allowing MAE to accept this date as valid. 10 The Hotline Client for MAE does not work. 11
