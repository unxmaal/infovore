N64 devkit

Claims: 6 Part of N64

The N64 devkit is rare and expensive, with plans and schematics available but no firmware needed, and it is valuable for reverse engineering. 12 The development tools were sometimes Windows-only, even though the link is for IRIX, and the software was found on a zip disk but was just Windows-related material. 34 Once the tool is working, the N64 devkit can run on the machine. 5 Nintendo, Rareware, and other companies used SGI hardware and software to create game assets and interface with N64 devkits, with the Indy using an N64 add-in board for debugging. 6
