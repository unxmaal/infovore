Flint

Claims: 7

Flint was a special version of Flame for the O2, functioning as a compositing and editing tool, and was also known as Effect. 123 Flint lacked many advanced nodes, and Smoke Advanced was Smoke with all Flame nodes. 4 Dongles integrated with FlexLM were required for floating or permanent licenses, while Flint/Flame 4.4 and earlier supported a custom Discreet dongle. 5 Flint was likely intended for the Indy rather than as a dev station, and it can handle editing but may run out of space on an O2. 67
