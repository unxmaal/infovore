3D modeling

Claims: 5 Part of 3D

3D modeling software can run on IRIX and SGI machines, producing high-quality models that can be exported to other devices, and most formats remain semi-current. 12 Models can be exported to a format and rendered with POV-Ray, even without a direct plugin in professional programs. 3 Prior to around 1998, no non-SGI option was available for 3D modeling on consoles. 4
