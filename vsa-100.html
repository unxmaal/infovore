VSA-100

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Voodoo2.

The VSA-100 chips on a Voodoo 5 5500 graphics card are both connected to the same PCI bus. 1 The SN74CB3T3245 IC can operate at speeds above 95MHz, but the VSA-100 limits the bus speed to 95MHz. 2
