Wavefront

Claims: 20 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Wavefront Technologies, Autodesk Alias.

Wavefront was a 3D graphics software package, with the Advanced Visualizer version spanning from 1985/86 to 1997, the last known version being 4.3.2. 1 It was advertised as the first commercial computer graphics package a studio could buy turnkey, followed by Alias. 2 Wavefront files can be exported as .obj, supported by modern 3D software. 3 The Advanced Visualizer was used for modeling, animating, and rendering with Wavefront's classic Model-Preview-Image modules. 4 Alias was originally made, then bought out Wavefront, then SGI, then Autodesk. 5 Autodesk no longer holds Wavefront's IP as they were destroyed upon acquisition, with focus on Maya. 6
