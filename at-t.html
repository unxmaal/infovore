AT&T

Claims: 6

BSD was an attempt to avoid AT&T's license fee for Unix, which ended up costing more due to legal issues. 1 AT&T made a variety of machines in the 80s, some ran DOS, and AT&T made their own SGI machines. 23 Sun could open Solaris because Sun had licensed from AT&T rights equivalent to ownership of System V, while this is not true for other SysV derivatives. 4 Micro Focus International now owns the Unix copyright, and AT&T no longer owns the Unix trademark. 45 The IRIX kernel has contributions from SGI and AT&T, with some Sun code in NFS. 6
