POV-Ray

Claims: 7

POV-Ray is a 3D modeling program that was used on 486 machines and vintage Macs in the 90s-2000s era, and was ported to SGI. 12 POV-Ray is single-threaded and consumes one CPU core. 3 Models can be exported to a format and rendered with POVRAY, even without a direct plugin in professional programs. 4 It supports a .inc format and likely requires conversion to DXF before use with POV-Ray, which can handle it with later versions. 5 Users might have to compile a newer version of povray and save that job for the o350's/Tezro's. 6 Replacing boost::condition_variable with C++11 std::condition_variable works for a small example in POV-Ray. 7
