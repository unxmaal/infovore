ProPack

Claims: 19

The SGI Prism requires SLES 9 with ProPack for full hardware support, and attempting to install other operating systems crashes the system partway through the install. 12 ProPack is required for graphics, imaging, and ALE3, and the Prism is only compatible with SGI ProPack with limited software support. 345 The Prism GPU driver is closed source, wrapped into SGI's custom kernel, and available with ProPack for kernel 2.4. 6 The fglrx driver in ProPack is tailored for SGI-supported setups and requires SGI-specific extensions, while the standard radeon driver is included in SLES 10. 78 Propack would not work on SLES 10 due to the wrong libc version and likely not on SLES 11, and the matching ProPack is needed for the correct SLES SP version. 910 The PCI Radeon 9200 SE can run X with the radeon driver on SLES 9 with ProPack but lacks 3D acceleration and crashes with fglrx, and SLES 11 fails to display properly. 11 Debian ran well on IA64 but lost support without ProPack, and ProPack likely never worked officially on Debian. 1213 ProPack 4 SP4 is the last supported disc for the Prism, and there is a ProPack for SLES 11. 1415
