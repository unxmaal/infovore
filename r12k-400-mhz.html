R12k 400 MHz

Claims: 5

The SGI O2 can be configured with an R12k 400 MHz CPU, an R5k cooler, maximum RAM, and two 15k RPM drives. 1 Darkplaces runs well on the R12k 400 MHz variant of the O2. 2 The R12k 400 MHz with 2MB cache performs competitively against the 600 MHz R12000 with 512KB cache due to the larger L2 cache. 3 A 400 MHz R12k is slower than a R12000 CPU in the same machine. 4 The R12k 400 MHz/1 GB RAM/Xbow 1.4 Octane runs SE/ESI graphics with no TRAM, limiting its performance. 5
