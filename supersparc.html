SuperSPARC

Claims: 25

SuperSPARC was a RISC CPU released in 1992 that outperformed contemporary Intel CPUs. 1 It was faster than the SPARCv7 at the same clock speed and faster than the equivalent SuperSPARC by a good margin, though it was slower compared to other RISC chips like the HP9000 or Alpha. 234 The SuperSPARC has no L2 cache, a 2k data cache, and a 4k instruction cache. 5 The SS20 supports SuperSPARC CPUs with large caches, which offer better performance than HyperSPARC CPUs despite having less MHz. 6 The SS20 has a 70MHz SuperSPARC CPU and 96MB of RAM. 7 The ZX was used in an SS10 with a 90 MHz SuperSPARC. 8 SuperSPARC II is rarer than SuperSPARC and HyperSPARC. 9 HyperSPARCs were slower than SuperSPARC and had limited interest due to their speed and lack of proper cases. 10
