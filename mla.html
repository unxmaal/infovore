MLA

Claims: 9

The MLA is a metal construction containing a shiny board and SMT components. 1 Modifying DVI cables allows the MLA to work on an Octane, but the DVI from a DCD has issues with the DVI on the MLA. 23 On a 1600sw, the MLA shows a 1280x1024 resolution even when running a VFO that should support 1600x1024, causing vertical artifacting. 4 The 1600sw relies on the MLA or FPA for control and will flash orange when unplugged. 5 The DCD is fussy and works for monitors that are not the MLA. 6 The 13W3-VGA into an MLA works with a monitor profile, while the V10 on an Octane with analog output has issues with the MLA. 78 The MLA only needs 0.5A, maybe less, but SGI likely saved money by shipping two identical power bricks instead of a specific MLA one. 9
