gcc 4.7.4

Claims: 6 Part of GCC

gcc 4.7.4 is one of several C++ compilers available for IRIX, alongside gcc 3.3, gcc 3.4, mipspro, and the 8.user-b4f4 build. 1 irixports now allows choosing between gcc 4.7.4 and 8.2.0 through a gccversion variable, with both versions bootstrapped. 2 gcc 4.7.4 compiles and runs some software on IRIX, though not everything, and EPM can be compiled with it. 34 A rebuilt dosbox works with gcc 4.7.4, though it previously dumped core. 5
