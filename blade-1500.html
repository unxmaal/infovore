Blade 1500

Claims: 17

The Sun Blade 1500 is one of the only vaguely modern Sparc workstations, alongside the Blade 2500 and the Ultra 25/45. 1 A Blade 1500 with an XVR-600 can run Solaris 9 without special patches, and the XVR1200 can also be used, though not officially supported. 23 The Sun Blade 1500 has an IDE controller that maxes out at 128GB, and the 375-0005, 375-0006, and 375-0097 are not recognized by the OBP or probe-scsi-all and are not bootable devices. 45 The dual-channel SCSI card can be booted from, but the internal port on the Blade 1500 does not work. 6 The Sun Blade 1500 does not support a Type 6 keyboard with a PC-compatible layout, causing a kernel core dump when a USB hub is used with a second keyboard. 7 The Blade 1500 can run for 18 hours straight without fail, but it may also have a failing SMD cap, and its motherboard has rubber bumpers that are degrading over time. 8910
