performance

Claims: 22

Technologies like NUMAlink address performance but make price, reliability, and availability worse, especially when you have many moving parts and it's hard to do maintenance without downtime. 1 A/UX runs on SE/30 but is sluggish at 16 MHz with DiiMo at 50 MHz. 2 Accurate emulation isn't the goal; performance is. 3 The project prioritizes portability within performance limits. 4 Consumer SATA SSDs can provide better performance than 2000s-era FC SANs. 5 Itanium was not x86 and building VLIW compilers was difficult, leading to performance and efficiency issues. 6 The system runs at a 30% higher resolution with better performance. 7 Unless you're trying to set a record, the method for measuring performance doesn't matter as long as it's consistent across tests. 8
