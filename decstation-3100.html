DECstation 3100

Claims: 3

The VAX9000 was intended as a new mainframe but failed, while the DECstation 3100, a new RISC workstation, sold well. 1 The DECstation 3100 is a 16.67MHz version of the R2000A, compared with the 12MHz DECstation 2100, making it slightly faster but with abysmal graphics and a buggy Unix. 2 VRAM modules are used in the DECstation 2100/3100. 3

Sources

  1. The VAX9000 was intended as a new mainframe but failed, while the DECstation 3100, a new RISC workstation, sold well. (user-03ad, 2022-12-29, exchange 222758, check: supported)
  2. The DECstation 2100 is a 12MHz R2000A, while the 3100 is a 16.67MHz version. The 3100 is slightly faster but has abysmal graphics and a buggy Unix. (user-03ad, 2023-06-04, exchange 235355, check: supported)
  3. VRAM modules are used in DECstation 2100/3100 and RAM modules in VAXstation 4000 and some MicroVAX 3100, while Sun SPARCstation 10/20 or Ultra use different modules. (user-03ad, 2025-05-15, exchange 116827, check: supported)
