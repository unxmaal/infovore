Solaris 10

Claims: 118 Part of Solaris Background: 18 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Oracle Solaris, OpenGL, Ultra 60, ZFS.

Solaris 10 is a Sun product that was originally made by Sun before Oracle acquired the company, and it is the latest version. 12 Solaris 10 can be installed from eBay CDs, and some Suns were not picky about CD drives for installing it, though they might have been for earlier versions. 34 Solaris 10 can be installed on an Ultra 5, though it may not run well, and it should work on an Ultra 10 with 440 MHz and 1 GB of RAM. 567 Solaris 10 runs on Sun machines from Solaris 2.5.1 to Solaris 10, and the final Solaris 10 was installed on the Oracle Enterprise T5120. 89 Solaris 10 ZFS pools are compatible with OpenZFS, and it uses sysvinit for init scripts, unlike Solaris 11 which uses SMF. 1011 Solaris 10 jumpstart installs can be straightforward off a Solaris 10 server, and the Solaris 10 installer does not automatically format disks. 1213 gcc versions 4.7 and later are available for Solaris 10, with 5.5 and 10.2.0 documented to support SPARC, requiring adjustments to the linker and assembler. 14 user-5510 Studio 12.5 is available for Solaris 10, and CDE on Solaris 10 runs with 1.6 and supports a few new icon/backdrop formats, better wheel mouse support, and a few other tiny things. 1516 Solaris 10 is a UNIX operating system that supports IPv6 out of the box and includes ZFS as one of its notable features. 1718 Solaris 10 uses bash as its shell, available in /usr/bin/bash and /bin/bash. 1920 The pkg package format changed between Solaris 10 and Solaris 11, and the luxadm tool was introduced in Solaris 10. 2122 Solaris 10's graphical installer does not support setting up ZFS, so only the text installer can do so. 23 OpenGL can be installed as a selectable package during a custom Solaris 10 installation. 24 Solaris 10 on SPARC uses Xsun instead of Xorg, and fbconfig should be used for configuration. 25 Solaris 10 still doesn't natively integrate with Active Directory. 26 Solaris 10 isn't patched for 64-bit epoch time, though the latest OpenSSL works on Solaris. 27 Solaris 10 is required for T series chips, and the T1000 and T2000 only run Solaris 10 and later. 282930 Solaris 10 is notably faster than Solaris 8/9 for almost everything, especially on multithreaded software and disk I/O, with services starting in parallel and a network stack rewritten with zero/minimal copy. 3132 Solaris 10 is a significant upgrade, even on less powerful machines like the Blade 150, and a Blade 100 with 512MB of RAM can run Solaris 8 and 10, though performance is better with upgrades. 3334 Solaris 10 on SPARC systems has slow boot times, but the operating system itself is not the bottleneck; it's the software and its inability to fully utilize certain hardware. 35 The Solaris 10 install DVD can be used for installation and works with modern SSL and crypto certs, and Solaris 10 was free at one point, but Oracle ended that, with Solaris 8 making it easier to obtain and Solaris 10 downloadable from Sun's site. 363738 Solaris 10 was not widely adopted due to limited qualification and lack of new application support, making it irrelevant for some users. 39 Solaris 10 lacks rcpbind and rcpinfo commands, and cosf was added in Solaris 10.
