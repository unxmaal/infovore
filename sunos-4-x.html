SunOS 4.x

Claims: 10 Part of SunOS

SunOS 4.x is based on BSD, while SunOS 5.x (Solaris 2.x) is based on System V. 1 SunOS 4.x was referred to as Solaris 1.x. 2 SunOS 4.x comes with a K&R C compiler by default, while Solaris needs the Sun Workshop compiler. 3 SunOS 4.x ran NeWS, the windowing system SGI used before X11. 4 Oracle maintains that the SunOS 4.x floppy disk label format is read-only compatible with modern UFS for the same CPU arch. 5 SunOS 4.x is BSD-derived. 6
