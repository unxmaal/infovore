T2000

Claims: 8

The T2000 is one of a group of laptop cards that also includes the T1000, RTX 3000, 4000, and 5000. 1 The T2000 runs Solaris 10 or later, and its first T CPUs had low single-core performance while using a thread-like multi-core system similar to Pentium 4 Hyperthreading. 23 The T2000 was used as a web server, and its ALOM console can be accessed via telnet with one additional cable. 24 A T2000 server cost about $15k, compared with about $60k for a V240 and about $30k for an M3000. 5 The Nvidia K620 is supported on Solaris/Intel but not on the T2000, which runs Solaris/Sparc. 6
