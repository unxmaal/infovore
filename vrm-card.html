VRM card

Claims: 3 Part of VRM

The VRM card is a removable component used in dual socket P3 machines, allowing for upgrades or swapping if power delivery fails. 1 Itanium machines and some P4 systems also use removable VRM cards. 2 The battery pack is easily identifiable on the card, and caching writes without a battery is not recommended for data integrity. 3
