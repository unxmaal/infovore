GCC9

Claims: 9 Part of GCC

GCC9 was built incrementally, starting with gcc8 and gcc7, with IRIX support dropped in gcc 4.8, and is now easiest to build from a prior version. 1 The gcc9 in Didbs is built using a chain of earlier gcc versions. 2 Didbs includes a GCC9 binary and other tools, available as a large .tar file via torrent, for IRIX development. 3 GCC9 should support C++20, with most C++20 core language features present, though some library functionality may be missing. 45 Lambdas work, suggesting coroutines may also, and sgug-rse can be tested on an SGI. 4 IRIX does not support ELF thread local sections, so thread local support in GCC9 is emulated. 6 GCC9 compiles std threading to POSIX thread APIs when linking in lpthread, making it not necessarily different. 7 GCC 9 was available as a standalone version but may not be easily found, and golang is possible. 89
