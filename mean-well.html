Mean Well

Claims: 4

Older systems' power supplies often require rebuilding due to capacitor issues, especially tantalum capacitors which can short. 1 Replacing the PSU with a Mean Well for 5V and an ATX PSU for other voltages can resolve this. 1 To replace the PSU, one needs to match Sony and Nidec connector pinouts and power inputs for Meanwell. 2
