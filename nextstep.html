NeXTSTEP

Claims: 69 Background: 9 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXTSTEP, A/UX, NeXTstation, Quake (video game).

NextStep (NS) is a computing environment that can be installed on a range of SPARC hardware, including the SPARCstation 20, SPARCstation 5, and Fujitsu TurboSPARC, though it is not officially supported on the latter. 1234567 Some SPARC machines are incompatible with NextStep due to their architecture, such as the SPARCstation IPC and SPARCclassic, which use Sun-4c rather than Sun-4m, and Ross CPUs are not compatible at all. 8910 NextStep also saw releases for PA-RISC hardware, including HP-UX 9 machines such as the HP Analyzer 16505A and the 700 series, as well as PA 2.0 systems. 11121314 OPENSTEP for Solaris is a repackaging of NextStep for SPARC that runs on Ultras and Blades, with the latest SPARC support being an SPARCstation 20. 15 NeXTSTEP systems do not support resource forks, do not use the right mouse button by default, and come with zsh. 161718 NeXTSTEP was used for software development, with Application Builder supporting a wide range of development, and id Software used NeXT machines, especially NextStep, for development. 1920 Microsoft CEO Bill Gates said in 1991 that developing for NextStep would be a waste of time, though Microsoft Office on NextStep would have been rock solid. 2122 NeXTSTEP is an operating system that was ported to multiple architectures, including NeXT 68k, PA-RISC, SPARC, and x86, and its desktop environment, OpenStep, was ported to Solaris/SPARC and possibly Windows. 23 It is a gen 1 ukernel based on Mach, not a true Unix. 24 NeXTSTEP is known for its advanced GUI, which is very good and also looked good in monochrome. 252627 It is considered to have the best user interface of any commercial Unix, alongside IRIX. 28 The NeXT software was the better part of the company, since the hardware was not well-regarded. 29 NeXTSTEP could run on unmodified PCs. 30
