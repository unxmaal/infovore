RS232

Claims: 18

RS232 is a complex standard with many connectors and varying support for baud rates and handshaking, making it difficult to use without documentation. 1 Before RS232, compatibility over serial was non-existent. 2 RS232 serial connections can be handled with a variety of cables and converters. 3 Some conversions require no jumpers, such as DB25 to RS232 conversion, and DB9 to DB25 can be connected directly with jumper wires without a null modem cable. 45 RS422 geoports can be used as RS232, and a Mac mini DIN 8 RS422 to DE9 RS232 cable can work, though not guaranteed. 6 Some Mac serial cables use RS232 and others RS422 for higher transfer rates. 7 The Arduino uses TTL levels, incompatible with RS423 and RS232. 8
