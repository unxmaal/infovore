General

The Sun Ultra 5 is a single-processor system with PCI, while the Ultra 2 is dual-processor with SBus. 1 The Ultra 5 and Ultra 10 are lower-cost machines that were often given away with server purchases, and the Ultra 10 is identical to the Ultra 5 in terms of the mainboard, just with more PCI slots via a risercard. 23 The Ultra 5 has onboard graphics limited to 8-bit color with 4MB of RAM, and it does not support UPA or 64-bit PCI, limiting upgrade options to PGX24, PGX32, or PGX64 cards. 4567 The Ultra 5 has cooling issues, is more cramped, and lacks SCSI unless a slot is consumed, and it has a defective motherboard with some DIMM slots not working. 89 The Ultra 5 requires a hard disk sled or hard disk, has 128MB EDO DRAM minimum, and has buggy IDE chipsets that require workarounds in Linux. 1011 The Ultra 5 can run Solaris 2.6, 2.5.1 32-bit, 8, and 10, and it is a good choice for Solaris users. 1213141516 The Sun Ultra 5 is a SPARC-line workstation considered one of the top models in the line, alongside the SPARCstation 4 and SPARCstation 5. 17 It was the cheapest RISC/Unix workstation and was widely used for Solaris coding by computer science students. 18 The Ultra 5 has PCI expansion and no built-in SCSI, but can use SCSI boards via PCI. 1920 It lacks a UPA slot, so cards such as the Creator3D and Elite3D-Lite do not fit, though direct installation of a Creator3D may require case modifications. 2122 The Ultra 5 and Ultra 10 share the same motherboard but have different RAM capacity limits, with the Ultra 5 maxing out at 512MB. 2324 It can be operated via X server using XDMCP and SSH. 25 The Ultra 5 may require NVRAM surgery or battery replacement, and a dead NVRAM can produce POST beep codes while failing to display video. 262728 The Ultra 5 used commodity parts and a PCI bus, and has IDE connectors that can use any IDE drive, though some drives may perform better. 2930 The Ultra 5's RAM has ECC and a different bit-width than other RAM, as indicated by the labels. 31 Solaris 2.6 works on the Ultra 5, and 2.8 installs without issues, though later versions of 2.8 remove XGL. 32 The Ultra 5 runs CDE instead of Gnome. 33 NetBSD has issues with X11 on the Ultra 5, possibly due to graphics card support. 34 A Sun Ultra 5 desktop is available, working except for a dead Dallas battery. 35
