SAS cards

Claims: 6 Part of SAS Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX, SATA.

SAS cards generally can also do SATA, though old SAS cards could not. 12 SAS cards can work under IRIX for PCI-X systems, and IRIX supports SAS cards with working drivers. 34 SAS cards are limited to the Chimera line of machines due to driver differences. 5 CXFS is a clustered XFS filesystem for SAN access. 6
