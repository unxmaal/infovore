unzip

Claims: 7

unzip is a command that extracts files from compressed archives and may be included in distros on some systems. 1 It supports many old operating systems. 2 On Linux, files can be compressed with tar and transferred to IRIX for unpacking with unzip. 3 libarchive replaced unzip on *nix systems, though Libarchive 3.7.7 compiles but has a SIGBUS issue with unzip. 45 The file misc-5.3-files.iso contains unzip along with nedit, bzip2, gimp, and netscape. 6
