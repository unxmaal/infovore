LTO tapes

Claims: 4

LTO tapes are used with backup solutions such as Restrospect Pro, which requires ATTO drivers. 1 LTO tapes have had issues with restores. 2 DAT, 8mm DDS and LTO tapes work flawlessly on IRIX. 3 For long-term storage, LTO tapes are considered unreliable, with spinning rust in ZFS RAIDz 2 regarded as more resilient. 4
