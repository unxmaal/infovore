XVR-500

Claims: 10

The XVR-500 is a member of the 3Dlabs Wildcat series, which also includes the Expert3D, Expert3D-Lite, XVR-600, and XVR-1200. 1 The XVR-500 is similar to the Expert3D-Lite but faster, and it supports 24-bit and 3D acceleration in OpenBSD. 23 3D acceleration on the XVR-500 is only available in Solaris. 4 The XVR-500 can run Quake2 at 640x480 at 29.7 FPS. 5 The XVR-500 has been used successfully in SGI systems including an Ultra 10 and a Blade 150, but swapping graphics cards on a system with an XVR-500 requires careful handling. 67
