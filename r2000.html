R2000

Claims: 17

With R3000

The R2000A is an R3000 core in an R2000 package, and R2000 and R3000 processors have different packages, with the R2000A being a variant. 12 The Personal IRIS IP6 was an R2000-based system, followed by the IP7 PowerSeries with R3000-based 4D/2xx and 4D/3xx systems. 3

General

The R2000 is a 32-bit MIPS processor and the first commercial MIPS CPU, used in the Professional IRIS, Personal IRIS, and the 4D/120 GTX. 45 The R2k refers to the R2000 and R2300, very early MIPS used in the Professional IRIS. 6 The machine is a SE with a 4D/80, 85 processor and R2000 at 16.6 MHz on a VME bus. 7 NCSA Mosaic can run on an R2000. 8
