PC

Claims: 74 Background: 9 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Central processing unit, Distcc, Emulator, Mac (computer), PC game, PCI-X, SD card, Silicon Graphics, Video games and Linux.

A PC can support 2 or 3 parallel ports on an ISA interface, or one or more on PCI. 1 The 1600SW can be used with an O2 via an adapter card, but not with a PC. 2 The best way to play 21st-century games on an Indy is to put a PC in the case. 3 Writing Amiga floppies on a PC requires two floppy drives and an old OS, and is not recommended; without a Catweasel it is ancient and not recommended. 45 PC controllers can handle Amiga format with a hack. 6 distcc can utilize a PC or a Talos PPC workstation for compilation. 7 The HP firmware for the FireGL worked with a PC, but the SGI firmware had issues. 8 Files should be uncompressed on a PC before transferring to an SGI to save time. 9 The PC likely runs Windows NT, which would be compatible with proper drivers. 10 No PC on a SBus card had a PS/2 port. 11 SGI tried to develop 3D graphics cards for the PC but failed. 12 If you're asking about missing libraries and header files, it should be enough to copy them from your SGI machine to the PC's sysroot directory in the cross-compiler. 13
