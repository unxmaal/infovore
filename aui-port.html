AUI port

Claims: 5 Part of AUI

The Challenge S has an RJ45 port that is different from the AUI port and cannot be used for net-booting. 1 The AUI port is 10 Mb/s, and for 100 Mb/s Ethernet, a dual 100 Mb/s Ethernet VME board is available but has poor performance and doesn't support full duplex; FDDI, ATM, and FC are other options requiring additional infrastructure. 2 The Indy's AUI port disables the 10baseT port, but with GIO ethernet you can have two network interfaces. 3
