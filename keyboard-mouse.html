keyboard/mouse

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see KVM switch.

Sun monitors and keyboard/mouse can be connected to VGA and PS/2 ports using adapters such as the Sun 13W484948Whatever to VGA adapter, the special box that converts Sun monitor and keyboard/mouse to VGA and PS/2, or the Raritan APSSUN adapter that converts 13w3 and Sun keyboard/mouse to PS/2 ports and VGA. 12 These adapters allow the use of a standard KVM, and a KVM with 4 USB ports can solve port issues. 13 A monitor and keyboard/mouse that folds down and slides into a 1U enclosure is a neat setup for vintage systems. 4 A KVM and keyboard/mouse can be hot-swapped to work together. 3 Disconnecting video and keyboard/mouse, and using a null modem cable for serial debugging can help diagnose issues. 5 Connecting an SGI keyboard/mouse to a PS/2 port using adapters may not work. 6 OS9.2 made my keyboard/mouse usable after a fresh install, and ADB was connected anyway on 8.6. 7
