blower

Claims: 5

The VPro in a rackmount server cannot use the ip59 CPU-boards, which have 2 or 4x 1GHz processors, and other blower models can blow air laterally through the case. 1 The Onyx350/RM Tezro has a special cage with a plastic air baffle and a side hole for the remaining blower. 2 The mainboard has limited space for the Vpro and blower, especially with a V10 or Tez Vpro housing installed. 3 Some rack Tezro models appear to be missing the blower on the PSU side. 4 The system requires L1 configuration to fully power on, but the blower and display work with a different configuration. 5
