soldering iron

Claims: 7

The soldering iron has temperature control issues, randomly thinking it's too hot and cooling down, requiring restarts to stabilize; it's about 7 years old. 1 The soldering iron has a RISC-V CPU. 2 A method for removing SMD electrolytic caps involves cutting into the can with flushcutters, trimming leads, and using a soldering iron to remove remaining bits without damaging the board. 3 Fixing cold joints with a hot air station might work temporarily, but a proper soldering iron is recommended. 4 When replacing an IC with broken legs, use a heat gun and light pressure with tweezers to lift the chip, then install it with flux and a soldering iron, but avoid applying too much pressure or drag. 5 ABS glue and an old soldering iron can be used to repair melted seams on systems. 6 A Pinecil is a good soldering iron; it costs 25 dollars. 7
