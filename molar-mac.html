Molar Mac

Claims: 12

The Molar Mac is an expandable G3 machine, more so than the iMac, and can accept a ZIF 1GHz G4 SONNET card. 123 It uses a standard desktop board and requires an external hard drive controller because its internal one is faulty. 4 Its CRT can be swapped with those from the Powermac/Performa 5XXX series, except for the 5260, including the 5200 CRT. 56 The CRT is sometimes unreliable, though the only issue the author's unit had was cold solder joints on the internal power connector. 7 The author's Molar Mac has a Mac OS 9 installation that suffered a system error and cannot find its own hard drive. 8
