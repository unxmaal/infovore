SX

Claims: 4

SX and DX refer to 16-bit and 32-bit data buses respectively, with SX also having a shortened address bus. 1 According to the Solaris 7 OpenGL guide, SX, GX, GX+, TGX, TGX+, and S24 cards perform OpenGL functionality in software. 2 The Aurora accelerators achieve 12.399 GFLOPS/W with an x86 host and SX using 14/16 nm technology, which is more efficient than 14.67 GFLOPS/W on 7 nm technology, offering about a 2X efficiency uplift. 3
