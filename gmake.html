gmake

Claims: 13

gmake is a version of GNU make, which is often called 'make' and can be renamed to 'gmake'; some installations, such as 'sgug', may install 'gnumake' or 'gmake' specifically. 1 gmake is a symlink to make, and the executable is located at /usr/sgug/bin/gmake, with related files at /usr/sgug/share/man/man1/gmake.1.gz and /usr/sgug/share/bash-completion/completions/gmake. 23 gmake can be built without automake and autoconf, and it is available for IRIX. 45 To compile and install software such as aewan on IRIX, one generally needs to use ./configure, gmake, and gmake install, though this can vary based on the environment; compiling with 'make -f Makefile.sgi' or 'gmake' is also required in some cases. 67 gmake can produce a 'missing separator' error when used with the mipspro compiler, and the gmake check for grep can fail on IRIX due to missing tests or system Perl differences. 89 gmake -j43 runs faster initially but then slower than gmake -j4, and using gmake instead of make did not resolve the issue. 1011
