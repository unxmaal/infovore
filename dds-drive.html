DDS drive

Claims: 8

Users have installed DDS drives in SGI systems, including an Indigo2 and an external unit connected to an Octane. 12 The DDS drive can be updated with DAT firmware to identify as ARCHIVE Python 01931-XXX5AC, but this requires a specific firmware for DAT support that is not universally available. 3 One user obtained a working DDS drive after three attempts, and it accepted the DAT firmware. 4 Turning on parity on the DDS drive resolved bus errors with the BlueSCSI. 5
