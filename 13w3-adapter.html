13W3 adapter

Claims: 14 Part of 13W3

The 13W3 adapter is compatible with both Sun and SGI systems, and is used for graphics in the Indigo2, where issues may persist despite reseating the graphics board, suggesting the adapter might be the problem. 12 The adapter has different variants with varying dip switch settings, and can be configured with or without a sync separator for RGBS and RGBHV, which is useful for monitors that expect composite sync on the horizontal sync pin. 345 SGI Indigo1s require a 13W3 adapter, a proprietary mouse/keyboard, or an active adapter, and possibly hard-to-find software packages, and both Indigo1 keyboards and 13W3 adapters are hard to find. 67 A 13w3 adapter costs around 10 euros and can be used to connect a vintage SGI Indy to a modern monitor. 8 Dells work with the Sun 13w3 adapter on an Octane even though it is not supposed to, and every 13w3 adapter used has worked just fine. 910
