SLES11

Claims: 21

ProPack 6 is available for SLES11 on x86_64 and IA64, and ProPack 7 is compatible with Altix 350 gear and can enhance SLES11 support on Altix and Prism systems. 12 SLES11 is not available for Itanium, though ProPack 7 ISOs for SLES11 on Itanium are available from the original Novell sources. 34 SLES11 does not support 3D graphics on SGI Prism but might support 2D, while it supports 3D graphics on Altix 4000 and an Altix 4700 might have had 2D graphics support. 5 The SLES 11 update repos provide access to the IA64 version of the *fglrx* driver package, which improves 3D performance and stability. 6 SLES11 can be installed but does not bring up graphics, and `radeonhd` crashes in SLES11 while `radeon` is still present but crashes. 78 Netbooting can install SLES11 on an Altix 350 without an IO9 card, and netboot sources for SLES11 are being prepared. 910 SLES11 runs on the Altix 350, can work with USB sound out of the box, and using VMs allows for easy setup of specialized environments like SLES11 for an Altix install server. 111213
