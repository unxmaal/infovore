040 CPU

Claims: 4 Part of 040 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see NeXTstation.

The 040 CPU can be used to upgrade the A2000, which is faster than a stock A2000. 1 The Color Classic 2 has a 040 CPU. 2 The NextStation supports 24-bit graphics with a 040 CPU. 3
