P3

Claims: 15 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Video games and Linux.

The P3 is a VME standard connector that is entirely user-defined, whereas P1 includes power, 8 data bits, 24 address bits, interrupt, arbitration, and an interrupt line, and P2 adds 8 more data bits, 8 more address bits, and 2 rows of user-defined pins. 1 The VME 9U board has three connectors labeled P1, P2, and P3, with P3 unused by the VME 32 standard but used by SGI for the MP bus. 2 The R14k and P3 share the same clock speed of 700 MHz, but the R14k performs more operations per cycle while the P3 wastes cycles on setup. 34 The P3 is slightly faster per clock than the P2, and the Athlon is slightly faster than the P3 for bus-heavy tasks. 5 Athlons usually outperform P3s at the same clock speed on RDRAM-intensive tasks, and a P3 with Rambus memory can benefit from it, though Rambus boards are hard to find. 67 A 2.0 Pentium 4 barely edged out a P3 1.4S, and a 1GHz Itanium 2 is roughly comparable to a 500MHz P3 in terms of performance as measured by Cinebench. 89 The P3 and Athlon were the closest competition between Intel and AMD until Ryzen 3 and later generations. 10 BeOS R5.0.3 runs on dual P3s. 11
