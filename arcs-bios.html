ARCS BIOS

Claims: 3 Part of ARCS

Quansheng UV-Kx radios are not professional quality; their RX front end lacks track-tuned band pass filtering and is wideband. 1 The ARCS BIOS is expected to save and restore the MIPS coprocessor state and handle exceptions over to the ARCS firmware, which NetBSD may not be doing. 2 The FPU window bug in the ARCS BIOS caused screen scrolling to hang. 3
