P2

Claims: 6

The VME 9U board has three connectors labeled P1, P2, and P3, with P1 being a VME standard with power, 8 data bits, 24 address bits, interrupt, arbitration, and an interrupt line; P2 extends with 8 more data bits, 8 more address bits, and 2 rows of user-defined pins; and P3 is all user-defined. 12 P3 is unused by the VME 32 standard but is used by SGI for the MP bus. 2 P3 is slightly faster per clock than P2, and Athlon is slightly faster than P3 for bus-heavy tasks. 3 P2 was a poor performer for floating-point work but was somewhat competitive for integer tasks. 4 PPro and P2 were very similar in performance. 5
