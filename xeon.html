Xeon

Claims: 19

The higher-end desktop Xeon processors are intended for powerful workstations without a GPU. 1 The T3500 was a pre-refresh Xeon machine. 2 ms supports certain Xeons but not contemporary Intel chips. 3 Virtualization and crypto features in the CPU are big limits for older Xeon processors. 4 HIPPI cards are available for G4 Xerves and regular Xeon boxes. 5 Some Opteron/Xeon systems from 10-15 years ago would be best for rendering tasks from an SGI. 6 It's a Supermicro P4DC6+ build with dual Xeon processors in Socket 603 and RDRAM, housed in an Intel SC5300 case. 7 The O200 had a Xeon processor; it was a stubby tower, model 1400. 8
