NVMe

Claims: 34 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see NVM Express, SD card.

NVMe is a storage interface that uses available PCIe lanes, unlike SATA/SAS, which are less constrained by them. 1 It delivers high performance, with higher-end drives such as the Rocket 4 Plus offering 6850MB/s sequential write, and over Fibre Channel providing 58% higher IOPS and 34% lower latency than SCSI-based Fibre Channel. 23 NVMe significantly improves disk I/O, making batch jobs run much faster on newer laptops and making video capture and editing more accessible. 45 It can be used for OS storage, with SATA reserved for other purposes such as VM storage. 6 SD Express is a form of NVMe in an SD card form factor, but with higher clock rates. 7
