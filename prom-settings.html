PROM settings

Claims: 4 Part of PROM

PROM settings can point to the wrong partition as root, which is partition 0 by SGI's standard, and the miniroot boots from the swap partition if IRIX hasn't used it for swap yet. 1 The Indy may default to a specific resolution and refresh rate despite using a known good monitor and cable setup, possibly due to PROM settings or corruption. 2 When cloning an SGI system, it's important to printenv after inserting the cloned disk to check for PROM settings that may not have transferred. 3 To preserve PROM settings, one should print them with 'printenv' and reset them with 'resetenv'. 4
