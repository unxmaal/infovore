SPARCbook

Claims: 15

The SPARCBook was a laptop made by SGI. 1 It ran Solaris 2.6, though this required Tadpole patches, and Solaris 7 was too heavy to run on it. 23 The display offered a resolution of 640x480 for low-end models and 800x600 for high-end models. 4 It could mount a 340MB ATA flash PCMCIA card with a DOS partition using a specific device name. 5 The machine suffered from packaging damage, magnesium corrosion inside, a partially functional screen, and keyboard problems including a damaged mylar. 67 The screen hinges could break and may need repair with JBWeld. 2
