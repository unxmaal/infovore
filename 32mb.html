32MB

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indy.

32MB was the norm for servers in 1997, and the existing memory was 32 MB. 12 IRIX 5.3 was optimized for 64MB of RAM and performed better than 5.1 on an Indy with 32MB, with 6.x recommended for 128MB or more. 3 IRIX 6.5.22 takes 15 minutes from jingle to login screen on a 100 MHz Indy with 32 MB. 4 Solaris 2.4 should be comfortable with 32MB, and the NeXT Dimension has an i860 processor and up to 32MB of RAM. 56 Upgrading from 16 MB to 32 MB resolved all problems. 7
