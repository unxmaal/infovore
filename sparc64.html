SPARC64

Claims: 4 Part of SPARC

SPARC64 is a 64-bit architecture, as opposed to the 32-bit SPARC32PLUS used by ArcInfo 9.3, while ArcInfo 10.0 uses SPARC64. 1 SPARC64 CPUs are faster per clock than UltraSPARC CPUs, though Sun did not upgrade UltraSPARC systems to PCIe and DDR RAM. 2 OpenBSD runs on SPARC64, and its install ISO is small and is enough to at least test things out. 34
