G4

Claims: 79 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Mac OS 9, Video games and Linux.

With G3

Deus Ex requires a fast G3 or G4 and a decent GPU, specifically an ATI Rage 128 or 3dfx, but runs well on modern OS 9 GPUs; it can be played on macOS with a Glide wrapper. 1 G4s are slower than G5s and G3s. 2 High-end G4s beat high-end G3s easily, and G5s beat both G4 and G3, including dual-core G5s. 345 A G3 can support up to 1GB of RAM, while a G4 can support more. 6 Many G3 and G4 towers had Zip drives. 7 The gap between a lower-end G4 and G3 is not significant. 8 G4 transplants are possible on G3 systems, but a G3 cannot be transplanted into a G4. 9 A 400 MHz G3/Rage 128 runs Quake III Arena with some settings turned down, an 800 MHz G4/Voodoo3 runs it playably, and dual 1.42 GHz G4s/9250 PCI run it smoothly. 10

With G5

Some open source software may not be compatible with all PowerPC processors, such as Apple's PowerPC G4 and G5. 11 The G5 supports PCIe GPUs, whereas the G4 does not, and the G5 is slightly faster than a G4 in 32-bit mode. 1213 G4s are all plastic, unlike the metal G5, and the last generation 'jet engine' G4s are noisy and not much better than lower end G5s. 1415 A G4 or G5 can run Linux or OS X. 16 A G4 or G5 with an Avid Adrenaline setup is a better box for post-production than an SGI. 17 The G4 or G5 Xserves struggle with NFS handling at gigabit speeds. 18 The Wii U, with 2 GB of RAM, a tri-core CPU, and Radeon graphics, places it between a G4 and G5 era system. 19

General

The G4 is a 32-bit PowerPC processor that runs Windows 95 on a Sun version of SoftPC at 800x600 resolution. 2021 Linux has altivec extensions for PPC and works well on the G4. 22 Gentoo can be built on a 733 MHz G4, and a dual G4 can be used for compiling. 2324 The G4's performance increased with higher frequencies, with the fastest G4 in a Mac being a 1.67 GHz G4 Powerbook. 25 IBM never made a G4. 26 The G4 can be used for MIDI, archiving VHS tapes, and potentially as a server, though the size limit makes the server impractical. 27 The RM5200 MIPS chip performs better than the G4's at the same clock speed. 28
