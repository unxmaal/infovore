SUSE

Claims: 9

SUSE came with many choices of packages on physical CDs. 1 SUSE and RHEL are the only options for operating systems on G3 iMacs. 2 Manjaro and SUSE have ARM distributions. 3 SUSE is nice on an Altix and is not preferred as a modern distro, but SLES is acceptable. 4 The radeonhd driver has limited capabilities but may be worth trying; it is SUSE-centric and existed on Debian. 5 A professionally developed *nix OS like SUSE can be as secure or more secure than Windows. 6 The Prism runs a special version of SuSE, with Red Hat as an optional CLI-only option. 7 The touchpad drivers are super weird; if SSH doesn't work, SUSE support will be contacted for help. 8
