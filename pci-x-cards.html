PCI-X cards

Claims: 7 Part of PCI-X Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Mac (computer), Peripheral Component Interconnect.

Matrox made PCI-X cards for Macs. 1 PCI-X cards are 3.3V and 64-bit, and there are also 32-bit PCI-X cards. 23 PCI-X cards cannot be directly used in a PCI slot. 4 PCI motherboards are still available, and PCI-X cards can work at reduced speed on them. 5 The system has issues with PCI-X cards causing a general exception. 6
