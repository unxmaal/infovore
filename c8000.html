C8000

Claims: 37

The HP C8000 is a high-end workstation that uses the zx1 chipset and 8800/8900 CPU, and is the only workstation to do so. 123 It is a PA-RISC machine rather than an Itanium machine, though it can accept an Itanium CPU. 43 The C8000 uses Itanium-style AGP cards and cannot use classic HP9000 cards, and it does not support AGP. 56 The C8000 has issues with its power supply, graphics card, IDE and SCSI controllers. 7 It can run FireGL X3 GPUs, but they may have issues with heat and support. 8 The C8000 can accept IDE hard drives on the primary controller, but may have issues booting with an IDE hard drive connected. 910 It can use a SCSI bus but has issues with SCA disks. 1112 The C8000 has issues with SGI_PARTITION support in Linux, which is related to the Linux kernel rather than the disk itself. 13
