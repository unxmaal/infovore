VCAM

Claims: 3

Linear regulators in a VCAM can fail, causing voltages to exceed twice their intended value, and may need to be replaced. 1 The Onyx1 has known issues with the VCAM, which has a 12V regulator that tends to fail, followed by power converters and the PSU. 2 The VCAM and power converters can be DIY fixed, but the PSU is complex and challenging to repair. 2 The VCAM on the Onyx's IO4 is the only place with -12VDC, and troubleshooting involves checking the Diagnostics Roadmap and power system, where the system controller's monitoring system can fail. 3
