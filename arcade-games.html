arcade games

Claims: 5

Pinball machines use custom PCBs that can be easily replaced when hardware fails, unlike some arcade games. 1 The shift to standard hardware in modern arcade games is driven by cost considerations, unlike the custom PCBs of the past. 2 Many early arcade games had multiple CPUs in addition to independent FM synth chips and such. 3 Arcade games are less picky about emulation accuracy compared to a full workstation OS, so the R5K core in the emulator is good enough to play arcade games but not to boot IRIX. 45
