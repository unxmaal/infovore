PCMCIA slot

Claims: 4 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Amiga 1200.

The PCMCIA slot supports WinNT/Win9x/OS/2/DOS. 1 The A1200 comes with a PCMCIA slot among its features, including internal IDE and an AGA chipset. 2 The U10 lacks a PCMCIA slot, as it is not officially documented and has no part or FRU number. 3
