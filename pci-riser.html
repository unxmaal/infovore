PCI riser

Claims: 21 Part of PCI

The PCI riser is attached to a cage with a metal tab that holds it onto the module cage, and the plastic cover needs to be removed to access it. 1 The PCI riser holds the Ethernet MAC address via a Dallas DS2505 IC, which stores the original Ethernet address and system serial number for licensing. 23 The MAC address on SGI machines can be used as a unique identifier for the system, often stored in specific hardware components like the frontplane, PCI riser, or ethernet riser, and it can also be programmable for O2. 4 The O2 has a system ID in the PCI riser which otherwise looks like bare PCB, and it complains about no MAC address if run without one. 5 Power for SCSI drives can be provided by a white connector on the PCI riser, which usually connects to a SCSI backplane. 6 A PCI riser with two slots requires a PCI to PCI bridge. 7
