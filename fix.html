Fix a mistake

Every page here is generated from the SGUG Discord archive. Nobody edits the pages by hand, and a hand edit would vanish at the next build. Fixes go into the generator, infovore on GitHub, as a pull request, and reach the site with the next build.

If you would rather not open a pull request, open an issue naming the page and quoting the text. Someone else will do the rest.

A claim is wrong

Find the claim under Sources at the bottom of its page. Note the exchange number and the statement, exactly as printed. Add an entry to corrections.toml:

[[claim]]
exchange = 1956
statement = "The Octane is more expandable than the O2."
drop = true

To reword the claim instead of removing it, put a replace line in place of the drop line:

replace = "The Octane has more expansion slots than the O2."

A dropped claim and every sentence that relied on it leave the page at the next build. A replaced claim shows its new wording under Sources at once, and the prose above it catches up on the next article run.

A topic has the wrong kind, the wrong name, or two spellings

Topics live in topics.toml. An entry names the topic and its kind: machine, cpu, graphics, os, software, part, standard, company or other. The redirects list holds spellings that should land on this page. The aliases list holds words that mean this topic when they appear in a claim.

[[topic]]
name = "Indigo2"
kind = "machine"
aliases = ["indigo2", "indigo 2"]
redirects = ["I2"]

A name that means several things gets a disambiguation entry listing its candidates:

[[disambiguation]]
name = "Ultra"
candidates = ["Ultra 5", "Ultra64", "SCSI"]

Anything else

A page that should not exist, a section that reads wrong, a broken link: open an issue. Who said what is pseudonymous by design and cannot be changed.
