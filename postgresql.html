PostgreSQL

Claims: 5

PostgreSQL is used almost exclusively with Ruby for HTTP services, though it is occasionally paired with MongoDB or Cassandra, and is considered more versatile than those alternatives. 1 The SGUG-RSE project ported various software, including PostgreSQL, to IRIX. 2 Btrieve, which was used before PostgreSQL, requires tuning and is slower out of the box, though it can be fast with tuning while being harder to manage than Oracle. 3 Oracle databases have a known history of difficult bug resolution compared to PostgreSQL and SQLite. 4 Postgres became PostgreSQL after gaining SQL support. 5
