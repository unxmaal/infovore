recapping

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Hard disk drive.

Hard drives require proper recapping to function correctly, and they pretty much all need recapping along with a rebuilt battery. 12 Rust damage can be mitigated with recapping and cleaning. 3 Octane's PSUs need recapping, and the Onyx2 is similar. 4 Recapping a PSU may not revive a broken one, as re-capping alone won't fix a broken one; re-capping and replacing faulty components are necessary. 56 Recapping SGI machines is quick if done before capacitors leak. 7
