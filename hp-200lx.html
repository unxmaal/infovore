HP 200LX

Claims: 3 Part of HP

The HP 200LX has an Intel 80186 CPU running at 7.91 MHz that can be overclocked to 15.8 MHz, with 1, 2, or 4 MB of memory, of which 640 KB is RAM and the rest can be used for expanded memory (EMS) or memory-based storage. 1 Forth 83 can run in DOS on the HP 200LX. 2 The batteries in the device can be removed with a screwdriver. 3
