AUI

Claims: 10

AUI is the name for 100Mbit Ethernet, and its port is often referred to as an AUI port, commonly used for connecting network interfaces. 12 AAUI and AUI are electrically compatible but not mechanically, and AAUI is superior to AUI but not standard. 34 AUI has an annoying feature where it needs to be manually un-initialized. 5 Classic and IPC machines use AUI and 10BaseT for Ethernet, respectively, and can support 100BaseT but are too slow for 100Mbit Ethernet. 6 The SS1 only has AUI. 7
