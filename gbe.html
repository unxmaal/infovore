GBE

Claims: 3

The GBE contains a PLL that generates a pixel clock up to 140 MHz for driving the pixel pipeline and video timing controller, with separate signals for CRT and flat panel displays. 1 IRIX supports GbE, and the O2 graphics implementation can start now that GBE draw routines are refactored. 2
