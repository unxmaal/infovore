MiSTer

Claims: 19

The MiSTer is a reimplementation of old hardware using hybrid emulation, which allows it to run the Linux OS and receive controller data from USB controllers. 12 It is an FPGA-based emulator option for ARM-based retro systems, and is considered amazing for systems with coprocessors or SMP. 34 The MiSTer project's emulation list includes the Mac Plus. 5 The chip in the MiSTer is considered low-end compared to other FPGAs, and low-end chips are used in the analogue part due to cost constraints. 6
