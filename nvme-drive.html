NVMe drive

Claims: 16 Part of NVMe

An NVMe drive can get hot, often requiring a heat sink or heat gumstick for cooling, and not every NVMe drive has 4 lanes—good ones do, but cheaper ones might have only 1 lane. 12 Adding an NVMe drive can change the device names of other interfaces due to their location on the PCI bus, and without bifurcation it can be tried in both slots of a card. 34 The IRIX kernel can potentially boot from an NVMe drive if the kernel has the necessary driver built in, and a setup with the root filesystem on a spinny disk and the rest on an NVMe drive is possible. 56 Booting from an NVMe drive without a spinner or TFTP server works if the system drive is pulled, changing the OSLoadPartition is not necessary, and the NVMe drive is seen on 2008SP6 but causes segmentation faults with sw_config. 78 Zapit is an Alias/Wavefront program for editing NTSC video with zero lag using an NVMe drive on an SGI O2. 9
