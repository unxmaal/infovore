SCSI termination

Claims: 5 Part of SCSI

SCSI termination is necessary to prevent data line interference and ensure proper function of the SCSI bus, though some drives can auto-terminate while external terminators may be required in certain cases. 1 Termination is required on the Maxtor Atlas 10K V hard disk drives because they do not support on-board SCSI termination. 2 The cable on older Crimson/Power series desksides is for SCSI termination, and the Blade 1000 can have issues with SCSI termination that may require checking the external SCSI bus. 34 Fixing SCSI termination issues ensures correct values in prom envs and proper device recognition in hinv. 5
