heatsink

Claims: 35 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Heat sink.

Heatsinks are used to cool various processors and electronics, ranging from small copper units without fans to large copper heatsinks weighing around 10 lbs and incorporating heat pipes. 12 Heatsinks may be attached using thermal adhesive or pads rather than thermal paste, with some pads being adhesive on both sides to glue a heatsink to a CPU. 34 A thermal adhesive in a tube can also be used to stick a heatsink on a CPU, though it takes about a day to set. 5 Some heatsinks are connected to the CPU via a thermal pad, while others are held in place with screws or retention clips. 67 Heatsinks can be modified using a band saw, cutoff wheel, or waterjet cutting. 8 Cleaning underneath a heatsink can be done with a qtip and 99% IPA or compressed air. 9
