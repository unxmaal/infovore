battery

Claims: 59 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Electric battery, Motherboard.

Batteries serve various functions across devices, including keeping RAM powered in case of failure, keeping time, and maintaining PRAM. 123 Some machines require a battery to boot, while others can still operate without one. 4567 A dead or flat battery can cause incorrect date issues, slow POSTing, prolonged boot processes, machine complaints, and fatal errors during sleep. 89101112 Batteries can leak and damage components, requiring replacement, cleaning, and recapping. 13141516 Battery types vary, including lithium camera batteries, alkaline packs, NiCd packs, and CR2032 coin cells. 171816 Some batteries are user-replaceable, while others require specialized kits or modifications. 191820 A common battery type, the 18650 cell, can be used as a replacement battery and can be harvested from old laptops. 21 The Atari TT030 has a battery in a plastic case that takes a few seconds to change. 22 The Indigo2's battery is not easily replaceable, which is considered lazy design given the cost of the machine. 23 The battery is a common failure in vintage SGI machines and can be fixed by replacing it or dremeling and replacing the battery. 24 Replacing the battery is necessary for the software driver to load in NVSIMMs. 25 A battery may be dead but have some charge remaining. 26
