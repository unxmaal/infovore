Ultra 60

Claims: 46 Background: 8 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Ultra 60, Debian.

The Ultra 60 has two CPUs, described as dual 450 MHz UltraSPARC IIe processors with 4 MB cache per CPU. 123 Graphics options include the Elite3D GPU, the Expert 3D, and the SunPCI III, and the machine can also use a Radeon 9250 PCI card. 14567 The Ultra 60 can output 1920x1200 resolution and supports UTF-8 during the Solaris installer. 89 The Ultra 60 uses SCSI rather than IDE, and its UPA interconnect delivers up to 1.92 GB/s to the processor and 960 MB/s to a graphics accelerator. 1011 The Ultra 60 has 64-bit PCI slots, including one 64-bit slot at 66 MHz. 1213 The Ultra 60 can be upgraded with multiple 440 MHz CPU cards for around $30, and Infiniband can be added using PCI-X cards. 1415 The Ultra 60 is faster than the Octane and more reliable than the user-5510 Blade. 1617 The Elite3D-m6 provides better texturing on the Ultra 60 than older hardware does. 18 The Indigo2Video can use the same 26-pin 3-row connector as the Galileo Box and Video Framer box. 19 The Elite3D on an Ultra60 can run without vsync, but the XVR600 on Solaris10 cannot. 20
