pfSense

Claims: 11

pfSense is used for firewall and network configuration, with some manual sync required between firewall and Unifi. 1 A Celeron machine running pfSense can handle router duties well, and pfSense with a Celeron G5905 can provide better ping times but slower speeds, with turning on CODEL making things faster. 23 Known issues include the DNS resolver stopping every few hours due to the unbound service stopping without reason, and high CPU usage with IPsec. 45 pfSense can be migrated to OPNsense for router functions, and VyOS is more powerful than pfSense but requires CLI proficiency. 67 pfSense runs virtualized on ESXi, but VMware is considered a dead end for future migration. 8
