CPMS

Claims: 3

CPMS is battery-backed RAM. 1 The CPMS jumper, when plugged in, prevents the power supply units from powering up unless the UPS is active, while disconnecting the jumper allows the system to power up directly from the PSU. 2 The solution to revive batteries is to disable CPMS. 3
