Challenge XL

Claims: 10 Part of Challenge

The Challenge XL is a large multi-CPU capable machine, about the size of two back-to-back standard 19-inch racks, that fits through a standard door with a little clearance. 12 It has over 100 SCSI channels and various HIPPI, FDDI, and Gig Ethernet connections, with SCSI, HIPPI, and ATM VME boards for disks, content generation, and network communication. 34 Challenge XLs could bridge Power Path 2 busses. 5 Rare used a Challenge XL for DKC graphics work, and it had graphical output. 6 A Challenge XL is used for graphics work in Digital Color Kinetics' projects, and was the best machine for rendering at the time. 78
