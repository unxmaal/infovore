GX

Claims: 5

The GameCube uses a proprietary API called GX that is similar to OpenGL but not the same. 1 The Solaris 7 OpenGL guide indicates that SX, GX, GX+, TGX, TGX+, and S24 cards perform OpenGL functionality in software. 2 The A3000UX shipped with the A2410 graphics card, comparable in capability to systems like the Sun GX or SPARCStation IPC. 3 A GX and GT in the same machine would be ideal for desktop use, especially with a SS2. 4 There are minor differences between GX and GS models, including screen and CPU frequency. 5
