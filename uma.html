UMA

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Graphics processing unit.

UMA (Unified Memory Access) systems prioritize RAM configured for scanline output to meet display timing requirements, balancing this with CPU access, as seen in SGI's VLB design. 1 The Nintendo 64's RCP runs a MIPS R4k series core and uses SGI UMA. 2 Offloading 3D and imaging operations to the GPU can improve usability on UMA systems by accelerating the imaging and rendering pipelines, as done on the Octane and Onyx. 3 The SGI O2 was valued for its UMA, easy video options, small footprint, and CDROM. 4 The O2 never received a full CRM upgrade, only chip revisions, and the UMA concept at SGI was abandoned due to failed 18-month upgrade promises. 5
