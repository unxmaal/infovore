Intel CPUs

Claims: 5 Part of Intel Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see XFS.

Standard off-the-shelf Intel CPUs can have up to 28 cores per socket and up to 8 sockets in a machine, but linking them as a single system is only useful in very specialized applications. 1 Intel CPUs used in Premiere had speed limitations due to software optimizations, saw limited speed improvements, and took time to release newer models. 2 When Intel CPUs first came out, a Unix-certified OS ran Windows at native speed in a VM on fantastic hardware. 3 Exploits targeting workstations often rely on social engineering rather than remote exploitation, impacting performance especially on Intel CPUs but less so on AMD. 4 XFS has issues with large disk sizes on specific Intel CPUs from Sandybridge and earlier. 5
