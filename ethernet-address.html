Ethernet address

Claims: 9 Part of Ethernet

The Ethernet address is stored in non-volatile RAM (NVRAM) and is read during controller initialization, but a corrupted address may require a Dallas chip upgrade or manual setting of the MAC address. 1 A bad serial and Ethernet address can prevent OpenBSD from booting, and manually setting them to correct values resolves the issue. 2 The Ethernet address can be obtained by configuring Prom to bootp and reading the bootp logfile, or by using Booterizer to force a bootp request as seen in tftpd log entries. 34 The hostid and Ethernet address can be reprogrammed, the first two numbers indicate the machine type, and the hostid/MAC address is often noted on the NVRAM chip for newer machines. 5 The location of the PROM holding the Ethernet address can cause problems when swapping mainboards, and the R3000 can be upgraded to an R4000 CPU board with the PROM routine copying the Ethernet address to the new location, though downgrading to R3000 is not easily possible. 67 A dead NVRAM chip causing a wrong Ethernet address can prevent booting diagnostics over the network, and fixing NVRAM and resetting the Ethernet address might help on an Indy. 89
