NIS

Claims: 7

NIS is used alongside NFS to provide services to a range of systems, including SGIs, FreeBSD, IRIX, Solaris, and HP-UX machines. 12 Early Unix machines often had filesystems automounted off NIS, and autofs works on SGI with NIS, whereas FreeBSD does not support autofs. 34 Old Unix machines sometimes attempt to resolve names via DNS or NIS, even for localhost, and may have NIS configured in resolv.conf and nsswitch.conf. 56 Using a VM for the NIS primary and a physical machine for the secondary is a reliable approach. 7
