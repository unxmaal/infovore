IP17

Claims: 7 Part of IP

IP17 is the version for Crimson, a power series that uses a different CPU board, for which an IP7 or IP15 can be substituted for the IP17. 12 ip17 stopped outputting over serial and never brought up VGA after the clearing line, without throwing an unknown graphics error. 3 Their Octane V10 has a dead GPU but works in an IP17. 4 An IP17 can be upgraded from 100 to 150 MHz by replacing the CPU and a ROM with CPU boot parameters; the ROM to replace has part# 070-1200-001 / BD20 and is not an EPROM, and the blue wire mods are not related to the upgrade. 5 IP7 with GTX uses 1000w, IP17 with RE uses 1100w, and RE is more efficient per watt. 6 Boards 5 and 6 are graphics boards, possibly RM, while CPU boards are IP17 and IP19. 7
