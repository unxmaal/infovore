9595

Claims: 4

The IBM 9595 is a PS/2 board that shares a bezel notch with a button or door catch on a different drive, possibly an older caddy-loader. 12 It has a unique bidirectional high-speed parallel port and a second standard port. 3 The parallel port can be configured for bidirectional or EPP mode, and the DOS driver works on the first port. 4
