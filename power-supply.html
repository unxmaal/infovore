power supply

Claims: 144 Background: 12 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Power supply unit (computer), Capacitor, Central processing unit, Graphics card, SGI Octane.

With O2

The O2 can be sensitive to RAM seating and power supply issues, and it can red light due to problems with the power supply, CPU, or other components like the PIMM or MRE. 12 The O2's power supply has a yellow line outputting 3.43v and a red line outputting 5v, and its front connector is a 24-pin ATX with a different pinout. 3 The O2 power supply is original and may need recapping, and a new disk experiencing XFS file system errors after a few days may indicate issues with the power supply or power in general. 45 The O2 power supply can fail to power up/down, and the power on jumper on the motherboard might be the cause; setting the jumper to the correct state can resolve the issue if the power button is prone to sticking. 6 The O2 can use a spare power supply, so having backups is helpful, and a replacement power supply did not fix the O2, meaning the original one might have been working. 78 To safely remove or install the motherboard from an O2, always disconnect the power supply first. 9

With Octane

  • The screenshots show the processors failing a self test. (user-5926, exchange 38020, 2023-05-15; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • A certain Octane2 purchased from eBay had its PSU handle removed. (user-8de9, exchange 33044, 2022-09-29; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Mystery Octane dead-on-arrival ratio has been around 80% for years, mostly due to power supplies, mainboards, or frontplanes. (user-b7c4, exchange 3469, 2019-09-10; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The Octane had issues with certain black handled power supplies, but not all, and it's recommended to use a silver one for reliability. (user-6d41, exchange 5695, 2020-01-24; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Rotating Octane power supplies may reduce the use of each PSU and is easy to do, though it's debatable. (user-17cb, exchange 98985, 2021-05-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • An Octane power supply can be found on eBay for a lower price but may be in poor condition. (user-b1da, exchange 204358, 2020-07-18; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The Octane can use an adapter board to replace its power supply, which is often dead, and can have up to 4GB of RAM with proprietary sticks. (user-b7c4, exchange 210260, 2024-04-20; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The Octane's power supply is proprietary and can be easily swapped with another Octane's PSU. (user-baf6, exchange 33044, 2022-09-29; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))

General

The Octane's power supply is proprietary and can be easily swapped with another Octane's PSU, though it may have issues with certain black handled units, so a silver one is recommended for reliability. 1011 Common Octane problems include compression connectors and reseating the power supply to help. 12 A leaking capacitor can cause voltage ripple but the power supply might still work, and an Indy's power supply can fail due to leaking electrolytic capacitors, which can be repaired by replacing the caps. 1314 The RDI PowerLite laptop had a heavy power supply that could fail on startup but worked intermittently. 15 The 4D/35 uses a power supply that can melt and fail due to lightning strikes. 16 The power supply fan did not spin and there was no power good signal, 5V, 10V, or 3.5V detected. 17 The power supply provides 5V at 120A, two channels of +12V at 15A each, and -12V, with -5V at 15A for standby. 18 Power supply problems are a common cause of issues in various systems, often involving faulty capacitors, unregulated output, or rail failures. 19202122 Symptoms may include failure to boot, presence test failures at specific codes, or image fuzz caused by power supply ripple. 232422 Diagnosis typically involves checking power supply voltages, ensuring proper seating with no bent pins, and reseating cards. 252627 Repair options include rebuilding the power supply due to capacitor issues, recapping capacitors, or replacing it with a modern or custom unit. 2829303132
