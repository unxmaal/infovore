SuperSPARC modules

Claims: 4 Part of SuperSPARC

Uncached SuperSPARC modules run at the speed of the Mbus and are synchronous with the Mbus clock, while modules with cache are asynchronous and clocked by the module itself. 12 SS10 has a 40MHz Mbus, and SS20 has its Mbus run at 50MHz. 1 SS10 motherboard jumpers configure the SuperSPARC module to run at either 40MHz or 36MHz (newer model) or 40MHz or 33MHz (model 20), with 40MHz recommended unless using very old modules. 3 The SuperSPARC modules produce a lot of heat, especially in dual configurations, and can cause thermal issues or hardware failures in SPARCstation systems. 4
