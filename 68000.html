68000

Claims: 9 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Raspberry Pi.

The 68000's instruction set architecture is considered elegant, unlike x86, but the 68000 is much more complicated in every way than simpler designs such as the 6502, which can be implemented with fewer components, and RV32I, which is the easiest instruction set for a human to understand and implement. 12 The 68000 did not have paging, but the 68010 fixed that (mostly), and it handles page faults by resetting the CPU. 34 The 68000 can be found in DIP64 packages, and a 16 MHz 68000 daughter board can be modded or built from scratch. 45 An 8 MHz 68000 does about 1.4 MIPS, making it about 50 to 100% faster than a VAX 11/750, and you can do near instantaneous approximations of Mandelbrot sets at low resolution and low fidelity on a 68000. 67 A Raspberry Pi has 16 1 GHz cores, significantly more powerful than an 8 MHz 68000. 8 The JJ has a 68000 CPU with an EPROM and SRAM, and its serial port is connected to a SCN68681 DUART. 9
