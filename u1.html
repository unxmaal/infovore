U1

Claims: 10

The Sun UltraSPARC U1 is a slab-style Ultra, distinct from the big slab U2, the small tower U10, and the slabs U5, B100, and B150. 1 The U1 can run Solaris 9, while the U2 can run Solaris 10. 2 The U1 uses a 167 MHz CPU and retains the 10Mbit AMD Lance chip, and a U1 with UPA has a different board layout. 345 The U2 improved significantly over the U1 with better CPU options, more RAM, faster disks, and 24-bit accelerated graphics without sacrificing a RAM slot. 6 The U1 was used as a server, never used the Sun monitor, and was eventually replaced by Ultra 10s. 7 The U1 and U2 plastics degrade over time, while the U5-80 plastics yellow. 8 Chemical treatments designed for car plastics are ineffective at restoring brittle plastic parts. 9 A U1 could often be obtained for a lower price than an SS10 or SS20. 10
