320

Claims: 25 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Visual Workstation.

The SGI 320 is a bespoke x86 workstation design, unlike other models that are rebadges. 12 It uses O2-derived Cobalt graphics, which include onboard 1600sw support. 34 The 320 can boot a 320-compatible HAL and slot in 1 GHz processors for cheaper than other models. 56 It has three PCIe slots, a SCSI controller card, and a bracket for the riser, but lacks the additional connector for drives underneath. 78 The 320, 330, and 550 share the basic x86 box case. 9
