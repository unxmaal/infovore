SD card

Claims: 52 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SD card.

SD cards can be used as SCSI devices in various setups, though they may produce random read/write errors possibly due to the card or filesystem issues. 12 Random access on an SD card can be much faster than from spinning rust, even on a bad card, and performance can be improved with a class 10 or better card. 34 SD cards can be imaged and restored easily in Linux, and dd can be used to write an IRIX disk image to an SD card if the card is formatted correctly and the image is in the correct format. 56 Formatting the SD card can resolve various issues, including fragmentation, error patterns, and performance problems, though some images may not be compatible with all SD cards. 78910 SD cards can be used to boot systems, run different IRIX versions, and clone installations, though benchmarks show ZuluSCSI with an SD card can be slower than the original HDD. 111213 SD cards are less durable than physical drives, and some setups, such as the gotek firmware, only support partition-based images and may fail POST with certain cards. 1415
