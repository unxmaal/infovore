256MB

Claims: 7

Several systems support a maximum of 256 MB of memory. The Indy accepts up to 256 MB, and the 360 has a POWER1+ CPU at 50 MHz with a maximum of 256 MB. The memory was maxed out to 256 MB, with boards slotted in from the back and compatible with larger Sun 3xx series machines. 123
