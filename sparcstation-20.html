SPARCstation 20

Claims: 28 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARCstation 20.

The SPARCstation 20 can run NextStep, which can be installed easily on inexpensive SPARC machines like the SPARCstation 20, though NextStep runs on the SPARCstation 20 but not on HyperSPARC. 123 The SPARCstation 20 and SPARCserver 20 are otherwise identical. 4 The SPARCstation 20 can have an odd number of RAM slots with a VSIMM installed, leading to some interesting amounts like 400 MB with 64 * 6 + 16 RAM slots, and SX24 is the VSIMM option in the SPARCstation 20. 56 Solaris 2.6 runs well on an emulated SPARCstation 20 after formatting disks, Solaris 2.3 runs on a SPARCstation 20 with a 75 MHz SuperSPARC-II and 128 MB RAM, and SunOS 5.7 runs on SunW,SPARCstation-20 hardware. 789 A battery mod similar to one done on a Sun SPARCstation 20 might be necessary for the Origin 300, and a boot ROM command allows booting NextStep with a dead NVRAM battery on a SPARCstation 20; it involves setting specific values and sometimes requires multiple attempts. 1011 The UltraSPARC II processor in the SPARCstation 20 can give an error code on the keyboard. 12 The Zulu SCSI on the SPARCstation 20 features 10Mb/s read/6Mb/s write speed, matching the theoretical max of the SS20 SCSI controller. 13 The O2, Indy, Octane, SPARCStation 20, Alpha DS10, and x86 Dell all work flawlessly with VGA cables that have only colors and sync but no EDID. 14
