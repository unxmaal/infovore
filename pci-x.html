PCI-X

Claims: 39 Background: 15 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see PCI-X.

PCI-X is a 64-bit bus that is backwards compatible with PCI, though most PCI-X cards are 64-bit and 3.3V only. 123 PCI-X doubles the maximum clock speed of conventional PCI from 66 MHz to 133 MHz, increasing the theoretical maximum data exchange between the processor and peripherals to 1.06 GB/s. 4 PCI-X is typically 100 or 133 MHz, and is faster than the shared PCI bus. 56 PCI-X and PCI64 devices can identify themselves with vendor IDs, but SGI's kernel lacked drivers for them. 7
