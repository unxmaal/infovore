ISDN port

Claims: 4 Part of ISDN

The Indy has an ISDN port on the back, but ISDN is a digital system and cannot directly dial into an analog POTS network. 1 The proximity of the Ethernet port to the ISDN port can cause analog POTS ring voltage to damage the ISDN port. 2 The ISDN port is covered with a sticker due to missing FCC approval. 3
