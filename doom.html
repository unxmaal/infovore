Doom

Claims: 61 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Doom (1993 video game), Quake (video game), Blender (software).

Doom can be run on a wide variety of machines, including the SGI Octane, Indy, O2, and RS/6000, as well as the Dreamcast, Falcon, and 3DO. 1234567 It can also run on RISC-V boards, and a stripped-down version can run on the TI-92, though the TI-92 has insufficient RAM to run standard Doom. 8910 Doom requires a 386-class CPU or higher and 4MB of RAM, and uses fixed-point math for its 3D effects, which doesn't require an FPU. 1112 It performs poorly on a 486/66 with ISA graphics, but better on a 486/100 with VLB graphics. 13 The 3DO port was difficult and rushed, and its music is considered good. 147 The Saturn port was software-rendered due to hardware acceleration issues, and John Carmack insisted on this due to affine texture mapping artifacts. 1516 Doom has been ported to and run on a wide range of hardware, including the SGI L2 system controller, the Indigo2 (using software rendering only), the Atari Falcon (with real-time MIDI music and animated textures), the Amiga, the Rigol (with a Windows-N installation and a wifi dongle), and a cobalt RaQ running SLACKWARE 14.2. 171819202122 Several ports of DOOM to IRIX exist beyond those shipped on demo CDs, including a GL accelerated version available through some FTP sites, and IRIX 6.5 applications ship basic demos on the foundations discs that include doom. 232425 Performance varies by platform: Doom ran poorly on a 68030 with 882 and was awful on an 040 2000, while the SGI port is considered fun even if not great. 262728 Doom's PS1 version uses a technique to split walls into 1px-wide columns for perfect perspective correction because the view cannot be tilted. 29 Doom was developed on full Next hardware, while Quake was developed on Nextstep running on Intel. 30 Doom's performance on PC versus Mac is unfairly compared due to Doom's design around PC-specific features. 31 Netware and IPX/SPX were used for networking in the 90s, with Doom over IPX/SPX causing network outages. 32
