Solaris 2.5

Claims: 6 Part of Solaris

Solaris 2.5 can be run on the Ultra 1. 1 The HME card works with Solaris 2.5 and later. 2 Adobe Photoshop for Solaris 3.0.1 is compatible with Solaris 2.5, requires approximately 32MB of disk space, and supports Sparc v7 and v8 chips but may not work with different window managers or zones/containers. 3 Basilisk II has been ported to Solaris 2.5, among other systems. 4 For Solaris 2.5, the partition size is up to the user, whereas for SunOS 4 the maximum partition size is 2GB. 5
