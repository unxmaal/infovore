Video Toaster

Claims: 15 Part of video

The Video Toaster is an NTSC-specific SGI hardware add-on for video processing that included a Targa Truevision video card and lacked 3D acceleration. 1234 It was a cheap solution for amateur or B-tier production and was used by community and local government studios rather than major TV stations. 56 The Video Toaster was finicky, requiring precise calibration and steady voltages, and had a dust issue severe enough to require cleaning. 78 On Amigas, the Video Toaster was a significant feature that sparked interest, though PC-based alternatives diminished that interest. 91011 Discreet FX bought out NewTek's Video Toaster rights and open-sourced some of the code, but some is lost. 12
