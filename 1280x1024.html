1280x1024

Claims: 7

The Dell 4:3 flat panels commonly found in goodwills and schools use 1280x1024 resolution. 1 1280x1024 is considered the sweet spot for retro kit and SGI resolutions. 23 The O2 can run at 1280x1024 without issues, and Irix was designed around this resolution. 45 The O2 is limited to 1440x900, 1280x1024, or 1600x1024 for flat panels, cannot do 1600x1200, and cannot run 1920x1080, though 1920x1200 works well. 64 The V6/V8 has a pixel clock bug. 6 A common approach is to set a custom resolution to 1280x1024 and then set the window to twice that. 7
