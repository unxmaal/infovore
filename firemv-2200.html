FireMV 2200

Claims: 3

The FireMV 2200 is not designed for 3D gaming and only supports basic desktop 3D functionality. 1 The ATI driver for Debian 6 did not work properly with the FireMV 2200. 2 The FireMV 2200 is not supported by the SLES 9 and SGI ProPack kernels, causing X to crash with 'cannot read V_BIOS' even with the radeon module. 3
