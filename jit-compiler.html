JIT compiler

Claims: 8 Part of JIT

The JIT compiler can have variable performance, sometimes slower and sometimes faster, depending on the amount of code and TLB cache limits, and manually flushing the JIT cache can improve performance. 1 The Jit compiler for Rex3 is marginally faster than the interpreter but can bog down the CPU under heavy GPU use, causing the fasttick to slow down significantly. 2 The JIT compiler works on basic ALU blocks and extends to loads and stores if they do not diverge or cause exceptions, with a limit of about 30 instructions to avoid high latency interrupts. 3 Optimizing return values from bus transactions could improve performance in a JIT compiler. 4 The JIT compiler had a bug where the second instruction in the PROM BD slot was not correctly jumping to the BD target, causing it to fall through. 5 The JIT compiler was miscompiling instructions, leading to issues with processes like xdm, 4Dwm, and clogin getting stuck sleeping and unkillable, which was resolved by fixing 512K aliasing for lowmem. 6 Stopping and restarting the graphics system can resolve an idle loop issue, possibly related to the Just-In-Time (JIT) compiler or cache, on IRIX. 7
