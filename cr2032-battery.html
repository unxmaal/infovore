CR2032 battery

Claims: 11

The CR2032 battery is a 3V cell that has been used in PCs for decades to hold CMOS, while the CR1225 is also used but may be harder to find. 1 The R3K Indigo uses a socketed CR2032 battery with an elastic strap, while the R4K uses a soldered connection. 2 The battery holder for a CR2032 battery resembles a crystal oscillator. 3 The CR2032 battery does not maintain a consistent voltage over its lifetime, and street lithium batteries have much higher capacity than CR batteries. 4 The CR2032 battery can leak in IBM PSValuePoint 425Sx/Si computers. 5 A CR2032 battery produces a 1-2-1 beep sequence and no settings are saved. 6 The PS2 Tool requires a CR2032 battery, and if the battery is dead, it will not boot until a keyboard and monitor are connected to the SBC, but replacing the battery is only worthwhile if you plan to boot it frequently. 7 A CR2032 battery replacement did not resolve the 'tod error' issue. 8
