DDS

Claims: 3

DAT or DDS is natively supported in IRIX, while other formats may require drivers that may or may not exist, and AIT and DLT are also options. 1 LTO was preferred over DDS for larger archives, though it was expensive. 2 To update a DDS drive's firmware, one needs a DOS bootable USB with drivers for a SCSI card and Seagate's firmware update program. 3
