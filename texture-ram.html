texture RAM

Claims: 14 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2, SGI Octane.

Q3 required 4 MB of texture RAM, more than the 1 MB available on the Impact card, while the ESI has 1 MB of texture RAM. 12 O2 texture RAM is expandable, and the O2 with 1 GB of RAM had more texture RAM than most systems until the 2010s. 34 Setting textures to low detail and 16-bit can fit within 4 MB texture RAM on an EMXI. 5 The SI/SSI/SE/SSE cards without texture RAM are decent for non-textured objects but require texture RAM for textured objects. 6 The RS/6000 800P with texture RAM is the best slot adapter for MCA RS/6000. 7 Using firewire for IRIX display requires texture RAM, and an X11 fallback would be an option but slower. 8 Textures are commonly used for remote desktop on SGIs but require significant texture RAM. 9 A stock N64 has 4 MB of texture RAM, which is its total RAM capacity. 10
