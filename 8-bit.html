8-bit

Claims: 9

  • The 8bits has a PBI for fast serial, but cannot use the SIO bus for this purpose. (user-f229, exchange 2419, 2019-07-10; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The 800's memory modules are expandable, while the later 8bits had no internal memory. (user-f229, exchange 2419, 2019-07-10; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • But then, relatively few 8-bit machines interest me much. (user-7b1c, exchange 7575, 2020-04-17; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • 8-bit systems had poor keyboards. (user-f229, exchange 168246, 2021-08-12; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • For an 8-bit system, a buffer and a hot swap voltage controller can manage hot swapping, with software handling on the OS side. (user-5510, exchange 30561, 2022-06-29; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Graphics are impressive but limited to 8-bit. (user-03ad, exchange 177623, 2024-02-05; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • 8-bit machines are slower but potentially better for certain tasks. (user-ae91, exchange 46573, 2024-06-09; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • A 12-bit flat framebuffer wouldn't work well since it's not byte-packed/aligned; 8 bits would work better, and palletizing is just something you have to do with 8 bits. (user-8203, exchange 126988, 2025-01-31; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The default Xorg root window was often 8-bit with dithering on older systems, and this was done for a reason related to window decorations by 4dwm. (user-231e, exchange 66248, 2026-09-05; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
