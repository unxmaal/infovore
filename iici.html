IIci

Claims: 19

The IIci is a 32-bit clean Macintosh with a 25 MHz CPU that supports 040 upgrades or cache cards, 128 MB of RAM without Mode32, and 3 Nubus slots. 12 It has onboard graphics with shared lower bank memory, providing 64k-320k VRAM and supporting 640x480 at 8 bit. 34 The IIci can run A/UX, which requires a IIci with a portrait display, and with the Appearance Manager can make it look like OS 8. 567 The Quadra 700 is not compatible with A/UX on anything slower than a IIci, and the Q700 outperforms the IIci unless the IIci is upgraded. 89 The IIcx and IIci have similar hardware, but the IIcx is rarer and may have issues with its ROM or startup circuit. 10 An SE/30 or IIci is much more capable than classics. 11
