AmigaOS

Claims: 15

AmigaOS was closer to OS/2 than to classic Mac or DOS, and was better than TOS or Macintosh System 1. 12 It had 90% of its core libraries in ROM and was user friendly despite being a UNIX system, though it is less sane than UNIX. 345 It does not include a TCP/IP stack by default, requiring third-party installation such as the paid Roadshow. 67 Various versions have quirks and bloat, with 3.1 from 1994 being preferred by some, and post-3.1 releases were community-driven. 89
