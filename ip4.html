IP4

Claims: 9 Part of IP

The IP4 used the R2000/R2300 CPU running at 12.5 MHz, as there was no R3000 CPU for the IP4. 1 Diagnostics 4.05J and 5.2 are platform-specific and may not work on the IP4, while user-1a5a Diagnostics should work. 2 The IP4 uses the same keyboard protocol as the ip6, ip12, and ip20, but with a different pinout. 3 The IP4 can be emulated with different firmware and CPU clock speed, retains audio connectors and discrete logic, and has a hard-coded interrupt acknowledge status of 0xc1. 4 The 'Server' graphics system lacks graphics capabilities, and the Professional IRIS is a type of IP4 that fits in a 12-slot VME chassis. 5 The 4D/60T is an upgrade for existing 4D/60 owners, replacing the R2300 MBC with an IP4, while the 4D/70 uses an SBC chassis with the same IP4 and SCSI. 6
