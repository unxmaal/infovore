164LX

Claims: 4

The 164PC and 164LX mainboards are compatible with single CPU machines. 1 The 164LX manual specifies a maximum of 128 MiB per module, though some systems like the PWS 500au can use 256 MiB modules. 2 A 164LX system with 1 GB RAM (4 * 256 MiB) was functional over 20 years ago and required ECC RAM. 3 The PWS requires different RAM modules than the 164LX. 4 The PSU should handle the ratings, but the board should be placed on a non-conductive base like cardboard when powered with direct current at max 12 V. 4
