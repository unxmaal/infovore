Y2K

Claims: 6

Several computer systems had Y2K compliance problems: the MIPS Magnum was not Y2K compliant and required 4-digit years in the operating system, the NetPower could accept 4-digit years in the operating system but not in the BIOS, and the PROM of older IRIX systems, including 4.x, was not Y2K compatible, causing issues. 123 Windows 3.11 could be patched to support Y2K and ISO-8859-15. 4 The Y2K issue was less eventful than expected due to extensive patching in advance, and SkyTrain simulated Y2K weeks before the event while BCLC did a dry run on the old Spectra terminal system. 56
