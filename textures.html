textures

Claims: 14

SGI systems were optimized for geometry performance, while consumer cards focused on textures. 1 SGI textures support border color and can be sorted by texture if memory is insufficient, while bilinear and trilinear filtering requires more sampling but not more texture memory. 2 Textures are commonly used for remote desktop on SGIs, but require significant texture RAM. 3 IMPACT machines with 1 MB of TRAM only support up to 16-bit textures, and the Impact requires the height and width of textures to be the same, with 256x256 textures used for 3dfx compatibility. 45 Video players can use glDrawBuffers or textures to draw video, with glDrawBuffers being faster on older hardware. 6 Some models can be optimized by grayscaling textures and using vertex colors for coloration. 7 Some N64 models converted from quads to triangles for performance can misalign textures, leading to visual issues like stretched or broken textures, especially on full quad textures such as pillars in Pokémon Stadium. 8 The textures from the Ultra-64 demos are likely the same as those from Mario 64 and not higher resolution. 9
