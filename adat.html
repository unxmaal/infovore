ADAT

Claims: 9

ADAT is an 8-channel audio protocol that requires a receiver to convert to analog I/O. 1 ADAT is used in professional audio setups but is not suitable for home audio use. 2 Behringer ADA8200 is a cheap audio interface that can convert ADAT to XLR inputs. 3 Optical ports can support ADAT, which can be used with Pd for audio processing. 4 ADAT optical devices are limited to the RAD audio on IRIX, and for ADAT with RAD audio, specific initialization is often required, especially on light pipe interfaces, with SGI using the Lucid interface. 56 The Tezro class uses ADAT for digital audio input, similar to the Lucid ADA 8824. 7 Nuendo had a prototype for the Octane due to ADAT, but it was not released due to high maintenance costs and a smaller target market compared to Mac. 8
