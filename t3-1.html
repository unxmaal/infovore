T3-1

Claims: 9

The T3-1 supports Solaris 11.3 but not later versions, and it would be a good candidate for porting OpenIndiana. 12 The T3-1 has 128 threads, is 10G Ethernet capable, and takes SATA drives. 34 Installing 64 GB of RAM in a T3-1 nearly doubled the peak bandwidth to 20 GB/s from 12 GB/s. 5 The T3-1 is relatively noisy compared to other rack-mounted servers such as the HP DL385 and Xserves, making it unsuitable for quiet environments like offices or bedrooms. 6
