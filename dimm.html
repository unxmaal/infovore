DIMM

Claims: 32 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see DIMM.

DIMM stands for Dual In-Line Memory Module. 1 DIMM is preferred over SIMM for easier removal and better clips. 2 The SIMMs are 4Mx4 chips, so each DIMM with 16 chips has 32MB of DRAM. 3 A capacitor was knocked off a DIMM, likely during packaging, and can be replaced to fix the issue. 4 DIMM errors render the system unusable. 5 A soft ECC error was detected on one DIMM, which needs to be retested after removal. 6 Cleaning and lubing DIMM and card edge contacts with DeOxit Gold is a routine maintenance practice. 7 A missing bank or DIMM would explain the complaint about contiguous memory, so cleaning and re-seating them is recommended. 8
