Lightwave

Claims: 34 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see LightWave 3D, Amiga.

LightWave was originally an Amiga program that was later ported to SGI, Mac, and Windows, and was used for VFX in TV shows and films such as Babylon 5 and Voyager. 12345 It was a primary application for FPU use in the 90s and required an FPU. 67 LightWave for IRIX is a boxed program that uses a custom file selector (fsel) instead of standard OS file selectors, and it breaks with fsel/segfault issues after IRIX 6.5.22 that can be fixed with a custom fsel. 891011 Rendering scenes on LightWave could take weeks. 12
