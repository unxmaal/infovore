R16000A

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see R8000.

The R16000A is a quad 1GHz processor, and its CPU configuration includes dual IP59s. 1 The R16000A was fabbed by NEC. 2 The R16000A has more than double the transistor count of the R8000, which had 2.6 million transistors and measured 299.98 mm². 3
