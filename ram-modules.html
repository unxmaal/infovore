RAM modules

Claims: 9 Part of RAM

The Altix 350 requires at least four RAM modules for operation. 1 Optane NVDIMMs and regular RAM modules are not interchangeable, and specific combinations are allowed. 2 There is no visual distinction between 16MB and 8MB RAM modules. 3 Resetting the BIOS CMOS can fix USB port functionality issues, and corrupted file downloads can be caused by bad RAM modules. 4 After thorough testing, the RAM modules are confirmed not to be the issue. 5 The RAM modules are likely 4 MB. 6 The Indigo2 IMPACT R10k supports up to 1 GB of RAM unofficially using specific RAM modules, but official support is 768 MB with 64 MB sticks. 7 The modules I made will probably cost about half of that. 8
