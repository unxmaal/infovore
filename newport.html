Newport

Claims: 11

The Newport is a graphics board that must be inserted into the correct riser slot, otherwise issues may arise, and the ip28 PROM does not drive it, though IRIX can. 12 The Newport has primitives for accelerating trapezoid fills with a bitpattern from z test and doing shading interpolation, but has no directly mapped framebuffer. 3 The DDC on Newport for RGB and alpha interpolates pixel values in CI mode, with RGB DDA values wrapping on under/overflow, limiting certain effects. 4 A dual head board is an 8 bit Newport. 5 The Indy can boot from PROM; the Newport has buggy SCSI and Ethernet controllers, which are slightly faster than 10mbit in reality. 6 Graphics emulation for GR1, LG1, and Newport is partial, but Eclipse graphics are close to working. 7 Work is ongoing on X11 acceleration and a DMA driver for Newport, and developing a GL driver from scratch would be easier and faster than working with existing X11 drivers. 89 A smashed Sun monitor's cable can be repurposed for IMPACT, Newport, Express, and Octane MGRAS systems. 10
