cable

Claims: 49

A loose, damaged, or poorly made cable can cause display problems such as an orange hue, vertical banding lines, or other issues, and cold solder joints or forcing a screwed-down cable to disconnect can cause damage. 1234 Cables can be configured for different sync modes, such as Sync on Green (SoG) or H/V sync, and a manual often comes with the cable to set it for various systems like SGI or Sun. 562 Some cables serve multiple functions, such as carrying video and PS/2 data or connecting an Ethernet transceiver, and a single cable for video and PS/2 ports is possible but requires using sync pins for additional data. 78 Cable quality and compatibility can affect performance, with poor quality cables or mismatched support downgrading link speed, and aftermarket tools, especially for pin crimping, can fail. 910
