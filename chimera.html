Chimera

Claims: 22

Chimera is a system line for SGI IRIX that can be used for molecular visualization, and it provides IRIX builds (such as Chimera 1.2540, which runs on IRIX 6.5.10+ with current C++ runtime patches). 123 Several components share connectors with Chimera systems: Numalink 5 (used on Altix UV systems), Numalink 3 and 4, and SAS cards (which are limited to the Chimera line due to driver differences). 456 Chimera systems differ from Octanes in connector/form factor and chip version, and Chimera backplanes and PCI risers differ from those of the IO10. 78 A Chimera system does not boot straight into an OS by default, and its environment monitoring is unreliable and constantly breaks. 910 Octane and Chimera systems are generally reliable, though power supplies can be an issue. 11
