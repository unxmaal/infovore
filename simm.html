SIMM

Claims: 24 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see DIMM.

A SIMM is a simple buffer using PC133 SDRAM, though a schematic for it is not readily available. 1 The maximum density for a SIMM is 32MB. 2 EV4 CPUs require banks of 5 SIMMs but only provide the capacity of 4. 3 DIMM is preferred over SIMM for easier removal and better clips. 4 It is now possible to make a new SIMM. 5 Both machines show only 2 MB of 24 MB RAM, with one machine failing to start due to incorrect SIMM placement. 6
