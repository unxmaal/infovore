VAXstation 2000

Claims: 5 Part of VAXstation

The VAXstation 2000 was designed as a low-cost, single-user VAXstation based on the MicroVAX CPU and FPU chip set, and it became the highest volume workstation in the industry. 1 The VAXstation 2000 uses the same CPU as the MV II but lacks cache. 2 The VAXstation 2000 is 3x the speed of a VAXstation I from a VUPS-rating point of view. 3 The VAXstation 2000 uses microprocessor implementations, while the VAXstation I is a mix of VLSI and discrete TTL. 4
