Celeron

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Pentium.

Celeron is a CPU brand that Intel has used as a cool-sounding name, with the claim that Intel has not had a cool name since Celeron (though Core 2 Duo is arguably comparable, being less flat than PENTIUM). 1 Mainboards supporting Celeron CPUs also support Pentiums of the same era running on the same socket. 2 At the same clock speed, there is likely not a significant thermal difference between Celeron and Pentium CPUs of the same era. 3 There was a bug in Photoshop on a Gigabyte motherboard with dual Celeron CPUs that caused the magic wand tool to crash the OS. 4
