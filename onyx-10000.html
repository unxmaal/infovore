Onyx 10000

Claims: 4 Part of Onyx

The Onyx 10000 is an R10000-based Onyx system, whereas the Power Challenge used an R8K CPU and there was no Challenge 10000 model. 12 Onyx 10000 backplanes, power subsystems, and system controllers differ from other Onyx systems, and big rack setups require three-phase power and can use up to nine CPUs. 1 An Onyx 10000 cabinet is available for purchase on Facebook for $300 in Texas. 3
