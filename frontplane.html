frontplane

Claims: 26

The frontplane is a passive component containing many gold and white connectors with small pins, SMD capacitors, and big diodes that could fail. 1234 On some SGI machines, the frontplane has a DB15 connector that may have been used for serial debugging or testing boards at the factory, and old revisions of Octane frontplanes have a cutout for a DB15 port. 56 In SGI Octane systems, the SCSI backplane connects to the frontplane. 7 The frontplane may store the MAC address, which can be used as a unique identifier for the system. 8 The frontplane is necessary for system operation on the O2, and reseating or mishandling it can cause failure. 9 An Octane's frontplane failure prevents boot and serial output, leaving only fan operation upon power-on, even with a good PSU. 10 The frontplane may be physically cracked, and accessing it requires removing the front plastic by squeezing top tabs and unscrewing six screws after removing other components. 11 When upgrading the frontplane, it is meant to be swappable so that licenses can be kept. 12
