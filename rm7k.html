RM7K

Claims: 10

The RM7K has the same pinout as the RM5200 O2 motherboard, and its motherboard is compatible with the O2 CPU, but it is not the same as the R5000. 12 You can mod RM5200 boards for RM7K's, and you can swap the 300MHz R5K for the RM7K with the same daughtercard. 34 The RM7K uses a BGA chip for its swap, has limited cache, and delivers a fair amount of performance per dollar. 567 The O2 runs an RM5K or RM7K CPU at a higher multiplier while keeping the bus speed the same. 8 There was never a 400MHz RM7K released by SGI, and you have a PROM emulator and a flex cable for the RM7K. 910
