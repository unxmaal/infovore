Win2k

Claims: 19

Win2K is closely related to NT4, which is generally considered lighter than Win2K on a classic Pentium with enough RAM, and the two are sometimes described as essentially the same. 12 There is a fair amount of NT4 software that should run on Win2K, including Office, IIS, Quake, and Emacs. 3 Most games work fine in Win2K, and no games ran on Win98 that weren't also on Win2K or DOS. 45 Win2K had stability issues but was more reliable than Win95/98. 6 Win2K was the first Windows OS to support USB Mass Storage. 7 Photoshop 7 works on Windows from Win2K to Win10 but not on Mac after a few years. 8 Win2K requires additional 3rd-party software to connect to a wifi card, while XP has built-in connection management that works but is not as nice. 9 Win2K cannot enable virtualization by default in the BIOS, and can probably run kvm. 1011
