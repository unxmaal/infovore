1280x1024 resolution

Claims: 7

The Eizo S1721 supports DVI and VGA, has 1280x1024 resolution, and can support sync-on-green. 1 The O2 can run at 1280x1024 resolution on a 17" SGI GDM Trinitron monitor. 2 The IBM POWER GXT800M Graphics Adapter supports 1280x1024 resolution and 8/24-bit double-buffered color. 3 The CRIME TLB limitation breaks at 2 full TLBs, which is related to the 1280x1024 resolution. 4 Entry graphics can display up to 1280x1024 resolution, but the Entry model is limited to 1024x768 with 4Dwm. 5 SoftImage 3D requires a single-channel display for 1920x1080 resolution, otherwise it defaults to 1280x1024; changing the VFO dynamically is not supported without logging out and back in. 6 The PMAGB-B is an 8bpp color/grayscale turbochannel card for SGI systems, offering 1280x1024 resolution at 66 or 72 Hz, or 1024x864 at 72 Hz (in 300LX). 7
