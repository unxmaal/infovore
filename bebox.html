BeBox

Claims: 17

The BeBox is a computer that runs the BeOS. 1 The BeBox is a Mac clone with four 132 MHz PowerPC 604 CPUs. 2 The BeBox used a technique to achieve symmetric multiprocessing (SMP) on non-SMP CPUs, and it used dual 603 CPUs. 34 BeOS runs efficiently on dual 604 CPUs in the BeBox, with load balancing between the CPUs. 5 The BeBox used a PCI video card, likely an ATI Rage. 6 The BeBox had built-in MIDI and audio capabilities and aimed to be a multimedia powerhouse for audio and video. 78 The BeBox prototypes used a Hobbit RISC processor, a stack-oriented design used for implementing C. 9 The BeBox can be emulated using MESS. 10
