A600

Claims: 10

The A600 is a bare minimum choice for those who want an Amiga with AGA capabilities, featuring a 68000 CPU that is slightly faster but has worse audio and video capabilities, except for a better mono high res mode. 12 Given IDE and PCMCIA, which are incredibly useful today, the A600 is arguably one of the better choices, though 030 accelerators are expensive and rare for the A600. 34 SMD caps are problematic on both the A600 and A1200, and an A600 can have a cracked solder joint causing random black and white display. 56 An A600 can have an internal fault causing the green signal to go to 0 and everything to turn purple, and it can run in black & white, possibly requiring composite sync, though RF modulator removal is common. 78 An A600 cannot do all-digital RGB to HDMI, while CF cards in the A1200 and A600 work fine. 910
