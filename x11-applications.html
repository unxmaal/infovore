X11 applications

Claims: 3 Part of X11

The Octane can run X11 applications with X11 forwarding and has passed its diagnostic test. 1 Wavefront TDV and IDL are X11 applications, not NeWS. 2 Conventional X11 applications do not require SGI graphics hardware; the X11 server's location is specified by the DISPLAY environment variable and can be managed by Xdmcp. 3
