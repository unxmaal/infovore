SCSI adapter

Claims: 12 Part of SCSI Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see CompactFlash.

Early parallel drives were largely the same as SCSI models with a parallel SCSI adapter, and later SGI created a multi-protocol chip for both protocols. 1 A SCSI adapter can improve drive performance by up to 8X on a G3. 2 A faulty SCSI adapter caused intermittent bus resets and may cause an Indy running IRIX 5.3 to crash and not reboot. 34 A SCSI adapter for Blue SCSI or similar can be made with a 3D printed mount and a passive adapter. 5
