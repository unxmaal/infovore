SPARC T3-1

Claims: 4 Part of SPARC

The SPARC T3-1 is a 16-core/128-thread UltraSPARC T3 server with a single processor, similar to a dual T2 in one chip, and has performance issues for single task workloads. 12 The SPARC T4-1 has a higher single thread execution advantage over the SPARC T3-1 and supports higher density 16-GByte DIMMs. 3 The SPARC T4-1 has a better cache setup with dedicated secondary and shared tertiary caches, while the SPARC T3-1 has shared secondary and no tertiary caches. 4
