Pi4

Claims: 9 Part of PI

A standard Ethernet cable can be used with a Pi4 for booting. 1 Using the booterizer image on a Pi4 allows configuration by editing SD card files instead of a terminal, though terminal instructions are recommended. 2 6.5.30 has no issues mounting NFS from a Pi4. 3 rascsi works nicely on a Pi4, and a Pi4 can be used with Rascsi for debugging the SCSI stack and BlueSCSI. 45 The PISCI with Pi4 is possible for I2 but not newer systems, while Azul, BS, and scsi2sd are also good options. 6 The Octane has high IO throughput but the Pi 4 is faster in every other way. 7 The Mashek fuel case for the Pi4 is expensive, but the graphics card was undamaged after some repairs and a new power supply fixed it. 8
