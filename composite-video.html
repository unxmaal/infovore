composite video

Claims: 5 Part of composite

The O2 can convert composite video to SDI. 1 Many discs were encoded in Composite video, so the output format depends on the disc's encoding. 2 Using Composite video can reduce interference and encoder chip loss. 3 The AV2 board provides better SDI output and can handle composite video with a converter. 4 Both VideoFramer and VideoLab have digital I/O on the card, but need a breakout box for the analogue component video, and Composite video is not supported by the VideoLab. 5
