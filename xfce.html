XFCE

Claims: 12

XFCE is a desktop environment that is considered productive, alongside GNOME, KDE, SWAY, and i3, and is preferred over GNOME by some users. 12 XFCE is a good fit for older machines compared to KDE, LXDE, or LXQT, and it can be built and run on Irix with available RPMs for tools and libraries such as python3.8, meson, ninja, and xorg-x11 prerequisites. 34 XFCE runs on Debian 7 with the radeon driver on an Altix 350 with an ATI FireMV 2200, where it functions as a working window manager. 56 Solaris 11 can run XFCE, but 3D acceleration is limited to proprietary drivers, and XFCE can stop the clock on an M4000/M5000, prolonging battery life. 78 XFCE aims for a unifying vision with GTK making composited windows easier, though it historically avoided GNOME's desktop-mobile unification, and it has no sound or SSL support but works with a mousewheel on a compiled installation. 910
