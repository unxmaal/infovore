6.3

Claims: 8

6.3 was tested on 5.3 and 6.5, had performance improvements, but no software was installed, and 3.8 might work on 6.2. 1 5.3 and 6 up to 6.3 are very similar, as 6.2 is just 64-bit 6.3. 2 6.3 requires a minimal install to run efficiently. 3 6.3 installation failed on R10000 O2. 4 Plain 6.3 doesn't support hardware from systems after IP26. 5 6.3 is only for the O2. 6 6.3 runs well on older hardware with EFS and ZULUSCSI, though XFS may not be supported. 7 6.2 and 6.3 are good for IMPACT systems and provide a middle ground between older and newer versions. 8
