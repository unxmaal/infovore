Nuendo

Claims: 7

Nuendo was first developed for IRIX and later ported to other platforms. 1 Steinberg released Nuendo for IRIX in the late 90s, but no copies exist anymore. 2 Nuendo was not widely available for IRIX and may have been a beta product rather than a commercial release. 3 A prototype of Nuendo existed for the Octane due to ADAT, but it was not released because of high maintenance costs and a smaller target market compared to Mac. 4 The 2000s version of Cubase was developed off of Nuendo, which was built on IRIX. 5
