SCSI2SD adapter

Claims: 8 Part of SCSI2SD

The SCSI2SD adapter is a software-controlled device compatible with V6 systems. 1 It can be connected to an A2000, used on an O2 (though slower for sequential IO), and used to replace a failing SCSI drive on an Indy. 234 It requires a 3.5-inch mounting bracket, which can be provided by a 5.25-inch to 3.5-inch adapter. 5 It can improve disc throughput on the original hardware, but it is currently crashing on the PI due to misconfiguration. 67
