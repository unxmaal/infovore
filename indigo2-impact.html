Indigo2 Impact

Claims: 28 Part of Indigo2 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX.

The Indigo2 Impact is a decent starter SGI system, though it may require tracking down a 13w3 adapter, and disk sleds can be hard to find. 12 It can use fx.64, a 15k UW SCSI drive, and an 8 gigabyte drive under EFS with multiple partitions possible. 345 It supports an ATLIGHTSPEED TIME DRIVER ultra-acceleration plug-in board that accelerates image processing software like Media Illusion. 6 The Indigo2 IMPACT 5.3 runs IRIX 5.3 with the XFS file system. 7 It can be booted from a Jaz drive and its serial settings should be 9800 baud, no parity, 8 bits, and 1 stop bit. 89 The power supplies reportedly go bad frequently, and it may have a fatal memory error on boot after sitting off for a while, which a reboot fixes. 1011 It may need a functional mid plane or motherboard to turn on, and its behavior does not change with the presence or absence of the Dallas chip. 1213 It uses a serial port that may run at 422 rather than the commonly assumed 232, and it can be water damaged internally and may collect hair due to its fans. 1415
