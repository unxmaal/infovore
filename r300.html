R300

Claims: 7

The R300 was a discrete GPU project developed by the ArtX team that became part of ATI's lineup, alongside its successor the R400. 12 The R300 and R400 series, including cards such as the Radeon 9000, 9500-9800, X800, and X850, outperformed SGI's V12 and InfiniteReality GPUs. 34 The R400 was intended to succeed the R300 but failed due to texture cache issues, leading to the R420, R520, R580, and C1 derivatives. 2 Every AMD GPU carries the lineage of the N64 and O2 after the R300. 5
