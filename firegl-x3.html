FireGL X3

Claims: 12 Part of FireGL

The FireGL X3 is a high-end graphics card that supports OpenGL 2.0, though this support may not work under IRIX. 1 It has more VRAM than the 7800 GTX, which outperforms the FireGL X3. 2 The FireGL X3 is similar to the X800, which is only tolerable (about 5/10). 2 The FireGL X3 is one of the only officially supported graphics cards for SGI Prism, along with the FireGL T2 and X2. 3 These cards require SGI firmware and ProPack 4 for SLES 9. 3 The FireGL X3 does not support the HP graphics library PEX on HP-UX 11i v1 11.11. 4 The FireGL X3 can be flashed to support PC cards. 5 The FireGL X3 has multiple DVI ports and an internal NL port. 6
