VGA adapter

Claims: 7 Part of VGA

A VGA adapter is needed to enable DVI output on deskside prism machines, which otherwise output analog at EFI by default. 1 Using a VGA adapter on the first port can make the monitor compatible with SoG. 2 You can remove 4 or 5 pins from a 'SUN' branded 13w3 to VGA adapter, and you can remove pins from a 13w3 to VGA adapter to enable sync on green if your monitor supports it. 34 A VGA adapter can help with compatibility issues, and setmon is a command to configure monitor settings, though it may not persist across reboots in IRIX 5.3. 52
