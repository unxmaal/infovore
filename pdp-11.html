PDP-11

Claims: 38 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see PDP-11, VAX.

The PDP-11 is a minicomputer line from Digital Equipment Corporation (DEC) that was miniaturized over time and was part of UNIBUS systems, either as a UNIBUS PDP-11 or a UNIBUS VAX. 12 The VAX descended from the PDP line and was software compatible with the PDP-11, and the VAX was a 32-bit architecture from the 1980s that was faster than the PDP-11 and capable of more than 4MB of RAM. 34 The PDP-11 has memory protection and no hardware support for unsigned types, only signed operations like ADD and SUB with an overflow flag for sign changes. 56 The BA11-K is a UNIBUS chassis used primarily by PDP-11 models 780, 750, 730, and 725, with some 8000 series models also supporting UNIBUS. 7 Early PDP-11s required manual entry of a bootstrap sequence to load block 0 from disk and jump to it, with variations for different disk controllers, and bringup requires probing the grant card. 89 The Unibone can emulate disk drives for PDP-11 models, including the 11/34, while the TU58 emulator is incompatible with the DHV11 and SLU ports. 1011 DEC's hardware and software, especially from the PDP-11 era, were known for durability and innovation, with a strong appeal to academic and scientific institutions. 12 PDP-11 documentation was good, while VAX documentation was less useful. 13
