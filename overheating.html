overheating

Claims: 4

Overheating can cause RAM issues, and adding fans can help address the problem. 1 The O2 can overheat, especially with a 400 MHz R12000 CPU and maxed RAM, though some users report issues while others run them without problems. 2 The SFX2270 server may shut off due to overheating, which could be related to a failing cooling system. 3 Multias are known for dying due to overheating, even with proper cooling. 4
