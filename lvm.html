LVM

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see XFS.

LVM is often preferred over alternatives because bundled LVM frequently provides functionality similar to other systems without additional cost or complexity. 1 Some users consider LVM to have write performance issues. 2 HP-UX on the C3700 uses the Veritas File System together with LVM. 3 Red Hat uses XFS and LVM instead of btrfs. 4 XFS on LVM can be backed up with xfsdump. 5 dd can be used to move an HP-UX installation between disks, though LVM might require additional steps or a live CD/USB for recovery. 6
