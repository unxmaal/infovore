bzip2

Claims: 9

bzip2 is a software package that installs along with symbolic links for bunzip2 and bzcat, with a wrapper script pointing to the installed bzip2. 1 It can be compiled with -mabi=n32 using an assembler in the PATH, such as /opt/local/binutils/bin/as. 2 Compilation time varies by machine, taking 9 minutes on an O2 with R10K and 2 minutes on an O300, though it still may not install properly. 3 The O2 requires bzip2 for Ansible provisioning but lacks it, and CDs are unavailable. 4 Nekoware mirrors contain a newer version of bzip2 that can be installed with 'inst' or 'swmgr'. 5 bzip2 is included in misc-5.3-files.iso alongside unzip, nedit, gimp, and netscape. 6 A bzip2 process has been observed consuming 100% CPU after disabling IPsec. 7
