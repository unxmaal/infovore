Red Rock

Claims: 14

The Tezro's controller is Ultra160, and the RedRock drive is likely U320. 1 The Red Rock uses a SATA drive internally; it is self-contained for IRIX, holding everything in a single drive that is easy to clone, service, etc. 2 The Red Rock has a Hagiwara LFD25S 60GB SATA drive and uses firmware 2025. 3 The Red Rock team chose an SSD for the Red Rock that does not require TRIM for read-intensive operations. 4 The Red Rock can be cloned to a new drive using the SCA COMPAQ HDD. 5 The Red Rock overrides SCSI PID/VID. 6 The Red Rock can have issues with SCSI timeouts during boot, but these can be mitigated by warming it up before booting the Tezro. 7 Setting debug level to 2 on the Red Rock can make it boot reliably from cold. 8
