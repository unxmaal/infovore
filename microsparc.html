MicroSPARC

Claims: 11

The microSPARC is a processor with a small form factor, exemplified by a 50MHz version from 1992 that had no heatsink. 12 The microSPARC was used in all 5s except the 170 MHz version, and Sun used it in Java stations. 34 The JavaStation had a MicroSPARC while the SPARCstation had a TurboSPARC; both are similar to the SPARCstation 5 but with different details. 5 SunOS 4 is not expected to run on the TurboSPARC; the last supported model was the 110 MHz MicroSPARC. 6 An 80MHz sun4c is faster than a 50MHz MicroSPARC, and Sun's MicroSPARC benchmarks were inflated. 78 The Serengeti used a microSPARC for the LOM. 9
