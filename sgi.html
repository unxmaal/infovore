With IRIX

IRIX was SGI's operating system, running on SGI hardware and on non-SGI platforms such as the MIPS Decstation, and every SGI machine before the Itanium ran IRIX. 1234 SGI attempted to port IRIX off MIPS twice but missed both opportunities, and it announced QuickTransit in October 2004 to run IRIX applications on Itanium 2 processors and Linux. 56 SGI's focus shifted away from its strengths, attempting to compete with Dell's cheaper offerings even though IRIX could have been a differentiator, and it ported more of IRIX's tools and features to Linux. 78 SGI offered versions of CATIA V4 and V5 for IRIX, and the government still uses IRIX while resellers buy up high-end SGI equipment to sell to the government. 910 IRIX COFF can emulate older systems for running COFF applications, forwarding graphics to a newer system using IrisGL or OpenGL, and SGI hardware can run OpenGL programs on IRIX, GNU/Linux, and Windows with minimal changes. 1112 The SGI IRIX system has a built-in screenshot program called screenshot, and CLOCK_SGI_CYCLE can be used as a low-overhead hardware counter in SGI systems, making code portable between IRIX and Linux. 1314 The 32/64 distinction for SGI kit indicates whether IRIX can run 64-bit software on the hardware, and the SGI documentation uses 'IRIX' to refer to early versions, including GL2, which can cause confusion with version numbers. 1516 SGI does not offer MIPSpro and IRIX licenses for hobbyists due to the small market size, and there are fewer freeware packages compatible with IRIX, though SGI freeware discs and tgcware are available. 1718 SGI's IRIX source code includes the O2 PROM source, available up to version 6.5.7. 19 SGI's IRIX did not support a 64-bit ABI until the R8000 series. 20 The SGI image format library was developed by SGI and shipped with IRIX, providing standard image loading, saving, and processing capabilities. 21 SGI, IRIX, and related software are considered important for historical preservation. 22

With Sun

Sun was one of the first companies to offer network boot, with the slogan 'the network is the computer', while SGI had network boot built in from the beginning. 23 SGIs lack a deep firmware-level diagnostic similar to Suns, which can output diag/boot data over serial even if the video or keyboard fails. 24 SGI drivers often checked the serial number of their own cards to ensure compatibility, while Sun's drivers were more flexible with third-party cards. 25 The design philosophy between SGI and Sun differs; SGI prioritized fast graphics, even if it only works in one system, while Sun aimed for compatibility and adhered to internal standards. 26 The 13W3 output uses an adapter to convert to a VGA plug that is special to SGI and does not work with Sun models, and the 13W3 pinout evolved over time with different configurations used by Sun, SGI, and others. 2728 Plugging a VME Sun into the SGI backplane causes the SGI to lose its mind due to communication issues over VME. 29

With O2

The O2 is a vintage SGI machine that was marketed as an entry-level offering, though opinions on it vary between describing it as fun but limiting and as a good entry-level system. 303132 SGI's strategy for the O2 involved using a less expensive graphics pipeline to reduce the price point, which resulted in a poor integrated GPU that was 32-bit while other SGI machines with similar CPUs were 64-bit. 3334 The O2 can use a presenter card, which is compatible with the O2 cam. 35 There was a dual processor O2 in the prototype stage, and the O2+ was a rare model sold for about a year between 2001 and 2002. 3637 Most O2 and later SGIs are RAM limited, often using proprietary RAM, with 128 MB being a common limit for running systems efficiently, and the memory modules are timing critical and may require specific types and layouts to function properly. 3839 There is software that was used to cluster O2s and other SGI systems for rendering and other tasks. 40

With Octane

The Octane is an SGI machine that most SGIs, including the Octane with its MXE, run OpenGL 1.1 on. 4142 The Octane 2 is a fast SGI suitable as a ready-to-use desk machine, and SGI gave away Octane2 computers for free when its Cortaillod factory in Switzerland closed. 434445 The Octane 2 board uses tiny pure gold pads for compression connectors, known as C-POPS, which were developed by IBM for SGI. 46 SGI used standard PS/2 keyboards and mice on later systems like the Octane, and a non-functional Octane can be purchased as a parts machine. 4748 An Onyx, Octane, or newer SGI system can emulate a N64 adequately for playing games, though emulating an SGI is significantly slower than running it natively. 4950 Octanes and other SGIs take more power and generate more heat, so they are often turned off for that reason rather than because of explosion risk. 51

With HP

SGI and HP both made 68k machines and both run UNIX, and SGI provided HP with some of their CPU supply. 5253 Intel convinced DEC, SGI, and HP to abandon their own designs and join the Itanium project, which was supposed to dominate the market and kill AMD, and HP and SGI became the main Itanium vendors. 5455 The merger of Intel, HP, and SGI was problematic, and Belluzzo led both HP and SGI through controversial transitions to industry-standard solutions that led to financial losses for SGI. 5657 By the time HP bought SGI, it was already dead, and nearly everyone in the HPC market jumped on Itanium, including SGI, DEC, HP, and Sun. 5859 HP now uses the SGI moniker for supercomputers running x86 CPUs with Linux distributions. 60

With Indy

The Indy was the most produced SGI workstation, and SGI made products used by ordinary people, such as the Indy and the N64 development SDK, which was designed for SGI workstations including the Indy, Onyx, and O2 due to their similar architectures. 616263 The Indy struggled to run IRIX 5.3 with 16MB of RAM, so SGI upgraded all base models to 32MB shortly after. 64 Real Indy units are picky about CD drive compatibility, and some Indy models have the SGI logo next to 'Indy' while others have it next to 'Silicon Graphics'. 6566 The Indy's graphics were one of the few cases where SGI published detailed information, leading to its emulation. 67

With Linux

SGI open sourced a lot of their software when moving to Linux, and Linux benefited greatly from SGI's contributions, including a huge amount of kernel work to get Linux to run on more than one CPU, which was then extended to scale to hundreds. 686970 XFS was included in Linux due to SGI's Itanium efforts for HPC customers and SGI's code contributions, while BSD implemented its own version, likely less feature-complete. 7172 NFS shares worked across SGI, Linux, and Mac systems for easy file movement. 73 Mistika is a version of jaleo with a different UI, available for SGI and Linux. 74 Linux was being pushed in the visual workstation days, so it never happened on SGI, and although SGI ported Linux, it didn't gain much traction despite Linux servers becoming popular. 757677 Linux is cheaper than an SGI service contract. 78
