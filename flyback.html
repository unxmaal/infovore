flyback

Claims: 16

The flyback was a common failure on CRT Studio Displays, iMacs, and Molar Macs, where a dead Molar Mac might have been caused by a dead flyback. 12 LC topper monitors have a bad flyback that can arc to the metal bracket, and the plastic casing can crack with time, especially due to heat, losing its coronal insulation barrier. 3 Flybacks in those monitors can fail on their own, and de-dusting and resoldering may not be a catch-all repair, so it is more likely something has gone short if the flyback made that noise and had worked previously. 45 There is a fix for 17in Studio Display flyback issues involving a new flyback that can be modified to work, the same for slot loading G3s, and flyback replacements are needed for 1084s. 67 A new flyback for a CRT monitor costs around a certain amount and is recommended due to known issues, with shipping options available for $39 including international tracking. 8
