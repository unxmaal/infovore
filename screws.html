screws

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Motherboard.

Screws are pretty well standardized. 1 Octane drive sleds use 6/32 x 9mm or 3/8" screws. 2 Mounting the card requires longer screws with a standoff, as shown in the video. 3 The system has screws behind the motherboard. 4 The handle is secured with two long screws that thread into the handle. 5 The screws pass through the fan mounting holes from inside the PSU up and out, then screw into the handle; only two of the four screws are threaded, the others secure into threads milled into the metal handle portion. 6 Many screws on an I2 system were corroded or stripped, possibly requiring all to be replaced. 7 The screws are fixed with lock tight and will not turn; they are clean with no corrosion. 8
