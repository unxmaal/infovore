Indigo R3K

Claims: 9 Part of Indigo

The Indigo R3K is a MIPS machine from SGI, part of a group of vintage SGI machines that also includes an Indy R4K, two Indigo2 models, two O2 models, an Octane2 with a R12K CPU and V6, and a Fuel model. 12 It supports up to 48 MB of RAM, with some units having double the advertised amount, less than the 128 MB that the PI can support. 34 It can run 4D, which was known as IRIX before that name was used, but IRIX 6 cannot run on it. 56 It boots poorly, with an old disk on its last legs but still functional. 7 Running IRIX 5.3 SEG faults when running certain tgcware programs but not others, and setting the inst skip_rqs option to true does not help. 8 OpenBSD supports all of the SGI machines except for the Indigo R3K. 9
