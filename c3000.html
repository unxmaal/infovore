C3000

Claims: 3 Part of C

The C3000 supported up to 8GB of RAM, the maximum for PA-RISC machines, while the J6000 reached 16GB. 1 The C3000 originally had panels with nice graphics, which were later removed. 2 Reseating memory modules multiple times may resolve issues with a C3000's LCD panel showing blocks, especially if the machine is powered down but still connected to mains. 3
