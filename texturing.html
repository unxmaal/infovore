texturing

Claims: 8

BRL-CAD's wireframe and flat shaded polygon rendering should run well on IRIX, but texturing or ray tracing might be problematic. 1 Elite3D has faster geometry processing and limited texturing compared to Creator3D. 2 There is an issue with freeglut on RPi with texturing enabled. 3 Enabling texturing on the Solid Impact significantly reduces performance, making it slow. 4 GL programs that do texturing fall back to software routines if the underlying hardware can't do it, as is the case with XZ without TRAMs. 5 Texturing in XZ is done in software, with texture data fed from system RAM, not cached on the card. 6 The Indigo2 High/Max Impact comes with 1 MB of RAM and cannot perform 24-bit texturing. 7
