Firewire

Claims: 76 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see FireWire, SGI Visual Workstation.

With IRIX

IRIX supports FireWire storage, but mounting FireWire disks requires specific steps and does not always work. 12 FireWire storage is available on later IRIX systems, at least for external optical devices and CF cards. 34 IRIX supports FireWire storage but not USB storage, with FireWire having better native support than USB. 56 FireWire device attachment can cause system crashes in IRIX. 7 IRIX supports FireWire storage, potentially limited by the PCI card, and can convert SCSI devices to FireWire. 8

General

FireWire is a bus technology that supports external mass storage, CD drives, and DV data transfer at recording speed, with stills saved as DV. 910 It performs better than USB in terms of system resource usage and throughput due to DMA transfers and full-duplex capability. 11 On IRIX, FireWire devices use emulated SCSI, and FireWire mass storage is essentially an external hard drive enclosure over a slow bus. 1213 FireWire does not outperform internal IDE for SSDs, with benchmarks showing IDE is 3x faster than FireWire 400. 1415 FireWire is used in aircraft and weapons systems for reliability, and was more prevalent than USB for professional use, especially for DV cameras, before USB hard drives became common in 2004-2005. 1617 FireWire can be made to work on Solaris 9 with a TI PCI-1394 card, and it can be used to mount compact flash cards on an SGI Octane running IRIX. 1819 Commercial UNIX vendors neglected FireWire and USB, expecting Macs to fade, and FireWire support on some UNIX systems was never fully fixed. 2021 FireWire has specific PCI IDs for different chipsets, and driver support is tied to these IDs. 22 A combo FireWire/USB card can be used on SGI Fuel machines, with FireWire detected as DM10, though some software support for FireWire on the Fuel was incomplete. 2324 FireWire can be used to connect a SCSI CD drive, and a FireWire system can use a SCSI drive, though most FireWire enclosures are IDE, which can cause compatibility issues. 252627 FireWire typically offers 40MB/s, while SCSI on IP35 is at least Ultra160. 28 You can boot later machines in target disk mode from a G3, but not necessarily from earlier ones, and you'd be limited to FireWire speeds. 29 FireWire doesn't work very well in Classic MacOS, and the FireWire bus is probed at boot without error messages. 3031
