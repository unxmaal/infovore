603

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see QEMU.

The 603 is a PowerPC processor that is a cut-down version of the PowerPC. 1 QEMU can emulate 603 and 604 processors, though its PowerPC emulation may be slow. 2 QEMU can also emulate Mac startup chimes. 2 A 200MHz 603ev on a 64-bit bus outperforms a 75MHz 603 on a 32-bit bus running MacOS 9. 3

Sources

  1. The PowerPC inside is a cut-down 603. (user-d903, 2023-01-06, exchange 222776, check: supported)
  2. QEMU's PowerPC emulation may be slow, but it can emulate 603 and 604 processors. QEMU can emulate Mac startup chimes. (user-d492, 2024-11-14, exchange 149250, check: supported)
  3. A 200MHz 603ev on a 64-bit bus outperforms a 75MHz 603 on a 32-bit bus with MacOS 9. (user-1361, 2021-02-18, exchange 14859, check: supported)
