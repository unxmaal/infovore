HP-UX 9

Claims: 9

HP-UX 9 is a version of HP-UX that runs on m68k hardware, such as the hp-425t machine. 1 HP-UX 9 versions differ between the 700s, 800s, and 300/400 series, and patches and applications must match the specific version. 2 HP-UX 9.10 for the 300/400 series has substantial differences from HP-UX 9 for the 800 series. 3 HP-UX 9 does not support disks larger than 2GB, though it supports some old Seagate disks (1GB) out of the box. 45 A gcc for HP-UX 9 is available, and the HP-UX 9 source has leaked. 67
