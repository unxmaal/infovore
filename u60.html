U60

Claims: 37

The SGI U60 is a workstation that is popular among retro computing enthusiasts and is considered a reliable machine. 12 It can run Solaris 2.5.1 to 10 and is regarded as a better Solaris 8 or 9 machine. 34 A U60 with a dead NVRAM battery does not boot, and opening the system is required to replace the NVRAM, which is needed for the machine to power on and run diagnostics. 56 The U60 can outperform a V12 Octane in certain areas but not most, and it can use a copper gigabit card while the U2 requires fiber for gigabit networking. 78 The XVR-1000 works in the U30/60/80 and U10, and the XVR-600 works in the U60. 910 The U60 lacks onboard 1394, but a PCI card can be used, and it can use a USB keyboard but requires a Sun keyboard for graphics. 1112
