Deoxit

Claims: 15

Deoxit is a non-conductive lubricant used to clean and lubricate card-edge and other connectors, making them easier to install and remove and reducing insertion force, even on large chassis such as SGI systems. 123 It can be applied to card edges, SCSI connectors, PCI slots, and DIMMs to resolve connection issues, and reseating or applying Deoxit to connections can resolve problems. 45678 The D-chips on the IO4, which handle data passage between the processor board and MC3, can be pulled, reseated, and treated with Deoxit. 9 Deoxit must dry overnight to avoid problems and should be applied carefully to avoid over-saturating slots. 1011 However, Deoxit may not be suitable for poorly aligned PC hardware, and it did not solve certain issues such as those involving MOSFETs or after retorquing. 212
