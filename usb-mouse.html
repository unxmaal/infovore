USB mouse

Claims: 8 Part of USB Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see PS/2 port.

The USB keyboard and mouse spec includes backwards compatibility with PS/2, and the adapters used are 'dumb' wired straight through. 1 IRIX supports USB mouse but converts it to PS/2 internally, preventing proper absolute positioning. 2 USB mouse and keyboard work in PROM but fail to load the OS, while USB audio adapters are the only useful USB devices in IRIX. 3 HP-UX 11.23 with patches can render a USB mouse unusable due to updated USB drivers. 4 A USB mouse issue was suspected, but the mouse worked on a PC. 5 Solaris commands like 'cat' and 'ls' are similar to Linux, but require spaces between command and filename; a USB mouse is needed for X, and can be connected to a B100 through a USB port. 6 A cheap PS/2 mouse from Amazon may intermittently fail to work on the XDM login screen, a USB mouse with a converter is a better choice. 7 We have Audio Dongle, USB Keyboard, USB Mouse, Keyboard / Mouse PS/2 USB Adapter, and SGI Compositor. 8
