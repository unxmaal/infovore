SS4

Claims: 4

X terminals were available for both classic and SS4 models. 1 The SS4 or SS5 at 110 MHz can run NEXTSTEP 3.3 or OpenStep 4.2, faster than the 10/20 SMP system, and supports 24-bit graphics. 2 Solaris 9 can run on a minimum-spec SS4, but it is not recommended due to JDS being a memory hog. 3 The ZX was an SBus graphics card, often called user-5510, for SS1/2, and sometimes in 5/10/20, U1, and U2, but not in SS4. 4
