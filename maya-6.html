Maya 6

Claims: 7 Part of Maya

DistCC can distribute rendering tasks from Maya 6 and other applications, allowing rendering to occur concurrently with desktop use. 1 Maya 6 for IRIX was available on disc but is no longer obtainable. 2 Alias' findkey program for Irix decrypts a large file of DES-encrypted licenses to plaintext in /var/tmp, making the security non-existent before Maya 6. 3 Maya 6 can cause issues with Fedora installs due to missing dependencies. 4 Rendering from Maya 6 and older, Softimage, and Lightwave requires specific hardware, though speed is a common desire. 5
