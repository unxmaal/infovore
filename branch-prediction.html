branch prediction

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see CPU cache.

Branch prediction is a major feature of most modern CPU architectures, to the point of taking extra precautions to minimize the performance hit of getting it wrong and flushing the pipeline. 1 Performance improvements in CPUs include superscalar and out-of-order execution, caches, and branch prediction. 2 The R12k improved branch prediction, contrary to the common belief that the R10k and R12k were just die shrinks. 3 Optimizing branch prediction better than CPU engineers over many years is unlikely. 4 The cache, TLB table, and branch prediction improvements should enhance IPC, but there is likely another bottleneck. 5
