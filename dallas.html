Dallas

Claims: 13

Dallas (now Maxim) OE replacements for Dallas 82C255 NVRAM are available for around $10 each, and Dallas replacements for the RTC are available on Tindie and eBay, though they might die in storage. 12 The nidec works fine, but the Dallas top has burst and needs replacement. 3 A DS1742W chip is needed for a Dallas, and they are likely old chips relabeled, with no reputable sources available. 4 On ip35 systems, L1 requires the dallas to boot; if L1 can't boot, the system can't either. 5 The timekeeper chip in the Tezro is likely the Dallas on the PCI interface board running L1, which could cause issues if it has problems, and the Dallas on the PCI interface board is the L1 controller. 67 The Snaphat handles environment monitoring and PROM environment, while the Dallas handles IDs and L1 variables. 8 The L1 will come up with a dead Dallas, but a dead battery won't, and a dead Dallas prevents L1 activity, including serial communication and LCD display. 910 The Fuel has a 24-pin connector that can be used as a replacement for the Dallas. 11
