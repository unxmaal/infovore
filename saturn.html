Saturn

Claims: 23 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Sega Saturn.

The Sega Saturn shipped with a stock controller featuring six buttons and no analog stick, and it also had a cool special edition bundled with a Japanese horse racing game. 12 Its architecture consisted of two Hitachi SH-2 CPUs, a Motorola 68EC000 for sound, a Yamaha FH1 DSP, two video processors, and a Hitachi SH-1 for CD-ROM control. 3 This complex architecture with multiple processors is appreciated by some, but it is also considered difficult to program due to its separate processors and bus contention issues, with design issues and potential bottlenecks. 456 The Dreamcast is easier to develop for than the Saturn, and the Saturn's use of GD-ROM instead of DVD was a mistake that smashed any momentum Sega had made with the Mega Drive. 789 The Saturn lacks Genesis backwards compatibility and has control issues for some games, but it has great titles. 10 Duke Nukem 3D on the Saturn has poor controls, especially without the 3D controller, due to limited d-pad functionality. 1112 The Quake engine was adapted for the Saturn rather than ported directly from Ultima Underworld, using a custom engine that employed both CPUs and GPUs, but it was a bad port due to Carmack's restrictions. 1314 John Carmack insisted on a software-rendered port of Doom for the Saturn due to affine texture mapping artifacts, and it performed poorly on the hardware due to hardware acceleration issues. 1516
