IP30

Claims: 19

IP30 is a system that runs IRIX, which supports all three ELF ABIs (o32, n32, and n64), with the compiler likely emitting n32 by default if not specified. 12 The ip30 and earlier systems require a Fuel, Tezro, O350, or similar system for Linux Itanium support; Altix series might have drivers; it is a PCI-X card, and the shoebox/shoehorn provides only PCI slots. 3 rigel 9# hinv shows 2 400 MHz IP30 processors with MIPS R12000 and R12010 chips. 4 There are no kX Project drivers for IRIX on IP30. 5 OpenBSD 6.9 can boot on an IP30. 6 The ip30 kernel lacks USB support. 7 Compilation times vary: around 40 minutes on dual 300 IP30 machines and 1 hour 12 minutes on a 400 MHz IP32. 8 The PLX bridge works fine in IP30, similar to PERICOM; setting the barrier flag on read requests fixes hanging issues with certain drives. 9
