3dcad

Claims: 9

The SGI 320 with early POWER CPUs was effective for 3D CAD due to good I/O and memory bandwidth, and 2D CAD was common on Solaris workstations while 3D CAD required Ultras with Creator/Elite graphics cards. 12 PTC democratized 3D CAD, making it accessible to small firms by the early 90s. 3 The 3dcad app runs on IRIX and uses the ToolBox.a lib, which is COFF and not easily buildable on IRIX 4, and 3dcad is a MIPSEB ECOFF executable (paged) not stripped. 45 imhex fails to parse 3dcad COFF executable due to a header issue. 6 3dcad now supports Japanese text through a reimplementation using the IRIS emulator, with issues in EUC conversion, and does not select an appropriate Visual, using the default screen when run in pseudo color on an IRIS setup. 78 3DCAD has a limit of 200 triangles, with crashes at 500. 9
