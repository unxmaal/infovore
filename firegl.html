FireGL

Claims: 15

There were IRIX machines with FireGL GPUs, and later the Itaniums used them as well. 1 Once SGI started using FireGL, their graphics architecture was no longer unique. 2 FireGL support was at version 1.4 but uses ATI drivers, and these drivers may work on AGP or PCI cards. 34 The HP firmware for the FireGL worked with a PC, but the SGI firmware had issues. 5 The FireGL card was incompatible due to the chipset. 6 IRIX gets wonky with FireGL cards at resolutions past 1280x1024 per image. 7 The Onyx4/Onyx350 hybrid may have compatibility issues with the VPro and FireGL cards coexisting, and the VPro and FireGL are common in the Onyx line, which is essentially a rackmount Tezro. 89
