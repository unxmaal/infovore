PCI bus

Claims: 10 Part of PCI

The FW800 port on a PowerBook may not work due to issues with the PCI bus or soldering. 1 An S3 Vision chip can be on the PCI bus even if the riser has only ISA or VLB slots. 2 The shared bus nature of PCI on stock PCs was a reason Unix systems on Sparc/SGI were faster. 3 SBS bus adapters connect VMEbus and PCI bus, enabling seamless operation and superior performance. 4 The PCI bus on O2, Octane, and Origin 2K is 5V. 5 The PCI bus in the O2 may be a limiting factor for data transfer speeds, as it is not very efficient, limiting gigabit network IO, and adapters are 32-bit only, halving the theoretical bandwidth. 67 Network packets may be CPU-limited before hitting PCI bus limits. 7 Drivers output showing no children on the PCI Bus suggests a riser issue; the IST tool `errdmp` on an x86 machine with l1usb driver can provide hardware hints. 8
