RIFA caps

Claims: 3

RIFA caps soldered onto the mains input connector often fail. 1 Safety caps are almost definitely present on Octanes, while RIFA caps are probably not. 2 A power supply with RIFA caps may fail on power-on and may not recover even after recapping, so check availability on eBay for local pickup or arrange shipping. 3
