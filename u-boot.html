U-Boot

Claims: 6

U-Boot is a bootloader that can load kernels in ELF format, which is a format that can be embedded in various ways. 1 U-Boot can hand off to Linux and can boot directly to a kernel. 23 When U-Boot does not bring up the keyboard or monitor, a special serial cable is needed to access it. 3 The cavium octeon card requires U-Boot to be flashed over serial for proper configuration. 4 DTL-H2500 is a PCI card with a PlayStation CPU that boots directly to U-Boot with no keyboard, mouse, or screen support, requiring a serial connection for interaction. 5 U-Boot prevents regular Linux from running on the STM32MP1 ARM evaluation board. 6
