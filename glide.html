Glide

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

Glide is a low-level version of OpenGL, similar to Vulkan in 1998. 1 3dfx took OpenGL, removed unnecessary parts for gaming, and created Glide. 2 Deus ex on os9 uses a Glide wrapper for hardware acceleration. 3 The G4 Cube can play hardware accelerated Glide/GL with its NV6200 using a Glide wrapper. 4 The system was intended to support all contemporary 3D accelerators, including Glide rendering for Voodoo cards though it was not officially supported. 5 On IRIX, Voodoo2 cards can be used with some root permissions and Glide tweaks, but acceleration is limited. 6 The Glide library manually initializes the card in usermode, which could break drivers. 7 Glide requires manual management of texture units for multitexturing effects, and OpenGL does not emulate multiple texture units. 8
