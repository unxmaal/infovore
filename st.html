ST

Claims: 23 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Atari ST.

The TT and Falcon are the highest-end models in the ST lineup, while the 020 and Accelerated A600 are lower-end models. 12 The TT and Falcon trade off features instead of one being definitively higher-end, and enhanced STs are rare. 3 The ST's RAM can be extended to 14MB with mods, but software support is necessary. 4 Installing an accelerator on a ST required removing brackets from PLCC sockets and shielding. 5 The ST is compatible with WinUAE and can be used as an alternative to the original hardware, especially due to its cost. 6
