LK201

Claims: 3

The SGI LK201 is a step down from the Type 5 and is not as nice as the later Type 7. 1 The standard VAX keyboards, including the LK201 and LK401, are not well-regarded, as they are regular rubber dome keyboards. 2 The LK201 can suffer from membrane failure, presenting as a stuck key and preventing the keyboard from initializing; it can be troubleshooted but may be difficult to repair. 3
