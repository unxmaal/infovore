Dallas clock chip

Claims: 5 Part of Dallas

The Dallas clock chip is a replaceable battery/crystal unit on the mainboard that provides the RTC and NVRAM for the system, along with the L1 controller. 12 The Octane uses a Dallas clock chip with a built-in battery, and a replacement can be obtained from Tindie using a regular battery on a custom PCB. 1 The only power supplied to the main board when the system is off is to the Dallas clock chip to keep the battery charged, and it is possible that the only thing that gets fried is the Dallas chip. 3 The l1 cache on the graphics brick is different, and there is no Dallas clock chip. 4
