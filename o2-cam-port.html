O2 Cam port

Claims: 3 Part of O2 cam

The O2 Cam port is found on the AV1 or Octane Personal Video option, where the Miranda device connects to provide DIVO In/Out. 1 The Ensemble Carbon breakout converts the O2 cam port into analog component video I/O, which is better than the composite video of the O2. 2 If the O2Cam port is populated, it is likely a full AV module, whereas if it is not there, it is an audio-only module. 3
