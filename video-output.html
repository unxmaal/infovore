video output

Claims: 17 Part of video

Video output problems on some machines may cause the display to go to sleep quickly when logging in as root, requiring a system restart to recover. 1 The Octane may have issues with RAM slots and video output, reseating RAM does not always fix problems, and the PSU might be a common failure. 2 A machine may no longer show an error but still have no video output, and the system may try to boot even if no keyboard is detected. 34 SGI video output (vo:sgi:pixeltex) uses pixel_texture (rgb) and glDrawPixels for drawing, and using -vo sgi:pixeltex:async can improve performance but may not fix visual issues. 56 Double buffered video output uses two buffers, one for screen display and one for drawing, with a swap once drawing is complete to ensure smooth graphics but requires more VRAM. 7 Indy can give video output even if the keyboard can't be detected, and in serial tests video output can take a long time, especially if the NVRAM battery has failed, leading to diag-mode and verbose POST. 89 Laserdisc video output uses the internal RF signal, and the video output works until Frost takes over. 1011 The O2 motherboard can have a slightly cooked video output which manifests as excessive green, possibly due to issues with a bridge pack between the ports and the motherboard. 12
