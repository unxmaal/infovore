DM3

Claims: 11

The DM2 and DM3 are cards used with the VBOB for video input, while DM5 is located inside the VBOB. 12 The DM3 has a card edge XIO connector for Tezro and Onyx350, and can be installed in an Onyx3 to improve its capabilities. 34 For SDI capture, a DM2 or DM3 is needed in addition to the VBOB. 5 The HD-GVO can connect to a DM5 in a VBOB but not directly to a screen; adding a DM2 or DM3 enables a Discreet Inferno setup. 6 A VPro or DM3 card is required to convert a P serial to another type in an O350, and a DM3 can convert the serial from P to M. 78 The DM2 and DM3 cards are hard to find and available from sgidepot.co.uk. 49 There are no working LVDS cables for DM3. 10
