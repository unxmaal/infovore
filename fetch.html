fetch

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Wget.

The IRIXports fetch tool downloads and extracts source code, copies it to orig, modifies it to compile, diffs the changes, and applies patches. 1 Fetch worked well for FTP on 68k Macs. 2 Old Mac apps like iCab and Fetch are still $10 shareware, but they are developed and maintained. 3 The U60 can use wget, curl, or fetch for downloading, with ftp as another option. 4 fetch hangs at the end when saving files, and basilisk ii causes issues with the mb pro and TB5 dock. 5
