Quadro Plex

Claims: 5 Part of Quadro

The Quadro Plex is a compute accelerator using four Tesla GPUs with a PCI-express interface, providing 32 cores and 128GB RAM but requiring significant power. 1 The Quadro Plex devices require a significant power supply, with even the basic model needing nearly 15A, and most other devices blow at 12.5A sustained. 12 The Quadro Plex works with a modern version of Windows and is easy to set up. 3 The Quadro Plex uses a PCI-E bridge; it was available new (open box) in 2014 but is now rare. 4 The Quadro Plex has two PCIe cards: the top one is the Quadro Plex interface card and the bottom one is an Fusion-io ioDrive2 Duo MLC. 5
