50-pin SCSI drive

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

Most Indigo2 drive sleds accept any 50-pin SCSI drive. 1 The Indigo2's 50-pin SCSI drive can be used on an Octane with a sled that includes a 50-pin converter, though it may take a long time for the Octane to fully diagnose the drive. 2

Sources

  1. most Indigo2 drive sleds want any old 50-pin scsi drive (user-b7c4, 2020-09-20, exchange 97563, check: supported)
