68020

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Video games and Linux.

The default emulator mimics the 020 instruction set, and most software doesn't need more than that. 1 GNOME 1 is written in C so honestly you can probably run it on m68k; the slowest box capable of Linux is likely a 68020. 2 The 68020 is slower than the SPARC v7. 3 The 68020 is slowest and the SPARC v7 is fastest. 4 The GL pipeline for early 4D systems used a 68020 for command interpretation and an IRIS ASIC for graphics processing. 5 motion v0.1 will emulate a 68020 iris 3000 series machine up to the graphical prom monitor, allowing debugging and reconfiguration. 6
