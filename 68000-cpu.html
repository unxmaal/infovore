68000 CPU

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Amiga.

The HP Integral PC can run HP-UX in ROM on a 68000 CPU. 1 The Amiga has a 68000 CPU with 1MB RAM and an OS in ROM. 2 The ST uses an 8 MHz 68000 CPU with DRAM, a DMA-based video chip, and AY-3 compatible sound. 3 The Cisco 2500 has a 68030 CPU, while the Amiga 2000/2500 has a 68000 CPU, and the 2500 may have an accelerator board for dual-head support. 4 The 68000 CPU on the A500 can be challenging to program, as getting even a pixel on the screen requires significant effort. 5
