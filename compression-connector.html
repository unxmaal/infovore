compression connector

Claims: 45

The compression connector has very tight tolerances, and SGI could not use screws to attach the connectors because they would cause signal interference. 1 The compression connector is a latching mechanism whose lockbar orientation can compress the connector, and the springs under it may shift during shipping. 23 The rounded edge of the compression connector is closed, while the squared edge has an opening, so spraying into the squared edge can destroy it. 4 A flake of pollen or a dust bunny in the compression connector can cause Octane systems to fail to POST or otherwise affect functionality. 567 The MEG-array connector, though also finicky, replaced the compression connector as a superior and more widely accepted solution. 8
