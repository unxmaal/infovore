user-5510

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see PCI-X.

PCI motherboards from the Pentium 2 era had one bus, while modern systems like user-5510, HP, or SGI had multiple buses. 1 The DB-13W3 connector is used for digital video output, but user-5510 and SGI have different sync implementations, requiring adapters for compatibility. 2 user-5510 machines do not use the standard VESA DIN-3 stereo connector and require an adapter, and they output video via composite sync. 34 If a system is wired for user-5510 it will not work with SGI, as there is no voltage on the connector. 5 User-5510 boxes were about $10k-20k a piece, and the render farm cost about $1M. 6 OpenBoot firmware uses FCode for device drivers, with 4 manuals released (3 by user-5510 and 1 by Firmworks), and is a loose standard that varies by hardware vendor. 7 FCode for user-5510 OpenBoot and Apple Open Firmware should be the same, but hardware vendors' implementations can differ, preventing compatibility. 8
