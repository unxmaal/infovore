SS10

Claims: 65 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARCstation 10.

With SS20

The SS10 has 3 fans facing the CPUs, while the SS20 has no fans, but both require two 501-2840 Fan Cards. 12 Uncached SuperSPARC modules run at the speed of the Mbus; SS10 has a 40MHz Mbus, SS20 has its Mbus run at 50MHz. 3 SS10 motherboard jumpers configure the SuperSPARC module to run at either 40MHz or 36MHz (newer model) or 40MHz or 33MHz (model 20); 40MHz is the recommended setting unless using very old modules. 4 An SS10 or SS20 does not have SBus connectors on the left side. 5 zulu pre-2040 works inside an SS20 but may not be recommended for disk use; a ZuluSCSI v1.2 is fine in an SS10. 6 ZuluSCSI maxes out Fast SCSI-II in SS10 and 20; 10k rpm disks won't exceed controller limits. 7 If you want a machine from that generation, get an SS10 or SS20. 8 Overheating issues are common with Sparc systems, especially with SS10/20 and SS1000 in max config; running 4-cpu SS10 is possible but not recommended. 9

General

The SS10 is a Sun workstation that generally runs in good condition, with some clones having different specifications. 1011 The system uses Ross CPU modules, with SM50 modules running at 40MHz due to Mbus clock limitations while SM51 and SM41 modules run asynchronously at higher speeds. 121314 It supports SCSI-2 fast/narrow storage, including external CD-ROM drives, though RaSCSI can cause SCSI bus parity errors and boot failures. 151617 The SS10 uses a custom Crystal chip for sound and modem functionality, and can play MP3s comfortably. 181920 The PSUs for the SS2 and SS10 are completely different. 21 Only a few 24bit framebuffers are worth having for an SS10: Fujitsu AG10 and some Rasterflex/Rasterops cards. 22 The cable for the multiport serial card on an SS10 has a gender that is opposite to the Sun cable. 23 Solaris 2.6 works on Ultra 5 and SS10, but later versions of 2.8 remove XGL which is needed; 2.8 installs without issues on Ultra 5. 24 The SS10 is expensive to run but has not had hardware failures and is fully operational. 25 The SS10 is in the same room as servers used for research and classes. 26 The last surviving manuals for the SS10 were physical copies in the office of the last Themis support engineer, who scanned them. 27 J0201 is the lowest memory DIMM in a SS10. 28
