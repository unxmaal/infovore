Numalink

Claims: 58 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Altix.

NUMALink is a high-speed interconnect for clustering SGI Origin or Onyx nodes into a single-system-image with low latency, typically 1.6 Gb/s or 3.2 Gb/s, and is used for clustering servers into a single-system-image. 12 It allows multiple systems to share unified memory and run a single kernel, tying connected systems together on the RAM level and making NUMA feel like UMA in terms of interconnected systems. 345 The technology was a feature of SGI systems that was later acquired by HPE, which still uses it in their superdome systems as of 2017. 67 It requires a Numalink router to connect multiple O300 machines, and the cabling on SGI systems can connect up to eight systems without a router. 89 The O300 can be NUMAlinked with other O300 and O350 machines, which are compatible for NUMAlinking. 1011 An expansion chassis is a secondary system designed to be numalinked to a primary system for adding processors. 12 Numalink was SGI's longest-lasting legacy. 13 Numalink 3, 4, and 5 use the same connector as Chimera systems, and Numalink 5 is also used on Altix UV systems. 1415 Numalink supports clusters, as seen in systems such as the Prism, Prism DS, Onyx350 InfinitePerformance, and the Fuel. 16171819 A single Numalink brick can connect up to 8 C bricks, with the top 4 ports not carrying L1 USB. 20 The USB bus carried through Numalink is called junkbus. 21 Numalink is described as extremely complicated and possibly impossible even for a well-funded team of developers. 22
