floppy disk

Claims: 21 Part of floppy Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Floppy disk.

A floppy disk should be inserted for the A500 to boot to it. 1 Ripping out a floppy disk destroys it, even if it was new and unused. 2 The boot process of SCO Unix System V/386 starts with a bootloader that, when given the 'ENTER' key, attempts to boot from the first floppy disk using a specific configuration, but may fail due to insufficient swap space. 3 A floppy disk for the Multia is still available. 4 IBM invented the floppy disk due to microcode. 5 Formatting the floppy disk from the desktop allows direct use, but switching the disk makes the device act weird. 6
