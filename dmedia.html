dmedia

Claims: 7

DMedia was a company that made hardware suites for video editing and composition, and its video breakout box is labelled DMediaPro, more commonly called the VBOB. 1 The dmedia package can work with the dvlink package, but it requires modifying system libraries and has reliability issues. 2 MIPS VSTs are not supported by many open source projects, with SDL1 being an exception for dmedia. 3 There is a Dmedia plugin available in nekoware for XMMS, but the output plugin produces noise, is an improvement, and is difficult to diagnose. 45 The VBOB for dmedia on the Octane2 supports up to 1080p24, with 720p60 and 1080i60 also possible, while the Flame/Tezro setup can only achieve 1080i50, with 1080p causing framedrops. 67
