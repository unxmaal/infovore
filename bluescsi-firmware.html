BlueSCSI firmware

Claims: 5 Part of BlueSCSI

BlueSCSI firmware makes scsi2sd easier for virtual drives and partitions, and supports ISO images as scsi CD-ROM drives. 1 It can be used on both Indigo1 and Indigo2, though Indigo1 uses a different SCSI controller than Indigo2. 2 BlueSCSI firmware with phase control can fix Indigo2's SCSI issues, while downgrading the firmware allows it to work on an Indigo1. 23 Batching writes into 4k chunks can significantly improve performance. 4 Users may need to fix CRC errors on writes to emulated drives. 5
