IRIX driver

Claims: 5 Part of IRIX

A PCI ethernet card may need an IRIX driver, and Alias has built-in support for the default IRIX driver in IRIX 6.x. 12 To debug an IRIX driver, one must include debug symbols in the kernel by altering `/var/sysgen/system/irix.sm` and running `setsym`, but symmon size does not affect kernel size. 3
