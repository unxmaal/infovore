Indigo2 R4k

Claims: 5 Part of Indigo2

The Indigo2 R4k is an IP22 machine that runs IRIX 5.3 and 6.5, with significant speed differences between the two versions. 1 It uses the GIO64 bus, unlike the Indy, which is also an IP22 but uses the GIO32 bus. 2 The Indigo2/R4k does not ship with graphics, while the Challenge M does. 3 Minecraft could run on a maxed RAM (384 mb max) Indigo2 R4k with sound turned off. 4
