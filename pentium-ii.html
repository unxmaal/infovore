Pentium II

Claims: 8 Part of Pentium

The i686 architecture was the best x86 architecture, with the Pentium II as a strong example. 1 The Pentium II is to the 8086 what the TOAD-2 is to a KL10. 2 SuperSPARC 90 is something like a low-end Pentium II. 3 Trident 9750 RAM can be used in a Pentium II rig. 4 The goal was at least a 400 MHz minimum spec for all supported architectures, including PowerPC, SPARC, MIPS, and Pentium II. 5 Passive matrix LCD systems were common until the Pentium II era, making active matrix screens more desirable. 6 The user was booted off the Pentium II and is now compiling 23.0 profiles. 7 The dual Pentium Pro card is more common than the dual Pentium II card. 8
