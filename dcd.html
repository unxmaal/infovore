DCD

Claims: 37 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane, HDMI.

The DCD (Dual Channel Display) provides two DVI ports, effectively giving two screens or one ultrawide screen split across two DVI outputs. 123 The DCD is a new product rather than a replacement for the 13w3 monitor port. 4 The VPro series, including the V12, can support DCD for two DVI ports, but the V8 and V10 may run out of VRAM, and 32MB of VRAM is not enough for DCD. 56 The Octane with a V8 and DCD was not officially sold that way, but the V6 has the connectors for DCD, and the Octane V10 requires a bracket for the DCD while the IP35 can use the VPro bracket. 78 The DCD can support resolutions such as 3200x1200, 1920x1200, and 4k, though it may squish 1920x1200 to 16:9. 91011 The DCD is fussy and works for monitors that are not the MLA, and the DVI from a DCD has issues with the DVI on the MLA. 1213 The DCD for a V12 is expensive, and investing in a DCD is better than buying overpriced 1600sws for image quality. 1415
