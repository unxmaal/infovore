XVR-4000

Claims: 15

It slots into a CPU slot, which fits into the same slots as CPUs on the XVR-4000 but not on the V890, making it incompatible with the V890. 12 It will not physically fit in a V880 without swapping or cutting one of the back cover plates, and it works with 900MHz CPUs in a V880 but may not work with faster CPUs. 34 The XVR-1000 is supported by the V880z, but the XVR-4000 is not, despite using similar processors. 5 It uses a compression connector for daughter boards, a unique feature, and has two Mbus and eight Sbus slots. 67 It is a power-hungry component that requires careful electrical circuit planning, needing a 15A circuit for dual CPUs. 89
