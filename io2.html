IO2

Claims: 6

The IO2, IO3, and IO3B bridge VME to PowerPath, while the GM board bridges PowerPath to graphics, and both boards have massive connectors. 1 The IO3 SCSI interface is faster than the IO2, supporting 10MB/s versus 1MB/s, though the difference might not be noticeable. 23 An IO3 or IO3B can connect both drive bays to separate SCSI channels, while an IO2 with a single channel requires a jumper to connect all bays to one chain. 4 The IP7 board uses a different backplane than the IO2, and the graphics boards use MPLink on the backplane. 5 The IO3B switch is necessary, and the PROM revision is not the issue. 5 A working set of GTX cards was found for an IO2 and IP7 33mhz, with plans to run IRIX 3.3.2 to keep power consumption low. 6
