GIMP

Claims: 21 Background: 9 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see GIMP.

GIMP is a free image editing tool that performs 99% of what 90% of users need, though its UI can be confusing for Photoshop users while remaining accessible to new users. 12 GIMP can convert SGI-specific image files to common formats such as JPG, GIF, and PNG, and it supports the .sgi format alongside ImageMagick and GraphicsMagick. 34 GIMP is used for resizing and converting images to .rgb for icon making on IRIX, customizing login screens on IRIX machines, and adjusting prerendered cutscenes. 567 GIMP runs in true colour on an Atari Falcon, can run unaccelerated video and photo editing on Linux, and can be compiled on an Octane. 8910 Compiled versions of early GIMP, GIMP extras, freefont, glib, gtk, and sane are available for IRIX starting from version 1.0.0, and the transition from GTK 1 to GTK 2 makes the former easier to build on IRIX. 1112
