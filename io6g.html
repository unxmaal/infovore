IO6G

Claims: 11

An IO6G is required for an MXI board in an O2k from an Onyx2, and it is also required for an Onyx2 IMPACT build. 12 The IO6G can be installed in an XIO slot to add keyboard and mouse ports, and it is used in the Onyx2 MXI, which is a deskside O2k with an IO6G and different sheet metal to accommodate a double-height MXI card. 34 The base PCB is the same for the IO6 and IO6G, and the ioc3 is also the keyboard controller. 5 The PS2 position on an IO6 lacks an ASIC to the left of the Qlogic chip that is present in the IO6G, which identifies as IO6G and is checked by the software. 6 The IO6G has two sets of PS/2 ports, and the Onyx2 has a SCSI controller on the IO6G rather than the midplane. 78 Turning off the system for about an hour may cause SCSI probes to not see the drives until the IO6G is reseated. 9 The Origin 2000 can be configured with multiple CADduo PCI cards and IO6-G for multi-user CAD workstations, and it can support two IO6-G cards with non-destructive modifications. 1011

  7. The IO6G has two sets of PS/2 ports but bifurcating the heads for multiple sessions per pipe is not explicitly mentioned. (user-37bb, 2025-06-24, exchange 212629, check: supported)
