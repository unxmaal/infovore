GUI

Claims: 21 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Graphical user interface, Apple IIc, Atari ST.

Old versions of IRIX have a simpler GUI with fewer features, but modern versions in 6.x added many new tweaks. 1 Most of the important settings on IRIX are accessible via GUI, even networking. 2 The GUI is not required for any functionality. 3 Running UNIX with a full GUI requires a decent amount of RAM. 4 Programming around GUI is tedious due to lack of design support. 5
