gold

Claims: 6

Gold is a linker that is lighter weight and has fewer lines of code than alternatives, which is why it was suggested as an option. 1 Modern linkers like gold or lld only look in specified libraries and differ in how they handle undefined symbols of dependent libraries. 2 Gold has a patch for MIPS64 and could improve IRIX's ld/bfd. 3 Someone managed to get GOLD to compile, but it was segfaulting with gcc4, and the C++ issues with gcc8 may be related to stack unwinding, which uses DWARF sections. 4
