IIe Platinum

Claims: 3

The Apple IIe Platinum is a heavily configured machine that includes a sound card, 1MB of RAM, a monitor, a mouse, and two DuoDisks. 12 It also lacks a serial card, which requires ADTPro to use the cassette port instead, a solution that is slow but functional. 3
