genlock

Claims: 10

Genlock is used for syncing video to other equipment in professional video production, synchronizing the refresh rates of video devices to ensure consistent frame alignment, which is useful for filming CRT displays or aligning multiple displays. 123 Analog broadcast TV production requires genlock and Time Base Correctors (TBCs). 4 The second row of the 4D/440 VGX bulkhead panel belongs to the Broadcast Video Option, which can do Y/C video and genlock to a house sync. 5 The A2000 has issues with genlock and requires a Black Burst Generator or video camera for video input. 6 The Indycam stops working when the video for impact is faulty and a stable genlock on the composite input is not achievable. 7 Frost works with live video through the SDI board but genlocks the monitor with Avocent. 8 The Toaster software does not support genlock, and an FPGA-based Amiga with genlock compatible with the Toaster software would be desirable but unlikely due to low market demand. 910
