Tesla cards

Claims: 5 Part of Tesla

Tesla cards are released by Nvidia in a 'tick tock' cycle, featuring high power cards every other generation. 1 Tesla cards and gaming cards used the same dies in older days but are now based on entirely different silicon. 2 Tesla cards have had additional FP64 since Fermi, whereas Quadro cards have not. 3 Tesla cards can have video outputs, and their display stream might be mirrorable using Windows DXGI. 4 Tesla cards never had the name 'GPU' in them. 5
