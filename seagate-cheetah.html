Seagate Cheetah

Claims: 5 Part of Seagate

The Seagate Cheetah SCSI disks do not spin up and are not recognized by any system, even with different power supplies. 1 The controller boards of the Seagate Cheetah disks do not provide any indication of why they do not work. 2 SCSI disks like the Seagate Cheetah have hidden serial console ports that can be used to diagnose issues. 3 The Seagate Cheetah drive in an Onyx4 makes a clicking sound on boot, indicating a bad drive that needs replacement. 4
