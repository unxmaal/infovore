secondary cache

Claims: 9

The 133MHz R4600SC processor module has 512KB of secondary cache, and most R4k Indys still have secondary cache. 12 Secondary cache is not in the CPU but consists of 4 SIMMs per CPU, with 1MB or 4MB available depending on the type, and 250MHz CPUs typically have 4MB. 3 The ROctane has secondary cache, and the RM5271 can have 512kB, 1MB, 2MB, or 4MB secondary cache, depending on the PROM configuration. 45 IRIX 5 requires more RAM and secondary cache than 16MB to run properly, but SGI shipped systems with insufficient components to cut costs. 6 Modifying an R5000PC to have secondary cache involves obtaining NOS cache chips and having them installed by a specialist. 7 The RM7000A Issue No. 5 from August 2002 mentions that the mode bit sets the write mode to the internal I/D and secondary cache and JTLB, but not the tertiary cache. 8 A full schematic is needed to figure out how the secondary cache is implemented, as it seems they are not using the R5k's external cache controller. 9
