SPARC

Claims: 138 Background: 11 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARC, NeXTSTEP, Oracle Solaris, Pkgsrc, QEMU, Sun-3, Video games and Linux.

With Solaris

SPARC hardware is transitioning to support Linux over Solaris, and Oracle's statements about Solaris were made around the same time as its statements about SPARC. 12 Solaris could be used as a daily driver on SPARC for longer than many other systems, and weapons control used Solaris on some old SPARC laptops. 34 Solaris 7 on x86 was interesting but not as good as Solaris on SPARC, while the CP1200 is a 32-bit SPARC with PCI that can run Solaris but has issues with graphics cards and SCSI controllers. 56 There might be issues with Solaris code generation for SPARC, and Xsun, the default X server for Solaris, is poorly designed and may not perform well on SPARC hardware. 78 Solaris is still around but barely hanging on by this point, while SPARC is dead. 9

General

SPARC is a RISC architecture that has become more CISC-like over time, alongside ARM, POWER, and RISC-V, and features register windows that make it more complex than simpler designs like RV32I. 1011 SPARC was not superscalar, which made it slower compared to other architectures, and it was not affected by most superscalar execution vulnerabilities. 1213 SPARC sales were falling even before Oracle bought them, and the architecture faded into obscurity. 1415 SPARC is dead, with Oracle having killed it. 16 Fujitsu made its own SPARC systems, and the Fujitsu A64FX is considered its replacement. 1718 SPARC and POWER CPUs can operate in both big and little endian modes, with SPARC capable of loading data in either mode. 19 Debian has good support for SPARC, but most Illumos distros do not, and Tribblix was the only distribution that supported SPARC decently, primarily supporting Sun-era hardware often poorly. 2021 SPARC is a RISC architecture that was still being actively developed, and Fujitsu continued producing SPARC hardware until recently to support existing customers. 2223 Sun encouraged cloning of their hardware, with many companies producing SPARC and SBus clones, and OpenSPARC is Sun's free release of UltraSPARC T1 and T2 Verilog that allows free development of SPARC-compliant processors (though using trademarks requires paid membership). 2425 SPARC is bi-endian, and using -mlittle can allow little-endian compatibility. 26 Linux on SPARC works quite well, as is the case for BSD, though modern Linux has poor support for SPARC, working only on Ultra 1/2 class systems. 2728 Solaris 10 on SPARC uses Xsun instead of Xorg, but Xorg can also be started on SPARC with documentation available. 2930 pkgsrc can be used on SPARC for obtaining software, and there is a prerelease available at https://dlc.openindiana.aurora-opencloud.org/SPARC/, though it looks rough. 3132 Blender 2.48 is available for SPARC, and Sun Studio 11 for SPARC can be found at http://tenox.pdp-11.ru/os/sunos_solaris/Apps/Compiler/Studio%2011/. 3334 Minecraft cannot run natively on SPARC and would require special compilation to target Solaris x86, and LWJGL was never compiled for SPARC but supports x86 Solaris. 3536 SPARC is a processor architecture that runs on multiple platforms, including Solaris/SPARC, and has been supported by operating systems such as NextStep and OpenStep, as well as by Wikipedia's list of supported architectures (MIPS, Alpha, SPARC, PPC, and others). 3738 Various SPARC machines exist, ranging from the slow and given-away SPARCstation 1 to the fast and underrated Ultra 1/140 and 170 models. 3940 SPARC hardware includes X terminals compatible with both classic and SS4 models, and the SPARC E3000 can achieve 8-way RAM interleaving for improved performance. 4142 Some SPARC modules (100-125 MHz) have uncertain CPU cores and are difficult to mix, while 128-thread SPARC processors exist but are less powerful per thread than Xeon due to a shared ALU in a 2 ALU core. 4344 Older SPARC and SGI machines use specific network drivers such as sq and le, and old SPARC boxes may experience SCSI errors or fail to detect hard drives if the NVRAM is invalid. 4546 The Macintosh Application Environment was available for SPARC and PA-RISC but not for IRIX. 47 Tribblix focuses on x86, with SPARC support being beta and less stable. 48
