termpwr

Claims: 3

The Sun3's TERMPWR should be set to 21-22 on and 23-24 off to power terminators internally, or to 23-24 open and 21-22 short to enable termination by the drive. 1 There are different 68-to-50 pin SCSI adapters, and the high byte termination needs to connect to TERMPWR on the correct ones, as they are not all created equal. 2 Enabling TERMPWR from the CD-ROM drive resolved SCSI issues in one case. 3

Sources

  1. The Sun3's TERMPWR should be set to 21-22 on, 23-24 off to power terminators internally, with 23-24 open and 21-22 short to enable termination by the drive. (user-d903, 2021-04-20, exchange 180708, check: supported)
  2. That will work, there are however 'different' 68 - 50 pin scsi adapters, the high byte termination needs to go to termpwr on the correct ones - they're not all created equal it seems (user-c799, 2024-12-30, exchange 224349, check: supported)
  3. Enabling termpwr from the CDROM drive resolved SCSI issues in one case. (user-24f0, 2025-09-30, exchange 118934, check: uncheckable)
