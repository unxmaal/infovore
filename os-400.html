OS/400

Claims: 26 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see IBM AS/400.

OS/400, also known as IBM i or i5/OS, is an operating system that runs on the AS/400. 1 It is a big-endian operating system that runs on PowerPC and Power chips. 2 OS/400 is not UNIX and is not directly related to System/38, System/36, System/34, System/3, System/32, or System/3. 34 It uses a single-level store where objects persist in very large, flat virtual memory. 5 OS/400 has built-in documentation and prompting command lines, and you can make custom prompts for programs. 6 It supports multiple users, remote login, and networking. 7 OS/400 uses a 2-digit date field with a range of 1940-2039, with an epoch of January 1, 1988. 8 DB2 runs on OS/400, along with Linux, z/OS, Windows, OS/2, and AIX. 9
