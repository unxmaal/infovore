Northbridge

Claims: 3

The AMD-based DV6 laptop experiences Northbridge issues and runs very hot, requiring vent cleaning, thermal paste replacement, and possibly reflowing the Northbridge die. 12 These Northbridge issues can also be resolved by heating, power cycling, and replacing the thermal pad. 1 Apple relies on third parties for its CPU, northbridge, and southbridge, limiting its ability to innovate within these components. 3
