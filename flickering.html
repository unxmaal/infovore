flickering

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

Flickering in OpenGL rendering can be caused by double buffering issues that arise without explicit vsync support, and adjusting the swap interval can affect the rendering output. 1 The flickering occurs regardless of render type settings, but is less intense at 30fps. 2 The flickering is due to a single buffered visual being selected, which might also lead to unsupported texture formats and empty textures. 3
