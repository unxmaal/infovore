zx6000

Claims: 36

The HP ZX6000 is a 2U 19" rackmount Itanium-based workstation with an AGP slot for a graphics card, office-friendly fans, and a distinctive case resembling a space heater. 1234 The ZX2000 is a lower-end, single-CPU variant with onboard audio and a quieter tower form factor, while the ZX6000 is thinner than the ZX2000, leading to worse thermals with dual CPUs. 5678 The ZX6000 runs HP-UX 11i v2 and v3 (the latter supporting OpenGL), OpenVMS, and IA64 Windows XP, but does not support RHEL. 910111213 The ZX6000 has reliability issues, including crashes caused by Red Hat changing graphics modes on boot, an X server problem, and cooling issues with less aggressive fans. 141516 The ZX6000 is rare and collectable, can be used as a parts machine, and has a distinctive Radeon 7500 PCI card and AGP riser. 1718192021
