Matrox card

Claims: 6 Part of Matrox

The Matrox card is a rare custom card possibly for video input, with a PCI-X connector and may require being a Mac card to work. 1 The Matrox card was popular for 2D quality but not primarily for gaming, while the Number Nine was less successful. 2 There were 3rd party graphics options for VAX systems, including a Matrox card for QBUS that might work with a UNIBUS through an Able Qinverter, but details are scarce. 3 The PBXGA is a cheap, basic graphics option for a PWS, providing an 8-bit X display suitable for moving xterms around; it is older and less capable than a Matrox card and appropriate for an Alphastation 200. 4
