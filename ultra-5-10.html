Ultra 5/10

Claims: 9

The Sun Ultra 5/10 is a cheap and easy-to-work-on workstation suitable for starting with Solaris or Unix. 12 It uses an UltraSPARC-IIi 333MHz CPU and has 256MB of memory. 3 Sun did not deliberately cripple its hardware, though some of its designs were cheap or poor. 4 Sun machines used fewer custom chips than SGI systems, with CPU and video being mostly custom, but video became more standard starting with the Ultra 5/10. 5 The IDE controller on the Ultra 5/10 is problematic, so a SCSI controller is recommended for better performance. 6 The Ultra 5/10 becomes a usable computer when you add SCSI and a decent Ethernet card. 7 You need a new NVRAM battery, which the Ultra 5/10 can reprogram from the ok prompt. 8
