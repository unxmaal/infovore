R4600 CPU

Claims: 6 Part of R4600

The Indy has an R4600 CPU and 64 MB of RAM. 1 The Indigo2 board can have an R4000, R4400, or R4600 CPU, all with the same heatsink. 2 Performance can be slow, as the Indy running IRIX 6.5.22 can be slow, possibly due to its R4600 CPU or being defective, and the Indy with R4000 or R4600 CPUs at 100 MHz was even slower than the lowest spec Indy. 34 The XZ card is 24-bit and accelerated; it improves performance on an SC with R4600 CPU. 5 An NIDEC PSU can crowbar an R4600 CPU if it outputs 10V on the 5V line, and a partially plugged-in control board due to cracked glue can cause similar issues. 6
