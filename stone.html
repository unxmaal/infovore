stone

Claims: 3

  • The older *stones* have a DB9-like connector with 1 blind pin, seen on the XIO-FC-card for the Octane. (user-bf68, exchange 231053, 2021-02-20; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • To create a 'stone' disk or file, edit the .cfg file to point to a filesystem or use a full disk array; there's also a file to put in /etc for Discreet to use any disk. (user-164b, exchange 231122, 2026-08-02; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Discreet had a license to open the stone for hard disks, but finding a compatible one was difficult. In the Octane, a sun s1 array with 3x18GB sun hdds worked with the stone soft, but 3 external seagate drives in AVID (user-04a2, exchange 231054, 2021-02-21; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
