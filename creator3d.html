Creator3D

Claims: 23 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Silicon Graphics.

With Elite3D

The Creator3D was a separate product line from the Elite3D series, and the Ultra 60 has an Elite3D GPU while the Ultra 10 has a Creator3D GPU. 12 The Creator3D, Elite3D, and XVR-1000 use 3DRAM, and Creator3D and Elite3D use system RAM for texture storage via UPA. 34 Creator3D series 2 and 3 support 1600x1200 resolution, while Elite3D supports only 1280x1024, and a U60 with a Creator3D can display 1600x1200 while an Elite3D cannot. 56 XGL was the API for the graphics tower and ZX, also supported on Creator3D and Elite3D, with OpenGL support on those but not much on ZX. 7 The high poly/no texture mode will work better on machines with slow texture support, like the Creator3D or Elite3D. 8

General

Sun designed the Creator 3D, along with the ZX and Elite 3D cards, while the Expert 3D is based on a Wildcat design. 9 The Creator3D is a 2D card with some 3D capability and 3D acceleration, and it can run natively on PCI. 101112 The Creator3D can run Quake I/II but not Quake III without an XVR-500/600/1000/1200. 13 The Creator3D can be used in the Ultra 2 and Ultra 10, with the Ultra 10 able to use a Sun 13W3 adapter. 1415 Most SGI machines came with Creator3D graphics. 16
