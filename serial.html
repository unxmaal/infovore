serial

Claims: 48 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Video Graphics Array.

Serial ports are used for firmware access, booting, and console use on various systems, though they may not output video due to hardware issues. 12345 Serial communication can be configured with specific settings, such as 38400/8/N/1 with RTS/CTS flow control or 9600 8N1. 36 Serial can be used for installation, debugging, and transferring programs, but setup can be challenging and may require specific configuration. 7891011 Serial and SCSI controllers do not interfere with each other, and serial is compatible with certain devices. 1213 Failed RAM or hardware issues can cause problems with serial communication, and nothing may show up on serial. 141516 MIDI on IRIX is used via serial, and someone was recently selling MIDI adapters on eBay. 17 A FPGA could implement legacy genlock compatibility and provide serial, parallel, and joystick/mouse ports. 18 The ROM limit for kernel size via serial is 4MB. 19 The Origin 350 can use multiple L2 modules and a Tezro L1 on serial for toggling between systems. 20 Basic decoding should be spit out to serial for diagnostics. 21 RAD + dual serial (the original that came with tezro) and also replacement quad usb 2.0, they all work in other slots. 22
