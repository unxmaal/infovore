Ethernet port

Claims: 7 Part of Ethernet

Some aircraft models have an onboard screen with an Ethernet port that can be used to communicate with the gogo box. 1 The Ethernet port on the modem has RJ45 connectors. 2 The proximity of the Ethernet port to the ISDN port can cause analog POTS ring voltage to damage the ISDN port. 3 A bent pin in the Ethernet port can cause connectivity issues and can be fixed with a small pick. 4
