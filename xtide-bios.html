XTIDE BIOS

Claims: 4 Part of XTIDE

The XTIDE BIOS is a niche product that can also handle CDROMs, costs $65, and is being considered for use in older models with ESDI issues. 1 The XT-IDE BIOS on the McIDE is not well-suited for operating systems beyond DOS. 2 Checking if a Gridcase decodes the whole 64KB BIOS area might allow shoehorning the XTIDE BIOS there. 3 The XTIDE BIOS allows using a CF card, with access gained by drilling a hole in the side. 4
