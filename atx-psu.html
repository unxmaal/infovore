ATX PSU

Claims: 21 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Power supply unit (computer).

An ATX PSU can be adapted for older machines, but this involves soldering a connector into the ATX PSU board, which can be done with an ATX extension cable or by having someone solder the connector. 1 Modding an ATX PSU can cause fires due to insufficient power distribution. 2 An ATX PSU supplies most power to the 12V rail, while some systems like the Indigo demand nearly all power on the 5V rail. 3 An ATX PSU with 1200W is needed to provide enough amperage on the 3.3V line to replace certain PSUs, as the low 3.3V and 5V rails are challenging. 45 A basic ATX PSU uses a common ground for all rails. 6 The power supply of older systems often requires rebuilding due to capacitor issues, especially tantalum capacitors which can short. 7 Replacing the PSU with a Mean Well for 5V and an ATX PSU for other voltages can resolve these issues. 78 Someone managed to fix an Indigo PSU using an ATX PSU. 9
