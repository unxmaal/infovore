Onyx1

Claims: 29 Part of Onyx

The Onyx1 was an early SGI system, used for early N64 development and reporting 2 WD33C95A SCSI chips via `hinv`, though the IO4 PCB actually has 6 WD33C95A chips and 6 external connectors. 12 It was the last SGI to use the SGI bus, the IRIS port, and the proprietary keyboard, sharing the same keyboard port as the Indigo1 and Personal IRIS 4D/30/35. 3456 The Onyx1 uses HVD, supports 4D systems up to Onyx1 with serial #2 to #4 available in DE9 and DIN8 connectors, and has network cards behind a bulkhead panel requiring special cables to connect to the Sirius VO2 board, which lacks smart modules and cannot be connected to VME cards directly. 78910 Known issues include the VCAM, which has a 12v regulator that tends to fail, followed by power converters and the PSU, which can be DIY fixed but is complex and challenging to repair. 11 Prices range from $200 to $1000, with PSU rebuilds often needed, and NetBSD has not been reported to boot on an Onyx1. 1213 The Onyx1 with Reality Engine graphics is a specific configuration for collecting SGI systems, and Flame 4 is an IRIX application requiring an Onyx1 for full functionality, which may run on IRIX 6.5. 1415
