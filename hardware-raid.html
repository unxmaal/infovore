hardware RAID

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see ZFS.

Proxmox can handle hardware RAID, JBOD, or ZFS, and some systems use ZFS instead of hardware RAID. 12 The hardware RAID is essentially a stock LSI chip, and one user reflashed the hardware RAID firmware to JBOD. 34 The E450 is known for its ability to handle a large number of disks with hardware RAID support. 5 Linux has supported hardware RAID without issues in the past, even with SCSI and RAID cards. 6
