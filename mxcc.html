MXCC

Claims: 3

The MXCC heatsink design is correct for an SM81, but the chip could be an underclocked SM81 with an 85MHz rated MXCC, which is not uncommon, since the speed rating on the CPU itself is covered and cannot be seen and the MXCC can be a later revision. 1 Later SM71 modules have an enhanced MXCC that can work with older SM71s. 2
