TBC

Claims: 3

Video capture requires a TBC, and mediarecorder is not ideal, so dmrecorder should be used instead; fast disk speed is necessary for capturing video, and 2 SCSI channels with a PCI SCSI card were used to achieve about 90 MB/sec read. 1 For VHS with a TBC, a smart choice is an S-VHS/W-VHS VCR with a built-in TBC and field/frame sync ability, along with an AJA capture board with a mini-TBC for additional stability. 2 A TBC can often be done in software with VHS-decode projects, eliminating the need for a dedicated TBC. 3
