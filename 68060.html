68060

Claims: 11

The Svethlana contains a 68030 CPU at 50 MHz, a 68080 CPU, and a 68060 CPU at 90 MHz. 1 The 68060 was faster than the Pentium for integer math but slower for floating-point operations due to opcode optimization. 2 Full-fat 68060s became expensive due to demand from Amiga accelerators and lack of viable upgrades for other platforms. 3 Foundation Imaging used an Amiga render farm with 68060 and 68040 processors for Babylon 5 CGI, but switched to Pentium-based machines after NewTek ported Lightwave to WindowsNT. 4 A 68060 or a PiStorm CPU is not desirable for an Amiga because they are not what the Amiga team designed for, even if they could provide better performance. 5 The FPGA Arcade Replay supports a real version 6 68060 and a real floppy drive. 6
