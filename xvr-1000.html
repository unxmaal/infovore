XVR-1000

Claims: 13

The XVR-1000 is a UPA card using MAJC (VLIW CPU design) technology, one of only two MAJC cards alongside the XVR-4000, and only a few Suns can mount them. 123 It has 256MB of TRAM, 72MB of framebuffer, and 32MB of system RAM for the MAJC CPU, using 3DRAM like the Creator3D and Elite3D. 45 It is capable for its time, similar to the V12 but with better texture handling and dual outputs, though it has poor geometry performance. 46 It has native dual-head capability, requires the primary output to be the 13W3 connector, and cannot do 1920x1200 on both outputs. 789 It works in U30/60/80 and U10, though documentation limits it to the B1000/B2000. 10 It is supported by the V880z, but the XVR-4000 is not, despite using similar processors. 11 It is expensive and performs poorly in Q3A compared to 3Dlabs-based offerings, but has good image quality. 12
