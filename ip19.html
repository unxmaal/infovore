IP19

Claims: 9 Part of IP Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx2.

The IP19 is a processor board used in Silicon Graphics systems, appearing in the Onyx, Onyx2, Indigo2, and Onyx/Challenge families. 1234 The IP19 corresponds to the R4K processor, and it is the board the Indigo2 started with. 254 The older IP19 could run IRIX 5.3, where the o32 vs. n32 distinction was relevant, and IRIX 6.2 is required for a 64-bit kernel on IP19. 67 The install list for IRIX 6.5.22 excludes machines below R4K but includes the IP19. 8 The system controller monitors backplane voltages but does not monitor the IP19. 9
