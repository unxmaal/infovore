Voodoo

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Voodoo2.

The Voodoo performs well at lower resolutions, achieving 48.4 FPS at 640x480 and 45.4 FPS at 800x600. 1 The Voodoo2 is faster than the Voodoo and can operate at higher clock speeds, with the Voodoo running at 45-55MHz and the Voodoo2 at 85-95MHz. 2
