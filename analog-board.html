analog board

Claims: 7

The analog board initializes the system, which chimes, passes self-test, cycles the degauss coil, and then shuts down high voltage when protection triggers. 1 The CRT is likely okay unless it took a big hit; most likely something on the analog board died from heat or old age, possibly both. 2 An NOS analog board can be sourced from eBay for CRT repair, and one such board was recapped along with the SE/30 PSU without failing. 34 Reballing the analog board of a Macintosh Plus can be painful and potentially carcinogenic due to cap juice. 5 One analog board was found to be in good condition. 6
