Plan 9

Claims: 20 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Plan 9 from Bell Labs.

Plan 9 is a minimalist, research operating system with a clean design and new ideas, including an approach to documenting an operating system. 123 It was a mature OS developed by Bell Labs, which sold and shipped it, but it never achieved mainstream success. 4 Plan 9 is primarily backend-focused with common terminal use, is supposed to be headless so the host does not need graphics, and can easily build a single system image cluster with CPU servers. 567 It has a dedicated community, and 9front is a derivative with 64-bit addressing and modern features. 89 Porting software to Plan 9 is difficult, as evidenced by the ./configure script, and it requires porting or writing software. 1011 Plan 9 ran on various hardware, including an Indy port, the DEC Alpha PC164, LX164, 500au, sd550, and Dec Personal Workstation with limited support, the SGI PowerSeries, and there is an Itanium port. 12131415
