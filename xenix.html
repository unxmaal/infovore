Xenix

Claims: 13

Xenix is an officially licensed UNIX (R) that originated as a port of UNIX v7, which gave it limitations compared to UNIX. 12 Xenix, along with Coherent and Venix, is an operating system for SGI machines. 3 Xenix, Coherent, Venix, PC/IX, Minix (V1.2), QNX, Singlix, ELKS, and Proolix run on an 8086, while AIX requires a 386. 4 Some implementations, including Venix or Xenix, write to the RTC and read back to check for Y2K compatibility, which could fail and damage CMOS RAM on some clones. 5 DOS had pipes starting in version 2.0, derived from XENIX, and Word had a XENIX version, reflecting Microsoft's historical ties to SCO. 67
