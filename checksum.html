checksum

Claims: 6

A checksum is used to validate the integrity of downloaded packages when patches are applied via a `.patch` file. 1 Checksums can reveal data corruption, as mismatched values between a downloaded image and a flash drive suggest the drive or the computer's USB ports may be corrupting data. 2 Checksums are also used in other schemes, such as the DES encryption combined with Base58 encoding and a checksum used for license keys in pre-v7 Alias software. 3 For 3C503 users, enabling 'Generate checksum byte' in an 8KB build is required to avoid compatibility errors. 4 A checksum issue can cause problems in bootstrapping if the values are not set. 5
