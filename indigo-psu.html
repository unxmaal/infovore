Indigo PSU

Claims: 5 Part of Indigo

Indigo PSUs may develop problems, as rusty green capacitors in the unit might indicate a close-to-failing power supply. 1 Replacing an Indigo PSU involves finding a 5v supply that fits, as the 12v need is minimal and can be handled by a tiny buck/boost, but most off-the-shelf 5v PSUs are too long and have a squarish form factor. 2 Someone managed to fix an Indigo PSU using an ATX PSU. 3
