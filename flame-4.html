Flame 4

Claims: 3 Part of Flame

Flame 4 is an IRIX application requiring an Onyx1 for full functionality, and may run on IRIX 6.5. 1 Dongles integrated with FlexLM were required for floating or permanent licenses, while Flint/Flame 4.4 and earlier supported a custom Discreet dongle. 2 A license patch for Flame 4 for IRIX 5.3 might work on R3Ks due to similar graphics architecture. 3
