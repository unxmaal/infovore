Voyager

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

SGI used the name Voyager for ATI cards in the Onyx4, and the VPRO (Octane and ip35) and Voyager (Onyx4) are ultimate graphics systems. 12 The VPro and Voyager series require addon DCD cards for DVI, and the Onyx350, later Octane, and Onyx4 also need addon DCD cards. 3 Trusted Solaris 2.5 can be installed on a Voyager with a battery and specific commands. 4 The Voyager has a working bottom half but a non-functional screen backlight, which needs capacitor replacement. 5 Lightwave was used for both the intro and the show of Voyager, but the formats weren't fully compatible, leading to texture issues in the intro that were never fixed. 6 Scanning the idb files of IRIX 6.5.22 shows there is no support for MGRAS (Impact) on IP35, and the combination of CPUBOARD=IP35 GFXBOARD=VOYAGER exists. 7
