8-bit graphics

Claims: 8 Part of 8-bit Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indy.

The SGI Indy often uses 8-bit graphics, which support all features and have the same performance as 24-bit graphics, with no performance hit for additional colors. 12 The Indy can display 8-bit graphics with impressive dithering, especially compared to other systems. 3 Some users prefer 8-bit graphics for its charm and for fitting the Indy's 'little guy' personality. 4 The Indigo2 can be used as a controller for offline video editing, but its 8-bit graphics limit the quality of the preview. 5 The PA-RISC Next always uses color, while the HP 712 has a funky color system that looks great for 8-bit graphics. 6
