Ultra 2

Claims: 47

The Sun Ultra 2 is a dual-processor workstation that uses SBus, unlike the single-processor, PCI-based Ultra 5 and 10. 1 It is considered a "cake box" rather than a "pizza box" due to its lower profile, and it is a favorite for its form factor. 23 It was used at a university Unix lab and is popular for number crunching due to its dual CPUs. 45 It can be used as a firewall, and it has CD drives. 67 It can support up to 28 146GB disks in external multi-disk cages, though disks over 2TB might have issues with some controllers. 89 The Ultra 2 is compared to the Indigo2 as a current-generation Unix, RISC, high-end workstation, and it can support about 24 X terminals with multiple graphics seats. 1011 23-inch telco racks were used for Sun Ultra 2s and wider PCs in some shops starting in late 2000. 12 The SGI Ultra 2 has an Amiga UI. 13
