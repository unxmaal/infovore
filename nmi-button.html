NMI button

Claims: 3

The NMI button on an Octane dumps a debug log and shuts down, and it is like a soft reset that is not commonly used for anything on an Octane. 12 On an Altix, the NMI button is one of the ways to enter the POD prompt, alongside setting the debug switches to 'debug 0x08' or entering the command `nmi` in the L2. 3

Sources
