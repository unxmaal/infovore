With O2

The O2 is a simple system built around a CPU and RAM, with no graphics issues. 12 The O2 uses an R5k CPU and proprietary RAM, with 128 MB modules being common and kits available inexpensively on eBay for $20 to $30. 3456 The O2 can support up to 1 GB of RAM, though 128 MB is the largest module available and 1 GB can be finicky. 789 The O2 likes paired RAM with bank A holding the largest SIMs, and PCI mapping exposes up to 1 GB of RAM in the upper 4 GB of address space. 1011 Dual O2 and Octane configurations are possible, with RAM that can be sourced from various SGI machines. 12 The O2 may require RAM to be reseated or tried in different configurations before it powers on, and issues may point to a CPU problem or IM1 failure. 131415

With Octane

The SGI Octane can support up to 8 GiB of RAM, though it is unlikely to reach that amount, and it maxes out at 2 GB with high-density RAM before it can be upgraded further. 1617 An Octane can support a gig of RAM, be upgraded to 3 GB, and use up to 4 GB with proprietary sticks. 181920 Octane RAM is different from O2000 RAM, which is compatible with the O200 and Onyx2. 21 Stacked RAM is possible in an Octane but is not common. 22 An Octane with dual R14K CPUs at 600 MHz, V12 graphics, and 2 GB RAM costs around 600 Euros, and 2 GB of RAM is enough for an Octane2. 2324 The Octane may hang and show a blinking red LED on the lightbar due to issues with the RAM or the XIO connectors, and its CPU board can be challenging to remove, with RAM issues causing it to get stuck. 2526
