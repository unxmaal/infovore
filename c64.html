C64

Claims: 42 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Commodore 64.

The C64 is a personal computer that is well emulated today and has a large software library alongside an active modern hardware and software market. 12 The C64 requires using PEEK and POKE to access its video and sound chips, and it has similar color limitations that can be overcome with tricks. 34 The C64's video output port evolved from a 5-pin to an 8-pin connector, with later models supporting separate luma/chroma signals for better s-video compatibility. 5 The C64 frequently fails due to PLA chips, which can be replaced with modern replicas that are generally compatible though some may have issues with specific software. 67 Early C64s and C64Cs are generally more reliable than those from the middle years. 8 The C64 has an Ethernet card, and a C64 running GEOS could connect to IRC via an Ethernet adapter 64nic+ in RR-Net mode. 91011 The C64 can be reset without losing memory using a cartridge with a reset switch and fastload, which gives modifiable BASIC and a quick reboot time that is beneficial for development. 121314 The REU enhances the C64's capabilities, particularly for developers, by providing faster I/O and better build times. 15 The TRS-80 Model 2 and several C64s were fun to restore. 16 C64 users sometimes used the built-in memory to store programs by typing them in, often leaving the C64 powered on for extended periods. 17
