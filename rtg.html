RTG

Claims: 7

The RTG is a graphics accelerator used in big box machines, functioning like a graphics card (unlike the HDMI board, which is more like a retrotink). 12 RTG is useful for every system-compatible software, including text editors, word processors, 3d software, and music sequencers, not just Workbench or web browsing. 3 The MiSTer supports RTG, AGA, and Akiko, and has a 020 CPU. 4 The Pi's RTG does not output to HDMI via RGB signals, but it will be firmware updated to support audio decoding on the ARM in the future. 56
