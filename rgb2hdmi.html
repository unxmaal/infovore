RGB2HDMI

Claims: 4 Part of RGB

The RGB2HDMI works on many older RGB systems, but it requires a special A2000 video slot carrier. 1 The RGB2HDMI has better picture quality than the OSSC. 2 A 500 Amiga with an RGB2HDMI, a joystick, and a CF card set up with WHDLoad packed with games is 90+% of the Amiga experience. 3 The rgb2hdmi has different behavior based on the presence of a non-CPLD board, flipping the meaning of the one button config entry; this issue is now fixed. 4
