SCSI interface

Claims: 6 Part of SCSI

The Yamaha A4000 from 1999 has a SCSI interface and is the last of the true hardware synths. 1 An Indy does not have a fast enough SCSI interface for it to make a meaningful difference. 2 The SGI Octane's SCSI interface limits performance, and upgrading with a modern SSD would likely result in bottlenecking. 3 The Indigo2 Video card requires a compression card to record video, and its SCSI interface isn't fast enough for uncompressed video. 4 zuluscsi on the Indigo gives SCSI interface parity errors when using the default settings, and backing off to SCSI1 and enabling parity does not resolve the issue. 5 PROM commands like 'init' can restart the PROM and rescan the SCSI interface. 6
