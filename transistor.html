transistor

Claims: 6

A transistor can fail in ways that cause other components to fail, such as an exploded SCR in a switching power supply causing a transistor in a different section to fail. 1 A transistor in the switcher itself being blown can cause a device to make a cycling/resetting sound, as the startup circuit tries but the output voltage is not produced. 2 One of the tiny transistors gave the author issues but was successfully reinstalled after some effort. 3

Sources

  1. An exploded SCR in a switching power supply can cause other components to fail, such as a transistor in a different section. (user-4c09, 2021-05-21, exchange 17450, check: uncheckable)
  2. If it's making that cycling/resetting sound, I'd think about output capacitors being shorted /or/ a transistor in the switcher itself being blown - the startup circuit is trying, but the output voltage isn't happening. (user-07ed, 2022-05-16, exchange 161912, check: uncheckable)
  3. One of the tiny transistors gave me issues but I got it back on there after a while; it was really annoying. (user-bc18, 2022-09-15, exchange 207674, check: uncheckable)
