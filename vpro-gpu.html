VPro GPU

Claims: 4 Part of VPro

The V12 and VPro GPUs share many features but differ in quality and speed, with VPro built for speed at the expense of quality, and it supports extensions that V12 does not. 1 For H264 playback, an SSI/MXI/SSE/MXE or VPro GPU might be necessary for better performance. 2 The radeon GPU is significantly faster than the VPro GPU, with benchmark results showing up to 1200% faster performance in some tests. 3
