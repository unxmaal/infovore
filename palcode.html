PALcode

Claims: 7

Writing your own kernel on a VAX or DEC PC Alpha involves using PALcode. 1 PALcode was intended to allow operating systems to call into CPU-specific firmware for tasks like context switching and interrupt handling, but its execution resulted in inconsistent implementations across different OSes. 2 VMS PALcode implemented VAX atomic queue instructions and VAX-style interrupt handling, running uninterruptibly at a higher privilege level than kernel mode. 3 PALcode calls behave like macro-instructions in the architecture, but this approach didn't succeed. 4 The BIOS is regular code accessed through interrupts, while PALcode has special behaviors accessed via a dedicated instruction opcode range. 5 The Alpha was fast enough that they could compile VAX assembly code to it, emulate a whole bunch of VAXisms in PALcode, and still be much faster than the VAX. 6 The PALcode and firmware are specific to the machine; EV5 machines like yours do not support ARC firmware. 7
