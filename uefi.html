UEFI

Claims: 18 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see BIOS, Video games and Linux.

UEFI is a specification for firmware that replaces BIOS. 1 UEFI is a PC thing, and other platforms like SGI have different things. 2 UEFI boots COFF, and some find UEFI on PCs lacking compared to Open Firmware. 34 UEFI bootloaders look for the ESP, not the MBR. 5 Linux can be loaded directly by UEFI, and you can build the Linux kernel as a standalone COFF EFI executable that can be booted directly by the UEFI. 67 UEFI supports NVDIMMs. 8 UEFI is often set up in compatibility mode to maintain backward compatibility, even though it has potential to improve. 9 Installation steps differ based on whether you use MBR with BIOS or UEFI. 10
