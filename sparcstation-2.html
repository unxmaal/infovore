SPARCstation 2

Claims: 19

A SPARCstation 2 clone is based on a SPARCstation 2 chassis but is not a genuine Sun case, resembling the SPARCstation 2 in layout instead. 12 The clone has 3 SBus slots, no MBus, 30-pin SIMMs, a CG3 framebuffer, and AUI Ethernet. 3 The SPARCstation 2 board may work with a Super Sparc II PSU and requires a specific ROM image, which can be sourced from retrostic.com. 45 A SPARCstation 2 with a 100MHz crystal running at a 50MHz CPU clock and 25MHz SBus clock requires 60ns RAM for stable operation. 6 The SPARCstation 2 has been used as a firewall with OpenBSD 2.x, though key exchange was slow, and can use an ATM card and a framebuffer plus keyboard card via SBus. 78 The SPARCstation 2 outperformed the NeXTCube, and the Netra replaced an Ultra 10 and a SPARCstation 2. 910
