/usr

Claims: 5

For performance, an ideal setup was to have separate partitions for /, /var, and /usr, with /usr as a raided partition for redundancy. 1 On IRIX 5.3, /usr can be split across multiple EFS filesystems, best done during initial installation or by migrating afterwards. 2 IRIX 6.5.30 installs most software in /usr, with home directories in /usr/people. 3
