SCSI card

Claims: 26 Part of SCSI

A SCSI card can provide an extra channel in a card cage and may support an older Debian IA-32 disc. 12 SCSI cards come in various forms, including the 2Gb qla2340, a QLogic PCI-E controller, and a card from a Panasonic CD drive usable as an all-purpose SCSI card on a laptop with the right adapter. 345 Some SCSI cards can fail, such as a QLogic PCI-E controller that may explode capacitors, a failing SCSI card that may cause frequent I/O errors on an old XP industrial computer, and a disk not detected by the SCSI card that shows no LEDs or spinup. 467 SCSI cards can be replaced or swapped to fix issues, as with an Onyx2's internal SCSI controller that started working again after installing a different SCSI card. 8 SCSI cards can be used with emulators and for storage, such as BlueSCSI for the IIgs, and the Apple II can use a SCSI card for a hard disk allowing 35MB of storage. 910
