Rage 128

Claims: 8

The Rage 128 was one of several video cards used on Mac systems over time, following Apple video cards and preceding the Radeon 9000 series and GeForce 6800 ultra. 1 Adding a Rage 128 video card would allow dual monitors. 2 Rage 128 graphics cards may not work with Xsun on SPARC, even if they have a SPARC OpenFirmware ROM. 3 A 400 MHz G3/Rage 128 runs Quake III Arena with some settings turned down, while a G4 Digital Audio with OS 9.2.2 and Rage 128 can run Quake 3 but displays a grey screen with a movable mouse cursor. 45 A 450MHz G3 with Radeon 9200 LE runs better than a G4 Digital Audio with Rage 128 under the same OS, and the Radeon 9200 LE runs in a 66MHz PCI slot while the Rage 128 runs in a 33MHz slot. 67 The sparcprinter card is a generic card with ATIMach64 or Rage128 and Sun firmware. 8

Sources

  1. Mac systems used different video cards over time, starting with Apple video cards, then Rage 128, Radeon 9000 series, and finally GeForce 6800 ultra. (user-79ed, 2020-11-07, exchange 231311, check: supported)
  2. Adding a Rage 128 video card would allow dual monitors. (user-79ed, 2020-11-07, exchange 231311, check: supported)
  3. Rage 128 graphics cards may not work with Xsun on SPARC, even if they have a SPARC OpenFirmware ROM. (user-7b1c, 2022-04-18, exchange 181859, check: supported)
  4. A 400 MHz G3/Rage 128 runs Quake III Arena (Q3A) with some settings turned down, an 800 MHz G4/Voodoo3 runs it playably, and dual 1.42 GHz G4s/9250 PCI run it smoothly. (user-baf6, 2023-07-26, exchange 147850, check: supported)
  5. The G4 Digital Audio with OS 9.2.2 and Rage 128 can run Quake 3 but displays a grey screen with a movable mouse cursor. (user-59df, 2024-06-18, exchange 148792, check: supported)
  6. A 450MHz G3 with Radeon 9200 LE runs better than a G4 Digital Audio with Rage 128 under the same OS. (user-59df, 2024-06-18, exchange 148792, check: supported)
  7. The Radeon 9200 LE runs in a 66MHz PCI slot while the Rage 128 runs in a 33MHz slot. (user-59df, 2024-06-18, exchange 148792, check: supported)
  8. An sbus GPIB card and a sparcprinter card were found in a collection. The sparcprinter card is a generic card with ATIMach64 or Rage128 and Sun firmware. (user-07ed, 2026-01-07, exchange 240766, check: uncheckable)
