ls

Claims: 9

`ls /hw/module/` shows module identifiers, which may differ from L1 controller identifiers. 1 The 'sl' program is a troll program that replaces 'ls' with a similar command, and its build requires the termcap dependency, which may be named differently in Fedora. 2 OpenBSD is slow on Alpha, taking 10 seconds to register `ls`. 3 Sanity checks that `ls` works and that a newly created file has a newer timestamp than `configure` itself. 4 Solaris has a statically linked `ls` and `ln`. 5 AIX has commands prefixed with `ls, mk, ch` for listing, making, and changing things. 6 Check `zuluscsi.ini` for SCSI configuration or try `ls` with different combinations. 7 Installing sgug into your path by default breaks Maya iconcatalog due to differences in the output of /bin/ls and /usr/sgug/bin/ls -Hl; changing 'ls -Hl' to '/bin/ls -Hl' in /usr/lib/filetype/install/aw*.ftr fixes it. 8
