PowerPC processor

Claims: 7 Part of PowerPC

The PowerPC processor and RAM are located on a separate board, while the MFIOP board includes disk controllers, a twinax interface, expansion interfaces, and the clock battery. 1 The MPC8544FS is a capable and modern PowerPC processor with PCI Express support. 2 The PowerPC processor is radiation-hardened for deep space missions, though the Mars Ingenuity helicopter used a Snapdragon instead. 3
