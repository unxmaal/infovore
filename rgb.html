RGB

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Dreamcast.

RGB signal handling varies across hardware: 2600RGB converts TIA signals to RGB, while UAV cleans video signals. 1 The Atari ST used coax for RGB signals, but the STe added 15-pin ports. 2 RGB has uses beyond 2D games, such as for lighting and effects in modern pixel graphic games. 3 A machine's colour display can be affected by capacitor aging and PAL frequency shifts, but RGB can improve the quality. 4 The DCDigital HDMI adapter for the Dreamcast supports RGB and VGA, with some games needing patches. 5
