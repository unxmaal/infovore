GL programs

Claims: 5 Part of GL

GL programs require the GLX extension to function. 1 They can use pixel shaders to peg the GPU, even with GL 1.x through extensions, and complex geometry or many fullscreen quads can also stress it. 2 GL programs that do texturing fall back to software routines if the underlying hardware can't do it, as is the case with XZ without TRAMs. 3 Examples of emboss bump mapping and other graphics techniques can be found in GL programs and experiments. 4 There are issues building GL programs against RSE right now, and it is something that still needs to be solved. 5
