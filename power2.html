POWER2

Claims: 4 Part of POWER

POWER2 was one of the two original POWER processors, alongside POWER1. 1 POWER2 was a poor floating-point processor, but was somewhat competitive for integer work. 2 Alpha's integer performance was unimpressive. 2 Comparing POWER2 to a Sun is difficult, because multi-processor Suns could beat a single Power2 and were cheaper. 3
