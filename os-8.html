OS 8

Claims: 6 Part of OS

A FireWire/USB/SATA combo card can be used under Classic Mac OS with compatible firmware, allowing SATA drives to be seen as SCSI, and FireWire and USB to work in certain versions of OS 8 and up. 1 The StuffIt expander is available on OS 8 or 9 install CDs that came with the internet browser. 2 OS 8 and OS 9 systems should run on 68040 systems without issues, but PPC systems shouldn't have any trouble. 3
