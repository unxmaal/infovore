4D/380

Claims: 11 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

The 4D/380, 4D/440, and Crimson share a configuration of 4 CPU boards and two MC boards, with 256MB RAM limited to one MC board using high-density SIMMs. 1 The 4D/380 has a construction defect in which the blinkenlights panel can overheat and melt the connecting cable, potentially causing a fire. 2 A tantalum capacitor in the 4D/380 can fail and release magic smoke, and the same PP2 assembly is used in PowerSeries and Crimson desksides. 3 The 4D/380 can boot from an IPI-II disk, a high-end alternative disk interface from the early 1990s that did not succeed, with SMD and ESDI being older options and SCSI being the winner. 4 Drivers for FDDI or video options for IRIX 3 are needed to run IRIX 3 on a 4D/380. 5 The 4D/380 VGX can do textures but has CPU-based limitations in OpenGL, so IRIX 4 or 4D1-3.3 should be run. 6 The VideoLab can be placed in the rightmost slot (19) or the fourth slot of a 4D/380, but requires a special 4-slot edge connector for the video option. 7
