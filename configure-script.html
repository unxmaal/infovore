configure script

Claims: 13 Part of configure

The configure script is problematic. 1 It may fail to locate compilers even when 'which' finds them, as seen with mrxvt. 2 It can fail to find libraries, such as glBegin in -lGL, suggesting a problem with the linker or library paths. 3 It may fail to compute sizeof long long, as seen with libgomp, which stops with a bad exit status. 4 It may pick up local definitions from header files, leading to compilation errors, as seen when compiling xz with gcc 8.2.0. 5 It may fail on broken compilers or shells, as seen with Perl, requiring workarounds. 6 It may require additional flags such as -L/usr/local/lib -R/usr/local/lib for linking, as seen with egcc. 7 It may overwrite manual edits to makefiles when re-running during make, as seen in the NTP project. 8
