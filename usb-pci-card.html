USB PCI card

Claims: 3 Part of USB

The Octane does not have a USB PCI card solution because its fuel-specific drivers might overlap with their FireWire problem and only support keyboard and mouse. 1 Adding a USB PCI card to an O2 should provide power even if the card does not work. 2 The Sun U60 can use a USB keyboard but requires a Sun keyboard for graphics, and a USB PCI card can add gigabit Ethernet and XVR-600 graphics with 2GB RAM. 3
