VT100

Claims: 15

The VT100 is a widely used terminal standard, with its terminal type considered a safe bet in most terminal emulators and capable of performing all applicable VT100 functions perfectly. 12 The VT100 keyboard layout was cloned by most systems at the time. 3 The VT100 technical manual explains its operation in detail, and the VT220 is similar. 4 The VT100 and VT102 differed in screen update speed, and real VT100s can operate at 9600 baud while the VT101 can only do 4800 baud. 56 A real DEC VT100 or similar model, such as a 101 or 102, remains useful as a vintage terminal that is still mostly usable. 7 The VT100 is a basic standard, whereas XTerm is more advanced but often lacks features like sixel, regis, and tektronix. 8 Various programs support the VT100, including termtris, and MacTerminal could emulate a VT100. 910
