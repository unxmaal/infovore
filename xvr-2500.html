XVR-2500

Claims: 4 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

The Ultra 45 uses an XVR-2500 graphics card along with dual 1.6 GHz UltraSPARC IIIi CPUs and 16 GB of RAM. 1 The XVR-2500 supports OpenGL 1.3 with extensions. 2 Some SPARC servers can be adapted with an XVR-2500 and a sound card, though this is not recommended, and no SPARC workstations run Solaris 11. 3 SGIs can run CATIA, but not on an XVR-2500. 4
