MXI

Claims: 15 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

MXI is first-generation Octane graphics, essentially the same as the I2, and can run demos and 3D modeling software on Octanes. 12 The SGI Octane has two different Impact chipsets, mxi and emxi, and MXI by definition has two TRAM modules. 34 MXI has texture memory, but MX and MXI texture RAM can be problematic with Rambus overclocked parts. 56 MXI has better performance stability with PowerAnimator than Odyssey, and the V8 is faster than MXI and MXE. 78 An Ultra 60 with a full Expert3D is comparable to a dual 300 with MXI, but can only support up to 2GB of RAM. 9 Dual MXI in an Octane was never a supported configuration due to heat production issues in the graphics section, though MXI+SSI was, and the Octane should run with maximum fans. 10 Octanes require a CPU to power up, and an Octane with an SE TRAM can recognize an MXI card after reseating the TRAM. 1112
