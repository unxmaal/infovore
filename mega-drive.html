Mega Drive

Claims: 4

The Mega Drive was more popular than the Nintendos of the era and was Z80 based. 1 Revisions of the Mega Drive can be distinguished by the presence and location of the RF shield, particularly on the model 2 by examining the bottom vents. 2 The white revision is significantly heavier than the red one due to the inclusion of an RF shield. 3 The MegaDrive had ideas for a keyboard and floppy drive add-on, and had a setup for a modem to add a keypad and printer for online banking in 1990 in Japan. 4
