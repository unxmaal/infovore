MIPS binary

Claims: 3 Part of MIPS

  • Sash is a single-file MIPS binary linked at 0x80C00000 for 32-bit systems and 0xA800000000C00000 for 64-bit systems. (user-e04c, exchange 216934, 2020-05-23; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • QEMU-IRIX supports building 32-bit MIPS binaries using the n32 ABI, and does not automatically enable 64-bit mode with -march. (user-5510, exchange 77686, 2023-08-11; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • IDA Pro can be used to reverse engineer binaries, and Ghidra supports MIPS but may have issues with SGI MIPS binaries. (user-b4f4, exchange 214753, 2019-05-11; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
