13w3 cable

Claims: 14 Part of 13W3

The Sun 13W3 cable has a bank of DIP switches that can be set for different machines, and it uses an hd15 connector. 1 The 13W3 cable has a different pinout than SGI, which can cause compatibility issues with the Sparcstation 10. 2 Setting the correct DIP switch configuration can resolve video issues, and the cable can be used instead of a SoG monitor, though the green color might be washed out. 34 The cable can be passively converted to VGA, and a VGA version works fine and can bring out composite sync, vsync, and hsync. 56 The 13W3 cable works well with VGA displays and can be purchased from eBay, though not cheaply. 7 Using the DIP switches might damage the monitor with SoG, some TV models do not support SoG, and a hacky cable can be modified to work with an Indy by removing pins. 89
