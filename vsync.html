vsync

Claims: 9 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

Double buffering can cause flickering in OpenGL rendering without explicit vsync support, and this flicker can also occur on older systems like IRIX, though it can still work without vsync on modern systems. 12 Without double buffering there is no buffer to sync, so disabling double buffering can eliminate vsync-related flicker. 3 Disabling vsync can be done with -single or environment variables on modern systems, but not with glXSwapIntervalSGI on IRIX. 4 Vsync makes the graphics card wait for sync or lower the FPS to match the display's refresh rate, even if it is capable of rendering at a higher rate. 5 G-Sync reduces latency compared to VSync for modern gaming. 6
