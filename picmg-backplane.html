PICMG backplane

Claims: 3

The PISA connector is the same as EISA-regular, and the SBC slot on a PICMG backplane is typically to the left. 1 A PICMG backplane can feature 64-bit PCI and ISA slots. 2 Such a backplane can host an Alpha system-on-a-card, a SCSI/NIC PCI card, and a PowerStorm 3D30 graphics card. 2
