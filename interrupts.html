interrupts

Claims: 4

In privilege mode 3, interrupts are always enabled for modes 4+ and always disabled for modes 2-, with handling cascading down to lower modes if not handled. 1 Writing code without memory protection and masking interrupts can lead to immediate crashes due to overwriting interrupt vectors or code, but running it in ROM avoids these issues. 2 IP32 PCI drivers override interrupt levels, and setting them to specific values does not work; interrupt handlers are not called despite enabling interrupts. 3 Polling can be slower for smaller chunks but is comparable to interrupts for larger chunks, and it avoids a fault issue. 4
