DECnet

Claims: 6

DECnet is a protocol rather than a physical layer, operating at a higher layer than raw Ethernet. 1 Because it operates above raw Ethernet, traffic inspection tools above DECnet will misinterpret the traffic. 2 DECnet changes the MAC address to be in sync with the DECnet address. 3 DECnet can work on older Ubiquity switches with the right configuration, and a Ubiquiti switch supports DECnet and can handle multiple clients simultaneously. 45
