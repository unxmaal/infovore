Tribblix

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Pkgsrc.

Tribblix is a Solaris-based distribution for SPARC systems, and it is one of several Solaris offshoots, alongside OpenIndiana and Nexenta, that can run on the machine. 12 Tribblix runs on any sun4u, including the Ultra 1, and on UltraSPARC III and newer systems, with CoolThread systems (sun4v) being the intended platform. 34 Tribblix focuses on x86, with SPARC support being beta and less stable, though it was historically the only distribution that supported SPARC decently, primarily supporting Sun-era hardware. 56 Pkgin is available for pkgsrc on Tribblix. 7
