recap

Claims: 4

Recapping involves tearing down, cleaning, and reassembling the device, followed by slowly testing everything. 1 Ordering extras of cheaper capacitors with BOM orders can help build up stock, as modern designs use fewer capacitors than older designs like the Octane Lucent supply. 2 A motherboard can be recap'd without a preheater by flooding the holes with solder, cutting the capacitor legs short, and wiggling the capacitor in. 3
