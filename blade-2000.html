Blade 2000

Claims: 44

The Sun Blade 2000 is a UNIX workstation that can run Solaris and, with SunPCi, OS X Leopard, though it is heavy and bulky, making shipping costs significant. 123 It uses FC-AL SSDs and has an HSSDC port, but lacks IDE, SATA, and external SCSI support, offering only FC-AL and IDC-50 SCSI. 415 The machine has reliability problems, including motherboard failures, unreliable CPU connectors, and overheating in standby mode, and it is generally considered less reliable than the Blade 100. 6789 It can boot Solaris 10 relatively quickly, but may fail to boot after a power cycle due to disk label issues, and Solaris 10 may not have been optimal for the machine. 101112 Performance-wise, it outperforms the Ultra 10 and Blade 100, but users report it can feel slow or less satisfying than the Octane despite having more cores, higher clock speed, more RAM, and better storage and graphics. 1314151617 It can be difficult to work with but functions properly, and it is recommended for Solaris use, though it was difficult to sell due to its incomplete nature. 18192021 The Sun Blade 2000 is a notable SGI machine, alongside the SPARCserver 5, Sun Blade 100, Sun Blade 1000, the Netfinity 5000, and Origin 300, with the Sun Blade 1000 now in parts. 22 The Blade2000's performance is influenced by the MIPS architecture and R10k CPUs. 23 A user has a Blade2000 with a full GNU toolchain including GCC 8.5 and can run Quake2. 24
