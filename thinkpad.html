ThinkPad

Claims: 22 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see ThinkPad, AMD.

The ThinkPad 7-row keyboard is considered the best laptop keyboard, and some 750/360 keyboards share the same layout, though ThinkPads have only two mouse buttons instead of three. 12 ThinkPad plastic is generally better than that of other plastic laptops, and one user reports their ThinkPad remaining functional after two years of heavy travel and replaced parts. 34 A ThinkPad pen works on a Cintiq, though its buttons may behave oddly. 5 Autocad 2000 runs on a ThinkPad, and compiling Python on a ThinkPad takes less time than on an Octane2. 67 Small magnets like those in a typical ThinkPad do not affect modern hard drives. 8 The BitLocker-encrypted SSD in a ThinkPad failed diagnostics, which may indicate a bad disk or may simply be due to encryption. 9 A ThinkPad prompting to set the time once and then not again may indicate an issue with the clock battery or motherboard. 10 Every ThinkPad one user has used has had firmware bugs, a subpar battery, and a rushed build. 11
