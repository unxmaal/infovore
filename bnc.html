BNC

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Video Graphics Array.

  • Removing the genlock cable can affect display synchronization; their setup with BNCs disconnected shows no change. (user-59b9, exchange 36944, 2023-03-26; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The O2 uses VGA with sync on green, while 4D machines use BNC for the same. (user-b7c4, exchange 95843, 2019-11-26; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • SGI and Sun used 13W3 connectors for their monitors because older machines used BNC jacks. (user-b1da, exchange 11109, 2020-09-22; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • 3W3 to BNC, then BNC-to-VGA. I've done this for an IBM RS/6000 video card and used tees for the couplers. (user-07ed, exchange 235323, 2023-02-17; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • BNC can be used to serialize SCSI over multiple BNC connectors. (user-0de0, exchange 226485, 2026-05-19; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • BNC to VGA or 13w3 connectors have the same color order, but if not, BNC plugs can be swapped. (user-07ed, exchange 235815, 2025-10-16; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • BNC to VGA can handle HD15 connections easily. (user-7b1c, exchange 207147, 2022-06-08; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
