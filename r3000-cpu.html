R3000 CPU

Claims: 13 Part of R3000

The R3000 is a 32-bit CPU that runs at 36 MHz and has store delay slots, with 64-bit values not present in 32-bit mode. 123 It was used in DECstation /240 systems and in the large, heavy SGI Indigo, and was outdated by 1993. 456 It was necessary for some very old software that does not run on newer SGI CPUs, and NWS 3860, 3260, or 1580 can run on it at 100% speed. 78 The R3000 CPU was not available for the IP4, where the R2000/R2300 ran at 12.5 MHz, and boards with an R3000 CPU are rare on the professional IRIX platform, especially at 12 MHz. 910 The 4D/120 GTX did not sell well due to the expensive PowerPath architecture and initial supply issues with the R3000 CPU. 11
