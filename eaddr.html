eaddr

Claims: 8

The eaddr is the MAC address stored in the EEPROM at 0xfa to 0xff. 1 The PROM does not include eaddr, so alternative methods such as using ping, examining router attached devices, capturing MAC addresses with Wireshark, or checking bootp logs in BootRizer may be required to determine the address. 2 The eaddr is not set on the Fuel, and the setting in /etc/nvram may have been reset, so it should be checked and set if necessary. 345 If the console settings or MAC address are incorrect, Irix may crash, and setting eaddr may fix it. 6 If you have the new ROM, you can set eaddr using setenv -f eaddr with the MAC address on the back of the machine. 7
