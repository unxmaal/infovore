HPIB

Claims: 5

HPIB is an industrial control bus used for test equipment, originally called HPIB and later known as GPIB or IEEE-488, with slow speeds of about 1 MByte/s. 1 The box uses MFM for the hard drive and connects everything over HP-IB, assigning SCSI IDs via rotary switches. 2 The MFM interface is used for hard drives, can be connected to an HPIB board, and also supports floppy drives. 3 HP-IB terminations are large blocks of metal. 4 There is no information confirming whether a digital scope for HPIB exists. 5
