G5 Quad

Claims: 14 Part of G5

The G5 Quad is a liquid-cooled machine that generally has fewer issues than earlier models, though it may have problems waking up the monitor on the ATI card. 1 It can use PCIe SSDs, though SATA SSDs are also viable, and can boot from USB and FireWire. 23 The CPU and RAM board can be removed by undoing screws and is a direct swap, and the machine does not use ECC RAM. 45 Coolant leakage and internal damage have been reported, and a CPU can fail due to thermal shock and may require reballing. 67 A power supply or motherboard can cause issues, and converting to air cooling is a significant project that improves reliability and can be downloaded from a Mac Rumors thread. 89 A pristine G5 Quad is difficult to source today. 10
