JIT

Claims: 12 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see MAME.

The Just-In-Time compiler (JIT) is used for performance and is the method that usually gets the fastest results. 12 The JIT approach for N64 emulation has compatibility issues, and the JIT would likely be the most difficult part. 34 The porting process will start with a sans-JIT JVM to ensure everything works, then move to implementing the JIT. 5 The optional JIT from unxmaal is not used, as the interpreter is faster. 6 A Berkeley packet filter JIT would allow for a modern firewall. 7 JIT needs to be compiled for the platform and is in development. 8
