fglrx

Claims: 9 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Radeon.

The fglrx driver was available for ia64 platforms but not currently supported, whereas radeon is supported on i386 and x86_64. 1 The SGI 3450 requires a supported graphics card; the radeon driver does not work properly, and on SLES9 only older cards are supported. 2 Newer cards like the Radeon 9200 and FireGL R200-series may work with the fglrx driver, and SLES9 is used with fglrx. 23 The X1s work with the radeon driver but lack 3D acceleration, so fglrx may be required for 3D acceleration. 4 The PCI Radeon 9200 SE can run X with the radeon driver on SLES9 with ProPack, but lacks 3D acceleration and crashes with fglrx due to undefined symbols, while SLES11 fails to display properly. 5 Using fglrx instead of radeon might resolve the issue, and Debian 7 with fglrx running in realtime can provide acceleration on Altixes. 67
