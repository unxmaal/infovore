E10k

Claims: 11

The E10k is a system consisting of many CPUs with no graphics pipeline, capable of performing large CPU renders, and can function as a good email server. 12 It supports SBus and can run Notes. 34 The E10k requires significant power, drawing up to 40A 3-phase power, which necessitates calling an electrician to add additional circuits. 56 The E10k can continue running without its management station, but it cannot be reconfigured or rebooted in that state. 7 Control workstations like the Sun e10k were not common, though similar systems existed, such as HP's V class. 8 The E10k should have been discontinued. 9
