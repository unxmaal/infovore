MultiLink

Claims: 13

The MultiLink is a device that converts digital output from an O2 to DVI, and it takes an analog input. 12 It allows for 1600 usage on any machine with DVI, and it is available for the 1600SW. 34 The 1600SW is a barebones panel with signal processing handled by the MultiLink or an add-on card. 5 You can use a MultiLink to convert VGA/DVI to OpenLDI for the O2. 6 The V10 requires a MultiLink, a 13W3 to VGA cable, and a VGA to analog VGA adapter to support 1600x1200 resolution, and it can use a MultiLink for video output, though it may have issues with artifacts. 78 The V10 had issues with analog video from VPro and MultiLink, showing jailbars and artifacts, and the quality of caps in MultiLink and V10 can affect video output. 910 The Octane mirrors the display on the DCD, and in IRIX, MultiLink can fail. 11 Apple's MultiLink cards support 1600x1024 resolution in OSX. 12
