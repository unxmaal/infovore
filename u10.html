U10

Claims: 21

The U10 defaults to DB25 serial output, which requires an adapter or cable conversion. 1 The U10 implements PCI 2.1, while the Blade 150 uses PCI 2.2 or 2.3. 2 On U5/U10, the onboard Mach64 is disabled if another card is installed, but not on Blade 100/150. 3 The U5 can support up to 512MB of RAM, while the U10 can support up to 1GB, due to the original RAM modules being double-height and requiring the removal of the floppy drive to fit. 4 The U5 and U10 IDE controllers could not do DMA as implemented; they could only do PIO Mode 4 unless properly configured. 5 The U10 lacks a PCMCIA slot, as it is not officially documented and has no part or FRU number. 6 The U10 was intended to have a smart card reader. 7 The UltraAXi is a rackmount version of the U10 with an onboard Symbios 875 SCSI controller. 8
