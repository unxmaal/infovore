P1

Claims: 4

The VME 9U board has three connectors labeled P1, P2, and P3, with P3 unused by the VME 32 standard but used by SGI for the MP bus. 1 P1 is a VME standard with power, 8 data bits, 24 address bits, interrupt, arbitration, and an interrupt line. 2 P2 extends P1 with 8 more data bits, 8 more address bits, and 2 rows of user-defined pins, while P3 is all user-defined. 2 OpenStep might run on P1, but it is picky about hardware. 3
