IBM Model F keyboard

Claims: 13 Part of IBM

The IBM Model F keyboard has a unique layout with a click that becomes more of a bang at a certain point, and its layout is XT and considered insane. 12 The keyboard is durable, with no bad keys after 37 years. 3 The tactile feel of the Model F keyboard is sharper and crisper than the Model M, but the tactile bump is lighter and takes less force to actuate, with the overall sound dominated by the spring pinging and echoing afterkey. 4 The new-fangled reproduction Model F keyboard is a mixed experience compared to the regular Model M. 5 AT to PS/2 adapters were common in the '90s but modern equivalents are hard to find, so refurbishing a Model F keyboard is a better option. 6 The keyboard is compatible with an IBM 5155 machine. 7
