GLX

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

GLX is the X server library that supports glx, the standard for forwarded OpenGL, while GLU is the math utility library. 12 GLX distinguishes two states, indirect state and direct state, and chooses visuals, sometimes not optimally. 34 GLX over network is built into the protocol and just works for old GL programs but may choke with modern 3D programs, and it is suitable for static display but not interactive use due to potential latency. 56 With an X server running on a GL-capable system, remote OpenGL over GLX works great, but localhost:0.0 forces GLX through TCP instead of direct rendering. 78 GLX did not exist until IRIX 4; X in IRIX 3 was optional until 3.1G; 4sight fused NeWS with older mex GL windows baked into the kernel. 9 Xephyr does not support GLX, which is required for running OpenGL applications from an SGI box. 10
