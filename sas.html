SAS

Claims: 25 Background: 6 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SATA, SCSI.

SAS is distinct from SCSI in terms of compatibility and limitations, as SAS controllers can support SATA drives but not SCSI drives. 1 SCSI can be implemented over FC, SAS, iSCSI, or FireWire. 2 LVD SCA SCSI was used before SSDs became prevalent, and FC-AL was emerging before SAS became dominant. 3 Parallel SCSI disks were rarely larger than 300GB nominal, while larger capacities were more common with SAS. 4 HP-UX supported SAS, but the support might have been over PCIe, requiring a newer OS version or patch set for PCI-X SAS cards. 5 RAID cards could only do SAS until Westmere days. 6 SAS data and power connectors are connected. 7
