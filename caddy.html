caddy

Claims: 11

A caddy is a device used to hold and swap drives in older SGI systems, which use a proprietary connector requiring a caddy for easy swapping. 1 The caddy's latching mechanism is simple, involving a rotating handle and plastic prongs, and can be assembled without screws by printing parts hollow or with axles. 2 A caddy is useless without its IDE interface board, which can be stolen from an Acer laptop, leaving no way to use a caddy without a replacement PCB. 34 A caddy should always be used with its original IDE interface board, but a caddy without its IDE interface board can be fixed by stealing the IDE channel from elsewhere on the board. 56
