Max Impact

Claims: 15 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo2.

It offers faster performance and more graphics pipelines than Solid Impact, which has no texturing. 12 Max Impact can hold up to 4 MB of TRAM, while High Impact may have 1 or 4 MB of texture RAM. 32 Max Impact graphics cards are limited by VRAM and bus speed, and TRAM improves texture handling but requires testing. 4 It is considered excellent for CAD, even though the texture RAM is not required by the application. 5 The Indigo 2 with Max Impact was slightly better than the entry Octane, but the Octane had better I/O capabilities. 6 Max Impact has limited space for cooling solutions and may have issues with framebuffer memory or the data line to memory. 78
