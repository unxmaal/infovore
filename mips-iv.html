MIPS IV

Claims: 9 Part of MIPS Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see R8000, SGI O2.

MIPS IV is a superset of MIPS III and is compatible with all existing versions of MIPS. 1 The MIPS IV architecture is found in MIPS processors 10k and later, and R8000 is the first to have MIPS-IV instructions. 23 MIPS IV processors are required for newer software, limiting older systems to ancient software. 4 The 68k architecture has divide and multiply instructions, which MIPS IV lacks. 5 GCC can generate code optimized for MIPS64, which is a more modern architecture than MIPS IV. 6 The MIPS-IV version compiled with MIPSpro does not necessarily perform better than the MIPS-III version compiled with GCC. 7 An emulated Indy R5k can run MIPS IV code, though it's limited to IRIX 6.5.22. 8 The O2 emulation in MAME runs MIPS IV and can support IRIX 6.5.30. 9
