R8K CPU

Claims: 10

The R8K CPU is a somewhat rare processor specialized for double floating-point number crunching, offering impressive floating-point performance but weaker integer capabilities. 12 It was not faster than the R10k for floating-point operations; it was slightly slower and less usable than the R4k at 200 MHz. 3 The R8K CPU is notorious for reliability issues due to sensitive cache chips that can fail from static, and the older PROM revision can fail the CPU POST test with a warm CPU. 45 It can be swapped in an Indigo2, making it a practical option for specific tasks, though NetBSD does not support the R8k CPU. 67
