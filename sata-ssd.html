SATA SSD

Claims: 17 Part of SATA Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see ZFS.

SATA SSDs can be used as OS drives in various systems through different adapters, such as an ATA66-to-SATA adapter in G4 Quicksilver and MDD machines, where performance is probably as fast as SCSI HDDs. 1 An O2 and Octane2 can use a SATA SSD via a SATA to IDE adapter combined with an Acard IDE 133 MB/s to 68 pin SCSI adapter, achieving up to 33 MB/s on an O2 and 38-39 MB/s on an Octane2. 2 A reverse 68-pin to SCA adapter (AEC-7722) and StarTech PATA to SATA adapter with a SATA SSD can potentially saturate an O2's bus. 3 A SATA SSD connected to the ATA 100 Drive controller using a port converter is as fast as a SATA card linked to an SSD via a SATA cable. 4 The only widely available adapter to use a SATA SSD in an SGI costs around $1,000 and is from 2sans on eBay. 5
