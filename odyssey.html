Odyssey

Claims: 14

The Odyssey series of GRX cards can have 8MB or 104MB TRAM and are functionally similar to a small IR. 12 The ODYSSEY supports two 1920x1200_60 video modes with different pixel clocks, where the `1920x1200_60-U2410` mode matches the monitor's EDID but does not work well while the stock `195.000MHz` mode works fine. 3 The Odyssey series of GRX cards do not support multitexturing in Quake ports, even though they have multiple texture units. 4 The original version of GL Quake only runs on O2 and MGRAS hardware and not on Odyssey, so a modern port like Neko's Darkplaces is needed. 5 EMXI hardware drew a lot of power while Odyssey drew very little, and MXI has better performance stability with PowerAnimator than Odyssey. 67 Odyssey diagnostics are on one of the 6.5 discs, and the Odyssey has a PROM code, with the 657 model seeming to have most of the graphics PROM code. 89 ODYSSEY shows up in env, temp, and fans, likely via I2C, though environmental monitoring chips on the Odyssey board and motherboard may report incorrect temperatures or voltages, potentially causing issues. 1011 Xsgi is monolithic, so there are no per-card modules, and Odyssey cannot be mixed with Mardi Gras. 12
