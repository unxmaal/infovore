SAS ports

Claims: 3 Part of SAS

SAS ports generally support SATA drives, but SATA drives do not work with SAS ports in the reverse direction, and the reverse connection is possible but not always supported. 1 This enclosure probably has two 6Gb or 12Gb SAS ports on the back, with each disk dual-connected to each channel. 2 The motherboard and CPU require more than 130 watts of 12V power. 3
