SkyWriter

Claims: 11

The SkyWriter was a 4D rack system with dual graphics pipes, often designed for VGX or VGXT graphics using RM3 boards instead of RM2 boards, and could drive a single display. 12 The SkyWriter had 4 CPUs and dual graphics pipes, whereas the Predator had 8 CPUs and 1 graphics pipe. 3 The SkyWriter was SGI's first system to run REACT, the realtime kernel. 4 The PSU for a tall rack PowerSeries, like a SkyWriter, has triple modules, a 5V@250A module, cannot run on 110VAC, is 'always on', and doesn't respond to the power switch. 5
