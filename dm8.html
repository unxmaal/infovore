DM8

Claims: 4

The SGI DM8 is a rebranded M-Audio 7.1 sound card, also available from SGI as the DM 8, and is the same as the SGI RAD Digital Audio PCI card, which is the M-Audio Revolution 7.1. 12 The DM8 lacks digital audio input, which is inconvenient given the speaker's proximity to a mixing console with coax digital audio input. 3 The DM8 was intended for Tezro/Fuel/O350 machines, while the RAD was intended for Octane-era machines. 4

Sources

  2. The SGI RAD Digital Audio PCI is the M-Audio Revolution 7.1, also available from SGI as the DM 8. (user-0251, 2022-02-10, exchange 102250, check: supported)
