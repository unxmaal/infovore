IP35

Claims: 80 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

IRIX 6.5.30 is good for IP35 machines, and the Synergy 1.4.2 client works on IRIX64 6.5 07202013 IP35. 12 The IP35 uses 16 kB pages and supports USB in PROM, with many units coming with USB PCI cards from the factory. 34 The IP35 has an Iris Audio Processor version EMU revision A4, and the M-Audio Revolution 7.1 works on the Octane and IP35. 56 The IP35 supports SAS/SATA PCI cards, including the LSI SAS3041X-R, LSI SAS3442X, LSI SAS3442X-R, and LSI SAS3080X-R. 7 Connecting an SSD to a SAS adapter supported by an IP35 will not boot the system; the system HD must be connected to the onboard SCSI adapter. 8 The IP35 does not support dumping the boot ROM contents over serial with the standard command, and its serial port baud rate is 38400. 910 The IP35 uses a different Dallas chip than the Snaphat, and requires initialization for the Dallas chips to boot. 1112 The IP35 does not have bodge wires, and its boards commonly have a bank of RAM that goes bad. 1314 The IP35 platform is used by the Origin 300, Fuel, and Tezro machines, and its diagnostics come in flavors for Fuel, Tezro, and Origin class hardware. 1516 IP35 systems use three-phase power and, like the O350, consume only a few watts when powered down with only L1 running. 1718 The IP35 runs at 66 MHz, faster than the O2/Octane's 33 MHz, and does not use a bridge. 1920 IP35 is SN1, whereas the IP27 is SN0. 21 The IP35 supports standard block sizes including 512B, SAS PCI cards, and NVMe drives, and its IO9 includes SCSI (controllers 0/1) and IDE (controller 2) controllers. 22232425 The IP35 has rudimentary USB HID support at the PROM level, works before IRIX boots, and supports tablets. 2627 Installing IRIX via LOVE on IP35 hardware causes TLB errors and crashes, while Reanimator works fine, and certain TFTP servers can cause issues. 28 IP35 has different allowed binaries than the O350/Tezro, and IRIX 6.5.28 works on Octane but has glitches on IP35. 2930
