Dials

Claims: 3

Dials are input devices for SGI systems, alongside light-up keyboards, and provide the same functions as the Spaceball and Spacemouse, namely local graphical transformations such as rotation, pan, and zoom. 12 The SGI dials require a power adapter with a five-pin DIN connector, which plugs into a fused cable that also connects to the Octane. 3
