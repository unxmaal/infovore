Newtek

Claims: 6

Discreet FX bought out NewTek's Video Toaster rights and open-sourced some of the code, though some is lost, and NewTek had earlier gave away the Amiga toaster suite software and source code. 12 NewTek used MIPS PCs as render slaves for the initial Lightwave Amiga version, but after SGI canceled MIPS-PCs, they switched to Dec Alpha nodes for rendering, branding them as ScreamerRender for MIPS and Raptor for Dec. 3 NewTek created custom plastic cases for these render solutions and sold them as exclusive products. 4 NewTek had a beta release of Lightwave 3D 4.0 for PC in 1995, which was originally ported by XAOS, which didn't share its licensing system, so NewTek removed copy protection in Lightwave 5.0 until a compatible solution could be implemented. 56
