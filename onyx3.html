Onyx3

Claims: 10 Part of Onyx

The Onyx3 is equivalent to the Origin 300, 350, and 3k models, and is more space-efficient than the Onyx2/Origin2k, which is more compact but requires more setup and space. 123 An Onyx3 IR contains two O350 bricks and a g-brick. 4 The G-brick is limited to four RM11s due to power draw, and compute bricks are powered separately with grounding. 5 Onyx3 can have fake Vpros, which shouldn't be used. 6 Removing `graphics_sgi` from an Onyx3 can affect texture compatibility on lower tram amounts and non-24-bit color systems, resulting in non-functional textures whether present or absent. 7 An Onyx3 PSU has failed. 8
