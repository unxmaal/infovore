floppy drive

Claims: 71 Background: 5 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Floppy disk variants, Amiga 2000, SCSI.

Floppy drives are used across many machines, including the ST, IIgs, Amiga A2000 and A500, Mac, C64, SGI Ultra 5, PDP-11/03, and MegaDrive add-ons. 1234567 Floppy drives can fail for various reasons, including stuck mechanisms, gear problems, faulty components, leaking SMD capacitors, corrosion, and seeking errors. 8910111213 A floppy drive not properly plugged in can cause a computer to hang on boot, and a DOS virus can render a floppy drive unusable with DOS disks. 1415 Some systems require a working floppy drive, such as PS/2 machines for reconfiguration, SoftWindows for drive A or B, and devices needing firmware images. 161718 Real floppy drives are generally recommended over USB floppy drives for imaging disks, as USB floppies can have more errors. 19 Several systems include a floppy drive, including the Indigo1, the Quadra 660AV (which has it in the front), and the Power Macintosh 4400 (which has it in a non-traditional position). 202122 The Indy model has a cutout for a floppy drive, which was used for transporting documents, though FAT support for IRIX via floppy exchange was never implemented. 2023 The T1000 laptop also had a floppy drive, and files can be transferred to a floppy drive on HPUX by writing a tar file directly to the floppy and reading it back. 2425 A floppy drive can be the newest part in system rebuilds, and a ZIP drive may be missing and need to be sourced. 2627
