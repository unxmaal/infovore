GUS

Claims: 5

The GUS is a demoscene sound card with good compatibility for Sound Blaster games, though some games would crash. 1 There are two different ways to emulate Sound Blaster with a GUS. 2 The GUS sound card can have DMA issues, and configuring the same DMA for playing and recording may resolve it. 3 Second Reality crashes when loading samples to GUS if the secondary IDE is the primary boot drive, suggesting a false assumption in the GUS code. 4 Orpheus II works out of the box with the same drivers as GUS and SB Pro. 5
