picopsu

Claims: 4

The internal picopsu can be replaced with a female ATX to male ATX connector from the ct60e and a small FlexATX PSU. 1 Silent operation is possible with a PICO PSU or a modern silent PSU and some Noctua fans. 2 GDemu should work everywhere without any problems with a picoPSU, but without a picoPSU, extra resistors can be added to the GDROM drive supply line or a YouTube video can be consulted for alternative solutions. 3 A smaller PICMG backplane can be used to consider a compact system with a picopsu. 4
