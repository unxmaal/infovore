SCA80

Claims: 8 Part of SCA

SCA80 is a form of parallel SCSI, distinct from SCA40, which is Fibre Channel. 1 SCA80 SCSI disks can be challenging to interface with HBAs that use different connectors. 2 There is a 68-pin to SCA-80 adapter available for use with SCA80 ports. 3 For cheap storage, a SCA80 to 50pin SCSI adapter with a U160 or U320 disk is recommended. 4 Older 50-pin or HD50 based SCSI disks cannot fit an SCA adapter, so SCA80 should be used from the beginning. 5 The SCA80 drive has 32 MB RAM and can run hinv in the monitor shell. 6
