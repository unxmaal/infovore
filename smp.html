SMP

Claims: 11

In a multi-processor system, NUMA architecture is simpler to implement than SMP for design purposes. 1 Traditional Unixes did not support SMP in that year. 2 The OS/2 can support multiprocessors, but it does not make it faster; a version supporting SMP is needed. 3 eComStation added ACPI support which enabled SMP without needing IBM's SMP driver, making it standard for OS/2 after that point. 4 Quake can be compiled to support SMP, which is currently disabled in existing ports. 5 The DECsystem 5800 is notable for offering SMP with ULTRIX, making it the only DECstation to do so. 6 The VWs are SMP machines with a unified graphics chip and compatibility issues with standard consumer add-on graphics cards even when running Windows. 7 A dual 20MHz 88k was difficult to boot and build software on, despite being an SMP machine. 8
