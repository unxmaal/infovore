4D/35

Claims: 31

The 4D/30 and 4D/35 are IP12 systems, the same as i1 and Onyx boards, while the 4D/20 and 4D/25 are IP6 systems. 12 The 4D/35 has a 6U VME slot, whereas the Indigo uses two GIO slots. 3 The 4D/35 used either Eclipse or Express graphics systems, and it can use the Elan graphics board, though this is rare. 45 The Elan graphics board exists for the IP12 (4D/30, 4D/35) but requires some impossible to find adapter boards, and a 4D/30 or 4D/35 can use a GR2 with the adapter board. 67 The 4D/35 requires careful cooling, as the fan should be replaced if noisy or slow, since it can cause crashes or lockups. 8 The 4D/35 blows air out the back and takes it in at the front, and there is a noise reduction kit that mounts to the back of the chassis. 9 The 4D/35 machine can use Elan, but it's rare. 5
