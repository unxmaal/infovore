Onyx2

Claims: 235 Background: 14 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx2, SGI Origin 2000, SGI Octane.

With Octane

The Octane resembles a single node Onyx2, and the two machines are part of the O2/Octane/Onyx2 triad, with an Octane being a Numalinkless Origin200 and an Onyx2 being an Origin2k. 123 The SGI Origin 2000 and Onyx 2 used the same drive sleds as the Octane, and Octanes are lighter than Tezros and Onyx2s. 45 Octane RAM is different from O2000 RAM, which is compatible with O200 and Onyx2, and the O2K and Onyx2 have a different shoebox than the Octane, allowing for PCI shoebox module installation under the IO6G. 67 The Onyx2 graphics can be more modular, allowing options like TVO and GVO, while the Octane's video options are more integrated, and Onyx2 options can work on Octane2 for similar functionality. 8 Fusion and Jaleo run on Octane or Onyx2, and an Alesis ADAT3 interface can be used with an Onyx2 and Octane. 910 The DM2 card is for the HD DMediaPro option on Onyx2 and Octane2, and SGI made DVCPro hardware for the Onyx2/3x00/Octane. 1112 The Octane's PSUs need recapping, and the Onyx2 is similar. 13

With Origin 2000

The Onyx2 is based on the Origin 2000, which was a Cray design SGI acquired in the nineties. 14 The Onyx2 adds a graphics module above the O2000 processor module, and its compute module is basically an O2k. 1516 The Onyx2 deskside compute section is half an O2K deskside in terms of node boards, crossbars, and routers, which is not enough for a valid NUMA topology. 17 Onyx2, O200, and O2000 share compatible memory, and the IO9 motherboard is the same as used in the Onyx2 and O2k. 1819 The Origin 2000 and Onyx2 power supplies are the same, with a redundant PSU part number of 013-2469-00x. 2021 The destination kit for Onyx2 and O2K includes installation discs, serial cables, a microphone, speakers, a mouse, some books, and other items. 22

With Onyx

The Onyx and Onyx2 are SGI systems featuring CPU boards (IP19, IP21, IP25, IP27, IP29) with two CPU options, graphics boards (G, GT, GTX with GM1/GM2, and MG1), and available Reality Engine firmware. 23 The Onyx uses a single massive centrifugal blower, while the Onyx2 uses a different cooling approach. 24 The Onyx2 is more reliable and more upgradable than the Onyx, with XIO cards readily available, though both can suffer from power supply issues. 2526 The Onyx2 is more compact but heavier than the Onyx, with wheels that shed and leave marks on floors, and it is easier to disassemble for transport. 27 OpenGL Multipipe 1.4.2 requires IRIX 6.5.15 or later, one of the Onyx, Onyx2, or Onyx 3000 servers, and a MIPS R10000 or later processor. 28 The Onyx or Onyx2 is less likely to have compatibility issues than VPro but is very heavy and bulky. 29

General

The Onyx2 is a separate machine from the original Onyx, sharing no common hardware and using a different backplane system, while having a smaller footprint. 3031 It uses NUMA architecture and can support up to 128 CPUs. 3233 The Onyx2 can be configured with one nodeboard and one RM card, or as a multi-rack system, and can support up to two node boards with booting possible over Numalink without a boot drive on the secondary board. 343536 It is used for 3D graphics and can be equipped with InfiniteReality4 for high graphical performance. 3738 The Onyx2 has a malfunctioning internal SCSI controller that prevents it from reading drives, but it can boot with external storage. 3940 It can run at 2560x1080 resolution with dual RM IR3. 41 The Onyx2 weighs over 200 lbs when fully loaded, and its noise level depends on its load. 424344 It can support four CADDuos for multiplayer Quake. 45 The Onyx2 is a Silicon Graphics workstation that is more graphically powerful than a Tezro for intensive workloads. 46 The Onyx2+ has two additional raster managers compared to a standard desk side Onyx2, which is not multipipe. 4748 Multi-seat mode requires additional monitors, keyboards, mice, and Base Graphics I/O cards for a multipipe Onyx2 system. 49 The Onyx2 is noisy, especially if the fans ramp up, and is more bearable but still not something you really want to be in the same room as compared to the Onyx1. 5051 The Onyx2 draws significantly less power than a well-equipped IR3 deskside, which can draw up to 900W. 52 The Onyx2 has a faulty internal SCSI plane and requires external disks. 53 The Onyx2 has a quad 400 MHz R12000 CPU, 2 GB RAM, IR3 graphics with two SCA sleds and drives; it has some corrosion, missing caps, and dust. 54 Corrosion can be removed with Scotch brite pads. 55 It contains the full runtime and development system of the Paradigm Simulations VEGA suite, which was used by the State Rail Authority of New South Wales to run a train driving simulator for several rail lines in NSW, Australia. 565758 The Onyx2 can be set up with two workspaces/screens using the Video Format Combiner, which can be saved to the EEPROM and accessed after reboot. 59 It can use RM11 raster memory boards, and a plan exists to use two RM11s in an Onyx2 for a cave setup. 6061 Onyx2 systems have experienced various hardware failures including PSU issues, CPU board malfunctions, XIO bridge explosions, and flaky fan trays. 62 Onyx2 systems sell for $1200 or less when parted out. 63 The Onyx2 is a chassis model produced by SGI, and it is the Origin 2k series. 6465 It is a desk-side system that can hold up to 4 nodes for 8 processors, though it loses XIO slots and can still have a PCI card cage. 66 The Onyx2 requires 240V power at 16A for a full configuration, though it can run on 120V if not maxed out. 67 It can perform surprisingly well in benchmarks, beating a 1GHz Tezro in Q3 benchmarks, and has impressive graphics capabilities. 686970 The Onyx2 chassis are distinctive, with a Kego backplane, and are difficult to move because they are missing trolleys. 7172 The midplane and IO6(g) capacitors on Onyx2 systems can leak and show signs of corrosion, and replacement requires matching ESRs and ripple. 73 The Onyx2 is more compact than the Onyx3, which requires more setup and space. 74
