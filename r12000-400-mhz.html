R12000 400 MHz

Claims: 3 Part of R12000

The default CPUs for the O2 were the RM7000 350 MHz and R12000 400 MHz, but due to software compatibility issues, some were swapped to R5200 300 MHz. 1 An Octane with a single R12000 400 MHz, 1 GB of RAM, and a V10 can run faster than some setups others have. 2 Measuring the PSU exhaust temperature gives a good indication of the internal temperature of an O2's CPU, which is about 108 F for an idle R12000 400 MHz. 3
