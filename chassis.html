chassis

Claims: 16

The HP C7000 chassis can accommodate different types of blades, but it requires at least power supplies and switches installed; the Itanium blades are large and heavy. 1 A chassis holding a group of blades (a 'brick') can be brought up independently of individual nodes. 2 Blades without a chassis cannot be NUMAlinkable. 3 The Sun Blade center has a switch for the whole chassis and each blade has its own network connection. 4 These chassis were used for Origin, Onyx 350s and the Tezro rack, they all had different side flaps. 5 You cannot put RE1 board set cards in a Sun chassis, as it will cause them to fail. 6
