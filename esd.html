ESD

Claims: 9

ESD (esound) is a sound API for portable sound, used by some applications such as xmms, and it runs on top of IRIX's own audio system, alongside OpenAL. 12 It supports IRIX audio devices, is used by many applications, and was responsible for the Indy and O2 systems. 34 On IRIX, esd has no startup script and must be started manually, and starting it as the user is necessary to avoid seg faults and ensure proper audio playback with XMMS. 35 XMMS is available on SGI systems from RSE and requires launching ESD for sound, and starting ESD before running Lamerdemo allows it to play at full FPS on some SGI systems. 67 xmms has problems with ESD for streaming audio but works with MP3s, while xmms2 does not support esound, which is the current audio system. 89
