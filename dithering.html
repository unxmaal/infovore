dithering

Claims: 6

The Indy can dither reasonably well, and running the application on an Indigo2 may require a fallback to 12-bit 444 color depth for double-buffered visuals, which could make dithering as noticeable as in the shots. 12 The Odyssey (V12) uses dithering, which anti-aliasing can sometimes mitigate. 3 VPro renders smoke trails from projectiles in Scorched3D with dithering, unlike PC versions. 4 Disabling dithering can only gain performance in visuals with 8 to 16 bit RGB or palette-based systems. 5 The default Xorg root window was often 8-bit with dithering on older systems, and this was done for a reason related to window decorations by 4dwm. 6
