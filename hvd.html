HVD

Claims: 22

HVD (High Voltage Differential) SCSI uses differential signaling, which reduces interference and signal degradation, making it more reliable and resistant to noise over longer distances and allowing longer cable lengths than SE or LVD. 12 HVD was primarily an interconnect for disk arrays and external storage, used for long-distance connections to disk arrays and disk shelves that might be distant, and was used in tape libraries and tape drives with long cables. 345 HVD and LVD are incompatible due to different voltage differences, and HVD can cause damage ("magic smoke") if mixed with SE or LVD. 67 HVD can use standard HD50, HD68, and VHDCI connectors in addition to its specific connector, and can work on LVD but not guaranteed. 8 HVD was used by Sun and DEC for external storage arrays, by Alphas for SCSI clustering and shared SCSI buses under VMS, by some HP9000 systems, and on Onyx1. 910 HVD drives were difficult to handle and rebuild, could throw medium errors, and required careful handling. 1112
