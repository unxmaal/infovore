LSI SATA controller

Claims: 3 Part of LSI

  • LSI SATA controllers work in Fuel but require 3G SATA SSDs, like those from late 2012 MacBook Airs, and are picky about compatibility. (user-3e2d, exchange 125173, 2026-08-27; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The LSI SATA controller with older 3Gbps SSDs works better on the Fuel than modern SSDs. (user-3e2d, exchange 226310, 2026-04-04; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The LSI SATA controller can be used with IRIX, but the drive must be detected in the LSI BIOS configuration on an x86 machine before it will work. (user-95db, exchange 106916, 2023-03-23; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
