IRIX headers

Claims: 4 Part of IRIX

Running `vagrant provision` repeatedly ensures the VM has the crosscompiler and Irix headers installed in /opt/gcc and /opt/binutils. 1 IRIX headers provide compatibility for SVR4 and IRIX code since 1983 but can introduce issues with defining standard macros like PATH_MAX. 2 The save/load functionality may have an off-by-1 error or be related to nonstandard syntax in IRIX headers, possibly requiring a GCC fixincludes patch. 3
