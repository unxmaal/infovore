O300

Claims: 53

With Origin 350

The O300 can use O350 PCI-X and MPX units for more RAM and PCI-X slots, and it has only PCI slots. 12 The O300 can use VPro cards internally, but the O350 cannot. 3 The O300 is much noisier than the O350, which is better engineered than the O300's problematic internal design. 45 The O300 and O350 have M serial numbers, while larger machines have L serial numbers, and reserializing bricks is possible but cannot force a nonstandard prefix. 67 The O300 and O350 use the same 2GB RAM kits as the O350, Fuel, and Tezro. 8 The O300/O350 lines are more common than the Tezro/Fuel. 9

General

The O300 main unit has four 500 MHz MIPS R14000 processors with 2 MB secondary cache running at 250 MHz, and it has 4 RAM slots that can take up to 4GB of RAM. 1011 The O300 can be booted from either of its two bricks, but a general exception error occurs on one brick (001c25) and can be resolved by issuing a 'reset' command, after which the machine boots fine. 12 The O300 can boot from TFTP and uses the SCSI emulator for booting. 1314 The O300 supports USB and can Numalink to another brick to make it Onyx. 1516
