Flame 2008

Claims: 4 Part of Flame

Flame 2008 uses N64 sparks, which are harder to find, whereas Flame 9 and 9.5 use N32 sparks. 1 The AXUS fibre does not work with Flame 2008 but works well with earlier versions of Flame and Smoke. 2 StoneWire software from a previous version is required to run Flame 2008 without crashing. 3 The Flame 2008 install guide works well for IRIX 5.5.28. 4
