30 pin SIMMs

Claims: 7

30-pin SIMMs are used for RAM in Apple IIgs computers, the Sun 3/60 (which uses SCSI for its HDD interface), and SGI systems. 123 They are reliable but expensive, with 64 MB units available for around $50. 34 Individual chips can be stolen from 30-pin SIMMs and used to upgrade lower-density ones. 5 Old RAM testers can identify faulty 30-pin SIMMs. 6
