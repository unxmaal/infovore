Unreal

Claims: 6

Unreal is a Windows-centric engine, so porting it to IRIX would be feasible but effort-intensive due to its Windows-centric nature. 1 Porting Unreal to IRIX requires fixing big endian and aligned access issues, but parts of the code are not open source. 2 Unreal and Unreal Tournament have OS X versions but lack background music due to being alpha. 3 Duke3D, Unreal, and Diablo run from mounted disk images in Toast on OS9. 4 In Unreal, you can bring down the console and type in timedemo 1. 5
