RM7000C

Claims: 12

The RM7000 kept data registers the same as the 5200, while the RM7000C added support for 64MB of L3 cache. 1 The RM7000C has a 512kB cache that sits on the SysAD bus and cannot run as fast as the CPU. 23 The RM7000C has more cache lines than the RM7000/A/B, which SGI cards are not wired for, making compatible cache ICs difficult to find. 4 The RM7000C works with primary, secondary and tertiary caches enabled. 5 FPGA was used on the first revisions of the RM7000C, while later SGI branded ASICs were used. 6 RM7000C CPUs with specific markings are fake and reballed parts, which might actually be RM7000, RM7000A, or RM7000B; they are not genuine and were likely removed from old boards and relabeled. 7 The RM7000C is a chip that was likely pulled rather than remarked. 8 DBX cannot identify an RM7000C in an O2 and assumes an R4k. 9
