thermal pad

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Heat sink.

  • SGI O2s typically use thermal pads between the CPU and heatsink, not grease, and replacing fans is common maintenance. (+1 similar) (user-17cb, exchange 11375, 2020-10-08 to 2026-06-14; 2 speaker(s), 2 conversation(s))
  • If thermal pads decompose, use light petroleum distillate naptha for cleaning. (user-c799, exchange 224454, 2025-01-25; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Replacing thermal pads on an Onyx IP25 board requires resetting and re-tightening CPU chips multiple times to ensure proper detection. (user-22f9, exchange 113879, 2025-01-25; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Thermal pads for R12K CPUs come in standard thicknesses and can deteriorate, becoming leaky. (user-c799, exchange 113881, 2025-01-25; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • Modern thermal pads can also degrade and leak, as experienced with a Lenovo PC's NVME SSD. (user-8de9, exchange 55024, 2025-03-08; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • A CX486DRX2 CPU works fine without a thermal pad, but the thermal pad alone prevents the system from booting from the hard disk. (user-3e2d, exchange 159394, 2025-05-10; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The Octane VPro uses thermal pads, not paste. (user-24f0, exchange 56473, 2025-05-10; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The thermal pads on SGI CPUs can degrade and leak liquid, which can damage the CPU and its socket. (user-17cb, exchange 55024, 2025-03-08; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
