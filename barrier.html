Barrier

Claims: 12

Barrier is a modern fork of the last open-source Synergy release, included in SGUG-RSE, and serves as a compatible alternative to Synergy2 for desktop setups. 123 It works well for combining two Linux boxes and SGI gear across multiple monitors, works with the SGUG Barrier client, and works with Teleffect. 245 Barrier has been forked to Input-Leap by its active maintainers and still works with the SGUG Barrier client. 5 It can be used to trap out to OF on both IRIX and Solaris by sending a break on the serial console, though the exact escape sequence for a break is unknown. 6 To set up Barrier on IRIX, ensure barrierc connects manually, store the SSH fingerprint, add the command to /usr/lib/X11/xdm/Xlogin, and setenv nogfxkbd 1 to allow starting without keyboard and mouse. 7 Barrier for ppc is not possible at the moment; it was ported to the X environment but not to IRIX. 8 Barrier works great but does not support Wayland, introduces some CPU overhead (more noticeable with intense mouse movement), and has a bug with mice having many buttons, though buttons 1/2/3 and scroll (4/5) work. 91011
