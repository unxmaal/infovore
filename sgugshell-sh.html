sgugshell.sh

Claims: 4

The `sgugshell.sh` script sets `LD_LIBRARYN32_PATH` until rse 0.0.4 is released. 1 Running via X on a Mac works, but running locally on IRIX gives an Xlib error; sourcing `sgugshell.sh` from `.bashrc` can help, but may require exiting and re-running bash. 2 Running `sgugshell.sh` results in a crash due to an unresolvable symbol in bash, and tweaking `LD_LIBRARYN32_PATH` did not resolve the issue. 3 Setting `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` is necessary for IRIX to find libraries, and running `sgugshell.sh` can manually set up library paths; reinstalling the tarball fixed the issue with 'libsudo_util.so.0' not being found. 4
