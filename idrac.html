iDRAC

Claims: 6

The iDRAC on the DL360 G7 has compatibility issues with certain HBAs and weak CPUs. 1 iDRAC can use HTML5 instead of the Java web app. 2 The iDRAC allows booting from a virtual CD over the network, a process that involves logging into the iDRAC, launching the virtual console, creating a virtual CD, mounting an ISO, and setting the boot device temporarily. 3 The PERC HBA can be switched to HBA mode using LSI firmware, which disables monitoring from iDRAC and requires a system reboot, may need alternative firmware reflashing, and involves finicky BIOS settings that may not work. 4 Dell PSUs have firmware for out-of-band management through iDRAC. 5 Reports exist of people unbricking iDRAC on Dell servers, which might help if the BMC fails to fully boot, but no such documentation exists for Lenovo servers, which require replacing the whole mobo in such cases. 6
