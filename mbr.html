MBR

Claims: 6

MBR supports 2TB volumes, though not all controllers can handle that capacity, and SGI systems manage multi-TB volumes over Fiber Channel with unknown specifics. 1 GPT is the replacement for MBR, providing a different disklabel and including a fallback MBR for BIOS compatibility. 2 UEFI bootloaders look for the ESP rather than the MBR. 3 Modern SSDs recognize when a new MBR or GPT partition table is written and interpret it as a full trim. 4 Some controllers can understand MBR and GPT partition tables and parse the free/in-use blocks directly from FAT/EXFAT/maybe NTFS, but not UNIX filesystems. 5 Installation steps differ based on whether you use MBR with BIOS or UEFI. 6
