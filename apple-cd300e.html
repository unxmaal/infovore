Apple CD300e

Claims: 3 Part of Apple

The Apple CD300e is a slow but very compatible CD drive used to install IRIX. 1 It can be used with an Octane for SCSI drives. 2 However, the PI does not recognize the Apple CD300e CD-ROM, causing SCSI bus errors during installation. 3
