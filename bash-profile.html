.bash_profile

Claims: 5

Shell startup scripts can be added by creating a .bash_profile or .profile in the user's home directory. 1 .bash_profile is for settings that should be inherited by everything, while .bashrc is for transient stuff, and .bashrc is not always run depending on how the client requests a shell. 2 To automatically include all binaries in /opt/local, add `export PATH=/opt/local/bin:$PATH` to your .bashrc or .bash_profile. 3 .bash_profile loads .bashrc, so .bashrc is not necessary for root, and .bash_profile is sourced before .bashrc in IRIX. 45
