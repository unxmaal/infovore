4sight

Claims: 10 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see IRIX, NeWS.

4sight was a NeWS-based window manager that replaced MEX on SGI systems, running alongside Xsgi to provide vector graphics isometric icons. It came after MEX and before 4DWM, and NeWS influenced the look and feel of both 4Sight and 4DWM. 4Sight came bundled with the Lite toolkit in NeWS 1.1. 123 A 68k SGI machine is required to run 4sight. It is available on up to 4D1-3.3.2, which works on systems as new as a Personal IRIS 4D/35. A screenshot of a very early version of 4sight running on an IRIS 3030 appears on page 223 of the document. 456 IRIX 4's desktop interface is similar to 4sight's. IRIX 5.1 does not have 4sight. 78
