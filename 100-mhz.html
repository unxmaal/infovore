100 MHz

Claims: 6

The R4400SC runs at 100 MHz with 1 MB cache, 64 MB of memory, and a 16 GB SSD. 1 DEC marketed R4000 upgrades for some DECstation 5000 models as 50 MHz, but they are essentially 100 MHz R4000 CPUs. 2 The Amiga runs at 100 MHz while the AA3000+ runs at 50 MHz, and 100 MHz can cause SCSI instability. 3 The SAS/SATA firmware version 1.26.0.0 supports SATA, and the card runs at 100 MHz. 4 The CPU SysAD bus has a maximum clock of 100 MHz. 5
