X2

Claims: 4 Part of X

The Onyx4 supports X1 and two types of X2. 1 The X1 is a 9700 and the X2 is a 9800, with X2 variants including R350 and R360 cores. 2 The `sgflash` tool for the Prism includes BIOS files for X1, X2, and X3 cards as well as Radeon 9600 GPUs, and these files are on the ProPack4 CD3. 3 Power for the X1 and X2 is via AGP Pro, using the extra edge connector fingers. 4
