Ultra160

Claims: 3

The Octane's SCSI ports are limited to Ultra160 because PCI drive controllers cannot be mounted in it. 1 The Tezro's controller is Ultra160, and the RedRock drive is likely U320. 2 For IIs, use Ultra160 or faster HD68 or SCA80 SCSI devices for better performance. 3
