Win9x

Claims: 6

Win9x was considered a poor daily driver, in contrast to Windows 2000 and XP, which were regarded as a significant improvement. 1 Win9x is one of several platforms targeted for a native port of a fork of Minecraft, alongside Solaris, IRIX, and PowerPC OSX. 2 Software requiring DirectX did not run on Win9x, unlike NT4, which ran Tyrian 2000 without issues. 3 A system includes DOS and Win9x clients, and possibly a Win 3.x client. 4 The GeForce 3080 GPU is not supported by Win9x. 5 Diablo can be played on Win9x, which some users prefer over running it through the devilutionX project on SGI. 6
