Quadro

Claims: 9

Quadro GPUs are designed for professional use, offering more VRAM, ECC VRAM, and better drivers compared to consumer cards. 1 Quadro GPUs are better for rendering wireframes and double-sided polygons, while GeForce GPUs focus on single-sided polygons used in games. 2 For Solidworks, Quadro and Radeon Pro WX cards are recommended due to specialized drivers and features like RealView mode. 3 Nvidia created a sync card for pass-through of SDI using DVI from Quadro. 4 Grid GPUs allow passing through GPUs to VMs, unlike Tesla and Quadro cards. 5 Only GTX/RTX doesn't play nice with passthrough - it has to be Quadro, Tesla, or Grid. 6
