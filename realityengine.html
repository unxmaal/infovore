RealityEngine

Claims: 40 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Onyx.

The RealityEngine is a graphics boardset that is one of the most complex pieces of hardware ever developed, containing at least ten extremely complex ASICs, some of which incorporate custom-designed CPUs. 12 Emulation of the RealityEngine is very unlikely due to its complexity and lack of documented hardware or microcode interfaces, though it is not entirely undocumented. 34 The RealityEngine handles matrix math for the GPU through an i860 processor, performing geometry and lighting calculations. 5 The RealityEngine is used for rendering tasks with a slow CPU and a powerful GPU, and is loud. 6 The RealityEngine is an unreliable component, appearing in systems such as the Indigo, Crimson, and PowerSeries. 78910 The RealityEngine requires a 6-fan module and disabling the fan speed controller to run at high fan speed, and is only supported in the Predator chassis. 111213 The RealityEngine can be used to upgrade the Onyx to InfiniteReality or a Crimson to RealityEngine. 14
