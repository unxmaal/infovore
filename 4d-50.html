4D/50

Claims: 12

The 4D/70 GT and 4D/50 boards use the same PROM, and the 4D/70 GT can be run as a 4D/50 with an IP4 at 8 MHz. 12 4D1-1 was exclusively for the 4D/60, while 4D/70 and 4D/50 did not get supported until 4D1-2. 3 4D/50 and 4D/70 should be working in the next release, but with no graphics at all. 4 The 4D/50 has no ZB2 and is likely broken without RAM, lacks a tape drive capstan, has a CDROM unrecognized by its PROM, and does not have a VME network card. 56 The 4D/50 'G' has issues with its tape drive, graphics, and SCSI devices, and may have problems with the backplane's IACK/BG jumpers. 7 A 4D/50 with a broken IP4 CPU board and hard disk can be repaired by replacing the CPU board and using another hard disk, and a Toshiba MK156FB hard disk was found later, tested on a PC, then installed in the 4D/50. 8 The ENP10 is rebadged as a Motorola MVME330, and manuals for the MVME330 help emulate the ENP10, allowing 4D/50 and 4D/70 to connect to the internet. 9 The MAME rom for 4D/50 and 70 has pre-set monitor variables in the nvram, and the most recent rom bundle for the ENP10 ethernet card is available. 10
