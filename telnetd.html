telnetd

Claims: 4

A/UX can run telnetd, along with NCSA httpd and ftpd, and can be configured with modern web servers like Apache, though exposing it to the internet is not recommended. 1 telnetd is part of gnu inetutils and can be built for mipsel-linux-uclibc. 2 ULTRIX 4.1 telnetd requires /bin/sh to be the ancient BSD /bin/sh, and an alternative compatible telnetd may be needed for other /bin/sh implementations. 3 Telnetd is often used in legacy embedded devices without regular updates, suggesting it should not be used in modern designs. 4
