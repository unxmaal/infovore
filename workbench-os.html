Workbench OS

Claims: 3 Part of Workbench Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Amiga.

The Workbench OS was Amiga's own operating system. 1 On Atari systems, the Workbench OS is compatible with a wide range of graphics cards and monitors. 2 The Workbench OS version numbering is inconsistent, with versions such as 24, 25, and 26 having different license version numbers, and the system uses flexlm for licensing. 3
