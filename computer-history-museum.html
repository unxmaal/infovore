Computer History Museum

Claims: 6 Part of computer

Shoreline Blvd in California, in facilities formerly used by SGI. 12 The museum holds the real teapot and contains a Cray that visitors can sit on. 34 LCM released Xerox Alto documentation and assets to the museum, most now available on Bitsavers, and a tape containing a copy of UNIX v4 from Bell Labs was discovered at the University of Utah and will be sent to the museum for recovery. 56
