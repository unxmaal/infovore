Sharp

Claims: 3

Sharp is a manufacturer of second-hand 4:3 LCDs, some of which (along with models from NEC and older Sony) support SOG without requiring any prom settings changes. 1 Sharp produced a CPU variant for the Game Boy that omits some status flags and instructions but adds others, including a zeropage-like addressing mode. 2 SANE supports a wide range of SCSI generic scanners, including models from Sharp. 3
