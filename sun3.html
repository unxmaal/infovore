Sun3

Claims: 20 Part of Sun Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Sun-3.

NetBSD runs on Sun2 and Sun3 architectures, and its first version was released at least 3 years after the Sun-3. 12 A 68k-based Sun3 is slow and difficult to use for compiling. 3 The Professional Iris boards can be plugged into a Sun-3, while SGI VME boards are not compatible with Sun3 or Sun4 systems due to user-defined P2 connectors. 45 Trade-up programs for Sun3 and Apollo systems contributed to the scarcity of these machines. 6 The Sun3's TERMPWR should be set to 21-22 on, 23-24 off to power terminators internally, with 23-24 open and 21-22 short to enable termination by the drive. 7 IPI is a high-speed serial bus for Xylogics disks, common on Sun3 machines. 8 Most Sun3 systems used the P2 bus, usually on the VME P2 connector, though not always; the P4 bus on 3/60 and 3/80 systems was different and not VME; Sbus was a standardized expansion bus that was both fast. 9 The Sun 3/80 uses a different keyboard than earlier Sun3s, Sun2s, and SPARCs, while sun2 and sun3 keyboards are the same (DB15 plug, but same protocol as later type 4/5 keyboards). 1011
