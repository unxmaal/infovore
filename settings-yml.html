settings.yml

Claims: 4

Ansible relies on Vagrant to map its variables, which Vagrant reads from settings.yml and a distro yml before provisioning the VM. 1 The settings for Vagrant are in settings.yml, which should be edited according to the README. 2 The clientether in settings.yml does not match the MAC address shown by the booterizer. 3 The installmirror setting in settings.yml for IRIX 6.5.22 bootstrapping should be 'http://' instead of 'https://sgi-irix.s3.amazonaws.com' to avoid access denied errors. 4
