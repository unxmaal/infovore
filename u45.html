U45

Claims: 13

The Sun U45 is a fast, usable workstation in the Sun systems lineup, which also includes the Blade 2500, and is about the same size as a Power Mac G5. 1234 The U45 ships with all 4 HDD trays and has a dual CPU board with one CPU installed. 56 The U25 and U45 workstations have PCI-X slots, with one 64/133 slot and the rest 64/66. 7 The U45 can netboot and root on iSCSI, and can boot from 10Gbit Ethernet. 8 The U45 can have bad components and may require replacement parts, and if TX, RX, and ground are connected correctly with minicom and you still get nothing from the U45, it likely has hardware failure. 910 The U45 has a header (J9) that controls power-on stability; moving it to '2 3' allows power-on but no serial communication. 11 The U45 is difficult to find at a reasonable price. 12
