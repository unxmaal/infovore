Octane III

Claims: 10 Part of Octane

The SGI Octane III is marketed as a personal supercomputer and exists as a real product. 12 It is a Supermicro slim twin design housed in a deskside case. 3 The Octane III is available in several configurations, including dual-socket and single-socket nodes with different processors, and could have up to 96 Intel Atoms. 45 It had two different versions regarding CPUs. 6 The Octane III was not a high-end machine like the original Octane, but more of a rebadged Octane. 78 There is a manual for the SGI Octane III, but it was never released. 9
