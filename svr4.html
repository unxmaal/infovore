SVR4

Claims: 20 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Sun Microsystems.

SVR4 is a UNIX release developed jointly by AT&T's Unix System Laboratories and Sun Microsystems, and it introduced the ELF format, which was later inherited by IRIX 5 and later versions. 1 SVR4 is the basis for SCO UNIX SVR5, HP-UX, and SINIX-Z 5.42, while A/UX is based on SVR2. 23 SVR4 can boot without a network cable and can run from floppies. 45 SVR4 has become irrelevant, similar to other UNIX variants. 6
