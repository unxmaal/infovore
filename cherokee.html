Cherokee

Claims: 12

The Cherokee is a vintage SGI machine whose power supply was manufactured by OEMs including LH Research, ACDC, PowerOne, and Cherokee. 12 Cherokee PSUs are common for the Octane, and both Cherokee and Lucent PSUs commonly fail. 34 A Cherokee PSU can fail due to leaking output capacitors or standby failures, and its Nichicon capacitors are known suspects that may leak eventually, though some have not failed. 56 The Cherokee PSU can be repaired for triage purposes, but unlike the PowerOne, a failing Cherokee in the Crimson can burn a PCB and is harder to repair. 78 The Octane's Cherokee PSU is easier to work with than the Lucent model, which is difficult to disassemble and has many large capacitors. 9 Schematics for the Lucius version (or Cherokee) are not readily available, and the Cherokee can boot without its fan but may overheat and freeze. 1011
