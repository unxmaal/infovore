devilutionX

Claims: 13

devilutionX is a port of Diablo to IRIX, part of a broader effort that has brought software such as GemRB, Doom, and Quake to the platform. 12 It works with SDL2 GL on the Octane and runs well at 1280x960 on an Octane2 2x600MHz V12, though it is slow at 1600x1200. 34 The original resolution for devilutionX was 800x600. 5 It does not work on MGRAS or O2, where an input bug causes it to die at launch. 67 user-bf68's build of devilutionX runs on the O2 but stops getting input after a few seconds. 8 devilutionX failed to load due to alignment issues with the MPQ library rather than SDL1.2, and it may report that it has no video device available. 910
