i860

Claims: 11

The i860 is a processor that saw limited use alongside the 88k and i960 processors. 1 It was often used for geometry processing, such as handling matrix math for geometry and lighting calculations in SGI's Reality Engine. 23 It was also used as a geometry unit in graphics systems like the 3DR/3DRender and in the NeXT Dimension, which contained an i860 processor and up to 32MB of RAM. 45 The i860 functioned as a coprocessor rather than running operating systems like IRIX directly. 6 Windows NT development started on the i860, codenamed 'N10'. 7 The i860 never achieved commercial success and was terminated in the mid-1990s, unlike the i960, which was successful in some embedded systems. 8 The Itanium processor design is similar to the i860 and faced similar issues. 9
