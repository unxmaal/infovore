bus bars

Claims: 4 Part of bus

Bus bars are usually made of aluminum or copper, with tinned copper being one common variant, and they cannot handle excessive torque. 12 The aux cables attached to the bus bars can cause short circuits that damage the blinkenlight panel and cable but not the PSU. 3 Bus bars can be recapped if needed after other components are replaced. 4
