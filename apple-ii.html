Apple II

Claims: 41 Part of Apple Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Apple II, Apple IIGS, Apple IIc.

The Apple II is known as the computer that took off, rather than the Apple 1. 1 It has 7 expansion slots, a 1 MHz CPU, and is easy to program for. 2 Its openness in architecture allows for custom hardware expansion. 3 The Apple II is a 16-bit computer, with the IIgs being 24-bit. 4 It had huge business market share for 8-bit systems, although it was not the most capable, cheapest, or fastest. 5 The IIc is a highly integrated Apple II with a built-in floppy and is the only one with a GUI. 6
