IRIX 6.x

Claims: 20 Part of IRIX

IRIX 6.x is a 64-bit release that introduced the o32 ABI alongside the n32 and n64 ABIs. 12 The first IRIX 6.x release came shortly after 5.0 as machine-specific 64-bit builds before becoming a universal upgrade. 3 IRIX 6.x dropped COFF support, which IRIX 5.3 retained for backwards compatibility. 4 The R10K required IRIX 6.x, and the R8K required version 6.0 or later. 5 6.x versions of IRIX might require enough RAM to work properly. 6

  4. IRIX 5.3 supports COFF binaries for backwards compatibility, while IRIX 6.x dropped COFF support. (user-d903, 2021-07-12, exchange 217161, check: supported)
  5. The R10K required IRIX 6.x, and the R8K required 6.0 or later. (user-b7c4, 2022-01-05, exchange 101438, check: supported)
