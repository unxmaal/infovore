Altix 4700

Claims: 3 Part of Altix

The Altix 4700 was formed by using IRUs to fill racks, with a NUMA switch at the top linking to other cabinets. 1 The Altix 4700 and the 350 use the same NUMA cables. 2 SLES 11 does not support 3D graphics on SGI Prism but might support 2D, and supports 3D graphics on Altix 4000. 3 An Altix 4700 might have had 2D graphics support. 3

Sources

  1. IRUs were used to fill racks in larger systems, with a NUMA switch at the top to link to other cabinets, forming the Altix 4700. (user-4e1e, 2019-11-23, exchange 4331, check: supported)
  2. the Altix 4700 and the 350 use the same NUMA cables (user-6d41, 2020-02-01, exchange 75044, check: supported)
  3. SLES 11 does not support 3D graphics on SGI Prism but might support 2D. SLES 11 supports 3D graphics on Altix 4000. An Altix 4700 might have had 2D graphics support. (user-03ad, 2023-07-08, exchange 216464, check: supported)
