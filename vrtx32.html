VRTX32

Claims: 5

The VRTX32 has issues with FIFO access collisions and requires an RTOS for semaphore tasks. 1 The VRTX32's graphics access to main RAM is paged and the register is write-only, requiring atomic operations. 2 The VRTX32's framebuffer control is coupled with FPGAs, which are difficult to reverse engineer. 3 The VRTX32 uses 3 priority IPC pipelines between the CPU and GPU for non-colliding operations. 4 The VRTX32 uses VBI interrupts on the 68K to track screen activity. 5
