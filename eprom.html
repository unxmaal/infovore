EPROM

Claims: 22 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see EEPROM, EPROM.

EPROMs must be burned by the user, as there is no built-in flashing capability. 1 The new EPROM was included in the package when purchased from Ross. 2 OBP on older machines is not flashable and requires burning an EPROM. 3 The mystery PROM (070-0815-000x or 070-1200-00x) is not included but should be readable like the EPROM. 4 The JJ has a 68000 CPU with an EPROM and SRAM, and its serial port is connected to a SCN68681 DUART. 5 You can probably use the DIP EEPROM socket and burn the EPROM onto one and disable the onboard PROM to recover a PROM line. 6 EPROM programmers, such as those offered by the German c't magazine, can be used to program EPROMs, including those without windows that can only be programmed once. 7
