compute bricks

Claims: 4

The SGI Altix350 line only sold bootable base-bricks and compute bricks; MPX and PCI-X bricks were not available, and the A350 compute brick lacked the riserboard with PCI-X slots. 1 The Altix systems have compute bricks with unique hexagonal skins and similar but not identical backplanes. 2 The G-brick in an Onyx3 is limited to four RM11s due to power draw, and compute bricks are powered separately with grounding. 3
