600 MHz

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The Octane and Octane2 are similar systems with different CPU options, and an Octane can have a V12 CPU running at 600 MHz. 1 Octane2 likely cannot exceed 600 MHz due to chipset limitations, and better MIPS CPUs could work with source code modifications. 2 Dual-R14000 600MHz CPUs for an SGI Octane cost £950. 3 user-ce6b paid 9250 yen for an Octane2 600 MHz with V12 in 2021. 4 Wipeout runs well on Octane2/V12/600mhz, looking like high-quality SGI era aesthetic. 5
