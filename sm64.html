SM64

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

SM64 runs on IRIX, but requires a workaround for GL_multitexture, and the IRIX bits of the port were incomplete. 12 The port can build with SDL1 or SDL2, and sm64-IRIX can build the US, JP, and EU versions of SM64 into an IRIX binary. 23 The O2 can run SM64 with some porting, and the SDL port for IRIX is fine. 4 Joystick usage is possible on a SGI and could be cool for game emulation and the IRIX port of sm64. 5
