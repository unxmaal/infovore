General

The Sun Ultra 10 is a single-processor workstation featuring a SPARC CPU, SBUS, and PCI slots, along with a UPA slot and tower chassis. 1234 It is closely related to the Ultra 5, sharing the same mainboard but adding more PCI slots via a risercard, and both models suffered from cooling issues. 15 The Ultra 10 ships with a 440 MHz CPU and 1 GB RAM, a maximum of 1 GB RAM, and supports an optional Creator 3D 3D graphics card. 678 It requires a Dallas battery to function properly, and maintenance may include replacing the hard drive and Dallas battery. 910 The Ultra 10 can run Solaris 10, OpenBSD, and Gentoo Linux, and can serve as a firewall or run business applications such as Cadence FlexLM and Informix. 1112131415 Its performance is limited, with reports of slow operation, and PCI compatibility is constrained by power consumption limits and requirements for 5V, 33MHz/32bit controllers. 161718192021 The Ultra 10 is an SGI workstation in the Ultra family, which also includes the Ultra 1E, 2, 30, 60, 80, and various Blade systems, all with varying UltraSPARC processors and features. 22 The Ultra 10 can run UPA cards, including XVR-600, Expert3D-Lite, and XVR-500 cards, as well as a Sun 13W3 adapter with a Creator3D. 232425 The Ultra 10 maxes out at 1GB of RAM, compared to the Ultra 5's 512MB, despite sharing the same motherboard. 2627 The Ultra 10 requires OBP firmware to boot Solaris, and upgrading OBP may resolve boot issues or be needed to install newer versions of Solaris. 2829 Booting Solaris 9 from DVD on an Ultra 10 fails due to a panic during vfs_mountroot, while Solaris 7 boots fine. 30 Ultra 5s and 10s may need NVRAM battery replacement. 31 An IDE to CF widget can work in an Ultra 10, though performance may be slow, and it can be used as a firewall. 3233 An Ultra 10 can use a 4-port zynx card under Linux. 34
