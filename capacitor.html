capacitor

Claims: 63 Background: 14 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Capacitor.

A capacitor can fail short or open, risking pram settings or system failure, and a shorted capacitor can cause a fuse to blow, with replacing the capacitor fixing the problem. 12 Signs of a failing capacitor include an electronic smell, a capacitor that broke off, a voltage drop from 2.48V to 1.2V causing the machine to shut down, and a small leak causing a pop, crackle, and machine shutdown. 3456 Capacitor problems can manifest as brightness distortion on a CRT TV that varies with the brightness of the line, color warbling on the neckboard of a PC CRT, and potential issues with the O2 speaker. 789 The resistance of a capacitor, known as ESR, should be as low as possible for optimal performance, and a power connector can be identified by the diode and capacitor at its end. 1011 An O2 PSU failure was caused by a capacitor issue, and a capacitor on the CPU board of a battered-down SS10 was the first thing that broke. 1213
