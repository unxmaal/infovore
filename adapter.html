adapter

Claims: 42 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Adapter, Video Graphics Array.

Adapters are used to connect various devices, such as allowing the Toughbook to use an IBM adapter or connecting older SGIs with proprietary keyboards to modern KVMs via an Indigo to PS/2 converter. 12 SCA 10K or 15K drives can be used with an adapter, providing faster, cheaper, and easier storage options, though some cheaper eBay drives require an adapter to 50pin and Indigo2 drives may need an adapter. 345 An SCSI3 drive can be used with an adapter, which may require different adapter heights depending on the system, and an Indy can fit both a smaller and a taller adapter. 6 Adapters without a termination jumper can cause issues, but the VHDCI to Centronics-50 adapter works for auto-termination. 78 The DB25 to DB9 adapter is likely a straight through adapter, and its pinout does not indicate it is a crossover cable. 910 SoG removal refers to disconnecting sync generation, allowing the adapter to work with various LCDs, and removing non-bnc pins from a Sun adapter disables monitor-id and allows it to work with any SoG monitor on any system. 1112 Using a Sun adapter on an SoG monitor is easier than trying to get a non-SoG monitor to work, and if you have a monitor which supports sync on green then a cheap adapter will get you video. 1314 Using a Sun adapter with VGX and RE configurations can damage the monitor. 15
