AutoCAD

Claims: 14

AutoCAD has been available on multiple Unix-based platforms, including IRIX (up to version R13) for machines such as the R4K Indigo2, and SunOS/Solaris for systems like the Sun IPX lunchbox, which featured a drawing pad. 123 The IRISvision has drivers for AutoCAD, and Columbia was included in early versions of the software. 45 DXF is a format originally intended for interchange between different versions of AutoCAD, but due to political wrangling it became a de facto standard despite not being a true CAD-interchange standard. 6 AutoCAD can convert old files, but not if the directory is marked read-only, and it may crash when opening a sample file, reporting an internal error related to CONFIGIO. 78 AutoCAD for Macs targets the BIM market, likely due to the absence of mechanical features in the Mac version, and macOS has limited default software compatibility with the engineering market, with only AutoCAD commonly installed. 910 A simple AutoCAD clone is somewhat working. 11
