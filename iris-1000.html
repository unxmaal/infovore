IRIS 1000

Claims: 6 Part of IRIS

The IRIS 1000 could run GL2, a proprietary SGI OS, or operate as a terminal for another system. 1 The IRIS 1000/1200/2000 series were diskless and ran the V Kernel, while the IRIS 1400/1500 could run standalone from April 1984. 2 The PM1 board used in the IRIS 1000/1200 terminal was a SUN design running the Stanford vkernel with original iris graphics. 3 The IRIS 1000 graphics board includes a special chip and features geometry engines and advanced TTL logic. 45 The OS tape of the IRIS 1000 is being dumped and scanned. 6
