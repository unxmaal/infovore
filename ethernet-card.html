Ethernet card

Claims: 11 Part of Ethernet

Ethernet cards are available for various bus types, including the MCA bus (DEC Ethernet card) and buses running at 33 MHz or 66 MHz. 12 Some Ethernet cards work with Debian 7 without issues, allowing access to the drive and the Internet. 3 Fixing an Ethernet card may involve a DOS-only firmware tool that is also available for Linux but may require a live distribution. 4 Ethernet cards have been used to access BBSes via telnet and to run retro web servers with WAIS and gopher on Unix machines. 56 Adding a decent Ethernet card can make a computer, such as the Ultra 5/10, usable. 7
