OpenSPARC

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SPARC.

OpenSPARC is a GPLv2 implementation of the UltraSPARC T2 processor. 1 OpenSPARC is Sun's free release of the UltraSPARC T1 and T2 Verilog, allowing free development of SPARC-compliant processors. 2 SPARC and OpenSPARC are distinct. 2 Using the trademarks requires paid membership, similar to RISC-V. 2 RISC-V and OpenSPARC are open-source architectures, but they have limitations. 3
