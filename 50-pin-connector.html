50-pin connector

Claims: 7

A 50-pin connector is standard. 1 The L2 uses a 50-pin connector for power, which is unusual and requires specific wiring. 2 The internal SCSI interface for the Personal IRIS is a standard 50-pin connector plus a Molex power connector. 3 SCSI enclosures without DAT or CD-ROM drives have a 50-pin connector. 4 The Indigo2 has a 50-pin connector on the backplane that isn't SCA; you can't directly connect an SCA disk to the backplane. 5 Using the 50-pin connector instead of the SCA80 or external 68-pin connector can result in poor performance when connecting an SSD via an Acard IDE to SCSI adapter. 6
