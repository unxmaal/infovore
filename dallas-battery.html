Dallas battery

Claims: 7 Part of Dallas

The Ultra 1 enterprise requires a hard drive, and the Ultra 10 requires an HDD along with a Dallas battery replacement. 1 The Ultra 10 requires a Dallas battery to function properly, and all Sun systems need a new Dallas battery. 23 An O350 system with a particular serial number requires a Dallas battery; without it, the system won't boot and will show no L1 display. 4 With the Fuel, when its Dallas battery is flat, powering on within 1-2 seconds after mains power off can make the L1 come up every time. 5 An old expired Dallas battery allows the system to boot into arcs-NT BIOS with a 'CMOS expired' message. 6
