PRAM

Claims: 5

PRAM is stored in battery-backed memory, and its contents (such as the Indy's Ethernet MAC address) are blank if unused, which can cause issues on first startup. 1 Laptops without a PRAM battery can boot but may have unreliable NVRAM settings such as the time. 2 Resetting the PRAM might clear a display setting issue when booting with a KVM, and a ROM SIMM is required for the chime and startup process involving PRAM and the clock. 34 The Monterey installer can be difficult to run on an MBP, requiring multiple PRAM resets and formatting the drive to succeed. 5
