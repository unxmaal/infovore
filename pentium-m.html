Pentium M

Claims: 6 Part of Pentium Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Pentium 4.

The Pentium M is an iteration of the P5 architecture, which continued until the Core 2. 1 It was not a P4, and some people used it in desktops instead of the Pentium 4. 23 The Pentium M was more efficient but not faster at the top-end. 4 The first-generation Apple TV was Pentium M-based, while every Apple TV from the second generation onwards used ARM. 5
