VAXstation 4000

Claims: 11 Part of VAXstation

The VAXstation 4000 is a valuable machine, and previous owners may not have cared about its disposal, so units can cost around $5,000, especially in California, and a million corroded ones may appear for sale on eBay for $4,000. 123 VAXstation 4000 models that can use TURBOchannel have a MIPS emulator in ROM to run option ROMs on cards, and they share TURBOchannel, 3W3 video, and the same keyboard/mouse (after an adapter) with DECstation, Alpha AXP, and DECstation models. 45 The keyboard and mouse connect through a DB15 port that is serial rather than DB9, using an Lk201 or Lk401 style splitter cable compatible with DECstation 5000 and some VAXstation 4000. 6 VAXstation 4000 models use RAM modules rather than the VRAM modules found in DECstation 2100/3100, and NetBSD patches exist for VAXstation 4000 and VAX 4000/300 to support SCSI. 78 The VAXstation 4000 has onboard sound, possibly 8-bit mono and ancient. 9
