DEC RM05

Claims: 3 Part of DEC

  • The RM05 and TU77 peripherals do not require 3 phase power, only the RP06/RP07 disks do. (user-b1da, exchange 24586, 2022-01-12; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • DEC RM05 disks are actually rebranded CDC 9766 SMD disks and may require conversion to MASSBUS. (user-b1da, exchange 27123, 2022-03-11; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
  • The RM05s use the SMD interface, and user-b1da has adapter boards for connecting SMD disks to MASSBUS. These adapters could potentially allow using normal SMD disks, saving a UNIBUS chassis. (user-b1da, exchange 161785, 2022-04-11; 1 speaker(s), 1 conversation(s))
