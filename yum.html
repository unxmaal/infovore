yum

Claims: 6

yum and rpm are considered notorious bloatware, even on early Linux, and rpm was not a very good solution, though it is better than having a source port system. 1 rpm does not have out-of-box auto dependency management and relies on external tools like yum for dependency resolution. 2 DNF, or Dandified yum, is the next generation version of yum, roughly maintaining CLI compatibility with yum and defining a strict API for extensions and plugins. 3 DNF has larger dependencies than yum, including bash and python3-dnf, and requires additional python packages that have not been ported yet. 4 RSE has additional packages installable from the yum cloud, requiring about 4 commands to set up. 5 SGUG does not have yum or the new package manager. 6
