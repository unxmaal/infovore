skins

Claims: 11

SGI skins are large pieces of solid plastic, as seen on the massive rackmount Onyx and Challenge XL. 1 The Prism chassis has metal skins except for the front panel. 2 The O2+ has better and more solid skins than the O2, but this is a minor difference. 3 An Indy with skins can have a near-perfect appearance. 4 Hobbyists will pay for an Onyx2 deskside only if it comes with presentable skins, while an Onyx2 rack is not interesting without presentable skins. 56 Consider backing SGI skins with heat-welded copper mesh for long-term preservation. 7
