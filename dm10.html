DM10

Claims: 17

The DM10 is a FireWire card (as opposed to the SDI-based DM6), and it installs the drivers and API for FireWire devices, providing sample code. 12 The DM10 or DM11 FireWire card can control and import from FireWire decks and cameras, including consumer cameras with a 1394 output. 34 The DM10 works in the IP35 (O2), and it also works with DVLink in an O2. 56 The Miranda DV bridge converts SDI to FireWire and vice versa, requiring a DM10. 7 The DM10 uses a different ASIC than the DVLink-based cards, and it is not compatible with the Adaptec or SCSI FireWire cards. 89 Irix has base drivers for one chipset, while the DM10 and DVLink are special, fancier options. 10
