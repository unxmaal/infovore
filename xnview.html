xnview

Claims: 4

XnView can convert pix files, a type of RGB file, to other formats on IRIX. 12 It can also save images in SGI RGB format, resizing them to a maximum of 100x100 pixels. 3 XnView has been used to transfer renders, such as one made in Alias PowerAnimator. 4
