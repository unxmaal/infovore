Galileo

Claims: 7

The Galileo is a card that shares a similar connector and design with the Videoframer, so it might work with VideoFramer. 12 The 6.2 installation distribution requires the Galileo software distribution, and while installing all patches may break compatibility, the system can work without them. 3 When Galileo software is installed, IRIX may hang on startup and cannot be detected by hinv. 4 The teal r4400-250 Indigo 2 requires a matching Galileo setup. 5 The Galileo, Cindy, and Centaur cards could send either a still frame or live video, but not key signals, for Liberty. 6 Media Suite has issues with the Indy lacking Galileo for broadcasting. 7
