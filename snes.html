SNES

Claims: 11

The SNES uses a modified 65c816 CPU, while the Game Boy, Game Boy Color, and Game Boy Advance all use a variant of the Z80 CPU. 1 The SNES, Game Boy, Game Boy Color, and Game Boy Advance are all backwards compatible. 2 In Starfox on the SNES, a custom chip (the SuperFX chip) was used, functioning as a separate computer that pushes to the SNES's VRAM for display, yet it still did not achieve what would be considered decent frame rates. 34 With SNES and older carts, they were treated as part of RAM. 5 The SuperNT FPGA system uses SNES peripherals for carts and controllers. 6 The SNES has a larger than usual barrel jack; finding an original is very hard, though some cheaper alternatives exist. 7 SNES and NES systems are fragile. 8
