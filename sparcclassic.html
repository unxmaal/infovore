SPARCclassic

Claims: 28

The SPARCclassic is a lunchbox-style computer that ships with a matching external SCSI box and was released in December 1992, when contemporaneous PCs such as the 486DX/33 cost around $2500. 12 It is a MicroSPARC I machine, and NextStep only supports the MicroSPARC II, so the SPARCclassic cannot run NextStep. 3 The SPARCclassic has double the maximum RAM of the IPX (not counting expansions), and it has a CG3 onboard video card, which is considered better than the CG6 found in the IPX. 45 The machine is slow, but performance improves with a CG6, and it can boot Solaris 9 but not Solaris 10. 67 Solaris runs on a SPARCclassic with 96 MB of RAM but is slow, and Solaris 9 final runs like mud on a SPARCclassic. 89 Sun offered Solaris across a wide range of machines, from the SPARCcenter 2000 to the SPARCclassic, with the latter running Solaris 2 on 16 MB of RAM being particularly challenging, and SunOS 4 was ported to the SPARCclassic and later SPARCstations at customer request. 1011 The SPARCclassic can serve as a router, httpd, and mush server, and FluxBox runs on a SPARCclassic under OpenBSD. 1213 The SPARCclassic has native SCA drives, and it has a slow 3.5" drive that will be replaced with a U320 drive for better performance. 1415
