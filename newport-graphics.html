Newport graphics

Claims: 5 Part of Newport

An updated mplayer build improves support for Newport graphics, slightly speeds up a couple of things, and cleans up issues with the SGI VO, making video output 20% faster but not the codec. 1 The GIO device requires configuration options such as REX3 in Newport Graphics, which has gio32/gio64 and gio32/gio32bis timing settings. 2 There is documentation for Newport graphics, though it requires using three 8-bit cards to simulate a 24-bit card. 3 Newport graphics cannot be efficiently emulated by GPU. 4
