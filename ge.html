GE

Claims: 11

The gr3-XZ configuration can be either 2 or 4 GEs. 1 Onyx deskside supports one GE, one GM, one DG, and up to four RM boards. 2 RM boards generate pixels, while GM boards operate at the triangle level, and a dead RM can cause tests for the GM and GE to fail due to a pixel problem. 3 GEs send higher level graphics primitives to the GPU than Newport, allowing higher level emulation by passing triangles directly to the GPU. 4 With a BP4 installed, the GE crashes and graphics are lost until reset, and some menus in Alias also crash the GE with and without a BP4 installed. 5 The B2K machine was made in the USA by GE for their MRI machines. 6 GE was on HP workstations for at least a decade. 7
