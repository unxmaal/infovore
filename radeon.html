Radeon

Claims: 21 Background: 4 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Radeon.

The Radeon is a graphics technology used in SGI systems for 20 years, alongside GeForce graphics, and can be equipped with AMDGPU drivers on a PCIe slot. 12 The Radeon provides a GUI but lacks 3D acceleration due to driver limitations, and the radeon and amdgpu drivers can be patched to work with GPUs connected to a PCI riser or a PCI to PCI-E riser adapter. 34 The Radeon is 1000% to 1200% faster than the V12 in rendering, depending on the workload, and IR4 graphics cards are outperformed by modern Radeon cards. 56 1.25-1.5 GHz G4 CPUs with Radeon graphics can run OS 9 with a hack, and there are utilities from Radeon or ATI for it, but the BIOS is unknown. 78 The R-464L APU runs Frigate well with its Radeon for video encoding and Coral TPU for AI detection. 9
