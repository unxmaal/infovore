DIP switches

Claims: 15

DIP switches are used to configure 13W3-to-VGA cables and adapters for compatibility with SGI monitors that use Sync on Green, ensuring the correct pinout. 123 These DIP switches can also be adjusted to change image brightness and to convert a 13W3 signal to BNC, which requires a VGA-to-BNC cable. 45 Reliable SGI-compatible video cables with DIP switches are available for purchase, including on eBay for around $20, and can be used on SGI and Intergraph systems but might not work on NeXT. 678 DIP switches can also be a point of failure, as seen with stuck switches on HHKB units that required disassembly to fix. 910 In other contexts, DIP switches set the configuration byte for Indigo (and earlier) RS-232 keyboards, toggle the heavy memory test on POST for the L1, and were used to resolve a SCSI bus error by setting termination back to 'on' on a CD-ROM drive. 111213
