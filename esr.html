ESR

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Capacitor.

ESR (equivalent series resistance) is the resistance of a capacitor, and it should be as low as possible for optimal performance. 1 Replacement capacitors should have low ESR, especially in high current output filter circuits, and slightly larger capacity is fine but much larger might cause inrush current issues. 2 For capacitor replacements, look for an 85 degree C rating or higher, 8000 hours or better, and the lowest ESR. 3 For VRMs or PSUs, consider ESR and temperature ratings when replacing electrolytic capacitors. 4 For vintage SGI equipment, matching ESR is generally not necessary, but can be important for older or lower-quality components. 5
