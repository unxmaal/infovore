video

Claims: 28

Several machines fail to display video due to hardware faults: the I2 cannot output video due to potential riser board issues, the Pismo boots with a chime but no video due to RAM issues, an Ultra 5 with a dead NVRAM may produce POST beep codes but fail to display video with garbage serial output, and a lack of both video and serial output suggests something more serious. 1234 Other machines simply cannot use video: the octane2, the Xserve Cluster Node option, and some machines that require specific conditions, such as old UNIX boxes that may not display video over a KVM due to USB requirements. 567 Video issues can be diagnosed by disconnecting video and keyboard/mouse and using a null modem cable for serial debugging, and some problems persist even after reflashing the PROM. 89 Video output can be routed through various ports and methods: the DB15 connector is used for video on older Macs, the O2 can display video on its composite video output via a Raspberry Pi backend, and IRIX installation may require console connection before video works over DVI to analog VGA. 101112 Some machines allocate card slots for video, such as the Indigo and Indigo 2, which have two video card slots each, while others, like the IBM 76i, work well with VLB video. 131415
