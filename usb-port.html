USB port

Claims: 12 Part of USB Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see KVM switch.

A small board wired to a USB port could play a sound when it receives power. 1 The pico has only one USB port, requiring a hub or keyboard with ports. 2 The second revision adds a USB port for protocol selection and saving, possibly with a client program. 3 The fuel L1 port is inside the device; connecting fuel to an L2 or L3 port over a USB port allows communication. 4 Power buttons may be the issue; check solder connections on the serial header or try the USB port. 5 For L1 access, the USB port on the mainboard is used; the machine takes about 10 seconds to become responsive after power is restored. 6
