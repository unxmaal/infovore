Mekton

Claims: 7

Mekton is a real-time 3D WYSIWYG modeler and player built on the Activation engine, which was ahead of its time. 1 It is reasonably well known, appearing on several common demo discs, and information about it is readily available. 2 The version on the Octane or O2 demo discs for IRIX 6.5 is available, though the version on the Impact 6.2 disc is missing dependencies. 3 Certain Impact and Mekton demos require VPro compatibility to run properly and are limited to Impact hardware. 45 Mekton requires correct `GFXBOARD` entries in `demos.idb` for proper functioning; missing entries cause texture problems, and VPro's fallback to RealityEngine breaks texture rendering in older software. 6
