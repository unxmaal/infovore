Altix 330

Claims: 8 Part of Altix Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Altix.

The Altix 330 is a 1U Itanium machine from SGI, released in 2005 with Itanium2 1.5 GHz CPUs, and was on the market for about a year. 1 It was designed as an expansion module for the Altix 350, adding processors, memory, and a single PCI slot, and can be numalinked with an Altix 350, though cables are required. 23 It can be networked but does not follow the IP assignment guidelines in the owner's manual, and the L1 USB port's network connection is not immediately obvious from standard network tools. 4 It comes with a NIC and theoretically has everything needed without an IO9 or PCI riser, but may require additional components for netboot. 5 It did not sell well and could run on 48V or mains power; it's less known than 350s and Integrities, making it potentially cheaper. 6 Someone running Windows on an Altix 330 exists. 7
