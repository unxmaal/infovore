SCSI DVD drive

Claims: 7 Part of SCSI Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Optical disc drive, SGI Indigo2.

A SCSI DVD drive may fail to open due to cheap plastic cogs from Toshiba. 1 A SCSI CD-ROM can be used to fit a SCSI DVD drive into an Indigo2. 2 An R10k/195 could probably handle a SCSI DVD drive, but an R4600/133 almost certainly cannot. 3 A SCSI DVD drive can read RW discs. 4 Booting Solaris 9 from DVD on an Ultra 10 fails due to a panic during vfs_mountroot, while Solaris 7 boots fine, and a SCSI DVD drive works. 5
