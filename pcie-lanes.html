PCIe lanes

Claims: 3 Part of PCIe

The jumper card position sets the PCIe lanes for the two x16 connectors to either x16/disabled or 8x/8x. 1 Each PCIe lane on the EVGA nForce 680i SLI motherboard only allows for 250MB per second, so the maximum speed for the ioDrive is 2GB per second. 2 NVMe is limited by available PCIe lanes in a way that SATA/SAS aren't as much. 3
