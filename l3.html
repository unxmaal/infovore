L3

Claims: 13

L3 is a software suite for x86 that runs under several old versions of Red Hat Linux, emulating the L2 controller. 123 L1 controls a single brick, L2 controls a rack, and L3 controls a network. 4 The L3 manages multiple L2s, acting as a terminal server, and L2/L3 controllers are needed to talk to the Router. 56 The router connects to L2 or L3 over USB rather than L1, and L2 can see all connected bricks but not vice versa. 7 The L3 is an SGI 230 PC running RedHat 6, and it can be installed on a commodity Slot 2 Xeon PC that runs a normal BIOS and can even run DOS. 8910
