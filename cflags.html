CFLAGS

Claims: 4

CFLAGS is the variable used to hold compiler flags, while CC is used for the compiler and LDFLAGS for linker flags. 1 Cflags relate to how software packages are compiled, and one should set specific Cflags for the extensions relevant to one's CPU. 2 To address an endian issue, one can add `-D__BIG_ENDIAN__=1` to CFLAGS in the Makefile, rebuild, and observe the result; if the problem persists, actual fixes are needed. 3
