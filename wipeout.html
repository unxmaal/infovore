Wipeout

Claims: 8

Wipeout shares similarities with SGI SpeedScape from the Onyx Rolls demo CD in 1994, and its futuristic/rollercoaster vibes may have been influenced by it. 12 Running Wipeout on IRIX requires modifications to load npot textures and porting to IRIX. 3 The game can be downloaded and run on IRIX using SDL2 with GL support, which is not supported by the SDL2 from the SGUG-RSE repo. 4 Wipeout runs well on the Octane2/V12/600mhz and the Reality Engine Onyx with a 600 MHz V12, looking like high-quality SGI era aesthetic. 56 On the O2, Wipeout has trouble running, experiencing FPS drops, 3D scene freezes, and audio loop due to CPU limitations, even with no-texture mode. 7 Wipeout on the Indigo2 Solid Impact can be made playable with the --no-textures option and a small window size. 8
