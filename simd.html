SIMD

Claims: 7 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Itanium.

SIMD (Single Instruction Multiple Data) is not common in ISAs I-IV but is available in high-end current generation MIPS32/64 CPUs, with floating point being standard in R5K and newer CPUs and available on earlier ISAs. 1 SGI's mainline MIPS CPUs did not support MMX or SIMD instructions, and SIMD was not implemented in MIPS by SGI, though supplementary SIMD chips were available for the O2. 23 Most extensions to x86 since SIMD have been related to SIMD, and Intel enforced patents relating to SIMD instruction set enhancements against Transmeta's x86 implementation even though it used emulation. 45 The ICE chip supports SIMD, and assembly is preferred for instruction space efficiency. 6 Itanium processors have SIMD capabilities, though not as excellent as expected. 7
