WindView

Claims: 3

WindView is a logic analyzer for real-time software that allows developers to observe the instantaneous timing of the IRIX kernel and its interactions with applications; it was available for IRIX 5.3 and required a license. 12 WindView for IRIX became IrixView and was rebuilt in Linux, with plans to release it as open-source in 2005. 3
