BFG9060

Claims: 5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Quake (video game).

Finding a 68060 accelerator for A4000 systems other than the A3660 is difficult, with the BFG9060 being a potential alternative. 1 The BFG9060 card circumvents the A3640's biggest flaw, which is the lack of onboard Fast RAM, and is not cheap even without a CPU. 2 Quake runs well on a BFG9060 with 100MHz 060 because the BFG has significantly faster RAM access than every other Amiga accelerator (other than Vampire), which is what Quake needs. 3 The ReSDMAC firmware fixes motherboard SCSI issues in AA3000+ systems and allows the BFG9060 to run at 50 or 100MHz without SCSI errors, also resolving DSP crashes. 4
