ENP10

Claims: 4

The ENP10 is a generic module available from Meret Optical Osicom, with versions differing in density of TTL chips. 1 The ENP10 is rebadged as a Motorola MVME330, and manuals for the MVME330 help emulate the ENP10, allowing 4D/50 and 4D/70 to connect to the internet. 2 There are two firmwares for the ENP10: the original and the SGI modified version, with the latter offering bug fixes and performance improvements, and it can be identified by the '(SGI)' suffix in hinv. 34
