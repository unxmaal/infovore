5V rail

Claims: 10 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Power supply unit (computer).

The 5V rail of the bus bar has four 10000uF capacitors that leak, and the personal IRIS PSU has a common failure in which these capacitors leak, though some still work. 12 A large tantalum capacitor on the 5V rail might be failing and causing the power connector to brown. 3 The 5V rail has 5.4 ohms to ground. 4 The 5V rail in the PSU of the Asrock machine has failed. 5 A Mean Well PSU was used for the 5V rail and a small ATX for other rails. 6
