400 MHz

Claims: 8 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

Anything 400 MHz and up is BGA soldered. 1 The SGI O2 can be upgraded to 400 MHz; it has 800 MB RAM, an A/V board with a Moosecam port and FPA, and does not need additional components, though the Moosecam port is optional and some boards may have the port without the circuit. 2 An O2 can have an R12K CPU running at 400 MHz, and the O2 takes an R12000 CPU, which is underpowered at 400 MHz. 34 An O2 with an R12000 CPU at 400 MHz can almost do DVD decoding in realtime. 5 An SGI O2 runs IRIX 6.5.30 with 1024MB of RAM and a R12000 CPU at 400 MHz. 6
