Systune

Claims: 6 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Kernel (operating system).

Systune is a console command used to set variables, and it may involve re-linking with new settings and drivers, as seen in Tru64. 12 It is used for tasks such as enabling the scroll wheel for IRIX (`systune pcmouse_mode #`) and increasing `ncargs` to 131072 to avoid 'Arg list too long' errors when building openssl with `irix64-mips4-gcc`. 13 Systune often requires rebuilding the kernel. 4 Folks also discussed systune recompiling for SunOS 4.x, which is BSD-derived, though the command to enable IPv6 is not systune. 56
