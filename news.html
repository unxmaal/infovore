NeWS

Claims: 37 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see NeWS.

NeWS (Network Window System) was developed by Sun Microsystems and is not related to Sony NEWS. 1 SunOS 4.x and Solaris 4.x and below ran NeWS, which required those versions. 23 NeWS was used by SGI before X11 and influenced the look and feel of 4Sight and 4DWM. 24 MEX was an in-house SGI window system before NeWS, and SGI used MEX until 1988, then NeWS until 1991, switching to X. 56 4Sight, which came after MEX, was NeWS based, and 4D1 4.x introduced X11. 7 SunView, NeWS, OpenWindows (X11/NeWS), and then X11 only. 8 The system uses NeWS rather than Xorg. 9 FrameMaker has a version of Frame for SGI that uses NeWS. 10
