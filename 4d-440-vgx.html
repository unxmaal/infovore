4D/440 VGX

Claims: 7 Part of 4D/440

The 4D/440 VGX can pass diagnostics after some work. 1 It draws around 600-700W of power. 2 It runs SoftPC 3.03 with Windows 3.1 on an R3K CPU, which emulates a 286 and is slower than SoftWindows 1.25 on a 250 MHz R4400 Indigo2. 3 It can use a 50pin SCSI disk such as a Seagate Barracuda 18XL, and has a 4GB IBM DDRS which may or may not have a SCA adapter. 4 Its bulkhead panel has monitor RGB, a single SYNC BNC for composite sync, and an Alpha output for external compositing. 5 The second row of the bulkhead panel belongs to the Broadcast Video Option, which can do Y/C video and genlock to a house sync. 6 The Extron RGB-DVI 300 can convert the 4D/440 VGX output into RGB with SOG using only BNCs. 7
