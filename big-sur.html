Big Sur

Claims: 8

Big Sur performs reasonably well with 4GB of RAM, though an 8GB upgrade and faster CPU would be preferable for better performance. 1 A 2015 MacBook Air with an i5 and 4GB of RAM runs Big Sur without issues, while a 2013 MacBook Air with an i7 and 8GB of RAM struggles to run Opera. 23 Python and pip work poorly on Big Sur, and all booter versions require specific versions of ruby, ansible, and vagrant, with BigSur being incompatible. 45 dlopen() serves libraries from a cache directly on Big Sur, making disassembly harder due to lack of on-disk copies of libsystem. 6 Big Sur's trustd stuff is avoidable, Catalina gets security updates, and iOS is nice on tablets. 7
