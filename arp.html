ARP

Claims: 3

ARP resolves network addresses by associating a device's MAC address with its IP address, and a failure to do so typically indicates a network issue or routing problem. 1 Jumpstart uses TFTP to load data over the network, relying on ARP and RARP for connection establishment. 2 A packet may show a different lladdr in the ARP message and its header. 3
