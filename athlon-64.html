Athlon 64

Claims: 4 Part of Athlon

The Athlon 64 was based on the K8 CPU, which was an AMD precursor to the Athlon 64. 1 During the Athlon XP era, Intel's Netburst maintained performance leadership through higher clock speeds, but was overtaken by the Athlon 64 due to improved instructions per clock. 2 Early Opterons were Athlon 64s with more cache that made good workstations. 3 The Athlon 64 was rehashed for 10 years. 4
