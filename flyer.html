Flyer

Claims: 7

The Flyer uses a proprietary wavelet compression algorithm known as VTASC, which was well-regarded at the time for offering better visual quality than comparable motion-JPEG-based nonlinear editing systems. 1 The original Toaster setup was ~$5k, and a Flyer upgrade in '94 cost around $15k, mostly due to SCSI drives in a separate tower enclosure. 2 The Flyer was more focused on democratizing video, but the Toaster was abandoned for PC migration. 3 The Indy computer lasts longer than a base Flyer setup, but the Flyer has hardware glitches. 4
