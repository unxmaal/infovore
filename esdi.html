ESDI

Claims: 7

Professional IRIS has a built-in SCSI controller and offers ESDI as an additional VME board, and ESDI was more developed and cheaper in the early days. 1 The very oldest systems used ESDI, for which a VME controller made by Interphase is needed. 2 The fixed disk on an Iris is ESDI off the storage card and cannot boot from 9-track. 3 PS/2 hard drives with ESDI and SCSI disks were common, and spares are useful for exhibits. 4 Fx was mainly for SCSI and ESDI. 5 The 4D/380 can boot from an IPI-II disk, which was a high-end alternative disk interface in the early 1990s that didn't succeed, with SMD and ESDI being older options and SCSI being the winner. 6
