PIMM

Claims: 7

The PIMM attaches to the socket and screws down, sitting under the second heatsink where the Bedrock ASIC is located. 1 PIMM CPU sockets require longer CPU pins and a plastic CPU seat. 2 The PIMM stores model and serial numbers for the CPU and cache, which the `system` command can read from the number in a can chips. 3 Baking the PIMM slowly releases tension between PCB layers and removes moisture to prevent cracking and splintering during BGA soldering, using an infrared preheater for a slow temperature ramp up and down. 4 On O2K/Onyx2 nodes, removing all memory before changing the PIMM prevents the new PIMM from being tested by another node, avoiding long test times. 5 The O2 can red light due to issues with the power supply, CPU, or other components like the PIMM or MRE. 6
