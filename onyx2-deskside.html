Onyx2 deskside

Claims: 21 Part of Onyx2

The Onyx2 deskside supports only one display generator, not two separate 'displays' in X terminology. 1 The Onyx 2 deskside has a PS/2 keyboard port. 2 The Onyx 2 deskside requires a single router board and does not support linking to another Onyx. 3 The Onyx 2 deskside can have a maximum of 4 CPUs in a 2-node configuration, but some models are limited to 2 CPUs. 4 The Onyx2 deskside is 4U tall, taller than the Windows workstation tower SGI made, more than 2m tall, heavier than another machine, not space efficient, and noisy. 567 The Onyx2 deskside has half the CPU nodes and router slots of an O2K deskside, with an internal topology similar to a crossbar switch with more dimensions, and only one router slot. 89 An Onyx2 rackmount configuration is an Onyx2 brick with an O2K for compute, and linking it to other systems requires a router, which an Onyx2 deskside lacks. 10 The O2K and Onyx2 deskside were used together but not the O3K. 11
