G4 Cube

Claims: 13 Part of G4

The G4 Cube has a 2MB L3 cache that significantly improves performance compared to a 1.5GHz G4 laptop. 1 The G4 Cube can boot Tiger in 2 seconds. 2 The G4 Cube can use an external SATA firewire hard drive connected via a firewire to SSD adapter for better performance than IDE. 3 The G4 Cube can play hardware accelerated Glide/GL with its NV6200 using a Glide wrapper, and Quake1 and Quake2 demos run smoothly at 1920x1200. 45 The G4 Cube had an ATI card and can overheat with a 6200 GPU, causing texture glitches and capacitor damage. 67 The G4 Cube, despite being a failed product, has become collectable due to its limited production. 8
