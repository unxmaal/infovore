SCSI2SD V5

Claims: 3 Part of SCSI2SD

SCSI2SD V5 is finicky on Amiga, and its multidevice feature benefits hardware that cannot address larger drives. 1 SCSI2SD V5 is slower than almost anything but still saturates the SCSI card in the author's Amiga. 2 SCSI2SD V5 supported wide SCSI, V6 enhanced it to support up to seven devices, and all versions of ZuluSCSI have supported wide SCSI. 3
