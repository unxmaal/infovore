power consumption

Claims: 7 Part of POWER Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Solid-state drive.

The O2 does not draw much power when off, though older PSUs are not always reliable for extended periods. 1 The LOM of the UltraSPARC IIIi uses 40 watts of power by itself. 2 Using an SSD can reduce power consumption compared to a spinning disk. 3 The original N64 controller can interface with the O2 via RS232, but the power consumption is too high for a 16 MHz clock; an 8 MHz clock might work, but 1 MHz would be insufficient. 4 Custom brackets allow monitoring power consumption of an Onyx4 by fitting a meter into the rack and moving the power socket to the rear of the switch. 5 The Octane consumes 311W when powered on and 9W when powered off. 6 The Onyx 350 consumes power even when off. 7
