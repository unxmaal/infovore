IRIX 4.x

Claims: 15 Part of IRIX Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Indigo.

IRIX 4.x runs on R3k and R4k Indigo1 machines and early R4k Indigo2 machines, and the Indigo2 was launched during the IRIX 4.x era, though it may require a CPU swap to run IRIX 5.3. 12 IRIX 4.x uses X11 as its window system, making it the first version with X and thus more portable for writing applications. 34 IRIX 4.x is usable on very old machines such as Personal IRIS, Power Series, and Professional IRIS, or on machines with very little RAM. 5 Indigos can generally run IRIX 4.x, with an R4400 plus Elan being very fast. 6 The Barco Creator 6 requires IRIX 4.x and an IPI12 or later, and the IrisVision can run from IRIX 4.x to 6.5.22. 78 IRIX 4.x is System V Release 3, whereas SVR4 introduced ELF, first seen in IRIX 5. 9 IRIX 4.x uses MIPS 3.x compilers, while IRIX 3.x uses MIPS 2.20 compilers and IDO 4.0.5 uses MIPS 3.10 compilers. 10 The IRIX 5.x compilers provide a compatibility mode for compiling code in the COFF format used in IRIX 4.x, activated by setting the SGI_IRIX4 environment. 11
