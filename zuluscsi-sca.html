Zuluscsi SCA

Claims: 5 Part of ZuluSCSI

The SS5 can use a ZuluSCSI SCA for storage, and both the ZuluSCSI SCA from the US and the SCSI pocket knife one from France would be good enough for miniroot. 12 If a ZuluSCSI SCA in a slot with a SCSI ID exposes a disk image with a different SCSI ID, it can cause problems if the image is assigned to a SCSI ID already in use. 3 The ZuluSCSI SCA works well in the O2 and Octane but requires a custom solution for O2 drive sled attachment. 4 On the O2, the ZuluSCSI SCA did not show a speed difference compared to the original 15K drive, while on the Octane it felt slower. 5
