32-bit ABI

Claims: 4 Part of 32-bit

The 32-bit ABI does not work with it. 1 The 64-bit ABI is preferred over the 32-bit ABI because most software no longer supports SOM and pkgsrc developers are not interested in keeping it alive. 2 However, when bootstrapping pkgsrc for HP-UX, using a 32-bit ABI is recommended over a 64-bit ABI to avoid partial functionality and errors, especially with Python. 34
