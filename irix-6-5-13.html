IRIX 6.5.13

Claims: 7 Part of IRIX 6.5 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI O2.

The IRIX 6.5.13 overlay is included on disc 1 of some O2 systems. 1 IRIX 6.5.13 runs slowly on an R4k Indigo2 even at 200MHz, and is noticeably slower than 5.3 even with services disabled, though not unbearably so. 23 You cannot downgrade a r12k O2 to an OS past 6.5.13. 4 Every commercial point release of IRIX includes debug symbols for most of the OS, and some versions contain additional debug code and beta strings that reveal original C code, as seen in IRIX 6.5.13. 5 Installing IRIX repeatedly on an Octane with IRIX 6.5.13 can be quick but may encounter issues. 6 Jaleo is a compositing software that can run on SGI machines with OS versions 6.5.13 and possibly earlier, but not on 6.5.30; it identifies 600 MHz CPUs as 500 MHz ones. 7
