XIO

Claims: 36 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see XIO, SGI Octane.

XIO is a high-speed interconnect used on the Altix line, capable of 400 MBps on the Octane, and involves a PIC ASIC for PCI interfacing. 1 It is used for expansion systems such as peripherals and possibly memory expansions, and is a better option than the Xtalk adapter because the card cage offers more slots. 23 XIO is used on the Fuel and Tezro for their V12 and DM3 cards, on the Octane2 for fiber channel cards with SFP modules, and on the O2000 to interconnect nodes in a brick. 456 It is faster and more expensive than PCI, with a peak bandwidth of 1.6 GB that is theoretical and not consistently achievable. 78 XIO connectors are incredibly sensitive and, like AGP, had durability issues with compression connectors compared to PCIe. 910 It can be connected to various devices, including an Origin 350 c-brick, a G-Brick or KCar, and a V-Brick, and can be used to install SE or SI graphics. 11121314
