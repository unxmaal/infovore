VGX

Claims: 18 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see OpenGL.

The VGX is a graphics boardset that was released before Reality Engine and was first supported in IRIX 3.3. 12345 The VGX chassis is blue, and its startup screen displays the VGX logo and RealityEngine word art in pink. 67 VGX is very robust and can handle low texture demands as well as Reality Engine, which is super fragile. 89 VGX does not support R3K CPUs, and the 4D/380 VGX can do textures but has CPU-based limitations in OpenGL, requiring IRIX 4 or 4D1-3.3. 1011 The geometry board on VGX, GT, and GTX has a serial connector for diagnostics, allowing an interactive prompt with limited options, and running diagnostics on the VGX can be very time-consuming. 1213 The Virtual Reality CD requires VGX for compatibility, and using a Sun adapter with VGX and Reality Engine configurations can damage the monitor. 1415
