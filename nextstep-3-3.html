NextStep 3.3

Claims: 5 Part of NeXTSTEP

NeXTStep 3.3 was released in 1995 and likely required a CD drive. 1 It runs on the NextStation, the SS4, and the SS5, and ships with zsh. 23 The SS4 or SS5 at 110 MHz can run NextStep 3.3 or OpenStep 4.2, faster than the 10/20 SMP system, and supports 24-bit graphics. 4 The I2 can run NextStep 3.3 but lacks a top command. 5
