RAM chips

Claims: 7 Part of RAM

The V10 has 16 RAM chips, each 16777 kbits, totaling 32 MiB. 1 The V8 and V12 RAM chips are twice the size of the V10 chip, and the V6 is different. 2 All RAM chips and logic chips will be good from 0°C to 55°C or more. 3 The XPS motherboard can have issues with RAM chips, and the IIfx uses replacement RAM chips. 45 Replacing faulty RAM chips and redoing solder joints can resolve issues with DIMMs, and providing RAM chips for assembly with JLCPCB might be an alternative to manual soldering. 67
