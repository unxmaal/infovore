AIX 4.1.5

Claims: 6 Part of AIX

The IBM 320H can run AIX 3.x and 4.1.5, while the 39H runs AIX 4.1.5. 12 AIX 4.1.5 can be installed on a 7011-220. 3 The base OS is provided by LCD4-0237-00.1_2.iso, and pexphigs415.iso contains graphics software. 4 At least XLC 3.3.6 (including C and C++) runs on AIX 4.1.5, with the recommended /etc/ibmcxx.cfg options -qmaxmem=-1 and -qnolm. 5 Patches for AIX 4.1.5 and compiler patches for XLC 3.3.6 are available. 6
