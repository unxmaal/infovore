AGP

Claims: 35 Background: 9 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Accelerated Graphics Port, Graphics card.

AGP is a dedicated high-speed interface for graphics that provided a significant performance boost compared to previous interfaces, and it is implemented as a PCI device class within Linux. 1234 AGP cards do not physically fit in PCI slots, even though AGP signaling is similar to PCI. 56 AGP and PCIe connectors are incompatible and cannot be used interchangeably, though both are PCI from a software perspective. 78 AGP and PCI use GART, while PCIe uses its own GART or full VM system. 9 Some motherboards may have trouble with onboard graphics when an AGP card is installed. 10
