Mac OS 9

Claims: 18 Part of Mac

Mac OS 9 was still an important OS when Windows XP was released, and it is still used today, for example by some people who favor it specifically for their DAWs (which is why Mac OS 9 Lives exists). 12 Mac OS 9 users prefer single-tasking to avoid interference from other programs, and Mac OS 9 had more games than Mac OS X. 34 Mac OS 9 has a flexible memory model that allowed for more direct memory manipulation, unlike IRIX, and it requires special handling for GLUT fullscreen due to its menu bar. 56 Mac OS 9 can run on a variety of machines, including a 200MHz 603ev on a 64-bit bus, a 400 MHz G3 with 256 MB RAM, and a G4 1 GHz Mac Mini, on which its image runs 30x faster than on a Mac IIfx. 789 Some hardware is incompatible with Mac OS 9 but supported by OS X, such as certain Adaptec cards, FireWire cards, and NICs, while dual booting a Cube with macOS 9 requires a compatible graphics card that supports OpenGL. 101112
