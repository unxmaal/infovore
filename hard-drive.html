hard drive

Claims: 83 Background: 7 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Hard disk drive.

Hard drives are frequently replaced when they fail, though a failing drive can also cause intermittent software failures caused by slowly developing bad sectors, and using a different hard drive can resolve disk errors. 123 A failed hard drive can prevent a machine from booting, and replacing it can fix boot issues, though reseating a drive may instead lead to a fatal error on the root filesystem. 45 Some machines require a hard drive to operate, and a system may not proceed without one, with a red light indicating it is waiting for a hard drive. 67 Hard drives may use MFM or RLL encoding and need to be imaged over the network, and a 1:1 drive image of a failing hard drive may be made using ddrescue. 89 Installing IRIX requires processing the hard drive with an SGI label using the prom monitor, and a hard drive that has never been used for IRIX may need to be cleaned or formatted before use. 1011 A hard drive needs a Sun partition table to boot but should still be detected on the SCSI bus, and a hard drive may emit two loud clicks during diagnostics while continuing to function normally. 1213 A hard drive can be emulated using a GPIB card in a separate computer, and EFS images can be used by dd'ing them to a hard drive. 1415 Hard drives can fail in various ways, including a broken or non-functional motor, metal dust coating the inside of the drive, or a small chance of being faulty. 161718 A failed hard drive can cause a kernel panic on a fresh install, preventing boot, and may make unhappy noises that require replacement. 1920 Hard drives can be swapped out for BlueSCSI or ZuluSCSI units, which can help with thermals, and an Octane2 can have its hard drive swapped quickly. 2122 IRIX can be installed from a hard drive using sash and fx, partitioning, and copying miniroot, and this method is faster and doesn't require network compared to reanimator. 2324 Instructions for installing IRIX from a hard drive are available at doogielabs.com, and a CD image can be dd'd to a hard drive and used for installation, requiring minor adjustments to /dev symlinks. 2526
