beige G3

Claims: 13

The beige G3 has an ATA133 interface, ADB, built-in SCSI, and old-style serial ports, and offers better expansion and I/O than the B&W model. 123 The 43p-150 and beige G3 share the same CPU pinout, which is a standard not widely adopted. 4 A beige G3 can be overclocked, but it has a problematic stock disk controller. 56 Proper USB support requires installing a special driver, so booting off USB is not possible; the driver is a small file that fits on a floppy disc. 7 Jaguar OS ran on a beige G3 for a long time, and Rhapsody DR2 runs nicely on it. 89 OS X consumes a lot of RAM, while OS 9 and NetBSD might be better for benchmarking a beige G3 because BSDs are similar to older UNIX systems and consume fewer resources. 1011
