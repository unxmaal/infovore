RAMWorks III

Claims: 3

The Apple IIe can be upgraded with a Ramworks III board for 1MB RAM. 1 A fully loaded RAMWorks III causes late-model IIgs power supplies to make a nasty squealing sound, sounding like they're going to explode, but the RAM isn't broken. 23
