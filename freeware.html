freeware

Claims: 18 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Bash (Unix shell).

Freeware was a package of free software maintained by SGI early on, and it was later replaced by nekoware, which was in turn replaced by sgug-rse (also known as RSE). 1 SGI Freeware is another name for GNU/Irix, and its last update was in May 2004 with IRIX 6.5.24; it was included only in full IRIX disc sets, not as standalone CDs. 23 Nekoware is the most recent comprehensive archive, followed by tgcware and then SGI Freeware, and it likely took over from SGI's official freeware distribution. 45 The freeware and nekoware archives are available for download. 6 Various packages can be installed from these sources, including bash, openssh (for basic SSH features on IRIX 6.5), and sshd (which can run as a native system service). 789 Tru64 5.0 and 5.1 added more modern freeware and offered a free C compiler license. 10 The WordPerfect office suite included a presentation program for IRIX, which SGI distributed as freeware. 11 DF Classic is freeware but not open source. 12
