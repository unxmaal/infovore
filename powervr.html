PowerVR

Claims: 6

PowerVR uses tile-based rendering, calculating depths internally before texturing, which made it more efficient than other GPUs of its time. 1 Sony used PowerVR graphics in the PSP and Vita, with performance similar to the iPad 2. 2 PowerVR was never compatible with hobbyists due to its closed, proprietary nature. 3 The PowerVR method of outputting graphics was not supported by 3dfx, was supported by few games, and is now expensive with a high resale value. 4 An alternative to PowerVR is the NXP iMX6 with a Vivante video core. 5
