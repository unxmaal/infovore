YouCube

Claims: 3

YouCube is a tool for playing YouTube videos on SGI systems, consisting of a C program, shell scripts, and youtube-dl running on SGUG's Python3, or alternatively a script using mplayer. 12 It requires a YouTube API key and patience due to slow decoding and URL fetching. 2 Playback quality is limited by the SGI model and supports 144p, 240p, and 360p, with better performance on the Octane2 compared to the O2 and Indy. 13
