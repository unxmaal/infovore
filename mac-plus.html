Mac Plus

Claims: 23 Part of Mac Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see Adobe Photoshop.

The Mac Plus is a classic Macintosh computer that uses SCSI and was released in 1987, when it was competitive with Commodore's A2000 and IBM's PC-AT. 12 The Mac Plus keyboard is mechanical with Alps switches, and its labels face each other, making them hard to read. 34 The Mac Plus needs capacitor recapping, and one owner recapped his grandfather's machine with polymer caps. 56 The Mac Plus can boot from external SCSI storage, including a SCSI enclosure, a ZIP drive, or BlueSCSI, though BlueSCSI is needed and disk formatting can take a long time. 789 The Mac Plus is included in the MiSTer project's emulation list. 10
