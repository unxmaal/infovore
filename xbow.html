Xbow

Claims: 20 Background: 2 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The XBOW is the backplane that holds XIO boards, not the motherboard. 1 Xbow is an operating system for SGI machines, with Xbow 1.4 being a newer version. 2 Revisions F, G, and H of the Xbow are 1.4, while earlier revisions are 1.3 or below. 3 Not all late Octanes are Xbow 1.4; some are Xbow 1.3. 4 The Octane 1.5 has newer psi, xbow, and vpro. 5 The Octane technical report indicates that Heart ASIC revision, not XBOW, determines CPU compatibility. 6 V6 and V8 models are supported by Xbow, with V6 able to use Xbow 1.3 and V10 requiring Xbow 1.4. 78 The 1.4 xbow can be used in an Octane2 chassis. 9
