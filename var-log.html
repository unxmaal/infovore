/var/log

Claims: 4

The /etc/syslog.conf file typically configures log files, placing them in /var/adm on SysV-style systems and in /var/log on BSD-style systems, with IRIX following SysV conventions. 1 Keeping /var and /var/log on separate partitions is recommended to avoid filling up the root partition with logs and audit trails. 2 On the O2, DHCP's automatic startup can be checked with 'chkconfig | grep autoconfig', and Ethernet link issues can be investigated by examining /var/adm/SYSLOG for 'no carrier' messages after unplugging the cable. 3
