NeXT monitor

Claims: 4 Part of NeXT

The NeXTstation with a NeXT monitor is used for video conferences at work. 1 The NeXT monitor can have focus issues that appear as fuzzy vision or scan lines, which might be due to a failing flyback transformer or a leaky capacitor. 2 The NeXT monitor makes an audible mids sound when the cap whines, indicating a potential issue. 3
