CATIA V5

Claims: 21 Part of CATIA

CATIA V5 is a CAD program that was the last Unix release of the product line, available for AIX and Solaris workstations, and used on AIX workstations. 1234 CATIA V5 is recommended over V4 for compatibility, as V4 has an unappealing interface and uses an entirely different geometry engine that is re-implemented in V5. 156 CATIA V5 requires OpenGL for graphics acceleration and works on Sun and AIX but not IRIX, though SGI had versions of CATIA V4 and V5 for IRIX. 78 CATIA supports IRIX until v5r17, and CATIA v5 for Solaris comes as a service pack ISO that includes shared libraries and other files, but the installer requires an existing installation. 910 CATIA V4 and V5 can be installed in the same system, but their coexistence is not straightforward, as the installation places files in /usr/catia without version distinction. 11 CATIA V5 still exists and has enough crossover with modern tutorials to be useful for 3D printing. 12
