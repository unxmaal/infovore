PPro

Claims: 14

The Pentium Pro (PPro) supports dual-CPU operating systems such as NT, while single-CPU PPros run DOS. 1 It is an i686-architecture processor, with the Pentium II as a closely related example, and the two were very similar in performance. 23 The PPro required cooling, using fans, and can use an Ultra wide SCSI card. 45 Several systems used the PPro, including a dual-PPro board (the D970) supporting up to 1 GiB of FPM or EDO DRAM, a Compaq server with six PPro CPUs, and the AS800, which can use double-height modules from PPro and early Pii-Xeon systems. 678 DOS games are considered slow, which keeps PPro prices down, and the PPro is underrated for DOS use. 910
