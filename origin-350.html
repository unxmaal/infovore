Origin 350

Claims: 286 Background: 3 claims already covered by Wikipedia, see Distcc, Firefox, SGI Origin 350.

With Tezro

The Tezro is an Origin 350 in a case with graphics and is similar to other Chimera systems, using the same CPU board as the Origin 350 but with more PCI slots and onboard video. 12 Tezro bricks require a special serial number to work with a router, otherwise they are equivalent to O350s without graphics. 3 Tezro, O350, Altix 350, and Prism use the same sleds, and VPro, Octane, Tezro, O350, and Onyx2 use VBOB. 45 The Tezro and O350 have built-in gigabit Ethernet, which is useful for the O350. 6 Transferring a HDD from a Tezro to an O350 should allow the O350 to boot, assuming the O350 has a VPro, and cloning a drive with VPro from an O350 to a Tezro should work. 78 An Origin 350 can be modified into a Tezro with a V10/V12 card. 9

With O300

The O300 and O350 are compatible for NUMAlinking, and the O300 can be linked to an O350. 101112 The O300 can use O350 PCI-X and MPX units for more RAM and PCI-X slots, but it has only PCI slots and cannot use VPro cards internally, unlike the O350. 131415 The O300 has a problematic internal design while the O350 is better engineered, and the O300 is much noisier than the O350. 1617 The O300 and O350 bricks have M serial numbers, while larger machines have L serial numbers, and reserializing bricks is possible but cannot force a nonstandard prefix. 1819 The O300 and O350 show as C bricks, indicated by a display showing 'xxxCxx'. 20 The O350, O300, Fuel, and Tezro use the same 2GB RAM kits. 21

General

The Origin 350 is a 2U rack-mounted Linux machine with a V10 CPU (030-1812-002), and it is not compatible with the Altix 350 chassis. 222324 The bottom unit is a 2U PCI-X brick (not a 4U P-brick), and the O350 and A350 share the same case including blowers. 25 The Origin 350 can be equipped with VPro or Radeon video, making it an Onyx350 or Onyx4 respectively, and the Onyx350 is an Origin 350 with a different name on the plate. 2627 An Onyx350 can comprise 1 to 8 Origin 350s with either IP or IR graphics, some of which were sold with IR graphics. 28 An Origin 350 c-brick can be connected to an Onyx2 graphics module over XIO, creating an unusual but valid configuration, and it can also be linked to an InfiniteReality3/4 module for rendering. 2930 The O350 comes with a USB card, and it can be made bootable with an IO card or NUMA-linking. 3132 The O350 can consume up to 210W of power, and the single brick is max 250W. 3334 The Origin 350 (O350) is an SGI server that can have up to 8 GB of RAM and 4 processors, and can be expanded with additional bricks and PCI cards. 35 The O350 can use up to 16 GB of RAM, with 1 GB sticks being rare. 36 The O350 has a DVD drive by default, hidden under the left door. 37 The O350 is less noisy than other servers due to larger blowers, and is quieter than the Octane. 3839 The O350 can do everything the Fuel can, and better, though it is less upgradable than a Fuel even when numalinked to many devices, unless needing a fourth PCI card. 4041 The O350 can take R16K CPUs, up to 8 GB of RAM per brick, and has better cooling and quieter operation compared to the O300; it has 4 PCI slots and can mount graphics. 42 The O350 can build software that runs on the Octane. 43 The O350 can have a VPro card installed, and can also support an Onyx4 Graphics card, specifically a G2 or G2N Brick, or a V10. 444546 The Onyx 350 is essentially an Origin 350 with 3D pipelines, and the Onyx4 is currently just the CPU brick, referred to as an O350 until a cable is installed. 4748 The O350 uses a 68-pin SCSI connector on the enclosure, and its 68-pin SCSI cable does not provide termination without a drive setting it. 4950 The O350 can have SATA with an IO10, and its firmware for an IO10 is located in /usr/cpu/firmware/sysco, indicating compatibility with different IO types. 515253 The O350's NUMAlink port can be in a POWER DOWN state even if the system is powered on, and it requires setting an environment variable to auto-boot instead of showing the system menu. 5455 The O350 is too narrow for most universal rail kits, but some shelf rails with inward sticking bits allow it to fit snugly by flexing the rails outwards, and this site has information on rails for SGI O350, including V20z rails. 565758 The O350 uses specific bolts, and nodeboards for O350 can be sourced from goldscholl. 5960 The Origin 350 (O350) is a rack-mount system that is compatible only with VPro or IR4 PCI graphics cards, and it is similar to the Altix 350, which shares the same expansion cards and IO boards but has a different mainboard. 6162 The O350 uses stock fans for cooling and consumes a constant 300W of power, though some configurations consume 250W per node. 636465 The O350 is SCA, has a FACE, and has a serial number format of dNNNNNNNN where NNNNNNN is a seven-digit number. 666768 The O350 can netboot from a supported NIC to upgrade its PROM, and it can connect to an Origin 300. 6970 The O350 is shockingly quiet for a 2U, which helps its desktopability greatly. 71 The Origin 350 is a low-end SGI system, and the Onyx350 is an Origin 350 plugged into an InfiniteReality3 or 4. 7273 The O350 supports M serial numbers and only one router, and two routers halt boot with an invalid config. 74 The O350 can be used in Onyx350 setups, and the Onyx350 contains two O350 bricks and a g-brick. 7576 The O350 can run on both 120V and 240V, and the NL3 router comes in AC versions used with O350s. 7778 The O350 does not support hot swapping of hard disks while powered on, and an O350 without a disk backplane can't boot diskless. 7980 The O350 blows air from front to back over the PCI cards, and it has telescopic rails. 8182 The Origin 350 can be a single 2U brick, while the Altix and Origin 350 differ in their server/node interface. 8384 The Origin 350 came with many SGI software titles preinstalled. 85 The O350 CPU does not
