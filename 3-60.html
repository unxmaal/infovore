3/60

Claims: 11

The Sun 3/60 is a Sun computer that is being reproduced from available schematics as part of a project. 1 It is a VME single-board computer that fits in a 1-slot VME case with only one connector and draws its power from VME. 2 The 3/60 uses 30-pin SIMMs for RAM and SCSI for the hard disk drive interface, and the motherboard with a Colour framebuffer has limited RAM slots. 34 The 3/50 and 3/60 models were similar except for RAM differences, and the 3/60 is not a 9U machine. 5 The 3/60, 3/150, and 3/160 are among the machines in question, with one of them allegedly booting. 6
