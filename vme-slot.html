VME slot

Claims: 9 Part of VME

The 745i and 748i share the same CPU board but differ in size and VME slot availability. 1 A VME slot is required for ethernet (VME ENP-10), and the 100Mbps ethernet board for deskside systems uses only the top two connectors, leaving the bottom connector unconnected. 123 The IP4 CPU board with integrated memory and SCSI fills 6 of 7 non-graphics slots in a 12-slot chassis, while the 4D/440 chassis is mostly empty and the Crimson chassis is not. 3 The adhesive on the VME slot of the PIs is causing rusting, possibly because it contains an oxidizer or is good at retaining water. 45
