internal bus

Claims: 3 Background: 1 claim already covered by Wikipedia, see SGI Octane.

The internal bus is exposed for expansion through a special slot on Amigas. 1 The SGI Indy's external SCSI port is part of the internal bus and can cause issues if unterminated, especially with multiple drives. 2 Octane has 40MB/sec SCSI channels, internal bus 0, external bus 1, and can add U160 PCI cards. 3

Sources

  3. Octane has 40MB/sec SCSI channels, internal bus 0, external bus 1, and can add U160 PCI cards. (user-8547, 2019-06-17, exchange 2087, check: supported)
