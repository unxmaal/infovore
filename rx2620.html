rx2620

Claims: 14

The HP rx2620 supports Intel Itanium 2 9000 series processors, has 4 logical processors per socket, and can take dual-core CPUs, which require an adapter cable to work. 123 It can use higher clock Itanium dual-core CPUs due to improved fans. 4 The RX2620 has a VGA output and can output graphics, with a configurable console path that can be set to the serial port; the GSP manual can provide details on configuration. 56 Runelite may fall back to software rendering due to lack of GL support. 5 Debian 7 on an RX2620 shows a red LED. 7 Kernel module loading is broken on zx1, specifically on rx2620s, with the 5.15 kernel, but works on later Itaniums as fixed by Debian people; using a static kernel is an alternative. 89 The rx2620 BMC warns of high ambient temperature with error code 0EA0. 10 The PSUs of the rx2620, rx2600, zx6000, and rx2660 may not actively ventilate in standby, potentially leading to overheating over time if connected to mains without running. 11
