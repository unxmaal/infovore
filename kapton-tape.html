Kapton tape

Claims: 6

Kapton tape was extensively used in SGI's repair style, with extra components secured by it on SGI boards. 12 Rev B stickers and bodges were added to SGI boards with extra components secured by kapton tape. 1 The heatsink on the MXE system is held together with kapton tape. 3 TRAM cards tend to bend due to kapton tape causing some of the legs on the chips to lift, but TRAMS are easily fixable with hot air and some flux. 4 Octane motherboards sometimes use components on kapton tape for bodge jobs. 5 A CR1620 CMOS battery can be replaced with a CR1220 and secured it with Kapton tape. 6
